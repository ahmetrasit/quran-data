# Mechanism

## Primary pathway

The passage is recited under the basmala, **بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**, so command, worship, purification, judgment, and fire remain framed by divine mercy:

> وَمَآ أُمِرُوٓا۟ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ حُنَفَآءَ وَيُقِيمُوا۟ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤْتُوا۟ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ ۚ وَذَٰلِكَ دِينُ ٱلْقَيِّمَةِ  
> إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ مِنْ أَهْلِ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ وَٱلْمُشْرِكِينَ فِى نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ ۚ أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمْ شَرُّ ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ

Verse 5 presents one integrated command rather than a list of unrelated duties. Divine command (**ء م ر B002**) directs creaturely servanthood and worship (**ع ب د B002/B003**) exclusively to Allah (**ء ل ه B001/B002**). Sincerity purifies religion and intention (**خ ل ص B001/B005**) by separating devotion from partnership (**خ ل ص B004**). The worshippers incline toward the straight religion (**ح ن ف B002/B003**), establish and maintain prayer (**ق و م B005**, **ص ل و B003**), and transfer the charitable due (**ء ت ي B002**, **ز ك و B003**). This inward, directional, ritual, and economic integration is named upright religion (**د ي ن B001**, **ق و م B008**).

Verse 6 supplies the inverse pathway. Those who cover and reject truth (**ك ف ر B001/B003/B006**) among the scriptural community (**ء ه ل B001**, **ك ت ب B002**) and the associators (**ش ر ك B002**) enter the burning fire (**ن و ر B002**) and remain there (**خ ل د B001/B002**). Their classification as the worst (**ش ر ر B001**) of created beings (**ب ر ء root_000099:B001**, **ب ر ء root_000100:B001**) judges a response to command within creaturely existence; it does not make rejection an autonomous power outside divine creation.

The primary mechanism is therefore: **command establishes exclusive allegiance; sincerity removes divided ownership of religion; upright inclination gives that allegiance direction; prayer stabilizes it ritually; zakat realizes it through transfer and purification; the integrated whole becomes upright religion. Covering truth and associating partners reverse exclusivity and uprightness, producing an enduring punitive state and a moral ranking within creation**.

## Secondary activations

### All active/latent readings

- **Command includes matter, authority, and binding decree.** Surface directive (**ء م ر B002**) activates the commanded religious affair (**ء م ر B001**), the authority presupposed by command (**ء م ر B003**), a binding written judgment (**ء م ر B002 ↔ ك ت ب B003**), and growth or blessing through compliance (**ء م ر B004**). The commanded acts form an authoritative norm with productive consequences.

- **Servanthood is willing devotion under real lordship.** Owned status (**ع ب د B001**) and coercive subjugation (**ع ب د B004**) remain as contrastive ports beneath creaturely worship (**ع ب د B002/B003**). Sincerity prevents divine command from being reduced to external compulsion: the worshipper freely purifies allegiance to the One who truly possesses authority.

- **Sincerity is separation, concentration, and relationship.** Purification into an unmixed state (**خ ل ص B001**) and religion made pure for God (**خ ل ص B005**) activate exclusive separation from partnership (**خ ل ص B004**), special selection (**خ ل ص B006**), and pure relationship with God and community (**خ ل ص B007**). The inward state reorganizes both vertical allegiance and social belonging.

- **Religion joins obedience to future reckoning.** Religion as submission (**د ي ن B001**) carries an established way (**د ي ن B005**), conscientious trust (**د ي ن B007**), financial obligation (**د ي ن B003**), and recompense or judgment (**د ي ن B002**). The same commanded order that structures worship and zakat becomes the norm by which rejection is judged.

- **Inclination and standing make uprightness dynamic.** **حُنَفَاءَ** supplies intentional directional inclination (**ح ن ف B002/B003**); **يُقِيمُوا** supplies bodily standing, resolve, maintenance, and guardianship (**ق و م B002/B003/B004/B005**); **ٱلْقَيِّمَةِ** supplies straightness (**ق و م B008**). Upright religion is not static correctness but repeatedly turning, rising, and maintaining.

- **Prayer concentrates devotion and mercy.** Prescribed prayer (**ص ل و B003**) activates supplication, praise, and mercy (**ص ل و B002**) and places of worship (**ص ل و B007**). Its establishment gives sincere religion a durable communal and temporal form rather than leaving it as private intention.

- **Zakat combines transfer, growth, and purity.** Giving (**ء ت ي B002**) carries imposed payment (**ء ت ي B008**) and yield or increase (**ء ت ي B007**). Zakat is simultaneously charitable due (**ز ك و B003**), growth (**ز ك و B001**), purity and rectitude (**ز ك و B002**), and fitness of practice (**ز ك و B004**). What leaves the giver's possession is represented as productive purification, not simple loss.

- **Community labels become accountable identities.** Peoplehood (**ء ه ل B001**), written affiliation (**ك ت ب B002/B004**), religious association (**ش ر ك B001/B002**), and unbelieving designation (**ك ف ر B006**) show that communal belonging does not substitute for response. The passage names groups, but the mechanism evaluates how belonging relates to exclusive worship and upright practice.

- **Fire, permanence, evil, and creation form the judgment field.** Fire (**ن و ر B002**) activates visible warning and illumination (**ن و ر B001/B003/B005**) as well as enmity flaring outward (**ن و ر B007**). Remaining in punishment (**خ ل د B001/B002**) carries settled conviction (**خ ل د B004**). Evil becomes manifest (**ش ر ر B008**) and consequential (**ش ر ر B006**), while the duplicated creation branches keep the condemned within creaturely accountability (**ب ر ء root_000099:B001**, **ب ر ء root_000100:B001**).

### Surprising discovery readings

These are discovery-level syntheses selected from `03-candidate-bridges.agent.json` and the mechanical ranking. They preserve the active/latent readings rather than disambiguating them away.

1. **Upright religion contains an obligation-and-clearance economy.** The highest passage-grounded queue bridge joins financial obligation in religion with release of a claim (**د ي ن B003 ↔ ب ر ء root_000099:B004**); the parallel source also supports **د ي ن B003 ↔ ب ر ء root_000100:B004**. Contractual inscription and release reinforce the cluster (**ك ت ب B005 ↔ ب ر ء root_000099:B004**, **ك ت ب B005 ↔ ب ر ء root_000100:B004**), while valuation appears through **د ي ن B003 ↔ ق و م B010**. The point is not that **دِين** or **بَرِيَّة** should be reduced to debt law. The underlay explains why zakat, command, and judgment cohere: creatures receive an obligation, acts acquire assessed value, payment embodies response, and judgment determines liability or clearance.

2. **Sincerity is also an ownership boundary.** Exclusive separation from partnership (**خ ل ص B004**) bridges to sharing/partnership (**خ ل ص B004 ↔ ش ر ك B001**) and to inscription in a group or register (**خ ل ص B004 ↔ ك ت ب B004**). Partnership itself bridges to property obligation and release (**ش ر ك B001 ↔ ب ر ء root_000099:B004**). Sincere religion therefore has a surprising allocative logic: devotion, obedience, and the charitable due must be assigned to their rightful objects. Polytheism divides divine allegiance; withheld or distorted obligation divides material allegiance.

3. **Giving produces rather than merely diminishes.** The command's blessing port joins zakat's growth (**ء م ر B004 ↔ ز ك و B001**), and yielded increase joins the same root (**ء ت ي B007 ↔ ز ك و B001**). The queue extends this into harvest imagery through **ء ت ي B007 ↔ ك ف ر B010**, where a fruit-covering port sits beside yield, and through **ك ف ر B010 ↔ ن و ر B004**, where covering opens toward blossom. These are not literal crop claims. They make the economic paradox legible: transfer in zakat resembles sowing—what is given leaves immediate possession yet participates in increase, while refusal can turn protective covering into obstructive concealment.

4. **Purification and covering are rival operations.** Purity and rectitude bridge strongly to the covering or effacing of sin (**ز ك و B002 ↔ ك ف ر B009**), and that covering reaches moral clearance through both source branches (**ك ف ر B009 ↔ ب ر ء root_000099:B002**, **ك ف ر B009 ↔ ب ر ء root_000100:B002**). Surface unbelief, however, uses **ك ف ر B003** for covering truth and links it to association (**ك ف ر B003 ↔ ش ر ك B002**). The discovery is a process distinction: sincere practice purifies the agent and clears responsibility; unbelief covers the evidence and preserves the liability. A shared covering image can therefore lead to opposite moral states depending on what is covered.

5. **Prayer and fire occupy opposing ports of one root.** Verse 5 establishes prayer through **ص ل و B003**, while verse 6 activates meeting punitive heat through **ص ل و B001** alongside **ن و ر B002**. This does not collapse prayer into fire-contact. It sharpens the transition: one relation to divine command is deliberately established as worship; the opposed relation becomes an enduring encounter with judgment. The same root reservoir makes the contrast experiential—established devotional contact versus imposed punitive contact.

6. **“Worst of creation” retains a release possibility in its underlay.** Both merged-source creation branches are surface-active and mechanically linked (**ب ر ء root_000099:B001 ↔ ب ر ء root_000100:B001**). Their secondary ports include moral separation and clearance (**ب ر ء root_000099:B002/B004**, **ب ر ء root_000100:B002/B004**). Thus the final classification is not merely an insult attached to a group. It closes a mechanism in which created beings were offered an upright route toward purified obligation and clearance but instead fixed themselves in covering and partnership.

7. **The bounded queue also contains locally weak material clusters.** Melted fat and roasting (**ء ه ل B006 ↔ ش ر ر B005**) receive high mechanical scores because Hellfire is present, but the culinary detail is not locally asserted. The same applies to perfume containers (**ع ب د B012 ↔ ك ف ر B010**), race motion (**ع ب د B009 ↔ ص ل و B006**), livestock/youth (**ء م ر B009 ↔ ش ر ر B010**), and domestic pounding/food preparation (**ص ل و B008 ↔ ش ر ر B002**). These remain preserved queue readings, not central mechanism claims.

## What activates what

- **ء م ر** activates **ع ب د / ء ل ه**: divine command is realized as exclusive worship of Allah.
- **ع ب د / ء ل ه** activate **خ ل ص / د ي ن**: worship becomes unmixed allegiance and obedient religion.
- **خ ل ص** activates **ح ن ف** by removing partnership and giving devotion a single direction.
- **ح ن ف** activates **ق و م**: directional inclination is stabilized as uprightness, resolve, and maintenance.
- **ق و م** activates **ص ل و** by establishing prayer as a durable practice.
- **ء ت ي** activates **ز ك و**: transfer of the due produces purification and growth.
- **ص ل و / ز ك و** complete the practical content of **د ي ن / ق و م** as upright religion.
- **ك ف ر / ش ر ك** reverse **خ ل ص / ح ن ف**: covering truth and dividing allegiance undo sincerity and straight orientation.
- **ك ف ر / ش ر ك** activate **ن و ر / خ ل د** as rejection reaches fire and enduring residence.
- **ن و ر / خ ل د** activate **ش ر ر / ب ر ء** as punishment culminates in a moral classification within creation.
- In the discovery layer, **د ي ن → ب ر ء / ق و م** creates obligation, clearance, and valuation; **خ ل ص → ش ر ك / ك ت ب** defines ownership and membership boundaries; **ء ت ي → ز ك و** converts giving into yield; and **ك ف ر → ب ر ء** distinguishes concealment from moral clearance.

## What the underlay explains

A surface gloss can list worship, sincerity, prayer, zakat, unbelief, Hell, and “worst of creation.” The branch underlay explains why they form a single institutional and moral mechanism. Sincerity concentrates allegiance; upright inclination supplies direction; prayer maintains the vertical relation; zakat tests the same exclusivity in material possession; religion joins these acts into an accountable order. Unbelief and partnership are the inverse operations: they cover truth and divide what should be undivided. The discovery clusters add the missing transactional logic—command creates obligation, zakat embodies assessed response, sincere separation establishes rightful ownership, and judgment settles liability. The final ranking within creation is therefore the outcome of an integrated response or refusal, not an isolated label.
