# Mechanism

## Primary pathway

The analyzed recitation includes the basmala and the complete surah:

> أَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ  
> وَوَضَعْنَا عَنكَ وِزْرَكَ  
> ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنقَضَ ظَهْرَكَ  
> وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ  
> فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا  
> إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًۭا  
> فَإِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَٱنصَبْ  
> وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرْغَب

The primary mechanism moves from divine relief to renewed, directed exertion.

1. **Inner capacity is expanded.** The opening rhetorical question affirms that the addressee's chest has been divinely opened and expanded (`ش ر ح B003`; `ص د ر B001`). The act creates receptivity and room within the person rather than merely changing an external circumstance.
2. **The crushing load is removed.** The burden is put down from the addressee (`و ض ع B001`; `و ز ر B002`), and its severity is made bodily and audible: it had made the back creak (`ن ق ض B005`; `ظ ه ر B002`). Relief therefore answers a load that had reached the structure carrying it.
3. **Relief is paired with elevation.** Divine action not only lowers the burden but raises the addressee's honorable mention (`ر ف ع B002`; `ذ ك ر B007`). The movement is coordinated: weight is taken off, while renown and public remembrance are lifted.
4. **The past acts establish the rule repeated in verses 5-6.** Hardship and ease are presented together (`ع س ر B001`; `ي س ر B001`). The repeated clause does not simply promise that difficulty will eventually vanish; `مَعَ` makes ease a co-present opening within hardship's field.
5. **Freed capacity becomes a new task.** When an occupation is completed (`ف ر غ B001`), the command is to set oneself to fresh labor and exertion (`ن ص ب B004`), not to treat relief as permanent inactivity.
6. **Desire gives exertion its direction.** The final imperative directs aspiration toward the Lord (`ر ب ب B001`; `ر غ ب B001`). The surah closes the loop opened in verse 1: expanded inward capacity becomes focused work, and focused work becomes Lord-directed desire.

The basmala, **بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**, frames expansion, relief, ease, and direction as mercy. That mercy does not cancel exertion; it repairs capacity so that exertion can continue without the former crushing load. The supplied QAC inventory begins at verse 1, so no basmala-only roots or branch IDs are invented below.

In compact form:

```text
chest expanded
  -> burden put down from the creaking back
  -> honorable mention raised
  -> ease disclosed as accompanying hardship
  -> one completed task frees capacity for renewed exertion
  -> renewed exertion is oriented by desire toward the Lord
```

## Secondary activations

### All active/latent readings

These readings deepen the surface mechanism without replacing the QAC-resolved senses.

**Chest-expansion is cognitive and motivational as well as spatial.** Surface expansion (`ش ر ح B003`) acts on the inward/anatomical chest (`ص د ر B001`). Secondary `ش ر ح B001` adds clarification and disclosure, while `ذ ك ر B009` adds reminder and renewed attention. `ش ر ح B005` adds expansive desire, activated by the closing `فَٱرْغَب`. The chest is therefore made roomy for understanding, attention, intention, and aspiration—not merely enlarged as a bodily object.

**Chest and back form a whole-person load system.** The chest and back are mechanically paired through anatomy (`ص د ر B001 ↔ ظ ه ر B002`). The chest receives expansion; the back is released from what it could scarcely carry. Secondary back branches add burden-bearing (`ظ ه ر B005`) and support or strengthening (`ظ ه ر B006`). A flat gloss treats the first three verses as separate favors; the body underlay shows one rehabilitation: inward room is increased while posterior load is decreased.

**Putting down and raising create a vertical transformation.** Concrete lowering/removal (`و ض ع B001`) activates literal lifting (`ر ف ع B001`), while lowered rank (`و ض ع B005`) contrasts raised rank and renown (`ر ف ع B002`). `ظ ه ر B024` adds honor visibly displayed; `ظ ه ر B013` adds removal of shame or blame. The movement is not only from heavy to light but from compressed or lowered standing to publicly recognized dignity.

**The burden can be undone and shared.** `ن ق ض B001` adds undoing what was bound or constructed beneath the explicit creaking (`ن ق ض B005`). The burden-root also supplies a helper who bears another's load (`و ز ر B004`), joined to back-support and strengthening (`ظ ه ر B006`) and, more remotely, supporting allies (`ظ ه ر B016`). Divine removal therefore has a relational underlay: relief is not self-extraction but help that changes what the addressee must carry alone.

**Raised mention becomes communicative.** Surface honorable renown (`ذ ك ر B007`) is joined to spoken mention and naming (`ذ ك ر B004`), while elevation includes broadcasting news (`ر ف ع B005`) and raising the voice (`ر ف ع B010`). Remembrance of God (`ذ ك ر B005`), revealed instruction (`ذ ك ر B006`), and renewed attention (`ذ ك ر B009`) connect public mention with the devotional close. The raised `ذِكْر` is thus not static status only; it can circulate through speech, testimony, remembrance, and teaching.

**Hardship and ease include process and material condition.** Surface difficulty (`ع س ر B001`) activates resistance and obstruction (`ع س ر B004`) and concrete financial straitness (`ع س ر B002`). Ease (`ي س ر B001`) adds tractable movement (`ي س ر B005`) and ample means (`ي س ر B003`). The material branches do not promise wealth as the verse's sole meaning. They make the contrast concrete: hardship constricts agency and provision, while ease opens motion and capacity.

**Repetition stabilizes co-presence rather than simple succession.** Both occurrences pair the same definite hardship with an indefinite ease. The branch layer reinforces the syntax: obstruction (`ع س ر B004`) and opening (`ي س ر B001`) are processual conditions that can occupy one field. The preceding verses instantiate the claim—burden and support, creaking and removal, low weight and raised mention—before it is stated twice.

**Finishing is available capacity, not vacancy as an end.** Surface `فَرَغْتَ` means finishing and becoming free from an occupation (`ف ر غ B001`). Secondary `ف ر غ B006` turns that freedom into devoted intention. `ن ص ب B001` adds standing or setting oneself upright beneath the direct command to labor and exert (`ن ص ب B004`). Ease is therefore productive: it releases enough capacity to stand into the next task.

**Exertion is gathered by a single orientation.** Lordship includes sovereign ownership (`ر ب ب B001`), nurture and completion (`ر ب ب B002`), and divinely grounded instruction (`ر ب ب B003`). Desire is directly aimed toward the Lord (`ر غ ب B001`), while `ر غ ب B004` adds desirable abundant gift as a secondary provision pole. The ending prevents ceaseless effort from becoming aimless busyness: completion, exertion, and aspiration all receive one direction.

### What activates what

- `نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ` activates inward expansion, clarification, disclosure, receptivity, and spacious desire (`ش ر ح B001/B003/B005`; `ص د ر B001`).
- `وَوَضَعْنَا عَنكَ وِزْرَكَ` activates putting down, removal, heavy burden, lowering, undoing, and supportive help (`و ض ع B001/B005`; `و ز ر B002/B004`; `ن ق ض B001`).
- `أَنقَضَ ظَهْرَكَ` activates acoustic creaking, bodily overload, burden-bearing, and the need for back-support (`ن ق ض B005/B006`; `ظ ه ر B002/B005/B006`).
- `وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ` activates elevation, rank, broadcast speech, raised voice, spoken mention, honorable renown, praise, and renewed attention (`ر ف ع B001/B002/B005/B010`; `ذ ك ر B004/B005/B007/B009`).
- Repeated `مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا` activates severity, obstruction, financial straitness, opening, tractability, and provision (`ع س ر B001/B002/B004`; `ي س ر B001/B003/B005`).
- `فَإِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَٱنصَبْ` activates completion, available capacity, devoted intention, upright setting, focused exertion, and endurance (`ف ر غ B001/B006`; `ن ص ب B001/B004`; `ع س ر B001`).
- `وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرْغَب` activates sovereign lordship, nurture, instruction, directed aspiration, and desirable provision (`ر ب ب B001/B002/B003`; `ر غ ب B001/B004`; `ش ر ح B005`).

What the secondary layer explains that a surface gloss misses is the passage's capacity cycle. Expansion is not comfort detached from action; burden-removal repairs the person, raised mention externalizes the repaired capacity, ease appears within hardship as usable opening, and completed work becomes the threshold of fresh exertion. The final desire clause ensures that the cycle is devotional rather than merely productive.

## Surprising discovery readings

The discovery ranking is a mechanical queue for likely surprise value, not an interpretive verdict. These readings are therefore separated from the active/latent mechanism.

### 1. Raised renown becomes broadcast testimony

The queue's strongest locally controlled edge is `ر ف ع B005 ↔ ذ ك ر B004` (discovery hint 17; evidence hint 19), joining broadcasting or making news known to spoken mention through communication, speech, and the rare testimony/witness theme. `ر ف ع B010` adds raised voice, while `ذ ك ر B007` remains the surface honorable renown. The discovery shows how elevated mention operates: honor is not merely placed at a higher rank but carried publicly in speech and testimony. It does not reduce `ذِكْرَكَ` to a single utterance or claim that reputation is self-promotion.

### 2. The expanded chest becomes directed aspiration

`ش ر ح B005 ↔ ر غ ب B001` links expansive desire with directed aspiration through emotion, motivation, intention, and attachment (discovery hint 12; evidence hint 20). Though not the queue's highest numerical surprise, it is structurally exact: the surah opens by expanding the chest and closes by directing desire. The internal space created by divine expansion becomes motivational room for Lord-directed longing. This does not make `نَشْرَحْ` mean “desire”; it explains what the opened capacity can do.

### 3. Burden-removal opens a support-and-alliance underlay

`و ز ر B004 ↔ ظ ه ر B006` joins a load-bearing helper to back-support through alliance, cooperation, dependence, and loyalty (discovery hint 14; evidence hint 14). The explicit burden had made the back creak; the secondary network supplies the relational opposite—another bears with and strengthens the carrier. Divine relief can therefore be read as more than deletion of weight: it creates supported capacity and breaks the isolation of bearing alone.

### 4. Ease and desire carry a provision-and-generosity economy

The high-ranked edge `ظ ه ر B023 ↔ ر غ ب B004` joins giving from surplus wealth to a desirable abundant gift through economy, exchange, generosity, and welfare (discovery hint 16; evidence hint 24). `ي س ر B003 ↔ ر غ ب B004` connects ample means to that gift, while `ع س ر B002 ↔ ر غ ب B004` supplies the scarcity contrast (both hint 14; evidence hint 18). This gives the hardship/ease pair a social-economic analogue: opening is not hoarded capacity but surplus capable of becoming desired benefit. The passage does not explicitly command almsgiving or promise material affluence.

### 5. Putting down can become protected entrustment

`و ض ع B010 ↔ ر ب ب B011` joins depositing something with covenantal protection, law, trust, and loyalty (discovery hint 14; evidence hint 16). This offers a cautious secondary distinction: the burden is removed, but freed capacity is not ownerless or purposeless; the closing turn to the Lord places it within an entrusted relation. The edge does not make `وَضَعْنَا` mean “deposited” in verse 2 or convert the surah into contract law.

### 6. High-ranked clusters retained but not promoted

Several mechanically strong discoveries lack adequate local control:

- Left/right bodily orientation (`ع س ر B005 ↔ ي س ر B004`, discovery hint 16) is a homonymic pair, not the meaning of hardship and ease here.
- Reproduction and sexuality (`ش ر ح B004 ↔ ف ر غ B005`; `ش ر ح B004 ↔ ذ ك ر B001`), difficult birth, and livestock fertility/dairy (`ع س ر B007 ↔ ي س ر B006`; `ي س ر B006 ↔ ر ب ب B009`) have no local trigger.
- Ritual sacrifice and gambling lots (`ي س ر B007 ↔ ن ص ب B002`, hint 16), warfare (`و ز ر B003 ↔ ن ص ب B008`), and competitive dominance (`و ز ر B006 ↔ ظ ه ر B007`) are not promoted.
- Travel and migration (`ص د ر B003 ↔ ر ف ع B011`), grammar (`ر ف ع B012 ↔ ن ص ب B007`), proper names (`ع س ر B012 ↔ ي س ر B010`), food preparation, textile/body, plant, pasture, and animal-motion clusters remain mechanical evidence only.

The useful discovery-level synthesis is:

```text
inner expansion -> directed aspiration
burden removal -> supported capacity
raised renown -> circulating testimony
ease within hardship -> surplus opening for renewed work and benefit
all freed capacity -> entrusted orientation toward the Lord
```
