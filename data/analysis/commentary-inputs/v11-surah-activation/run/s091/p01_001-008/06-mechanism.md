# Mechanism

## Primary pathway

The analyzed recitation includes the basmala and the assigned oath sequence:

> وَٱلشَّمْسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا  
> وَٱلْقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا  
> وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّىٰهَا  
> وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا يَغْشَىٰهَا  
> وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنَىٰهَا  
> وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا طَحَىٰهَا  
> وَنَفْسٍۢ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا  
> فَأَلْهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقْوَىٰهَا

The primary mechanism moves from recurring cosmic signs to the moral constitution of the soul.

1. **Illumination is presented as an ordered cycle.** The sun and its extending forenoon brightness provide the primary light (`ش م س B001`; `ض ح و B001`). The moon follows that light in sequence (`ق م ر B001`; `ت ل و B001`); day uncovers and manifests it (`ن ه ر B002`; `ج ل و B001`); night covers it (`ل ي ل B001`; `غ ش و B001`). Following, disclosure, and covering are coordinated phases, not rival cosmic powers.
2. **The cycle is placed inside a constructed and extended cosmos.** The elevated sky is built by joining and ordering its parts (`س م و B004`; `ب ن ي B001`), while the lower earth is spread and extended (`ء ر ض B001`; `ط ح و B001`). Height and breadth supply the stable spatial field in which the light-cycle operates.
3. **Cosmic ordering is transferred to the human interior.** The soul-self (`ن ف س B011`) is formed in sound, ordered completeness (`س و ي B002`). The movement from “built the sky” and “spread the earth” to “proportioned the soul” makes the soul the inward counterpart of the ordered cosmos: it too is composed, oriented, and given a field of possible action.
4. **Proportion culminates in morally differentiated awareness.** The causative `فَأَلْهَمَهَا` casts recognition into the soul's interior (`ل ه م B002`), and the two objects identify contrary moral directions: breach and departure from right (`ف ج ر B004`) versus disciplined protective piety (`و ق ي B002`). The verse presents both as recognized possibilities within one proportioned moral subject; it does not collapse them into one value or say that moral breach is itself sound proportion.

The basmala, **بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**, belongs to the analysis frame. Its mercy formula reinforces relief and expansion within the soul (`ن ف س B002`) and the protective rather than merely fearful character of taqwā (`و ق ي B001/B002`). Because the supplied QAC root inventory for this unit starts with verse 1, no basmala-only roots or branch IDs are invented below.

In compact form:

```text
sunlight extends
  -> moon follows in sequence
  -> day discloses || night covers
  -> sky is constructed above || earth is spread below
  -> the soul is proportioned as an inward ordered field
  -> moral recognition distinguishes breach from protective piety
```

## Secondary activations

### All active/latent readings

These readings deepen the surface mechanism without replacing the QAC-resolved senses.

**The oath is a recited sequence about sequence.** Following is surface-active in the moon's succession (`ت ل و B001`), while `ت ل و B002/B003/B007` add recited order, continuity after what precedes, and one oath-unit or voice following another. The repeated initial `وَ` and repeated `إِذَا` make the form enact its content: clause follows clause as moon follows sun and night follows day. `ت ل و B004` adds a consequence or obligation that follows its bearer, weakly preparing the transfer from cosmic succession to moral consequence without importing verses 9-10 into the assigned unit.

**Light is disclosure, not just brightness.** Forenoon activates exposure and visible emergence (`ض ح و B002`) as well as clear brilliance (`ض ح و B005`). Day opens and widens (`ن ه ر B002/B003`); its causative unveiling polishes into clarity (`ج ل و B002`) and makes identity publicly recognizable (`ج ل و B006`). The solar sequence therefore provides an epistemic model: what is present becomes perceptible and distinguishable.

**Night supplies the contrary operation of veiling.** Night is the direct dark phase (`ل ي ل B001`) and an arena of activity within the recurring cycle (`ل ي ل B002/B003`). Covering is surface-active (`غ ش و B001`), while its secondary branches add enveloping (`غ ش و B003`) and veiled or overcome consciousness (`غ ش و B005`). The last branch is an embodied analogue, not a claim that ordinary night destroys awareness. Together with daylight disclosure, it explains why inspiration can be described as moral recognition: the soul can apprehend what has been made inwardly clear, or allow awareness to be covered.

**Building, spreading, and proportioning form one design vocabulary.** Sky-construction includes composed constitution (`ب ن ي B002`), stabilizing supports (`ب ن ي B009`), and embodied formation (`ب ن ي B010`). Sky-height and visible elevation (`س م و B001/B002`) contrast with earth's gravity and stasis (`ء ر ض B006`), while earth-spreading adds great scale, extended motion, and ground contact (`ط ح و B002/B005/B006`). The soul's fashioning then activates balance, intentional orientation, maturation, fairness, and differentiation (`س و ي B001/B004/B005/B006/B007`). A surface gloss lists three created objects; the underlay shows one continuous operation of composition, extension, stabilization, maturation, and directed balance.

**The soul is simultaneously embodied, identical with itself, aware, and morally disposed.** Beneath the surface soul-self (`ن ف س B011`) are breath (`ن ف س B001`), sustaining life-blood (`ن ف س B004`), the self's identity (`ن ف س B012`), inner awareness and intention (`ن ف س B013`), and moral character or appetite (`ن ف س B014`). Inspiration addresses this whole subject: moral cognition is inward, but it concerns an embodied agent whose intention and appetite can become action.

**Inspiration is reception without compulsion.** The direct sense is moral recognition cast into the inner mind (`ل ه م B002`). `ل ه م B001` adds the bodily image of taking something fully into the interior, and `ل ه م B004` adds breadth and power. These branches thicken inspiration as inward reception, but the paired alternatives and the orientation branches of `س و ي B004/B007` preserve moral differentiation. The passage gives the soul recognition of both courses; it does not gloss inspiration as forcing the soul to enact fujūr.

**Fujūr is a breach; taqwā is a maintained boundary.** Moral departure is surface-active (`ف ج ر B004`), with rupture, splitting, and loss of restraint beneath it (`ف ج ر B001/B003`). Taqwā is surface-active as disciplined protective piety (`و ق ي B002`), grounded in a barrier that wards harm (`و ق ي B001`). Balance and fair position (`س و ي B001/B006`) do not mean moral neutrality between the two. They mean that the proportioned soul can recognize two genuinely distinct orientations (`س و ي B004/B007`): one breaks the protective limit, while the other maintains it.

**The cosmic field is life-supporting as well as visually ordered.** Forenoon carries grazing and nourishment (`ض ح و B003`); the lunar cycle carries wakefulness and broad water/pasture associations (`ق م ر B008/B009`); earth carries fertility (`ء ر ض B002`); and the soul carries life-sustaining provision (`ن ف س B008`). This ecological underlay keeps the oath from becoming an abstract astronomy lesson: cycles of light, atmosphere, ground, growth, bodily life, and moral awareness belong to one created field.

### What activates what

- `ٱلشَّمْسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا` activates solar illumination, extension, exposure, and atmospheric clarity (`ش م س B001`; `ض ح و B001/B002/B005`).
- `ٱلْقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا` activates ordered succession, temporal continuity, and the recited clause-chain itself (`ق م ر B001/B009`; `ت ل و B001/B002/B003/B007`).
- `ٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّىٰهَا` activates opening, perceptibility, polishing, recognition, and clear sky (`ن ه ر B002/B003`; `ج ل و B001/B002/B006/B007`).
- `ٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا يَغْشَىٰهَا` activates darkness, recurrence, enveloping cover, and the analogue of veiled awareness (`ل ي ل B001/B002/B003`; `غ ش و B001/B003/B005`).
- `ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنَىٰهَا` activates height, visible expanse, composition, support, and ordered structure (`س م و B001/B002/B004`; `ب ن ي B001/B002/B009`).
- `ٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا طَحَىٰهَا` activates lower ground, fertility, gravity, horizontal extension, great scale, and ground contact (`ء ر ض B001/B002/B006`; `ط ح و B001/B002/B005/B006/B008`).
- `نَفْسٍۢ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا` activates embodied selfhood, identity, intention, moral character, balance, orientation, maturity, and differentiated alternatives (`ن ف س B001/B004/B011/B012/B013/B014`; `س و ي B001/B002/B004/B005/B006/B007`).
- `فَأَلْهَمَهَا` activates inward moral cognition and complete interior reception (`ل ه م B001/B002/B004`; `ن ف س B013`).
- `فُجُورَهَا وَتَقْوَىٰهَا` activates rupture and loss of restraint against boundary, protection, and disciplined piety (`ف ج ر B001/B003/B004`; `و ق ي B001/B002`).

What the secondary layer explains that a surface gloss misses is the passage's transfer mechanism: the cosmos repeatedly makes distinctions perceptible—sun/moon, day/night, above/below, construction/extension—then the same logic is installed within the soul as awareness of two moral directions. Inspiration is thus not an isolated insertion after a list of oaths; it is the inward culmination of a passage about ordered succession, disclosure, boundaries, and proportion.

## Surprising discovery readings

The discovery ranking is a mechanical queue for likely surprise value and does not prune or adjudicate the candidate reservoir. The following readings are kept separate from the active/latent account.

### 1. The root of moral breach also carries the passage's dawn mechanism

The strongest locally controlled surprise joins day opening to dawn breaking: `ن ه ر B002 ↔ ف ج ر B002` (discovery hint 15; evidence hint 26), reinforced by `ج ل و B007 ↔ ف ج ر B002`, `ق م ر B001 ↔ ف ج ر B002`, and `ن ف س B009 ↔ ف ج ر B002`. The same `ف ج ر` inventory that supplies surface fujūr as moral breach (`ف ج ر B004`) also contains rupture/splitting (`B001`) and dawn bursting from night (`B002`). This does not make `فُجُورَهَا` mean “its dawn.” It reveals a shared mechanism of breaking through a limit. In the cosmic sequence, rupture opens darkness into light; in the moral register, fujūr is the agent's wrongful breach of a protective boundary. The passage distinguishes the value of the act even while reusing its structural image.

### 2. Cosmic illumination becomes an inner dawn

`ن ف س B009` describes dawn-like opening and expansion and is mechanically linked to `ف ج ر B002` and `ج ل و B007`. Together with exposure (`ض ح و B002`), opening (`ن ه ر B003`), and inspiration into inner awareness (`ل ه م B002`; `ن ف س B013`), the light-cycle becomes an analogue for moral disclosure: the soul is made able to recognize its two directions as day makes the sun visible. The reading is an underlay, not a claim that the soul literally emits dawn-light.

### 3. The astronomical cycle is also an ecology of provision

The queue strongly joins lunar water/pasture with fertile earth (`ق م ر B008 ↔ ء ر ض B002`, discovery hint 16; evidence hint 30), lunar abundance with life-sustaining provision (`ق م ر B008 ↔ ن ف س B008`, hint 15), and forenoon grazing with both (`ض ح و B003 ↔ ق م ر B008/ء ر ض B002`, hint 15). This cluster turns the oath's celestial sequence into a habitat machine: timed light and lunar cycles accompany pasture, water, fertile ground, nourishment, and embodied life. The soul is therefore proportioned inside a sustaining ecology rather than detached from matter. Water and grazing remain latent; neither is a surface referent in verses 1-8.

### 4. Brightness and taqwā meet at purity

`ض ح و B005 ↔ و ق ي B002` is mechanically modest in raw evidence but highly ranked for its rare purity/cleansing bridge (discovery hint 15; evidence hint 7). Clear forenoon brilliance gives protective piety an optical analogue: taqwā maintains an inner clarity in which moral directions remain distinguishable. The edge is reinforced by `س و ي B006` (fair balance) and the direct ethical opposition `ف ج ر B004 ↔ و ق ي B002`. It does not reduce taqwā to brightness or ritual cleansing.

### 5. Covering divides into shelter and obscuration

`غ ش و B001 ↔ ب ن ي B004` joins covering with protective shelter through security, containment, equipment, and textile themes (discovery hint 15; evidence hint 24). This creates a useful distinction inside the passage: night covers the sun, built shelter covers and protects, and taqwā is itself a protective barrier (`و ق ي B001`). Covering is therefore not intrinsically negative. Its moral value depends on function—protection preserves the subject, whereas `غ ش و B005` supplies the contrary image of awareness being veiled. The material container/textile details remain secondary.

### 6. Inspiration has an embodied nourishment analogue

`ب ن ي B010 ↔ ل ه م B001` links bodily building through nourishment to taking something fully into the interior (discovery hint 14; evidence hint 14); `ن ف س B011 ↔ ل ه م B001` adds embodiment (hint 13). The useful mechanism is assimilation: just as nourishment is taken inward and helps constitute embodied life, inspired moral recognition is received into the self's awareness and character. This does not make `أَلْهَمَ` mean feeding or swallowing.

### 7. High-ranked clusters retained but not promoted

Several top discoveries have no adequate local trigger:

- The queue's highest edge, `ق م ر B007 ↔ ن ف س B016` (discovery hint 18), concerns gambling, chance, competition, and a gaming stake. Although deceptive gain could exemplify fujūr elsewhere, no game of chance appears here.
- `ن ه ر B007 ↔ ل ي ل B004` (hint 18) joins proper-name branches, including Laylā, through onomastics and cultural identity. Day and night are common nouns in this unit, not personal names.
- `ت ل و B008 ↔ ن ف س B004/B011` (hint 16) joins a last breath with blood, vitality, and the living soul. The passage contains a soul but no death scene, so terminal physiology is not promoted.
- `ض ح و B004 ↔ ب ن ي B003` (hint 15) joins the Feast-sacrifice branch with the Sacred House. The basmala supplies a devotional frame, but neither sacrifice nor sanctuary is named.
- Reproduction and household edges (`غ ش و B004 ↔ ب ن ي B006`; `ت ل و B006 ↔ ن ف س B005`), anatomy and illness edges (`ج ل و B005 ↔ ن ف س B004`; `ء ر ض B011 ↔ ن ف س B004`), bird/hunting clusters (`ق م ر B012 ↔ و ق ي B005`; `ق م ر B003 ↔ ج ل و B008`), and accounting/weight (`س و ي B013 ↔ و ق ي B004`) remain mechanical evidence only.

The useful discovery-level synthesis is:

```text
cosmic opening and covering
  -> an ecology that sustains embodied life
  -> inward assimilation of moral recognition
  -> breach as wrongful boundary-breaking || taqwā as protective clarity
```
