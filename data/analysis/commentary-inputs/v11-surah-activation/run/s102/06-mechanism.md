# Mechanism

## Primary pathway

The basmala places the passage under divine mercy before it names human diversion. That frame matters at the end: **al-naʿīm** is not an autonomous possession but a favor received from God and therefore a trust about which its recipient can be questioned.

The surface pathway begins when diversion (`ل ه و B001`) is supplied by competitive increase (`ك ث ر B001`, `ك ث ر B002`). Quantity becomes a contest: more goods, claims, people, or status occupy attention until the competitors reach the graves. The wording of visitation (`ز و ر B003`) makes the grave a reached station rather than a permanent possession, while burial and death (`ق ب ر B001`, `ق ب ر B005`) terminate the worldly comparison.

The repeated **kallā**, **sawfa**, and **thumma** then interrupt that diversion and stage a forced change in epistemic condition. Deferred knowledge (`ع ل م B001`) becomes knowledge with certainty (`ي ق ن B001`), then seeing (`ر ء ي B001`) becomes direct eye-verification (`ع ي ن B001`, `ع ي ن B002`, `ع ي ن B013`). The thing verified is the Blaze (`ج ح م B001`). What could be ignored as information becomes encountered evidence.

The final questioning (`س ء ل B001`) reverses the meaning of abundance. Enjoyment and favor (`ن ع م B001`, `ن ع م B002`) cease to be entries on a competitive scoreboard and become entrusted benefits whose use must be answered for. The primary mechanism is therefore:

**competitive abundance diverts → death closes the contest → deferred knowledge becomes certainty and direct sight → bounty is disclosed as an accountable trust.**

## Secondary activations

### All active/latent readings

- Diversion carries a pleasure-and-attention layer (`ل ه و B002`), while increase can form escalating or self-reinforcing clusters (`ك ث ر B007`, `ن ع م B010`). The problem is not merely possession but an attention system continually fed by comparison.
- **Takāthur** can gather numerical increase, rivalry, wealth, claims, and social rank (`ك ث ر B001`, `ك ث ر B002`, `ك ث ر B003`, `ع ي ن B011`, `ع ي ن B012`, `ع ي ن B015`, `ن ع م B002`). These readings broaden the object of competition without limiting it to one kind of property.
- Visitation also activates deviation and strenuous movement (`ز و ر B001`, `ز و ر B007`), while the grave activates concealment and death as metaphorical closure (`ق ب ر B002`, `ق ب ر B005`). The competitors move toward a boundary they have excluded from their valuation.
- The epistemic sequence includes knowledge, sign, judgment, visible evidence, manifestation, and actuality (`ع ل م B001`, `ع ل م B002`, `ي ق ن B001`, `ر ء ي B001`, `ر ء ي B002`, `ر ء ي B004`, `ر ء ي B011`, `ر ء ي B012`, `ع ي ن B001`, `ع ي ن B002`, `ع ي ن B013`). It is a change in mode of access, not only an increase in information.
- False valuation and public display remain available beneath the rivalry (`ز و ر B002`, `ز و ر B006`, `ر ء ي B005`, `ر ء ي B006`, `ج ح م B005`, `ن ع م B003`). Social appearance can make an apparent good look decisive until direct verification removes the audience-dependent measure.
- The Blaze is both fire and mortal boundary (`ج ح م B001`, `ج ح م B002`); its glaring-eye underlay (`ج ح م B003`) gives the act of seeing a reciprocal force. The subject does not merely inspect an object but faces an exposure from which attention cannot turn away.
- Questioning includes accountability, desired objects, provision received, and answer or response (`س ء ل B001`, `س ء ل B002`, `س ء ل B003`, `س ء ل B004`). Favor includes enjoyment, luxury, praise, affirmation, and what gladdens the eye (`ن ع م B001`, `ن ع م B002`, `ن ع م B003`, `ن ع م B004`, `ن ع م B013`). This makes the last verse an audit of desire, reception, valuation, and response.
- The more remote anatomical, zoological, botanical, meteorological, agricultural, textile, and tool senses remain part of the activated inventory. They are preserved as secondary possibilities because their branches recur strongly in the queue, but the local wording does not supply enough passage-level pressure to make them explanatory pathways.

### Surprising discovery readings

1. **What is counted becomes what must be answered for.** The claims-and-demands sense of increase connects directly with accountability (`ك ث ر B003 ↔ س ء ل B001`), while the cash/credit pair (`ع ي ن B011 ↔ ع ي ن B012`) supplies a financial underlay. The passage changes wealth from apparent credit into an account whose holder must answer.

2. **Favor is an input to the diversion system, not proof that the system is sound.** Provision and productive economy connect diversion with livestock wealth (`ل ه و B003 ↔ ن ع م B005`), and received provision connects diversion with questioning (`ل ه و B003 ↔ س ء ل B003`). The agricultural and mill imagery stays figurative here: gifts are fed into, processed by, and consumed within the mechanism of distraction before their use is audited.

3. **Competitive increase depends on an audience and a false measure.** Pleasure and kin-status connect (`ل ه و B002 ↔ ع ي ن B015`); false valuation connects with ostentatious display (`ز و ر B002 ↔ ر ء ي B005`); and display connects with shameless social exposure (`ر ء ي B005 ↔ ج ح م B005`). Direct certainty dismantles a hierarchy sustained by being seen by others.

4. **The epistemic staircase changes the kind of evidence.** Certainty connects with seeing and judgment (`ي ق ن B001 ↔ ر ء ي B001`, `ي ق ن B001 ↔ ر ء ي B002`) and with direct verification (`ي ق ن B001 ↔ ع ي ن B002`); manifestation also connects with direct verification (`ر ء ي B012 ↔ ع ي ن B002`). A sign known at a distance becomes actuality encountered without an interpretive escape.

5. **The grave is a boundary but not a home.** The ordinary visit connects with temporary lodging (`ز و ر B003 ↔ ن ع م B011`), strenuous movement connects with travel (`ز و ر B007 ↔ ن ع م B012`), and burial connects with death before the Blaze (`ق ب ر B001 ↔ ج ح م B002`). These links strengthen the force of “visit”: death ends takāthur but begins the passage toward disclosure.

6. **Sight and fire form a reciprocal encounter.** The glaring fire-eye connects with the literal eye (`ج ح م B003 ↔ ع ي ن B001`). The discovery does not require turning Hell into a literal eye; it adds the sense that the object of final vision confronts and exposes the viewer in return.

7. **The contest uses the wrong unit of value.** Increase connects with escalation (`ك ث ر B001 ↔ ن ع م B010`) and rivalry connects with luxurious status (`ك ث ر B002 ↔ ن ع م B002`). The latent contrast with true abundant good (`ك ث ر B004`) allows “more” and “good” to separate: the final question concerns the quality and use of favor, not its winning quantity.

8. **Mechanically strong remote clusters are retained but not promoted.** Weather (`ع ي ن B010 ↔ ن ع م B009`), livestock visibility (`ر ء ي B010 ↔ ن ع م B005`), astronomy and tool-shape (`ع ي ن B008 ↔ ن ع م B007`, `ع ي ن B009 ↔ ن ع م B007`), and the anatomical cluster around lung, eye, bodily mark, nose, and anger are genuine queue discoveries. They remain `C` or `C/B` inventory because no local trigger makes them govern the surah's sequence.

## What activates what

- Diversion (`ل ه و`) activates increase (`ك ث ر`): attention is captured by the practice of comparison, not by an isolated object.
- Increase (`ك ث ر`) activates visitation and burial (`ز و ر`, `ق ب ر`): an open-ended race is bounded by the grave.
- Burial (`ق ب ر`) activates knowledge (`ع ل م`): the excluded terminus forces the truth of the contest into view.
- Knowledge (`ع ل م`) activates certainty (`ي ق ن`): repeated warning is intensified from proposition to settled epistemic state.
- Certainty (`ي ق ن`) activates seeing (`ر ء ي`), and seeing activates direct verification (`ع ي ن`): cognition becomes encounter.
- Seeing and direct verification (`ر ء ي`, `ع ي ن`) activate the Blaze (`ج ح م`): the consequence is no longer abstract.
- The Blaze and direct verification activate questioning (`س ء ل`): exposure issues in accountability rather than detached observation.
- Questioning (`س ء ل`) activates favor (`ن ع م`): the final object of inquiry reveals what the opening competition had misclassified.
- In the discovery feedback loop, favor (`ن ع م`) can feed diversion and increase (`ل ه و`, `ك ث ر`), while questioning breaks that loop by changing gift into trust.

## What the underlay adds

The underlay keeps the warning from becoming a simple rejection of material goods. Favor is real, pleasurable, and divinely given; the failure lies in converting it into distraction, social display, numerical superiority, or imagined independence. The final audit therefore concerns reception and use, not merely possession.

It also explains why the middle of the surah is epistemic. The competitors do not merely lack data. Their attention and valuation practices suppress the consequence already named by the grave. The sequence from knowledge to certainty to eye-verification removes, one by one, the distances on which that suppression depends.

Finally, the visitation language prevents the grave from functioning as the mechanism's endpoint. It closes worldly comparison while opening compulsory disclosure. Under the basmala's frame, the same **naʿīm** that can be absorbed into takāthur remains mercy; precisely as mercy received, it becomes responsibility.

## Root-level graph

- `ل ه و → ك ث ر` — diversion feeds increase and rivalry (`ل ه و B001`, `ل ه و B002`; `ك ث ر B001`, `ك ث ر B002`, `ك ث ر B007`).
- `ك ث ر → ز و ر → ق ب ر` — competitive increase runs until visitation reaches burial and death (`ك ث ر B001`, `ك ث ر B002`; `ز و ر B003`, `ز و ر B007`; `ق ب ر B001`, `ق ب ر B005`).
- `ق ب ر → ع ل م → ي ق ن` — the mortal boundary forces knowledge toward certainty (`ق ب ر B001`; `ع ل م B001`, `ع ل م B002`; `ي ق ن B001`).
- `ي ق ن → ر ء ي → ع ي ن` — certainty becomes sight, manifestation, and direct actuality (`ي ق ن B001`; `ر ء ي B001`, `ر ء ي B012`; `ع ي ن B001`, `ع ي ن B002`, `ع ي ن B013`).
- `ر ء ي + ع ي ن → ج ح م` — direct vision takes the Blaze as its object (`ر ء ي B001`, `ر ء ي B004`; `ع ي ن B001`, `ع ي ن B002`; `ج ح م B001`, `ج ح م B003`).
- `ج ح م → س ء ل → ن ع م` — encountered consequence leads to accountability for favor (`ج ح م B001`, `ج ح م B002`; `س ء ل B001`, `س ء ل B003`, `س ء ل B004`; `ن ع م B001`, `ن ع م B002`, `ن ع م B004`).
- `ك ث ر ↔ س ء ل` — claims and accumulation become demands answered in judgment (`ك ث ر B003 ↔ س ء ل B001`).
- `ن ع م → ل ه و → ك ث ر` — provision can be misprocessed as distraction and escalation (`ل ه و B003 ↔ ن ع م B005`; `ك ث ر B001 ↔ ن ع م B010`; `ك ث ر B002 ↔ ن ع م B002`).
- `ز و ر ↔ ر ء ي` — false valuation becomes public display (`ز و ر B002 ↔ ر ء ي B005`).
- `ق ب ر ↔ ج ح م` — burial opens onto the mortal encounter with the Blaze (`ق ب ر B001 ↔ ج ح م B002`).
- `ج ح م ↔ ع ي ن` — final seeing has the force of reciprocal exposure (`ج ح م B003 ↔ ع ي ن B001`).
- `ز و ر ↔ ن ع م` — visitation and travel keep the grave's station temporary (`ز و ر B003 ↔ ن ع م B011`; `ز و ر B007 ↔ ن ع م B012`).
