# Mechanism

## Primary pathway

The basmala places the passage under divine mercy before the opening question asks, **“Have you seen?”** (`ر ء ي B001`, `ر ء ي B013`). The question does not request detached observation. It teaches the hearer how to recognize denial of reckoning (`ك ذ ب B001`, `ك ذ ب B002`; `د ي ن B002`) by looking at its visible social and ritual effects.

The first proof is treatment of the vulnerable. Denial becomes rough expulsion (`د ع ع B001`) directed at the orphan cut off from protection (`ي ت م B001`). It then becomes failure to urge or mobilize feeding (`ح ض ض B001`) for the person constrained by poverty (`ط ع م B001`, `س ك ن B006`). The passage therefore moves from a stated denial to two forms of enacted denial: actively pushing away a claimant and passively refusing to generate support for another.

The **fa-waylun** transition reveals that the same mechanism can inhabit visible worship. Prescribed prayer (`ص ل و B003`) remains externally present, but heartfelt attention is absent (`س ه و B001`). Prayer is redirected from supplication, mercy, and obedience (`ص ل و B002`, `د ي ن B001`) toward being seen by people (`ر ء ي B005`, `ر ء ي B012`). Ritual form survives while its accountability and mercy functions are severed.

The final act—holding back ordinary aid (`م ن ع B001`) and **al-ma'un** (`م ع ن B005`)—compresses the entire diagnosis into a small test. One who denies reckoning pushes away the unprotected, does not mobilize subsistence, performs worship for an audience, and withholds even an available minor benefit. The primary mechanism is therefore:

**denial of reckoning removes the vulnerable person's claim → force and non-mobilization replace care → worship becomes inattentive public display → withholding the smallest useful aid exposes the denial in practice.**

## Secondary activations

### All active/latent readings

- `ر ء ي` governs both poles of the surah. The opening activates seeing, reflection, and attention-directing inquiry (`ر ء ي B001`, `ر ء ي B002`, `ر ء ي B013`); the later verses activate mutual visibility, ostentation, outward appearance, and deliberate display (`ر ء ي B004`, `ر ء ي B005`, `ر ء ي B006`, `ر ء ي B012`). The visible-marker branch (`ر ء ي B011`) remains `C/B`: public prayer can function as a social sign, though no literal flag is named.
- `ك ذ ب` extends denial from false assertion to character and failed obligation. Direct falsehood and declaring something false (`ك ذ ب B001`, `ك ذ ب B002`) are joined by the deceptive self (`ك ذ ب B008`), deceptive appearance (`ك ذ ب B009`), and failure to discharge a charge (`ك ذ ب B004`). Prompt action, failed provision, and stop-start motion (`ك ذ ب B005`, `ك ذ ب B006`, `ك ذ ب B007`) remain exploratory contrasts or remote material branches.
- `د ي ن` does more than name final recompense (`د ي ن B002`). It activates obedience (`د ي ن B001`), debt and liability (`د ي ن B003`), social domination (`د ي ن B004`), habitual moral order (`د ي ن B005`), communal law (`د ي ن B006`), and trust or conscience (`د ي ن B007`). These readings preserve reckoning as the primary sense while showing how denial reorganizes worship, economics, authority, and responsibility.
- The orphan field includes forceful expulsion and verbal chiding (`د ع ع B001`, `د ع ع B003`), but also the rejected alternative of helping one who has stumbled (`د ع ع B004`). The orphan is not only a named child (`ي ت م B001`) but a person isolated from protection (`ي ت م B002`), neglected through ethical failure or delayed care (`ي ت م B003`, `ي ت م B004`), within a wider field of unsupported dependents (`د ع ع B009`, `ي ت م B005`).
- The feeding field distinguishes personal consumption, feeding others, livelihood, harvest source, ethical discernment, and capacity (`ط ع م B001`, `ط ع م B002`, `ط ع م B004`, `ط ع م B005`, `ط ع م B008`, `ط ع م B011`). Poverty likewise includes constrained agency, dwelling insecurity, household membership, abasement, and life-sustaining provision (`س ك ن B001`, `س ك ن B002`, `س ك ن B003`, `س ك ن B006`, `س ك ن B010`). Thus “food of the poor” is not reduced to one meal.
- Failure to urge charity has an inward counterpart: seeking increase for oneself (`ح ض ض B004`) instead of urging aid (`ح ض ض B001`). The low-ground and medicinal-plant branches (`ح ض ض B002`, `ح ض ض B003`) remain exploratory spatial and botanical senses rather than governing readings.
- Prayer carries supplication, praise, mercy, prescribed worship, and communal institution (`ص ل و B002`, `ص ل و B003`, `ص ل و B007`). Heedlessness includes the direct lapse of the heart (`س ه و B001`) and preserves quieter contrasts of inert ritual, self-excusing gentleness, and missed faint signs (`س ه و B002`, `س ه و B003`, `س ه و B005`). Punitive heat (`ص ل و B001`) remains `C/B` beneath **wayl**, because no fire is explicitly named.
- Withholding includes refusing to give, blocking access, monopolizing protection, issuing commands of prevention, and resisting obligation (`م ن ع B001`, `م ن ع B002`, `م ن ع B003`, `م ن ع B005`, `م ن ع B006`). **Al-ma'un** includes flowing water, ease and smallness, acknowledgment of another's right, ordinary useful aid, and household setting (`م ع ن B001`, `م ع ن B003`, `م ع ن B004`, `م ع ن B005`, `م ع ن B006`). These are complementary latent readings, not mutually exclusive definitions of one object.
- The full recall reservoir also preserves dream, supernatural, purity, reproductive, anatomical, animal, livestock, agriculture, plant, hunting, tool, transport, geography, architecture, and astronomy branches. Especially prominent are the fodder-plant and pasture cluster (`د ع ع B008`, `ص ل و B009`), animal provision and anatomy (`ط ع م B006`, `ط ع م B007`, `ص ل و B004`, `ص ل و B005`), motion and racing (`ط ع م B009`, `ص ل و B006`, `س ك ن B008`), and body/reproduction (`ر ء ي B007`, `ر ء ي B009`, `ر ء ي B010`, `س ه و B006`). They remain `C` because mechanical connectivity does not supply a local plant, animal, body, or transport scene.

### Surprising discovery readings

1. **The surah contrasts seeing with being seen.** The opening inquiry (`ر ء ي B001`, `ر ء ي B013`) trains the hearer to see through behavior, while ostentatious worship (`ر ء ي B005`, `ر ء ي B012`) tries to control what other people see. The deceptive-self bridge (`ر ء ي B005 ↔ ك ذ ب B008`) and deceptive-appearance bridge (`ر ء ي B006 ↔ ك ذ ب B009`) make visibility the surah's diagnostic medium: correct seeing unmasks manufactured appearance.

2. **Denied reckoning becomes unpaid social debt.** The financial-liability branch of `د ي ن` connects livelihood (`د ي ن B003 ↔ ط ع م B004`), withheld property (`د ي ن B003 ↔ م ن ع B001`), and dwelling (`د ي ن B003 ↔ س ك ن B002`). This does not replace final judgment with finance. It shows that disbelief in the final account appears now as refusal to recognize what is owed to vulnerable people.

3. **Not urging others is a social failure, not merely private stinginess.** `ح ض ض B001 ↔ س ك ن B006` joins persuasion, charity, and poverty; `د ع ع B009 ↔ س ك ن B003` and `د ع ع B009 ↔ ي ت م B001` widen the orphan into a household-and-dependent field. The passage condemns failure to build a culture of feeding, not only failure to hand over one portion of food.

4. **Power is inverted from protection into exclusion.** The orphan lacks protective support (`ي ت م B001`, `ي ت م B002`), while social power and abasement connect through `د ي ن B004 ↔ س ك ن B006`. Forceful expulsion also connects directly to blocked access (`د ع ع B001 ↔ م ن ع B002`). The denier possesses enough agency to create a boundary but does not convert that agency into guardianship (`م ن ع B003`).

5. **Food becomes livelihood, housing, and moral discernment.** Feeding the poor links directly with poverty (`ط ع م B002 ↔ س ك ن B006`), while provision connects to debt and dwelling (`د ي ن B003 ↔ ط ع م B004`, `ط ع م B004 ↔ س ك ن B002`). The ethical-taste bridges (`ي ت م B003 ↔ ط ع م B008`, `ك ذ ب B008 ↔ ط ع م B008`) add that treatment of food need reveals the giver's own moral quality.

6. **The smallest withheld thing is the clearest diagnostic.** `م ع ن B003` emphasizes ease and slightness; `م ع ن B004` frames aid as an acknowledged or denied right; `م ع ن B005` keeps the object broad—water, alms, a household implement, a loaned tool, or another ordinary benefit. Because the cost can be trivial, refusal cannot easily be explained by scarcity; it exposes the orientation of the will (`م ن ع B001`, `م ن ع B002`).

7. **Ritual neglect and social neglect share one interior mechanism.** The deceptive self links showing off with selfish desire (`ر ء ي B005 ↔ ك ذ ب B008`, `ك ذ ب B008 ↔ ح ض ض B004`), while ethical failure joins orphanhood and discernment (`ي ت م B003 ↔ ط ع م B008`). Prayer heedlessness (`س ه و B001`) is therefore not an isolated scheduling defect; it is ritual evidence of the same attention and responsibility failure visible in verses 2–3 and 7.

8. **The top-ranked pasture, livestock, anatomy, and motion clusters remain discoveries without becoming the mechanism.** The strongest mechanical bridge, `د ع ع B008 ↔ ص ل و B009`, creates a coherent fodder-and-pasture ecology; `ط ع م B009 ↔ ص ل و B006` creates a race-and-motion pair; and `ط ع م B007 ↔ ص ل و B005` creates an animal-body pair. They are preserved at `C`, since the local passage supplies food but not the plants, livestock, race, or anatomy needed to promote them.

## What activates what

- The opening inquiry (`ر ء ي`) activates denial and reckoning (`ك ذ ب`, `د ي ن`): the addressee is taught to infer an invisible stance from visible conduct.
- Denial of reckoning (`ك ذ ب`, `د ي ن`) activates force against the orphan (`د ع ع`, `ي ت م`): removing accountability removes the vulnerable person's recognized claim.
- The orphan (`ي ت م`) activates urging, feeding, and poverty (`ح ض ض`, `ط ع م`, `س ك ن`): individual vulnerability opens into a household and subsistence system.
- Failure to urge and feed (`ح ض ض`, `ط ع م`, `س ك ن`) activates the critique of prayer (`ص ل و`, `س ه و`): worship is measured by whether it produces attentive mercy and obligation.
- Heedless prayer (`ص ل و`, `س ه و`) activates public display (`ر ء ي`): when relation to God and neighbor is emptied, the remaining audience is other people.
- Public display (`ر ء ي`) activates the deceptive self (`ك ذ ب`): visible piety masks the same denial announced in verse 1.
- The display-and-denial loop activates withholding (`م ن ع`, `م ع ن`): the final ordinary refusal provides a concrete test of the whole system.
- Withholding **al-ma'un** (`م ن ع`, `م ع ن`) feeds back to denial of reckoning (`ك ذ ب`, `د ي ن`): the final deed verifies the opening diagnosis.

## What the underlay adds

The underlay explains why the social and ritual halves are one argument. Verses 2–3 are not an unrelated preface to a criticism of prayer. The treatment of the orphan, failure to mobilize feeding, heedlessness in prayer, ostentation, and withholding are successive expressions of one broken relation to accountability.

It also explains why the passage targets worshippers rather than worship. Prayer remains prescribed worship, supplication, mercy, and communal obedience (`ص ل و B002`, `ص ل و B003`, `ص ل و B007`). **Wayl** falls on prayer emptied of attention and redirected toward social visibility. The basmala sharpens the test: worship under the name of the Merciful is contradicted when mercy is withheld from dependent people.

The economic and household branches prevent “feeding” from shrinking into occasional generosity. Debt, property, dwelling, livelihood, dependence, and household responsibility show that the passage concerns access to the conditions of life. Likewise, **al-ma'un** is kept broad so that no single object exhausts the final charge.

Finally, the smallness of **al-ma'un** makes the moral diagnosis unusually severe. If even an easy, low-cost, commonly available benefit is withheld, the failure lies less in capacity than in recognition: the other person's need is not being admitted as a claim. That returns the reader to `د ي ن` as reckoning, debt, obedience, trust, and justice.

## Root-level graph

- `ر ء ي → ك ذ ب` — attentive seeing diagnoses denial, while later ostentation manufactures appearance (`ر ء ي B001`, `ر ء ي B005`, `ر ء ي B013`; `ك ذ ب B001`, `ك ذ ب B002`, `ك ذ ب B008`, `ك ذ ب B009`).
- `ك ذ ب ↔ د ي ن` — declaring judgment false opposes trust, testimony, and conscience (`ك ذ ب B002 ↔ د ي ن B007`; `ك ذ ب B001 ↔ د ي ن B007`).
- `د ي ن → د ع ع` — denied reckoning and activated domination become coercive treatment (`د ي ن B002`, `د ي ن B004`; `د ع ع B001`, `د ع ع B003`).
- `د ع ع → ي ت م` — force is directed toward the orphan and expands to dependent children (`د ع ع B001`, `د ع ع B009`; `ي ت م B001`, `ي ت م B002`, `ي ت م B005`; exact queue support: `د ع ع B009 ↔ ي ت م B001`, `د ع ع B009 ↔ ي ت م B005`).
- `ي ت م → ح ض ض` — vulnerability creates an obligation to mobilize care (`ي ت م B001`, `ي ت م B003`, `ي ت م B004`; `ح ض ض B001`, `ح ض ض B004`).
- `ح ض ض → ط ع م → س ك ن` — urging becomes feeding and life-sustaining provision for the poor (`ح ض ض B001`; `ط ع م B001`, `ط ع م B002`, `ط ع م B004`; `س ك ن B006`, `س ك ن B010`; exact queue support: `ح ض ض B001 ↔ س ك ن B006`, `ط ع م B002 ↔ س ك ن B006`, `ط ع م B001 ↔ س ك ن B010`).
- `د ي ن → ط ع م + س ك ن + م ن ع` — reckoning carries an economic-liability underlay (`د ي ن B003 ↔ ط ع م B004`; `د ي ن B003 ↔ س ك ن B002`; `د ي ن B003 ↔ م ن ع B001`).
- `د ي ن → ص ل و` — obedience and community become prescribed worship (`د ي ن B001 ↔ ص ل و B003`; `د ي ن B001 ↔ ص ل و B007`; `د ي ن B006 ↔ ص ل و B003`).
- `ص ل و → س ه و → ر ء ي` — prescribed prayer loses heartfelt attention and becomes public display (`ص ل و B003`; `س ه و B001`, `س ه و B002`; `ر ء ي B005`, `ر ء ي B012`).
- `ر ء ي ↔ ك ذ ب` — ostentation and deceptive selfhood reinforce one another (`ر ء ي B005 ↔ ك ذ ب B008`; `ر ء ي B006 ↔ ك ذ ب B009`).
- `د ع ع ↔ م ن ع` — forceful expulsion and withholding share the structure of blocked access (`د ع ع B001 ↔ م ن ع B002`).
- `ر ء ي ↔ م ن ع` — the opening inquiry and the latent command-to-prevent form a speech-act contrast (`ر ء ي B013 ↔ م ن ع B005`).
- `م ن ع → م ع ن` — withholding takes an easy, small, useful benefit as its object (`م ن ع B001`, `م ن ع B002`; `م ع ن B001`, `م ع ن B003`, `م ع ن B004`, `م ع ن B005`).
- `م ع ن → د ي ن` — denied ordinary rights return the graph to reckoning, liability, and conscience (`م ع ن B004`, `م ع ن B005`; `د ي ن B002`, `د ي ن B003`, `د ي ن B007`).
- `د ع ع ↔ ص ل و` — retained remote ecology edge, not part of the primary path (`د ع ع B008 ↔ ص ل و B009`).
