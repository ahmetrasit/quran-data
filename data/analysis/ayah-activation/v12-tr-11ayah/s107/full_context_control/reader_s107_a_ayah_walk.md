# Surah 107 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

Each fixed ayah is read through its own five-before/five-after window, clipped at the packet boundaries.

## 107:0 - بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

*Focus window: 107:0-107:5.*

### Activated readings

1. **A raised designation becomes an ethical criterion.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `س م و/B005` is “name as raised designation,” while fixed `ء ل ه/B002` is the fixed divine name used in invocation. **Structural cue:** the name stands first, before the alerting question of 107:1 (`ر ء ي/B013`, the “tell me” formula) and the conduct exposed in 107:2-107:5. **Assigned role:** the designation supplies the standard under which the following conduct is made visible. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies only the opening position and the later diagnostic sequence; I infer that the invocation governs that sequence rather than merely preceding it. **Reading change:** `بسم الله` reads as raising a named criterion over the surah's social and ritual tests, not as a detachable formula.

2. **Naming the worshipped one fixes the direction of worship.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ء ل ه/B001` covers worship, devotion, and the worshipped one; 107:4-107:5 supply `ص ل و/B003`, prescribed worship. **Structural cue:** the divine name precedes the later pair “those who pray / their prayer.” **Assigned role:** `الله` is the intended object that should orient the later ritual. **Abductive move:** the packet gives the named deity and the later worshippers; I infer that genuine orientation toward the former should govern the latter. **Reading change:** the opening anticipates a contrast between naming the proper object of devotion and performing prayer while being turned away from its demands.

3. **Repeated mercy becomes enacted beneficence toward the vulnerable.** **Lexical evidence:** the two fixed forms of `ر ح م/B001` carry tenderness, compassion, and beneficence toward one shown mercy; 107:2 supplies `ي ت م/B001`, offspring cut off from a protecting parent, and 107:3 supplies `ط ع م/B002`, feeding the needy. **Structural cue:** two mercy-derived adjectives are followed by two concrete failures of care. **Assigned role:** mercy supplies the positive action-schema against which thrusting away and non-feeding register as reversals. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the lexical capacities and their sequence; I infer that a divine attribute named in invocation is meant to orient the invoker's treatment of exposed dependents. **Reading change:** `الرحمن الرحيم` becomes operational rather than merely affective: mercy is tested in protection and provision.

4. **Mercy also carries a kinship-and-enclosure model.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ر ح م/B002` is the kinship bond and fixed `ر ح م/B003` is the womb as the child's containing place; nearby `ي ت م/B001` marks loss of a protecting parent, and `س ك ن/B006` marks poverty, weakness, and abasement. **Structural cue:** the paired mercy forms precede a child severed from protection and a needy person lacking support. **Assigned role:** kinship and womb provide a material-social image of holding, connection, and growth. **Abductive move:** the packet does not state that mercy substitutes for lost kin; I infer that protective care should recreate an enclosure where ordinary protection has failed. **Reading change:** the basmalah frames the following harm as a failure to extend kin-like shelter, not only a failure to feel pity.

### Retrospective surprises

107:2-107:3 made the main retrospective change: the kinship and womb branches of `ر ح م/B002-B003` no longer remained biological side-images, because the orphan and needy person supplied concrete sites where protective enclosure is absent. Then 107:4-107:5 corrected any merely formulaic reading of `ء ل ه/B001`: naming the worshipped one matters precisely because later prayer can retain its form while losing its orientation.

## 107:1 - أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ

*Focus window: 107:0-107:6.*

### Activated readings

1. **Seeing becomes a behavioral diagnosis.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ر ء ي/B013` is the alerting “tell me” formula, and fixed `ر ء ي/B001` includes seeing by eye or insight. **Structural cue:** the question is answered deictically by `فذلك` in 107:2 and then by a sequence of observable acts through 107:6. **Assigned role:** the opening verb recruits the addressee as an examiner of conduct. **Abductive move:** the packet gives the question and the demonstrative sequence; I infer that the acts are evidence by which the otherwise inward denial is recognized. **Reading change:** `أرأيت` asks for perceptive identification, not merely prior knowledge of a doctrinal label.

2. **`الدين` becomes a ledger of owed care.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `د ي ن/B002` is reckoning and recompense, while fixed `د ي ن/B003` is debt and credit; 107:2's `ي ت م/B001` identifies a child without a protecting parent, and 107:3's `ط ع م/B002` supplies feeding another. **Structural cue:** denial is immediately equated with repelling that dependent and refusing even to mobilize food. **Assigned role:** debt supplies the vulnerable person's claim, and reckoning supplies the horizon in which that claim is answered. **Abductive move:** the packet does not call the food a debt; I infer from the causal `فذلك` sequence that unserved dependency functions as an unpaid liability. **Reading change:** denying `الدين` becomes practical repudiation of accountable social debt as well as verbal rejection of judgment.

3. **Ritual can coexist with practical non-submission.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `د ي ن/B001` covers obedience, submission, worship, and the followed way; 107:4-107:5 supply `ص ل و/B003`, prescribed worship, qualified by `س ه و/B001`, the heart turning away. **Structural cue:** the packet moves from denial of `الدين` to people explicitly called worshippers, then qualifies their prayer adversely. **Assigned role:** the obedience branch tests whether ritual form actually produces submission. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies prayer and social harm in one chain; I infer that enacted submission includes the obligations disclosed by 107:2-107:3. **Reading change:** the denier need not be ritually inactive; `يكذب بالدين` can name a life that performs worship while falsifying its rule in practice.

4. **Denial acquires the image of a surface that lies by its condition.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ك ذ ب/B002` is declaring a thing false, while fixed `ك ذ ب/B009` is the variegated cloth whose appearance deceives; 107:6 activates `ر ء ي/B005`, action performed to be seen. **Structural cue:** a seeing-question opens the ayah, prescribed prayer supplies a visible pattern, and showmanship closes this fixed window. **Assigned role:** the cloth image models a crafted exterior whose displayed pattern contradicts the wearer's social conduct. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies appearance, prayer, and failures of care; I infer that the ritual surface can itself “lie” by presenting devotion that the conduct does not sustain. **Reading change:** `يكذب` describes not only a proposition about `الدين` but an embodied false appearance.

### Retrospective surprises

107:2's `فذلك` retrospectively made `ر ء ي/B013` diagnostic: the answer to “have you considered?” arrives as conduct rather than definition. More surprisingly, 107:4-107:6 corrected the easy assumption that denial means absence of ritual; visible prayer plus showmanship activated the deceptive-surface image of `ك ذ ب/B009`, so practical falsification can wear a devotional pattern.

## 107:2 - فَذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِى يَدُعُّ ٱلْيَتِيمَ

*Focus window: 107:0-107:7.*

### Activated readings

1. **The thrust actively reproduces orphanhood.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `د ع ع/B001` is rough thrusting, harsh driving, and pushing someone away from a due right; fixed `ي ت م/B001` is offspring cut off from a protecting parent. **Structural cue:** `فذلك` makes this action the identifying consequence of 107:1, while 107:3 and 107:7 continue the pattern of blocked provision. **Assigned role:** the thrust is not incidental violence; it extends the orphan's prior severance into a present social exclusion. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies bereavement, force, and later withholding; I infer that driving the child away causes continued loss of substitute protection. **Reading change:** the subject does not merely encounter an orphan but helps manufacture the orphan's ongoing social isolation.

2. **Force used for expulsion is the inverse of force used for filling.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `د ع ع/B002` is agitating a measure, vessel, or sack until it fills; 107:3 supplies `ط ع م/B002`, feeding another, and 107:7 supplies `م ع ن/B005`, small household benefit, gift, or alms made available. **Structural cue:** rough movement of the child is followed by refusal to mobilize food and finally by withholding useful goods. **Assigned role:** the vessel branch supplies a material logistics model in which motion should make provision fit and become full. **Abductive move:** the packet does not equate the child with a vessel; I infer a directional reversal: the agent applies force to eject a dependent where social force could instead organize filling and supply. **Reading change:** `يدع` becomes an anti-provisioning action, displacing the recipient rather than arranging resources around the need.

3. **A restorative cry is darkly reversed.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `د ع ع/B004` is the cry said to a stumbler, “rise” or “recover,” while fixed `ي ت م/B004` includes kindness delayed from the orphan. **Structural cue:** the same root that can accompany raising a fallen person here governs the orphan as the object of a harsh transitive act; later non-urging and withholding prolong the delay. **Assigned role:** the recovery branch marks the help that this encounter could have supplied. **Abductive move:** the packet does not call the orphan a literal stumbler; I infer social stumbling from lost protection and postponed care. **Reading change:** `يدع اليتيم` reads as the reversal of restoration: an occasion to raise the exposed person is converted into renewed rejection.

4. **`اليتيم` names a condition that society can maintain.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ي ت م/B002` is isolated singleness, and fixed `ي ت م/B004` is slowness or delay in care. **Structural cue:** after the initial loss encoded by the noun, 107:3 adds failure to exhort, 107:5 adds heedlessness, and 107:7 adds active withholding. **Assigned role:** singleness describes the social position; delay describes the temporal process that keeps it in place. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies repeated omissions by the surrounding group, but the arrow from those omissions to sustained isolation is my inference. **Reading change:** “orphan” is not only a static biographical status; the ayah exposes an orphaning process continually renewed by delayed communal care.

### Retrospective surprises

107:3 first exposed a reversal I could not see from the thrust alone: the force of `د ع ع/B002` that can pack a vessel toward fullness is followed by refusal to mobilize food. 107:7 then completed the correction by making delayed care (`ي ت م/B004`) an active blockade, showing that the push is one moment in a longer system of sustained isolation rather than a self-contained outburst.

## 107:3 - وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلْمِسْكِينِ

*Focus window: 107:0-107:7.*

### Activated readings

1. **The failure is a misallocation of social force.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ح ض ض/B001` is urging or inciting toward a thing; immediately prior `د ع ع/B001` is rough thrusting and forceful driving. **Structural cue:** the same identified agent is forceful in 107:2, but `لا` removes force precisely where exhortation toward food is required in 107:3. **Assigned role:** `حض` is beneficial propulsion, set against the harmful propulsion of `دع`. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the two opposite actions; I infer a common reserve of agency whose direction the subject chooses. **Reading change:** “does not urge” is not passive incapacity but force deliberately spent on exclusion rather than mobilized for provision.

2. **Urging is spatialized as lifting someone from a low, immobilized position.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ح ض ض/B002` is low ground at a mountain's foot; fixed `س ك ن/B006` is poverty, weakness, and abasement, while fixed `س ك ن/B010` is provision that enables staying. **Structural cue:** the locative image of low ground meets `على` and `طعام`, with the needy person as the endpoint of the construction. **Assigned role:** low ground maps the social position, and sustaining provision supplies the means of stable elevation rather than expulsion. **Abductive move:** this is exploratory because `B002` is a locative noun-image rather than the verbal sense of `يحض`; I infer the upward social arrow from its proximity to `على` and the abasement branch. **Reading change:** the absent exhortation becomes a refusal to organize a lift from debased precarity into sustainable standing.

3. **The food reads as livelihood and an accountable share.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ط ع م/B001` is food that satisfies hunger, but fixed `ط ع م/B004` expands to provision, livelihood, revenue, and a legal share; nearby `د ي ن/B003` is debt, and 107:7's `م ع ن/B005` includes gifts, alms, household loans, and other available benefit. **Structural cue:** `طعام` is attached to `المسكين`, and the sequence runs from denial of liability to refusal of food to final withholding. **Assigned role:** the livelihood branch makes food part of a distributive system rather than a single morsel. **Abductive move:** the packet does not grammatically label the food a due; I infer entitlement from the possessive relation and the debt/withholding frame. **Reading change:** failure to urge is failure to recognize and mobilize the needy person's share in communal provision.

4. **The needy person's missing rest becomes the test of prayer.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `س ك ن/B004` is an object of rest and comfort and explicitly includes prayer among what the self rests in; 107:4-107:5 supply `ص ل و/B003`, prescribed prayer, and 107:7 supplies `م ن ع/B001`, withholding good. **Structural cue:** `المسكين` stands immediately before the warning to worshippers, whose sequence ends in withheld benefit. **Assigned role:** the comfort branch links the needy person's required stability to prayer's potential calming function. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies lexical overlap but not a stated transfer; I infer that worship's rest should issue in making social rest possible. **Reading change:** 107:3 becomes the hinge by which prayer is judged: ritual repose that never reaches the destabilized needy is exposed as blocked comfort.

### Retrospective surprises

107:4-107:5 made the adjacency to prayer unexpectedly functional: because `س ك ن/B004` includes prayer as a source of rest, `المسكين` became the person to whom that rest fails to travel. 107:7 broadened the correction from one meal to a circulation system of small household benefits (`م ع ن/B005`), reinforcing `ط ع م/B004` as livelihood and share rather than isolated food.

## 107:4 - فَوَيْلٌۭ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ

*Focus window: 107:0-107:7.*

### Activated readings

1. **The worshipper label is placed under the preceding social indictment.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ص ل و/B003` is prescribed worship with its required performance; 107:2's `د ع ع/B001` is harsh exclusion and 107:3's `ح ض ض/B001` is the exhortation that is withheld. **Structural cue:** initial `فـ` carries the warning directly out of the orphan-and-food sequence, while 107:5-107:7 then qualify the worshippers. **Assigned role:** prescribed prayer provides the formal status whose adequacy the surrounding conduct tests. **Abductive move:** the packet does not state a general legal condition for prayer; I infer from the connective sequence that social effects expose whether the prescribed act is being fulfilled in substance. **Reading change:** `للمصلين` is not a protected honorific here; it is a role made answerable to the care it fails to generate.

2. **Prayer is expected to conduct mercy outward.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ص ل و/B002` includes supplication, blessing, mercy, praise, and purification; 107:0's `ر ح م/B001` supplies beneficent mercy, while 107:3's `ط ع م/B002` and 107:7's `م ن ع/B001` mark feeding and its opposite, withholding. **Structural cue:** mercy opens the window, vulnerable need precedes the prayer label, and blocked benefit closes the sequence. **Assigned role:** prayer is a conduit through which invoked mercy should become blessing for another. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies those endpoints; I infer the transmission arrow from prayer to social beneficence. **Reading change:** the warning falls on prayer that names or seeks mercy but fails to conduct it into food and ordinary aid.

3. **Prayer can be converted into a snare for public regard.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ص ل و/B004` is a set snare or trap, and 107:6's `ر ء ي/B005` is doing an act so people see it. **Structural cue:** the later relative clause `الذين هم يراءون` specifies the public-facing use of the prayer named here. **Assigned role:** the snare image makes ritual the instrument, with spectators' esteem as the sought catch. **Abductive move:** neither prey nor esteem is named in the packet; I infer them from the trap branch and the explicit desire to be seen. **Reading change:** `المصلين` carries an activated shadow of agents who set prayer as a device for social capture.

4. **The race-follower image exposes a reversal of priority.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ص ل و/B006` is the second horse following at the leader's rear flank; 107:6's `ر ء ي/B005` supplies showmanship before people. **Structural cue:** the worshipper noun is followed by clauses that reveal heedlessness, audience-seeking, and withheld aid. **Assigned role:** the second-place branch models prayer as following another leader rather than directing conduct. **Abductive move:** this is exploratory because the equine image belongs to a distinct nominal use; I infer from 107:6 that public approval leads and ritual trails behind it. **Reading change:** the worshippers can be heard as followers whose prayer occupies second place to reputation, explaining why it does not govern their treatment of others.

### Retrospective surprises

107:5 corrected an overbroad first reaction to the warning: the target is not prescribed prayer as such but prayer held in a relation of heedless distance. 107:6 then activated both the snare (`ص ل و/B004`) and second-place follower (`ص ل و/B006`) images, and 107:7 supplied their social result: ritual captures regard and follows reputation while ordinary benefit remains blocked.

## 107:5 - ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُونَ

*Focus window: 107:0-107:7.*

### Activated readings

1. **Heedlessness is an orientation away, not merely a lapse inside prayer.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ص ل و/B003` is prescribed worship, and fixed `س ه و/B001` is heedlessness in which the heart turns away from a thing, including forgetfulness in prayer. **Structural cue:** the construction uses `عن صلاتهم`; 107:6-107:7 then show stable audience-seeking and withholding rather than a momentary ritual mistake. **Assigned role:** `سهو` supplies the absent attention, while `عن` gives that absence a direction. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the grammar and repeated conduct; I infer disposition from their recurrence. **Reading change:** the ayah diagnoses a life turned away from what prayer requires, not simply accidental inattention during one performance.

2. **The prayer has movement but no ethical output.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `س ه و/B002` is stillness; fixed `ص ل و/B003` includes a prescribed act with standing, bowing, and prostration, while nearby `س ك ن/B001` is cessation of movement and 107:7's `م ن ع/B002` is a barrier. **Structural cue:** bodily worship is followed by showmanship and a blocked benefit, after earlier failure to mobilize food. **Assigned role:** the stillness branch marks arrested consequence rather than absent bodily motion. **Abductive move:** the packet does not state that prayer must cause aid; I infer that a properly orienting ritual should move the agent toward the obligations already placed beside it. **Reading change:** `ساهون` describes ethically inert prayer: much performed motion, but no movement toward the vulnerable.

3. **Leniency is applied inward and denied outward.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `س ه و/B003` is easy dealing through overlooking another's slip; 107:2's `د ع ع/B001` is harsh rebuffing, and fixed `صلاتهم` marks the prayer as “their” own. **Structural cue:** the group overlooks the failure attached to its own prayer while its conduct toward the orphan is forceful rather than easy or forgiving. **Assigned role:** the easy-dealing branch exposes an asymmetry in where forbearance is spent. **Abductive move:** the packet does not explicitly say they excuse themselves; I infer self-exemption from the possessive prayer, adverse qualification, and contrasting harshness. **Reading change:** heedlessness becomes selective moral indulgence: their own ritual failure is passed over, another's vulnerability is not.

4. **A front-stage visibility system makes subtle duties disappear.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `س ه و/B004` is a porch or front rack on which goods are placed, and fixed `س ه و/B005` is the tiny, hidden star missed by sight; 107:6's `ر ء ي/B005` is showing off, while 107:7's `م ع ن/B005` includes household goods and small available benefits. **Structural cue:** `ساهون` is followed first by public display and then by withheld household-scale aid. **Assigned role:** the front rack models staged visibility, while the hidden star models the low-visibility obligation attention fails to register. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the material and optical images; I infer one attentional economy in which prayer is placed on the visible frontage and unshowy assistance is screened out. **Reading change:** being “heedless of their prayer” includes converting it into display while missing the small practical duties that would realize it.

### Retrospective surprises

107:6-107:7 together produced the strongest later surprise. Public visibility activated the front rack of `س ه و/B004`, while the withheld, household-scale `الماعون` activated both the goods placed there and the easily missed smallness of `س ه و/B005`; this corrected “heedlessness” from a generic cognitive lapse to an attention system that stages prayer and filters out unshowy duty.

## 107:6 - ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ يُرَآءُونَ

*Focus window: 107:1-107:7.*

### Activated readings

1. **The surah closes a reciprocal visibility loop.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ر ء ي/B005` is performing an act so people see it, and fixed `ر ء ي/B004` is mutual visibility and facing; 107:1's `ر ء ي/B013` is the opening “tell me” alert. **Structural cue:** the addressee is first told to look, and the identified people are later said to seek being looked at. **Assigned role:** mutual visibility makes observer and performer two sides of one social circuit. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies both acts of seeing; I infer that the performers select conduct in anticipation of the observer introduced at 107:1. **Reading change:** `يراءون` is not merely private insincerity but calibrated action within a feedback loop of gaze and recognition.

2. **Engineered visibility becomes evidence against its engineer.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ر ء ي/B012` is causing another to see or deliberately showing something; fixed `ر ء ي/B001` includes insight as well as eyesight. **Structural cue:** 107:2-107:5 teach the invited viewer what conduct diagnoses denial before 107:6 reveals deliberate self-display. **Assigned role:** showing produces the visible evidence, while insight reads that evidence against the surrounding social record. **Abductive move:** the packet does not state that display self-incriminates; I infer this reversal because the same observer sees both ritual appearance and failures of care. **Reading change:** the attempt to manufacture a favorable view paradoxically makes the disjunction more legible.

3. **Public opinion replaces accountable judgment.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ر ء ي/B002` is opinion and reflective judgment, and fixed `ر ء ي/B006` is visible appearance or mirror-image; 107:1's `د ي ن/B002` is reckoning and judgment. **Structural cue:** the sequence moves from accountable `الدين` to visible prayer and then to conduct aimed at people's eyes. **Assigned role:** the mirror-image is the managed surface, and human opinion is the substitute tribunal. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies two kinds of judgment but does not explicitly rank them; I infer displacement because the action is optimized for viewers while obligations remain unmet. **Reading change:** `يراءون` names a competing economy of judgment in which appearance before people displaces reckoning for deeds.

4. **Prayer is raised as an identity-marker rather than allowed to govern action.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ر ء ي/B011` is a flag or marker set up to be seen; 107:4-107:5 supply `ص ل و/B003`, prescribed worship, and 107:7 supplies `م ن ع/B001`, withholding good. **Structural cue:** the showing-off clause directly modifies the worshippers and is immediately followed by conduct that contradicts the displayed identity. **Assigned role:** the flag branch turns prayer into a public sign of affiliation. **Abductive move:** no literal flag appears; I infer signaling from the marker image and the explicit effort to be seen. **Reading change:** `يراءون` becomes identity display: worship is hoisted as a sign while being denied control over ordinary giving.

### Retrospective surprises

107:7 supplied a material control condition for the public display: the identity-marker is visible, but the ordinary benefit is withheld. That later contrast sharpened `ر ء ي/B012` from neutral showing into self-defeating evidence production, because what is made seen can now be tested against what the same agents prevent from reaching others.

## 107:7 - وَيَمْنَعُونَ ٱلْمَاعُونَ

*Focus window: 107:2-107:7.*

### Activated readings

1. **The verb formally encloses and cancels the benefit.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `م ن ع/B001` is holding back the hand from giving, and fixed `م ع ن/B005` is `الماعون` as small available household benefit, loan, gift, kindness, or alms. **Structural cue:** the adjacent roots contain the same three consonants with `ن` and `ع` reordered, reinforced by the surface echo of `يمنعون الماعون`. **Assigned role:** the first word names obstruction and the second names what should be available, making act and object a compact opposed pair. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies roots, order, and scopes; I infer that the formal near-reversal enacts the semantic reversal from availability to refusal. **Reading change:** the closing clause sounds and reads like benefit being caught inside its own cancellation.

2. **Withholding is a barrier that dams a necessary flow.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `م ن ع/B002` is a barrier between a person and what is sought; fixed `م ع ن/B001` is visible flowing water, including a small running stream. **Structural cue:** the barrier-verb directly governs the flow-capable object after 107:3's unmet need for food. **Assigned role:** `منع` is the obstruction and `ماعون` is the social current that should pass through ordinary channels. **Abductive move:** the packet does not state hydraulic causation; I supply the directional arrow that a barrier stops a flow. **Reading change:** `يمنعون الماعون` becomes a circulation failure: assistance is not absent at source but deliberately dammed before reaching need.

3. **Protective capacity is inverted into a fortress around resources.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `م ن ع/B003` is protective strength, an inaccessible fortification, or defenders who guard; 107:2's `ي ت م/B001` is a child lacking a protecting parent, and 107:3's `س ك ن/B006` is poverty and weakness. **Structural cue:** those most in need of protection appear before goods are made inaccessible. **Assigned role:** the protective branch represents real capacity, but its object has shifted from vulnerable people to possessed benefit. **Abductive move:** the packet gives protection and withholding but not that object-shift; I infer it from the unprotected orphan and the fortified resource. **Reading change:** the final act is perverse protection: strength that could shelter people instead shelters goods from people.

4. **A slight household act can have dwelling-scale consequences.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `م ع ن/B003` is ease, smallness, and light weight; fixed `م ع ن/B005` includes minor household utility, and fixed `م ع ن/B006` is a dwelling or stopping-place. Nearby `س ك ن/B010` is provision that enables staying. **Structural cue:** the surah moves from food and poverty to refusal of the smallest domestic-scale utility. **Assigned role:** smallness measures low cost to the holder, while the dwelling branches measure high stabilizing value to the recipient. **Abductive move:** the packet does not quantify either side; I infer the asymmetry by joining “slight/easy” benefit to provision-enabled staying. **Reading change:** `الماعون` is not trivial residue: it is low-cost social infrastructure whose denial can destabilize a household.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah occurs in this fixed ayah's clipped focus window, so no later surprise or correction emerged.
