# Surah 67 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

## 67:0: بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A designation raised into the created order.** Lexically, `س م و` `B005` gives `اسم` the image "name as raised designation," while `B001` supplies rising or elevation. The structural cue in the clipped 67:0-5 window is the same root's movement from `بسم` to the elevated `سماوات` (67:3) and `السماء` (67:5). The designation's role is to frame the ensuing vertical cosmos. The packet supplies the root recurrence and spatial sequence; I infer the upward mapping from verbal designation to cosmic elevation. The opening therefore reads as raising the named authority over what follows, not merely attaching a label.

2. **Mercy as generative enclosure.** `ر ح م` `B001` supplies tenderness and mercy, and `B003` supplies the womb. The nearby return of `الرحمن` inside layered creation (67:3) gives the doubled adjectives a material-generative role: mercy contains, forms, and brings forth an ordered whole. The packet supplies repetition, creation, and layering; I infer that the womb image explains how mercy relates causally to that order. The formula consequently reads as entry into a containing and generative mercy, not only an appeal to benevolence.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:3 and 67:5:** The same `س م و` root unexpectedly moved from `اسم` into elevated heavens, while 67:3 placed `الرحمن` inside creation. Together they activated raised designation and generative enclosure more strongly than the opening alone.


## 67:1: تَبَٰرَكَ ٱلَّذِى بِيَدِهِ ٱلْمُلْكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ قَدِيرٌ

### Activated readings

1. **Durable good expressed as operative sovereignty.** `ب ر ك` `B004` images a fixed, growing good; `ي د ي` `B005` makes the hand authority; and `م ل ك` `B002` makes dominion ownership with disposal. In 67:1-6 that hand is followed by creating death and life, proportioning layered heavens, assigning lamps a second function, and preparing consequences. These acts give the three branches distinct roles: settled increase is the quality, the hand is the operating instrument, and dominion is the field of disposal. The packet supplies that sequence; I infer that the later acts exhibit the opening claim. `تبارك` thus reads as continuously productive rule rather than static praise.

2. **Capacity as measured reach.** `ق د ر` `B001` is measure reaching a thing's extent, while `B003` is capacity reaching effective control. The totalizing `كل شيء` is followed by measured creation and matched layers (67:2-3), so `قدير` functions as exact reach across differentiated objects, not undirected force. The packet supplies totality followed by proportion; I infer that proportion is the mode by which capacity becomes effective. The close of the ayah therefore reads as calibrated control of every thing's extent.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:2-5:** Creation, matched layers, and the reassignment of lamps supplied concrete operations for hand and dominion. The strongest correction was 67:3: effective capacity looked calibrated by proportion rather than like undifferentiated force.

## 67:2: ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلْمَوْتَ وَٱلْحَيَوٰةَ لِيَبْلُوَكُمْ أَيُّكُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًۭا ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْغَفُورُ

### Activated readings

1. **Life and death as disclosure media.** `ب ل و` `B002` is testing that discloses a state; `ح س ن` `B002` is skillful or beneficent action, and `ع م ل` `B001` is intentional doing. The 67:2-7 sequence immediately models testing through repeated inspection (67:3-4) and then exposes opposed outcomes (67:5-7). Those cues assign death and life the role of conditions under which intentional quality becomes visible. The packet supplies creation, comparison, and later inspection; I infer that exposure under changing conditions is the causal mechanism of disclosure. The ayah shifts from a generic ordeal to a controlled revelation of what action is actually well made.

2. **Testing includes wear, not just examination.** `ب ل و` `B001` images wearing out and decay alongside the disclosure branch `B002`. Death before life, the gaze returned exhausted in 67:4, and the ensuing fire scene give duration and depletion a structural role. The packet supplies those endpoints; I infer that sustained wear reveals quality more sharply than a single question would. `ليبلوكم` can therefore be heard as stress-testing action until its durable character appears.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:4:** The gaze returning `حسيرا` unexpectedly activated `ب ل و` `B001` (wear) beside disclosure. Testing became iterative stress that depletes the instrument and reveals durable quality.

## 67:3: ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمَٰوَٰتٍۢ طِبَاقًۭا ۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِى خَلْقِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ مِن تَفَٰوُتٍۢ ۖ فَٱرْجِعِ ٱلْبَصَرَ هَلْ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Integrity as relational fit.** `ط ب ق` `B002` gives layers one above another and `B003` gives one thing matching another; `ف و ت` `B002` and `B004` give divergence and a gap between two things; `ف ط ر` `B001` gives opening or splitting. Repeated `ترى` and `فارجع البصر` turn the observer into an inspector of interfaces between layers. The packet supplies stacking, matching, gaps, and iterative sight; I infer that an interface is sound when adjacent levels continue to fit. The denial of defect therefore becomes a claim of relational coherence, not merely an unblemished surface.

2. **Discernment without disintegration.** The fixed `فطور` remains anchored in `ف ط ر` `B001` (splitting), while 67:8 supplies `تميز` through `م ي ز` `B002` (coming apart as torn pieces). The window juxtaposes a created order that survives inspection with a punitive enclosure that almost tears under rage. The packet supplies the contrast; I infer that disciplined differentiation need not become rupture, whereas pent-up force threatens it. The ayah now reads as a test of whether distinctions remain joined under scrutiny.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:8:** `تكاد تميز` supplied the surprising counterexample of a structure nearly tearing apart. It sharpened `فطور` from a generic flaw into failure of distinctions to remain joined.

## 67:4: ثُمَّ ٱرْجِعِ ٱلْبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيْنِ يَنقَلِبْ إِلَيْكَ ٱلْبَصَرُ خَاسِئًۭا وَهُوَ حَسِيرٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Inspection reverses onto the inspector.** `ق ل ب` `B004` is turning and reversing; `خ س ء` `B002` is sight recoiling dim and exhausted; and `ح س ر` `B001` and `B003` supply uncovering and depletion. The doubled return command, `كرتين`, and especially `إليك` make the direction reverse: sight goes outward to expose a flaw but comes back itself exposed and spent. The packet supplies the trajectory and states; I infer that failed detection transfers the burden of inadequacy from object to observer. The ayah thus reads as an epistemic reversal in which scrutiny diagnoses the scrutinizer.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:8-9:** The later enclosure nearly tears under pressure and its entrants repeat a failed judgment. This made the sight's reversal in 67:4 look epistemic as well as physical: the tested object holds, while the observer's evaluative posture fails.

## 67:5: وَلَقَدْ زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنْيَا بِمَصَٰبِيحَ وَجَعَلْنَٰهَا رُجُومًۭا لِّلشَّيَٰطِينِ ۖ وَأَعْتَدْنَا لَهُمْ عَذَابَ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

### Activated readings

1. **Adornment doubles as boundary enforcement.** `ز ي ن` `B002` is making beauty show; `ج ع ل` `B002` is turning something into a state; and `ر ج م` `B003` supplies curse, expulsion, and banishment. The sequence `زينا ... وجعلناها` assigns the same lights first a visible and then an expulsive role. The preceding no-fissure inspection and following scenes of rejection make boundary maintenance the common mechanism. The packet supplies the dual assignment; I infer that displayed beauty and exclusion are two operations of one ordered perimeter. The sky's ornament is therefore functional security, not decoration alone.

2. **Near light and distant exclusion form one spatial system.** `د ن و` `B002` marks the nearer or lower side, while `ش ط ن` `B001` marks distance and remoteness. The fixed ayah places `الدنيا` and `الشياطين` on opposite sides of the lamp/projectile construction. The packet supplies that lexical opposition; I infer that the lights regulate what may approach the near boundary and what is driven remote. The scene reads as controlled access across distance.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:9:** The denial that anything was sent down unexpectedly made the fixed lamps/projectiles part of a larger access problem: the ordered sky both displays its boundary and regulates what may cross or be expelled from it.

## 67:6: وَلِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ بِرَبِّهِمْ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ ۖ وَبِئْسَ ٱلْمَصِيرُ

### Activated readings

1. **Covered nurture becomes a completed trajectory.** `ك ف ر` `B004` is covering over a favor, `ر ب ب` `B002` is repair, nurture, and completion, and `ص ي ر` `B001` is becoming into an endpoint. Creation, proportion, and ornament in 67:2-5 specify what has been nurtured; casting, hearing, and confession in 67:7-11 show the result arriving by stages. The branches therefore assign denial the role of covering a formative relation, and `المصير` the role of its eventual completion. The packet supplies the before-and-after sequence; I infer the causal trajectory between them. The ayah reads as a bad becoming, not only a named destination.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:11:** Confession completed the nearby sequence from covering nurture to recognizing consequence. It confirmed that `المصير` is a becoming whose endpoint is disclosed only after its stages have accumulated.

## 67:7: إِذَآ أُلْقُوا۟ فِيهَا سَمِعُوا۟ لَهَا شَهِيقًۭا وَهِىَ تَفُورُ

### Activated readings

1. **Physical hearing replaces refused reception.** In the fixed `سمعوا`, `س م ع` `B001` is acoustic hearing, while `B003` is understanding and compliance. `ل ق ي` `B005` makes `ألقوا` a casting into the place of encounter, and `ف و ر` `B001` makes that encounter a boiling surge. The later confession `لو كنا نسمع` (67:10) activates the dormant receptive branch by contrast: they now hear sound after failing to hear meaning. The packet supplies the two hearings; I infer that rejected reception leads to compulsory sensation. The ayah becomes a reversal of the hearing faculty, not just an acoustic detail.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:10:** The explicit regret `لو كنا نسمع` forced a correction from hearing as mere sensation to a split between acoustic `B001` and compliant `B003`. That later ayah generated the fixed verse's central irony.

## 67:8: تَكَادُ تَمَيَّزُ مِنَ ٱلْغَيْظِ ۖ كُلَّمَآ أُلْقِىَ فِيهَا فَوْجٌۭ سَأَلَهُمْ خَزَنَتُهَآ أَلَمْ يَأْتِكُمْ نَذِيرٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **A containment system at the edge of rupture.** `م ي ز` `B002` gives coming apart as if torn into pieces; `غ ي ظ` `B001` gives pent-up anger; and `خ ز ن` `B001` gives storing and safeguarding. Against the unsplit heavens of 67:3, `تكاد` marks an enclosure almost failing under internal pressure, while `كلما` and the keepers organize repeated intake. The packet supplies pressure, near-rupture, and guarded recurrence; I infer that containment holds the force short of total disintegration. The scene reads as regulated punishment under extreme pressure, not formless chaos.

2. **Punishment also sorts and audits.** `م ي ز` `B001` is sorting one thing from another, `ف و ج` `B001` is a group, and `خ ز ن` `B003` is a keeper. Each group is separately cast and questioned, so the dormant sorting branch assigns `تميز` an administrative role alongside tearing. The packet supplies grouped intake and questioning; I infer that the recurrence classifies entrants by their response to warning. The ayah can simultaneously depict threatened material separation and deliberate social differentiation.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:9-11:** The entrants' answer, regret, and confession strengthened the sorting/audit reading: each `فوج` is not only consumed but made to state the warning history by which it is differentiated.

## 67:9: قَالُوا۟ بَلَىٰ قَدْ جَآءَنَا نَذِيرٌۭ فَكَذَّبْنَا وَقُلْنَا مَا نَزَّلَ ٱللَّهُ مِن شَىْءٍ إِنْ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا فِى ضَلَٰلٍۢ كَبِيرٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Denial refuses arrival a place to land.** `ن ز ل` `B001` is descent and alighting and `B003` is lodging; `ك ذ ب` `B002` is declaring false; and `ض ل ل` `B003` is failing to locate something. The ayah first concedes that a warner came, then denies that anything was sent down, then relocates error onto the warners. This reversal makes reception spatial: arrival occurred, but the speakers refused it lodging and mislocated the loss. The packet supplies those speech moves; I infer that categorical denial blocks reception rather than preventing descent. The claim `ما نزل ... من شيء` thus reads as a failed hosting of communication.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:10-14:** Regret over hearing and reasoning, followed by knowledge of concealed speech and chests, made the denied descent look like a reception failure inside the speakers. Their accusation of `ضلال` is retrospectively reversed as their own failure to locate what arrived.

## 67:10: وَقَالُوا۟ لَوْ كُنَّا نَسْمَعُ أَوْ نَعْقِلُ مَا كُنَّا فِىٓ أَصْحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

### Activated readings

1. **Reception and restraint interrupt affiliation.** `س م ع` `B003` is understanding with compliance; `ع ق ل` `B001` is understanding as restraint from ignorance and `B002` is tethering; `ص ح ب` `B001` is close association. The `أو` preserves hearing and reasoning as distinct gates: one receives the warning, the other binds its implications tightly enough to restrain action. The fire heard in 67:7 and warning admitted in 67:9 expose the failed sequence. The packet supplies the faculties and resulting companionship; I infer that either effective gate would have interrupted the path into affiliation. `نعقل` therefore reads as practical braking, not abstract intelligence.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:11 and 67:15:** Immediate confession shows restraint arriving too late, while the later contrast between modes of terrestrial movement gives `عقل`'s tether image a practical directional force. Reason would have changed the route, not merely the opinion.

## 67:11: فَٱعْتَرَفُوا۟ بِذَنۢبِهِمْ فَسُحْقًۭا لِّأَصْحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

### Activated readings

1. **Recognition catches the consequence at its tail end.** `ع ر ف` `B003` is recognition by a trace and `B009` is confession; `ذ ن ب` `B001` joins sin to culpable consequence while `B002` gives a tail; `س ح ق` `B002` is distance and driving away. The two consecutive `ف` links recognition, attached consequence, and distance. The packet supplies that sequence; I infer that the deed's consequence follows like a tail until its trace is undeniable, but recognition now occurs only after it has carried the speakers far away. Confession therefore reads as belated causal recognition, not merely a verbal admission.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:12:** Protective `مغفرة` supplied the unexpected opposite to the fixed tail-and-distance mechanism. Consequence may follow an act, but shielding from consequence presents another possible completion once recognition is not delayed.

## 67:12: إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخْشَوْنَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلْغَيْبِ لَهُم مَّغْفِرَةٌۭ وَأَجْرٌۭ كَبِيرٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Hidden attention receives protective covering.** `غ ي ب` `B001` is what is hidden from sight or knowledge; `غ ف ر` `B001` is protective covering and `B002` shielding from consequence; `خ ش ي` `B001` is reverent awe. The preceding group responds only after audible and visible compulsion, whereas 67:13-14 moves further inward to secret speech, chests, and subtle knowledge. The fixed fear therefore functions before forced disclosure. The packet supplies hiddenness on both sides; I infer that inwardly sustained caution enables the protective rather than punitive covering. `بالغيب` reads as disciplined response in advance of sensory crisis, not uncertainty mistaken for fear.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:13-14:** Secret speech, chests, subtlety, and tested inner knowledge clarified that `الغيب` is hiddenness rather than doubt. The later physical threats in 67:16-17 then sharpened the contrast between anticipatory awe and compelled recognition.

## 67:13: وَأَسِرُّوا۟ قَوْلَكُمْ أَوِ ٱجْهَرُوا۟ بِهِۦٓ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمٌۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

### Activated readings

1. **Secret and voiced speech are stages of one issuing process.** `س ر ر` `B001` is keeping something hidden inwardly, `ج ه ر` `B001` is open voicing, and `ص د ر` `B004` is a source from which forms issue, alongside the bodily front in `B001`. The `أو` contrasts output states, but `ذات الصدور` relocates both to a common source. The packet supplies concealment, manifestation, and source; I infer that speech forms inwardly before crossing into sound. The knowledge asserted here therefore reaches the production process before utterance, rather than merely overhearing a hidden sentence.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:14:** `ألا يعلم من خلق` corrected a possible surveillance reading. Knowledge reaches secret and voiced speech because it knows the source's formation, not because one output channel is harder to conceal.

## 67:14: أَلَا يَعْلَمُ مَنْ خَلَقَ وَهُوَ ٱللَّطِيفُ ٱلْخَبِيرُ

### Activated readings

1. **Maker's knowledge is fine-grained and tested from within.** `خ ل ق` `B001` is measuring and proportioning and `B002` is bringing into being; `ل ط ف` `B002` gives fineness and subtle hiddenness; `خ ب ر` `B001` gives tested inner knowledge as well as knowledge by report. Secret speech and chests in 67:13 supply the hidden object, while the yielding earth (67:15) and held birds (67:19) supply subtle, ongoing effects. The packet supplies making, hidden scale, and sustained effects; I infer that formative involvement yields inside knowledge. The rhetorical question reads as material intimacy between maker and made, not remote surveillance.

2. **Subtlety also describes unobtrusive support.** `ل ط ف` `B001` supplies gentle kindness in addition to hidden fineness. The next ayah makes earth traversable and provision available, while 67:19 shows bodies held aloft without a visible support. The packet does not state that `اللطيف` causes each support; I supply that arrow because the effects are proximate and share the branch's quiet gentleness. The name can therefore activate a mode of governance whose efficacy is experienced before its mechanism is seen.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:15 and 67:19:** Yielding earth and invisibly held birds unexpectedly activated both accepted sides of `لطيف`: fine hidden operation and gentle kindness. The later bird scene made unobtrusive support especially visible as a mechanism.

## 67:15: هُوَ ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلْأَرْضَ ذَلُولًۭا فَٱمْشُوا۟ فِى مَنَاكِبِهَا وَكُلُوا۟ مِن رِّزْقِهِۦ ۖ وَإِلَيْهِ ٱلنُّشُورُ

### Activated readings

1. **Traversability is a maintained condition, not an inherent possession.** `ذ ل ل` `B002` is gentle yielding without humiliation and `B003` is pliant ease after difficulty; `ن ك ب` `B004` gives shoulder and flank. The sequence makes earth a supporting body whose flanks have been rendered passable, then 67:16 immediately shows the same ground able to sink and surge. The packet supplies the state assignment in `جعل` and its reversal; I infer that a continuing restraint maintains the useful condition. Walking and eating therefore occur on granted infrastructure, not autonomous terrain.

2. **Present dispersal folds into later revival.** `م ش ي` `B001` is walking and movement; `ن ش ر` `B001` is opening and spreading while `B002` is reviving the dead. The imperatives move people outward over earth, but `وإليه` reverses the vector at the close. The packet supplies movement, provision, and return; I infer that ordinary terrestrial spread rehearses the larger motion from emergence to revival. `النشور` thus gathers spatial spreading and renewed life into the ayah's travel arc.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:16 and 67:19-20:** The immediate possibility of sinking corrected any reading of `ذلولا` as intrinsic passivity; birds then showed another body maintained in motion, and the failed-army question removed substitute support. Traversability emerged as continuously granted.

## 67:16: ءَأَمِنتُم مَّن فِى ٱلسَّمَآءِ أَن يَخْسِفَ بِكُمُ ٱلْأَرْضَ فَإِذَا هِىَ تَمُورُ

### Activated readings

1. **Security mistakes restrained stability for autonomy.** `ء م ن` `B001` is settled security and trust; `خ س ف` `B001` is ground sinking in; and `م و ر` `B001` is oscillating, surging motion. The gently yielding earth of 67:15 becomes, after the sudden `فإذا`, a moving medium; 67:19 and 67:21 then repeat divine holding with birds and provision. The packet supplies stable use, abrupt reversal, and later holding. I infer that restraint is what had made stability appear settled. The question therefore challenges confidence in an effect whose sustaining cause has been ignored.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:19 and 67:21:** `يمسكهن` and `أمسك رزقه` repeated holding across flight and provision. That later recurrence made the fixed settled-security question specifically about mistaking a held condition for a self-maintaining one.

## 67:17: أَمْ أَمِنتُم مَّن فِى ٱلسَّمَآءِ أَن يُرْسِلَ عَلَيْكُمْ حَاصِبًۭا ۖ فَسَتَعْلَمُونَ كَيْفَ نَذِيرِ

### Activated readings

1. **Warning can change medium while preserving its function.** `ر س ل` `B001` is being sent forth or released; `ح ص ب` `B003` is a dust-raising pebble wind; and `ن ذ ر` `B001` is warning that awakens fear and caution. The ayah itself pairs a material payload with `نذير`, 67:18 adds prior deniers, and `فستعلمون` postpones recognition until impact. The packet supplies released matter, warning, and delayed knowing; I infer a rhetorical migration from speech to matter, not that denial mechanically causes the storm. `كيف نذير` thus asks how warning is experienced when its medium can no longer be dismissed as talk.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:18-19:** Earlier deniers supply recurrence, then the command to look at held birds supplies a nonverbal sign. Warning unexpectedly spans historical report, threatened material release, and present observation without surrendering its cautionary role.

## 67:18: وَلَقَدْ كَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ فَكَيْفَ كَانَ نَكِيرِ

### Activated readings

1. **Rebuke as transformation into an unfamiliar state.** `ن ك ر` `B003` is the severe, difficult, unfamiliar thing and `B004` is change into an unrecognized or disliked state; `ك ذ ب` `B002` is declaring false. The preceding earth and pebble-wind threats and the following withheld support, provision, and inverted gait (67:19-22) give `فكيف` concrete modes of alteration. The packet supplies denial plus surrounding state changes; I infer that `نكير` answers denial by making the familiar environment no longer recognizable as secure. The noun therefore reads as corrective transformation, not only disapproval.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:21-23:** Withheld provision, opposed bodily orientation, and created faculties supplied three concrete ways a familiar human world can be altered or exposed. The face-down gait in 67:22 most sharply activated `ن ك ر` `B004` as change into a disliked state.

## 67:19: أَوَلَمْ يَرَوْا۟ إِلَى ٱلطَّيْرِ فَوْقَهُمْ صَٰٓفَّٰتٍۢ وَيَقْبِضْنَ ۚ مَا يُمْسِكُهُنَّ إِلَّا ٱلرَّحْمَٰنُ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِكُلِّ شَىْءٍۭ بَصِيرٌ

### Activated readings

1. **Flight is continuously renewed coordination, not self-sufficient suspension.** `ص ف ف` `B001` is straight-row arrangement, `ق ب ض` `B006` is contraction after expansion, and `م س ك` `B001` is holding and restraining. The participial `صافات` followed by finite `يقبضن` stages extension and contraction, while the restrictive `ما ... إلا` assigns continued holding beyond either wing state. The packet supplies the kinematics and restriction; I infer that sustained flight requires those alternating states to remain coordinated. `الرحمن` (`ر ح م` `B001`, mercy) consequently reads as ongoing support within motion, not merely original design.

### Retrospective surprises

- **67:21:** The exact return of `م س ك` for provision was the main surprise. It generalized the fixed holding mechanism from aerodynamic coordination to every sustaining flow, while preserving the birds as the fixed ayah's visible case.

## 67:20: أَمَّنْ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِى هُوَ جُندٌۭ لَّكُمْ يَنصُرُكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ۚ إِنِ ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ إِلَّا فِى غُرُورٍ

### Activated readings

1. **Assembled force becomes a misleading appearance of support.** `ج ن د` `B001` is a gathered force of helpers; `ن ص ر` `B001` is aid that makes someone prevail; `د و ن` `B003` is other than or besides; and `غ ر ر` `B008` is deception by misleading appearance, with `B009` adding risk. The held birds of 67:19 and potentially withheld provision of 67:21 bracket the imagined army with dependencies no aggregate can replace.
