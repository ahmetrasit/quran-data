# Surah 71 Full-Context Eleven-Ayah Reader Walk

## 71:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Mercy as a timed rescue mechanism.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر ح م B001` (mercy and tenderness) anchors both `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` and `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`. **Structural cue:** within 71:0-5, the invocation precedes warning before pain (71:1), protective obedience (71:3), forgiveness and a deferred but fixed term (71:4), and sustained calling (71:5). **Assigned role:** mercy supplies the motive for warning and respite. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies those acts; I infer that they are channels through which mercy prevents an avoidable consequence. **Reading change:** the opening mercy is not ornamental reassurance but the governing logic of intervention before a limit arrives.

2. **The Name and the named limit.** **Lexical evidence:** `س م و B005` (name as raised designation) anchors `بِسْمِ`; the same root appears in `مُّسَمًّى` at 71:4. **Structural cue:** a named source at the opening and a named term later bracket the first commission. **Assigned role:** designation places both action and limit under one authority. **Abductive move:** the packet gives the repeated root; I infer that the naming relation authorizes the mission and bounds its opportunity. **Reading change:** `بسم` reads as entering an assigned order, not merely pronouncing a label.

### Retrospective surprises

Rereading 71:0-5 made the naming link operational: the named term at 71:4 and the sustained call at 71:5 place repeated mercy before a designated boundary. This sharpened mercy from general benevolence into authorized, time-bounded rescue.

## 71:1 — إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوْمِهِۦٓ أَنْ أَنذِرْ قَوْمَكَ مِن قَبْلِ أَن يَأْتِيَهُمْ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Dispatch into an opposing flow.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر س ل B001` (being sent forth and released) anchors `أَرْسَلْنَا`; `ف ر ر B001` (flight and escape) appears as the outcome of the call in 71:6. **Structural cue:** within 71:0-6, Noah is released toward the same `قَوْم` that moves away from his call. **Assigned role:** sending is the incoming vector meant to intercept an outgoing one. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the opposed motions; I infer that the dispatch is preventative rather than merely locative. **Reading change:** the sending reads as an intervention against accelerating escape.

2. **Warning as equipment for protection.** **Lexical evidence:** `ن ذ ر B001` is a warning that awakens fear and caution; `و ق ي B002` at 71:3 is placing the self in protective caution. **Structural cue:** `مِن قَبْلِ` places warning before arrival, and 71:3 converts it into a command to guard oneself. **Assigned role:** warning creates the temporal and cognitive space in which protection can be taken. **Abductive move:** the packet states sequence, not causation; I infer that awakened caution enables protective conduct. **Reading change:** `أَنذِرْ` is not simply a threat announcement but an offered means of avoidance.

### Retrospective surprises

The later `ف ر ر B001` at 71:6 exposed the full directional contrast: Noah's release toward the people is answered by their flight away. The dispatch therefore proved more specifically interceptive than the initial warning language alone showed.

## 71:2 — قَالَ يَٰقَوْمِ إِنِّى لَكُمْ نَذِيرٌۭ مُّبِينٌ

### Activated readings

1. **Clarity that makes refusal legible.** **Lexical evidence:** `ب ي ن B005` (disclosing meaning by expression or sign) anchors `مُّبِينٌ`; `ء ذ ن B002` (receptive hearing) and `غ ش و B001` (covering veil) are embodied at 71:7. **Structural cue:** within 71:0-7, a disclosed warning is followed by fingers in ears and garments drawn over bodies. **Assigned role:** clarity removes lack of access as an explanation for refusal. **Abductive move:** the packet gives disclosure and channel closure; I infer that deliberate closure, rather than obscurity in the message, blocks reception. **Reading change:** `مبين` reads as a diagnostic exposure of resistance as well as a quality of speech.

2. **The warner as a protective alarm.** **Lexical evidence:** `ن ذ ر B001` anchors `نَذِير`; `و ق ي B002` at 71:3 and `غ ف ر B002` at 71:4 supply self-protection and shielding from consequence. **Structural cue:** the nominal role is immediately unpacked by commands and a promised reprieve. **Assigned role:** the warner connects danger-recognition to a practicable escape route. **Abductive move:** I infer that the adjacent commands are the response the warning is designed to elicit. **Reading change:** Noah's role is not exhausted by predicting pain; it is to activate caution before the fixed limit.

### Retrospective surprises

The fingers, ears, and garments at 71:7 converted `ب ي ن B005` from an abstract claim of clarity into a testable communication event. The later bodily refusal confirmed that the warning discloses both danger and the hearers' active closure of access.

## 71:3 — أَنِ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَٱتَّقُوهُ وَأَطِيعُونِ

### Activated readings

1. **A three-stage protective sequence.** **Lexical evidence:** `ع ب د B003` is worship through submissive obedience, `و ق ي B002` is placing the self in protective caution, and `ط و ع B001` is pliant obedience. **Structural cue:** the imperatives proceed from orientation, to guarding, to responsive action; 71:4 then states forgiveness and delay. **Assigned role:** worship sets allegiance, caution creates a barrier against harm, and obedience operationalizes it. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the order; I infer that each command enables the next. **Reading change:** the verse reads as a compact safety mechanism rather than three interchangeable pieties.

2. **Pliancy versus self-enlargement.** **Lexical evidence:** `ط و ع B001` anchors `أَطِيعُونِ`, while `ك ب ر B006` at 71:7 marks self-magnification and refusal to submit to truth. **Structural cue:** the requested yielding is later reversed by bodily closure, insistence, and emphatic pride. **Assigned role:** obedience is the flexible posture that keeps the warning channel open. **Abductive move:** I infer from the antithetical sequence that pride blocks compliance by making the self its own authority. **Reading change:** obedience reads as relinquishing self-rule, not mere execution of an order.

### Retrospective surprises

The emphatic self-magnification at 71:7 supplied the exact reversal of `ط و ع B001`: pliancy keeps the warning channel usable, while pride makes bodily closure into self-rule. This made the three imperatives a coordinated posture-change, not merely adjacent duties.

## 71:4 — يَغْفِرْ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمْ وَيُؤَخِّرْكُمْ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٍۢ مُّسَمًّى ۚ إِنَّ أَجَلَ ٱللَّهِ إِذَا جَآءَ لَا يُؤَخَّرُ ۖ لَوْ كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Two opposed coverings.** **Lexical evidence:** `غ ف ر B001` is a protective covering and `غ ف ر B002` is shielding a person from a sin's consequence; at 71:7, `غ ش و B001` and `ث و ب B003` furnish self-covering with garments. **Structural cue:** within 71:0-9, offered divine covering is answered by defensive human covering. **Assigned role:** one cover protects by receiving the warning, while the other blocks reception. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the parallel material images; I infer their opposite causal directions. **Reading change:** forgiveness reads as genuine protection that cannot be replaced by hiding from the call.

2. **Wrongdoing as a trailing consequence.** **Lexical evidence:** `ذ ن ب B001` gives culpable action with bad consequence, while dormant `ذ ن ب B002` gives a tail or trailing rear. **Structural cue:** `ذُنُوبِكُمْ` is followed by delay, a fixed limit, and an arrival that cannot be delayed. **Assigned role:** the tail image makes each wrong carry a remainder that follows its doer through time. **Abductive move:** the packet gives the temporal chain; I infer that the trailing consequence explains why forgiveness changes the future relation to past acts. **Reading change:** sins are not isolated entries but consequences still attached behind the actors.

3. **A bounded response interval.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ج ل B001` is an appointed limit; `ء خ ر B002` is deferral to a later time; `س م و B005` supplies designation in `مُّسَمًّى`. **Structural cue:** delay is affirmed once, then denied once the divine term arrives. **Assigned role:** postponement opens a finite interval, while naming fixes its boundary. **Abductive move:** I infer that the varied calling modes of 71:5-9 fill this interval with opportunities for response. **Reading change:** postponement reads as purposeful respite, not indefinite elasticity.

### Retrospective surprises

The self-covering at 71:7 and the shift between open and secret address at 71:9 sharpened both initial findings. Protective `غ ف ر B001/B002` requires access to correction, while the finite respite is actively filled by varied attempts to reach hearers before the named limit.

## 71:5 — قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّى دَعَوْتُ قَوْمِى لَيْلًۭا وَنَهَارًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A call intended to reassemble.** **Lexical evidence:** `د ع و B001` is calling in order to draw near, while `ف ر ر B001` at 71:6 is escape. **Structural cue:** the call toward Noah is immediately followed by movement away from him. **Assigned role:** calling is centripetal social work directed at `قَوْمِى`; flight is its centrifugal reversal. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the opposed vectors; I infer that the object of the call is renewed communal alignment, not only transmission of content. **Reading change:** `دعوت قومي` reads as attempted reassembly of a dispersing people.

2. **Temporal totality becomes communicative adaptation.** **Lexical evidence:** `ل ي ل B001` is night and darkness, while `ن ه ر B002` is day opening with light. **Structural cue:** 71:8-9 add public and secret modes to the night/day pair. **Assigned role:** darkness/light and hidden/open form two axes across which the call is varied. **Abductive move:** I infer that Noah adjusts access conditions rather than merely repeating at all hours. **Reading change:** night and day mark comprehensive, adaptive outreach rather than a bare duration formula.

### Retrospective surprises

The immediate flight at 71:6 and the public/private variations at 71:8-9 changed temporal totality into adaptive reassembly across resistant settings. The return to `غ ف ر B001/B002` at 71:10 then confirmed that every mode serves one restorative invitation.

## 71:6 — فَلَمْ يَزِدْهُمْ دُعَآءِىٓ إِلَّا فِرَارًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A reversed growth loop.** **Lexical evidence:** `ز ي د B001` is increase and growth; `ف ر ر B001` is flight. **Structural cue:** within 71:1-11, repeated calling increases avoidance, whereas seeking forgiveness at 71:10 is followed by abundant release from the sky at 71:11. **Assigned role:** increase acts as a neutral amplifier whose output depends on the receiving posture. **Abductive move:** the packet gives the two input-output sequences; I infer that resistance converts each renewed call into further rehearsal of flight. **Reading change:** the verse describes a self-reinforcing feedback loop, not a single failed appeal.

2. **Flight as involuntary disclosure.** **Lexical evidence:** dormant `ف ر ر B002` includes probing and drawing out what is hidden; `د ع و B001` supplies the call that elicits a response. **Structural cue:** 71:7 anatomizes the flight as fingers, garments, insistence, and pride. **Assigned role:** flight reveals the internal mechanism that the call encounters. **Abductive move:** I infer that the call does not cause the hidden refusal but brings it into observable action. **Reading change:** `فرارا` reads diagnostically as the exposure of a concealed stance as well as spatial escape.

### Retrospective surprises

The bodily sequence at 71:7 confirmed that flight discloses an already organized refusal. The productive sequence at 71:10-11 also corrected any negative sense in `ز ي د B001`: increase is the neutral amplifier, while receptive or resistant posture determines whether it yields abundance or more flight.

## 71:7 — وَإِنِّى كُلَّمَا دَعَوْتُهُمْ لِتَغْفِرَ لَهُمْ جَعَلُوٓا۟ أَصَٰبِعَهُمْ فِىٓ ءَاذَانِهِمْ وَٱسْتَغْشَوْا۟ ثِيَابَهُمْ وَأَصَرُّوا۟ وَٱسْتَكْبَرُوا۟ ٱسْتِكْبَارًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Counterfeit protection.** **Lexical evidence:** `غ ف ر B001/B002` offers protective covering from consequence; `غ ش و B001` is a covering veil, and `ث و ب B003` is garment and garment-as-self. **Structural cue:** the purpose clause `لِتَغْفِرَ لَهُمْ` is immediately answered by covering themselves. **Assigned role:** forgiveness is the protection offered through hearing, while garments become a substitute barrier against hearing. **Abductive move:** the packet gives the juxtaposition; I infer that self-protection is misidentified with insulation from correction. **Reading change:** the bodily scene enacts an attempted replacement of saving cover with concealing cover.

2. **From channel closure to a settled regime.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ذ ن B002` is listening and receptive hearing, `ص ر ر B002` is insistence and refusal to desist, and `ك ب ر B006` is self-magnification that refuses truth. **Structural cue:** fingers and garments precede persistence and emphatic pride. **Assigned role:** sensory blockage initiates a sequence that hardens into volitional and social posture. **Abductive move:** I infer escalation from the ordered body-to-will sequence; the packet does not itself state that each step causes the next. **Reading change:** the verse reads as the manufacture of durable resistance, not a spontaneous gesture of discomfort.

3. **A returning defensive ritual.** **Lexical evidence:** `ث و ب B003` anchors the garments, while dormant `ث و ب B004` is a repeated or returning call; `ك ل ل B003` in `كُلَّمَا` supplies total recurrence. **Structural cue:** every renewed call is paired with renewed covering. **Assigned role:** the garment becomes the repeatable device by which the hearer restores enclosure. **Abductive move:** I infer a conditioned ritual from `whenever` plus the returning-call branch. **Reading change:** the clothes are not incidental objects but instruments in a recurrent refusal loop.

### Retrospective surprises

The forgiveness-and-abundance sequence at 71:10-12 supplied the missing positive comparator for the garments. It confirmed that the people's covering is counterfeit precisely because true cover restores circulation, provision, and growth instead of sealing the self.

## 71:8 — ثُمَّ إِنِّى دَعَوْتُهُمْ جِهَارًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Public calling as a channel test.** **Lexical evidence:** `ج ه ر B001` is open voicing and public declaration; `د ع و B001` is calling to draw near. **Structural cue:** after the embodied closure of 71:7, 71:8 shifts to public address, and 71:9 then pairs announcement with secrecy. **Assigned role:** openness changes the social and acoustic channel while preserving the invitation's direction. **Abductive move:** I infer that the method tests whether resistance belongs to one setting or persists across settings. **Reading change:** `جهارا` is strategic exposure, not merely louder speech.

2. **Exploratory: clearing an obstructed source.** **Lexical evidence:** dormant `ج ه ر B008` is clearing mud from a well until water appears; 71:11-12 supply pouring sky, gardens, and rivers. **Structural cue:** the public call lies between bodily obstruction and a water-abundance sequence. **Assigned role:** public disclosure functions like removing accumulated obstruction so a life-giving source can become visible. **Abductive move:** the material analogy is mine; the packet supplies only the branch image and nearby water sequence. **Reading change:** `جهارا` can be heard as clarification by clearing, not only publicity.

### Retrospective surprises

The secret mode at 71:9 corrected any public-only account of the strategy. The rain, gardens, and rivers at 71:11-12 then made dormant `ج ه ر B008` unexpectedly coherent: clearing obstruction can disclose a source whose benefit propagates beyond the initial opening.

## 71:9 — ثُمَّ إِنِّىٓ أَعْلَنتُ لَهُمْ وَأَسْرَرْتُ لَهُمْ إِسْرَارًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Address at public and inward scales.** **Lexical evidence:** `ع ل ن B001` is making a matter manifest after concealment; `س ر ر B001` is keeping or communicating something inwardly and privately. **Structural cue:** the two verbs are coordinated and repeat the same beneficiary `لَهُمْ`, after public calling at 71:8. **Assigned role:** announcement addresses collective visibility, while confidential address reaches the private interior. **Abductive move:** I infer that resistance has both public and inward supports, requiring both scales of communication. **Reading change:** the pair is not contradictory volume-setting but comprehensive social access.

2. **Private speech as an affective opening.** **Lexical evidence:** `س ر ر B010` is joy and ease, activated dormantly beside the explicit secrecy branch `س ر ر B001`; 71:10-12 follow with forgiveness, rain, wealth, children, gardens, and rivers. **Structural cue:** concealed address immediately opens into a sequence of relief and provision. **Assigned role:** intimacy permits the warning to be coupled with reassurance rather than public exposure alone. **Abductive move:** I infer an affective function from the sequence; the packet does not specify the content assigned to each mode. **Reading change:** `أسررت` can carry hospitable, hope-bearing access as well as secrecy.

### Retrospective surprises

The relief sequence at 71:10-12 activated the affective ease of dormant `س ر ر B010`, while the successive human stages at 71:14 showed why inward address matters at more than one condition of development. Private speech thus became both reassurance and scale-sensitive access.

## 71:10 — فَقُلْتُ ٱسْتَغْفِرُوا۟ رَبَّكُمْ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ غَفَّارًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Forgiveness as generative protection.** **Lexical evidence:** `غ ف ر B001` is protective covering and `غ ف ر B002` is shielding from a sin's consequence; the root is doubled in `ٱسْتَغْفِرُوا` and `غَفَّارًا`. **Structural cue:** within 71:5-15, human garment-covering at 71:7 is replaced by forgiveness, then an overhead sky releases rain at 71:11 and life-support at 71:12. **Assigned role:** forgiveness removes harmful exposure and restores access to a productive environment. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the sequence; I infer that forgiveness enables, rather than mechanically purchases, restored flourishing. **Reading change:** seeking forgiveness becomes re-entry into protection that generates life.

2. **The Lord as stage-by-stage cultivator.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر ب ب B002` is repair, nurture, and completion stage by stage; 71:14 uses `ط و ر B004` for successive stages of creation. **Structural cue:** `رَبَّكُمْ` stands before rain, wealth, offspring, gardens, and measured creation. **Assigned role:** lordship administers growth through successive provisions rather than only issuing authority. **Abductive move:** I infer that the material and developmental sequence specifies how nurturing lordship operates. **Reading change:** `ربكم` reads as the one who can restore and cultivate a damaged life-system.

3. **Speech joined by nonverbal indication.** **Lexical evidence:** `ق و ل B001` anchors Noah's utterance, while dormant `ق و ل B014` is a thing's “saying” through indication. **Structural cue:** Noah's statement is followed by material and cosmic signs in 71:11-15. **Assigned role:** spoken instruction names what rain, growth, stages, and layered heavens then indicate. **Abductive move:** I infer that the created sequence functions as corroborating, nonverbal discourse. **Reading change:** `فقلت` begins a multi-channel disclosure rather than an isolated proposition.

### Retrospective surprises

The measured stages at 71:14 and layered heavens at 71:15 made `ر ب ب B002` visibly developmental: repair and nurture proceed by proportioned phases. They also confirmed dormant `ق و ل B014`, because created order becomes the nonverbal indication that corroborates Noah's spoken call.

## 71:11 — يُرْسِلِ ٱلسَّمَآءَ عَلَيْكُم مِّدْرَارًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Released yield from an overhead source.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر س ل B001` is sending forth or releasing; `س م و B004` is the overhead sky/cover with an attested rain extension; `د ر ر B001` is abundant yielding from a source, explicitly including rain. **Structural cue:** the verse follows seeking forgiveness and precedes connected supplies in 71:12. **Assigned role:** sky is the source, release is the transfer, and abundant yield is the sustained output. **Abductive move:** I infer that restored relation opens a flow whose downstream effects appear next. **Reading change:** rain reads as the first stage of a connected provisioning system, not a detached reward.

2. **The beneficial cover versus the blocking cover.** **Lexical evidence:** `س م و B004` frames sky as an overhead cover; `غ ش و B001` and `ث و ب B003` at 71:7 frame the people's covering. **Structural cue:** within 71:6-16, a self-imposed cover blocks speech, while the higher cover yields water and contains layered heavens and lights at 71:15-16. **Assigned role:** one enclosure closes circulation; the other structures and sustains it. **Abductive move:** I infer the contrast from repeated cover imagery. **Reading change:** `السماء` becomes a corrective model of shelter: covering can enable life rather than prevent reception.

3. **Yield that propagates.** **Lexical evidence:** `د ر ر B001` includes continuous rain or milk-like flow; `م د د B002/B003` at 71:12 gives connected supply and water feeding further water. **Structural cue:** abundant release is immediately expanded into wealth, children, gardens, and rivers. **Assigned role:** `مِّدْرَارًا` is the upstream source of a cascade. **Abductive move:** the packet lists outputs; I infer ecological propagation from their order. **Reading change:** abundance is read as sustained source-yield, not merely a large one-time quantity.

### Retrospective surprises

The layered cover and differentiated lights at 71:15-16 clarified the contrast with the garments at 71:7. The sky is not merely another enclosure; its internal order receives, distributes, and sustains, confirming beneficial cover as structured circulation.

## 71:12 — وَيُمْدِدْكُم بِأَمْوَٰلٍۢ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجْعَل لَّكُمْ جَنَّٰتٍۢ وَيَجْعَل لَّكُمْ أَنْهَٰرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A connected ecology of replenishment.** **Lexical evidence:** `م د د B002` is connected increase that supplies another, and `م د د B003` is water swelling and feeding further water; `ن ه ر B001` is a river cutting ground with flow. **Structural cue:** rain at 71:11 becomes wealth and children, then gardens and rivers; human growth from earth follows at 71:17. **Assigned role:** each output sustains another material or social layer. **Abductive move:** I infer causal connection from sequence; the packet does not state a deterministic chain between every item. **Reading change:** `يمددكم` describes a replenishing system rather than a pile of independent benefits.

2. **Children as social growth.** **Lexical evidence:** `ب ن ي B007` is filiation and derived belonging; `ن ب ت B003` at 71:17 is young people arising within a group. **Structural cue:** `بَنِينَ` is placed among rain-fed wealth and gardens, then humans are explicitly described through plant growth. **Assigned role:** children extend a community as shoots extend a living stock. **Abductive move:** I infer a social-organic analogy from the plant and offspring branches. **Reading change:** children read as continuing communal life, not merely possessed assets.

3. **Shelter transformed into flourishing canopy.** **Lexical evidence:** `ج ن ن B003` is a tree-covered garden, with `ج ن ن B001` supplying its root image of concealment. **Structural cue:** 71:7 used garments to create sterile concealment; here vegetation creates productive cover, while 71:15 adds layered overhead cover. **Assigned role:** garden-cover shelters by generating habitat and food. **Abductive move:** I infer a correction between the two concealment mechanisms. **Reading change:** `جنات` activates concealment as life-bearing refuge, reversing defensive hiding.

### Retrospective surprises

Human planting at 71:17 recast the listed children, gardens, and rivers as one ecology rather than parallel prizes. It especially strengthened `ب ن ي B007` plus `ن ب ت B003`: communal continuation is itself a cultivated form of growth.

## 71:13 — مَّا لَكُمْ لَا تَرْجُونَ لِلَّهِ وَقَارًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Failure to project an outcome.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر ج و B001` is expectant hope or anticipation; `و ق ر B003` is dignified gravity and reverence. **Structural cue:** within 71:8-18, forgiveness and flourishing at 71:10-12 are followed by staged creation, cosmic order, human growth, return, and re-emergence. **Assigned role:** expectation links present conduct to both beneficial and terminal outcomes. **Abductive move:** I infer that failure to anticipate these outcomes prevents the evidence from acquiring moral weight. **Reading change:** `لا ترجون` is not only lack of hope but a failure of consequential projection.

2. **Reverence as giving due weight to patterned scale.** **Lexical evidence:** `و ق ر B003` includes gravity, sedateness, honoring, and magnifying. **Structural cue:** the question is immediately supported by human stages (71:14), layered heavens (71:15), differentiated lights (71:16), growth (71:17), and return (71:18). **Assigned role:** `وقارا` is the proportionate cognitive and ethical weight assigned to that ordered dependence. **Abductive move:** I infer that the following evidence supplies the grounds for gravity. **Reading change:** reverence reads as accurate weighting of reality, not an ungrounded emotional posture.

### Retrospective surprises

The patterned sequence at 71:14-18 supplied the weight missing from the question: proportioned stages, layers, lights, growth, and return make `و ق ر B003` an evidence-responsive gravity. They also made `ر ج و B001` temporal projection, because present posture must anticipate the system's later transitions.

## 71:14 — وَقَدْ خَلَقَكُمْ أَطْوَارًا

### Activated readings

1. **Measured formation through successive states.** **Lexical evidence:** `خ ل ق B001` is measuring and proportioning before action, while `ط و ر B004` is successive turns and stages. **Structural cue:** within 71:9-19, human stages are followed by layered heavens, assigned lights, growth from earth, return, and the earth spread for use. **Assigned role:** proportioning governs transition from one formed state to another. **Abductive move:** I infer from the macro/micro sequence that the stages are calibrated rather than accidental. **Reading change:** creation reads as an ordered developmental process, not only an originating event.

2. **Stages as bounded thresholds.** **Lexical evidence:** dormant `ط و ر B003` is a boundary limit reached or exceeded; explicit `ط و ر B004` supplies the succession. **Structural cue:** 71:17-18 moves from growth out of earth to return into it and later exit, making each state terminate at another threshold. **Assigned role:** a `طور` both contains a condition and yields to its successor. **Abductive move:** I infer threshold-crossing from the sequence of emergence, return, and re-emergence. **Reading change:** `أطوارا` denotes bounded transitions rather than a static assortment of forms.

### Retrospective surprises

The heavenly layers at 71:15 and the growth-return sequence at 71:17-18 exposed stages across bodily, cosmic, and terrestrial scales. The spread earth at 71:19 added functional outcome, confirming that each bounded transition belongs to a larger ordered habitat.

## 71:15 — أَلَمْ تَرَوْا۟ كَيْفَ خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ سَبْعَ سَمَٰوَٰتٍۢ طِبَاقًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Seeing as systems inference.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر ء ي B001` includes seeing by eye or insight; `خ ل ق B001` is measured proportioning.
