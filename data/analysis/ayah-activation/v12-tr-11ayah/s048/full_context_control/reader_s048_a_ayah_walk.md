# Surah 48 Full-Context Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

Each section treats the named ayah as fixed and uses only its clipped eleven-ayah focus window. Contextual material is distinguished from the directional relation inferred from it.

## 48:0 - بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A name that opens the sequence.** The fixed `ٱسْم` anchors س م و `B005`, specifically the variant image "name as raised designation." Ayah 48:1 immediately repeats ف ت ح as verb and event, while 48:2-5 unfold acts attributed to the named God. The packet supplies designation followed by opening and its consequences; I infer the arrow **invoked designation -> authorized access to the ensuing acts**. The basmalah therefore reads not only as a prefatory label but as the raised heading that functionally opens the surah's sequence.

2. **Mercy as an enclosing, generative interior.** Both fixed epithets anchor ر ح م `B003`, the "womb" variant, alongside `B001` tenderness. Within the window, tranquility is put *in* hearts (48:4), and believers are made to enter tree-covered gardens with flow beneath them (48:5). Those interiors are packet facts; I infer **merciful enclosure -> protected growth and entry**. The paired epithets consequently acquire a spatial and generative force: mercy surrounds and develops its recipients rather than naming affect alone.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayah 48:1 unexpectedly made the designation branch of `ٱسم` operational: the name is followed immediately by an opening. Ayat 48:4-5 then supplied nested hearts and gardens, turning the womb branch of ر ح م from a lexical possibility into the stronger enclosure-and-growth reading.

## 48:1 - إِنَّا فَتَحْنَا لَكَ فَتْحًۭا مُّبِينًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **An aperture that produces access.** Fixed `فَتَحْنَا ... فَتْحًا` anchors ف ت ح `B001`, "opening what is closed, with an open or widened aperture," and `مُبِينًا` anchors ب ي ن `B004`, appearance and becoming clear. The following sequence supplies guidance along a road (48:2), descent into hearts (48:4), and entry into gardens (48:5). I supply the arrow **opened blockage -> visible route -> admitted movement**. The emphatic cognate construction now reads as the creation of usable access, not merely the announcement of a result.

2. **A judgment that separates outcomes.** The same fixed ف ت ح activates `B003`, "settling a closed dispute by judgment." Ayat 48:5 and 48:6 form a sharp paired allocation: entry and covered wrongs on one side, punishment and an adverse turn on the other. The packet gives the two outcomes but does not call them the effect of adjudication; I infer **decisive settlement -> separated consequences**. `فَتْحًا مُبِينًا` can therefore coexistently denote a manifest ruling that closes a contested field.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayah 48:5 made "opening" spatial by supplying actual entry, while 48:6 corrected any purely celebratory reading: the paired allocations made ف ت ح `B003` as adjudicative settlement newly visible.

## 48:2 - لِّيَغْفِرَ لَكَ ٱللَّهُ مَا تَقَدَّمَ مِن ذَنۢبِكَ وَمَا تَأَخَّرَ وَيُتِمَّ نِعْمَتَهُۥ عَلَيْكَ وَيَهْدِيَكَ صِرَٰطًۭا مُّسْتَقِيمًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Protective coverage from front to rear.** Fixed `يَغْفِرَ` anchors غ ف ر `B001`, "protective covering." The fixed temporal construction also activates ق د م `B007` (forepart or leading edge), ذ ن ب `B002` (tail or rear end), and ء خ ر `B003` (rear or posterior part): `مَا تَقَدَّمَ ... وَمَا تَأَخَّرَ` already supplies a front/back sweep around `ذَنب`. I infer **cover laid across both ends -> no exposed temporal flank**. Forgiveness thus reads as comprehensive shielding of the whole before-and-after extent, not only cancellation of isolated items.

2. **A road kept upright by support.** Fixed `مُسْتَقِيمًا` anchors ق و م `B008` (straightness and evenness), while `B004` supplies care, guardianship, and preservation as an activated scope. Ayah 48:3 places aid immediately after the road, and 48:4 and 48:7 repeat the gathered forces of heaven and earth. The packet supplies route plus supporting forces; I infer **distributed guardianship -> maintained alignment**. Guidance therefore reads as being sustained on an upright course, not simply being shown its geometry.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayat 48:4 and 48:7 surprisingly converted the straight road from an inert line into a maintained course by surrounding it with gathered forces. Ayah 48:5's ك ف ر covering of bad acts also reinforced the protective, rather than merely eliminative, force of fixed `يغفر`.

## 48:3 - وَيَنصُرَكَ ٱللَّهُ نَصْرًا عَزِيزًا

### Activated readings

1. **Aid that irrigates inward growth.** The fixed cognate pair `يَنصُرَكَ ... نَصْرًا` anchors ن ص ر `B004`, "helping rain and relief by rain," alongside ordinary prevailing aid in `B001`. Ayah 48:4 supplies descent into hearts and increase of faith; 48:5 supplies water running underneath gardens. Those descent, growth, and flow images are explicit, while I infer **descending relief -> inward increase**, by material analogy to rain. The victory can thus read as nourishing intervention that makes a community grow, not only force exerted against an opponent.

2. **Strength as reinforcement rather than isolation.** Fixed `عَزِيزًا` anchors ع ز ز `B004`, "reinforcing and strengthening." The next ayah locates tranquility in multiple hearts and names a gathered force of helpers; 48:7 repeats that force. The packet gives inward stabilization plus an encompassing support body; I infer **reinforcement of the recipients -> aid becomes resistant cohesion**. `نَصْرًا عَزِيزًا` consequently reads as aid that strengthens its bearers from within, not merely an inaccessible or prestigious victory.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayah 48:4 supplied descent and growth, then 48:5 supplied flowing water; together they were the decisive later trigger for the rain-relief branch ن ص ر `B004`. Ayah 48:7's repeated gathered force confirmed that fixed `عزيزا` can assign reinforcement to the recipients.

## 48:4 - هُوَ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنزَلَ ٱلسَّكِينَةَ فِى قُلُوبِ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ لِيَزْدَادُوٓا۟ إِيمَٰنًۭا مَّعَ إِيمَٰنِهِمْ ۗ وَلِلَّهِ جُنُودُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A stabilizer installed in a turning organ.** Fixed `ٱلسَّكِينَة` anchors س ك ن `B001` (cessation of motion) and the concrete `B008` variant, "rudder steadying a ship"; fixed `قُلُوب` anchors ق ل ب `B004`, turning and reversing. Ayah 48:2 supplies an upright route, while 48:6 supplies an adverse circular turn and 48:7 repeats the encompassing forces. The packet gives a route, a turnable interior, and a descending stabilizer; I infer **installed restraint -> correction of inward yaw -> growth along the route**. Tranquility now reads as active guidance-control, not passive quiet.

2. **Increase by secure accumulation.** The three fixed occurrences of ء م ن activate `B001`, "settled security and trust," as well as assent in `B002`; fixed ز ي د `B001` supplies increase and growth. The grammar does not replace one faith with another but adds `إِيمَانًا مَعَ إِيمَانِهِمْ`, and 48:5 depicts continuing flow under an enduring dwelling. I infer **stabilized trust -> capacity to retain rather than lose increments**. Faith reads as cumulative secured capital rather than a binary state.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayah 48:6's adverse circle made the turning branch of fixed `قلوب` consequential: tranquility prevents an inward turn from becoming a destructive circuit. Ayah 48:7's repeated full-field force then sharpened the rudder image from private calm into supported navigation.

## 48:5 - لِّيُدْخِلَ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَٱلْمُؤْمِنَٰتِ جَنَّٰتٍۢ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا وَيُكَفِّرَ عَنْهُمْ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمْ ۚ وَكَانَ ذَٰلِكَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ فَوْزًا عَظِيمًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Double enclosure: dwelling and covered fault.** Fixed `جَنَّات` anchors ج ن ن `B003`, "tree-covered garden," with concealment in `B001`; fixed `يُكَفِّرَ` anchors ك ف ر `B009`, covering or effacing sin. Ayah 48:2 has already activated forgiveness as protective covering, and this ayah places entry into a covered habitat beside covering away bad acts. The packet supplies the two coverings; I infer **removal of exposed liability -> admission into protected enclosure**. Garden and forgiveness become coordinated outside-and-inside shelter rather than unrelated rewards.

2. **Success as passage out of peril.** Fixed `فَوْزًا` anchors ف و ز `B003`, "the desert passage between escape and peril," together with `B001`, winning good and escaping. The immediately following 48:6 supplies the adverse destination, while 48:10 later in this window supplies a binding hand-pledge whose breach recoils on the self. These are the packet's danger boundaries; I infer **protected passage through a consequential field -> safe arrival in the enduring interior**. `فَوْزًا عَظِيمًا` reads as an escape trajectory, not a generic success label.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayah 48:6 supplied the perilous alternate destination that made ف و ز `B003` read as passage and escape. Ayah 48:10 added a later risk boundary inside the window: the hand-pledge may be fulfilled or unmade against the self.

## 48:6 - وَيُعَذِّبَ ٱلْمُنَٰفِقِينَ وَٱلْمُنَٰفِقَٰتِ وَٱلْمُشْرِكِينَ وَٱلْمُشْرِكَٰتِ ٱلظَّآنِّينَ بِٱللَّهِ ظَنَّ ٱلسَّوْءِ ۚ عَلَيْهِمْ دَآئِرَةُ ٱلسَّوْءِ ۖ وَغَضِبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَعَنَهُمْ وَأَعَدَّ لَهُمْ جَهَنَّمَ ۖ وَسَآءَتْ مَصِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Hypocrisy as a two-exit structure.** Fixed `ٱلْمُنَافِقِين ... ٱلْمُنَافِقَات` anchors ن ف ق `B003`, a tunnel or burrow with an exit, and especially `B004`, "showing one face while concealing another exit." Ayah 48:11 later makes the architecture explicit: tongues say what hearts do not contain. The packet supplies the split channels; I infer **pressure on the public route -> escape through the concealed inward route**. The nouns thus read as a functional strategy of dual access, not merely a group label.

2. **Bad supposition as a returning circuit.** Fixed `دَائِرَة` anchors د و ر `B003` (circulation of states) and `B004` (an adverse turn), while ظ ن ن `B003` supplies uncertain supposition. Ayah 48:10 states that undoing the pledge recoils `عَلَى نَفْسِهِ`, and 48:11 places concealed speech inside the same social field. The packet gives the adverse circle and self-directed breach; I infer **bad model -> evasive action -> consequence returning to its modeller**. `عَلَيْهِمْ دَائِرَةُ ٱلسَّوْء` becomes a feedback mechanism rather than a free-floating curse.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayah 48:10 made the adverse circle reflexive when breach returned upon the breaker. Ayah 48:11 was the larger surprise: its explicit tongue/heart split supplied the two channels needed to activate the concealed-exit architecture of ن ف ق `B004`.

## 48:7 - وَلِلَّهِ جُنُودُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ عَزِيزًا حَكِيمًا

### Activated readings

1. **A force spanning the whole vertical field.** Fixed `جُنُود` anchors ج ن د `B001`, "a gathered force of helpers"; fixed heaven and earth activate س م و `B004` (overhead sky and cover) and ء ر ض `B001` (lower ground opposite the sky). Ayah 48:10 will show only visible human hands, whereas 48:11 denies that those humans control harm or benefit. The packet supplies a top-to-bottom force and limited hands; I infer **full-field agency -> visible participants are instruments, not the boundary of causation**. The repeated force formula therefore corrects a narrowly military reading of `جنود`.

2. **Power with an internal brake.** Fixed `عَزِيزًا` anchors ع ز ز `B004`, reinforcing and strengthening, while `حَكِيمًا` anchors ح ك م `B001`, "restraining and turning back to set right." The adverse turn of 48:6, the binding pledge of 48:10, and the failed expected return in 48:12 all make direction and reversal salient. Those turns are supplied; I infer **strength + corrective restraint -> controlled redirection**. The adjective pair reads as a coupled control system rather than unqualified force followed by an ornamental attribute.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayat 48:10-11 corrected a visible-army reading by placing limited human hands beneath agency they cannot control. Ayah 48:12's failed-return expectation then made ح ك م `B001`, corrective turning-back, more diagnostic in the fixed `عزيزا حكيما` pair.

## 48:8 - إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَٰكَ شَٰهِدًۭا وَمُبَشِّرًۭا وَنَذِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **An evidentiary office with prospective consequences.** Fixed `شَاهِدًا` anchors ش ه د `B001` (presence with witnessing) and `B002` (testifying from knowledge); `مُبَشِّرًا` anchors ب ش ر `B005`, face-brightening tidings; `نَذِيرًا` anchors ن ذ ر `B001`, warning that awakens caution. Ayat 48:10-12 supply a public pledge, a tongue/heart mismatch, and concealed supposition; 48:13 supplies a prepared consequence. I infer **witnessed state -> evidence -> differentiated encouragement and warning**. The three accusatives consequently form one adjudicative process, not a list of unrelated honorifics.

2. **Tidings as the first visible edge of an outcome.** The fixed ب ش ر also activates `B007`, "first visible signs or forerunners." Ayah 48:10 makes allegiance bodily visible through hands while placing its reward in the future. The packet supplies an early enacted sign before completed recompense; I infer **visible forerunner -> confidence in an outcome not yet fully present**. `مُبَشِّرًا` can therefore denote one who makes the coming result perceptible at its leading edge, not only one who reports pleasant news.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayat 48:10-12 turned the three fixed roles into an evidence sequence: public pledge, concealed mismatch, and inward supposition require witnessing before tidings or warning can be properly assigned. Ayah 48:13's prepared blaze made the warning's prospective consequence concrete.

## 48:9 - لِّتُؤْمِنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ وَتُعَزِّرُوهُ وَتُوَقِّرُوهُ وَتُسَبِّحُوهُ بُكْرَةًۭ وَأَصِيلًا

### Activated readings

1. **Reverence as bearing covenantal weight.** Fixed `تُعَزِّرُوهُ` anchors ع ز ر `B001`, support with reverence, and `تُوَقِّرُوهُ` anchors و ق ر `B002` (a borne load) as well as `B003` (dignified gravity). Ayah 48:10 immediately materializes allegiance as hands, covenant, fulfilment, and possible unweaving. The packet supplies respect-language followed by a binding obligation; I infer **support pledged -> weight accepted and carried**. Honoring the messenger thus reads as load-bearing fidelity, not etiquette alone.

2. **Praise as a complete daily course.** Fixed `تُسَبِّحُوهُ` anchors س ب ح `B004`, motion by swimming or coursing, alongside worship in `B001`; `بُكْرَة` anchors ب ك ر `B001` (early morning) and `أَصِيلًا` anchors ء ص ل `B003` (evening). The two temporal endpoints are packet facts; I infer **movement from first endpoint to last -> the interval is traversed by praise**. The phrase reads as a time-spanning rhythm of orientation, not two disconnected utterances.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayah 48:10 was the decisive later activation: hands, pledge, covenant, and possible unweaving transformed و ق ر `B002` from generic weight into an obligation borne through reverent support. No later ayah in the window materially changed the morning-to-evening coursing reading.

## 48:10 - إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يُبَايِعُونَكَ إِنَّمَا يُبَايِعُونَ ٱللَّهَ يَدُ ٱللَّهِ فَوْقَ أَيْدِيهِمْ ۚ فَمَن نَّكَثَ فَإِنَّمَا يَنكُثُ عَلَىٰ نَفْسِهِۦ ۖ وَمَنْ أَوْفَىٰ بِمَا عَٰهَدَ عَلَيْهُ ٱللَّهَ فَسَيُؤْتِيهِ أَجْرًا عَظِيمًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Allegiance as an exchange whose stake is the self.** Fixed doubled `يُبَايِعُون` anchors ب ي ع `B003` (pledge of allegiance) and coexistently `B001` (exchange for a price); `يَد ... أَيْدِيهِمْ` activates ي د ي `B007`, hand-to-hand transfer. Reward closes the fixed ayah, and 48:15 exposes people who want to follow only when gains are available. The packet supplies transaction, transfer, recompense, and opportunistic appetite; I infer **self committed in exchange -> reward for fulfilment / self-loss through breach**. The commercial branch does not replace allegiance; it makes its cost structure visible.

2. **Breach as material unweaving.** Fixed `نَكَثَ ... يَنكُثُ` anchors ن ك ث `B001`, "undoing what was firmly twisted or woven," and `B002`, breaking a binding covenant; fixed `أَوْفَى` anchors و ف ي `B001`, complete fullness without shortfall. Joined hands supply the material analogy. I infer **interlaced commitments -> a load-bearing bond; pulling one's strand out -> damage borne by that strand's owner**. The reflexive consequence `عَلَى نَفْسِهِ` reads as the mechanics of self-unbinding, sharpened by 48:11's divided speech and 48:15's attempted word-substitution.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayah 48:11 revealed how a strand may separate internally before it is publicly broken. Ayah 48:15 then supplied both gain-seeking and attempted substitution of prior speech, confirming the exchange reading and making covenantal unweaving more than a material metaphor.

## 48:11 - سَيَقُولُ لَكَ ٱلْمُخَلَّفُونَ مِنَ ٱلْأَعْرَابِ شَغَلَتْنَآ أَمْوَٰلُنَا وَأَهْلُونَا فَٱسْتَغْفِرْ لَنَا ۚ يَقُولُونَ بِأَلْسِنَتِهِم مَّا لَيْسَ فِى قُلُوبِهِمْ ۚ قُلْ فَمَن يَمْلِكُ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ شَيْـًٔا إِنْ أَرَادَ بِكُمْ ضَرًّا أَوْ أَرَادَ بِكُمْ نَفْعًۢا ۚ بَلْ كَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ خَبِيرًۢا

### Activated readings

1. **The rear position revealed as selective following.** Fixed `ٱلْمُخَلَّفُون` anchors خ ل ف `B003`, "people remain behind at the rear." Ayah 48:12 imagines the messenger and believers never returning to their households; 48:15 repeats the rear group when gains become available and records `نَتَّبِعْكُمْ`; 48:16 redirects them toward severe resistance. The packet supplies absence, desired pursuit, and a harder summons; I infer **expected cost -> rearward staying, expected gain -> attempted forward following**. The participle reads as a chosen position in a movement economy, not a fixed identity.

2. **Two speech channels, one audible and one inward.** Fixed repetitions of ق و ل activate `B012`, "unspoken inner saying," alongside uttered speech in `B001`; fixed `أَلْسِنَتِهِمْ` anchors ل س ن `B001`, the tongue as speech-organ, and `قُلُوبِهِمْ` anchors ق ل ب `B001`, the inward faculty. Ayah 48:12 then reports what was made attractive inside those hearts. The packet supplies outer statement and inner content; I infer **audible excuse masking inward forecast -> speech used as decoy rather than disclosure**. `يَقُولُون` therefore has two co-present theatres whose mismatch is the mechanism.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayah 48:12 disclosed the exact inward forecast hidden behind the audible excuse. Ayat 48:15-16 were the stronger retrospective correction: the rear group seeks forward motion toward gain but faces a separate costly summons, proving that `المخلفون` marks selective positioning.

## 48:12 - بَلْ ظَنَنتُمْ أَن لَّن يَنقَلِبَ ٱلرَّسُولُ وَٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ إِلَىٰٓ أَهْلِيهِمْ أَبَدًۭا وَزُيِّنَ ذَٰلِكَ فِى قُلُوبِكُمْ وَظَنَنتُمْ ظَنَّ ٱلسَّوْءِ وَكُنتُمْ قَوْمًۢا بُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A projected non-return generated by inward turning.** Fixed `يَنقَلِبَ` and `قُلُوبِكُمْ` share ق ل ب and activate `B004`, turning and reversing, across an outward verb and an inward noun. Ayah 48:11 has already separated tongue from heart; 48:15 and 48:16 show the same people trying to follow gain yet turning from costly obedience, and 48:17 repeats volitional turning away. The packet supplies these opposed orientations; I infer **an inwardly reversed evaluator -> projection of irreversible outward failure onto others**. The forecast of no return reads as a disclosure of the forecasters' own turned hearts.

2. **Decorated thought on uncultivated ground.** Fixed `زُيِّنَ` anchors ز ي ن `B002`, making beauty show, while `بُورًا` anchors ب و ر `B003`, "waste and uncultivated land." Ayah 48:17, at the forward edge of the window, contrasts this with entered gardens and running water. The packet gives cosmetic display, barren ground, and cultivated enclosure; I infer **surface beautification without truthful cultivation -> a persuasive but fruitless inner model**. `قَوْمًا بُورًا` thus diagnoses failed production, not only destruction.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayat 48:15-17 made the reversal reading behavioral: pursuit appears around gain, withdrawal around cost, and genuine bodily impediment is separately exempted. Ayah 48:17's watered gardens also supplied the productive landscape whose absence makes fixed `بورا` read as uncultivated inner ground.

## 48:13 - وَمَن لَّمْ يُؤْمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ فَإِنَّآ أَعْتَدْنَا لِلْكَٰفِرِينَ سَعِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Refusal of a security relation.** Fixed `يُؤْمِن` anchors ء م ن `B001`, settled security and trust, alongside confirming assent in `B002`; fixed `ٱلْكَافِرِين` anchors ك ف ر `B003`, covering or rejecting truth. Ayah 48:18 later embodies trust in a pledge beneath the tree and answers it with descending tranquility. The packet supplies rejected trust and enacted trust; I infer **covering the disclosed relation -> remaining outside its stabilizing security**. Non-belief therefore reads not only as a failed proposition but as refusal to enter a trust architecture.

2. **A consequence already provisioned.** Fixed `أَعْتَدْنَا` anchors ع ت د `B001`, "prepared and present," and `سَعِيرًا` anchors س ع ر `B001`, fire kindling and blazing. Within the same window, 48:14 places forgiveness and punishment under present dominion, 48:16 poses an immediate obedience/turning choice, and 48:18 presents a near opening as returned recompense. I infer **present moral configuration -> an already readied matching outcome**. The verse shifts from a distant threat to a consequence structurally on hand.

### Retrospective surprises

Ayah 48:18 supplied the surprising positive counterpart: trust is enacted under the tree, its interior is known, tranquility descends, and a near recompense is already available. That sequence sharpened both refusal-of-security and prepared-present consequence in the fixed ayah.

## 48:14 - وَلِلَّهِ مُلْكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ يَغْفِرُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيُعَذِّبُ مَن يَشَآءُ ۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورًۭا رَّحِيمًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Dominion as effective disposal, not a decorative preface.** Fixed `مُلْك` anchors م ل ك `B002` (ownership and disposal) and `B003` (kingship and dominion). Ayah 48:11 has asked who can control any harm or benefit against God; 48:15 exposes desire to acquire gains; 48:18-19 allocate approval, opening, and gains. These are packet-supplied claims and distributions; I infer **exclusive disposal -> authority to allocate both shielding and consequence**. The opening clause causally governs the two following verbs rather than merely magnifying their subject.

2. **Forgiveness and mercy as protective re-housing.** Fixed غ ف ر activates `B001`, protective covering, and fixed ر ح م activates the `B003` womb image alongside tenderness in `B001`. Ayah 48:17 supplies entry into tree-covered gardens, and 48:18 supplies shelter beneath a tree plus tranquility laid upon the believers.
