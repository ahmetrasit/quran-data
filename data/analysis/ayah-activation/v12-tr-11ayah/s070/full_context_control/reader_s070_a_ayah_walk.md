# Surah 70 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

## 70:0 | بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `س م و B005` supplies the name as a raised designation, while `س م و B001` supplies rising or elevation; `أ ل ه B002` is the fixed divine name. **Structural cue:** the opening `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ` precedes `ٱللَّهِ ذِى ٱلْمَعَارِجِ` (70:3) and the actual ascent `تَعْرُجُ ... إِلَيْهِ` (70:4). **Assigned role:** the designation is the verbal point from which the later vertical system is ordered. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the repeated divine name and the ascent; I infer that their placement activates the elevating image inside “name,” rather than merely sharing a topic. **Reading change:** the formula becomes an orientation under an elevated designation that the next ayat spatially enact.

2. **Lexical evidence:** both `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` and `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` retain `ر ح م B001`, mercy as tender care. **Structural cue:** the doubled mercy forms the immediate threshold to the requested, unavoidable punishment in 70:1-2. **Assigned role:** mercy establishes the relation within which the severe event is announced. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies adjacency and lexical opposition; I infer that the opening condition governs the announcement rather than being erased by it. **Reading change:** punishment is encountered after, and therefore under the pressure of, an emphatically merciful naming.

### Retrospective surprises

70:3-4 unexpectedly turned the opening “name” into a vertical orientation through owned ascent routes and enacted ascent. At the same time, 70:1-2 prevented the doubled mercy from reading as a detached formula: it now stands as the relational threshold to severity.

## 70:1 | سَأَلَ سَآئِلٌۢ بِعَذَابٍۢ وَاقِعٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** the verb and participle repeat `س أ ل B001`, asking or requesting; `ع ذ ب B005` is torment or punishment; `و ق ع B001` is falling into occurrence or becoming fixed reality, with `و ق ع B002` adding a hard, overwhelming descent. **Structural cue:** the requested object is immediately qualified as `وَاقِع`, then given no warder (70:2), a divine source (70:3), and an immense measured horizon (70:4). **Assigned role:** the participle converts the request’s object from a proposition into an event already characterized by realization. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies request plus fixed occurrence; I infer that the request does not initiate the event but exposes the requester to what is already on its way. **Reading change:** the ayah reads less like information-seeking and more like a provocative demand colliding with an unavoidable reality.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `و ق ع B012` permits expectation or conjecture, whereas `و ق ع B001` gives actual occurrence. **Structural cue:** those addressed judge the event remote in 70:6, while the construction here uses an active participle. **Assigned role:** the weaker expectation branch voices the human stance, and the occurrence branch corrects it from within the same root field. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the later remoteness judgment; I infer that it retrospectively activates, but does not replace, the conjectural branch. **Reading change:** `وَاقِع` carries a local contest between “merely expected” and “already fixed to occur.”

### Retrospective surprises

70:6 supplied the surprising correction: the later judgment of remoteness activates `و ق ع B012` as the requester’s conjectural stance, while 70:2-4 keeps `B001` dominant as fixed occurrence. The same word can therefore stage the dispute it resolves.

## 70:2 | لِّلْكَٰفِرِينَ لَيْسَ لَهُۥ دَافِعٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ك ف ر B003` is rejection of truth, while `ك ف ر B001` preserves covering or concealment; `د ف ع B001` is pushing away or warding off, and `ل ي س B001` negates a present state. **Structural cue:** 70:1 fixes the punishment as occurring, 70:3 names its source, and 70:6-7 oppose remote and near judgments. **Assigned role:** concealment supplies the attempted cognitive cover, while the negated warder denies that cover any protective force. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies rejection and the absence of a repelling agent; I infer the arrow “covering the truth cannot become a shield against the event.” **Reading change:** `لِّلْكَٰفِرِينَ` describes not only a class of recipients but a failed defensive mechanism.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `د ف ع B001` supplies a counter-push, while `ع ر ج B004` in 70:3-4 supplies ascent. **Structural cue:** the sequence places an absent opposing force beside a functioning upward route and upward motion. **Assigned role:** `دَافِع` would be the counter-vector, but its present-state negation leaves the event’s ordered movement uncontested. **Abductive move:** the packet gives the two directional elements; I infer their vectorial opposition. **Reading change:** “no warder” becomes a spatial claim that no force can redirect the announced process.

### Retrospective surprises

70:3-4 made `دَافِع` newly directional. Once ascent routes and upward motion appeared, “no warder” ceased to be only a legal impossibility and became the absence of any effective counter-vector; 70:6-7 then showed that contrary judgment is not contrary force.

## 70:3 | مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ ذِى ٱلْمَعَارِجِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ع ر ج B004` is ascent, and the plural `ٱلْمَعَارِجِ` turns it into ascent routes or means; `أ ل ه B002` is the fixed divine name. **Structural cue:** the nominal possession in 70:3 is immediately enacted by `تَعْرُجُ ... إِلَيْهِ` in 70:4, while 70:2 denies any counterforce. **Assigned role:** the plural routes provide the infrastructure through which the next ayah’s agents move. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies possession followed by cognate motion; I infer that the noun names an operating system rather than a decorative height-image. **Reading change:** the source of punishment is also presented as proprietor of ordered channels of ascent.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ع ر ج B002` includes curvature or turning from a straight line, alongside the dominant ascent of `B004`. **Structural cue:** 70:4 joins upward travel to a day whose measure vastly exceeds ordinary scale, and 70:6-7 split judgments of distance. **Assigned role:** curvature gives an exploratory model for why ordinary distance judgments fail inside the route system. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies unusual measure and opposed perspectives; I infer that non-straight routing could make those details cohere. The weak link is that the plural form itself does not explicitly assert curvature. **Reading change:** exploratorily, `ٱلْمَعَارِجِ` can suggest routes whose geometry defeats ordinary estimates of far and near.

### Retrospective surprises

70:4 confirmed that the plural noun names working infrastructure by immediately enacting its root as a verb. The later far/near split in 70:6-7 then made the curved-route branch visible, though still exploratory because no construction explicitly asserts curvature.

## 70:4 | تَعْرُجُ ٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ إِلَيْهِ فِى يَوْمٍۢ كَانَ مِقْدَارُهُۥ خَمْسِينَ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ع ر ج B004` supplies ascent; `ق د ر B001` supplies measured extent; `خ م س B001`, `أ ل ف B001`, and `س ن و B008` supply the articulated numerical span. **Structural cue:** 70:3 names plural ascent routes, and 70:4 follows with a finite verb, named ascending agents, destination, and measured temporal field. **Assigned role:** measure turns vertical movement into an ordered operation rather than an unbounded image. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies route, motion, agents, endpoint, and quantity; I infer that their syntax presents a coordinated transit system. **Reading change:** the ayah reads as calibrated movement across scales, not simply as “going upward for a long time.”

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ر و ح B001` is the life-spirit, while accepted `ر و ح B003` is wind or breeze; `س م و B004` in 70:8 is the overhead sky-cover. **Structural cue:** the fixed ayah places `ٱلرُّوحُ` in upward motion, and the nearby sequence later renders sky and mountains materially unstable (70:8-9). **Assigned role:** the wind branch supplies a mobile-medium resonance around the named spirit without displacing the personified co-agent. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies movement through a soon-transformed overhead field; I infer that this environment activates the branch. The weak link is that the definite singular and coordination with angels favor the spirit reading. **Reading change:** exploratorily, `ٱلرُّوحُ` carries both an ascending agent and an atmospheric mobility resonance.

### Retrospective surprises

70:8-9 retrospectively supplied an unstable sky-and-mountain medium around the ascent. That made the wind image of `ر و ح B003` newly audible, but the personified coordination with angels still corrects it back to a secondary resonance rather than a replacement reading.

## 70:5 | فَٱصْبِرْ صَبْرًۭا جَمِيلًا

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** the cognate verb and object repeat `ص ب ر B001`, self-restraint against panic; `ج م ل B004` is beauty or graceful goodness. **Structural cue:** the emphatic cognate construction is placed between the vast measure of 70:4 and the opposed estimates of remoteness and nearness in 70:6-7, before material collapse in 70:8-9. **Assigned role:** restraint governs conduct while scale and timing remain disputed. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies temporal disorientation and the command; I infer that panic is the response being blocked. **Reading change:** patience becomes an active regulation of reaction under distorted time perception, not passive waiting.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ج م ل B003` is a collected or coherent whole, alongside the overt evaluative `B004`. **Structural cue:** 70:8-9 will dissolve the stable material identities of sky and mountains, whereas the command intensifies `صبر` with an internal object. **Assigned role:** the “collected whole” branch gives formal coherence to the commanded restraint. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies forthcoming external disaggregation; I infer that “beautiful” restraint is conduct kept internally gathered while the surrounding order loses cohesion. **Reading change:** `جَمِيلًا` can make patience read as composed wholeness as well as moral beauty.

### Retrospective surprises

70:8-9 made the collected-whole image of `ج م ل B003` surprising and useful: external structures lose cohesion just after the command to keep conduct composed. This later material collapse sharpened patience from delay into active internal integration.

## 70:6 | إِنَّهُمْ يَرَوْنَهُۥ بَعِيدًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ر أ ي B002` is judgment or opinion, not only ocular seeing (`B001`); `ب ع د B001` is remoteness. **Structural cue:** 70:7 mirrors the same seeing construction but reverses the predicate to nearness, while 70:4 has already supplied an extraordinary temporal measure. **Assigned role:** the judgment branch locates distance in the evaluators’ assessment rather than in an uncontested geometry. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies mirrored syntax and opposed predicates; I infer that perspective, not the event, changes between them. **Reading change:** “they see it far” becomes a diagnosis of evaluative mis-scaling rather than a neutral report of distance.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ب ع د B003` is separation, and `ر أ ي B001` is seeing. **Structural cue:** 70:8-9 places a transformed sky and mountains between the observers and the event, while 70:10-11 turns familiar persons from relationally available to merely visible. **Assigned role:** separation expands “far” from time into failed access. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies later visibility without usable relation; I infer that this material and social separation sharpens the adjective here. **Reading change:** remoteness begins to mean not just “late” but “held outside effective contact.”

### Retrospective surprises

70:11 changed the earlier seeing most sharply. Its causative passive forces perception, revealing that 70:6 had been voluntary judgment rather than deficient eyesight; what was held far is later made unavoidably visible.

## 70:7 | وَنَرَىٰهُ قَرِيبًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ر أ ي B002` is judgment; `ق ر ب B002` is temporal nearness or an approaching completion, alongside general nearness in `B001`. **Structural cue:** the first-person judgment exactly reverses 70:6, then `يَوْمَ` in 70:8 begins the event sequence. **Assigned role:** temporal nearness corrects the prior estimate and acts as the hinge into realization. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the mirrored evaluations and immediate event-day syntax; I infer that 70:8 enacts the completion implicit in `قَرِيبًا`. **Reading change:** nearness is not merely spatial proximity but the event’s readiness to become present.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ق ر ب B003` supplies kin-nearness, while `ح م م B011` in 70:10 supplies intimate kin or close companionship and 70:11-12 names children, spouse, and brother. **Structural cue:** event-nearness is followed by the collapse of usable human closeness. **Assigned role:** the kin branch makes `قَرِيبًا` compete with the relational proximity that normally protects a person. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the lexical proximity and the nearby kin sequence; I infer that the event displaces kin as the most consequential “near” reality. **Reading change:** the ayah can carry a relational as well as temporal correction: what is truly near is not the bond on which the observers rely.

### Retrospective surprises

70:10-12 unexpectedly activated `ق ر ب B003`, kin-nearness. The event called near in 70:7 becomes operative just as intimate, filial, spousal, and fraternal closeness loses protective force, so temporal proximity displaces social proximity.

## 70:8 | يَوْمَ تَكُونُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ كَٱلْمُهْلِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `س م و B004` is the overhead sky or covering; `م ه ل B003` is molten or residual matter; `ك و ن B001` supplies becoming or occurrence under temporal predication. **Structural cue:** the simile changes the stable overhead cover into a processable substance, and 70:9 performs a parallel material conversion on mountains. **Assigned role:** `كَٱلْمُهْلِ` removes the sky’s architectural stability while `تَكُونُ` marks the state transition. **Abductive move:** the packet explicitly supplies the two material states; I infer the functional arrow “loss of stable cover removes ordinary spatial security.” **Reading change:** the ayah becomes a transformation of the world’s enclosing structure, not merely a color comparison.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `م ه ل B001` supplies deliberateness, postponement, or granted delay, alongside the overt molten-substance branch `B003`. **Structural cue:** 70:5 commands patience, 70:6 calls the event remote, and 70:7 calls it near before 70:8 names `ٱلْمُهْلِ`. **Assigned role:** the delay branch supplies a latent temporal echo inside the material simile. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the delay dispute and the polysemous noun; I infer that what was treated as postponement is now converted into the event’s visible material. The weak link is that the comparative noun directly favors `B003`. **Reading change:** exploratorily, the line makes presumed delay itself “melt” into realization.

### Retrospective surprises

70:9 confirmed that the sky simile belongs to a paired material-state mechanism rather than isolated imagery. Its parallel transformation strengthened molten `م ه ل B003` and corrected the delay branch to a secondary temporal echo only.

## 70:9 | وَتَكُونُ ٱلْجِبَالُ كَٱلْعِهْنِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ج ب ل B001` is a solid mountain mass; `ع ه ن B003` is dyed, soft wool; `ك و ن B001` marks the changed state. **Structural cue:** the syntax exactly parallels 70:8, but replaces an overhead cover with terrestrial solidity and molten matter with fiber. **Assigned role:** mountain and wool occupy opposite ends of a density and cohesion scale. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the material comparison; I infer that the mechanism is systematic de-solidification rather than decorative resemblance. **Reading change:** the mountains are read as losing the physical property by which “mountain” normally secures the landscape.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ع ه ن B002` is limp bending without complete separation, while `B003` supplies wool. **Structural cue:** after sky and mountains lose rigidity, 70:10-14 keeps human relations identifiable by name even as their protective cohesion fails. **Assigned role:** the limp-but-not-separated branch models persistence of outline after loss of structural force. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies material weakening followed by social weakening; I infer a cross-domain analogy, not a stated causal relation. **Reading change:** `كَٱلْعِهْنِ` can imply a world whose forms remain recognizable while their load-bearing bonds have gone slack.

### Retrospective surprises

70:10-14 supplied a social analogue that was not initially obvious: named relations remain recognizable while their load-bearing protection goes slack. That later sequence made limp-but-not-separated `ع ه ن B002` functionally relevant beside the direct wool image.

## 70:10 | وَلَا يَسْـَٔلُ حَمِيمٌ حَمِيمًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** the repeated `ح م م B011` supplies close kin or an intimate companion; `س أ ل B001` is asking or requesting. **Structural cue:** the reciprocal-looking pair `حَمِيمٌ حَمِيمًا` is negated, then 70:11-14 inventories children, spouse, brother, sheltering clan, and everyone on earth as possible ransom. **Assigned role:** intimacy supplies the social channel that asking should traverse, while negation closes that channel before relations become exchange objects. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies failed inquiry followed by attempted substitution; I infer that loss of communication enables the later commodification of bonds. **Reading change:** the ayah marks the first mechanical failure of intimacy, not merely a lack of conversation.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ح م م B006` is imminence, alongside the direct intimate-person branch `B011`. **Structural cue:** 70:6-7 contests whether the event is remote or near, and 70:8 begins its day before this doubled `حَمِيم`. **Assigned role:** imminence adds temporal pressure to the intimate pair. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies nearness and the repeated root form; I infer that event-imminence overwhelms interpersonal closeness. The person-noun syntax keeps `B011` primary. **Reading change:** exploratorily, the doubled word makes two kinds of closeness collide: close persons cease to function when the close event arrives.

### Retrospective surprises

70:11-14 turned failed asking into a more severe relation: intimates who no longer communicate become items in an expanding ransom inventory. The later list also confirmed that `ح م م B011`, not bare temporal imminence, remains the governing branch.

## 70:11 | يُبَصَّرُونَهُمْ ۚ يَوَدُّ ٱلْمُجْرِمُ لَوْ يَفْتَدِى مِنْ عَذَابِ يَوْمِئِذٍۭ بِبَنِيهِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ب ص ر B001` is ocular seeing and `ب ص ر B002` is inner discernment; the causative passive `يُبَصَّرُونَهُمْ` makes seeing something imposed on its recipients. **Structural cue:** 70:6-7 used active seeing for opposed judgments, whereas 70:10 denies inquiry and 70:11 forces mutual visibility. **Assigned role:** causation corrects voluntary misjudgment by making the previously mis-scaled reality perceptually unavoidable. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the shift in verbal agency; I infer the arrow “compelled exposure defeats perspectival control.” **Reading change:** the line does not merely say that people notice one another; it stages forced recognition after failed judgment and failed asking.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `و د د B002` is wishing, with `و د د B001` preserving affection; `ف د ي B001` is ransom by substitution; `ب ن ي B007` is filiation; `ع ذ ب B005` is punishment. **Structural cue:** the desired exchange begins with children, then expands through spouse, brother, clan, and everyone on earth in 70:12-14. **Assigned role:** affection identifies what is most relationally valued, while ransom converts that value into substitutable payment. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the wish and the expanding list; I infer that affection makes these persons exchange-worthy precisely by first making them precious. **Reading change:** the wish becomes a horrifying inversion of attachment, not a generic wish to escape.

### Retrospective surprises

70:12-14 revealed that the ransom wish has a controlled expansion from filial bond to universal substitute. Then 70:16 supplied the sharper surprise: desired self-extraction is answered by an extracting force applied to the self.

## 70:12 | وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَأَخِيهِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ص ح ب B001` is companionship and `ص ح ب B002` adds protective accompaniment; `أ خ و B001` is kinship, while `أ خ و B002` is a tethering bond. **Structural cue:** the conjuncts continue the ransom object begun in 70:11 and precede the sheltering clan in 70:13. **Assigned role:** the two nouns distinguish chosen/domestic accompaniment from natal tethering, then place both under the same substitution syntax. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies distinct bond types but one governing ransom verb; I infer that the shared syntax deliberately flattens normally non-equivalent relations into exchange units. **Reading change:** the terse pair becomes a compact map of protective bonds being stripped of their ordinary roles.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ص ح ب B002` specifically carries accompaniment that protects, and `أ خ و B002` carries binding or tethering. **Structural cue:** 70:16 names forceful extraction and 70:17 names those who turned away, both still inside this ayah’s local window. **Assigned role:** protection and tethering mark the resistance that the later extracting and reversing motions overcome. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies social bonds and later force verbs; I infer a cross-domain arrow from extraction to the failure of those bonds. **Reading change:** exploratorily, spouse and brother read not only as persons offered up but as attachments from which the offender is being forcibly detached.

### Retrospective surprises

70:16-17 made the pair’s protective and tethering branches newly mechanical. Extraction and reversed direction recast spouse and brother as attachments that neither shield the offender nor remain usable as substitute escape routes.

## 70:13 | وَفَصِيلَتِهِ ٱلَّتِى تُـْٔوِيهِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ف ص ل B009` is the near clan-segment, while `ف ص ل B001` is separation; `أ و ي B001` is a shelter or gathering point. **Structural cue:** the relative clause directly assigns sheltering to a noun whose root also carries separation, and the whole phrase remains governed by the ransom wish from 70:11. **Assigned role:** the clan gathers the person into refuge while delimiting that person’s segment from others. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies both accepted root images and the sheltering clause; I infer that bounded separation is what makes this group a usable refuge. **Reading change:** `فَصِيلَتِهِ` becomes paradoxically precise: the group that protects by enclosing him is the group he would sever and surrender.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `أ و ي B001` supplies a collecting refuge; `ج م ع B001` in 70:14 and 70:18 supplies aggregation, while `و ع ي B002` in 70:18 supplies containment. **Structural cue:** the sheltering unit is followed first by maximal gathering as imagined payment and then by gathering into a hoard. **Assigned role:** refuge becomes the first of several containers whose protective purpose is inverted. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the repeated gather/container relations; I infer that the sequence tests progressively larger enclosures and shows none capable of rescue. **Reading change:** the clan is read as one failed containment strategy in a local architecture of shelters and stores.

### Retrospective surprises

70:14 and 70:18 unexpectedly repeated gathering at larger and more material scales. They exposed the sheltering clan as the first failed container in a sequence that moves from refuge, to universal aggregation, to sealed hoard.

## 70:14 | وَمَن فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ جَمِيعًۭا ثُمَّ يُنجِيهِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ج م ع B001` gathers scattered parts into one whole, and `ج م ع B009` emphasizes intact totality; `ن ج و B001` is deliverance by separation. **Structural cue:** children, spouse, brother, and clan in 70:11-13 expand to everyone on the lower ground (`أ ر ض B001`), followed by `ثُمَّ` and singular rescue. **Assigned role:** total gathering constructs the maximal substitute, while deliverance isolates the offender from the gathered whole. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies aggregation followed by rescue; I infer the directional arrow “include everyone else so that one self may be separated out.” **Reading change:** the ayah exposes rescue as an imagined asymmetry: universal inclusion in loss for singular extraction from it.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ن ج و B003` is stripping or extraction, alongside deliverance in `B001`; `ن ز ع B001` in 70:16 is forceful pulling out. **Structural cue:** the desired `يُنجِيهِ` is shortly answered by an extractor that pulls at extremities. **Assigned role:** the extraction resonance links desired removal from punishment to punishment’s own removing action. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies two extraction roots but does not state that one answers the other; I infer the reversal from their proximity. **Reading change:** the wished-for extraction of the self is answered by extraction performed on the self.

### Retrospective surprises

70:16 corrected the fantasy of rescue by activating extraction in the opposite direction: not the offender from punishment, but bodily parts from the offender. 70:18 then showed that the same gather-and-isolate logic had already structured his hoarding.

## 70:16 | نَزَّاعَةًۭ لِّلشَّوَىٰ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** the intensive form activates `ن ز ع B001`, forceful pulling out; `ش و ي B002` is the bodily extremities or nonfatal outer parts. **Structural cue:** 70:11-14 tries to substitute other bodies for the offender, while 70:17 makes the force call the one who withdrew and 70:18 recalls what he enclosed. **Assigned role:** extraction relocates the exchange mechanism onto the offender’s own bodily perimeter. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies attempted substitution and bodily pulling; I infer that the latter reverses the former by making the self, not its social substitutes, the direct object of removal. **Reading change:** the compressed phrase becomes an anatomically targeted undoing of evasive substitution.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ش و ي B004` is a remainder or what is spared, while the feminine intensive `نَزَّاعَة` marks repeated or forceful removal. **Structural cue:** 70:14 had expanded the proposed payment to `جَمِيعًا`, leaving no one outside the offer. **Assigned role:** the remainder branch supplies the residual limit that the intensive extractor threatens to deny. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies totality and intensive extraction; I infer the arrow “a wish that spares only the self meets a force that does not preserve even the self’s remainder.” **Reading change:** `لِلشَّوَى` can resonate with the question of what, if anything, is left spared.

### Retrospective surprises

70:17-18 clarified what the extractor undoes: it calls back the one defined by withdrawal and answers the one who gathered and enclosed. The later verbs made bodily removal read as reversal of both evasive motion and possessive containment.

## 70:17 | تَدْعُوا۟ مَنْ أَدْبَرَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `د ع و B001` is calling in order to draw near; `د ب ر B003` is turning one’s back; `و ل ي B007` is turning away. **Structural cue:** a forward-directed call governs a relative clause with two accumulated withdrawal predicates. **Assigned role:** the call supplies an attracting vector, and the paired verbs specify the contrary vector it reverses. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies all three motions; I infer only the directional relation among them. **Reading change:** the ayah becomes a compact reversal mechanism: the force summons toward itself precisely the one whose defining action was doubled retreat.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `و ل ي B004` can denote alliance or close relation, and `و ل ي B001` adjacency; the surface predicate is best anchored in the withdrawal branch `B007`. **Structural cue:** 70:12-13 named companion, brother, and sheltering clan, while 70:17 describes turning away after those bonds have failed as substitutes. **Assigned role:** the alliance branch makes the retreat socially as well as spatially legible. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies nearby relations and the polysemous root; I infer that the person’s turning includes abandoned obligations of proximity. **Reading change:** `تَوَلَّى` can register withdrawal from relational responsibility, not only bodily departure.

### Retrospective surprises

70:18 made withdrawal unexpectedly material: turning away culminates in gathering and sealing away. 70:22 then supplied a contrasting approach practice, sharpening the call as compelled nearness after a life organized around retreat.

## 70:18 | وَجَمَعَ فَأَوْعَىٰٓ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ج م ع B001` is gathering scattered parts; `و ع ي B002` is containing something in a vessel. **Structural cue:** the conjunction gives collection and the `فَـ` sequence immediately gives enclosure; 70:13-14 had already moved from a sheltering container to maximal gathering, and 70:16-17 answers with extraction and summons. **Assigned role:** gathering supplies accumulation, while containment prevents recirculation. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the ordered verbs; I infer that enclosure is the purpose and completion of gathering. **Reading change:** the line diagnoses not possession alone but a two-stage hoarding mechanism: acquire, then seal.
