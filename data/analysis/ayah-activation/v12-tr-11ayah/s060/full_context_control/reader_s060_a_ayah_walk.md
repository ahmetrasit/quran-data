# Surah 60 Full-Context Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

Each analysis is anchored to its fixed ayah and uses only the clipped window of up to five ayat before and five after it.

## 60:0: بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `س م و` B005 supplies “name as a raised designation,” while `ء ل ه` B002 supplies the fixed divine-name invocation. **Structural cue and role:** the opening prepositional phrase places the designated divine name before any command, conflict, or petition in 60:1–5; it therefore serves as the governing point of orientation rather than as another participant inside the conflict. **Abductive move:** beginning action under a raised designation is inferred to relocate authority over all the relations that follow. **Reading change:** the basmalah becomes an orienting act: covert loyalty, kinship, enmity, and pardon are all evaluated under a name invoked above those competing claims.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ر ح م` B001 gives tender mercy, while B002 gives kinship and B003 the womb; the latter two are activated by `أَرْحَامُكُمْ` in 60:3, although the fixed forms `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` retain the mercy branch. **Structural cue and role:** the doubled mercy forms precede a window in which kin ties fail to save (60:3), Abraham addresses his father (60:4), and forgiveness is requested (60:5). Their role is to place mercy around, but not collapse it into, biological attachment. **Abductive move:** because the same root can connect tenderness to kin-generating relations, mercy is inferred to provide a higher model for care when lineage itself cannot govern allegiance. **Reading change:** the pair of names does not merely soften the opening; it distinguishes divine care from the partiality of family bonds.

### Retrospective surprises

The later appearance of `أَرْحَامُكُمْ` (`ر ح م` B002) in 60:3 makes the opening root unexpectedly diagnostic. The mercy names and kinship share a root, yet the window denies saving efficacy to kin and asks for divine forgiveness in 60:5 (`غ ف ر` B002). This sharpens the opening into a contrast between unrestricted protective care and care narrowed by lineage.

## 60:1: يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ لَا تَتَّخِذُوا۟ عَدُوِّى وَعَدُوَّكُمْ أَوْلِيَآءَ تُلْقُونَ إِلَيْهِم بِٱلْمَوَدَّةِ وَقَدْ كَفَرُوا۟ بِمَا جَآءَكُم مِّنَ ٱلْحَقِّ يُخْرِجُونَ ٱلرَّسُولَ وَإِيَّاكُمْ ۙ أَن تُؤْمِنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ رَبِّكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ خَرَجْتُمْ جِهَٰدًۭا فِى سَبِيلِى وَٱبْتِغَآءَ مَرْضَاتِى ۚ تُسِرُّونَ إِلَيْهِم بِٱلْمَوَدَّةِ وَأَنَا۠ أَعْلَمُ بِمَآ أَخْفَيْتُمْ وَمَآ أَعْلَنتُمْ ۚ وَمَن يَفْعَلْهُ مِنكُمْ فَقَدْ ضَلَّ سَوَآءَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ء خ ذ` B010 is taking or making something for oneself, `ع د و` B003 is enemy/enmity, and `و ل ي` B004 is loyal alliance and support. **Structural cue and role:** the prohibition joins the adoption verb directly to “my enemy and your enemy” as object and “allies” as the resulting role. The shared first- and second-person possessives make the hostility relationally established before affection is mentioned. **Abductive move:** an ally is inferred to receive usable loyalty, access, or support; that operational transfer, not the mere existence of feeling, explains why adoption is prohibited. **Reading change:** `لَا تَتَّخِذُوا ... أَوْلِيَاءَ` concerns making an adversarial party into a support-bearing relation, not a blanket ban on every benevolent disposition.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ل ق ي` B005 gives casting or throwing, `و د د` B001 affection, and `س ر ر` B001 an inward hidden matter. `ر س ل` B002, present in `ٱلرَّسُولَ`, also keeps sending and message-bearing active in the ayah. **Structural cue and role:** `تُلْقُونَ إِلَيْهِم بِٱلْمَوَدَّةِ` is later restated as `تُسِرُّونَ إِلَيْهِم بِٱلْمَوَدَّةِ`; the repeated destination and instrument frame affection as something directed and transmitted, with secrecy specifying the channel. **Abductive move:** the ayah does not state what the covert transfer accomplishes, so the inferred causal link is that secretly cast affection sustains a protected line of contact. **Reading change:** affection functions here as relational traffic, not only as an interior emotion.

3. **Lexical evidence:** the two forms from `خ ر ج` activate B002, causing another to emerge, in `يُخْرِجُونَ`, and B001, one’s own departure, in `خَرَجْتُمْ`; `ج ه د` B001 is full capacity under strain, `س ب ل` B001 a route or means, `ض ل ل` B001 deviation from an aim, and `س و ي` B006/B008 a fair middle and intended direction. **Structural cue and role:** forced expulsion of messenger and believers is set against their voluntary departure in strenuous pursuit, and the ayah closes by diagnosing the covert act as loss of the route’s center or aim. **Abductive move:** the secret alliance is inferred to reroute the effort outwardly declared for God’s path. **Reading change:** “straying from the level of the way” is a directional failure inside professed striving, not an unrelated verdict appended to it.

4. **Lexical evidence:** `س ر ر` B001 marks the inward secret, `خ ف ي` B001 concealment, `ع ل ن` B001 open manifestation, and `ع ل م` B001 knowing. **Structural cue and role:** secret action, hidden content, and declared content form a widening sequence under the encompassing first-person `أَعْلَمُ`. The knowledge clause acts as an evidentiary frame around both concealed and public selves. **Abductive move:** because no private channel lies outside this paired hidden/open totality, secrecy is inferred to alter human visibility but not accountability. **Reading change:** the warning is not simply that a secret may be discovered; it denies secrecy the power to create a separate moral domain.

### Retrospective surprises

In 60:4, `وَبَدَا بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَكُمُ` (`ب د و` B001, visible emergence; `ب ي ن` B001, separation) provides the unexpected inverse of 60:1’s concealed relation: Abraham’s group makes its alignment publicly legible. Then 60:5’s `فِتْنَةً` (`ف ت ن` B001, assay) suggests that hidden allegiance is eventually tested by what it makes visible. These later cues strengthen the reading of secrecy as a failed attempt to partition accountability.

## 60:2: إِن يَثْقَفُوكُمْ يَكُونُوا۟ لَكُمْ أَعْدَآءًۭ وَيَبْسُطُوٓا۟ إِلَيْكُمْ أَيْدِيَهُمْ وَأَلْسِنَتَهُم بِٱلسُّوٓءِ وَوَدُّوا۟ لَوْ تَكْفُرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ث ق ف` B003 is catching or overcoming, `ب س ط` B004 the extension of a hand, `ي د ي` B001 the bodily hand with B002/B005 available as strength and authority, `ل س ن` B001 the tongue with B002 verbal attack, and `س و ء` B002 harm or distress. **Structural cue and role:** the conditional capture clause releases two coordinated objects, hands and tongues, under one harmful instrument phrase. Hands occupy the coercive channel; tongues occupy the communicative one. **Abductive move:** capture is inferred to remove the restraint that previously kept both capacities latent. **Reading change:** the conditional does more than predict hostility; it models hostility as coordinated physical and discursive power that becomes executable upon mastery.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `و د د` B002 is wishing in `وَوَدُّوا`, while the same root’s B001 is affection in 60:1 and again in 60:7. **Structural cue and role:** the window places three distinct complements around one root: believers cast affection toward adversaries (60:1), adversaries wish for believers’ rejection (60:2), and God may place affection between the parties (60:7). In the fixed ayah, wishing supplies the adversary’s desired endpoint. **Abductive move:** because one root can carry both affect and volition, hostile wishing is inferred to be the directional interior that drives the outward hand/tongue pair. **Reading change:** `وَدُّوا` is not incidental malice; it names the outcome toward which the preceding coercion is organized, while the later use prevents treating that relation as immutable.

3. **Lexical evidence:** `ك ف ر` B003 is covering or rejecting truth; B007, forcing an obedient person toward disobedience, is contextually activated by the hostile agents’ wish, though the fixed second-person verb itself retains B003. **Structural cue and role:** `لَوْ تَكْفُرُونَ` closes the ayah as the object of desire after capture, harmful hands, and harmful tongues. It therefore supplies the intended result of both pressure channels. **Abductive move:** the unstated arrow is that bodily and verbal harm are means for producing the wished-for rejection. **Reading change:** the ayah depicts aggression as conversion pressure aimed at changing allegiance, rather than harm with no ideological endpoint.

### Retrospective surprises

The surprise arrives in 60:7: `مَوَدَّةً` returns with `و د د` B001, but now as a relation God may place between those who were enemies. That later state change (`ج ع ل` B002) retroactively limits 60:2’s hostile wish to a present causal orientation; it is serious enough to explain the coercion, but not an essence that forecloses transformed relations.

## 60:3: لَن تَنفَعَكُمْ أَرْحَامُكُمْ وَلَآ أَوْلَٰدُكُمْ ۚ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ يَفْصِلُ بَيْنَكُمْ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ن ف ع` B001 is benefit opposed to harm, `ر ح م` B002 kinship, and `و ل د` B001 offspring. **Structural cue and role:** kin relations and descendants are coordinated as subjects under an emphatic future negation of benefit. Their role is not denied as relations; their capacity to produce the needed future benefit is denied. **Abductive move:** the unstated premise is that kin attachment had been functioning as a reason for compromised allegiance in the preceding ayat. **Reading change:** the sentence does not condemn family care; it strips lineage of salvific instrumentality, blocking the inference that preserving a tie now can secure the decisive outcome later.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ق و م` B013 is resurrection, `ف ص ل` B002 adjudication that separates truth with B001 physical separation behind it, and `ب ي ن` B001 the separating interval while B003 is a connecting relation. **Structural cue and role:** `يَفْصِلُ بَيْنَكُمْ` places an adjudicative verb over the very interval that normally relates the parties. The resurrection-day phrase supplies the forum; separation supplies the judgment’s relational effect. **Abductive move:** judgment is inferred to disclose which ties can persist because it acts upon the “between,” not merely upon isolated persons. **Reading change:** the future scene is not only dispersal of relatives; it is an adjudication of relationship itself.

3. **Lexical evidence:** `ع م ل` B001 is intentional work, and `ب ص ر` B002 is discerning inward sight or insight. **Structural cue and role:** the final circumstantial object `بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ` replaces the preceding nouns of lineage with performed conduct as the object of divine discernment. **Abductive move:** if judgment separates relations while God sees deeds, conduct is inferred to be the evidence on which lineage is prevented from deciding the case. **Reading change:** the ayah’s center of gravity moves from inherited attachment to accountable action.

### Retrospective surprises

The same `بَيْنَكُمْ` interval is later capable of receiving `مَوَدَّةً` in 60:7 (`ب ي ن` B003; `و د د` B001), so separation in 60:3 is not the only imaginable state of a relation. More sharply, 60:8 distinguishes people by whether they fought and expelled and then authorizes `بِرّ` and `قِسْط` (`ب ر ر` B002/B003; `ق س ط` B001). Those later conduct criteria confirm that the fixed ayah demotes lineage in favor of action rather than abolishing social bonds.

## 60:4: قَدْ كَانَتْ لَكُمْ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌۭ فِىٓ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥٓ إِذْ قَالُوا۟ لِقَوْمِهِمْ إِنَّا بُرَءَٰٓؤُا۟ مِنكُمْ وَمِمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ كَفَرْنَا بِكُمْ وَبَدَا بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَكُمُ ٱلْعَدَٰوَةُ وَٱلْبَغْضَآءُ أَبَدًا حَتَّىٰ تُؤْمِنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ وَحْدَهُۥٓ إِلَّا قَوْلَ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ لِأَبِيهِ لَأَسْتَغْفِرَنَّ لَكَ وَمَآ أَمْلِكُ لَكَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مِن شَىْءٍۢ ۖ رَّبَّنَا عَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ أَنَبْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ ٱلْمَصِيرُ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** the fixed `أُسْوَةٌ` selects `ء س و` B003, exemplar; the root’s B001 healing a wound and B002 reconciliation or shared support become contextual echoes when 60:7 anticipates affection and 60:8 permits beneficence. `ح س ن` B001 supplies what is good, with B002 beneficent action reinforcing the echo. **Structural cue and role:** “a good exemplar in Abraham and those with him” introduces quoted boundary speech, but the later window refuses to freeze every outsider into one category. The exemplar’s role is therefore procedural: it models how allegiance is made clear. **Abductive move:** a declared boundary is inferred to stop an injurious confusion of loyalties and thereby make future repair possible. **Reading change:** the exemplar is not reducible to permanent hostility; it is a wound-limiting act whose surface severity can serve eventual relational repair.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ب ر ء` B002 is clearance or separation, `ك ف ر` B005 disavowal, `ب د و` B001 visible emergence with B004 open hostility contextually active, `ب ي ن` B001 separation, `ع د و` B003 enmity, and `ب غ ض` B001 aversion. **Structural cue and role:** first-person declarations move from clearance from the people and their worship, to disavowal, to something becoming manifest “between us and you.” This staged progression turns an internal distinction into a public relational state. **Abductive move:** because covert ambiguity can sustain competing alliances, explicit speech is inferred to make the boundary socially operative. **Reading change:** the enmity is presented as the visible consequence of declared allegiance, not as an unexplained private hatred.

3. **Lexical evidence:** `ء ب د` B001 supplies enduring duration, while the particle `حَتَّىٰ` grammatically gives that duration an endpoint condition; `ء م ن` B002 is trusting assent and `و ح د` B004 divine oneness. **Structural cue and role:** `أَبَدًا` intensifies the declared state, but `حَتَّىٰ تُؤْمِنُوا` immediately specifies the condition under which it ceases. **Abductive move:** the relation is inferred to remain stable only while the allegiance-producing cause remains. **Reading change:** “forever” functions as uncompromising persistence under a condition, not as an ontological claim that reconciliation can never occur.

4. **Lexical evidence:** `ء ب و` B001 carries fatherhood and nurture, `غ ف ر` B002 protective forgiveness, and `م ل ك` B002 ownership or power of disposal. For `ش ي ء` B001, only the packet’s “knowable thing” variant fits the fixed noun; its volitional variant is not activated. **Structural cue and role:** Abraham promises to seek forgiveness for his father, then immediately denies possessing any thing or power for him against God. The exception marks a care-bearing petition while the denial limits its efficacy. **Abductive move:** parental care is inferred to license intercessionary speech without transferring control over the outcome. **Reading change:** the exception neither erases the boundary nor criminalizes care; it distinguishes petition from possession of divine disposal.

5. **Lexical evidence:** `و ك ل` B002 is reliance, `ن و ب` B001 recurring return, and `ص ي ر` B001 becoming toward an endpoint. **Structural cue and role:** the three parallel `إِلَيْكَ/عَلَيْكَ` phrases redirect dependence, return, and final destination toward God immediately after the speech of separation. They supply the positive orientation that replaces the renounced relation. **Abductive move:** boundary-making is inferred to require an alternative support, path of return, and horizon; otherwise separation would leave a motivational vacuum. **Reading change:** the model is fundamentally reorientation, with disavowal as its relational consequence.

### Retrospective surprises

The strongest correction is 60:7, where God may place affection between the very parties described by enmity: `ج ع ل` B002 changes the relational state, and `و د د` B001 fills the `بَيْن` with affection. This confirms that `أَبَدًا حَتَّىٰ` is condition-bound. Verses 60:8–9 then divide outsiders by fighting, expulsion, and support for expulsion; their contrast shows that the exemplary declaration cannot be mechanically extended to people whose conduct differs.

## 60:5: رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةًۭ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ وَٱغْفِرْ لَنَا رَبَّنَآ ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ج ع ل` B002 is turning something into a state, `ف ت ن` B001 assaying or testing and B004 ordeal, and `ك ف ر` B003 rejection of truth. **Structural cue and role:** the prayer does not merely ask relief “from” a trial; it asks not to be made a `فِتْنَة` “for” those who reject. The believers themselves occupy the possible instrument role in another group’s testing. **Abductive move:** the unstated causal possibility is that believers’ defeat, failure, or compromised conduct could be read as evidence that reinforces rejection. **Reading change:** the petition asks not only for protection from persecution but also not to become a misleading test through which others are diverted.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `غ ف ر` B002 is forgiveness as shielding from consequence, `ع ز ز` B001/B002 might and overcoming power, and `ح ك م` B001 restraint that sets right together with B003 wisdom. **Structural cue and role:** the plea for forgiveness is justified by two names that combine power with corrective restraint. Might supplies capacity; wisdom supplies governed application. **Abductive move:** forgiveness is inferred to be effective repair precisely because the forgiver has power to enforce consequences and wisdom to restrain or redirect them. **Reading change:** pardon is not placed opposite strength; it is an exercise of strong, ordered governance.

3. **Lexical evidence:** `ر ب ب` B002 is nurturing, repairing, and bringing toward completion. **Structural cue and role:** `رَبَّنَا` encloses the two petitions, appearing before the request not to become a test and again after the request for forgiveness. The repeated address casts both prevention and pardon as stages of nurture. **Abductive move:** a community capable of becoming a harmful test is inferred to need both preservation before failure and repair after it. **Reading change:** the prayer describes formation under care, not a demand for exemption from all exposure.

### Retrospective surprises

Verse 60:10 later joins `فَٱمْتَحِنُوهُنَّ` (`م ح ن` B001, a test revealing the inner) to `حُكْمُ ٱللَّهِ` (`ح ك م` B002, adjudication). That procedural test clarifies the fixed prayer’s danger: testing itself is not rejected, but becoming a misleading or destructive test is. The later legal use also makes `ٱلْحَكِيمُ` in 60:5 read as disciplined judgment rather than abstract sagacity alone.

## 60:6: لَقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِيهِمْ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌۭ لِّمَن كَانَ يَرْجُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلْيَوْمَ ٱلْءَاخِرَ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلْغَنِىُّ ٱلْحَمِيدُ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ء س و` B003 is an exemplar, while B001 wound-healing and B002 reconciliation remain latent root echoes; `ح س ن` B001 marks the exemplar as good. **Structural cue and role:** the exemplar formula from 60:4 is repeated, but now a `لِمَن` phrase identifies the person for whom it functions. The model is not a free-standing spectacle; it has an orienting beneficiary. **Abductive move:** because 60:7–8 soon allow affection, kindness, and equity, the root’s healing/reconciliation echoes are inferred to belong to what makes this boundary practice “good.” **Reading change:** the repeated model is transferable discernment aimed at repair, not imitation of hostility as a permanent posture.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ر ج و` B001 is hope, `ي و م` B003 can mark a decisive occasion, and `ء خ ر` B001 is the later or final term. **Structural cue and role:** hoping for God and the Last Day stands as the condition under which the exemplar becomes usable. Hope supplies forward orientation, and the final occasion supplies the horizon against which present alliances are measured. **Abductive move:** one can bear the immediate cost of clarified allegiance only if a later good is inferred to outweigh present relational advantage. **Reading change:** hope is not passive optimism here; it is the causal capacity that makes the exemplar practicable.

3. **Lexical evidence:** `و ل ي` B007 is turning away in `يَتَوَلَّ`, while the nearby B004 alliance sense from 60:1 remains a contrastive echo; `غ ن ي` B001 is self-sufficiency, and `ح م د` B003 praiseworthy character. B002, finding something satisfactory after testing, is additionally activated by the testing procedure in 60:10. **Structural cue and role:** the one who turns away is set against God’s emphatic self-sufficiency and praiseworthiness. The refusal therefore cannot diminish the model’s source. **Abductive move:** withdrawal is inferred to deprive the refuser, not God, because the offered orientation answers a human need that divine sufficiency does not share. **Reading change:** `يَتَوَلَّ` is best read as refusing the exemplar and its horizon, with the alliance sense supplying an intentional echo rather than controlling the syntax.

### Retrospective surprises

In 60:7 the hoped-for future takes concrete relational form: God may make affection after enmity (`ج ع ل` B002; `و د د` B001). In 60:10, entrants are examined (`م ح ن` B001) and a rights-preserving judgment is applied (`ح ك م` B002). Together these later ayat show that hope does not bypass evidence or procedure; it sustains a model capable of moving from boundary to tested, equitable reintegration.

## 60:7: ۞ عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَجْعَلَ بَيْنَكُمْ وَبَيْنَ ٱلَّذِينَ عَادَيْتُم مِّنْهُم مَّوَدَّةًۭ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ قَدِيرٌۭ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٌۭ رَّحِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ج ع ل` B002 is transformation into a state, `ب ي ن` B001 is a separating interval while B003 is a connecting tie, `ع د و` B003 is enmity, and `و د د` B001 is affection. **Structural cue and role:** the construction places affection as the new object inside the doubled relational interval, `بَيْنَكُمْ وَبَيْنَ`, whose parties are identified through prior enmity. The fixed ayah therefore changes what occupies the “between” without erasing who stood on either side. **Abductive move:** the unstated mechanism is that God can reconfigure the relation itself even before either party’s identity is described as changing. **Reading change:** reconciliation is a structural conversion of the shared interval from enmity to affection, not merely warmer private feelings alongside an unchanged relation.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ق د ر` B003 is effective capacity and B005 deliberate ordering, `غ ف ر` B002 protective forgiveness, and `ر ح م` B001 tender mercy. **Structural cue and role:** three divine predicates follow the possibility of changed affection: capacity, forgiveness, and mercy. Capacity establishes feasibility; forgiveness can cover the consequences of the hostile past; mercy supplies the positive mode of the new relation. **Abductive move:** the ayah does not explicitly state how enmity becomes affection, so a sequence from capacity through release from past liability to renewed tenderness is inferred from the predicate order. **Reading change:** the closing names are not generic consolation; they supply a compact causal grammar for relational change.

3. **Lexical evidence:** the fixed `مَوَدَّة` retains `و د د` B001 affection, while `ب ر ر` B002/B003 in 60:8 gives expansive and kin-like goodness, `ق س ط` B001 gives equity, and `ح ك م` B002 in 60:10 gives adjudication. **Structural cue and role:** the ayat after the fixed one immediately divide conduct, authorize good and equity toward noncombatants, prohibit alliance with active aggressors, and settle marital and financial claims. These procedures occupy the path between possible affection and durable social relation. **Abductive move:** affection is inferred to require conduct-sensitive institutions if it is to survive unresolved harm and competing rights. **Reading change:** the hope of 60:7 is neither sentimentality nor indiscriminate reunion; it anticipates a legally and ethically mediated reconciliation.

### Retrospective surprises

Verses 60:8–9 make the possible affection unexpectedly discriminating: absence or presence of fighting, expulsion, and support for expulsion determines the permitted relation. Verses 60:10–11 then preserve claims through examination, release of incompatible bonds, and equivalent reimbursement. The later material turns 60:7’s state change into a sequence with prerequisites: classify conduct, protect rights, and only then let affection become socially sustainable.

## 60:8: لَّا يَنْهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمْ يُقَٰتِلُوكُمْ فِى ٱلدِّينِ وَلَمْ يُخْرِجُوكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمْ أَن تَبَرُّوهُمْ وَتُقْسِطُوٓا۟ إِلَيْهِمْ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلْمُقْسِطِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ن ه ي` B001 is restraint from an act, `ق ت ل` B011 reciprocal combat, `د ي ن` B001 religious obedience, `خ ر ج` B002 causing removal, and `د و ر` B007 attachment to a residence. **Structural cue and role:** a negated prohibition is governed by two negated conduct clauses: these people neither fought the addressees over religion nor removed them from their homes. Those absences define the class before any response is named. **Abductive move:** because the permission is keyed to observable conduct, nonparticipation in coercion is inferred to preserve the possibility of ordinary moral relation despite group difference. **Reading change:** the ayah does not classify every outsider by identity; it classifies the permitted relationship by what the other party has and has not done.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ب ر ر` B002 is expansive righteousness and B003 kin-like dutiful kindness; `ق س ط` B001 is justice or equity, with B003 an allotted due share. **Structural cue and role:** after merely saying God does not forbid, the ayah supplies two positive infinitival objects, `أَن تَبَرُّوهُمْ وَتُقْسِطُوا إِلَيْهِمْ`. `بِرّ` contributes generous good; `قِسْط` contributes measured fairness directed toward them. **Abductive move:** the move from nonprohibition to two positive acts implies that peaceful difference creates room for more than abstention from harm. **Reading change:** the minimum is not cold neutrality; the relation can combine beneficent generosity with exact regard for another’s due.

3. **Lexical evidence:** `ح ب ب` B002 is love, and `ق س ط` B001 returns in the agent noun `ٱلْمُقْسِطِينَ`. **Structural cue and role:** the closing causal clause shifts attention from the recipients of equity to God’s love for those who enact it. Equity is thus both an outward allocation and the conduct that generates a vertical relation of divine love. **Abductive move:** fair dealing with noncombatants is inferred to be intrinsically valued, not merely tolerated as a temporary tactic. **Reading change:** `قِسْط` becomes a relational bridge: it preserves justice across difference and locates the just actor within divine affection, extending 60:7’s `مَوَدَّة` without equating the two loves.

### Retrospective surprises

Verses 60:10–11 make the due-share branch of `ق س ط` B003 newly concrete. Expenditures are returned, reciprocal claims are heard, and a lost payment is replaced with `مِثْلَ` (`م ث ل` B001, equivalence). This later accounting shows that the equity of 60:8 is not only evenhanded demeanor; it can require precise material restoration across a ruptured boundary.

## 60:9: إِنَّمَا يَنْهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ قَٰتَلُوكُمْ فِى ٱلدِّينِ وَأَخْرَجُوكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمْ وَظَٰهَرُوا۟ عَلَىٰٓ إِخْرَاجِكُمْ أَن تَوَلَّوْهُمْ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّهُمْ فَأُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ن ه ي` B001 is restraint, `ق ت ل` B011 mutual combat, `خ ر ج` B002 caused removal, and `ظ ه ر` B006 support from the back together with B007 prevailing. **Structural cue and role:** `إِنَّمَا` restricts the prohibition to a three-part conduct chain: direct fighting, direct expulsion, and support for expulsion. The third clause extends responsibility beyond the actor who physically removes people to the coalition that enables the act. **Abductive move:** backing is inferred to increase the capacity and success of displacement even when the supporter does not execute it personally. **Reading change:** the prohibited class is defined by an aggression system, not just by the most visible aggressor.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `و ل ي` B004 is loyal alliance and support; its repetition as `تَوَلَّوْهُمْ` and `يَتَوَلَّهُمْ` is reinforced by `ظ ه ر` B006, backing or support, in the hostile conduct list. **Structural cue and role:** the same prohibited relation is first stated as an object of divine restraint and then made the condition of the closing verdict. Its lexical overlap with the attackers’ own support exposes a mirrored action. **Abductive move:** to ally with the supporting coalition is inferred to add one’s support to the very network that enabled expulsion. **Reading change:** `تَوَلِّي` here is operational alignment with an aggressive coalition, not ordinary contact, courtesy, or the `بِرّ` authorized in 60:8.

3. **Lexical evidence:** the accepted inventory for `ظ ل م` does not include a generic branch labeled “injustice”; B008 specifically gives withholding rights, while B004 gives concrete misplacement or mistiming. **Structural cue and role:** `ٱلظَّالِمُونَ` is the predicate reserved for those who choose the prohibited alliance after the conduct chain, and the following ayat repeatedly restore residence, marital, and financial claims. **Abductive move:** alliance with expellers is inferred both to place support on the wrong side of the relation and to withhold the displaced party’s due protection. **Reading change:** the verdict becomes a diagnosis of rights misallocation, not an unanalysed moral label.

### Retrospective surprises

The settlements in 60:10–11 retrospectively expose the rights hidden inside the expulsion language: returning migrants, dissolving or recognizing marital bonds, and reimbursing expenditures all answer losses produced by changed residence and allegiance. These later procedures make `ظ ل م` B008 especially salient in 60:9: supporting expulsion participates in a system that withholds concrete dues, while just judgment restores them.

## 60:10: يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ إِذَا جَآءَكُمُ ٱلْمُؤْمِنَٰتُ مُهَٰجِرَٰتٍۢ فَٱمْتَحِنُوهُنَّ ۖ ٱللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ بِإِيمَٰنِهِنَّ ۖ فَإِنْ عَلِمْتُمُوهُنَّ مُؤْمِنَٰتٍۢ فَلَا تَرْجِعُوهُنَّ إِلَى ٱلْكُفَّارِ ۖ لَا هُنَّ حِلٌّۭ لَّهُمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحِلُّونَ لَهُنَّ ۖ وَءَاتُوهُم مَّآ أَنفَقُوا۟ ۚ وَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيْكُمْ أَن تَنكِحُوهُنَّ إِذَآ ءَاتَيْتُمُوهُنَّ أُجُورَهُنَّ ۚ وَلَا تُمْسِكُوا۟ بِعِصَمِ ٱلْكَوَافِرِ وَسْـَٔلُوا۟ مَآ أَنفَقْتُمْ وَلْيَسْـَٔلُوا۟ مَآ أَنفَقُوا۟ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ حُكْمُ ٱللَّهِ ۖ يَحْكُمُ بَيْنَكُمْ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `م ح ن` B001 is a test that reveals the inner, `ع ل م` B001 is knowing, and `ء م ن` B001/B002 holds settled trust and trusting assent. **Structural cue and role:** the imperative to examine is enclosed by two unequal knowledge clauses: God knows their faith best, while the community may come to know them as believers for purposes of the conditional ruling. Human examination supplies an actionable classification; divine knowledge retains the inaccessible interior. **Abductive move:** communal law is inferred to require sufficient evidence without claiming exhaustive access to hearts. **Reading change:** `فَٱمْتَحِنُوهُنَّ` is a bounded administrative inquiry, not authorization to equate a human test with God’s knowledge.

2. **Lexical evidence:** the arrival form from `ج ي ء` selects the packet’s B001 “coming from one place” variant rather than B001’s alternate repeated-prevailing sense; `ه ج ر` B002 is migration from one abode to another, and `ر ج ع` B001 is return or sending back, with B004 marital return also activated. **Structural cue and role:** arrival as emigrants leads through examination to `فَلَا تَرْجِعُوهُنَّ`, so recognized faith blocks both geographic return to the rejecting group and restoration of the former marital arrangement. **Abductive move:** verified migration is inferred to alter legal affiliation because return would place the migrant back under the relation she left. **Reading change:** movement between abodes becomes a jurisdictional transition rather than a merely spatial journey.

3. **Lexical evidence:** `ح ل ل` B001 is untying, B003 removal of prohibition, and B006 spousal co-dwelling; `م س ك` B001 is holding, `ع ص م` B001 a protective hold and B002 a strap or holdfast, and `ن ك ح` B002 a marriage contract. **Structural cue and role:** reciprocal nonlawfulness is followed by a prohibition on holding the `عِصَم` of rejecting wives and permission for a new marriage after its due is paid. The ayah alternates release and reattachment. **Abductive move:** marriage is inferred to operate as a protective legal bond whose hold can cease under one affiliation and be legitimately reconstituted under another. **Reading change:** the ruling neither treats the prior bond as unreal nor allows it to be held indefinitely; it manages a binding relation through lawful untying and rebinding.

4. **Lexical evidence:** `ن ف ق` B002 is outgoing wealth, `ء ت ي` B002 giving, `ء ج ر` B001 recompense, wage, or dowry, `س ء ل` B001/B004 request and reciprocal request, and `ح ك م` B002 adjudication with B001 corrective restraint. **Structural cue and role:** expenditures recur on both sides; giving and asking are reciprocally distributed; only after these claims are stated does the text name the arrangement God’s judgment and say He judges `بَيْنَكُمْ`. The financial clauses keep value from disappearing when the marriage relation ends. **Abductive move:** reciprocal reimbursement is inferred to prevent a change of allegiance from becoming unauthorized enrichment or collective punishment. **Reading change:** the extended accounting is central to the ayah’s mechanism: divine judgment governs the interval between communities by preserving claims across rupture.

### Retrospective surprises

Verse 60:11 compresses the long reciprocal accounting into `مِثْلَ مَا أَنفَقُوا` (`م ث ل` B001; `ن ف ق` B002), confirming equivalence as the repair principle when ordinary recovery fails. Verse 60:12 then addresses arriving believing women as covenanting agents whose conduct is enumerated. Whether or not every individual is identical to those in 60:10, that later pledge corrects any reading in which women are merely examined or transferred objects: the procedure culminates in an accountable compact they themselves offer.

## 60:11: وَإِن فَاتَكُمْ شَىْءٌۭ مِّنْ أَزْوَٰجِكُمْ إِلَى ٱلْكُفَّارِ فَعَاقَبْتُمْ فَـَٔاتُوا۟ ٱلَّذِينَ ذَهَبَتْ أَزْوَٰجُهُم مِّثْلَ مَآ أَنفَقُوا۟ ۚ وَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنتُم بِهِۦ مُؤْمِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ف و ت` B001 is passing beyond reach, `ش ي ء` B001 is a knowable entity, `ز و ج` B002 is spouse, and `ذ ه ب` B006 departure. For `ش ي ء`, only the packet’s entity variant fits this fixed nominal form; the branch’s volitional variant is not used. **Structural cue and role:** the indefinite `شَيْءٌ مِنْ أَزْوَاجِكُمْ` enters a conditional loss construction and is later answered by a measurable payment. The wording makes an unrecovered marital case legally cognizable without claiming that a spouse’s personhood is exhausted by the compensable loss. **Abductive move:** adjudication is inferred to need a claim-unit that can be identified after the person has passed beyond the claimant’s reach. **Reading change:** the partitive expression converts rupture into a remediable case, not a metaphysical reduction of women to property.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ع ق ب` B007 is punitive or compensating consequence and explicitly includes the fixed `فَعَاقَبْتُمْ`; B010 is substitution and liability. `ء ت ي` B002 is giving. **Structural cue and role:** the conditional sequence runs from an unrecovered loss, through `فَعَاقَبْتُمْ`, to an imperative to give the affected husbands. The object of the command is restoration, not retaliation against the departed spouse. **Abductive move:** a later recovery or compensatory turn is inferred to create the pool or occasion from which the earlier uncompensated loss can be repaired. **Reading change:** the `ع ق ب` form is governed by substitution and liability: it is an aftermath mechanism for compensation, not a license for vengeance.

3. **Lexical evidence:** `م ث ل` B001 is likeness or equivalence, `ن ف ق` B002 outgoing wealth, `و ق ي` B002 placing oneself in protective moral caution, and `ء م ن` B001/B002 trust and assenting faith. **Structural cue and role:** `مِثْلَ مَا أَنفَقُوا` fixes the amount of restoration, and the closing command to guard oneself before the God in whom the addressees believe attaches moral restraint to that calculation. Equivalence limits both underpayment and opportunistic excess. **Abductive move:** precise replacement is inferred to protect a vulnerable compensation system from being turned into profit or retaliation. **Reading change:** faith appears as disciplined fidelity to measured redress, not only as the identity that separated the spouses.

### Retrospective surprises

Verse 60:12’s direct pledge with the women (`ب ي ع` B003) is the strongest retrospective correction. Its list makes them responsible moral agents and ends with forgiveness and mercy. The later agency and repair frame show why 60:11 compensates a financial loss without assigning ownership over the departing person: the recoverable due and the covenanting agent belong to different legal categories.

## 60:12: يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِىُّ إِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلْمُؤْمِنَٰتُ يُبَايِعْنَكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن لَّا يُشْرِكْنَ بِٱللَّهِ شَيْـًۭٔا وَلَا يَسْرِقْنَ وَلَا يَزْنِينَ وَلَا يَقْتُلْنَ أَوْلَٰدَهُنَّ وَلَا يَأْتِينَ بِبُهْتَٰنٍۢ يَفْتَرِينَهُۥ بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِنَّ وَأَرْجُلِهِنَّ وَلَا يَعْصِينَكَ فِى مَعْرُوفٍۢ ۙ فَبَايِعْهُنَّ وَٱسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُنَّ ٱللَّهَ ۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٌۭ رَّحِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `ن ب ء` B003 is prophethood as divine informing, and `ب ي ع` B003 is a pledge of obedience and allegiance; B001, exchange through sale and purchase, remains an operative compact metaphor. **Structural cue and role:** the women initiate `يُبَايِعْنَكَ`, the prophet is then commanded `فَبَايِعْهُنَّ`, and the compact is specified by explicit clauses. The mirrored verbs make acceptance reciprocal even though the obligations are enumerated for the entrants. **Abductive move:** allegiance is inferred to require both an offered commitment and authoritative reception rather than unilateral absorption into a group. **Reading change:** the pledge is a mutual compact mediated by prophetic informing, not a one-sided declaration of control.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ش ر ك` B002 is associating a partner with God, `ز ن ي` B001 unlawful intercourse, `ق ت ل` B001 killing, `و ل د` B001 offspring, `ب ه ت` B002 confounding false accusation, and `ف ر ي` B003 fabrication of a lie. **Structural cue and role:** the negative series moves from divine allegiance through property, sexual conduct, protection of offspring, truth about persons, and bounded obedience. Coming after 60:10’s admission based on tested faith, the list makes affiliation publicly accountable through conduct. **Abductive move:** because inward faith is fully known only to God, observable covenantal commitments are inferred to provide the social trust needed for incorporation. **Reading change:** the pledge is not a generic profession; it translates belief into a specified architecture protecting worship, property, bodies, children, reputation, and legitimate order.

3. **Lexical evidence:** `ب ه ت` B002 is an accusation that stuns or confounds, `ف ر ي` B003 is fabricated falsehood, `ب ي ن` B002 is the interval, `ي د ي` B001 the hands with B008 “before the hands,” and `ر ج ل` B001 the bodily feet or legs. **Structural cue and role:** the falsehood is both brought and fabricated, then located between the women’s own hands and feet. The doubled bodily frame attaches the manufactured claim to the agent’s embodied sphere of action. **Abductive move:** the lie is inferred to simulate evidence or lineage by constructing something and positioning it as though it arose from one’s own bodily reality. **Reading change:** `بُهْتَان` is not casual inaccurate speech; it is a deliberately manufactured claim capable of confounding social and familial attribution.

4. **Lexical evidence:** `ع ص ي` B001 is leaving obedience, while `ع ر ف` B005 is recognized good approved by reason, norm, or law. `غ ف ر` B002 is protective forgiveness and `ر ح م` B001 tender mercy. **Structural cue and role:** obedience to the prophet is explicitly restricted by `فِي مَعْرُوفٍ`; after the pledge is accepted, the prophet is told to seek forgiveness, and the ayah closes with forgiveness and mercy. Recognized good limits authority, while pardon supplies a repair path within the covenant. **Abductive move:** a durable pledge is inferred to need both a public criterion constraining commands and a means of restoration after human failure. **Reading change:** allegiance is bounded rather than absolute and reparative rather than perfectionist.

### Retrospective surprises

Verse 60:13 immediately contrasts this future-facing covenant with repeated despair (`ي ء س` B001). That contrast makes the forgiveness and mercy closing 60:12 structurally decisive: the pledge admits accountable agents into a relation where failure can be repaired, whereas the prohibited final affiliation is marked by a severed expectation of any final good.

## 60:13: يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ لَا تَتَوَلَّوْا۟ قَوْمًا غَضِبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيْهِمْ قَدْ يَئِسُوا۟ مِنَ ٱلْءَاخِرَةِ كَمَا يَئِسَ ٱلْكُفَّارُ مِنْ أَصْحَٰبِ ٱلْقُبُورِ

### Activated readings

1. **Lexical evidence:** `و ل ي` B004 is loyal alliance and support, `ق و م` B001 a social group, and `غ ض ب` B001 intensified displeasure rising toward consequence, including the packet’s divine-wrath sense. **Structural cue and role:** the prohibition takes a group as its direct object and qualifies that group first by divine displeasure and then by despair. In the 60:8–12 window, relations were classified by conduct, tested allegiance, restored rights, and bounded pledge; this final `تَوَلِّي` therefore occupies the same operational alliance register. **Abductive move:** joining the group is inferred to place one’s support inside a collective orientation already opposed to divine judgment. **Reading change:** the closing ban concerns adopting a consequential group alignment, not indiscriminate refusal of social contact.

2. **Lexical evidence:** `ي ء س` B001 is loss of hope and appears twice, `ء خ ر` B001 is the later or final horizon, and `ك ف ر` B003 is rejection of truth. **Structural cue and role:** the first despair has the Hereafter as its object; the second supplies an analogy involving rejecters and grave-companions. Repetition makes severed expectation, rather than wrath alone, the group’s defining interior orientation. **Abductive move:** when no final correction or good is expected, present allegiance is inferred to close around immediate power and become resistant to the accountability developed in 60:8–12. **Reading change:** despair is a causal social danger: it removes the future horizon that makes equity, repayment, and pledge intelligible.

3. **Lexical evidence:** `ص ح ب` B001 is sustained companionship, `ق ب ر` B001 burial and the grave as resting enclosure, and B002 hiddenness or inward sinking. **Structural cue and role:** the analogy does not mention death abstractly; it constructs `أَصْحَابِ ٱلْقُبُورِ`, a companionship fixed to graves. That terminal association supplies the image against which the second despair is measured. **Abductive move:** the group’s hopeless social horizon is inferred to resemble an association whose members are enclosed, hidden, and expected to yield no further relational future. **Reading change:** the grave image diagnoses a closed world of companionship rather than serving only as a reminder of mortality.

4. **Lexical evidence:** `غ ض ب` B001 is intensified displeasure oriented toward consequence, while `ح ب ب` B002 marks divine love in 60:8 and `غ ف ر` B002 with `ر ح م` B001 closes the pledge in 60:12. **Structural cue and role:** within the fixed window, the final wrath-and-despair pair follows two openings for changed relation: equitable conduct that God loves and a reparative pledge closed by forgiveness and mercy. The ending therefore sets two relational trajectories against each other, one open to pardon and one sealed by despair. **Abductive move:** despair is inferred to perpetuate exposure to consequence because it refuses the future in which repentance, repair, or changed affiliation could occur. **Reading change:** divine wrath is not presented as arbitrary group stigma; in this sequence it is bound to a future-denying orientation that rejects the available mechanisms of repair.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah exists within the clipped focus window. On retrospective review, the surprise is therefore structural rather than supplied by a later verse: the surah’s last relational prohibition is defined not by another act of fighting or expulsion but by despair, revealing the future horizon as the hidden condition beneath the preceding practices of justice, compensation, covenant, forgiveness, and mercy.
