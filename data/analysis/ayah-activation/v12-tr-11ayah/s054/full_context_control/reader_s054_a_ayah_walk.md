# Surah 54 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Analytical Walk

## 54:0 - بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **`س م و / B005` (name as raised designation).** The opening `ٱسْم` precedes a visible sign (54:2), consequential reports (54:4), and wisdom that reaches its limit (54:5). It therefore serves as the raised designation under which those later disclosures become legible. The packet supplies the order from naming to sign/report; I infer that the name -> authorizes and gathers the disclosures. **Reading change:** the basmalah is not only a formula before the run, but the run's first act of making its source publicly identifiable.
2. **`ر ح م / B001` (mercy and tenderness).** The doubled mercy forms stand before denial, deterrence, and warnings in 54:2-5. Mercy takes the functional role of the initiating condition under which restraint is offered before consequence. The packet supplies mercy followed by warning; I infer mercy -> makes warning a beneficent chance to turn, rather than importing mercy into the later punishment itself. **Reading change:** `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` frames even the surah's deterrent speech as prior care.

### Retrospective surprises

54:4-5 supplied the later surprise: reports containing restraint and fully reaching wisdom made the opening mercy read as preventive care, while the named source became the authority under which those reports arrive.

## 54:1 - ٱقْتَرَبَتِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ وَٱنشَقَّ ٱلْقَمَرُ

### Activated readings

1. **`ق ر ب / B002` (temporal nearness and nearing completion).** `ٱقْتَرَبَت` is followed by `مُّسْتَقِرّ` in 54:3 and `بَٰلِغَة` in 54:5. Those endpoint words make the branch's "turning toward its end" do causal work: nearness is a trajectory whose affairs reach and settle. The packet supplies the lexical sequence; I infer nearing -> culmination. **Reading change:** the first clause announces an advancing terminal process, not a vague reduction in temporal distance.
2. **`ش ق ق / B004` (group splitting in conflict), alongside `B001` (splitting open).** The physical fissure is immediately followed by people turning aside, denying, and following their own inclinations (54:2-3). The moon's opened division supplies a spatial model, while the social sequence supplies a divided common cause. I infer visible fissure -> exposes an already operative communal fissure; the packet does not state that the lunar event causes the social split. **Reading change:** `ٱنشَقَّ ٱلْقَمَرُ` can retain its physical reading while also making the audience's conflict newly visible.

### Retrospective surprises

54:3 and 54:5 corrected a merely calendrical reading of nearness by adding settlement and completed reach; 54:2-3 also made the physical split disclose a social one.

## 54:2 - وَإِن يَرَوْا۟ ءَايَةًۭ يُعْرِضُوا۟ وَيَقُولُوا۟ سِحْرٌۭ مُّسْتَمِرٌّۭ

### Activated readings

1. **`س ح ر / B002` (magic as diversion and deception) with `ع ر ض / B005` (turning one's side away).** Their accusation assigns diversion to the sign, but the grammar first shows them performing the diversion by turning away. The sequence creates a reversal: packet-supplied seeing -> side-turn -> accusation; I infer that the accusation projects the observer's evasive act onto the observed sign. **Reading change:** `سِحْر` reads as a defensive relabeling that conceals who is actually diverting whom.
2. **`م ر ر / B001` (passing through or continuing onward) against `ق ر ر / B003` (settling and remaining fixed) in 54:3.** `مُّسْتَمِرّ` and `مُّسْتَقِرّ` form a near phonetic and semantic counter-pair. The first branch lets their label cast the sign as something merely passing or perpetually carried along; the next ayah asserts terminal settlement. I infer that the juxtaposition corrects their attempted deferral. **Reading change:** the dismissal "continuing magic" is heard as an effort to keep the sign from acquiring a fixed consequence.
3. **`ع ر ض / B004` (interception across the way), activated by `B005`.** A sign that has just become visible can function as an obstacle crossing their habitual course; their side-turn is then an evasive maneuver around an intercepting demand. The packet supplies sign, sight, and turning; I infer sign -> blocks prior course -> prompts lateral avoidance. **Reading change:** `يُعْرِضُوا` becomes active rerouting, not merely lack of interest.

### Retrospective surprises

54:3 supplied the decisive correction through the مستمر/مستقر counter-pair, while 54:6-7 confirmed that turning aside was evasive motion that would later be replaced by compelled motion.

## 54:3 - وَكَذَّبُوا۟ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓا۟ أَهْوَآءَهُمْ ۚ وَكُلُّ أَمْرٍۢ مُّسْتَقِرٌّۭ

### Activated readings

1. **`ه و ي / B002` (falling into an abyss) coactivated with `B004` (desire and inclination of the self).** The fixed construction explicitly gives desire the role of guide through `ٱتَّبَعُوا`. The later bodily sequence of lowering, emerging, and hurrying to a caller (54:7-8) makes direction consequential. The packet supplies following an inclination and later compelled movement; I infer self-inclination -> downward loss of control. **Reading change:** following `أَهْوَاء` is not just preferring opinions; it is choosing a vector that becomes a fall.
2. **`ق ر ر / B003` (settling and remaining fixed).** Its position after `مُّسْتَمِرّ` in 54:2 and before the warnings and summons of 54:4-8 turns settlement into the endpoint of a moving sequence. The branch's role is terminal deposition: claims may circulate, but each affair arrives at a fixed state. The packet gives movement and settlement; I infer passage -> unavoidable outcome. **Reading change:** `كُلُّ أَمْرٍ مُّسْتَقِرّ` reads dynamically, as every process finding its terminus.
3. **`ء م ر / B002` (command or directive), beside `B001` (matter or affair).** The nearby caller who issues a summons (54:6-8) activates `أَمْر` as directive, not only affair. I infer command -> enacted realization because the later crowd cannot decline the call as it declined the sign. **Reading change:** the clause can say both that every affair settles and that every effective directive reaches establishment; these readings remain distinct.

### Retrospective surprises

54:6-8 was the later surprise: the caller's directive actually organizes bodies, making أمر as command and desire as a chosen vector newly visible.

## 54:4 - وَلَقَدْ جَآءَهُم مِّنَ ٱلْأَنۢبَآءِ مَا فِيهِ مُزْدَجَرٌ

### Activated readings

1. **`ن ب ء / B002` (news brought and reported), sharpened by `B001` (coming from one place to another).** The reports grammatically "come" to the audience, and 54:9 begins the first embodied earlier community. News therefore plays the role of a transported past crossing into the present focus window. The packet supplies arrival language and the subsequent historical scene; I infer report-as-arrival -> collapses safe temporal distance. **Reading change:** `ٱلْأَنۢبَاء` are not inert information about others, but arrivals that place the hearer inside the same causal pattern.
2. **`ز ج ر / B001` (rebuking, checking, or driving by voice).** The branch is reinforced by the caller in 54:6-8 and by Noah being `ٱزْدُجِرَ` in 54:9. `مُزْدَجَر` functions as behavioral force: a report can check its audience now, while ignored checking later becomes a voice that drives bodies. The packet supplies the repeated vocal-driving root; I infer refused restraint -> eventual compelled motion. **Reading change:** the ayah offers a practical braking mechanism, not merely a frightening topic.

### Retrospective surprises

54:6-9 sharpened the vocal mechanism: the caller drives bodies, and Noah is himself driven by rebuke, so the report's restraint can reverse from voluntary checking into compulsory movement.

## 54:5 - حِكْمَةٌۢ بَٰلِغَةٌۭ ۖ فَمَا تُغْنِ ٱلنُّذُرُ

### Activated readings

1. **`ح ك م / B001` (restraining and turning back to set right), with `ب ل غ / B005` (complete excellence).** The preceding `مُزْدَجَر` (54:4) and the subsequent summons (54:6-8) give wisdom an operational role like a restraint that has reached full extension. The packet supplies deterrence, perfected reach, and later compulsion; I infer perfected restraint -> final voluntary stopping point before forced response. **Reading change:** `حِكْمَةٌ بَالِغَةٌ` is effective governance aimed at correcting motion, not an abstract maxim.
2. **`غ ن ي / B002` (sufficing, availing, and replacing).** The negative `فَمَا تُغْنِ` follows a complete warning apparatus yet precedes continued refusal. The branch makes the causal limit precise: warnings cannot stand in for the addressee's own uptake. The packet supplies adequate warning and nonresponse; I infer available restraint -/-> substituted compliance. **Reading change:** the failure belongs neither to wisdom's reach nor to warning's clarity, but to the impossibility of warning replacing a will.
3. **`ب ل غ / B001` (reaching the limit).** Nearness in 54:1, settlement in 54:3, and `بَالِغَة` here form approach -> arrival -> settled-result. I infer this directional chain from the packet's sequence. **Reading change:** the wisdom has not merely been eloquently delivered; it has reached the limit at which no missing notice explains refusal.

### Retrospective surprises

54:6-8 confirmed that complete wisdom marks the last voluntary restraint before compulsion; 54:9-10 further showed that warning's failure lay in refusal, not absent notice.

## 54:6 - فَتَوَلَّ عَنْهُمْ ۘ يَوْمَ يَدْعُ ٱلدَّاعِ إِلَىٰ شَىْءٍۢ نُّكُرٍ

### Activated readings

1. **`و ل ي / B007` (turning away and withdrawal).** The imperative answers the audience's `يُعْرِضُوا` in 54:2 with a controlled reversal of orientation. Their side-turn is answered by the messenger's withdrawal, after which a caller they cannot evade takes over. The packet supplies the two turnings and later summons; I infer voluntary refusal -> withdrawal of address -> compulsory call. **Reading change:** `فَتَوَلَّ عَنْهُم` marks a transfer of agency, not simple abandonment.
2. **`د ع و / B001` (calling to draw near by voice or speech).** Verb and agent repeat the root (`يَدْعُ ٱلدَّاعِ`), and 54:7-8 supplies the resulting emergence and hurry. The call's assigned role is a vector that pulls a dispersed population toward one destination. The packet states call then motion; I infer call -> draws and aligns that motion. **Reading change:** the caller does not merely announce an event; the call organizes bodies.
3. **`ن ك ر / B004` (change into an unrecognized or disliked state), alongside `B003` (the severe unfamiliar thing).** Graves opening in 54:7 and the sky opening in 54:11 make alteration of the known order more salient than mere novelty. The packet supplies the unfamiliar destination and nearby reversals of enclosure; I infer altered order -> produces nonrecognition. **Reading change:** `شَيْءٍ نُكُرٍ` can be the world made unrecognizable, not only an unknown object within an unchanged world.

### Retrospective surprises

54:7-8 confirmed that the doubled call organizes gaze, neck, and speed; 54:11 then made نكر describe an altered order whose sky-boundary opens.

## 54:7 - خُشَّعًا أَبْصَٰرُهُمْ يَخْرُجُونَ مِنَ ٱلْأَجْدَاثِ كَأَنَّهُمْ جَرَادٌۭ مُّنتَشِرٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **`خ ش ع / B002` (low desolate ground), coactivated with `B001` (low humble submission).** Lowered eyes and emergence from burial ground collapse bodily posture into terrain. The later opening of earth into springs (54:12) strengthens the material analogy: ground that held bodies no longer holds its prior form. The packet supplies low posture, graves, and ruptured earth; I infer terrain-state -> bodily-social abasement. **Reading change:** `خُشَّعًا أَبْصَارُهُم` depicts people taking on the flattened condition of the ground they leave.
2. **`ن ش ر / B002` (reviving the dead after death) alongside `B001` (opening and spreading).** The grave source anchors revival while the locust simile anchors outward dispersal. These are distinct roles in one mechanism: emergence restores bodies; spreading distributes them across the field of summons. The packet directly supplies grave -> emergence -> spread; I add no causal agent beyond that sequence. **Reading change:** `مُنتَشِر` holds resurrection and spatial unfolding together without reducing either to the other.
3. **`ج ر د / B004` (locusts stripping vegetation).** The accepted branch preserves the locust's stripping function, while 54:11-12 soon presents sky and earth stripped of their ordinary closures by water. I infer that the simile assigns the crowd not only locust appearance but landscape-transforming scale; the packet does not say the people literally consume vegetation. **Reading change:** `جَرَادٌ مُنتَشِر` becomes an ecological force-image of order being rapidly bared.

### Retrospective surprises

54:12 was the strongest later surprise: earth itself opens, making the lowered bodies and burial ground participate in one terrain mechanism rather than remain a visual simile only.

## 54:8 - مُّهْطِعِينَ إِلَى ٱلدَّاعِ ۖ يَقُولُ ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ هَٰذَا يَوْمٌ عَسِرٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **`ه ط ع / B001` (fixing and sustaining the gaze) with `B002` (lowering or projecting the neck) and `B003` (hurrying forward).** The preceding lowered eyes and the repeated caller turn `مُهْطِعِين` into a coordinated body: gaze fixed, neck projected, movement accelerated toward one point. The packet supplies each bodily relation; I infer the caller -> synchronizes them. **Reading change:** the word describes compelled whole-body orientation, not speed alone.
2. **`ه ط ع / B004` (humility and compliance).** This branch forms a reversal with the refusal in 54:2-5: the same people who would not yield to a visible sign now move in fear-marked compliance. The packet supplies earlier refusal and later hurrying; I infer rejected voluntary compliance -> involuntary compliance. **Reading change:** `مُهْطِعِين` exposes the temporal cost of postponing response.
3. **`ك ف ر / B001` (covering and concealment), behind `B003` (covering or rejecting truth).** The deniers' identity is followed by Noah's uncovered sky and earth (54:11-12) and by a vessel that alone provides bounded protection (54:13). The packet supplies rejection, removed cosmic cover, and constructed refuge; I infer truth-covering -/-> actual protection. **Reading change:** `ٱلْكَافِرُون` names a failed covering strategy: concealment of truth cannot shelter them from the difficult day.

### Retrospective surprises

54:11-13 corrected the deniers' covering strategy: cosmic cover opens while a fitted craft becomes the only effective local enclosure.

## 54:9 - ۞ كَذَّبَتْ قَبْلَهُمْ قَوْمُ نُوحٍۢ فَكَذَّبُوا۟ عَبْدَنَا وَقَالُوا۟ مَجْنُونٌۭ وَٱزْدُجِرَ

### Activated readings

1. **`ز ج ر / B001` (rebuking, checking, or driving by voice).** Noah is first the object of vocal driving, then his own call in 54:10 is followed by opened sky and earth (54:11-12). The packet supplies suppression -> counter-call -> material response; I infer that failed social silencing becomes the threshold after which the effective voice changes sides. **Reading change:** `وَٱزْدُجِرَ` is not an incidental injury but the hinge in an acoustic reversal.
2. **`ج ن ن / B001` (covering and concealment) within `B006` (madness veiling the mind).** Their `مَجْنُون` label covers the messenger's report just before sky and earth are forcibly uncovered. The packet supplies accusation and a sequence of openings; I infer label-as-cover -> attempts to block evidentiary access. **Reading change:** the madness claim reads diagnostically as the community's concealment device, while retaining its overt accusatory sense.
3. **`ع ب د / B006` (honoring and magnifying), coactivated with `B003` (worship and submissive service).** The possessive `عَبْدَنَا` is followed by divine response, carriage, and watchful care in 54:10-14. Those acts assign honored protection to the servant whom the group demeans. The packet supplies the relational title and subsequent care; I infer service-relation -> honored custody. **Reading change:** `عَبْدَنَا` reverses the social verdict embedded in `مَجْنُون`.

### Retrospective surprises

54:10-14 completed the reversal: the silenced servant calls, water answers, and watchful care carries him, exposing both the madness label as concealment and عبدنا as honor.

## 54:10 - فَدَعَا رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّى مَغْلُوبٌۭ فَٱنتَصِرْ

### Activated readings

1. **`غ ل ب / B001` (overpowering force) with `ن ص ر / B002` (redress after oppression).** `مَغْلُوب` diagnoses a social force imbalance, while `فَٱنتَصِرْ` asks for that imbalance to be redressed. The openings and converging waters in 54:11-12 then provide the counterforce. The packet supplies overpowering, plea, and response; I infer oppression -> calls forth proportionate redress. **Reading change:** the prayer is a compact transfer of an unwinnable human contest into another jurisdiction.
2. **`ن ص ر / B004` (helping rain and relief by rain).** The immediately following downpour activates a materially specific accepted branch of `نصر`. Rain is destructive at the scale of Noah's opponents but functions as aid in the fixed prayer's relation, because 54:13-14 carries and preserves him. The packet supplies rain and rescue; I infer rain -> aid for the overpowered party, not that rain is generically benevolent. **Reading change:** `فَٱنتَصِرْ` unexpectedly includes hydrological assistance.
3. **`ر ب ب / B002` (repair, nurture, and completion), beside `B001` (lordship and authority).** Planks, fasteners, carriage, and watchful eyes in 54:13-14 show a staged preservation system rather than force alone. The branch assigns `رَبَّهُ` the role of one who completes care through ordered means. I infer appeal to nurturing lordship -> construction and custody. **Reading change:** Noah calls not only on superior power but on administration capable of carrying a vulnerable servant through the answer.

### Retrospective surprises

54:11-14 supplied the unexpected lexical confirmation of ن ص ر / B004: rain becomes aid through a staged system of converging water, constructed carriage, and watchful care.

## 54:11 - فَفَتَحْنَآ أَبْوَٰبَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ بِمَآءٍۢ مُّنْهَمِرٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **`ف ت ح / B005` (water released from its opening), grounded in `B001` (opening what is closed).** The sky aperture is immediately paired with earth bursting into springs in 54:12. The two verses supply opposed reservoirs and matching release operations; I infer upper release + lower release -> convergence. **Reading change:** opening the sky's doors is the first half of a coordinated hydraulic mechanism, not isolated meteorology.
2. **`ب و ب / B004` (limit or frontier), coactivated with `B001` (entrance or door).** A door normally regulates passage across a boundary; here the plural doors open until the sky-ground frontier no longer limits water. The vessel of planks and fasteners in 54:13 then rebuilds a small selective boundary. The packet supplies opened cosmic doors and a newly bounded craft; I infer failed large enclosure -> constructed local enclosure. **Reading change:** `أَبْوَابَ ٱلسَّمَاء` marks a controlled boundary reversal.
3. **`ه م ر / B006` (sweeping away and taking), alongside `B001` (pouring and flowing down).** The next ayat show waters meeting and Noah being carried. That sequence activates the downpour's transport capacity: it does not only add volume but sweeps one order away while another object is borne. The packet supplies pouring, meeting, and carriage; I infer flow -> differential transport. **Reading change:** `مُّنْهَمِر` describes an agentive current within the redress mechanism.

### Retrospective surprises

54:12-14 clarified that opening was coordinated rather than indiscriminate: lower water meets upper water, while a bound craft remains selectively supervised.

## 54:12 - وَفَجَّرْنَا ٱلْأَرْضَ عُيُونًۭا فَٱلْتَقَى ٱلْمَآءُ عَلَىٰٓ أَمْرٍۢ قَدْ قُدِرَ

### Activated readings

1. **`ع ي ن / B006` (the springing water-eye), retrospectively paired with `B003` (the eye of watchful care) in 54:14.** The earth's many water-eyes release the destructive flow; two ayat later, "our eyes" supervise the craft that survives it. The packet supplies the root repetition and opposite functions; I infer one controlled system -> source-eyes release while care-eyes preserve. **Reading change:** `عُيُونًا` becomes part of selective governance, not uncontrolled leakage.
2. **`ل ق ي / B010` (making two sides meet), sharpened by `B004` (two things coming face to face).** Upper water from 54:11 and lower water here are explicitly made to encounter one another. `عَلَىٰ أَمْرٍ قَدْ قُدِرَ` blocks a chance-collision reading. The packet supplies both streams and the measured affair; I infer prior arrangement -> engineered meeting. **Reading change:** `فَٱلْتَقَى` is a designed junction.
3. **`ق د ر / B005` (arranging by estimate and deliberation), with `B001` (measure reaching a thing's extent).** The following craft has planks and binding fasteners, and the waters meet at an assigned point. These material proportions activate planning rather than bare foreknowledge. The packet supplies measured language and fitted means; I infer estimate -> coordinated execution. **Reading change:** `قُدِرَ` reads as an event proportioned in actors, volume, junction, and protected payload.

### Retrospective surprises

54:14 was the later correction: the same ع ي ن root that names released springs becomes watchful care, showing that measured water and selective preservation belong to one controlled system.

## 54:13 - وَحَمَلْنَٰهُ عَلَىٰ ذَاتِ أَلْوَٰحٍۢ وَدُسُرٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **`ح م ل / B001` (bearing an outward load), coactivated with `B003` (bearing responsibility or message).** The craft bears Noah physically, while 54:15 says it is left as a sign and 54:17 turns the episode toward remembrance. The later communicative function makes the carried servant also a carried testimony. The packet supplies bodily carriage followed by sign/reminder; I infer transport -> preserves a message-bearing witness. **Reading change:** `حَمَلْنَاهُ` transfers both a body and the responsibility embodied by that body.
2. **`ل و ح / B001` (a thing glinting into view), activated through `B003` (a broad tablet or plank).** The planks are construction material here, then the completed craft becomes a visible `آية` in 54:15. The branch lets material surface acquire display-function. The packet supplies plank -> surviving object -> sign; I infer preservation -> visibility. **Reading change:** `أَلْوَاح` are not mute components only; they become the visible surfaces of a retained indication.
3. **`د س ر / B002` (ship fasteners binding planks).** The fasteners are juxtaposed with waters whose boundaries have broken and merged. Their assigned role is local cohesion within environmental dissolution. The packet supplies converging water and bound planks; I infer binding -> maintains an inhabitable distinction inside the flood. **Reading change:** the terse material inventory states the survival logic: held-together parts carry life through a world whose larger parts no longer stay apart.

### Retrospective surprises

54:15-17 made the construction retrospective evidence: its planks become a visible sign and its carried passenger becomes a carried message available for remembrance.

## 54:14 - تَجْرِى بِأَعْيُنِنَا جَزَآءًۭ لِّمَن كَانَ كُفِرَ

### Activated readings

1. **`ع ي ن / B003` (the eye of watchful care), answering `B006` (the springing water-eye) in 54:12.** Water-eyes open the hazard; watchful eyes maintain the vessel's course through it. The packet supplies identical root imagery with opposed assigned roles; I infer supervision -> makes a general destructive medium selectively navigable. **Reading change:** `بِأَعْيُنِنَا` is active piloting care, not observation at a distance.
2. **`ج ر ي / B006` (ongoing provision or grant), alongside `B001` (running and flowing).** `تَجْرِي` sits next to `جَزَاء`, and the voyage continuously supplies escape while the waters continue. The packet supplies motion as recompense; I infer continuing course -> continuing grant. **Reading change:** the vessel's sailing is itself the sustained form of payment, not merely movement toward a later reward.
3. **`ك ف ر / B001` (covering and concealment), sharpened by `B003` (rejecting truth).** The passive `كُفِرَ` identifies one whose truth or standing was covered by his people; the visible, watched vessel reverses that concealment. The packet supplies earlier denial of "our servant" and present honored carriage; I infer communal covering -> divine public vindication. **Reading change:** `لِمَن كَانَ كُفِرَ` marks recompense for a denied person, not only punishment of deniers.

### Retrospective surprises

54:15-17 supplied the later surprise: the watched voyage becomes a retained sign and a mnemonic object, so sailing as ongoing recompense also becomes public vindication of the denied servant.

## 54:15 - وَلَقَد تَّرَكْنَٰهَآ ءَايَةًۭ فَهَلْ مِن مُّدَّكِرٍۢ

### Activated readings

2. **`ذ ك ر / B003` (bringing a thing to mind) with `B009` (reminder or admonition).** `مُدَّكِر` follows a surviving sign and anticipates the repeated statement that the Quran is made ready for `الذكر`. The branch assigns the hearer an active retrieval role. The packet supplies retained object and repeated question; I infer sign + attention -> reactivated event. **Reading change:** the ayah tests whether preserved matter becomes present cognition.
3. **`ء ي ي / B003` (a visible sign).** The next wind account in 54:18-20 offers another material scene, so `آية` functions as a repeatable relation between visible remnant and causal warning, not as an isolated marvel. I infer that the retained object's visibility -> lets a later audience reconstruct the mechanism. **Reading change:** the sign is evidentiary infrastructure for remembrance.

### Retrospective surprises

54:17 confirmed that the retained object was designed for cognitive reuse, and 54:18-20 showed that its sign-function transfers to a new, materially different warning case.

## 54:16 - فَكَيْفَ كَانَ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ

### Activated readings

1. **`ن ذ ر / B001` (a warning that awakens fear and caution).** Positioned after the retained sign in 54:15 and before facilitated remembrance in 54:17, `نُذُر` includes both advance notice and the aftermath that validates it. The packet supplies warning, material trace, and mnemonic refrain; I infer fulfilled event -> reactivates the earlier warning for a new hearer. **Reading change:** the warning is temporally two-sided: speech before consequence and evidence after it.
