# Surah 25 Eleven-Ayah Full-Context Control: Ayah Walk

Each fixed ayah is read through its clipped eleven-ayah focus window: up to five ayat before and five after.

## 25:0 - بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A non-genealogical shelter of formation.** **Lexical:** `س م و B005` is the name as a raised designation, while `ر ح م B003` is the womb image. **Structure:** the doubled mercy root in the fixed formula precedes the descent of the differentiator in 25:1 and the denial of offspring alongside measured creation in 25:2. **Role:** the name raises the source into explicit invocation; the womb branch gives the two mercy adjectives an enclosing, life-forming function. **Abduction:** I infer that 25:2 redirects biological generativity away from literal descent and toward protected formation; that directional claim comes from the juxtaposition, not from the branch inventory itself. **Reading change:** the opening becomes an invocation of encompassing formation under a named source, not only a prefatory label. This womb activation is exploratory because the fixed forms are mercy adjectives, not the anatomical noun.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:2 surprisingly redirects the womb image: offspring is denied while creation and measuring remain, sharpening mercy as non-genealogical enclosure.

## 25:1 - تَبَارَكَ ٱلَّذِى نَزَّلَ ٱلْفُرْقَانَ عَلَىٰ عَبْدِهِۦ لِيَكُونَ لِلْعَٰلَمِينَ نَذِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **Calibrated descent rather than bare transfer.** **Lexical:** `ن ز ل B004` is placing in proper order, `ف ر ق B001` is distinguishing and separating, and the nearby `ق د ر B005` in 25:2 is arranging by estimate and deliberation. **Structure:** ordered descent leads to differentiation and then to the warning purpose; the next ayah immediately joins creation to repeated measuring. **Role:** descent meters the delivery, differentiation sorts what it reaches, and warning converts that sorting into caution. **Abduction:** I infer that the ordering of revelation enables its discriminating force; the packet supplies the sequence and the measuring analogy, while I supply that causal arrow. **Reading change:** the fixed ayah describes a designed communication process whose cadence produces discernment, not simply an object moved downward.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:2's doubled measuring confirms ordered descent as calibration; 25:5-6 then contrast alleged dictation with hidden knowing.

## 25:2 - ٱلَّذِى لَهُۥ مُلْكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَلَمْ يَتَّخِذْ وَلَدًۭا وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ شَرِيكٌۭ فِى ٱلْمُلْكِ وَخَلَقَ كُلَّ شَىْءٍۢ فَقَدَّرَهُۥ تَقْدِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Sovereignty as undivided design-cohesion.** **Lexical:** `م ل ك B001` is strength and cohesion, `خ ل ق B001` is measuring and proportioning, `ق د ر B006` is fitting proportion and middle measure, and `ش ر ك B008` is a divided or preoccupied mind. **Structure:** the two occurrences of *mulk* enclose denials of offspring and partner before creation culminates in doubled measuring. **Role:** cohesion names the governing whole; proportion supplies its internal fit; the rejected partnership image marks the division that would fracture it. **Abduction:** I infer that the two negations protect the causal unity required by measured creation. **Reading change:** dominion reads as the coherent integration of every proportioned thing, not merely possession of a territory.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:3 tests dominion by inability over self, death, life, and spreading, confirming cohesion as causal rather than titular.

## 25:3 - وَٱتَّخَذُوا۟ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ ءَالِهَةًۭ لَّا يَخْلُقُونَ شَيْـًۭٔا وَهُمْ يُخْلَقُونَ وَلَا يَمْلِكُونَ لِأَنفُسِهِمْ ضَرًّۭا وَلَا نَفْعًۭا وَلَا يَمْلِكُونَ مَوْتًۭا وَلَا حَيَوٰةًۭ وَلَا نُشُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **An audit of causal cohesion.** **Lexical:** `م ل ك B001` is strength and cohesion, `خ ل ق B001` is measuring and proportioning, and `ن ش ر B001` is opening, spreading, and branching out. **Structure:** active and passive creation are reversed around the alleged gods, then repeated negated ownership runs from self-directed harm and benefit to death, life, and final spreading. **Role:** *mulk* tests whether an agent can hold a causal system together; *nushur* extends the test from bare animation to generative unfolding. **Abduction:** I infer that the ordered list is a progressively wider agency test, rather than a heap of denied powers. **Reading change:** the ayah rejects the deities because no coherent causal field proceeds from them, even at the level of their own selves.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:8's garden and eating awaken *nushur* as generative spreading, so the final triad reaches beyond bare revival into provision-bearing expansion.

## 25:4 - وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓا۟ إِنْ هَٰذَآ إِلَّآ إِفْكٌ ٱفْتَرَىٰهُ وَأَعَانَهُۥ عَلَيْهِ قَوْمٌ ءَاخَرُونَ ۖ فَقَدْ جَآءُو ظُلْمًۭا وَزُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **The accusation exposes its own workshop.** **Lexical:** `ء ف ك B001` is turning something from its proper direction, `ف ر ي B001` is cutting in order to repair or stitch, and `ز و ر B006` is shaping or preparing speech. **Structure:** the claim moves from a diverted product, to alleged stitching, to helpers, and then to prepared false speech; 25:5 immediately adds rows, writing, and dictation. **Role:** diversion sets the false direction, stitching assembles pieces, and prepared speech packages the result. **Abduction:** I infer that the accusers project a craft-production model onto revelation while themselves assembling such a model out of allegations. **Reading change:** the closing verdict diagnoses not only false content but a manufactured redirection whose production steps are audible in the accusation.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:5's rows, writing, and dictation unexpectedly literalize the fixed ayah's stitching workshop; 25:9 shows its product as route-disabling representations.

## 25:5 - وَقَالُوٓا۟ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ ٱكْتَتَبَهَا فَهِىَ تُمْلَىٰ عَلَيْهِ بُكْرَةًۭ وَأَصِيلًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **An imagined textual supply chain.** **Lexical:** `س ط ر B001` is an aligned written row, `ك ت ب B001` is joining one thing to another, `م ل و B004` is dictating a written text, `ب ك ر B001` is early morning, and `ء ص ل B003` is evening after afternoon. **Structure:** the accusation joins inherited rows to self-procured writing and recurrent dictation at both ends of the day. **Role:** joining supplies compilation, dictation supplies replenishment, and the two time markers make the process continuous. **Abduction:** I infer that the speakers imagine a repeatable human production line, not one isolated act of plagiarism. **Reading change:** the ayah presents their theory as a mechanically maintained workflow whose visible routine will be opposed by the hidden knowledge named in 25:6.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:6 replaces the visible copying schedule with knowledge of the hidden, correcting the accusation's source model; 25:9 reveals such models as path-damaging images.

## 25:6 - قُلْ أَنزَلَهُ ٱلَّذِى يَعْلَمُ ٱلسِّرَّ فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ غَفُورًۭا رَّحِيمًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Known concealment becomes the ground of protective mercy.** **Lexical:** `س ر ر B001` is keeping something hidden inwardly, `غ ف ر B001` is protective covering, and `ر ح م B001` is mercy and tenderness. **Structure:** knowledge of the secret answers 25:5's public copying schedule, while the demands for visible angel, treasure, and garden in 25:7-8 intensify the contrast between hidden source and visible proof. **Role:** secret marks what is inaccessible to ordinary production; forgiveness covers what is nevertheless fully known; mercy directs that knowledge toward care. **Abduction:** I infer that covering here protects rather than conceals information from the knower. **Reading change:** forgiveness reads as deliberate shelter granted with complete inner knowledge, not lenience caused by ignorance.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:7-8's demand for a visible angel, treasure, and garden sharpens the difference between hidden knowledge and visible proof; 25:11 adds the denied unseen Hour.

## 25:7 - وَقَالُوا۟ مَالِ هَٰذَا ٱلرَّسُولِ يَأْكُلُ ٱلطَّعَامَ وَيَمْشِى فِى ٱلْأَسْوَاقِ ۙ لَوْلَآ أُنزِلَ إِلَيْهِ مَلَكٌۭ فَيَكُونَ مَعَهُۥ نَذِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **Ordinary circulation is the delivery infrastructure.** **Lexical:** `ر س ل B001` is being sent forth and released, `م ش ي B001` is walking and movement, `س و ق B005` is the marketplace, while `س و ق B001` is also driving forward. **Structure:** messenger, eating, walking, and markets form one movement chain; 25:9 then names the lost *sabil*, and 25:12 contrasts nearby human circulation with punishment perceptible from afar. **Role:** walking embeds the envoy in common space, the market supplies public circulation, and the dormant driving branch gives that space directional force. **Abduction:** I infer that bodily participation enables warning to travel through ordinary social routes. **Reading change:** what the objection treats as disqualification becomes the mechanism by which the mission reaches lived life.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:9's lost path confirms market-walking as route infrastructure; 25:12 surprisingly reverses proximity, with punishment detecting from afar.

## 25:8 - أَوْ يُلْقَىٰٓ إِلَيْهِ كَنزٌ أَوْ تَكُونُ لَهُۥ جَنَّةٌۭ يَأْكُلُ مِنْهَا ۚ وَقَالَ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ إِن تَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا رَجُلًۭا مَّسْحُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **Desired casting is ominously reversed.** **Lexical:** `ل ق ي B005` is casting something where it is met. **Structure:** the passive wish that treasure be cast to the messenger is answered, exactly five ayat later in 25:13, by the passive casting of the rejecters into a constricted place. **Role:** the same branch links desired bestowal and imposed emplacement while reversing recipient and value. **Abduction:** I infer that insistence on externally cast material proof places the speakers inside a grammar of external disposal; the packet supplies the reversal, while that warning relation is my inference. **Reading change:** the request is no longer neutral fantasy but the first half of a passive-voice reversal in which the demanders become what is cast.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:13 completes the exact passive casting reversal: the wished-for cast treasure becomes the speakers' own casting into constriction.

## 25:9 - ٱنظُرْ كَيْفَ ضَرَبُوا۟ لَكَ ٱلْأَمْثَٰلَ فَضَلُّوا۟ فَلَا يَسْتَطِيعُونَ سَبِيلًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Representations become impacts that disable a route.** **Lexical:** `ض ر ب B001` is making an impact, `م ث ل B008` is a representation or image, `ض ل ل B001` is going astray from an aim, and `ط و ع B003` is practical capacity. **Structure:** their portraits of the messenger in 25:7-8 are followed by loss and then inability; the causative sequence is reinforced by the command to look at how the portraits were made. **Role:** the image strikes cognition, straying bends orientation, and incapacity prevents route recovery. **Abduction:** I infer that repeated labels do causal work by substituting a manufactured representation for encounter. **Reading change:** the ayah diagnoses a representational mechanism that damages practical navigation, not merely a set of inaccurate comparisons.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:12 reverses the command to look: the punishment now sees them, while 25:13 makes route incapacity literal spatial confinement.

## 25:10 - تَبَارَكَ ٱلَّذِىٓ إِن شَآءَ جَعَلَ لَكَ خَيْرًۭا مِّن ذَٰلِكَ جَنَّٰتٍۢ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَٰرُ وَيَجْعَل لَّكَ قُصُورًۢا

### Activated readings

1. **The requested display is corrected in scale and duration.** **Lexical:** `ب ر ك B004` is a fixed, growing good, `ج ن ن B003` is a tree-covered garden, `ج ر ي B006` is an ongoing provision or grant, and `ق ص ر B005` is an enclosed monumental building. **Structure:** the singular garden demanded in 25:8 expands here into plural gardens, continuous rivers, and palaces; 25:15 then relocates comparison to the garden of permanence. **Role:** blessing stabilizes growth, flow makes provision continuous, and the palace supplies bounded habitation. **Abduction:** I infer that the conditional abundance demonstrates unconstrained capacity while postponing the decisive valuation to an enduring endpoint. **Reading change:** the fixed ayah is not merely a richer counteroffer; it corrects the demander's cramped scale and immediate time horizon.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:15 supplies the decisive correction: *khayr* is evaluated against a permanent garden, not by immediate palace-scale display.

## 25:11 - بَلْ كَذَّبُوا۟ بِٱلسَّاعَةِ ۖ وَأَعْتَدْنَا لِمَن كَذَّبَ بِٱلسَّاعَةِ سَعِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **Preparedness collapses the distance of denial.** **Lexical:** `ع ت د B001` is what is prepared and present, and `س ع ر B001` is fire kindling and blazing. **Structure:** repeated declarations of falsehood bracket the repeated Hour, but the response shifts from their future-denial to an already prepared blaze; in 25:12 that blaze perceives them from a far place. **Role:** preparedness makes the threatened consequence presently ready, while blazing gives readiness active intensity. **Abduction:** I infer that the ayah counters temporal dismissal by presenting the future event as an existing readiness. **Reading change:** denial of the Hour becomes a failure to perceive present preparation, not simply disbelief in a remote date.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:12 makes prepared fire an active perceiver from afar, turning readiness into anticipation rather than static storage.

## 25:12 - إِذَا رَأَتْهُم مِّن مَّكَانٍۭ بَعِيدٍۢ سَمِعُوا۟ لَهَا تَغَيُّظًۭا وَزَفِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Detection precedes confinement.** **Lexical:** `ر ء ي B004` is mutual visibility and facing, `ب ع د B001` is remoteness, `س م ع B011` is hearing without reaching, `غ ي ظ B001` is pent-up anger in the chest, and `ز ف ر B002` is heavy breath and harsh sound. **Structure:** sight travels from the blaze toward them while sound travels back toward them across distance; 25:13 then turns the far *makan* into a narrow *makan*. **Role:** remote sight detects, unreached hearing announces, and compressed anger becomes audible breath. **Abduction:** I infer that sensory capture begins before bodily arrival and prepares the later spatial capture. **Reading change:** punishment first appears as an inescapable relation across distance, not only as contact after entry.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:13 contracts the far *makan* into a narrow *makan*; 25:17 then completes detection as gathering and directed arrival.

## 25:13 - وَإِذَآ أُلْقُوا۟ مِنْهَا مَكَانًۭا ضَيِّقًۭا مُّقَرَّنِينَ دَعَوْا۟ هُنَالِكَ ثُبُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Spatial compression leaves only a blocked verbal exit.** **Lexical:** `ض ي ق B001` is narrowness against spaciousness, `ق ر ن B001` is joining and binding things together, `د ع و B001` is calling to draw near, and `ث ب ر B003` is restraint and blocking. **Structure:** casting is followed by a narrow place, bound bodies, and then a call; 25:14 multiplies the call without opening the space. **Role:** narrowness removes room, binding removes independent motion, and the ruin-call attempts to summon the only imaginable release while the dormant blocking branch denies it. **Abduction:** I infer that speech remains as the last form of movement after bodily movement is removed. **Reading change:** *thubur* reads as an attempted but blocked exit enacted by voice, not an abstract cry detached from the geometry.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:14 multiplies the call without opening the space; 25:18 strips away the allies whom calling might have drawn near.

## 25:14 - لَّا تَدْعُوا۟ ٱلْيَوْمَ ثُبُورًۭا وَٰحِدًۭا وَٱدْعُوا۟ ثُبُورًۭا كَثِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Numerical amplification exposes failed calling.** **Lexical:** `د ع و B001` is calling to draw near, `ث ب ر B002` is ruin and perdition, and `ك ث ر B001` is numerical abundance. **Structure:** call and ruin are repeated around the explicit contrast between one and many; 25:18 has the former allies disavow the callers, and 25:19 denies both diversion and help. **Role:** number intensifies the call's acknowledgement but cannot draw any rescuer near. **Abduction:** I infer that the imperative to multiply ruin-calls is ironic exposure of collapsed social efficacy. **Reading change:** the ayah is an arithmetic test showing that repetition cannot convert a severed relation into rescue.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:18's disavowal and 25:19's denial of diversion or help confirm that numerical abundance cannot restore a severed relation.

## 25:15 - قُلْ أَذَٰلِكَ خَيْرٌ أَمْ جَنَّةُ ٱلْخُلْدِ ٱلَّتِى وُعِدَ ٱلْمُتَّقُونَ ۚ كَانَتْ لَهُمْ جَزَآءًۭ وَمَصِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Good is evaluated by trajectory and endpoint.** **Lexical:** `خ ي ر B003` is choosing or seeking the better option, `و ق ي B002` is placing the self in protective caution, `ج ز ي B001` is recompense matching an act, and `ص ي ر B007` is a place of settling or destination. **Structure:** the question follows multiplied cries of ruin and contrasts them with a promised, guarded, matching destination; 25:20 later names patience inside the present test. **Role:** choice evaluates, caution governs the route, recompense links route to result, and destination stabilizes the comparison. **Abduction:** I infer that the two outcomes can be compared properly only by where their practices become settled. **Reading change:** the rhetorical question becomes a decision model in which protective conduct is a path toward a fitting end.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:16 makes the promise askable, while 25:20 identifies patience as the present discipline connecting guarded choice to destination.

## 25:16 - لَّهُمْ فِيهَا مَا يَشَآءُونَ خَٰلِدِينَ ۚ كَانَ عَلَىٰ رَبِّكَ وَعْدًۭا مَّسْـُٔولًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Legitimate asking answers an offered commitment.** **Lexical:** `ش ي ء B001` includes willing toward a thing, `و ع د B001` is a spoken promise that opens expectation, `س ء ل B002` is the sought request, and `ر ب ب B002` is nurture and completion. **Structure:** their willing occurs inside permanence and is followed by a promise described as asked; 25:21 contrasts this with a demand that angels descend or the Lord be seen on the demanders' terms. **Role:** promise opens expectation, asking receives what was opened, and lordly nurture brings it to completion. **Abduction:** I infer that this asking is authorized responsiveness rather than leverage imposed on the giver. **Reading change:** *mas'ulan* makes the promise relationally accessible and fulfillable, sharply different from coercive proof-demand.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:21 provides the correcting counterexample: an imposed demand for angels and direct sight differs from asking within an offered promise.

## 25:17 - وَيَوْمَ يَحْشُرُهُمْ وَمَا يَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَقُولُ ءَأَنتُمْ أَضْلَلْتُمْ عِبَادِى هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ أَمْ هُمْ ضَلُّوا۟ ٱلسَّبِيلَ

### Activated readings

1. **The gathering is a causal audit of misdirection.** **Lexical:** `ح ش ر B001` is gathering and driving a group to a destination, `ع ب د B003` is worship and submissive obedience, `ض ل ل B001` is straying from guidance and aim, and `س ب ل B001` is an extended traversed way. **Structure:** worshipped and worshippers are driven into one scene, then the repeated root alternates causative "you misled" with intransitive "they strayed." **Role:** gathering makes all candidate agents present, the verbal alternation distinguishes imposed causation from self-navigation, and the way supplies the tested domain. **Abduction:** I infer that the question partitions responsibility rather than requesting unknown information. **Reading change:** the ayah becomes a tribunal over how a path was lost, not merely a scene of collective assembly.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:18 answers the causal question with enjoyment, forgetting, and abandonment rather than direct misguidance; 25:19 then removes alleged allies' power.

## 25:18 - قَالُوا۟ سُبْحَٰنَكَ مَا كَانَ يَنۢبَغِى لَنَآ أَن نَّتَّخِذَ مِن دُونِكَ مِنْ أَوْلِيَآءَ وَلَٰكِن مَّتَّعْتَهُمْ وَءَابَآءَهُمْ حَتَّىٰ نَسُوا۟ ٱلذِّكْرَ وَكَانُوا۟ قَوْمًۢا بُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Prolonged benefit decays into abandoned memory.** **Lexical:** `س ب ح B002` is purification and declaring free, `م ت ع B007` is prolonged keeping for benefit, `ن س ي B002` is leaving or abandoning something, and `ب و ر B002` is stagnation into failure. **Structure:** the answer first clears the speakers of claimed alliance, then uses *hatta* to run enjoyment through generations into abandoned remembrance and finally collective failure. **Role:** prolonged benefit supplies duration, abandonment names the cognitive break, and stagnation names its social endpoint. **Abduction:** the grammar supplies succession; I infer that unconverted ease gradually erodes active remembrance. **Reading change:** ruin becomes a slow material and intergenerational decay process rather than a sudden lapse of information.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:20 recasts people-for-people contact as trial, and 25:23 materializes stagnant failure as work dispersed into dust.

## 25:19 - فَقَدْ كَذَّبُوكُم بِمَا تَقُولُونَ فَمَا تَسْتَطِيعُونَ صَرْفًۭا وَلَا نَصْرًۭا ۚ وَمَن يَظْلِم مِّنكُمْ نُذِقْهُ عَذَابًۭا كَبِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Experience replaces unsupported assertion.** **Lexical:** `ص ر ف B001` is turning back or diverting, `ن ص ر B001` is aid that makes someone prevail, and `ذ و ق B002` is experiencing and testing something. **Structure:** the worshipped beings first invalidate what their worshippers say; inability to divert or obtain aid follows, and tasted punishment then replaces speech with direct experience. **Role:** diversion would alter the trajectory, aid would overcome it, and tasting supplies an internal test when both external mechanisms fail. **Abduction:** I infer that a claim deprived of its represented allies loses performative leverage and is corrected by encounter. **Reading change:** the ayah shifts adjudication from what is said to what must be undergone and tested.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:23 removes the cohesion of accumulated work, while 25:24 contrasts that dispersal with protected, settled rest.

## 25:20 - وَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَا قَبْلَكَ مِنَ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ إِلَّآ إِنَّهُمْ لَيَأْكُلُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ وَيَمْشُونَ فِى ٱلْأَسْوَاقِ ۗ وَجَعَلْنَا بَعْضَكُمْ لِبَعْضٍۢ فِتْنَةً أَتَصْبِرُونَ ۗ وَكَانَ رَبُّكَ بَصِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Ordinary social adjacency is the assay medium.** **Lexical:** `ب ع ض B001` is parting a thing into portions, `ف ت ن B001` is assaying by trial, `ص ب ر B001` is self-restraint from panic, and `ب ص ر B002` is inner sight. **Structure:** food, walking, and markets repeat the objection in 25:7, but are now followed by reciprocal "some of you for others," a question about restraint, and inward seeing. **Role:** partition creates distinct social positions, contact between them performs the assay, restraint governs the response, and inner sight evaluates what appearance hides. **Abduction:** I infer that messenger ordinariness is deliberately retained because embodied social contact makes the test possible. **Reading change:** common bodily life is not incidental to mission; it is the designed apparatus through which responses are revealed.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:21 immediately performs the failed assay by rejecting ordinary mediation; 25:25 grants angelic descent only at the judgmental time they did not control.

## 25:21 - ۞ وَقَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرْجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا لَوْلَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيْنَا ٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ أَوْ نَرَىٰ رَبَّنَا ۗ لَقَدِ ٱسْتَكْبَرُوا۟ فِىٓ أَنفُسِهِمْ وَعَتَوْ عُتُوًّۭا كَبِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **The middle is a load-bearing regime, not an arithmetic midpoint.** **Lexical:** `ن ف ق B002` is wealth going out as expenditure, `س ر ف B001` is overstepping the limit, `ق ت ر B001` is stinting and narrowing, `ب ي ن B011` is an intermediate state between two ends, and `ق و م B009` is support, mainstay, and subsistence. **Structure:** an outflow is bounded by two negations and then located between their extremes as *qawam*. **Role:** expenditure moves resources, excess breaches system limits, constriction starves the system, and the middle sustains it upright. **Abduction:** I infer that the correct measure is whatever preserves the function and subsistence of the whole, not equal distance from two quantities. **Reading change:** moderation becomes dynamic structural support.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:68 adds hard protected bounds to the sustaining middle; 25:70-71 show correction, and 25:72 models nonparticipation without collapse.

## 25:68 - وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَدْعُونَ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ وَلَا يَقْتُلُونَ ٱلنَّفْسَ ٱلَّتِى حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ إِلَّا بِٱلْحَقِّ وَلَا يَزْنُونَ ۚ وَمَن يَفْعَلْ ذَٰلِكَ يَلْقَ أَثَامًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Three protected bounds become a path to encountered consequence.** **Lexical:** `ح ر م B003` is an inviolable precinct and protected bounds, `ن ف س B011` is the living soul-self, `ل ق ي B007` is what one meets of good or ill, and `ء ث م B001` is slowing and lagging from good. **Structure:** allegiance, protected life, and sexual relation are marked by repeated negation; the close shifts from doing to meeting. **Role:** inviolability unifies the prohibitions as boundaries, the living self identifies what one boundary protects, action crosses them, and meeting turns crossing into a future encounter. **Abduction:** I infer that transgression constructs the route on which its consequence comes face to face with the actor. **Reading change:** the list becomes one boundary system with a generated trajectory, not three unrelated rules.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:69 specifies the encountered consequence; 25:70-71 prevent fatalism by opening transformation and directed return; 25:72 extends boundary practice to speech.

## 25:69 - يُضَٰعَفْ لَهُ ٱلْعَذَابُ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ وَيَخْلُدْ فِيهِۦ مُهَانًا

### Activated readings

1. **Chosen posture is inverted and made to cling.** **Lexical:** `ض ع ف B002` is adding an equal amount, `ق و م B013` is resurrection and rising of the Hour, `خ ل د B002` is leaning, clinging, and staying close, and `ه و ن B003` is humiliation through contempt. **Structure:** doubled punishment occurs at the rising, then adheres in humiliating permanence; within the window, 25:63 used `ه و ن B001` for calm dignity and 25:64 used `ق و م B002` for upright devotion. **Role:** doubling multiplies consequence, resurrection raises the actor, clinging fixes the result, and humiliation reverses gentle dignity. **Abduction:** I infer that cultivated or rejected posture hardens into an outcome with an opposite moral quality. **Reading change:** doubling is qualitative inversion as well as numerical increase.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:70 immediately prevents a deterministic reading through transformation; 25:74 supplies an alternative multiplication through descendants and exemplarity.

## 25:70 - إِلَّا مَن تَابَ وَءَامَنَ وَعَمِلَ عَمَلًۭا صَٰلِحًۭا فَأُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ يُبَدِّلُ ٱللَّهُ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمْ حَسَنَٰتٍۢ ۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ غَفُورًۭا رَّحِيمًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Mercy reorganizes the record through enacted repair.** **Lexical:** `ء م ن B001` is settled security and trust, `ع م ل B001` is intentional doing, `ص ل ح B001` is soundness against corruption, `ب د ل B002` is alteration or transformation, `غ ف ر B002` is forgiving and shielding from consequence, and `ر ح م B001` is mercy. **Structure:** the exception inserts trust and doubled work before bad acts are transformed into good ones; 25:71 repeats return around sound work, and 25:72-75 display changed conduct. **Role:** trust stabilizes orientation, work makes repair concrete, soundness opposes corruption, transformation changes moral quality, and forgiveness shields the reworked agent. **Abduction:** I infer that repentance becomes effective through reparative action rather than verbal cancellation alone. **Reading change:** forgiveness is productive transformation of the record and person, not mere erasure.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:71's repeated return gives transformation a direction; 25:72-75 then show its conduct and matched reward.

## 25:71 - وَمَن تَابَ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحًۭا فَإِنَّهُۥ يَتُوبُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَتَابًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Sound work turns repeated return into a verified trajectory.** **Lexical:** `ع م ل B001` is intentional doing and work, and `ص ل ح B001` is soundness and rightness against corruption. The packet's only accepted `ت و ب` entry is causative `B003` (calling someone else to repent), so I do not force that branch onto these intransitive forms. **Structure:** three forms of the return root surround intentional, sound work and end with an explicit destination. **Role:** work supplies observable motion and soundness tests its direction; the repeated construction supplies initiation, continuation, and named course even where the inventory does not license a dormant branch. **Abduction:** I infer that the second clause verifies where the first return is actually going. **Reading change:** the apparent tautology becomes a distinction between beginning to turn and completing a God-directed return through repaired conduct.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:72-73 make repaired return externally testable as controlled attention and active reception; 25:75 supplies matched recompense.

## 25:72 - وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَشْهَدُونَ ٱلزُّورَ وَإِذَا مَرُّوا۟ بِٱللَّغْوِ مَرُّوا۟ كِرَامًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Dignity controls what receives validating presence and attention.** **Lexical:** `ش ه د B001` is presence with witnessing, `ز و ر B002` is falsehood and invalid speech, `م ر ر B001` is passing through, `ل غ و B001` is the thing set aside as not counting, and `ك ر م B001` is praiseworthy nobility. **Structure:** they deny falsehood their witnessing presence, then the doubled passing root moves them through noncounting speech without stopping. **Role:** witnessing could socially validate, noncounting speech solicits attention without worth, passing withholds capture, and nobility names the preserved agency. **Abduction:** I infer that ethical nonparticipation operates by controlling what is allowed to count, not by trying to suppress every utterance. **Reading change:** noble passage is an active attention filter rather than passive avoidance.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:73 contrasts noble passing with active engagement when a true reminder arrives; 25:77 makes genuine calling what gives people relational weight.

## 25:73 - وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِّرُوا۟ بِـَٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمْ لَمْ يَخِرُّوا۟ عَلَيْهَا صُمًّۭا وَعُمْيَانًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **The verse rejects inert collapse masquerading as response.** **Lexical:** `ذ ك ر B009` is reminder or admonition, `ء ي ي B003` is a visible sign, `خ ر ر B001` is falling with a rumbling drop, `ص م م B001` is loss of hearing and refusal to listen, and `ع م ي B002` is blindness of heart and insight. **Structure:** reminder by signs is followed by a negated fall qualified by deafness and blindness; 25:74 then turns eyes into settled family vision and asks for exemplary guidance. **Role:** reminder solicits active uptake, the fall supplies intense bodily reaction, and the two impairments expose reaction without perception. **Abduction:** I infer that physical intensity alone can imitate submission while leaving cognition closed. **Reading change:** the ayah criticizes performative collapse, not bodily reverence itself.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:74 converts restored seeing into intergenerational petition; 25:75 answers active reception with life-giving greeting.

## 25:74 - وَٱلَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَٰجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّٰتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍۢ وَٱجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

### Activated readings

1. **Family becomes an ethical transmission network.** **Lexical:** `و ه ب B001` is giving a gift without exchange, `ذ ر ر B004` is gentle spreading emergence, `ق ر ر B002` is settled comfort of the eye, `ع ي ن B003` is the eye of watchful care, and `ء م م B009` is a leader, guide, or exemplar followed. **Structure:** after 25:73 rejects blind reception, the prayer asks for settled sight through spouses and descendants, then expands outward from household gift to public exemplar. **Role:** gift prevents ownership-claim, descendants spread the pattern, settled eyes make it perceptually stable, and exemplar lets protected people follow it. **Abduction:** I infer that domestic delight is sought as reproducible guidance rather than private possession. **Reading change:** the prayer asks for intergenerational and public propagation of right perception.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:75 links patient formation to elevated reward, while 25:77 confirms that calling, not lineage by itself, gives relational weight.

## 25:75 - أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ يُجْزَوْنَ ٱلْغُرْفَةَ بِمَا صَبَرُوا۟ وَيُلَقَّوْنَ فِيهَا تَحِيَّةًۭ وَسَلَٰمًا

### Activated readings

1. **Restrained passage is answered by elevation and life-giving address.** **Lexical:** `ج ز ي B001` is recompense matching an act, `غ ر ف B001` is lifting and taking up, `غ ر ف B002` is an upper chamber, `ص ب ر B001` is self-restraint from panic, `ل ق ي B011` is receiving or imparting speech, `ح ي ي B007` is greeting as wishing life and peace, and `س ل م B001` is soundness and safety. **Structure:** the disciplined responses of 25:72-74 culminate in matched elevation and a received greeting rather than false or idle speech. **Role:** patience holds the self, lifting spatializes its reward, and reception supplies affirmative social recognition. **Abduction:** I infer that their controlled speech-world is answered by speech that confers life and safety. **Reading change:** reward is communicative welcome as well as elevated habitation.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:76 confirms that elevation and greeting settle into a durable abode; 25:77 contrasts welcomed address with binding aftermath.

## 25:76 - خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا ۚ حَسُنَتْ مُسْتَقَرًّۭا وَمُقَامًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Permanence is good because the settled relation is good.** **Lexical:** `خ ل د B001` is enduring permanence, `ح س ن B001` is goodness opposed to ugliness, `ق ر ر B003` is settling and remaining fixed, and `ق و م B006` is station and residence. **Structure:** abiding follows 25:75's elevation, greeting, and peace, then the paired place terms receive a positive verdict; 25:77 closes with a different kind of binding adherence. **Role:** permanence extends the condition, settlement fixes it, residence sustains it, and goodness evaluates the relational content already supplied. **Abduction:** I infer that duration alone is not the benefit; the welcomed and peaceful order makes duration desirable. **Reading change:** the abode is good as sustained relational stability, not merely because it never ends.

### Retrospective surprises

- 25:77 supplies the negative counterpart to permanence: fixed adherence can be welcome stability or inescapable consequence.

## 25:77 - قُلْ مَا يَعْبَؤُا۟ بِكُمْ رَبِّى لَوْلَا دُعَآؤُكُمْ ۖ فَقَدْ كَذَّبْتُمْ فَسَوْفَ يَكُونُ لِزَامًۢا

### Activated readings

1. **Voluntary calling gives way to compulsory adherence when relation is denied.** **Lexical:** `ع ب ء B002` is lack of weight or regard, `ع ب ء B001` is a heavy burden, `د ع و B001` is calling to draw near, `ك ذ ب B002` is declaring false, and `ل ز م B001` is continuous accompaniment and fixed adherence. **Structure:** lack of regard is made conditional on their call, then denial leads prospectively to something that will adhere; the nearby 25:72 treats idle speech as not counting, while 25:74 models genuine prayer. **Role:** calling establishes relational weight, denial rejects that relation, and adherence supplies its involuntary replacement. **Abduction:** I infer that the close opposes chosen nearness through address to consequence that cannot be shaken off. **Reading change:** the final ayah presents relation as nonneutral: it is either cultivated through calling or imposed as binding aftermath.

### Retrospective surprises

- No later ayah exists inside the clipped focus window; the closing adherence therefore receives no retrospective activation.
