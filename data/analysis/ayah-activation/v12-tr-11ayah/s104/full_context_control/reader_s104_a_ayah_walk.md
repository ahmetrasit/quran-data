# v12 11-Ayah Reader: Surah 104 Full-Context Control

## 104:0: بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Focus window: `104:0`-`104:5`.

### Activated readings

1. **Authoritative designation versus hostile designation.** Lexical evidence is fixed `بِسْمِ`, `س م و B005` (name as raised designation), sharpened by `ه م ز B004` and `ل م ز B001` in `104:1` (disparaging and covert fault-finding). Structurally, the opening act of naming precedes two habitual agent-nouns that reduce other people through speech. The packet supplies that order and the opposed naming practices; I infer that the first designation governs how persons and acts are to be valued. The basmalah therefore reads not only as a formula of commencement but as a contest over whose naming has standing: raised divine designation precedes and relativizes the assailant's degrading labels.

2. **A reordering of dependence.** Fixed `ٱللَّهِ` carries `ء ل ه B002` (the fixed divine name) and activates `ء ل ه B001` (the worshipped object). `104:2`-`104:3` then supply `م و ل B001` (possessed wealth), `ع د د B001` (enumeration), and a claim that wealth causes endurance. The text does not call wealth a deity; the directional move is mine: what is counted on for permanence can function as the practical object around which dependence is organized. On this reading, invoking Allah reorders allegiance before the hoarder's rival permanence-system appears.

3. **Tenderness as the anti-pressure mechanism.** The doubled fixed root `ر ح م` activates `ر ح م B001` (mercy, tenderness, and beneficence), while its relational branch `ر ح م B002` (kinship bond) is made newly salient by the social injury of `ه م ز B004` and `ل م ز B001` in `104:1`. The repetition of `رَّحْمَٰنِ` and `رَّحِيمِ` supplies sustained rather than incidental tenderness; I infer that tenderness blocks the movement from noticing another's defect to pressing that person socially. The opening consequently reads as a relational norm against the pressure-system condemned immediately afterward, not merely as two descriptive epithets.

### Retrospective surprises

`104:3` unexpectedly sharpened `ء ل ه B001`: the claim that wealth causes permanence made the opening invocation look like a prior correction of practical dependence, not just a formal preface. Within this ayah's clipped window, the repeated naming of `ٱلْحُطَمَةِ` in `104:4`-`104:5` also set up a striking counter-repetition to the two `ر ح م` forms. That later pair corrected an initially generic mercy-reading: doubled tenderness now stands as the opening relational alternative to a repeatedly named breaking mechanism.

## 104:1: وَيْلٌۭ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍۢ لُّمَزَةٍ

Focus window: `104:0`-`104:6`.

### Activated readings

1. **Speech functioning as applied force.** Fixed `هُمَزَةٍ` has `ه م ز B004` (taunting and backbiting), but `ه م ز B001` (squeezing by hand) and `B003` (prodding or impelling) become active beside fixed `لُّمَزَةٍ`, whose `ل م ز B001` is covert fault-finding and whose `B002` is pushing or striking. `104:4` then supplies casting (`ن ب ذ B001`) into the breaker (`ح ط م B001`). The packet supplies a repeated lexical bridge from social injury to bodily pressure and then material destruction; I infer that verbal disparagement transfers force to its target. The two nouns thus read as operators in a pressure mechanism, not as harmless speech-types.

2. **A spectrum from covert attack to exposed confrontation.** Fixed `ل م ز B001` specifically includes eye, mouth, and hidden-speech signaling, whereas fixed `ه م ز B003` supplies overt prodding and fixed `ه م ز B004` supplies public disparagement. Later `104:4` activates `ن ب ذ B002`, open repudiation or confrontation. The sequence is supplied, but I infer the escalation: concealed signaling licenses increasingly exposed force until the aggressor is himself openly repudiated. The paired agent-nouns therefore retain distinct roles rather than collapsing into synonyms: one covertly marks a defect, while the other drives the marked person through repeated pressure.

3. **Exploratory edge-reversal inside `لِّكُلِّ`.** The fixed quantifier is directly `ك ل ل B003` (totality), encompassing each habitual attacker; the dormant `ك ل ل B001` image of a blade or tongue losing sharpness is activated by the two speech-agents and by the later breaker `ح ط م B001`. Grammar supplies universal scope, while the transfer from the dulling branch to the agents is my abductive move. The line can consequently carry an irony: those who perform social sharpness are comprehensively placed under a process that removes edge and breaks the instrument. This remains exploratory because `كُلِّ` itself straightforwardly realizes totality, not dullness.

### Retrospective surprises

`104:4` made the physical branches of both fixed roots harder to dismiss: casting into `ح ط م B001` completes the movement already latent in squeezing, prodding, pushing, and striking. `104:6` then added sustained kindling rather than a single impact. The surprise was that the pair maps not merely two speech habits but a scalable force-process; the covert specificity of `ل م ز B001` still prevents the two nouns from being flattened into one sense.

## 104:2: ٱلَّذِى جَمَعَ مَالًۭا وَعَدَّدَهُۥ

Focus window: `104:0`-`104:7`.

### Activated readings

1. **Counting as a machine for manufacturing permanence.** Fixed `جَمَعَ` realizes `ج م ع B001` (collecting scattered parts into one whole), fixed `مَالًا` realizes `م و ل B001` (acquired wealth), and fixed `عَدَّدَهُ` realizes `ع د د B001` (enumeration). `104:3` adds `ح س ب B001` (reckoning), `ح س ب B002` (supposition), and `خ ل د B001` (enduring permanence). The packet supplies the exact chain gather, count, reckon, permanence; I infer the causal arrow by which repeated enumeration turns a stock into apparent evidence of endless duration. The ayah therefore reads as the construction of a false predictive model, not only as possession plus a repetitive habit.

2. **The hoard as defensive equipment.** Fixed `ع د د B002` supplies preparing equipment, stores, arms, or wealth for a coming need. That latent branch is activated by the abrupt corrective and emphatic passive casting in `104:4`, `ن ب ذ B001`. The packet gives stored wealth followed by an event for which it proves useless; I infer that the counting also functions as rehearsal and provisioning against contingency. `وَعَدَّدَهُ` can thus read as an attempt to make wealth ready as protection, with the next movement exposing the reserve's inability to block reversal.

3. **Exploratory closed-hand accumulation becoming fuel.** Alongside fixed `ج م ع B001`, `ج م ع B005` images the clenched hollow of the hand; it is activated backward by the squeezing image of `ه م ز B001` in `104:1` and forward by `و ق د B002` in `104:6`, material consumed as fuel. The packet supplies pressure, gathering, wealth, and a kindled fire, but it never states that the wealth is fuel; that conversion is my inference. The fixed act of gathering can therefore be read materially as a closed-handed compression that stockpiles what will feed, rather than prevent, the consuming mechanism. This reading is exploratory because the fuel identity is structurally suggested rather than grammatically asserted.

### Retrospective surprises

`104:3` confirmed that enumeration feeds a mental reckoning, but `104:6`-`104:7` supplied the less expected correction: the trajectory ends in kindling and an inner-kindling heart, not in an external balance sheet. This strengthened the reading of counting as a whole permanence-producing economy. It also left the closed-hand-to-fuel reading explicitly exploratory, since the later ayat provide a fuel slot and inward endpoint but never identify wealth itself as the fuel.

## 104:3: يَحْسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُۥٓ أَخْلَدَهُۥ

Focus window: `104:0`-`104:8`.

### Activated readings

1. **Calculation collapsing into supposition.** Fixed `يَحْسَبُ` holds together `ح س ب B001` (counting and reckoning) and `ح س ب B002` (mental supposition); fixed `مَالَهُ` is `م و ل B001`, and fixed `أَخْلَدَهُ` activates `خ ل د B001` (making something endure). The immediately preceding `عَدَّدَهُ`, `ع د د B001`, supplies the numerical input. The packet gives counting followed by supposition; I infer that the former licenses the latter. The ayah now diagnoses a category error in which a finite count is treated as proof of unbounded duration.

2. **Wealth imagined as sufficient against change.** Fixed `يَحْسَبُ` also activates `ح س ب B003` (sufficiency and enoughness) in proximity to fixed wealth, `م و ل B001`, and fixed permanence, `خ ل د B001`. `104:4`-`104:6` then supply casting, breaking, and kindling, all processes that wealth fails to stop. The packet supplies the claimed cause and its material defeat; I infer that the suppressed premise is "my wealth is enough." The reading changes from a mistaken forecast to a claim of self-sufficiency against transformation and judgment.

3. **Permanence as an inwardly settled thought.** Fixed `أَخْلَدَهُ` activates `خ ل د B004`, a thought settled in the heart, when `104:7` names the inner-kindling heart through `ف ء د B002` and says the fire rises upon it through `ط ل ع B003` (overlooking and disclosing an interior). The packet supplies the belief and the later heart-directed disclosure; I infer that what the fire exposes is the permanence-calculus lodged inwardly. The ayah consequently reads not only as an erroneous proposition but as a settled interior orientation that the later fire reaches.

4. **A reversal from chosen clinging to imposed staying.** Fixed `أَخْلَدَهُ` further activates `خ ل د B002` (clinging, settling, and remaining close). `104:4` casts the subject away, while `104:8` seals the fire over them with `و ص د B001`. Those spatial facts are supplied; I infer the reversal linking them: attachment to wealth does not secure free continuity but culminates in immobilized continuity inside closure. The causative phrase can therefore read as wealth training its owner to cling, a habit whose promised permanence returns as confinement.

### Retrospective surprises

`104:7` was the decisive lexical surprise: `ف ء د B002` made `خ ل د B004`, settled thought in the heart, directly functional rather than merely associative. `104:8` then corrected a purely temporal reading of permanence by adding sealed spatial arrest. Together they preserve two distinct activations of the fixed causative: wealth settles a permanence-belief inwardly, and chosen clinging is reversed into imposed staying.

## 104:4: كَلَّا ۖ لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ فِى ٱلْحُطَمَةِ

Focus window: `104:0`-`104:9`.

### Activated readings

1. **The gatherer becomes the discarded object.** Fixed `لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ` realizes `ن ب ذ B001`, casting something away from the hand and from regard. It reverses prior `جَمَعَ`, `ج م ع B001`, which draws scattered property into the possessor's control, and the emphatic passive removes that possessor's agency. The packet supplies gathering followed by casting; I infer an exchange of grammatical roles in which the collector is handled as an item. The ayah therefore reads as a precise status-reversal: one who constituted worth by retaining things is himself removed both from control and from regard.

2. **Covert repudiation becomes open repudiation.** Fixed `ن ب ذ B002` carries openly declaring separation or confrontation to an opponent. That branch is activated by the earlier contrast between covert fault-finding, `ل م ز B001`, and disparaging attack, `ه م ز B004`. The packet supplies concealed social aggression and then emphatic casting; I infer that the latter makes rejection public and unmistakable. `لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ` can thus mean more than physical hurling: the one who informally expelled others from social worth is formally and openly repudiated.

3. **A conversion from claimed durability to debris.** Fixed `ٱلْحُطَمَةِ` activates `ح ط م B001`, breaking dry or hard matter until it crumbles, and `ح ط م B009`, worldly display and wealth already viewed as perishing debris. Prior `مَالًا`, `م و ل B001`, and `أَخْلَدَهُ`, `خ ل د B001`, supply the claimed durable stock. The text gives wealth, imagined permanence, and then the breaker; I infer the conversion arrow from apparently hard reserve to exposed fragment. The destination consequently reads as a material truth-test that makes both wealth and the identity built on it assume their latent debris-state.

4. **The Crusher as a carceral pressure-agent.** Fixed `ح ط م B004` images a harsh driver who crowds livestock together or denies pasture, while `ح ط م B006` images a mass crushing what it meets. Later `مُّؤْصَدَةٌ`, `و ص د B001`, and `عَمَدٍ مُّمَدَّدَةٍ`, `ع م د B003` plus `م د د B001`, supply sealing and extended structural members. The packet does not explicitly say crowding causes the crushing; I infer that enclosure prevents dispersion and converts a gathered mass into mutual pressure. `فِى ٱلْحُطَمَةِ` then names not a passive location alone but an active confinement apparatus that drives, compacts, and breaks.

### Retrospective surprises

`104:8`-`104:9` retrospectively changed `فِى ٱلْحُطَمَةِ` from a destination into engineered confinement. Sealing and extended columns give `ح ط م B004` and `B006` a visible pressure-mechanism: occupants cannot disperse, so driving and crowding can become crushing. The later architecture also strengthens the contrast with `ن ب ذ B001`: the initial throw is free movement only until it terminates in a space designed to prevent any further movement.

## 104:5: وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْحُطَمَةُ

Focus window: `104:0`-`104:9`.

### Activated readings

1. **Knowledge delivered as an operational sequence.** Fixed `أَدْرَىٰكَ` realizes `د ر ي B001`, knowing and causing another to know. The repeated question is followed by fire (`104:6`), inward arrival (`104:7`), sealing (`104:8`), and extended columns (`104:9`). The packet supplies that ordered answer; I infer that each later clause defines a stage rather than merely adding attributes. The question therefore reads as an invitation to learn what `ٱلْحُطَمَةُ` does: ignite, penetrate, close, and structurally maintain pressure.

2. **Exploratory direction into the mechanism.** Fixed `أَدْرَىٰكَ` can activate `د ر ي B002`, aiming at or setting upon something, when later `ط ل ع B006` supplies an ascent or access-point and `ف ء د B003` supplies a heart reached or struck. The packet supplies the interrogative turn and the later inward route; I infer that causing knowledge also directs attention along that route, from name to interior target. On this exploratory reading, the question does not leave the reader outside an unknown object: it aims perception into the Crusher's access-path. The weak link is that the fixed causative form directly favors the knowledge branch.

3. **The Crusher as a consuming processor.** Fixed `ح ط م B008` images a grinder or digester that consumes, and this dormant branch is activated by `و ق د B001` (kindled burning), `ف ء د B001` (heat used to roast or bake), and `ف ء د B002` (the heart as inner kindling). Sealing through `و ص د B001` supplies a closed chamber. The packet gives grinding, heat, an inward object, and enclosure; I infer their coordination as processing stages. `مَا ٱلْحُطَمَةُ` can consequently ask after an apparatus that reduces and transforms its contents all the way to their inner core, not merely a blow that shatters once.

4. **A scarcity-reading that defeats stored wealth.** Fixed `ح ط م B003` directly includes a severe year, famine, or drought that breaks people and property. Its placement after gathering wealth (`ج م ع B001`, `م و ل B001`) and preparing a reserve (`ع د د B002`) makes that branch functional. The packet supplies accumulation followed by a named breaker; I infer scarcity as the contingency the reserve was meant to master but cannot. This reading coexists with the fiery one supplied next: `ٱلْحُطَمَةُ` can expose the hoarder's fantasy through an economic-ecological mechanism of depletion as well as through material crushing.

### Retrospective surprises

`104:6`-`104:9` answered the question with verbs and states rather than a synonym: kindling, inward arrival, sealing, and extension. That sequence made `د ر ي B001` causative in a stronger sense, because the packet causes knowledge by walking through the mechanism. The later fiery specification did not erase the separate `ح ط م B003` scarcity-reading; it left it as a parallel activation anchored in the repeated fixed noun and the earlier reserve-building sequence.

## 104:6: نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ

Focus window: `104:1`-`104:9`.

### Activated readings

1. **A fire that reveals and marks the inward target.** Fixed `نَارُ` directly realizes `ن و ر B002` (burning fire and branding), while activating `ن و ر B001` (illumination); fixed `ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ` realizes `و ق د B001` (kindling). `104:7` adds disclosure through `ط ل ع B003` and a heart reached through `ف ء د B003`. The genitive `ٱللَّهِ`, the fixed divine name of `ء ل ه B002`, supplies attribution, not by itself a causal theory. I infer that illumination and branding give the attributed fire a diagnostic role: it reaches, exposes, and marks the inward center rather than operating as undirected heat.

2. **A fire sustained by what was accumulated.** Fixed `ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ` activates `و ق د B002`, the material consumed as fuel. The focus window supplies gathered wealth (`ج م ع B001`, `م و ل B001`), repeated social pressure (`ه م ز B003`, `ل م ز B002`), and the kindled result. The packet never identifies a fuel, so the arrow is explicitly abductive: accumulated property and accumulated aggression are candidate inputs that sustain the consuming system they were expected to resist. The fire can therefore read as self-fed by the hoarder's own economy and practice, not as an unrelated external image.

3. **Social enmity returned as a fully kindled process.** Fixed `نَارُ` activates `ن و ر B007`, enmity flaring between people, and fixed `ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ` activates `و ق د B005`, readiness to flare in anger or conflict. `104:1` supplies the initial interpersonal spark through `ه م ز B004` and `ل م ز B001`; `104:7` moves the resulting fire to the hearts. The social-to-thermal escalation is my inferred arrow. The line can thus read as hostile relation brought to its mature energetic form, now placed under divine attribution and turned back upon its inward source.

4. **The impulse to recoil blocked by closure.** Fixed `نَارُ` also activates `ن و ر B006`, aversion, flight, and recoiling. `104:8` supplies a sealed door-state through `و ص د B001`, and `104:9` supplies extended columns through `ع م د B003` and `م د د B001`. The packet gives the aversive fire and the blocked enclosure; I infer that the natural movement away from fire is deliberately frustrated. The reading shifts from fire as a painful object to fire as the center of a trap whose architecture prevents the response it provokes.

### Retrospective surprises

`104:7` changed illumination and branding from surface effects into inward disclosure by naming the hearts as endpoint. `104:8`-`104:9` then made `ن و ر B006` unexpectedly structural: recoil is evoked by fire but defeated by sealing and bracing. The later ayat therefore corrected a free-burning image into a controlled system whose energy, target, and blocked escape are separately specified.

## 104:7: ٱلَّتِى تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ

Focus window: `104:2`-`104:9`.

### Activated readings

1. **Thermal penetration as disclosure of the interior.** Fixed `تَطَّلِعُ` activates `ط ل ع B003`, overlooking, gaining access to, and disclosing an inward matter; fixed `ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ` realizes `ف ء د B002`, the heart viewed as inner kindling. The prior kindled fire, `و ق د B001`, supplies heat, while the relative clause gives that heat an inward object. The packet supplies movement onto the hearts; I infer that reaching them also reveals the settled orientations within. The line therefore reads as diagnostic penetration: outer fire finds and discloses an already heat-like inner center.

2. **A reversed dawn inside the person.** Fixed `تَطَّلِعُ` activates `ط ل ع B001`, the rising of a luminary, in proximity to `نَارُ`, whose `ن و ر B001` is illumination and `B002` is fire. Unlike an ordinary luminary rising over a horizon, this one rises over plural hearts. The packet supplies fire, rising, and the shifted spatial object; I infer the inversion of the usual outward scene. The ayah can consequently read as an inward dawn whose illumination exposes rather than reassures, with each heart functioning as the horizon of disclosure.

3. **A targeted access-route rather than surface burning.** Fixed `تَطَّلِعُ` activates `ط ل ع B006`, ascent, approach, or access-point, and fixed `ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ` activates `ف ء د B003`, a heart struck or afflicted. The sequence from being cast into the Crusher (`104:4`), through kindling (`104:6`), to the heart, then sealing (`104:8`), supplies a one-way route. I infer that the fire uses the body or enclosure as an approach to a designated endpoint. The construction `عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ` therefore changes the fire from general atmosphere into a directed process that completes itself at the inward target.

4. **The heart as the final material in a consuming chamber.** Fixed `ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ` activates `ف ء د B001`, heat, fire, and implements or places for roasting and baking, alongside its direct heart branch `B002`. Earlier `ٱلْحُطَمَةُ` activates `ح ط م B008`, a grinder or digester, and later `مُّؤْصَدَةٌ` activates `و ص د B001`, a chamber sealed shut. The packet gives processing, heat, hearts, and closure; I infer an oven-like coordination without claiming that any single word literally names an oven. The line can thus read materially: the process does not stop at breaking the exterior but subjects the inward center to sustained transformation.

### Retrospective surprises

`104:8` made the access activated by `ط ل ع B006` one-directional: the fire reaches inward before the enclosure is sealed over its occupants. `104:9` then supplied the extended structure that maintains that asymmetry. The correction is spatially important: `تَطَّلِعُ` is not retained as unrestricted rising, but as successful ingress to the fixed heart-target without a corresponding path of egress.

## 104:8: إِنَّهَا عَلَيْهِم مُّؤْصَدَةٌۭ

Focus window: `104:3`-`104:9`.

### Activated readings

1. **Closure after penetration creates a one-way trap.** Fixed `مُّؤْصَدَةٌ` realizes `و ص د B001`, a door fastened or sealed shut. The immediately prior fire reaches the hearts through `ط ل ع B006` (an access-route) and `ف ء د B003` (a struck heart), while the pronoun and `عَلَيْهِم` place the closure over the occupants. The packet supplies inward access followed by sealing; I infer that entry remains possible precisely where exit is denied. The ayah therefore reads as the valve-stage of the mechanism, not as a static description of a closed place.

2. **A promised dwelling reversed into compulsory dwelling.** Fixed `مُّؤْصَدَةٌ` activates `و ص د B002`, the forecourt or doorway joined to a dwelling. Within the focus window, `104:3` supplies wealth (`م و ل B001`) and clinging or settling (`خ ل د B002`), while `104:4` supplies involuntary casting. The packet gives a permanence-claim, displacement, and an attached doorway; I infer a reversal from property-backed residence to residence without freedom of threshold. The closure reads as a grim house-relation: the person who expected wealth to stabilize a dwelling is made to remain where the doorway no longer functions as passage.

3. **The property-holder becomes enclosed property.** Fixed `مُّؤْصَدَةٌ` also activates `و ص د B003`, a stone pen made for livestock or property. `ٱلْحُطَمَةُ` activates `ح ط م B004`, the harsh driver who crowds livestock, while the earlier fixed `مَالَهُ`, `م و ل B001`, supplies the subject's property-system. The packet supplies pen, driver, property, and people enclosed under `عَلَيْهِم`; I infer the role reversal by which the owner is handled as guarded stock. The ayah can therefore read as dispossession more radical than loss of wealth: the former possessor becomes the contained possession.

### Retrospective surprises

`104:9` supplied the missing mechanics of `و ص د B001`: extended columns can brace what `مُّؤْصَدَةٌ` closes. This later detail changed closure from a momentary shut door into maintained infrastructure. It also sharpened the `و ص د B003` property-pen activation, because the former owner is not merely inside a boundary but held there by a reinforced containment system.

## 104:9: فِى عَمَدٍۢ مُّمَدَّدَةٍۭ

Focus window: `104:4`-`104:9`.

### Activated readings

1. **An extended fastening architecture.** Fixed `عَمَدٍ` directly realizes `ع م د B003`, concrete pillars, poles, or columns, and fixed `مُّمَدَّدَةٍ` realizes `م د د B001`, lengthwise drawing-out. Prior `مُّؤْصَدَةٌ`, `و ص د B001`, supplies a sealed door-state. The packet gives closure followed by elongated structural members; I infer that the members brace or bar the closure rather than merely decorate its surroundings. The final phrase thus reads as the mechanical completion of confinement: the seal is given stable length, leverage, and resistance.

2. **A punishment continuously supported and supplied.** Fixed `عَمَدٍ` activates `ع م د B002`, propping something with a support, while fixed `مُّمَدَّدَةٍ` activates `م د د B002`, connected increase that reinforces or supplies another thing. The nearby kindled fire supplies `و ق د B001` and its fuel-requiring branch `B002`. The packet does not state what the columns supply; I infer that their extended network maintains the enclosing and kindling system. On this reading, the phrase describes infrastructural continuity: the apparatus is propped and resupplied rather than allowed to exhaust itself.

3. **Extension converted into trapped pressure.** Fixed `عَمَدٍ` activates `ع م د B013`, blocking a stream so its flow gathers in one place, while fixed `مُّمَدَّدَةٍ` activates `م د د B003`, water flowing, swelling, and being fed by more water. Sealing in `104:8`, `و ص د B001`, makes the branch-pair spatially coherent. The packet supplies extension, blockage, and closure; the hydraulic transfer to heat and bodies is my abductive move. The phrase can therefore read as a dam-like mechanism in which continuing movement cannot disperse and instead accumulates as internal pressure.

4. **Exploratory directed lines toward the inward target.** Fixed `عَمَدٍ` activates `ع م د B001`, deliberate aiming, while fixed `مُّمَدَّدَةٍ` supplies elongated lines through `م د د B001`. `104:7` supplies hearts as the endpoint and `ط ل ع B006` as the route toward them. The columns are not grammatically said to point at the hearts, so that directional arrow is inferred. If retained, the final image is not only enclosure around persons but a deliberately aligned structure whose extensions preserve the targeting established two ayat earlier.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah exists in the packet, so no later-ayah surprise emerged. The retrospective comparison with the preceding sections nevertheless confirmed that the retained readings remain anchored to fixed `ع م د` and `م د د`: columns, support, blocked flow, and directed extension each explain a different function of the final structure without requiring a new external theme.
