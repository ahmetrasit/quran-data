# Surah 36 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

## 36:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A designation that initiates a merciful dispatch.** The fixed phrase supplies **س م و B005** (“name as raised designation”) in **سْمِ** and **ر ح م B001** (“mercy and tenderness”) in the doubled **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ**. Within the clipped window, 36:3 identifies a dispatched messenger, 36:5 calls the discourse a sending-down from **ٱلرَّحِيمِ**, and 36:6 gives that descent a warning function. Those sequence facts are packet-supplied; the arrow *invoked designation → authorization and beneficent orientation of the ensuing dispatch* is abductive. The opening consequently reads not as a detached label but as the operative naming under which warning descends as mercy.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:5 repeats **ٱلرَّحِيمِ** as the source of descent, and 36:6 gives that descent the unsettling task of warning; together they correct any soft reading of mercy by making admonition one of its operative forms.

## 36:2 — وَٱلْقُرْءَانِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Collected recitation as a restraining instrument.** **ق ر ء B001**, specifically its variant image “gathering speech into recitation,” anchors **ٱلْقُرْءَانِ**; **ح ك م B001** anchors **ٱلْحَكِيمِ** as restraint that turns back disorder. The following sequence places the messenger on a straight path (36:3–4), makes the recitation a descent (36:5), and aims it at inherited heedlessness (36:6). The packet gives collection, restraint, path, descent, and warning; the inferred arrow is *gathered utterance → reins attention back toward the path*. The adjective thus reads functionally: the recitation is “wise” by checking drift, not merely by containing abstract wisdom.
2. **A decisive recitation whose ordering becomes visible in effects.** **ح ك م B004** (“making firm, perfected, and decisive”) gives **ٱلْحَكِيمِ** a constructional role, while **ق ر ء B011** (“manner, pattern, and directed course”) supplies an exploratory secondary scope for **ٱلْقُرْءَانِ**. The ordered progression messenger → straight course → measured descent → warning (36:3–6) is supplied; the abduction is that this progression manifests the recitation's own firm directed architecture. The oath-object can therefore be heard as a discourse whose arrangement performs decisiveness.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:7 turns **ٱلْقَوْلُ** into something that becomes due, showing that the “decisive” quality in 36:2 can culminate as binding consequence when the restraining recitation is refused.

## 36:3 — إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Release into a directed task, not a status alone.** **ر س ل B001** (“being sent forth and released”) anchors **ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ**. Its placement between the gathered, restraining recitation (36:2) and the path/descent/warning sequence (36:4–6) supplies source, route, and task; the inferred arrow is *release from a sender → movement along a specified course for warning*. The assertion changes from a bare membership claim into a kinetic commission.
2. **A messenger whose pace is answered by blockage.** **ر س ل B004** (“gentleness and deliberate pace”) remains anchored in the same participle. The fixed emphatic assertion is followed, within the window, by hearers who do not believe (36:7), whose necks are forced upward (36:8), and whose sight is enclosed (36:9). Those contrasts are supplied; the abduction is that deliberate delivery exposes the resistance as located in reception rather than in hurried or defective transmission. The ayah can thus certify manner as well as mission.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:8 sets the messenger's released motion against neck-bound recipients; that later bodily asymmetry made **ر س ل B001** read more sharply as freedom into task rather than generic status.

## 36:4 — عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٍۢ مُّسْتَقِيمٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **A traversable road kept upright against bodily obstruction.** **ص ر ط B001** anchors **صِرَٰطٍ** as a road, and **ق و م B008** anchors **مُّسْتَقِيمٍ** as straightness and evenness. The preposition **عَلَىٰ** places the messenger upon it; 36:8–9 then supply neck restraint, barriers before and behind, and covered sight. The packet supplies road and obstructions; the inferred arrow is *an upright route is present, while imposed posture and blocked perception prevent traversal*. The ayah therefore distinguishes the route's integrity from the audience's incapacity to use it.
2. **A sustaining course.** **ق و م B009** (“support, mainstay, and subsistence”) gives the form **مُّسْتَقِيمٍ** a latent support function. Descent from the mighty/merciful (36:5), warning (36:6), and the later contrast of nonbelief (36:7–9) supply the relational frame; the abduction is *remaining on this course sustains the messenger's commission despite failed uptake*. “Straight” consequently also reads as structurally load-bearing.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** The neck restraint of 36:8 and the front/back barriers of 36:9 reveal that an intact straight road can coexist with a body unable to orient or move upon it, correcting any assumption that path-presence guarantees access.

## 36:5 — تَنزِيلَ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A measured placement reinforced by mercy.** **ن ز ل B004** (“placing in proper order”) gives **تَنزِيلَ** more than vertical motion; **ع ز ز B004** (“reinforcing and strengthening”) and **ر ح م B001** (“mercy and tenderness”) characterize its source. The preceding straight course (36:4) and following warning of an unwarned people (36:6) are supplied; the inferred arrow is *ordered placement + reinforcement → a warning fitted to restore attention*, with mercy specifying the intended benefit. The descent reads as calibrated intervention rather than mere transfer.
2. **Inaccessibility made accessible.** **ع ز ز B003** (“rarity and inaccessibility”) is activated in **ٱلْعَزِيزِ** by its genitive relation to **تَنزِيلَ**, while **ن ز ل B001** supplies descent. The fixed construction supplies an inaccessible source and downward arrival; 36:6 supplies recipients marked by heedlessness. The abductive arrow is *what is otherwise beyond reach descends into an address capable of warning*. This makes revelation an overcoming of distance without dissolving the source's inaccessibility.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:8–9 relocates inaccessibility from the descending message to the receivers' bound posture and covered sight; the descent reaches them, but their apparatus cannot receive what has become near.

## 36:6 — لِتُنذِرَ قَوْمًۭا مَّآ أُنذِرَ ءَابَآؤُهُمْ فَهُمْ غَٰفِلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Warning re-marks an inheritedly unmarked field.** **ن ذ ر B001** anchors both warning forms as information that awakens caution; **غ ف ل B006** gives **غَٰفِلُونَ** the image of unmarked land or a trackless road, and **ء ب و B001** anchors the parental chain. The ayah itself supplies the warning gap across fathers; 36:4 supplies a straight road and 36:9 a blocked visual field. The inferred arrow is *absence of intergenerational warning → a trackless attentional landscape; renewed warning → marks a route through it*. Heedlessness reads as inherited lack of orientation as well as mental inattention.
2. **A corporate body called to rise into an undertaking.** **ق و م B003** (“resolve and rising to undertake a matter”) is latent in **قَوْمًا**, alongside its ordinary collective scope in **ق و م B001**. The purposive **لِتُنذِرَ** supplies the summons, while 36:7–10 show the group remaining in nonbelief and immobilized perception. The packet gives call and failed response; the abduction is *warning seeks to convert a mere people-group into people who rise to act*. The fixed object is thus read against an unrealized capacity encoded in its own root.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:11 supplies the corrective exception: one who follows the reminder is actually warnable. This keeps 36:6's warning from reading as intrinsically futile and confines failure to a mode of reception.

## 36:7 — لَقَدْ حَقَّ ٱلْقَوْلُ عَلَىٰٓ أَكْثَرِهِمْ فَهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Circulating speech hardens into a due consequence.** **ق و ل B007** (“circulating talk or public report”) supplies a latent social scope for **ٱلْقَوْلُ**, while **ح ق ق B002** supplies becoming due or obligatory in **حَقَّ**. The nearby packet shows warning addressed to a people (36:6), equalized refusal (36:10), and a contrasting follower of the reminder (36:11). The inferred arrow is *repeated public refusal → the announced consequence becomes due over the majority*. The ayah reads dynamically as speech acquiring binding force through reception, not as an unexplained verbal object.
2. **Nonbelief as refusal of entrusted security.** **ء م ن B001** (“settled security and trust”) and **ء م ن B002** (“trusting assent and confirmation”) coexist in **لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ**. The restraints and barriers in 36:8–9 are supplied after the denial; the abduction is *refusal to entrust oneself to the warning leaves the hearers enclosed within their own defensive apparatus*. The negation therefore withholds both assent and the security that assent could open.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** The responsive follower in 36:11 retrospectively gives **أَكْثَرِهِمْ** real analytical force: 36:7 describes a majority hardened by reception, not an undifferentiated human condition; 36:12 then preserves the divergent traces of both responses.

## 36:8 — إِنَّا جَعَلْنَا فِىٓ أَعْنَٰقِهِمْ أَغْلَٰلًۭا فَهِىَ إِلَى ٱلْأَذْقَانِ فَهُم مُّقْمَحُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **A concealed interior disposition becomes bodily posture.** **غ ل ل B005** (“rancor hidden in the chest”) is activated behind the surface shackle sense **غ ل ل B006** in **أَغْلَٰلًا**; **ج ع ل B002** supplies transformation into a state. The preceding nonbelief (36:7) and following loss of sight (36:9) are packet facts. The inferred arrow is *inward refusal/rancor → binding at the neck → head forced away from receptive sight*, with the first causal direction supplied abductively. The image can therefore externalize an internal restraint rather than depict an unrelated punishment only.
2. **Refusal to drink as a model of refused warning.** **ق م ح B001** anchors **مُّقْمَحُونَ** in an animal raising its head away from water; **ذ ق ن B006** adds the support-under-the-chin image to **ٱلْأَذْقَانِ**. Descent and warning in 36:5–6 supply what is offered, while 36:9 supplies consequent non-seeing. The abduction is *warning is functionally drink; the collar props the chin so the offered provision cannot be taken*. The terminal participle now depicts deprivation through forced refusal.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:11's follower moves by an unseen reminder, showing that the fixed shackle is not ordinary lack of visual evidence but a posture that blocks receptive tracking; 36:12's preserved traces reinforce that contrast.

## 36:9 — وَجَعَلْنَا مِنۢ بَيْنِ أَيْدِيهِمْ سَدًّۭا وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ سَدًّۭا فَأَغْشَيْنَٰهُمْ فَهُمْ لَا يُبْصِرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **The whole temporal field is closed.** **ي د ي B008** anchors **بَيْنِ أَيْدِيهِمْ** as what lies just ahead; **خ ل ف B002** anchors **خَلْفِهِمْ** as behind; **س د د B001** supplies barriers closing breaches, and **غ ش و B001** supplies the covering veil. The symmetric repetition of **سَدًّا** is packet-supplied. The abductive arrow is *blocking ahead and behind removes both anticipation and retrospective correction → the covering becomes total non-seeing*. The ayah reads as foreclosure of temporal learning, not only enclosure in physical space.
2. **Loss of insight follows loss of relational interval.** **ب ي ن B002** supplies the interval in **مِنۢ بَيْنِ**, while **ب ص ر B002** gives **يُبْصِرُونَ** the scope of inner sight. The barriers occupy the interval that would normally connect observer and object; 36:10–11 then contrast indiscriminate nonresponse with following the reminder. The packet supplies occupied interval and contrast; the inference is *when the mediating interval is plugged, discernment cannot form*. “They do not see” therefore includes failed relational understanding.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:11 corrects a totalizing reading of non-seeing: following can proceed **بِٱلْغَيْبِ**, and 36:12 supplies traces that guide without direct sight. The blocked interval is therefore a failed relation, not the impossibility of all guidance.

## 36:10 — وَسَوَآءٌ عَلَيْهِمْ ءَأَنذَرْتَهُمْ أَمْ لَمْ تُنذِرْهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Warning and non-warning are made equivalent by the receiver.** **س و ي B001** anchors **سَوَاءٌ** as equality between two things; **ن ذ ر B001** supplies warning that should awaken caution, and **ء م ن B002** supplies trusting confirmation in the negated outcome. The explicit interrogative alternatives are packet-supplied, while 36:8–9 supply the receiving apparatus's restraint and covering. The inferred arrow is *blocked reception, not sameness in the two acts, collapses their outcomes into equivalence*. The ayah thus diagnoses an equalizing condition in the hearers rather than declaring warning intrinsically inert.
2. **An exploratory image of a fair meeting place refused.** **س و ي B006** (“middle, fairness, and an even meeting place”) is a secondary scope for **سَوَاءٌ**. The next ayah identifies the effective hearer as one who follows the reminder and fears without sight (36:11), reversing the fixed ayah's no-difference result. The supplied contrast supports the abductive claim *warning creates a meeting ground, but only responsive movement makes encounter occur*. The fixed **عَلَيْهِمْ** can consequently be heard as equality lying over them because they do not enter that meeting.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:11 immediately breaks the apparent equivalence by identifying a receiver for whom warning works; 36:15 then shows the opposing mechanism, where surface-based denial actively recreates the no-difference condition.

## 36:11 — إِنَّمَا تُنذِرُ مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلذِّكْرَ وَخَشِىَ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنَ بِٱلْغَيْبِ ۖ فَبَشِّرْهُ بِمَغْفِرَةٍۢ وَأَجْرٍۢ كَرِيمٍ

### Activated readings

1. **Following is trace-work through what cannot be seen.** **ت ب ع B003** (“stepwise tracking”) activates in **ٱتَّبَعَ**, **ذ ك ر B009** anchors **ٱلذِّكْرَ** as admonitory reminder, and **غ ي ب B001** supplies what is hidden from sight. Barriers and non-seeing in 36:8–10 precede the fixed ayah; writing, prior deeds, and remaining traces appear in 36:12. The packet supplies blindness, tracking, and traces; the inferred arrow is *the reminder supplies successive marks by which a receptive hearer moves despite absent visual access*. Following becomes active reconstruction, not passive affiliation.
2. **Good news is the first visible sign of protective covering.** **ب ش ر B007** gives **فَبَشِّرْهُ** the image of visible forerunners, while **غ ف ر B001** supplies protective covering in **مَغْفِرَةٍ** and **ك ر م B010** marks **كَرِيمٍ** as highly valued. Reverent fear occurs **بِٱلْغَيْبِ**, before the promised objects are visible. The abductive arrow is *response to an unseen source → announcement becomes an advance sign of future shielding and valued recompense*. Glad tidings therefore mediate hidden promise into present perceptibility.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:12's written deeds and remaining traces unexpectedly literalize the stepwise tracking in **ٱتَّبَعَ**; 36:15's fixation on visible human skin then clarifies why following “in the unseen” is a disciplined alternative to surface-only judgment.

## 36:12 — إِنَّا نَحْنُ نُحْىِ ٱلْمَوْتَىٰ وَنَكْتُبُ مَا قَدَّمُوا۟ وَءَاثَٰرَهُمْ ۚ وَكُلَّ شَىْءٍ أَحْصَيْنَٰهُ فِىٓ إِمَامٍۢ مُّبِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **A joined record reassembles deed and aftermath.** **ك ت ب B001** (“joining one thing to another”) underlies **نَكْتُبُ**; **ق د م B004** supplies beforeward advance in **قَدَّمُوا۟**; **ء ث ر B003** supplies the remaining sign in **ءَاثَٰرَهُمْ**; and **ح ص ي B002** supplies exhaustive encompassing count. The fixed coordination joins what agents send ahead to what remains behind them, while 36:9 had spatially opposed ahead and behind. The packet gives both poles and their inscription; the abduction is *recording stitches action to propagated consequence so neither can be isolated*. The ayah reads as causal accounting, not a flat list of entries.
2. **The manifest imam is both register and route-line.** **ء م م B009** permits **إِمَامٍ** to be a followed book, road, or construction line, and **ب ي ن B004** makes it visible/clear in **مُّبِينٍ**. The straight path of 36:4, the follower of 36:11, and the settlement example beginning in 36:13 are all packet-supplied within the window. The inferred arrow is *an encompassing record can guide because its ordered traces expose where acts lead*. The closing phrase consequently reads as a legible alignment-device as well as a repository.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** The town-example beginning at 36:13 makes the record operational: by 36:17, clear delivery has generated a traceable sequence of arrival, denial, and role separation. The “manifest imam” therefore reads as a guide through consequences, not a static archive.

## 36:13 — وَٱضْرِبْ لَهُم مَّثَلًا أَصْحَٰبَ ٱلْقَرْيَةِ إِذْ جَآءَهَا ٱلْمُرْسَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **The example is made to land as an impact.** **ض ر ب B003** anchors setting forth an example, but **ض ر ب B001** preserves the root's impact-image in **ٱضْرِبْ**; **م ث ل B011** gives **مَّثَلًا** the role of warning lesson. In the ensuing packet, arrival (36:14), denial (36:15–17), and the threat **لَنَرْجُمَنَّكُمْ** (36:18) stage increasingly literal impact. Those events are supplied; the arrow *narrated likeness → cognitive blow intended to interrupt the present hearers' pattern* is inferred. The command asks the example to act on its audience, not merely resemble something.
2. **A gathered settlement tested by incoming movement.** **ق ر ي B001** makes **ٱلْقَرْيَةِ** a gathering of people, while **ج ي ء B001** supplies arrival and **ر س ل B005** allows successive batches in **ٱلْمُرْسَلُونَ**. Two are sent and a third reinforces them in 36:14. The packet supplies gathered interior and repeated arrivals; the abduction is *successive outside address tests whether a settled collectivity can reorganize around truth*. “Companions of the town” thus names a social configuration under pressure, not a neutral location.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** The threatened **لَنَرْجُمَنَّكُمْ** in 36:18 literalizes the impact dormant in **ٱضْرِبْ**: the parable about rejected messengers itself advances toward a threatened blow, making its warning force materially visible.

## 36:14 — إِذْ أَرْسَلْنَآ إِلَيْهِمُ ٱثْنَيْنِ فَكَذَّبُوهُمَا فَعَزَّزْنَا بِثَالِثٍۢ فَقَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّآ إِلَيْكُم مُّرْسَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **A failed pair is made structurally three.** **ث ن ي B001** supplies “making two by joining one to one” in **ٱثْنَيْنِ**; **ع ز ز B004** supplies reinforcement in **فَعَزَّزْنَا**; **ث ل ث B003** supplies completing a group as the third. The sequence two → denial → strengthening by a third is explicit. The inferred arrow is *denial exposes vulnerability in reception, answered by added witness and relational reinforcement*. The third is therefore not mere arithmetic surplus but a load-bearing completion of the emissary configuration.
2. **Denial folds back what was sent toward them.** **ك ذ ب B002** anchors **فَكَذَّبُوهُمَا** as declaring persons false, while **ر س ل B001** supplies directed sending in both framing forms. The repeated **إِلَيْهِم / إِلَيْكُم** makes direction packet-visible, and 36:15 reverses the claim by denying that anything descended. The abductive arrow is *the town receives the arrival physically but turns its asserted source back*. The ayah reads as a directional struggle over whether arrival counts as dispatch.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:18 shows that adding a third witness does not mechanically produce acceptance, while 36:19 relocates failure into the recipients' excess. Reinforcement secures the mission's structure, not coercive control of response.

## 36:15 — قَالُوا۟ مَآ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا بَشَرٌۭ مِّثْلُنَا وَمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنُ مِن شَىْءٍ إِنْ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا تَكْذِبُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Visible skin is made an illegitimate ceiling on origin.** **ب ش ر B001** supplies visible outer skin and **ب ش ر B002** the visibly skinned human in **بَشَرٌ**; **م ث ل B001** supplies counterpart likeness in **مِّثْلُنَا**. The speakers infer from visible equivalence that no **ن ز ل B002** sending-down occurred; 36:16 answers with the Lord's knowledge, and 36:20 supplies another ordinary man who confirms the messengers. The packet supplies surface likeness and the counterexample; the arrow *same visible embodiment → impossible transcendent commission* belongs to the speakers and is exposed as unsound. Their claim reads as a category error that confuses carrier surface with message source.
2. **“Nothing” erases a presented, knowable entity.** **ش ي ء B001**, specifically “knowable thing,” anchors **شَىْءٍ**, while **ك ذ ب B001** anchors terminal falsehood against truth. The messengers have already arrived and spoken (36:14), and they restate a manifest delivery in 36:16–17. The abductive relation is *denial relabels an available communicative object as non-entity*. The sweeping **مِن شَىْءٍ** thus performs erasure rather than offering a measured evidentiary judgment.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** The ordinary **رَجُلٌ** arriving in 36:20 accepts the same messengers, decisively refuting the inference that shared human embodiment blocks transcendent dispatch. Human likeness becomes the medium of confirmation as well as the objectors' reason for denial.

## 36:16 — قَالُوا۟ رَبُّنَا يَعْلَمُ إِنَّآ إِلَيْكُمْ لَمُرْسَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Knowledge is invoked as a mark that survives rejected speech.** **ع ل م B001** anchors knowing in **يَعْلَمُ**, while **ع ل م B002** supplies the latent image of a distinguishing mark; **ر س ل B002** anchors messenger/message. Human speech has just labeled them liars (36:15), and their own task is limited to manifest delivery (36:17). The packet supplies conflicting utterances and divine knowledge; the inferred arrow is *the Lord's knowing marks the commission independently of local verbal recognition*. The oath-like assertion shifts validation from social acceptance to an enduring source-mark.
2. **Lordship nurtures the mission through rejection.** **ر ب ب B002** (“repair, nurture, and completion”) activates in **رَبُّنَا**, and **ر س ل B005** supplies emissaries in succession. Reinforcement by a third precedes (36:14), while threats and a new supporter follow (36:18–20). The supplied sequence shows replacement and continuation; the abductive arrow is *nurturing lordship maintains the mission stage by stage when one configuration meets resistance*. “Our Lord” therefore names the sustaining relation behind persistence.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** 36:20 supplies a supporter from the city's edge, and 36:21 confirms his no-wage alignment. The later arrival makes the Lord's nurturing of the mission visible through uptake beyond the original emissary group.

## 36:17 — وَمَا عَلَيْنَآ إِلَّا ٱلْبَلَٰغُ ٱلْمُبِينُ

### Activated readings

1. **Delivery is measured by arrival, not acceptance.** **ب ل غ B002** anchors conveying in **ٱلْبَلَٰغُ**, and **ب ل غ B001** adds reaching an endpoint; **ب ي ن B005** anchors **ٱلْمُبِينُ** as disclosure by expression. The town's denial precedes, but a man at the farthest city edge arrives already urging others to follow (36:20–21). Those are packet facts; the inferred arrow is *clear conveyance reaches its functional endpoint once it generates intelligible uptake somewhere, even if the collective rejects it*. The fixed limitation of responsibility becomes a transmission criterion, not a retreat.
2. **Manifestness is separation of roles.** **ب ي ن B001** supplies parting/separation beneath **ٱلْمُبِينُ**. The syntax sharply limits what is “upon us,” while 36:18–19 assigns threat and excess to the townspeople. The packet supplies this role contrast; the abduction is *clarity includes keeping sender-duty separate from receiver-response*. The ayah reads as an ethical boundary around communication.

### Retrospective surprises

**Later surprise.** The man from the farthest city edge in 36:20 arrives already carrying the message, and 36:21 restates its followability. This is concrete retrospective evidence that **ٱلْبَلَٰغُ** reached an endpoint despite collective rejection.

## 36:18 — قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّا تَطَيَّرْنَا بِكُمْ ۖ لَئِن لَّمْ تَنتَهُوا۟ لَنَرْجُمَنَّكُمْ وَلَيَمَسَّنَّكُم مِّنَّا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **An omen externalizes the speakers' own volatile agitation.** **ط ي ر B005** gives **تَطَيَّرْنَا** the image of unleashed sharpness and recklessness, while **ن ه ي B003** gives the root of **تَنتَهُوا۟** reason that restrains wrong. The speech moves from claimed omen to demanded cessation to threats of stoning and pain. That escalation is supplied; the inferred arrow is *internal agitation is projected onto the messengers, then converted into coercion*. The omen reads diagnostically as the crowd's flighty loss of restraint.
