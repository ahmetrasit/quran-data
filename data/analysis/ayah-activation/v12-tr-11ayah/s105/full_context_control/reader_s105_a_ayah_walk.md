# Surah 105 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Reader Walk

The clipped eleven-ayah focus window contains all six packet entries for every fixed ayah.

## 105:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **The opening designation becomes an agency frame.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `بِسْمِ` carries `س م و` B005, “name as raised designation,” while `ٱللَّهِ` carries `ء ل ه` B002, the fixed divine name in invocation formulas. **Structural cue:** the designation precedes a chain in which `رَبُّكَ` is attached to `فَعَلَ`, then to the transformations `يَجْعَلْ` and `جَعَلَهُمْ` and the dispatch `أَرْسَلَ`. **Assigned role:** B005 identifies whose agency governs that chain; B002 gives the naming an invocatory force. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the opening position and later divine predicates, while I infer that the initial name scopes the ensuing causal sequence. **Reading change:** the basmalah reads not as a detachable preface but as an attributional lens through which every later reversal is viewed.

2. **Mercy becomes preventive, completing governance.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` repeats `ر ح م` B001, “mercy and tenderness”; contextual `رَبُّكَ` activates `ر ب ب` B002, “repair, nurture, and completion.” **Structural cue:** the doubled mercy immediately precedes a process that places `كَيْدَهُمْ` in `تَضْلِيلٍ`, dispatches agents, and carries the intervention through to a final changed state. **Assigned role:** B001 supplies the governing disposition, while B002 supplies sustained administration rather than a momentary reaction. **Abductive move:** the packet states the disabling of the plot but not its beneficiary; I infer only that blocking a harmful operation can be the protective action of one who tends and completes. **Reading change:** mercy is activated as decisive prevention and ordered care, not only as gentle affect.

3. **The name acquires an exploratory overhead-cover image.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `بِسْمِ` is directly anchored in `س م و` B005, but the same root’s dormant B004 is “overhead sky and cover.” **Structural cue:** later `أَرْسَلَ عَلَيْهِمْ طَيْرًا` establishes an above/below relation, followed by downward casting and a result on the group beneath. **Assigned role:** B004 spatializes the raised designation as an upper governing frame, while B005 keeps the reading anchored to “name.” **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the aerial relation; I infer a correspondence between that physical arrangement and the opening name’s textual position. This is exploratory because `ٱسْم` itself directly realizes B005, not B004. **Reading change:** the invocation can also be heard as placing the narrated action under an overseeing name whose elevated position is later made concrete.

### Retrospective surprises

105:2 and 105:5 unexpectedly made the doubled mercy temporal and procedural: the plot is first deprived of efficacy, then its owners are brought to a terminal state. More surprisingly, 105:3-4 made the dormant overhead-cover image of `س م و` B004 visible through `عَلَيْهِمْ`, aerial agents, and downward casting. The severity of 105:5 also corrected any easy equation of mercy with softness; the retained claim is narrower—preventive governance inferred from stopping the presented plot, without inventing an unnamed beneficiary.

## 105:1 — أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصْحَٰبِ ٱلْفِيلِ

### Activated readings

1. **Seeing becomes causal recognition.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `تَرَ` carries `ر ء ي` B001, “seeing by eye or insight,” and B002, “opinion and reflective judgment.” **Structural cue:** the interrogative `كَيْفَ` is followed by an ordered answer: the plot is displaced, aerial groups are sent, they cast stones, and the actors reach a new material state. **Assigned role:** B001 makes the outcome perceptible, while B002 makes the sequence an object of reasoning. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies effects and their order; I infer that those effects make the otherwise unstated operation cognitively visible. **Reading change:** `أَلَمْ تَرَ` need not be restricted to eyewitness sight; it asks the addressee to reconstruct agency from a causal demonstration.

2. **The broad verb `فَعَلَ` unfolds into a staged operation.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `فَعَلَ` carries `ف ع ل` B001, “bringing an act into being”; contextual `ج ع ل` B002 is “turning something into a state,” and `ر س ل` B001 is dispatch/release. **Structural cue:** the initially unspecified act is subsequently distributed over two `جعل` clauses with `أرسل` and `ترميهم` between them. **Assigned role:** B001 names the whole act, the two state-transformations mark its abstract and material endpoints, and dispatch supplies the intervening means. **Abductive move:** the packet gives the sequence; I infer that the later clauses unpack the earlier `فَعَلَ` rather than merely list unrelated events. **Reading change:** the Lord’s act reads as an organized process that changes strategy first and actors last, not as one undifferentiated blow.

3. **`رَبُّكَ` is activated as completing care as well as authority.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `رَبُّكَ` carries `ر ب ب` B001, “lordship, ownership, and authority,” and B002, “repair, nurture, and completion”; the opening repeats `ر ح م` B001. **Structural cue:** the second-person bond in `رَبُّكَ` stands between the invitation to see and the completed chain of intervention. **Assigned role:** B001 identifies the authoritative actor; B002 explains the actor’s sustained management; contextual mercy supplies the disposition under which that management is read. **Abductive move:** the packet does not name what is being preserved, so I infer only that defeating the presented plot is maintenance within the Lord-addressee relation. **Reading change:** `رَبُّكَ` becomes the title of an involved governor who carries a counter-process to completion, not merely a rank of power.

4. **The elephant label contains an internal reversal of judgment.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ٱلْفِيلِ` directly realizes `ف ي ل` B004, “the elephant,” while dormant B001 is “slackness and weakness,” specifically weak or mistaken judgment. **Structural cue:** the group named by the elephant is immediately followed by `كَيْدَهُمْ` being placed in `تَضْلِيلٍ`. **Assigned role:** B004 supplies the group’s conspicuous material affiliation; B001 supplies a latent cognitive defect exposed by the failure of its strategy. **Abductive move:** I allow a dormant branch of the same root to color the animal label because the next ayah explicitly evaluates the group’s planning; this paronomastic link is exploratory, not a replacement of “elephant.” **Reading change:** the apparent emblem of massive force simultaneously forecasts failed discernment.

5. **Companionship becomes operational coupling.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `أَصْحَٰبِ` carries `ص ح ب` B001, “companionship and close association,” including possession or charge. **Structural cue:** the plural possessive in `كَيْدَهُمْ` treats the elephant-associated people as one planning body, while `طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ` later presents a counter-collective organized in groups. **Assigned role:** B001 binds people, animal power, and plan into one operating formation. **Abductive move:** the packet gives association and a collective strategy; I infer that the association is functional rather than a loose label. **Reading change:** `أَصْحَابِ ٱلْفِيلِ` reads as a coupled human-animal apparatus confronted by a differently organized animal collective.

### Retrospective surprises

105:2 supplied the sharpest surprise: `كَيْدَهُمْ ... تَضْلِيلٍ` activated the weak-judgment branch `ف ي ل` B001 inside the otherwise literal elephant label. Then 105:5’s repeated `ج ع ل` B002 retrospectively organized `فَعَلَ` into paired state changes, while 105:3’s grouped birds showed that `أَصْحَاب` names one operational collective facing another rather than a merely incidental association.

## 105:2 — أَلَمْ يَجْعَلْ كَيْدَهُمْ فِى تَضْلِيلٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **The first `جعل` begins a two-stage transformation.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `يَجْعَلْ` carries `ج ع ل` B002, “turning something into a state”; 105:5 repeats the same root and branch in `فَجَعَلَهُمْ`. **Structural cue:** the first construction takes `كَيْدَهُمْ` into the state-space `فِى تَضْلِيلٍ`, while the final construction takes `هُمْ` into the comparison `كَعَصْفٍ مَّأْكُولٍ`. **Assigned role:** the first B002 disables the collective’s operative design; the second makes the collective itself bear a material result. **Abductive move:** sequence is supplied, but I infer the directional arrow that strategic neutralization precedes and enables bodily/material reduction. **Reading change:** this ayah becomes the first half of a deliberate conversion pipeline: plan first, planners last.

2. **`كَيْد` expands from covert plotting into force-intensive battle.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `كَيْدَهُمْ` carries `ك ي د` B002, “plotting and stratagem,” but also B001, “forceful handling,” and B004, “war and battle.” **Structural cue:** the plot belongs to the elephant-associated collective and is answered by dispatched formations, projectiles, and a destroyed end-state. **Assigned role:** B002 supplies design, B001 supplies exerted implementation, and B004 supplies the martial scale of the encounter. **Abductive move:** the packet never explicitly labels the elephant association an army; I infer martial operation from the convergence of collective plan, counter-dispatch, targeting, and destruction. **Reading change:** `كَيْدَهُمْ` can denote a materially implemented campaign as well as cunning intent.

3. **`تَضْلِيل` becomes a reversal of aim.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `تَضْلِيلٍ` carries `ض ل ل` B001, “going astray from guidance and aim”; contextual `تَرْمِيهِم` carries `ر م ي` B001, physical projectile casting, and B006, progressing until a limit is reached. **Structural cue:** their operation is made to lose its aim, whereas the later projectiles are grammatically directed at `هُمْ` and are followed by a resulting transformation. **Assigned role:** B001 removes effective trajectory from the plot; the `ر م ي` branches give the counter-action a target and endpoint. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies direction toward them and the final result; I infer successful arrival and contrast it with their lost aim. **Reading change:** `تَضْلِيل` acquires an operational and almost ballistic precision: one trajectory is derailed while the counter-trajectory reaches its mark.

4. **Misguiding also becomes disappearance into residue.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `تَضْلِيلٍ` activates `ض ل ل` B002, “being hidden or disappearing,” and B003, “losing or failing to locate something”; later `مَّأْكُولٍ` activates `ء ك ل` B006, “being eaten away, decay,” beside `ع ص ف` B001, broken crop residue. **Structural cue:** the abstract loss of the plot precedes a simile of matter whose usable substance has been consumed and fragmented. **Assigned role:** B002-B003 remove the plot from effective presence; the final erosion image gives that removal a material analogue. **Abductive move:** the packet does not state that the plot physically decays; I infer that the final state concretizes the earlier abstract disappearance. **Reading change:** `فِى تَضْلِيلٍ` means more than wrong direction: the scheme is made to vanish as an effective, recoverable thing.

### Retrospective surprises

105:4 made the “aim” component of `ض ل ل` B001 newly concrete: the plot loses direction just before a cast explicitly directed at `هُمْ` reaches a result. 105:5 then supplied two retrospective activations at once—the repeated `ج ع ل` B002 revealed a plan-to-planners transformation, and eaten chaff activated `ض ل ل` B002-B003 as disappearance or unrecoverable loss rather than error alone.

## 105:3 — وَأَرْسَلَ عَلَيْهِمْ طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ

### Activated readings

1. **Dispatch becomes a distributed wave structure.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `أَرْسَلَ` carries `ر س ل` B001, “being sent forth and released,” and B005, “succession in batches”; fixed `أَبَابِيلَ` carries `ء ب ل` B003, “successive or scattered groups.” **Structural cue:** the singular dispatch verb governs a collective `طَيْرًا` whose adjective specifies multiple separate or successive bands. **Assigned role:** B001 supplies a single commissioning source, while the convergence of `ر س ل` B005 and `ء ب ل` B003 supplies repeated deployment. **Abductive move:** the packet gives one sender and grouped succession; I infer coordination across the separate bands. **Reading change:** `أَرْسَلَ` reads as an organized, scalable release in waves rather than one undifferentiated flock movement.

2. **Airborne lightness carries a latent burden downward.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `طَيْرًا` carries `ط ي ر` B001, “airborne lightness and birds”; fixed `أَبَابِيلَ` directly realizes `ء ب ل` B003 but activates dormant B004, “heaviness and burden”; contextual `حِجَارَةٍ` carries `ح ج ر` B003, “hard stone.” **Structural cue:** `عَلَيْهِمْ` places the light collective above the target, and the next ayah gives that collective hard projectiles. **Assigned role:** `ط ي ر` B001 supplies mobility, `ء ب ل` B004 supplies consequential weight, and the stones make that weight concrete. **Abductive move:** the aerial position and payload are supplied; I infer that the dormant burden branch colors `أبابيل` as bearers of weight. This is exploratory because B003 is the adjective’s direct group sense. **Reading change:** the birds’ lightness is not the opposite of force; it is the delivery system by which weight is imposed from above.

3. **Organized dispersion becomes the cause of destructive dispersion.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `طَيْرًا` activates `ط ي ر` B002, “spreading as if flying loose,” and fixed `أَبَابِيلَ` carries `ء ب ل` B003, scattered or successive groups; later `عَصْفٍ` activates `ع ص ف` B002, wind that scatters and breaks things, and B004, sweeping destructive force. **Structural cue:** the agents arrive dispersed yet ordered, then the target is compared with fragmented crop matter. **Assigned role:** the two fixed-ayah branches describe controlled distribution of the agents; the later `ع ص ف` branches describe uncontrolled disintegration of the recipients. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies formation and result but not the explicit causal geometry; I infer that distributed attack coverage produces distributed ruin. **Reading change:** `طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ` becomes a mechanism of reversal: coherent scattering above generates incoherent scattering below.

### Retrospective surprises

105:4 unexpectedly gave the light birds a hard payload, activating the burden branch `ء ب ل` B004 without displacing the direct grouped sense B003. 105:5 then corrected a possible reading of “scattered groups” as disorder: their dispersion is coordinated and productive, while the target’s chaff-like dispersion is the disordered result. Thus later context turns formation pattern into causal mechanism.

## 105:4 — تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍۢ مِّن سِجِّيلٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **The cast becomes the successful counterpart to a lost aim.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `تَرْمِيهِم` carries `ر م ي` B001, physical casting at a target, and B006, progressing until a limit or state is reached; earlier `تَضْلِيلٍ` carries `ض ل ل` B001, deviation from aim. **Structural cue:** the object pronoun `هُمْ` gives the casting an explicit target, and the following `فَجَعَلَهُمْ` gives the same pronoun a resulting state. **Assigned role:** `ر م ي` B001 supplies directed launch, B006 supplies endpoint, and `ض ل ل` B001 supplies the failed counter-trajectory. **Abductive move:** contact is not separately stated; I infer successful arrival from target continuity and the immediately resulting transformation. **Reading change:** `تَرْمِيهِم` is sharpened into the precise answering motion to `تَضْلِيل`: what they direct goes astray, while what is directed at them reaches completion.

2. **Discrete projectiles coexist with a storm-like outpouring.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `تَرْمِيهِم` directly realizes `ر م ي` B001 but activates B004, cloud or heavy-rain imagery; fixed `حِجَارَةٍ` carries `ح ج ر` B003, hard stones; fixed `سِجِّيلٍ` activates `س ج ل` B001, “the filled bucket and its outpouring.” **Structural cue:** aerial groups arrive successively in 105:3 and cast plural hard units in 105:4. **Assigned role:** `ح ج ر` B003 preserves the discrete payload, while `ر م ي` B004 and `س ج ل` B001 supply the volumetric pattern of a poured aerial volley. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies no literal cloud or bucket; I infer a material analogy from airborne source, repeated groups, plurality, and outpouring branches. **Reading change:** the ayah supports both targeted bombardment and precipitation-like saturation without collapsing one into the other.

3. **The stones enact interdiction as well as impact.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `حِجَارَةٍ` directly realizes `ح ج ر` B003, hard stone, while dormant B001 is “keeping back by enclosure,” including prevention and prohibition. **Structural cue:** the stones occur after `كَيْدَهُمْ` has been placed in loss and before the group is fixed in a ruined state. **Assigned role:** B003 supplies the physical instrument; B001 supplies its functional role as the barrier that stops further execution of the plan. **Abductive move:** the packet gives impact sequence but does not explicitly say “the stones prevent”; I infer that arrow because the targeted means stands between strategic nullification and terminal transformation. **Reading change:** `حِجَارَةٍ` reads not only as damaging matter but as materialized restraint that closes the plot’s path.

### Retrospective surprises

105:5’s immediate `فَجَعَلَهُمْ` carries forward the same object pronoun and supplies the unstated endpoint of `ر م ي` B006, retrospectively confirming effective arrival. Its `ع ص ف` B002-B004 branches also made the outpouring analogy in `س ج ل` B001 more visible: the volley behaves like a scattering, sweeping force. This remains a functional analogy, not a claim that 105:4 states literal rain or wind.

## 105:5 — فَجَعَلَهُمْ كَعَصْفٍۢ مَّأْكُولٍۭ

### Activated readings

1. **The final `جعل` completes the earlier transformation at a new level.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `جَعَلَهُمْ` carries `ج ع ل` B002, “turning something into a state”; 105:2 uses the same branch in `يَجْعَلْ كَيْدَهُمْ فِى تَضْلِيلٍ`, and 105:1 supplies `ر ب ب` B002, nurture and completion. **Structural cue:** the prefixed `فَـ` presents this clause as consequence, and the repeated object-group suffix links the ruined actors to the owners of the earlier plan. **Assigned role:** the first `جعل` changes efficacy, the final B002 changes the condition of the agents, and `ر ب ب` B002 makes completion a feature of the governing action. **Abductive move:** the text supplies order and repeated morphology; I infer that the two transformations form one administered process. **Reading change:** the closing clause is not an isolated simile but the completion of a plan-to-planners sequence.

2. **The end-state is consumed structure, not merely broken matter.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `عَصْفٍ` carries `ع ص ف` B001, dry crop chaff, husks, and broken straw; fixed `مَّأْكُولٍ` directly realizes `ء ك ل` B001, ingestion, and activates B006, being eaten away or eroded. **Structural cue:** the passive adjective modifies already fragmented crop residue and leaves the consumer unstated, emphasizing condition rather than eater. **Assigned role:** `ع ص ف` B001 supplies structural fragmentation; `ء ك ل` B001 removes what was usable; B006 extends that removal into erosion of material integrity. **Abductive move:** the packet gives a comparison, not a literal account of ingestion; I infer that consumed residue models exhaustive loss after the impacts. **Reading change:** the group is likened not simply to debris but to debris whose substance has already been used up and worn through.

3. **`عَصْف` compresses destructive process into its result.** **Lexical evidence:** alongside its direct chaff sense, fixed `عَصْفٍ` activates `ع ص ف` B002, violent wind that scatters and breaks, and B004, force that sweeps a people away; contextual `ط ي ر` B002 is spreading/scattering and `س ج ل` B001 is outpouring. **Structural cue:** dispersed aerial agents and poured hard units precede the chaff comparison. **Assigned role:** B002 supplies the breakup dynamics, B004 supplies collective destruction, and B001 preserves the visible residue. **Abductive move:** no wind is stated; I infer only that the coordinated aerial volley functions like the branch’s sweeping force. **Reading change:** `كَعَصْفٍ` simultaneously presents what remains and the kind of distributed force that made it remain that way.

4. **The finale reverses mass into light, depleted residue.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `عَصْفٍ` activates `ع ص ف` B003, light and swift motion, while `مَّأْكُولٍ` activates `ء ك ل` B012, material substance or density, here under a passive form of consumption; contextual `ٱلْفِيلِ` is `ف ي ل` B004, the elephant, and `حِجَارَةٍ` is `ح ج ر` B003, hard stone. **Structural cue:** an elephant-associated formation is met by light airborne carriers of hard payloads and ends in chaff imagery. **Assigned role:** the contextual animal and stones establish concentrated weight, `ع ص ف` B003 supplies lightness, and `ء ك ل` B012 supplies the dimension of substance that the consumed condition negates. **Abductive move:** the packet does not state weights or density; I infer a material inversion from the selected objects and accepted branch images. **Reading change:** the simile measures a collapse of coherence and substance: apparent mass is converted into matter light enough to scatter.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah exists in the packet, so no later-context surprise emerged. The retrospective reread strengthened earlier-to-final links—the paired `جعل`, the aerial outpouring, and the mass-to-residue reversal—but did not supply a subsequent correction.
