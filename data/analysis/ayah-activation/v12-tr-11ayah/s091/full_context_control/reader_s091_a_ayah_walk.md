# Surah 91 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

## 91:0 - بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Invocation as placement under a raised designation.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `س م و/B005` makes `اسم` a name as raised designation; 91:5 later uses the same root through `س م و/B004`, the overhead sky and cover. **Structural cue and role:** the name stands before the sequence of sun, moon, day, night, and built sky, so the designation functions as a verbal canopy over the sequence. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the shared root and first-position framing; the claim that designation thereby places the phenomena under named authority is the inferred arrow. **Reading change:** the basmalah reads not merely as a detachable heading but as an act of situating the coming cosmic relations under the divine name.

2. **A performative threshold to an oath sequence.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ء ل ه/B002` includes the fixed divine name in invocation and oath formulas. **Structural cue and role:** `ٱللَّهِ` occurs in an opening formula immediately before five clauses led by repeated *wa-* and named cosmic witnesses; the divine name supplies the threshold from invocation into attestation. **Abductive move:** the formula and the following coordinated sequence are packet-given, while treating them as one performative movement is inferred. **Reading change:** the opening becomes an invoked warrant for the witness-chain rather than prose external to it.

3. **Mercy as sustaining order.** **Lexical evidence:** the doubled fixed root `ر ح م/B001` carries mercy, tenderness, and beneficence; contextual `ب ن ي/B001` in 91:5 supplies construction by joined parts. **Structural cue and role:** two mercy adjectives precede orderly alternation among light and darkness and then a built canopy; mercy functions as the sustaining relation that keeps opposed phases inhabitable. **Abductive move:** the packet gives repetition, alternation, and construction, but the direction "merciful care enables stable order" is inferred. **Reading change:** `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` acquires an environmental and governing force, not only an affective one.

### Retrospective surprises

91:5 was the decisive later surprise: `سماء` returned the fixed ayah's `س م و` root in a different branch, turning raised designation (`B005`) into an overhead built cover (`B004`). That recurrence sharpened the opening name into a governing canopy, while the constructed order corrected the mercy reading away from sentiment alone and toward sustained relations.

## 91:1 - وَٱلشَّمْسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Brightness as public exposure.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ض ح و/B002` is open exposure to the sun and visible prominence, while 91:3 `ج ل و/B001` uncovers and 91:4 `غ ش و/B001` covers with a veil. **Structural cue and role:** exposure is placed before an exact reveal/cover reversal, so `ضحاها` serves as the field on which visibility can be opened and shut. **Abductive move:** the optical sequence is supplied; treating exposure as the condition that makes the later reversal operative is inferred. **Reading change:** the ayah invokes not just a bright time but the sun's power to put things openly on display.

2. **An uncontained leader of the procession.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ش م س/B002` carries the dormant image of a restive being that refuses to settle or be ridden; 91:2 `ت ل و/B001` supplies bodily or sequential following. **Structural cue and role:** the sun leads, the moon follows, day manifests, and night overtakes, giving the otherwise nominal sun a mobile role. **Abductive move:** animating the celestial succession as restive motion is an exploratory inference; the weak link is that the fixed surface is the ordinary noun `الشمس`, not a restive derivative. **Reading change:** alongside the literal luminary, the sun reads as a leading force that cannot be held in place.

3. **An extended operating interval.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ض ح و/B001` is the forenoon's rising and extension; contextual `ب ن ي/B001` in 91:5 joins a structure and `ط ح و/B001` in 91:6 spreads a surface. **Structural cue and role:** duration sits inside a built-above/spread-below spatial frame, so `ضحاها` supplies the interval during which that world is exposed and usable. **Abductive move:** sky and earth are packet-given; assigning their construction as what enables the recurring interval is inferred. **Reading change:** `ضحاها` becomes organized duration within a system, not a momentary flash of light.

### Retrospective surprises

91:3-4 made the latent exposure branch of `ضحاها` unexpectedly functional: manifestation and covering showed what exposure permits. Then 91:6 supplied a spread lower field, correcting the reading from brightness alone to an interval operating across an arranged world.

## 91:2 - وَٱلْقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Successor rather than independent light.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ت ل و/B001` is physical, ranked, or sequential following; fixed `ق م ر/B001` includes both moon and moonlight. **Structural cue and role:** the moon is grammatically subordinated by `إذا تلاها` between the sun's exposure and the day/night reversal, so following assigns it a relay position. **Abductive move:** the order is explicit; construing that order as dependence within a light-cycle rather than mere chronology is inferred. **Reading change:** the moon reads as a successor carrying a prior luminous phase forward, not as an isolated object.

2. **Following already contains displacement.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ت ل و/B005` is being left behind after companionship. **Structural cue and role:** the follower is immediately succeeded by day that manifests and night that covers the same feminine referent; each companion in the sequence is in turn overtaken. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies serial replacement, while importing the branch's abandonment-image into `تلاها` is exploratory. **Reading change:** following becomes temporary participation whose structure already anticipates being outstripped.

3. **Moonlight as a remainder of what precedes.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ق م ر/B001` includes lunar light, and fixed `ت ل و/B003` is a remainder that follows what came before. **Structural cue and role:** the repeated suffix `-ها` across 91:1-4 keeps one relational field in view while manifestation and covering alternately act on it; the remainder branch lets lunar light occupy the residual phase. **Abductive move:** the shared suffix and sequence are supplied, but treating moonlight as a carried remainder rather than only a later body is inferred. **Reading change:** `تلاها` can be heard as following while preserving a luminous trace.

### Retrospective surprises

91:3-4 surprisingly converted simple following into relay and remainder: the same suffix remains while day reveals and night covers, so what follows also carries and then yields a visible trace. 91:7's proportioning further sharpened succession as ordered placement rather than accidental lateness.

## 91:3 - وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Day as an active disclosure operation.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ن ه ر/B002` is day opening with light and fixed `ج ل و/B001` is uncovering after concealment; 91:4 answers with `غ ش و/B001`, a covering veil. **Structural cue and role:** the matched constructions `إذا جلاها` and `إذا يغشاها` reverse one another around the same suffix, assigning day the role of disclosure rather than background time. **Abductive move:** the grammatical reversal is explicit; treating the two clauses as opposed operations on one visible field is inferred. **Reading change:** day does not merely coincide with visibility; it actively makes the referent manifest.

2. **Perceptual polishing before inner discrimination.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ج ل و/B002` is polishing a blade or clearing sight; 91:7 `ن ف س/B013` is inner mind and discernment, followed in 91:8 by the paired contents given to it. **Structural cue and role:** outward clearing precedes the shaping and informing of an inward perceiver, so polishing serves as a material model for legibility. **Abductive move:** the packet gives outer disclosure followed by inner discrimination; the arrow from clarified perception to readable moral distinction is analogical and inferred. **Reading change:** `جلاها` acquires the force of sharpening the capacity to see, not only increasing illumination.

3. **A controlled opening that exposes the risk of breach.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ن ه ر/B003` is opening or widening until something flows; 91:8 `ف ج ر/B001` is a wide split and outflow, alongside `ف ج ر/B004`, breach of restraint. **Structural cue and role:** the focus window moves from day's opening to a self informed of `فجورها`, linking optical aperture to moral rupture. **Abductive move:** the two opening-images are lexical data; positing controlled disclosure and uncontrolled breach as two outcomes of aperture is inferred. **Reading change:** day becomes a channel of manifestation whose mechanics also make the later danger of boundary-breaking intelligible.

### Retrospective surprises

91:7-8 shifted the disclosure mechanism inward: a formed discerning self receives opposed contents. Most surprisingly, 91:8's `فجورها` activated the outflow branch `ف ج ر/B001`, revealing that the day's controlled opening and moral breach share aperture mechanics without sharing value.

## 91:4 - وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا يَغْشَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Night as an applied veil.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `غ ش و/B001` is a covering laid over sight, heart, garment, or object; fixed `ل ي ل/B001` supplies night and its darkness. **Structural cue and role:** the clause exactly reverses 91:3's `جلاها` while retaining `إذا` and the feminine object, so the veil is an event applied to what day had disclosed. **Abductive move:** reversal and shared object are supplied; construing darkness as an operation rather than a substance is inferred. **Reading change:** night reads as active concealment, not merely absence of day.

2. **A model for obscured inner discernment.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `غ ش و/B005` is a covering of consciousness or understanding; 91:7 `ن ف س/B013` is inner mind and discernment, and 91:8 places two opposed contents within that self. **Structural cue and role:** cosmic covering precedes the formed and informed interior, giving the night-clause the role of a visible analogue for cognitive opacity. **Abductive move:** the outer-to-inner analogy is inferred; the packet does not say that night causes moral obscurity. **Reading change:** alongside literal nightfall, `يغشاها` makes intelligible how an already endowed self can have its distinctions covered over.

3. **Covering as a recurring visit, not permanent possession.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `غ ش و/B003` is coming upon or visiting a person or place. **Structural cue and role:** the repeated `إذا` clauses and the moon/day/night succession make covering one arrival within a reversible cycle; the visit branch assigns temporariness to its role. **Abductive move:** recurrence is explicit, while treating the night as a visitor is a branch-driven personification. **Reading change:** the ayah can present darkness as something that comes upon the field and then yields, preserving the possibility of renewed disclosure.

### Retrospective surprises

91:7-8 activated `غ ش و/B005` by supplying an interior that can have discernment obscured. 91:9 then corrected a possible overreading: night itself is not equated with failure; purification shows that the cover is a reversible model for opacity, not a moral verdict on darkness.

## 91:5 - وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Sky as an assembled system.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ب ن ي/B001` is building by joining parts; fixed `س م و/B004` is the overhead sky or upper cover. **Structural cue and role:** the constructed canopy follows four interlocking phases of light and darkness and precedes the spread earth, so building supplies relations and joints rather than a single inert mass. **Abductive move:** the coordinated phases and the verb of building are supplied; taking their fit as evidence of assembly is inferred. **Reading change:** `بناها` reads as integration of a working celestial order, not only erection of height.

2. **A macro-constitution that activates the self's constitution.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ب ن ي/B002` is built form or constitution; 91:7 `س و ي/B002` is internal straightness and sound completion. **Structural cue and role:** the parallel clauses `وما بناها`, `وما طحاها`, and `وما سواها` move from sky to ground to self, assigning constitution at each scale. **Abductive move:** the parallel grammar is explicit; treating the outer constructions as models for the self's structured capacity is inferred. **Reading change:** the sky-clause becomes the first stage of a nested architecture culminating in moral agency.

3. **A load-bearing cover that regulates visibility.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `س م و/B004` includes an upper covering surface and fixed `ب ن ي/B009` includes ribs, posts, and settled supports. **Structural cue and role:** the sky is both above the spread ground and the arena for alternation between manifestation and covering; the support image gives the canopy a stabilizing role. **Abductive move:** no supports are stated, so the move from `بناها` to an unseen load-bearing order is explicitly inferred from the construction relation. **Reading change:** the sky reads as a maintained enclosure that makes regulated exposure possible, not a decorative backdrop.

### Retrospective surprises

91:6-7 completed the unexpected scale sequence: spread ground and proportioned self made `ب ن ي/B002` read as constitution, not only masonry. The divergent outcomes in 91:9-10 then showed why that constitution matters: an assembled capacity can be cultivated or concealed.

## 91:6 - وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا طَحَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Ground made into capacity.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ء ر ض/B001` is the lower ground opposite the sky, and fixed `ط ح و/B001` is spreading and extending. **Structural cue and role:** the clause answers the built-above of 91:5 with a spread-below and immediately supports the formed self of 91:7; the earth therefore functions as an intentionally enlarged field of action. **Abductive move:** vertical pairing and sequence are explicit; assigning usable capacity as the purpose of spreading is inferred. **Reading change:** earth is not passive matter but prepared room in which agency can occur.

2. **A cultivation model for self-purification.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ء ر ض/B002` is soft fertile ground; 91:9 activates `ف ل ح/B003`, the tiller who splits ground, and `ز ك و/B001`, growth and increase. **Structural cue and role:** spread earth is followed by a shaped self and then by success through `زكاها`, so fertile ground supplies the material role of receiving cultivation and producing increase. **Abductive move:** the agricultural branches are supplied; the arrow "prepared ground plus cultivation enables growth" is causal inference. **Reading change:** `طحاها` begins an ethical cultivation mechanism in which the self can be tended into increase.

3. **The same expanse can become a surface of self-burial.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ء ر ض/B006` is sticking or sinking toward the ground and fixed `ط ح و/B006` is sprawling against it; 91:10 `د س و/B001` is hidden insertion or concealment. **Structural cue and role:** the paired outcomes of growth in 91:9 and concealment in 91:10 turn the ground from neutral surface into an ambivalent medium: it can host cultivation or receive what is pressed down. **Abductive move:** linking the concealed self to ground-burial is exploratory because 91:10 does not name earth; the sequence and material branch supply, but do not state, that relation. **Reading change:** the spread earth also exposes the risk that made capacity will be used to flatten and hide the self.

### Retrospective surprises

91:9 activated fertile earth through tilling and growth, while 91:10 supplied the surprising reverse use of ground as a medium of concealment. The pair corrected `طحاها` from uncomplicated spaciousness to ambivalent capacity: spreading enables both cultivation and burial.

## 91:7 - وَنَفْسٍۢ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **A completed interior apparatus of discernment.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ن ف س/B013` is inner mind, intent, and discernment, while fixed `س و ي/B002` is internal straightness and sound completion. **Structural cue and role:** `سواها` is followed directly by the informing of `فجورها وتقواها`, so the formed self functions as a receiver able to distinguish opposed contents. **Abductive move:** formation and subsequent informing are explicit; construing sound formation as enabling discrimination is the inferred causal arrow. **Reading change:** `نفس` reads as an equipped interior faculty, not merely bare life.

2. **A calibrated arena for opposed capacities.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `س و ي/B001` is equality or equivalence and `س و ي/B006` is a middle or fair position. **Structural cue and role:** the paired objects in 91:8 and the balanced success/failure clauses in 91:9-10 surround the same feminine suffix, assigning `سواها` the role of calibration. **Abductive move:** the syntactic pairings are packet-given; inferring that the self is proportioned to hold both possibilities in accountable relation is mine. **Reading change:** shaping becomes moral calibration rather than only bodily perfection.

3. **An inner horizon that can open like dawn.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ن ف س/B009` is dawn or day opening and spreading like breath; 91:8 `ف ج ر/B002` is dawn breaking from night. **Structural cue and role:** day manifests in 91:3, night covers in 91:4, the self is shaped, and then `فجورها` repeats the dawn-root; the breath-opening branch lets the self internalize the cosmic alternation. **Abductive move:** the lexical recurrence is supplied, but reading the self as an inner horizon is analogical. **Reading change:** `سواها` can include opening an interior space in which alternatives break into awareness.

4. **Exploratory: the self as the stake in a contest, but not a game of chance.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ن ف س/B016` names a gambling arrow, while 91:2 `ق م ر/B007` carries gambling and contest; 91:9-10 then present gain and loss around the same self. **Structural cue and role:** these two dormant branches sit at the edges of the focus window and the paired outcomes make the self the contested object. **Abductive move:** the morphological distance from ordinary `نفس` to `النافس` is the weak link; the later agentive verbs `زكاها` and `دساها` correct any implication of randomness. **Reading change:** tentatively, the self appears as what is at stake in a consequential contest whose result is produced by action, not luck.

### Retrospective surprises

91:8-10 confirmed that proportioning is calibration for opposed, actionable possibilities. They also corrected the exploratory gambling activation: gain and loss are not allotted by chance, because `زكاها` and `دساها` assign agency. 91:12 then made the formed self socially visible when one representative converts disposition into motion.

## 91:8 - فَأَلْهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقْوَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Two opposed boundary mechanics.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ف ج ر/B001` is a wide split and outflow and `ف ج ر/B004` is a breach of restraint; fixed `و ق ي/B001` is a barrier that wards harm and `و ق ي/B002` is placing the self in protective caution. **Structural cue and role:** `فجورها` and `تقواها` are coordinated as contents of one formed self, so the first functions as rupture and the second as boundary maintenance. **Abductive move:** the opposed branch images and syntax are supplied; modeling moral alternatives as permeability control is inferred. **Reading change:** the pair becomes an operational contrast between breaking a limit and maintaining a guard, not merely two abstract labels.

2. **Inspiration as complete inward uptake.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ل ه م/B001` is swallowing or taking something in completely. **Structural cue and role:** the causative form `ألهم` acts on the self immediately after its formation and takes two explicit objects through the pronominal construction; the swallowing image gives the self the role of an inward recipient. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the root image and causative grammar; reversing ordinary ingestion into "a source causes the self to internalize" is the inferred directional arrow. **Reading change:** inspiration reads as embodied incorporation of moral knowledge rather than information merely placed nearby.

3. **`فجور` can arrive with the phenomenology of dawn.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ف ج ر/B002` is dawn breaking from night, while `ف ج ر/B004` is moral breach. **Structural cue and role:** 91:3-4 established manifestation against covering, and `فجورها` now carries both an opening-light image and a transgressive one. **Abductive move:** coexistence of accepted branches is supplied; inferring that a breach may first present itself as disclosure or release is exploratory and does not erase the wickedness branch. **Reading change:** awareness of `فجور` includes the danger that boundary-breaking can feel like illumination.

4. **`تقوى` as a guarded gait across bounded terrain.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `و ق ي/B003` is the cautious gait of an animal protecting a hurt hoof; contextual 91:6 spreads the earth, 91:12 has a forceful setting-out, and 91:13 names a she-camel and her water allotment. **Structural cue and role:** locomotion and a vulnerable resource boundary surround the fixed ayah, so caution functions as measured movement that avoids injuring what must be protected. **Abductive move:** the movement images are packet-given; applying the animal gait materially to moral conduct is analogical. **Reading change:** `تقواها` becomes practiced restraint in how one advances through shared space, not fear held only inside.

### Retrospective surprises

91:9-10 showed that inward uptake does not determine one outcome; it equips action in either direction. The sharper later surprise came from 91:12-13: forceful setting-out followed by a she-camel's water entitlement made `و ق ي/B003` legible as guarded movement around a vulnerable boundary.

## 91:9 - قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Success as sustained cultivation.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ف ل ح/B003` is the tiller who splits earth and fixed `ز ك و/B001` is growth and increase; 91:6 supplies fertile spread ground (`ء ر ض/B002`), 91:13 a water share (`س ق ي/B003`), and 91:14 cutting the source of growth (`ع ق ر/B007`). **Structural cue and role:** ground, tending, growth, watering, and later destructive cutting form one material mechanism; `زكاها` is the cultivated yield. **Abductive move:** the elements are supplied, while the causal chain from preparation and watering to growth is inferred. **Reading change:** flourishing is a maintained ecological practice, not a one-time declaration of success.

2. **Productive cleaving distinguished from destructive cutting.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ف ل ح/B001` is opening by cleaving; 91:8 `ف ج ر/B001` is splitting into outflow, while 91:14 `ع ق ر/B002` cuts an animal down. **Structural cue and role:** the success verb stands between a potentially life-opening split and a later life-disabling cut, assigning `أفلح` the role of opening constriction without severing vitality. **Abductive move:** the text does not explicitly classify cuts; the distinction between productive and annihilative openings is inferred from their outcomes. **Reading change:** success entails knowing how to open a field for life rather than treating every barrier-breaking act as liberation.

3. **Purification as bringing the self out of cover.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ز ك و/B002` is purification and rectitude; 91:4 `غ ش و/B001` covers, 91:10 `د س و/B001` conceals, and 91:11 and 91:14 use `ك ذ ب/B002` to deny or declare false. **Structural cue and role:** `زكاها` is the positive center of a visibility sequence whose negative side hides the self and then denies an external warning. **Abductive move:** the packet gives cover, purification, concealment, and denial; treating purification as restored visibility is inferred. **Reading change:** self-purification becomes an anti-concealment practice that lets acknowledged distinctions remain visible.

### Retrospective surprises

91:13-14 supplied the missing material completion of the cultivation reading: a water share sustains the animal order, while hamstringing activates `ع ق ر/B007`, cutting off regrowth. This made flourishing depend not only on inward cleansing but on preserving the conditions from which life continues.

## 91:10 - وَقَدْ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Failure by hiding an already informed self.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `د س و/B001` is hidden insertion or concealment and fixed `خ ي ب/B001` is missing what was sought and gaining no good. **Structural cue and role:** the object is the self already formed and informed in 91:7-8, and concealment directly reverses 91:9's cultivation; hiding is therefore the mechanism that produces deprivation. **Abductive move:** sequence and opposition are explicit, while the causal arrow "concealment blocks the sought good" is inferred. **Reading change:** failure is not ignorance but the active burial of known capacity.

2. **A fire-making implement that never catches.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `خ ي ب/B002` is a fire-stick that gives no spark; 91:8 `ف ج ر/B002` carries dawn breaking from darkness, and the self has already taken its distinctions inward through `ألهمها`. **Structural cue and role:** latent light is followed by a failed outcome, so the sparkless implement models discernment that never becomes visible action. **Abductive move:** no fire is stated; mapping inspiration to tinder and action to ignition is a material analogy inferred from the branches. **Reading change:** `خاب` becomes frustrated activation: endowed potential fails to kindle.

3. **Inner spoilage becoming collective motion.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `د س و/B003` is leading astray and spoiling; 91:11 supplies collective denial, 91:12 a representative who sets out, and 91:14 the completed violent act. **Structural cue and role:** an inwardly directed verb is immediately followed by social subject, mobilization, and action, so self-corruption serves as the latent stage of public harm. **Abductive move:** the later sequence is supplied; the direction from corrupted self to collective violence is inferred rather than stated. **Reading change:** `دساها` has a social causal horizon, not only a private inward one.

4. **Failure disclosed by its eventual aftermath.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `خ ي ب/B001` is deprivation of the sought result; 91:15 `ع ق ب/B006` is the end or aftermath an act brings after it. **Structural cue and role:** `خاب` precedes a narrative whose consequence closes on `عقباها`, so the end functions as the delayed proof of failure. **Abductive move:** the packet gives initial verdict and final outcome; taking the latter as evidentiary disclosure of the former is inferred. **Reading change:** failure is measured by what the act ultimately leaves behind, not by its immediate appearance.

### Retrospective surprises

91:11-14 exposed concealment as a social sequence: denial, mobilization, warning rejected, and bodily harm. 91:12 initially resembles ignition, but its destructive direction corrects the spark image: motion alone is not successful activation. 91:15 finally verifies `خاب` by naming the aftermath in which the failed trajectory is measured.

## 91:11 - كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ بِطَغْوَىٰهَآ

### Activated readings

1. **Transgressive overflow producing enacted denial.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ك ذ ب/B002` is declaring a person or report false and fixed `ط غ ي/B001` is overstepping a bound in rebellion. **Structural cue and role:** `بطغواها` grammatically attaches transgression to the denial; the warning is voiced in 91:13 and `فكذبوه فعقروها` in 91:14 repeats denial before bodily action. **Abductive move:** attachment and repetition are supplied; the arrow "habitual overstepping makes the warning intolerable and therefore denied" is inferred. **Reading change:** `كذبت` is not an isolated opinion but the epistemic operation by which rebellion protects itself and becomes action.

2. **A water-boundary overrun.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ط غ ي/B002` is water or another force rising beyond measure and overwhelming; 91:13 names the she-camel's watering through `س ق ي/B003`, an irrigation share, and 91:14 brings eradication through `د م د م/B001`. **Structural cue and role:** transgression is followed by an allocated water relation, its violation, and an overwhelming counter-event, so overflow functions as seizure beyond one's share. **Abductive move:** the water share is explicit, but reading `طغواها` materially as resource overrun is inferred from the branch. **Reading change:** Thamud's rebellion becomes a concrete failure to remain within a life-sustaining allocation.

3. **Making the she-camel's sustaining yield "lie."** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ك ذ ب/B006` is a she-camel's milk failing to last as expected; 91:13 supplies `ناقة` and, through `ر س ل/B006`, a dormant image of milk and continuous yield, before 91:14 disables her. **Structural cue and role:** the generic denial in 91:11 is retrospectively given an animal and a sustaining flow, then the community itself makes that flow fail. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the rare milk branch and later she-camel; construing human violence as causatively realizing the expectation-failure is inferred. **Reading change:** denial becomes the practical destruction of a promised provision, not only rejection of a proposition.

4. **Rebellion as an alternative command structure.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ط غ ي/B003` is a false authority or head of deviation; 91:12 raises `أشقاها`, while 91:13 repeats `ٱللَّهِ` around the protected she-camel. **Structural cue and role:** a superlative individual emerges from the collective just before divine ownership is announced, giving the false-authority branch a social role opposed to the messenger's command. **Abductive move:** the text does not title the individual a leader; construing him as the operative head of collective deviation is inferred from sequence and action. **Reading change:** `طغواها` includes a rival regime of obedience, not merely excess appetite.

### Retrospective surprises

91:13 produced the strongest lexical surprise: the she-camel, watering share, and `ر س ل/B006` continuous milk-yield activated fixed `ك ذ ب/B006`, milk that fails expectation. 91:14 then corrected any merely figurative reading by showing the community physically cause that failure, while repeated `كذب` converts the earlier denial into enacted policy.

## 91:12 - إِذِ ٱنۢبَعَثَ أَشْقَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **A human rising that inverts the camel image.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ب ع ث/B001` is rousing a kneeling or restrained camel into motion; 91:13 introduces the she-camel and 91:14 cuts her down through `ع ق ر/B002`. **Structural cue and role:** the human subject rises first and then disables the animal that the root-image ordinarily rouses, making his motion a reversal of animation into immobilization. **Abductive move:** the camel branch and later animal are supplied; treating their sequence as deliberate inversion is inferred. **Reading change:** `انبعث` becomes a grotesquely misdirected activation, not neutral standing up.

2. **A counter-mission.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ب ع ث/B002` is dispatching a person, messenger, camel, or force toward a task; 91:13 `ر س ل/B002` names messenger and message. **Structural cue and role:** the self-launched `أشقاها` is immediately answered by a messenger bearing a protected claim, so dispatch supplies two opposed trajectories. **Abductive move:** the two movements are explicit, while calling the first a counter-mission to the second is relational inference. **Reading change:** the ayah marks not only an individual's initiative but an anti-dispatch set against the communicated order.

3. **Latent corruption becoming kinetic.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ب ع ث/B004` is setting out and pressing onward in good or evil; 91:10 `د س و/B003` is inward spoilage and 91:14 supplies the completed act. **Structural cue and role:** concealed self, collective denial, individual setting-out, warning, and violence form successive stages; `انبعث` is the conversion point from disposition to motion. **Abductive move:** the stages are supplied; the causal direction from inner spoilage to mobilized harm is inferred. **Reading change:** this brief ayah becomes the hinge at which a latent moral state acquires a body and trajectory.

4. **A perverse winner whose rise is temporary.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ش ق و/B003` is prevailing in a hardship contest and `ش ق و/B004` is a long rising mountain flank; 91:11 `ط غ ي/B005` supplies a high outcrop and 91:14 `س و ي/B001` equalizes. **Structural cue and role:** the superlative `أشقاها` rises from the group, occupies the action, and is followed by leveling, so competitive and topographic prominence converge in a short-lived role. **Abductive move:** the contest and terrain images are dormant and their convergence is exploratory; sequence supplies the rise and leveling but not a literal mountain. **Reading change:** "most wretched" can denote the one who wins a destructive competition and mistakes prominence for durable elevation.

### Retrospective surprises

91:13 made `ب ع ث/B001` unexpectedly self-reversing: the human rises just before the she-camel appears, then 91:14 shows him participate in making her fall. The same later ayah levels the temporary elevation activated by `ش ق و/B004`, correcting prominence into a prelude to erasure.

## 91:13 - فَقَالَ لَهُمْ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ نَاقَةَ ٱللَّهِ وَسُقْيَٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Speech as notice of a protected resource relation.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ق و ل/B001` is articulated speech, fixed `ر س ل/B002` is messenger and carried message, and fixed `س ق ي/B003` is an irrigation share or channel. **Structural cue and role:** `قال لهم` introduces the compressed nominal pair `ناقة الله وسقياها`; the possessive suffix gives watering an attached entitlement, so the utterance functions as boundary notice. **Abductive move:** words, messenger, animal, and water share are supplied; taking the ellipsis as warning or injunction is the inferred performative force. **Reading change:** the saying does not merely identify an animal; it publicly establishes a protected allocation.

2. **A message that safeguards continuous yield.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ر س ل/B006` is milk and flowing yield, fixed `ن و ق/B002` is the she-camel, and fixed `س ق ي/B001` is giving drink. **Structural cue and role:** messenger, she-camel, and watering occur in one compact utterance, while denial and hamstringing follow; the dormant milk branch makes the message the carrier of a life-sustaining flow. **Abductive move:** the lexical materials are supplied, but inferring milk or wider provision as the protected yield is material analogy, not an explicit statement. **Reading change:** the messenger carries a resource-preservation claim as well as verbal content.

3. **A test of management rather than domination.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ن و ق/B004` is taming, training, and setting affairs right; fixed `س ق ي/B002` is providing access to a water source. **Structural cue and role:** a bounded animal-water arrangement is placed before the collective's disabling act, so management means maintaining access and restraint rather than mastering by injury. **Abductive move:** the arrangement and later violation are explicit; construing them as a governance test is inferred. **Reading change:** `ناقة الله وسقياها` becomes a compact social order whose success depends on disciplined stewardship.

4. **Legitimate elevation opposed to false ascent.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ن و ق/B001` carries elevation and the mountain's highest point; the she-camel is twice enclosed by the divine name through fixed `ء ل ه/B002`. **Structural cue and role:** 91:11-12 activate transgressive high ground and a rising superlative, whereas this ayah elevates the animal by attributed ownership before 91:14 levels the violators. **Abductive move:** status elevation is inferred from the root branch and genitive framing; no physical ascent of the animal is stated. **Reading change:** the protected she-camel occupies the legitimate high claim against the group's counterfeit prominence.

### Retrospective surprises

91:14 reveals that the compressed utterance was consequential notice: denying the messenger and hamstringing the animal violate its two named poles. The rapid *fa-* chain also sharpens the contrast between `ر س ل/B004` deliberate restraint and domination by impulsive injury.

## 91:14 - فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَعَقَرُوهَا فَدَمْدَمَ عَلَيْهِمْ رَبُّهُم بِذَنۢبِهِمْ فَسَوَّىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Denial completed as bodily disabling.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ك ذ ب/B002` is declaring the messenger or report false and fixed `ع ق ر/B002` is cutting an animal's legs or hocks so it falls. **Structural cue and role:** the two verbs are joined by consecutive *fa-* and preserve different pronominal objects, moving directly from rejecting him to disabling her; denial supplies authorization and hamstringing supplies enactment. **Abductive move:** succession is explicit; the causal claim that epistemic rejection enables bodily violence is inferred. **Reading change:** `فكذبوه` is exposed as an action-program rather than speech that remains verbal.

2. **Severing a source of generation and growth.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ع ق ر/B004` is barrenness or stopped generation and `ع ق ر/B007` is cutting a palm's or bird's source of regrowth; 91:9 `ز ك و/B001` is increase and 91:13 joins she-camel to watering. **Structural cue and role:** hamstringing interrupts an animal-water-yield mechanism, so the cut functions beyond one wound as termination of future increase. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the generative branches and resource relation; extending the injury to ecological and social futurity is inferred. **Reading change:** `فعقروها` becomes an assault on continuance, not only on one body.

3. **Eradication as removal of counterfeit elevation and restoration of evenness.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `د م د م/B001` is complete eradication; fixed `س و ي/B001` is equalization, `س و ي/B002` straight completion, and `س و ي/B009` smooth open ground. **Structural cue and role:** the high-outcrop branch of `طغوى` and rising-flank branch of `أشقاها` in 91:11-12 culminate in `فسواها`; leveling reverses their claimed prominence. **Abductive move:** eradication and equalizing are explicit, while construing the feminine object without resolving it as people, place, or event and reading the act topographically are inferred. **Reading change:** the close of the ayah is not undifferentiated destruction; it is a leveling of illicit asymmetry.

4. **The nurturer as accountable administrator.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ر ب ب/B001` is ownership and authority, while fixed `ر ب ب/B002` is nurturing, repairing, and administering to completion. **Structural cue and role:** `ربهم` acts after they destroy the protected animal-water order; lordship therefore combines ownership of the violated relation with responsibility for its reckoning. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies ownership, nurture, violation, and response; the arrow from destroyed care to accountable judgment is inferred. **Reading change:** `ربهم` names not a remote title but the administrator whose nurture makes the violation answerable.

5. **Sin as a tail that reaches its aftermath.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ذ ن ب/B001` is culpable offense with bad consequence and `ذ ن ب/B002` is a trailing tail or rear end; 91:15 `ع ق ب/B006` is the outcome brought after an act. **Structural cue and role:** `بذنبهم` sits inside the causal chain and is immediately followed by the surah's explicit aftermath-word, so the tail image makes offense trail into result. **Abductive move:** culpability and aftermath are supplied; turning their adjacency into a continuous causal tail is inferred. **Reading change:** the sin is not a sealed past act but something whose rear trace remains attached until consequence arrives.

### Retrospective surprises

91:15 activates the dormant tail-image of `ذ ن ب/B002` with `ع ق ب/B006`: offense retains a rear trace that arrives as aftermath. It also sharpens `فسواها` as final judgment, because `ع ق ب/B008` allows the next clause to deny any effective follow-up that could review or reverse the leveling.

## 91:15 - وَلَا يَخَافُ عُقْبَٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Consequence as the act's inevitable rear result.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ع ق ب/B006` is the end or aftermath an act brings, and fixed `خ و ف/B001` is anticipation of harm from a sign; 91:14 `ذ ن ب/B001` already defines offense through culpable consequence. **Structural cue and role:** the final clause follows the entire denial-to-leveling chain and names what comes after it, making `عقباها` the rear result toward which prior actions point. **Abductive move:** sequence and outcome vocabulary are supplied; treating the whole narrative as the sign from which an aftermath could be anticipated is inferred. **Reading change:** the close converts the preceding event into a causal history whose meaning lies in its end.

2. **Judgment with no successful reviewer or reverser.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ع ق ب/B008` is following up a judgment to question, challenge, or reverse it; fixed `خ و ف/B001` supplies apprehension. **Structural cue and role:** the nearest expressed agent is `ربهم` in 91:14, followed by completed eradication and equalization; the review branch gives `عقباها` the institutional role of a possible challenge after judgment. **Abductive move:** carrying the prior subject forward is a syntactic inference, and reading absence of fear as absence of a reversing claimant is branch-driven. **Reading change:** the clause can mark final, unreviewable execution rather than only an emotional state.

3. **No residue or posterity capable of answering back.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ع ق ب/B011` is residue or remaining trace and `ع ق ب/B004` is posterity; 91:14 activates `ع ق ر/B004`, stopped generation, and `د م د م/B001`, eradication. **Structural cue and role:** barrenness plus eradication leaves the aftermath without a surviving line or remainder, so the dormant branches assign absence to what follows. **Abductive move:** this is exploratory because `عقبى` most directly fits outcome; the no-posterity reading arises from the exact neighboring eradication and generation branches. **Reading change:** the ending can also register how comprehensively the event closes futurity.

4. **Aftermath as scheduled successor.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ع ق ب/B005` is one thing following another in succession. **Structural cue and role:** 91:14's tightly chained *fa-* verbs make each act follow the last, and this final coordinated clause names the successor to the whole chain. **Abductive move:** formal succession is explicit; treating aftermath as structurally scheduled rather than accidental is inferred. **Reading change:** `عقباها` becomes the necessary next position generated by the preceding sequence.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah exists in the clipped focus window, so no later surprise emerged. The retrospective pass retained the multiple outcome, review, residue, and succession readings without resolving them into one referent or scope.
