# Surah 84 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

## 84:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A designation placed over the cosmic sequence.** **Lexical evidence:** `س م و` B005 is “name as raised designation,” while the same root in 84:1 is `السَّماء`, `س م و` B004, the overhead sky or cover. **Structural cue and role:** the designation in 84:0 immediately precedes the raised cover splitting, then the lower earth being extended and emptied (84:1–5); the root recurrence lets the *name* function as a heading over the transformation of what is *above*. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the shared root and sequence, while I infer that the heading names the authority under which the sequence proceeds. **Reading change:** `بسم` becomes more than an introductory label: it raises a governing designation over the paired actions of sky and earth.

2. **The named one is identified through enacted worship.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ل ه` B001 is worship and the worshipped one, whereas B002 is the fixed divine name used in invocation. `ر ب ب` B001 in 84:2 and 84:5 supplies lordship and obeyed authority; `ء ذ ن` B002 supplies receptive hearing. **Structural cue and role:** the fixed name is followed by sky and earth each listening to “its Lord” in an exact refrain. **Abductive move:** the packet does not state that cosmic receptivity is worship; I infer that comprehensive obedience is the enacted relation that activates the worship branch of the fixed name. **Reading change:** `الله` is read simultaneously as invoked name and as the one to whom the two cosmic domains render obedience.

3. **Mercy as protective containment before containment opens.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر ح م` B001 supplies mercy and tenderness, while B003 supplies the womb as a containing vessel. **Structural cue and role:** the doubled mercy forms stand before an earth that has an inside, casts out what is in it, and becomes empty (84:3–5). **Abductive move:** the earth is not called a womb; I infer a material analogy between merciful containment and the packet’s immediately following container-and-release sequence. **Reading change:** the doubled mercy can be heard as sheltering, generative containment, made newly salient at the threshold of a scene in which containment itself is opened.

### Retrospective surprises

84:21–23 unexpectedly complete the opening `ء ل ه` B001 worship branch: the named one before whom prostration is refused is the same one who knows what is stored inwardly. 84:25 then gives the mercy opening a durable counterpart in an `م ن ن` B001 reward explicitly protected from being cut or diminished.

## 84:1 — إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنشَقَّتْ

### Activated readings

1. **The upper half of a matched cosmic dismantling.** **Lexical evidence:** `ش ق ق` B001 is physical splitting open and B002 is a half or corresponding side; `س م و` B004 is the overhead cover. `ء ر ض` B001 in 84:3 is the lower ground opposite the sky. **Structural cue and role:** the sky’s split is answered by the earth’s extension, discharge, and the same obedience refrain (84:2–5). **Abductive move:** the text supplies two opposed domains and parallel clauses; I infer that B002’s “corresponding side” organizes them as matched halves of one reconfiguration. **Reading change:** the ayah no longer presents an isolated rupture overhead; it opens a coordinated transformation whose lower counterpart immediately follows.

2. **A cover opened so that enclosure can no longer hide its contents.** **Lexical evidence:** `س م و` B004 includes an upper cover, and `ش ق ق` B001 supplies its fissuring. In 84:4, `ل ق ي` B005 is casting something out and `خ ل و` B001 is becoming empty. **Structural cue and role:** the upper cover opens before the lower container expels what is inside it. **Abductive move:** the packet does not explicitly say that the sky’s opening enables the earth’s exposure; I supply that directional link from the ordered upper/lower sequence. **Reading change:** `انشقت` reads as the first removal of cosmic enclosure in a larger disclosure mechanism.

3. **Cosmic strain anticipates human strain.** **Lexical evidence:** beside splitting, `ش ق ق` B003 is a burden that strains the self; 84:6 repeats `ك د ح` twice, with B001 denoting striving work with hardship. **Structural cue and role:** within this fixed ayah’s clipped window, the sky’s transformation and the human’s strenuous movement stand at opposite ends of the sequence. **Abductive move:** I infer that the material split gives scale to the later human strain; the packet supplies juxtaposition, not an explicit comparison. **Reading change:** the rupture can carry an activated undertone of yielding under strain, preparing the reader to hear human toil as participation in a cosmos under compelled transition.

### Retrospective surprises

84:17–19 correct a reading of the split as simple terminal destruction: night gathers, the moon completes an order, and passage continues layer after layer. The later reassembly sequence makes 84:1 the opening transition in a changing cosmos, while 84:22’s covering darkness sharpens the opened sky as a foil to chosen concealment.

## 84:2 — وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتْ

### Activated readings

1. **Receptive compliance, demonstrated twice.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ذ ن` B002 is listening with obedient or accepting hearing; `ر ب ب` B001 is lordship and obeyed authority; `ح ق ق` B002 is due obligation. **Structural cue and role:** the clause follows the sky’s split and recurs verbatim after the earth’s extension and emptying in 84:5. **Abductive move:** no audible command is supplied, so I infer from the completed transformations that “hearing” names effective compliance rather than sound perception alone. **Reading change:** the ayah becomes the response phase of a command-and-fulfillment mechanism shared by both cosmic domains.

2. **Authorized execution rather than merely passive submission.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ذ ن` B004 is permission and authorization; `ح ق ق` B005 is making truth stand or bringing a matter to completion. **Structural cue and role:** splitting has already occurred, and the earth will likewise act before the refrain repeats. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies actions and the authorization branch but no explicit grant of leave; I infer that each realm’s transformation is an authorized execution of its assigned act. **Reading change:** `أذنت` can mark not only receptivity but licensed enactment, while `حقت` marks the enacted order standing complete.

3. **Each realm fitted to its due transformation.** **Lexical evidence:** `ح ق ق` B011 is a fitted socket or exact correspondence, and `ر ب ب` B002 is repair, nurture, administration, and stagewise completion. **Structural cue and role:** sky and earth undergo different material acts but receive the identical closing clause. **Abductive move:** I infer that the repeated close signals functional fit: different parts satisfy different roles in one administered process. **Reading change:** `وحقت` reads not only as inevitability but as each domain coming exactly into the condition appointed for it.

### Retrospective surprises

84:19’s `ط ب ق` B003/B004 confirms the earlier fit-and-stage activation of `ح ق ق` B011 and `ر ب ب` B002. More sharply, 84:21–22 turns the sky’s receptive compliance into the standard against which human non-prostration and denial become conspicuous refusals.

## 84:3 — وَإِذَا ٱلْأَرْضُ مُدَّتْ

### Activated readings

1. **The lower surface drawn out against the opened upper cover.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ر ض` B001 is the lower ground opposite the sky; `م د د` B001 is lengthwise drawing-out and extension. `س م و` B004 and `ش ق ق` B001 in 84:1 supply the upper cover and its opening. **Structural cue and role:** “sky/split” and “earth/extended” are parallel `إذا` clauses, each followed by the same obedience refrain. **Abductive move:** I infer that opening above and extension below jointly remove the old spatial bounds; the packet gives the parallel but not that causal synthesis. **Reading change:** the earth’s extension becomes the lower operation in a two-surface spatial reconfiguration.

2. **A broad mat prepared for exposure and adjudication.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ر ض` B005 is a thick mat, and `م د د` B001 supplies spreading. In the same focus window, the earth casts out its contents (84:4), the human meets the Lord (84:6), and a `كتاب` is given for `حساب` (84:7–8). **Structural cue and role:** material spreading precedes discharge, encounter, record, and reckoning. **Abductive move:** the packet never calls the earth an adjudicative floor; I infer that the mat image makes the extended ground the prepared surface on which concealed contents become available to account. **Reading change:** `مدت` reads as functional preparation, not indefinite enlargement alone.

3. **Exploratory: extension as the material analogue of a ledger.** **Lexical evidence:** `م د د` B005 is an inkwell supplying writing ink; `ك ت ب` B002 in 84:7 is joined letters and a written book. **Structural cue and role:** the writing branch is activated by the near sequence from stretched earth to issued record and reckoning. **Abductive move:** the weak link is that no ink or inscription is stated; I infer an analogy in which the drawn-out earth supplies an evidentiary field that the later book formalizes. **Reading change:** the earth can be perceived as an expanded record-bearing substrate, while the explicit book remains the actual document.

### Retrospective surprises

84:19 makes the extended earth one traversed `ط ب ق` rather than the sequence’s final stable surface. 84:21’s gathered recitation and 84:23’s inward vessel also strengthen the exploratory ledger reading: the broad external field of disclosure is later answered by speech gathered into a text and meanings gathered inside people.

## 84:4 — وَأَلْقَتْ مَا فِيهَا وَتَخَلَّتْ

### Activated readings

1. **Casting as delivery into encounter.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `أَلْقَتْ` activates `ل ق ي` B005, casting something down or before another; 84:6 uses the same root in `ملاقيه`, `ل ق ي` B004, two things coming face to face. `خ ل و` B001 supplies becoming empty. **Structural cue and role:** the earth casts what is inside, becomes empty, and two ayat later the human is told that the directed toil ends in encounter. **Abductive move:** the packet does not identify the expelled contents with what is met; I infer that the casting is a delivery operation that makes encounter possible. **Reading change:** the earth’s emptying becomes an active handoff into confrontation, not mere evacuation.

2. **A custodian clearing itself of liability.** **Lexical evidence:** `خ ل و` B006 is being clear of liability, while `ل ق ي` B005 supplies depositing or setting something before another. The nearby `كتاب` (`ك ت ب` B002/B003) and `حساب` (`ح س ب` B001) supply document and reckoning. **Structural cue and role:** held contents pass out before a record is issued and account begins (84:7–8). **Abductive move:** the legal-custodial direction is inferred: the packet gives release, document, and account, but does not state that the earth was liable for what it held. **Reading change:** `وتخلت` can mean that the earth completes custody by surrendering its contents and stands discharged of them.

3. **Release that reverses enclosure.** **Lexical evidence:** `خ ل و` B008 is letting go or setting free; `ل ق ي` B005 is casting out. **Structural cue and role:** `ما فيها` first defines contents by enclosure, then the two verbs successively expel and release them. **Abductive move:** I infer that the second verb is not redundant but removes any residual claim or restraint after the first physical casting. **Reading change:** the ayah describes a two-step emptying: contents are expelled, then the container relinquishes them completely.

### Retrospective surprises

84:23 supplies the strongest later reversal: `و ع ي` B001/B002 shows humans inwardly storing after the earth has surrendered everything it stored. This corrects any idea that 84:4 ends concealment; disclosure moves the site of custody from the cosmic container to the human interior, where it remains known.

## 84:5 — وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتْ

### Activated readings

1. **The earth’s obedience establishes the norm against which human outcomes divide.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ذ ن` B002 is receptive, accepting hearing; `ر ب ب` B001 is obeyed authority; `ح ق ق` B002 is due obligation. **Structural cue and role:** this exact refrain answers the earth’s extension and emptying, then the packet moves to human toil and the opposed placements of the book at the right hand or behind the back (84:6–10). **Abductive move:** I infer a contrast between unresisting cosmic compliance and differentiated human account; the text places them in sequence but does not state the comparison. **Reading change:** the refrain becomes an ethical baseline, not just the close of the earth scene.

2. **An authorized transfer of contents.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ذ ن` B004 is authorization, `ح ق ق` B005 is establishing or completing a matter, and the preceding `ل ق ي` B005 is casting something before another. **Structural cue and role:** authorization follows the earth’s release, while the later books are passively “given” (`ء ت ي` B002) to their recipients. **Abductive move:** I infer a single administered chain of custody linking earth’s authorized discharge to the later documentary handoff; only the endpoints are explicit. **Reading change:** the earth’s listening is read as permission enacted through transfer, with `وحقت` sealing that transfer as complete.

3. **Lordship as stagewise administration.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر ب ب` B002 is administration, nurture, and completion stage by stage; `ح ق ق` B011 is exact fit. **Structural cue and role:** the earth is brought through extension, discharge, release, and closure before the human is brought through toil, encounter, and record placement. **Abductive move:** I infer that the shared `رب` names the administrator of both ordered progressions. **Reading change:** `لربها` points not only to possession but to the one who brings each participant through its fitted sequence.

### Retrospective surprises

84:19 retrospectively validates the Lord-as-stagewise-administrator reading, while 84:21–22 sharpens the contrast the primary pass only anticipated: earth listens and fulfills, but the addressed group hears recitation without lowering and then actively denies.

## 84:6 — يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدْحًۭا فَمُلَٰقِيهِ

### Activated readings

1. **Toil as mark-making that becomes account.** **Lexical evidence:** the fixed ayah repeats `ك د ح`; B001 is striving work with hardship, while B002 is the scratch or bite-mark left on a surface. In 84:7–8, `ك ت ب` B001/B002 supplies joining into a written record and `ح س ب` B001 supplies reckoning. **Structural cue and role:** agent noun plus cognate accusative intensifies the toil, after which book and account follow immediately. **Abductive move:** the packet does not say that the book contains scratches from the toil; I infer that B002 turns acts into durable traces and that the nearby book gathers those traces. **Reading change:** `كدحا` is not only strenuous motion toward an endpoint; it is a career of leaving marks that become encounterable evidence.

2. **Encounter prepared by the earth’s earlier casting.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ملاقيه` activates `ل ق ي` B004, two things coming face to face; 84:4 uses the same root in `ألقت`, B005, casting or setting something before another. **Structural cue and role:** the earth first casts what it held, and the addressed human is then told that toil culminates in meeting the pronominal object. **Abductive move:** I do not resolve the pronoun; I infer only that the prior casting is the material preparation for the encounter encoded by `ملاقيه`. **Reading change:** the meeting is no longer an unprepared arrival but the endpoint of a disclosure-and-delivery process already begun by the earth.

3. **The familiar social being is drawn beyond familiar society.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ن س` B001 is visible human presence and B003 is familiarity or companionship that removes estrangement. `ء ه ل` B001 and `س ر ر` B010 in 84:9 supply close belonging and joy, while `ر ب ب` B002 supplies stagewise administration. **Structural cue and role:** “the human” is addressed before one outcome returns to family in joy, yet the fixed trajectory is first `إلى ربك`. **Abductive move:** I infer that the root’s familiarity branch defines the social comfort from which and through which the human’s administered journey proceeds. **Reading change:** `الإنسان` becomes specifically the companion-seeking, familiarity-making creature whose whole familiar life is nevertheless directed toward the Lordly encounter.

### Retrospective surprises

84:19 expands the singular trajectory into passage through accumulated stages, and 84:25’s `ع م ل` B001 plus `ء ج ر` B001 clarifies that toil is not undifferentiated exertion: intentional sound work receives a distinct recompense. The closing contrast therefore corrects any fatalistic reading of the marks left by `كدح`.

## 84:7 — فَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِىَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ

### Activated readings

1. **A compiled record, not a merely handed object.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك ت ب` B002 is a written book, while B001 is joining one thing to another. The preceding `ك د ح` B002 supplies marks left by toil, and 84:8 repeats `ح س ب` as verb and noun. **Structural cue and role:** the book stands between accumulated toil and explicit reckoning. **Abductive move:** I infer that the document joins dispersed traces into one reviewable whole; the packet supplies the sequence but not the book’s contents. **Reading change:** `كتابه` reads as the integrated form of a life’s scattered work.

2. **Right-hand reception combines place, auspiciousness, and capacity.** **Lexical evidence:** `ي م ن` B002 is the right hand or side, B001 is blessing and good fortune, and B004 is right-hand power or rightful force. **Structural cue and role:** the placement is opposed to the book behind the back in 84:10 and is followed by easy reckoning and joyful return in 84:8–9. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies spatial polarity and outcomes; I infer that the right hand assigns an auspicious and empowered role to this reception rather than serving as neutral anatomy. **Reading change:** `بيمينه` marks a favorable capacity to receive and bear the record.

3. **A grant that arrives as binding judgment.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ت ي` B002 is granting or bringing something to someone; `ك ت ب` B003 is an inscription functioning as decree or judgment. **Structural cue and role:** the passive grant is duplicated for the opposite recipient in 84:10, after which each placement generates a distinct sequence. **Abductive move:** I infer an issuing authority and a binding force from the passive symmetry and outcomes; neither is named in this ayah. **Reading change:** the book is received as an operative adjudication, not simply information about adjudication.

### Retrospective surprises

84:23 makes the issued book an external counterpart to the inward vessel of `و ع ي` B001/B002: both gather contents, but only the book stands openly for review. 84:25 then prevents the document/account mechanism from enclosing the favorable outcome in a fixed quantity, since the reward also activates negated `م ن ن` B005 measurement.

## 84:8 — فَسَوْفَ يُحَاسَبُ حِسَابًۭا يَسِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Ease and limited extent coexist.** **Lexical evidence:** the fixed verb and cognate noun repeat `ح س ب` B001, counting and reckoning. `ي س ر` B001 is ease or ready opening after hardship, while B002 is a small amount or short duration. **Structural cue and role:** the doubled reckoning follows right-hand reception and precedes joyful return; the adverse sequence instead moves from a behind-back book to ruin and fire (84:10–12). **Abductive move:** the packet does not specify whether ease is procedural or quantitative, so I retain both accepted scopes rather than collapse them. **Reading change:** the account can be read both as unimpeded and as bounded or light in extent.

2. **An administered review that the record is sufficient to settle.** **Lexical evidence:** `ح س ب` B006 is supervision and accountable management, while B003 is sufficiency; `ك ت ب` B001/B002 supplies the integrated written record. **Structural cue and role:** the record is given first, account follows, and return follows account. **Abductive move:** I infer that the compiled book is sufficient evidence for an orderly review and that this sufficiency enables the ease; the causal arrow is not stated. **Reading change:** `حسابا` becomes a managed settlement process rather than bare arithmetic.

3. **Exploratory: a right/left directional afterimage.** **Lexical evidence:** `ي س ر` B004 is the left hand or side, explicitly opposite the right; the preceding ayah has `يمين`, `ي م ن` B002. **Structural cue and role:** the two roots are adjacent, but the fixed morphology and syntax assign `يسيرا` the adjectival role “easy/slight,” not a literal left-hand location. **Abductive move:** I infer only a latent polarity: mention of the right activates the root’s left-side branch as the unchosen directional foil. **Reading change:** the ease acquires a spatially sorted afterimage, belonging to the right-hand route while quietly evoking the absent opposite.

### Retrospective surprises

84:23 broadens the evidence beyond the visible book by asserting knowledge of inward storage. The decisive correction comes in 84:25: negated `م ن ن` B005 makes the reward unmeasured, so `يسيرا` B002 may qualify the account’s extent without implying a small or restricted recompense.

## 84:9 — وَيَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰٓ أَهْلِهِۦ مَسْرُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A return that reverses condition, not just location.** **Lexical evidence:** `ق ل ب` B005 is return and destination, while B004 is turning or changing from one side or state to another. `ء ه ل` B001 is close belonging and `س ر ر` B010 is joy and ease. **Structural cue and role:** 84:13 repeats `أهله مسرورا` but locates the adverse recipient’s joy in the past; 84:14 then exposes the belief that he would not return. **Abductive move:** I infer from the mirrored phrase and changed direction that this future return is also a validated reversal of state, unlike the earlier household joy that supported a false expectation. **Reading change:** `ينقلب` carries both homeward movement and transformation into a joy ratified by account.

2. **An inward turn made outwardly legible.** **Lexical evidence:** `ق ل ب` B001 is the heart or inward faculty; `س ر ر` B001 is what is hidden inwardly, alongside B010’s overt joy. **Structural cue and role:** the joyful return follows a book and reckoning, while the mirrored adverse joy is followed by an inward supposition in 84:14. **Abductive move:** I infer that account tests and externalizes the inward state; the packet does not explicitly call joy a heart-state. **Reading change:** the physical return doubles as a turning of the inward faculty into disclosed, warranted joy.

3. **Home as welcome into ease.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ه ل` B005 is welcome into spaciousness and familiar company; `س ر ر` B010 supplies joy and prosperity, and 84:8 contributes `ي س ر` B001, ease. **Structural cue and role:** easy account, return, household, and joy form one uninterrupted sequence. **Abductive move:** I infer that the destination actively receives the person into ease rather than merely identifying kin. **Reading change:** `إلى أهله` becomes a welcoming social restoration produced by the favorable account.

### Retrospective surprises

84:19’s stage-upon-stage movement confirms that `ينقلب` is one state-changing turn within a larger trajectory. 84:23’s inward storage also sharpens the contrast between warranted joy made outward after account and the concealed adverse disposition, while 84:25’s uncut reward gives the favorable joy a stability the past joy of 84:13 lacked.

## 84:10 — وَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِىَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ وَرَآءَ ظَهْرِهِۦ

### Activated readings

1. **The placement embodies prior concealment and neglect.** **Lexical evidence:** `و ر ي` B005 is covering something and putting it out of sight; B006 is the far side, behind or beyond. `ظ ه ر` B002 is the anatomical back, while B012 is putting something behind one’s back in neglect or rejection. **Structural cue and role:** this placement reverses the right-hand reception of 84:7; the later statement that the Lord saw him (84:15) answers the concealment branch. **Abductive move:** I infer a correspondence between how truth or account was treated and how the record is now received; the packet states placement and observation, not prior conduct. **Reading change:** `وراء ظهره` is an enacted exposure of a concealment posture, not an arbitrary location.

2. **The hidden record contains a latent blaze.** **Lexical evidence:** `و ر ي` B002 is hidden fire emerging from a fire-stick; 84:12 names `سعيرا`, `س ع ر` B001, fire kindling and blazing. `ك ت ب` B003 makes the book an operative decree. **Structural cue and role:** the behind-back book is followed by the call for ruin and entry into the blaze. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the root branch and sequence; I infer that the concealed document functions like a fire-stick whose latent consequence catches two ayat later. **Reading change:** `وراء` carries a dormant ignition image: what is put out of sight is precisely what kindles into visible outcome.

3. **A binding document delivered to the side least able to manage it.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ت ي` B002 is grant, `ك ت ب` B003 is binding decree, and `ظ ه ر` B006/B021 supplies backing and precautionary support. **Structural cue and role:** unlike the empowered right hand, the back receives the record without facing or handling it; ruin follows. **Abductive move:** I infer functional incapacity from the bodily contrast: a back can bear a load but cannot inspect or answer it as a hand can. **Reading change:** the placement dramatizes a judgment that is borne without usable agency or supporting reserve.

### Retrospective surprises

84:21–23 extends the behind-back posture into a reception mechanism: recitation arrives publicly, denial follows, and its contents are stored inwardly. Yet 84:23 also closes the hiding strategy, because divine knowledge reaches what `و ر ي` B005 and `ظ ه ر` B012 put out of sight.

## 84:11 — فَسَوْفَ يَدْعُوا۟ ثُبُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A call that tries to draw ruin near.** **Lexical evidence:** `د ع و` B001 is calling in order to draw something toward oneself; `ث ب ر` B002 is ruin, perdition, loss, and corruption. **Structural cue and role:** the call follows receipt of the book behind the back and is followed immediately by entry into the blaze (84:10–12). **Abductive move:** the packet supplies the speech act and its object; I infer from B001’s directional image that the caller is not merely naming ruin but summoning it toward himself. **Reading change:** `يدعو ثبورا` becomes a self-directed arrival mechanism in which the utterance pulls the recognized outcome close.

2. **Ruin as blocked return to good.** **Lexical evidence:** `ث ب ر` B003 is restraint, blocking, and being prevented from good. In the same focus window, `ق ل ب` B005 marks the favorable return to family (84:9), while `ح و ر` B005 names the return denied by the adverse person (84:14). **Structural cue and role:** the call for ruin stands between the two return statements and replaces the joyful family destination with fire. **Abductive move:** I infer that the ruin consists functionally in arrested access to the favorable return; the ayah itself does not name what is blocked. **Reading change:** `ثبورا` can denote deprivation and stopped passage, not annihilation alone.

3. **A collapse that unfolds one consequence after another.** **Lexical evidence:** `د ع و` B005 is successive falling or collapse; `ث ب ر` B002 supplies the ruinous endpoint. **Structural cue and role:** behind-back record, call, blaze, former joy, false supposition, and correction form a tightly ordered adverse chain (84:10–15). **Abductive move:** the fixed form is an ordinary call, not the reciprocal collapse form; I infer a dormant process image from the root inventory and the packet’s cascading sequence. **Reading change:** the short ayah becomes the audible hinge in a progressive collapse rather than a detached exclamation.

### Retrospective surprises

84:19 confirms the dormant successive-collapse image by naming unavoidable stage after stage. 84:24–25 then specifies what `ث ب ر` B003 blocks: the adverse chain ends in pain and excludes the `م ن ن` B001 recompense protected from cutting or diminution.

## 84:12 — وَيَصْلَىٰ سَعِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **Direct encounter with an actively kindled blaze.** **Lexical evidence:** `ص ل ي` B003 is encountering fire, entering it, or suffering its heat; `س ع ر` B001 is fire kindling and blazing. **Structural cue and role:** the adverse record leads to a call for ruin and then to this compact verb-object pair. **Abductive move:** no additional causal agent is named; I infer from the sequence that the operative book and call culminate in embodied exposure to the blaze. **Reading change:** the ayah presents consequence as face-to-face thermal encounter, not as an abstract label.

2. **Exploratory: the blaze as the price disclosed by account.** **Lexical evidence:** `س ع ر` B003 is market price; the focus window contains `ح س ب` B001, reckoning, and two `كتاب` placements with `ك ت ب` B003, binding judgment (84:7–10). **Structural cue and role:** accounting vocabulary precedes `سعيرا` on the adverse route. **Abductive move:** no sale or explicit exchange occurs, so the economic arrow is inferred: the account fixes a cost that the blaze then embodies. **Reading change:** the fire can carry the latent image of an exacted price, while remaining explicitly a blaze under B001.

3. **Exploratory: heat as coercive straightening after a false trajectory.** **Lexical evidence:** `ص ل ي` B004 includes turning a stick over fire to straighten it; `ح و ر` B005 in 84:14 is return or change of state, and `ب ص ر` B002 in 84:15 is penetrating knowledge. **Structural cue and role:** fire is followed by disclosure of the person’s mistaken no-return supposition. **Abductive move:** the packet does not state a corrective purpose, so I infer only a material analogy: heat that straightens corresponds to a trajectory forcibly corrected by the return he denied. **Reading change:** `يصلى` may be heard as exposure that reverses a crooked expectation, not passive contact alone.

### Retrospective surprises

84:21 produces the most surprising later activation: refusal to prostrate activates `ص ل ي` B001, ritual prayer with its bodily postures, beside the fixed fire-encounter branch B003. The blaze can therefore read as an inverted bodily sequel to omitted worship; 84:24’s `ء ل م` B002 then confirms that the encounter is pain-causing, not merely luminous.

## 84:13 — إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فِىٓ أَهْلِهِۦ مَسْرُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **Household joy exposed as temporally bounded.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك و ن` B001 marks being or predication in time; `ء ه ل` B001 is close belonging; `س ر ر` B010 is joy and ease. **Structural cue and role:** 84:9 uses the same `أهله مسرورا` pair for a future return after easy account, whereas this ayah puts the pair under past `كان` and follows it with the belief that no return would occur. **Abductive move:** I infer from the tense and mirrored phrase that the two joys differ in durability and validation. **Reading change:** the fixed joy is read as a past condition whose pleasantness could not guarantee its continuation.

2. **Visible pleasure carrying a concealed conviction.** **Lexical evidence:** alongside joy, `س ر ر` B001 is an inward secret; `ظ ن ن` B001/B003 in 84:14 supplies conviction or supposition, and `ب ص ر` B002 in 84:15 supplies inner sight. **Structural cue and role:** joy is immediately followed by what he thought, then by the statement that his Lord saw him. **Abductive move:** I infer that the joy housed the no-return conviction; the text sequences the states but does not explicitly make one the interior of the other. **Reading change:** `مسرورا` acquires an inward layer of complacency that the next two ayat disclose.

3. **Familiar habitation functioning as an enclosure.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ه ل` B004 is familiar habitation and domestication; `س ر ر` B011 is a place of settled support. The following focus-window images move through twilight, night, and the full moon (84:16–18). **Structural cue and role:** the fixed `في` locates joy inside a familiar social space, while the later sequence opens onto changing cosmic phases. **Abductive move:** I infer that settled familiarity narrowed the person’s horizon and helped make non-return seem plausible. **Reading change:** `في أهله` reads as both belonging and enclosure, a local stability mistakenly projected onto the whole trajectory.

### Retrospective surprises

84:16–19 confirms that the settled household state was only one enclosure inside a wider sequence of changing phases. 84:23’s `و ع ي` B001 makes the concealed-conviction reading more precise, and 84:25’s uncut reward distinguishes enduring favorable ease from this retrospectively bounded pleasure.

## 84:14 — إِنَّهُۥ ظَنَّ أَن لَّن يَحُورَ

### Activated readings

1. **A certainty built from a misleading indication.** **Lexical evidence:** `ظ ن ن` B001 is certainty or strong belief after an indication; `ح و ر` B005 is return and change of state. **Structural cue and role:** the immediately preceding indication is his joy among his family, while 84:9 has already shown another person genuinely returning to family in joy. **Abductive move:** I infer that he treated local household ease as evidence of permanent non-return; the source of his conviction is unstated. **Reading change:** `ظن` can register strong subjective certainty, which makes the following correction target an entrenched inference rather than a passing guess.

2. **An unreliable estimate corrected by superior sight.** **Lexical evidence:** `ظ ن ن` B003 is weak supposition and B006 is a matter whose outcome is not trustworthy; 84:15 activates `ب ص ر` B002, penetrating knowledge. **Structural cue and role:** `بلى` immediately reverses the negative proposition and grounds the reversal in the Lord’s seeing. **Abductive move:** I infer an epistemic hierarchy: his estimate is weak because it extrapolates from partial experience, while the following sight encompasses him. **Reading change:** `ظن` simultaneously remains an uncertain forecast whose failure is diagnosed by the next ayah; this reading coexists with the felt-certainty reading above.

3. **Denial of return is denial of successive change.** **Lexical evidence:** `ح و ر` B005 includes returning, decrease after increase, and change from one state to another. `ق ل ب` B004/B005 in 84:9 supplies reversal and return, while `ط ب ق` B004 in 84:19 supplies one state after another. **Structural cue and role:** the no-return claim is framed by a prior favorable return and the later declaration of stage upon stage. **Abductive move:** I infer that `يحور` is broader than homeward motion: it names the reversibility of the condition he assumed stable. **Reading change:** the denied return becomes a denied law of transition, including loss after ease and movement into another state.

4. **Exploratory: cosmic rotation refutes the no-return model.** **Lexical evidence:** `ح و ر` B007 is turning on an axis; 84:16–18 supplies twilight, gathering night, and the moon reaching ordered fullness. **Structural cue and role:** the celestial sequence immediately follows the correction and precedes stage-upon-stage movement. **Abductive move:** no axis is mentioned, so I infer a cyclic material analogy from the ordered celestial changes. **Reading change:** `لن يحور` can sound like denial of the visible world’s turning pattern, not merely denial of personal return.

### Retrospective surprises

84:19 directly overturns the proposition by asserting compulsory state after state. More unexpectedly, 84:21–22 activates `ح و ر` B006, speech turned back and forth: recitation receives neither the bodily reply of prostration nor the trusting reply of belief, so the denied “return” also acquires the shape of a broken response loop.

## 84:15 — بَلَىٰٓ إِنَّ رَبَّهُۥ كَانَ بِهِۦ بَصِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Sight that penetrates the concealed inward record.** **Lexical evidence:** `ب ص ر` B001 is seeing with the eye, while B002 is inner sight, knowledge, proof, and self-witness. `و ر ي` B005 and `ظ ه ر` B012 in 84:10 supply concealment and putting behind the back; `س ر ر` B001 and `ظ ن ن` in 84:13–14 supply hidden inwardness and supposition. **Structural cue and role:** the seeing clause answers both the hidden placement of the book and the newly disclosed thought. **Abductive move:** I infer that divine seeing reaches both external posture and internal rationale; the packet does not separate these objects. **Reading change:** `بصيرا` becomes evidentiary penetration, not optical observation alone.

2. **An observation as temporally extensive as the condition observed.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك و ن` B001 is temporal being or predication; the same `كان` marks his prior household joy in 84:13. `ب ص ر` B002 adds knowledge and verification. **Structural cue and role:** one `كان` locates his pleasure, and the second locates the Lord’s seeing “of him,” correcting the inference drawn from that pleasure. **Abductive move:** I infer that observation accompanies the whole prior condition rather than beginning only at its correction. **Reading change:** the ayah reads as sustained encompassment of his history, not a late discovery.

3. **Lordship as oversight across changing stages.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر ب ب` B002 is administration and completion stage by stage; `ب ص ر` B002 is informed insight. The following `ط ب ق` B004 in 84:19 names state after state. **Structural cue and role:** seeing stands before the twilight-night-moon sequence and the assertion of layered passage (84:16–19). **Abductive move:** I infer that the Lord’s knowledge is the oversight that holds successive states in one administered trajectory. **Reading change:** `ربه` is activated as the one who both observes and conducts the person through the transitions he denied.

### Retrospective surprises

84:23 restates the sight claim in the register of knowledge, explicitly reaching what people store inwardly; this confirms `ب ص ر` B002 over a merely optical reading. The divided outcomes of 84:24–25 further show that the oversight is discriminating and consequential across the stages.

## 84:16 — فَلَآ أُقْسِمُ بِٱلشَّفَقِ

### Activated readings

1. **An oath built from a thin temporal threshold.** **Lexical evidence:** `ق س م` B004 is oath-taking; `ش ف ق` B002 is red twilight, remaining day mixed with night, and B001 is thinness or scant remainder. **Structural cue and role:** twilight is followed by night, what night gathers, and the moon at ordered fullness (84:17–18). **Abductive move:** I infer that B001’s thinness sharpens B002’s mixed light into a narrow boundary state; the packet supplies the sequence, not an explicit theory of thresholds. **Reading change:** `الشفق` becomes the first transitional exhibit in the oath, a visibly thinning state rather than decorative color.

2. **Swearing also apportions the evidence into successive shares.** **Lexical evidence:** beside oath, `ق س م` B003 is dividing or apportioning into shares. `ط ب ق` B004 in 84:19 is one state after another. **Structural cue and role:** the oath construction distributes attention across twilight, night, the night’s gathered contents, and the full moon before announcing layered passage. **Abductive move:** the grammar supplies an oath, while I infer that the root’s division branch describes how the oath organizes its evidence. **Reading change:** `أقسم` functions both as asseveration and as an analytic partition of continuous change into inspectable stages.

3. **Twilight carries apprehensive care into the warning.** **Lexical evidence:** `ش ف ق` B003 is care mixed with fear and caution. The focus window places ruin and fire before the oath (84:11–12) and non-belief and non-prostration after it (84:20–21). **Structural cue and role:** the affective branch sits at the hinge between adverse consequence and refused response. **Abductive move:** I infer that the twilight image is assigned a warning role toward the addressees; the ayah names no emotional subject. **Reading change:** the oath’s atmosphere becomes solicitous and fearful, not merely spectacular.

### Retrospective surprises

84:24–25 extends `ق س م` B003 beyond apportioning signs: the run finally divides into painful announcement and uncut recompense. 84:21’s refusal to lower also gives `ش ف ق` B003’s apprehensive care a concrete object, the audience that does not answer the warning embodied in the oath.

## 84:17 — وَٱلَّيْلِ وَمَا وَسَقَ

### Activated readings

1. **Night as an active collector.** **Lexical evidence:** `ل ي ل` B001 is night and its darkness; `و س ق` B002 is gathering scattered things and explicitly includes night gathering what it covers. **Structural cue and role:** twilight narrows into night, then the next ayah shows the moon reaching orderly fullness with the same `و س ق` root (84:16–18). **Abductive move:** the packet directly supplies collection but not its purpose; I infer that night’s gathering prepares the completed, ordered phase displayed by the moon. **Reading change:** night is not empty backdrop but a container-like operation that draws dispersed contents under one cover.

2. **Gathering as load-bearing and driven passage.** **Lexical evidence:** `و س ق` B001 is bearing a load and B005 is a driven herd held together; `ر ك ب` B001 in 84:19 is riding or being carried through movement. **Structural cue and role:** what night gathers is followed by a mounted passage through layer after layer. **Abductive move:** no herd or vehicle is explicit; I infer a material-motion analogy in which the night bears and drives its collected load into the next stage. **Reading change:** `وسق` can make night a carrier of what it encloses, not only an assembler.

3. **Physical darkness becomes a foil for cognitive covering.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `ل ي ل` B001 supplies darkness; 84:22 activates `ك ف ر` B002, enveloping darkness, and B003, covering or rejecting truth. **Structural cue and role:** the night is followed by a full moon and recitation, yet the later group still covers and denies. **Abductive move:** I infer a contrast rather than identity: natural night gathers toward fullness, while cognitive covering persists in the presence of illumination. **Reading change:** `الليل` becomes a neutral darkness that exposes the abnormality of a later, chosen darkness.

### Retrospective surprises

84:23 supplies a human analogue to night’s collection: `و ع ي` B001/B002 gathers meaning in an inward vessel. The contrast is corrective: night’s gathering moves toward the moon’s ordered fullness, whereas the later human gathering can retain denial and therefore remains exposed to divine knowledge.

## 84:18 — وَٱلْقَمَرِ إِذَا ٱتَّسَقَ

### Activated readings

1. **Visible completion produced by gathering.** **Lexical evidence:** `ق م ر` B001 is the moon and its light; `و س ق` B004 is completion into orderly fullness, especially the moon becoming full. The preceding `وسق`, B002, is gathering the scattered. **Structural cue and role:** the same root moves from night’s transitive gathering to the moon’s intransitive completion. **Abductive move:** I infer a process/result relation between the two forms: gathering supplies the condition from which visible order emerges. **Reading change:** the full moon reads as a completed product of collection, not a static luminous object.

2. **Fullness as correspondence to an assigned pattern.** **Lexical evidence:** `و س ق` B006 is matching another’s level; `ط ب ق` B003 in 84:19 is correspondence or one thing fitting another, while B004 is stage after stage. **Structural cue and role:** lunar completion immediately precedes the assertion of layered passage. **Abductive move:** I infer that the moon models a stage reaching exact correspondence with its phase before passage continues. **Reading change:** `اتسق` means not only becoming full but arriving at the fitting form of its current stage.

3. **Brightness can bewilder rather than yield insight.** **Lexical evidence:** `ق م ر` B005 is sight confused by overwhelming whiteness. `ب ص ر` B002 in 84:15 is inner sight, and `ع ل م` B001 in 84:23 is knowledge; between them, 84:20–22 names non-belief, non-prostration, and denial. **Structural cue and role:** maximal lunar visibility is followed by failed human response despite recitation. **Abductive move:** the packet does not say moonlight causes that failure; I infer an epistemic analogy in which excessive visibility can dazzle rather than guide. **Reading change:** the full moon becomes a test of reception: a sign may be complete and bright while the viewer remains unresponsive.

### Retrospective surprises

84:23 confirms the distinction between brightness and insight: divine knowledge reaches inward storage even where the fully visible sign did not produce assent. 84:25 adds a useful correction to “fullness” as final limit: the moon reaches a measured phase, but the favorable reward negates `م ن ن` B005 measurement.

## 84:19 — لَتَرْكَبُنَّ طَبَقًا عَن طَبَقٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Compelled passage through one condition after another.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر ك ب` B001 is mounting and riding; B003 is undertaking or being burdened by a matter. `ط ب ق` B004 is one state, stage, or rank after another. **Structural cue and role:** the emphatic verb follows the staged twilight-night-moon sequence and precedes the response-and-outcome sequence of 84:20–24. **Abductive move:** the packet does not specify the mount or exact stages; I infer that the addressees both undergo and bear the succession displayed around them. **Reading change:** the ayah describes unavoidable carried passage, with each state also becoming a burden undertaken.

2. **Cumulative layers, not only chronological succession.** **Lexical evidence:** `ر ك ب` B002 is overlap and superposition; `ط ب ق` B002 is layers one above another, and the fixed noun is repeated. **Structural cue and role:** the moon’s fullness has just been assembled into order, and the next ayat add belief, recitation, bodily response, denial, inward storage, and consequence. **Abductive move:** I infer that later states retain and overlay earlier ones rather than simply replacing them. **Reading change:** `طبقا عن طبق` can depict a cumulative structure in which each traversed condition remains part of the load.

3. **Each stage corresponds to what precedes it.** **Lexical evidence:** `ط ب ق` B003 is matching or correspondence; B007 is a gait in which one footfall lands on the trace of another. `و س ق` B004 in 84:18 supplies ordered completion. **Structural cue and role:** the stage statement is followed by refusal to believe or prostrate, denial, known inward storage, and painful consequence (84:20–24). **Abductive move:** the causal matching of response and outcome is inferred; the packet gives sequence, not an explicit rule that each stage answers the previous one. **Reading change:** passage becomes trace-following: later conditions fit and disclose the path laid down by earlier responses.

### Retrospective surprises

84:24–25 makes the correspondence branch of `ط ب ق` B003 concrete by placing the two outcome tracks after the response track. At the same time, 84:25 corrects any assumption that every stage must terminate: `م ن ن` B001 is explicitly negated, leaving the favorable recompense uncut across further passage.

## 84:20 — فَمَا لَهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Failure of trusting assent after accumulated evidence.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء م ن` B002 is trusting assent, confirmation of a report or promise, and inward yielding to truth. **Structural cue and role:** the question follows the Lord’s seeing and the apportioned signs of twilight, gathering night, full moon, and stage upon stage (84:15–19); recitation follows immediately in 84:21. **Abductive move:** I infer that the question treats the preceding sequence as sufficient grounds for assent; no explicit premise list is supplied. **Reading change:** `لا يؤمنون` is not a deficit of available signs but a failure to entrust oneself to what the signs jointly establish.

2. **Refusal of the only stable security within unavoidable change.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء م ن` B001 is settled security, trust, and safe-conduct. `ط ب ق` B004 has just made transition from state to state unavoidable, while the closing contrast gives the believing group an uncut reward (84:25). **Structural cue and role:** non-belief is framed between compulsory transition and the two final outcomes. **Abductive move:** I infer that faith supplies security through change rather than exemption from change; the packet states the contrast but not this function. **Reading change:** the question asks why they refuse the trustworthy mode of passage through the stages they must ride.

### Retrospective surprises

84:21–22 corrects a purely mental account of non-belief: it appears bodily as refusal to lower and verbally as active denial. 84:25 then repeats `ء م ن` positively and couples assent with intentional sound work, revealing the complete response that the question finds absent.

## 84:21 — وَإِذَا قُرِئَ عَلَيْهِمُ ٱلْقُرْءَانُ لَا يَسْجُدُونَ ۩

### Activated readings

1. **Gathered speech calls for an integrated bodily answer.** **Lexical evidence:** `ق ر ء` B001 has two packet variants: variant 2 is gathering speech into recitation, while variant 1 is collecting dispersed things into a whole. The fixed ayah repeats the root in passive verb and proper noun. `س ج د` B001 is humbled prostration, and B003 names the several bodily members and mark of prostration. **Structural cue and role:** the recited collection is placed “upon them,” but their bodies do not collect into the coordinated lowered posture. **Abductive move:** I infer a form-to-response correspondence between verbal integration and bodily integration; the packet does not explicitly state that recitation should assemble the limbs. **Reading change:** `لا يسجدون` becomes failure to let the gathered utterance reorganize the whole body.

2. **A due response-point inside the sequence of stages.** **Lexical evidence:** `ق ر ء` B002’s second variant is an appointed time or cycle, while its first variant is reading and recitation; `ط ب ق` B004 in 84:19 is stage after stage. **Structural cue and role:** the fixed `إذا` presents each occurrence of recitation as a response condition within the ongoing sequence. **Abductive move:** I infer that the cycle variant makes recitation a recurrent due moment at which the next bodily state should occur. **Reading change:** the refusal is not static unbelief only; it is repeated interruption of the proper stage-response cycle.

3. **A posture lexically present in the identity that refuses it.** **Lexical evidence:** fixed `س ج د` B001 is lowered submission; the following `ك ف ر` inventory includes B014, bowed or lowered submission called `تكفير`, even though 84:22 names those who `كفروا`. **Structural cue and role:** explicit non-prostration is immediately followed by a root that contains a dormant submission branch. **Abductive move:** I infer deliberate lexical irony at root level, not equivalence between the two forms. **Reading change:** their refusal reads as suppression of a posture that their own next root can name, sharpening disbelief as embodied withholding.

### Retrospective surprises

84:22 confirms the root-level irony through `ك ف ر` B014’s dormant bowed-submission branch, while its actual verbs enact covering and denial. 84:23 then locates the failed bodily response inside an inward containment process, and 84:25 shows the positive alternative in which assent exits the interior as intentional action.

## 84:22 — بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ يُكَذِّبُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Covering becomes active declaration of falsehood.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك ف ر` B001 is covering and concealment, while B003 is covering or rejecting truth. `ك ذ ب` B002 is declaring a person or report false. **Structural cue and role:** `بل` replaces the unexplained failure to believe and prostrate (84:20–21) with a two-verb diagnosis: first covering, then ongoing denial. **Abductive move:** I infer a process relation between the verbs: concealment prevents receptive contact, and repeated declaration stabilizes that concealment. **Reading change:** the ayah describes an active mechanism of rejection rather than a passive absence of conviction.

2. **Chosen darkness persisting after maximal illumination.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك ف ر` B002 is enveloping darkness; 84:17 gives night (`ل ي ل` B001), and 84:18 gives the full moon and its light (`ق م ر` B001; `و س ق` B004). **Structural cue and role:** natural darkness gathers toward lunar fullness, yet the later group continues to cover and deny even after recitation. **Abductive move:** I infer an optical analogy between darkness and rejection; the packet does not describe their cognition as literal night. **Reading change:** `كفروا` becomes a self-maintained darkness that outlasts the packet’s movement from night to full light.

3. **The submission branch is enacted only as its negation.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك ف ر` B014 is bowed submission; the immediately preceding fixed phrase is `لا يسجدون`, with `س ج د` B001 denoting humble lowering. `ك ذ ب` B004 additionally names a charge that fails to be carried through. **Structural cue and role:** bodily refusal precedes verbal denial, after the command-like pressure of recitation. **Abductive move:** I infer that the latent submission capacity fails its charge and is converted into denial; neither causal statement is explicit. **Reading change:** the root `كفروا` carries an ironic, unrealized alternative: lowering was lexically available, but the actual form covers and rejects instead.

### Retrospective surprises

84:23 shows that ongoing denial is not exhausted in public speech: it is gathered inwardly and remains known. 84:24 converts that concealed-verbal mechanism into announced consequence, while 84:25 supplies its exact opposite, trusting assent that becomes sound work.

## 84:23 — وَٱللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ بِمَا يُوعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **The recited collection enters an inward container known from outside it.** **Lexical evidence:** `و ع ي` B001 is holding meaning in the heart and B002 is holding things in a vessel. `ع ل م` B001 is knowledge as a thing becomes clear. In 84:21, `ق ر ء` B001’s variants supply both collected speech and recitation. **Structural cue and role:** the Quran is recited upon them, they deny it, and God knows what they then contain or store. **Abductive move:** I infer a transfer-and-transformation mechanism: gathered speech reaches the human vessel, but the denial shows that what is retained may not be receptive assent. **Reading change:** `يوعون` becomes deliberate inward containment whose contents remain transparent to divine knowledge.

2. **Public sound contrasted with concealed accumulation.** **Lexical evidence:** `و ع ي` B005 is raised cry or clamor, alongside B001’s silent inward holding; `ق ر ء` B002 supplies audible recitation, and 84:24’s `ب ش ر` B005 supplies announced tidings. **Structural cue and role:** public recitation and public announcement surround the statement about what is privately stored. **Abductive move:** the packet does not say the stored material is speech; I infer that the sound/containment polarity exposes a gap between outward hearing and inward accumulation. **Reading change:** `بما يوعون` marks a hidden reservoir beneath the public verbal exchange.

3. **Knowledge belonging to the one whose worship was refused.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء ل ه` B002 is the fixed divine name, while B001 is worship and the worshipped one; `ع ل م` B001 is comprehensive knowing. **Structural cue and role:** `الله` follows non-prostration and denial, then stands as subject of superior knowledge. **Abductive move:** I infer that the worship branch gives the knowledge claim relational force: the one before whom they do not lower is also the one who knows their interior. **Reading change:** the divine name is not a detached identifier here; it binds refused worship to inescapable inward disclosure.

### Retrospective surprises

84:24 confirms the sound/private contrast by following hidden accumulation with a public command to announce. 84:25 then reveals an alternative container-output relation: `ء م ن` B002 is inward assent, but it does not remain stored; it issues as `ع م ل` B001 intentional work.

## 84:24 — فَبَشِّرْهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ

### Activated readings

1. **Face-brightening tidings deliberately inverted by their object.** **Lexical evidence:** `ب ش ر` B005 is normally face-brightening good news but explicitly permits evil news when qualified or used ironically. `ع ذ ب` B005 is torment and punishment; `ء ل م` B002 is pain-causing or painful. **Structural cue and role:** the command uses the tidings form while its prepositional content supplies the exact negative qualification, and 84:25 immediately gives the favorable alternative. **Abductive move:** no extra irony need be imported; I infer it from the lexical default colliding with the explicit painful object. **Reading change:** `فبشرهم` becomes a formally bright announcement whose content reverses the expected facial effect.

2. **Announcement as the first visible sign of what follows.** **Lexical evidence:** `ب ش ر` B007 is the first visible sign or forerunner of a coming thing; `ع ذ ب` B005 and `ء ل م` B002 define the coming severity. **Structural cue and role:** the command gives news before the consequence is realized, while the previous ayah exposes inward storage. **Abductive move:** I infer that speech converts concealed disposition into an advance, outward sign of its result. **Reading change:** the announcement is not the punishment itself but its first appearing edge.

3. **Painful punishment as exclusion from the uncut alternative.** **Lexical evidence:** besides punishment, `ع ذ ب` B003 is withholding or weaning away; `ء ل م` B001/B002 supplies experienced and caused pain. The next ayah gives `أجر غير ممنون`, with `م ن ن` B001 denoting reward that is not cut or diminished. **Structural cue and role:** the painful announcement and the uncut reward are adjacent opposed outcomes. **Abductive move:** I infer that part of the punishment’s function is deprivation of that continuing recompense; the text supplies contrast, not the causal definition. **Reading change:** `عذاب` can include being held away from the sustaining alternative, not inflicted pain alone.

### Retrospective surprises

84:25 immediately supplies the genuinely favorable counterpart to the inverted `ب ش ر` B005 announcement. Its uncut `م ن ن` B001 reward also sharpens `ع ذ ب` B003 withholding: the painful outcome is newly visible as exclusion from a recompense that neither stops nor diminishes.

## 84:25 — إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمْ أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Trust becomes intentional repair and receives fitting recompense.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء م ن` B002 is trusting assent; `ع م ل` B001 is intentional doing, while B004 is wage for work; `ص ل ح` B001 is soundness and repair against corruption; `ء ج ر` B001 is recompense or wage for work. **Structural cue and role:** the sequence reverses 84:20–22 point by point: instead of non-belief, non-response, and denial, it gives assent followed by purposeful sound action and recompense. **Abductive move:** I infer a functional chain in which inward assent becomes outward repair and the reward answers that work; the ayah states association, not a mechanical wage contract. **Reading change:** the exception describes an integrated faith-action economy rather than two unrelated qualifications.

2. **Reward whose extension is neither cut nor diminished.** **Lexical evidence:** `غ ي ر` B005 supplies negation or otherness, and `م ن ن` B001 supplies cutting, diminution, interruption, and specifically uncut or undiminished reward. `ء ج ر` B001 identifies the recompense. **Structural cue and role:** this ending follows `طبقا عن طبق` and the adverse chain terminating in pain. **Abductive move:** I infer that the negated cut stabilizes the favorable outcome beyond the changing stages; the text does not specify a temporal measure. **Reading change:** `غير ممنون` marks continuity and integrity of reward, not merely its existence.

3. **Reward not spoiled by reproachful reminders.** **Lexical evidence:** `م ن ن` B003 is benefaction and the burden of reminding a recipient of a favor; `غ ي ر` B005 negates that quality. **Structural cue and role:** the same clause calls the outcome `أجر`, a due recompense, then removes the interpersonal burden that can undo a gift. **Abductive move:** I infer that the negation preserves the recipient’s dignity by excluding reproach; the ayah does not state an ensuing reminder. **Reading change:** the reward can be read as a favor that does not become a social debt through being recounted.

4. **Reward not confined to a measured allotment.** **Lexical evidence:** `م ن ن` B005 is a measured weight and allows `ممنون` for what is measured or weighed; `غ ي ر` B005 negates it. `ق س م` B003 earlier apportions shares, and `ح س ب` B001 supplies reckoning. **Structural cue and role:** after a surah segment rich in division and account, the closing reward is grammatically set outside a `ممنون` measure. **Abductive move:** the packet does not say “unlimited”; I infer only that the measure branch is denied and therefore no quantified allotment defines the reward here. **Reading change:** `غير ممنون` can carry an unmetered scope alongside the distinct uncut and unreproached readings above.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah remains to produce a retrospective surprise. The full reread nevertheless confirmed that the three retained `م ن ن` scopes must remain distinct: uncut, unburdened by reproach, and unconfined by a stated measure.
