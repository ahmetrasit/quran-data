# Surah 31 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

## 31:0: بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Focus window: 31:0, 31:2-31:6.

### Activated readings

1. **An invoked designation that governs what follows.** Lexical evidence: `س م و B005` gives the name as a raised designation, while `ء ل ه B002` covers the fixed divine name in invocation formulas. Structural cue: the formula stands before the book, guidance, practices, and competing speech of 31:2-6. Assigned role: the name serves as the public heading under which those later acts and words are evaluated. Abductive move (inferred): opening under a designation transfers its authority and orientation to the discourse that follows. Reading change: the basmalah reads not merely as identification, but as an act of placing the coming textual and practical sequence under a governing name.

2. **Mercy as the operating mode of guidance.** Lexical evidence: the doubled adjectives retain `ر ح م B001`, mercy, tenderness, and beneficence. Structural cue: 31:3 names guidance and `رَحْمَةً` together, while 31:4-5 turn that pair into maintained worship, giving, certainty, and success. Assigned role: mercy explains how the named authority reaches and benefits its recipients. Abductive move (inferred): because mercy is repeated in the opening and then paired with guidance, the ensuing requirements are instruments of beneficent direction rather than detached demands. Reading change: `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` becomes a causal frame for the surah's first ethical pathway.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:3 unexpectedly repeated `ر ح م` as the noun paired with guidance, turning the opening adjectives into an operating relation rather than a static description. 31:6 then placed the divine name inside `سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ` against purchased rival speech, making the opening designation an authority boundary.

## 31:2: تِلْكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ

Focus window: 31:0, 31:2-31:7.

### Activated readings

1. **A joined text that restrains disorder.** Lexical evidence: `ك ت ب B001` is joining one thing to another, `ك ت ب B002` is joined letters in a written book, and `ح ك م B001` is restraint that turns corruption back toward rightness. Structural cue: the compact genitive construction joins signs, book, and wisdom; 31:4-5 then display ordered practice, whereas 31:6-7 display diversion and refusal. Assigned role: the book's joined units form a restraining architecture. Abductive move (inferred): textual joining enables ethical restraint because ordered signs can coordinate otherwise scattered action. Reading change: `ٱلْكِتَٰبِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ` reads as an operative ordering instrument, not only a book possessing abstract wisdom.

2. **Signs that demand both sight and receptive hearing.** Lexical evidence: `ء ي ي B003` is a visible sign or marked unit, while `ح ك م B004` supplies firmness and perfected decisiveness. Structural cue: the same `ءَايَٰت` are recited in 31:7, where the hearer turns away and imagines a weight in his ears. Assigned role: the sign is a stable perceptible marker that can cross from written/visible form into recited/audible sequence. Abductive move (inferred): the later refusal shows that the obstacle lies in reception, not in the signs' lack of perceptibility. Reading change: `ءَايَٰتُ` becomes a multi-modal evidence system whose marked clarity can still be actively resisted.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:7 was the decisive later surprise: the same signs became recited sequence and met a simulated ear-load. It sharpened the book's wisdom into an ordering force whose failure point is active reception, not lack of textual or perceptible form.

## 31:3: هُدًۭى وَرَحْمَةًۭ لِّلْمُحْسِنِينَ

Focus window: 31:0, 31:2-31:8.

### Activated readings

1. **Guidance as a graciously delivered gift.** Lexical evidence: alongside ordinary direction in `ه د ي B001`, `ه د ي B004` is a gracious gift sent to someone close; `ر ح م B001` supplies beneficent tenderness. Structural cue: guidance and mercy are coordinated as two indefinite gifts, 31:4 uses the granting verb `يُؤْتُونَ`, and 31:5 locates guidance as coming from their Lord. Assigned role: the gift branch makes guidance a transferred benefit rather than bare information. Abductive move (inferred): the adjacency of mercy and the local giving language activates the gift image without replacing the directional sense. Reading change: the ayah presents orientation itself as a merciful bestowal that establishes closeness and obligation.

2. **A direction made legible through beneficent action.** Lexical evidence: `ه د ي B002` covers course, manner, and intended direction, and `ح س ن B002` covers skillful, beneficent action. Structural cue: `لِّلْمُحْسِنِينَ` assigns the guidance-mercy pair to agents whose worship and giving are specified in 31:4 and whose faith and sound deeds recur in 31:8. Assigned role: beneficent action is the embodied bearing of the directed course. Abductive move (inferred): practice does not cause the gift, but makes its direction visible as a lived trajectory. Reading change: `لِّلْمُحْسِنِينَ` identifies readers who enact guidance, not merely a favored audience receiving it passively.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:4's granting language activated the dormant gift branch of `ه د ي`, while 31:8 corrected any passive reading of `لِّلْمُحْسِنِينَ` by rejoining trust to intentional sound work. The recipient is therefore both gifted and practically responsive.

## 31:4: ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤْتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُم بِٱلْءَاخِرَةِ هُمْ يُوقِنُونَ

Focus window: 31:0, 31:2-31:9.

### Activated readings

1. **A maintained cycle of worship, transfer, and growth.** Lexical evidence: `ق و م B004` is care, maintenance, and preservation; `ص ل و B003` is prescribed worship; `ء ت ي B002` is granting; and `ز ك و B001/B002` combines growth with purification. Structural cue: the two imperfect verbs are coordinated, and 31:5 names `ف ل ح`, whose `B003` branch is the tiller, before 31:8 introduces gardens. Assigned role: maintained prayer stabilizes the cycle, while transferred zakah circulates what can purify and grow. Abductive move (inferred): the agricultural echoes imply that regular maintenance and giving cultivate the social-moral field. Reading change: the paired practices read as a regenerative system rather than two independent checklist items.

2. **The later end exerting force on present conduct.** Lexical evidence: `ء خ ر B001` is what comes later, `ي ق ن B001` is settled knowledge with doubt removed, and `ق و م B003` is resolve to undertake a matter. Structural cue: the doubled pronoun frame, `وَهُم ... هُمْ`, encloses certainty, while 31:9 supplies enduring residence and a true promise. Assigned role: certainty about the later end anchors the repeated present-tense practices. Abductive move (inferred): settled knowledge of an outcome supplies resolve before that outcome arrives. Reading change: belief in the afterlife is not a third item after ritual and giving; it is the temporal force that keeps both standing.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:8's gardens made the growth branch of `ز ك و` materially visible, and 31:9's enduring true promise made the afterlife clause function retrospectively as the time-horizon sustaining the two present practices.

## 31:5: أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ عَلَىٰ هُدًۭى مِّن رَّبِّهِمْ ۖ وَأُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْمُفْلِحُونَ

Focus window: 31:0, 31:2-31:10.

### Activated readings

1. **Guidance as cultivated supporting ground.** Lexical evidence: `ه د ي B002` is a course or intended direction, `ر ب ب B002` is stage-by-stage nurture and completion, and `ف ل ح B001/B003` is cleaving and tilling the ground. Structural cue: `عَلَىٰ هُدًى` spatially places the group upon guidance; 31:10 later supplies earth, rain, germination, and noble plant kinds. Assigned role: guidance is the ground/course, lordly nurture supplies cultivation, and the successful are the resulting tillers or harvesters. Abductive move (inferred): the later material ecology activates the dormant agricultural mechanism in `مفلحون`. Reading change: success reads as growth produced by sustained cultivation on a divinely nurtured course.

2. **Success as reaching the aim of a traversed route.** Lexical evidence: `ه د ي B002` also carries intended direction, and `ف ل ح B005` is attaining one's aim with lasting good. Structural cue: the demonstrative is repeated on both sides of the ayah, while 31:6 immediately opposes this group with someone who diverts people from `سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ`. Assigned role: the repeated identification binds being on the course to arriving at its proper end. Abductive move (inferred): a stable route is what converts direction into attainment. Reading change: `ٱلْمُفْلِحُونَ` is not a static title; it names those whose present bearing is already aligned with a durable destination.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:10 supplied literal earth, rain, and germination. That later ecology was the strongest surprise in the window: it activated the tilling branch of `ف ل ح` and made being `عَلَىٰ هُدًى` resemble standing on cultivated, supporting ground.

## 31:6: وَمِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ مَن يَشْتَرِى لَهْوَ ٱلْحَدِيثِ لِيُضِلَّ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ بِغَيْرِ عِلْمٍۢ وَيَتَّخِذَهَا هُزُوًا ۚ أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمْ عَذَابٌۭ مُّهِينٌۭ

Focus window: 31:0, 31:2-31:11.

### Activated readings

1. **An attention market that purchases substitution.** Lexical evidence: `ش ر ي B001` is exchange through buying and selling, `ل ه و B001` is occupation that diverts from another concern, and `ح د ث B003` is speech arising as report or conversation. Structural cue: the purchased object is not speech alone but diverting speech, placed against the book and guidance of 31:2-5 and the recited signs of 31:7. Assigned role: exchange acquires a rival stream of attention that can displace the guiding stream. Abductive move (inferred): what is economically bought is a narrative product, but what it functionally captures is audience attention and allegiance. Reading change: the ayah diagnoses misguidance as an organized substitution economy, not casual amusement.

2. **Misguidance by removing landmarks.** Lexical evidence: `ض ل ل B002` is disappearance or hiddenness, `س ب ل B001` is an extended traversed way, and `ع ل م B002` is a distinguishing landmark or marker. Structural cue: the diversion is explicitly `عَن سَبِيلِ` and operates `بِغَيْرِ عِلْمٍ`; 31:10-11 answer with visible, challengeable creation. Assigned role: absent markers make a route disappear from practical view. Abductive move (inferred): the causal method of leading astray is not simply supplying a false destination, but occluding the signs by which the sound route is tracked. Reading change: lack of knowledge becomes loss of navigation infrastructure.

3. **Contempt reversed into abasement.** Lexical evidence: `ء خ ذ B010` is adopting something for oneself, `ه ز ء B001` is mockery, and `ه و ن B003` is humiliation through contempt. Structural cue: the agent adopts the signs/path as an object of ridicule, and the sentence closes with punishment qualified by the same social register in reversed direction. Assigned role: humiliation is the returned form of the contempt first projected outward. Abductive move (inferred): the punishment mirrors the social act so that the mocker's valuation is turned upon him. Reading change: `مُّهِينٌ` is a measured reversal of enacted contempt, not an unrelated penalty adjective.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:7 showed the purchased diversion's likely mechanism as manufactured non-reception, while 31:10-11 answered `بِغَيْرِ عِلْمٍ` with visible creation and a demand to show competing effects. Together they changed misguidance from generic error into active landmark occlusion.

## 31:7: وَإِذَا تُتْلَىٰ عَلَيْهِ ءَايَٰتُنَا وَلَّىٰ مُسْتَكْبِرًۭا كَأَن لَّمْ يَسْمَعْهَا كَأَنَّ فِىٓ أُذُنَيْهِ وَقْرًۭا ۖ فَبَشِّرْهُ بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ

Focus window: 31:2-31:12.

### Activated readings

1. **A sequence actively reversed by its hearer.** Lexical evidence: `ت ل و B001` is following in sequence, `و ل ي B007` is turning away, and `س م ع B003` is understanding and compliant reception. Structural cue: the signs follow one another toward the hearer, but his next movement is a turn in the opposite direction; the doubled `كَأَن` marks a performed counterfactual, as though no hearing occurred. Assigned role: recitation delivers sequence, while turning interrupts its conversion into understanding. Abductive move (inferred): arrogance supplies the directional reversal rather than an actual failure of sound transmission. Reading change: the ayah portrays rejection as an enacted undoing of reception.

2. **Self-magnification manufactured as an ear-load.** Lexical evidence: `ك ب ر B006` is self-magnification and refusal to submit to truth, `ء ذ ن B002` is receptive hearing, and `و ق ر B001/B002` is both ear-heaviness and a borne load. Structural cue: arrogance precedes the two hearing similes, and the second gives the imagined obstruction material weight. Assigned role: the load image turns an inward stance into a bodily impediment. Abductive move (inferred): self-magnification generates the burden that it then treats as if it were an involuntary sensory condition. Reading change: the heavy ear reads as self-produced moral mass, not an excuse of incapacity.

3. **Good-tiding language judicially inverted.** Lexical evidence: `ب ش ر B005` is face-brightening good news and `ب ش ر B007` is a visible forerunner, whereas `ع ذ ب B005` and `ء ل م B002` supply punishment that causes pain. Structural cue: `فَبَشِّرْهُ` is immediately assigned a painful object, while 31:8-9 provide the non-ironic garden and promise that normally fit good tidings. Assigned role: the command announces a sure forerunner while reversing the hearer's expectations. Abductive move (inferred): persistent inversion of the signs is answered by inversion of the announcement formula. Reading change: the irony becomes a precise judicial mirror rather than decorative sarcasm.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:8-9 supplied the garden and true promise that fit non-ironic good tidings, thereby exposing the exact force of `فَبَشِّرْهُ` as reversal. 31:10-11 then shifted from refused hearing to visible creation, showing that changing sensory channel does not repair a willed refusal.

## 31:8: إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمْ جَنَّٰتُ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

Focus window: 31:3-31:13.

### Activated readings

1. **Trust made executable as sound action.** Lexical evidence: `ء م ن B002` is trusting assent and confirmation, `ع م ل B001` is intentional doing, and `ص ل ح B001` is soundness opposed to corruption. Structural cue: faith and work are joined as coordinated completed actions; 31:4 gives their practical grammar, while 31:6-7 show speech and hearing detached from sound response. Assigned role: assent establishes trust, work externalizes it, and soundness qualifies its effect. Abductive move (inferred): confirmation that never crosses into intentional action remains structurally incomplete in this sequence. Reading change: faith reads as a trust that becomes publicly testable through repair-producing work.

2. **The garden as the ecological form of soundness.** Lexical evidence: `ج ن ن B003` is a tree-covered garden, with `ج ن ن B001` contributing protective covering; `ن ع م B001` is pleasant well-being and bestowed favor. Structural cue: 31:10 soon narrates rain, germination, and varied noble growth, while 31:12 frames benefit through gratitude. Assigned role: garden cover materializes the abundant, sheltered condition toward which sound acts point. Abductive move (inferred): the later growth chain activates an ecological analogy between moral repair and a flourishing habitat. Reading change: `جَنَّٰتُ ٱلنَّعِيمِ` becomes the fitting regenerative environment of repaired action, not an arbitrary prize attached to it.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:10's actual rain-to-growth chain made the garden a retrospective ecological image of repaired action. 31:12 then added that grateful reception benefits the self, sharpening bliss as flourishing produced in a sound giver-recipient relation.

## 31:9: خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا ۖ وَعْدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقًّۭا ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ

Focus window: 31:4-31:14.

### Activated readings

1. **A promise made structurally firm.** Lexical evidence: `و ع د B001` is a spoken promise opening expectation, `ح ق ق B005` is making truth stand, and `ح ك م B004` is making firm, perfected, and decisive. Structural cue: the absolute `حَقًّا` fastens the promise before the paired attributes; 31:10-11 then present a created order and a demand for counter-evidence. Assigned role: truth and wisdom convert expectation into warranted stability. Abductive move (inferred): the competence visible in ordered creation underwrites the promised outcome, although the transfer from cosmology to promise is supplied by inference. Reading change: the promise reads as an established commitment backed by demonstrated ordering power.

2. **Permanence as maintained security, not mere length.** Lexical evidence: `خ ل د B001` is enduring permanence, `ع ز ز B001` is invulnerable might, and `ح ك م B001` is restraint that sets right. Structural cue: everlasting residence is followed by attributes of protection and disciplined governance; 31:10's stabilizing mountains supply a nearby material analogy. Assigned role: might prevents defeat of the promised state, while wise restraint preserves its right order. Abductive move (inferred): duration can remain secure only if the sustaining order cannot be overcome or corrupted. Reading change: `خَٰلِدِينَ` gains the sense of continuously secured residence rather than empty unending time.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:10's differentiated stabilizing mechanisms gave material content to a promise made firm, and 31:11's counter-creation challenge supplied an evidentiary test of the promisor's unique competence. 31:12's renewed wisdom language confirmed that firmness here is ordered, not merely forceful.

## 31:10: خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ بِغَيْرِ عَمَدٍۢ تَرَوْنَهَا ۖ وَأَلْقَىٰ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ رَوَٰسِىَ أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمْ وَبَثَّ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ دَآبَّةٍۢ ۚ وَأَنزَلْنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءًۭ فَأَنۢبَتْنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوْجٍۢ كَرِيمٍ

Focus window: 31:5-31:15.

### Activated readings

1. **A contrast between unseen support and visible anchoring.** Lexical evidence: `ع م د B002/B003` is propping by supports and concrete pillars, `ر س و B001` is settling firm, `م ي د B001` is swaying or rocking, and `ر ء ي B001` is seeing. Structural cue: the heavens are presented without supports that are seen, then the earth receives cast firm masses expressly so it does not sway. Assigned role: the first half withholds a visible support mechanism; the second exposes a stabilizing mechanism through its effect. Abductive move (inferred): the juxtaposition invites comparison between stability whose props are unavailable to sight and stability mediated by visible anchors. Reading change: the ayah reads as a study in different modes of support, not a flat inventory of created objects.

2. **A generative chain from descent to diverse noble growth.** Lexical evidence: `ن ز ل B002` is bringing down, `م و ه B001` is water, `ن ب ت B001` is plant growth emerging from earth, `ز و ج B001/B004` is paired counterpart and kind, and `ك ر م B002` is good growth and rain-bearing bounty. Structural cue: descent is followed by causative germination with `فَ`, and the result is distributed across every kind; 31:14's pregnancy, bearing, and weaning later give the sequence a bodily analogue. Assigned role: water mediates emergence, pairing organizes reproduction and diversity, and nobility marks flourishing quality. Abductive move (inferred): the grammatical sequence licenses water-to-growth causation; the reproductive analogy is supplied by the neighboring human cycle. Reading change: creation appears as a connected reproductive ecology rather than disconnected acts of power.

3. **Dispersion held inside an ordered habitat.** Lexical evidence: `ب ث ث B001` is spreading and multiplying into dispersion, `د ب ب B001` is ground movement, and `ك ل ل B003` is encompassing totality. Structural cue: animals are spread through the same stabilized earth that receives water and plant kinds. Assigned role: dispersion populates the habitat while the repeated `كُلِّ` keeps multiplicity within an encompassing order. Abductive move (inferred): stable ground and provision enable scattered life to remain a functioning ecology. Reading change: `بَثَّ` is productive distribution within support constraints, not chaotic scattering.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:14's gestation, accumulated weakness, and weaning retrospectively made plant pairing and emergence part of a wider reproductive mechanism. 31:11 also corrected a merely scenic reading by converting the created order into evidence that must withstand a showing-test.

## 31:11: هَٰذَا خَلْقُ ٱللَّهِ فَأَرُونِى مَاذَا خَلَقَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن دُونِهِۦ ۚ بَلِ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ فِى ضَلَٰلٍۢ مُّبِينٍۢ

Focus window: 31:6-31:16.

### Activated readings

1. **A demand that claims become inspectable products.** Lexical evidence: `خ ل ق B002` is originating and bringing into being, `خ ل ق B007` is fabricating false speech, and `ر ء ي B012` is causing someone to see or show. Structural cue: `خَلْق` is first pointed out, then the same root is repeated in a challenge introduced by `فَأَرُونِي`; 31:6-7 have already staged rival talk and refused reception, while 31:16 will retrieve even a hidden seed. Assigned role: showing is the audit that separates origination from fabricated attribution. Abductive move (inferred): a causal claim about creation should yield an identifiable created effect. Reading change: the ayah becomes an evidentiary test that forces rival speech across the boundary into demonstrable production.

2. **Misplaced agency as visibly clear disorientation.** Lexical evidence: `د و ن B001/B003` is what falls short, below, or besides; `ظ ل م B004/B008` is concrete misplacement and withholding a due; `ض ل ل B001/B002` is departure from the aim and disappearance; and `ب ي ن B004` is becoming clear. Structural cue: lower substitutes are assigned creative agency, then the result is called both lostness and clear. Assigned role: misplacement names the allocation error, lostness names its navigational result, and clarity names how exposed the error becomes under the showing test. Abductive move (inferred): assigning the creator's due to what is below removes the true causal landmark. Reading change: `ضَلَٰلٍ مُّبِينٍ` is a paradox of conspicuous disorientation produced by a specific agency error.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:13 explicitly equated shirk with zulm, confirming that the challenge exposes misplaced creative agency rather than ignorance alone. 31:16 added the surprising correction that divine causal reach includes even what cannot be shown on demand because it is minutely hidden.

## 31:12: وَلَقَدْ ءَاتَيْنَا لُقْمَٰنَ ٱلْحِكْمَةَ أَنِ ٱشْكُرْ لِلَّهِ ۚ وَمَن يَشْكُرْ فَإِنَّمَا يَشْكُرُ لِنَفْسِهِۦ ۖ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِىٌّ حَمِيدٌۭ

Focus window: 31:7-31:17.

### Activated readings

1. **Wisdom expressed as grateful restraint and right placement.** Lexical evidence: `ح ك م B003` is knowledge that reaches truth, `ح ك م B001` is restraint that sets right, and `ش ك ر B001` is recognizing a favor and displaying that recognition in praise and action. Structural cue: the content attached to granted wisdom is the imperative `أَنِ ٱشْكُرْ`; 31:13-17 then unfold warnings, limits, and commands. Assigned role: gratitude is wisdom's first enacted judgment about source and benefit. Abductive move (inferred): correctly recognizing the source of favor restrains the self from misallocating allegiance. Reading change: wisdom reads as an ethical capacity operationalized by gratitude, not merely information deposited in Luqman's mind.

2. **Gratitude as self-nourishing amplification.** Lexical evidence: `ش ك ر B002` is thriving visibly on little provision, `ش ك ر B003` is fullness and abundant milk, and `غ ن ي B001` is self-sufficiency without need. Structural cue: the root is repeated three times and its benefit is reflexively restricted by `فَإِنَّمَا ... لِنَفْسِهِ`; 31:14 adds maternal bearing and weaning, and 31:16 adds the tiny mustard seed. Assigned role: gratitude metabolizes received provision into growth in the grateful self, while divine self-sufficiency blocks any reading of repayment. Abductive move (inferred): the nourishment images explain the explicitly reflexive benefit. Reading change: gratitude becomes a mode by which the recipient flourishes, not compensation needed by the giver.

3. **Ingratitude as covering a favor whose source remains praiseworthy.** Lexical evidence: `ك ف ر B004` is covering over or failing to acknowledge a favor, and `ح م د B001` is praise and thanksgiving for praiseworthy action. Structural cue: gratitude and kufr form a conditional antithesis, but only the human consequence changes; God remains `غَنِيٌّ حَمِيدٌ`. Assigned role: kufr occludes recognition without altering the benefactor's sufficiency or praiseworthiness. Abductive move (inferred): denial changes the receiver's relation to the favor, not the favor's source. Reading change: the closing attributes prevent ingratitude from being imagined as damage inflicted on God.

### Retrospective surprises

- 31:14's maternal nourishment and weaning activated the fullness/milk branch of `ش ك ر`; 31:16's tiny seed activated the little-provision-to-visible-effect branch. 31:17 then displayed wisdom's restraint as a sequence of maintained practice, public discernment, and patience.

## 31:13: وَإِذْ قَالَ لُقْمَٰنُ لِٱبْنِهِۦ وَهُوَ يَعِظُهُۥ يَٰبُنَىَّ لَا تُشْرِكْ بِٱللَّهِ ۖ إِنَّ ٱلشِّرْكَ لَظُلْمٌ عَظِيمٌۭ

Focus window: 31:8-31:18.

### Activated readings

1. **Admonition as relational construction.** Lexical evidence: `ب ن ي B007` is filiation and close derived belonging, `ب ن ي B001` is building by joining parts, and `و ع ظ B001` is a warning reminder that softens the heart. Structural cue: sonship is stated and then repeated in the affectionate diminutive vocative before the prohibition; 31:16-17 repeat that vocative as the counsel develops. Assigned role: filiation supplies the bond, while admonition builds the son's moral structure inside it. Abductive move (inferred): repeated affectionate address is the means by which warning can soften rather than merely coerce. Reading change: Luqman's prohibition reads as patient construction of agency within a relationship, not an isolated command.
