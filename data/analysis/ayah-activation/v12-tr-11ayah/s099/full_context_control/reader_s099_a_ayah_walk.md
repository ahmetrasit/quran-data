# Surah 99 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

## 99:0 - بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

*Focus window: 99:0-99:5.*

### Activated readings

1. **An elevated designation frames the lower realm.** Lexically, fixed `س م و/B005` supplies the name as a raised designation, while its `B001` supplies rising or raised mention; 99:1-2 supply repeated `ء ر ض/B001`, the lower ground opposite the sky. Structurally, `بِسْمِ` precedes the earth's two appearances. The fixed name therefore serves as the upper pole and the earth as the lower stage. The inferred move is that textual precedence maps onto a vertical relation of governance. The opening consequently reads not as a neutral label but as an elevated designation placed over the realm that is about to lose its stability.

2. **Invocation functions as an authorization heading.** Fixed `ء ل ه/B002` identifies the fixed divine name used in invocation, and `B001` adds the worshipped authority; 99:5 later supplies `ر ب ب/B001` (lordship and ownership) and `و ح ي/B004` (divine message). The sequence is invocation, earth-action, then the stated divine commission. The fixed name's role is to source the authority under which those actions occur. The packet supplies that sequence; the inferred arrow is that the opening invocation authorizes the later commission. This makes the basmalah a governing heading for the earth's acts rather than an ornament detached from them.

3. **Mercy acquires a gestational, not merely affective, dimension.** Fixed `ر ح م/B001` supplies mercy and tenderness, while `B003` supplies the womb; the root is doubled in `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`. In 99:2, the earth causes concealed contents to emerge (`خ ر ج/B002`) as its heavy loads (`ث ق ل/B001`), whose dormant `ث ق ل/B007` image is pregnancy becoming heavy. The womb branch assigns mercy the role of protective containment before release. The earth-to-womb analogy, and the claim that disruptive release can complete rather than negate mercy, are inferred. The two mercy names thus also admit a reading of carrying to term and delivery through rupture.

### Retrospective surprises

99:2 produced the main surprise: its earth carrying and releasing `أَثْقَالَهَا` activated fixed `ر ح م/B003` as womb through contextual `ث ق ل/B007`, so the mercy pair gained a material carrying-to-delivery mechanism. Then 99:5 supplied `ر ب ب/B001` and `و ح ي/B004`, correcting any reading in which the elevated divine designation merely watches the lower realm; within this clipped window, it heads a commissioned sequence.

## 99:1 - إِذَا زُلْزِلَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ زِلْزَالَهَا

*Focus window: 99:0-99:6.*

### Activated readings

1. **The ground is rendered as a trembling body.** Fixed `ز ل ز ل/B001` supplies shaking disturbance, while fixed `ء ر ض/B008` unexpectedly supplies trembling or shivering in a person. Structurally, the repeated root in `زُلْزِلَتِ ... زِلْزَالَهَا` encloses `ٱلْأَرْضُ` and gives the disturbance as its own suffixed quake. The earth branch's role is somatic: it makes the subject experience the motion as a body would. The projection of a human tremor onto the earth is the abductive move. The ayah then reads as the earth bodily trembling its particular tremor, not merely as terrain receiving external motion.

2. **The passive shock is a threshold into active testimony.** Fixed `ز ل ز ل/B001` supplies the initial disturbance and fixed `ء ر ض/B001` the lower substrate. The fixed ayah presents the earth as the patient of a passive verb; 99:2 and 99:4 then give it active feminine verbs, `أَخْرَجَتِ` and `تُحَدِّثُ`, and 99:5 supplies the commission `أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا`. The quake's assigned role is to open the transition from affected matter to acting witness. The packet does not state that shaking enables speech, so that directional arrow is inferred. On this reading the quake is a commissioning threshold, not an isolated catastrophe.

3. **A physical oscillation propagates into a temporal and social shock.** Fixed `ز ل ز ل/B002` supplies "time's shocks," and the later repetitions of `يَوْمَئِذٍ` in 99:4 and 99:6 provide an explicit temporal frame. At 99:6, `ن و س/B001` contributes swaying or wavering to `ٱلنَّاسُ`, and `ش ت ت/B001` contributes collective scattering. These branches assign the fixed quake a propagating role: lower ground oscillates, the human collective becomes unstable, and the group separates. The causal propagation from substrate to crowd is inferred, while the roots and sequence are packet-supplied. The quake consequently reads as a shock of time reorganizing society as well as a movement of earth.

### Retrospective surprises

99:4-5 retrospectively changed the passive quake into the first stage of an authorized testimony: the earth later reports because it was addressed. The farther surprise at 99:6 was that `ن و س/B001` and `ش ت ت/B001` carry oscillation into the human collective. That later social motion corrects a catastrophe-only reading of the fixed quake and activates `ز ل ز ل/B002` as a shock belonging to a decisive time.

## 99:2 - وَأَخْرَجَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ أَثْقَالَهَا

*Focus window: 99:0-99:7.*

### Activated readings

1. **Extraction begins an evidentiary pipeline.** Fixed `خ ر ج/B002` supplies causing something concealed to come out. Later, 99:4 supplies disclosure (`ح د ث/B006`) and tested inner knowledge reported outward (`خ ب ر/B001`), while 99:6 supplies causative showing (`ر ء ي/B012`). The fixed extraction's role is the material first stage of concealment becoming evidence: emergence, report, then display. The packet supplies those ordered operations; the inference is that they process one underlying record rather than unrelated objects. `أَخْرَجَتِ` therefore reads as disclosure in material form, not only spatial expulsion.

2. **The earth's plural loads resolve into exact, minute weights.** Fixed `ث ق ل/B001` supplies heaviness, and `B004` supplies a standard weight and the act of determining it. At the far edge of this focus window, 99:7 repeats the root in `مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ` after 99:6 has named people's deeds (`ع م ل/B001`). The structural movement is from the earth's plural macro-loads to one tiny measured unit. The weight branch assigns `أَثْقَالَهَا` an accounting role. The identification of those loads with assessable human outputs is inferred, but the root recurrence and deed sequence are supplied. The earth's burdens thus become an inventory capable of exact moral measurement.

3. **The earth behaves as a gestational vessel that delivers what it carried.** Fixed `ء ر ض/B002` supplies fertile, productive ground, fixed `ث ق ل/B007` supplies pregnancy becoming heavy, and fixed `خ ر ج/B001` supplies emergence from within. The opening `ر ح م/B003` provides the womb image, and 99:7's `ذ ر ر/B004` adds gentle emergence of herbage from earth. Together these material images assign the earth the role of a containing and productive body. The earth-womb equivalence and the treatment of convulsive expulsion as delivery are abductive moves. The ayah consequently permits "loads" to be read as carried contents reaching emergence, not merely alien debris being discarded.

4. **The output can be read exploratorily as a due yield.** Fixed `خ ر ج/B003` supplies wealth, yield, levy, or rent paid out in a known amount, while fixed `ء ر ض/B002` supplies fertile ground. The earth's `أَخْبَارَهَا` in 99:4 carries the dormant `خ ب ر/B003` image of cultivating land for a known share of its produce, and 99:6-7 connect the sequence to intentional acts (`ع م ل/B001`) and measurement (`ث ق ل/B004`). The assigned role is agricultural-accounting: the earth yields what human work has put into its domain. The claim that deeds function like cultivated deposits is inferred and makes this reading exploratory. It changes `أَثْقَالَهَا` from undifferentiated burdens into a due, measured return.

### Retrospective surprises

99:4 and 99:6 made the expelled contents newly legible as an evidence stream: disclosure and report lead to possessed deeds being shown. The strongest retrospective activation came at 99:7, where the same `ث ق ل` root reappears as `مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ`; this corrected an initial macro-material reading of `أَثْقَالَهَا` by revealing a load that can be resolved into exact individual measures. The cultivation branch in 99:4 also made the exploratory due-yield reading visible.

## 99:3 - وَقَالَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ مَا لَهَا

*Focus window: 99:0-99:8.*

### Activated readings

1. **The human is a perceiver whose perception must be corrected.** Fixed `ء ن س/B002` supplies becoming aware by seeing, sensing, or hearing. The question `مَا لَهَا` follows the material disturbance, but 99:6 later uses causative showing (`ر ء ي/B012`) and 99:7-8 use direct seeing (`ر ء ي/B001`). The fixed human noun's role is therefore epistemic: it names the creature trying to bring an unfamiliar event into awareness. The inference is that the failed first perception produces the question and that the later display answers it. `ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ` thus reads less as a generic species label and more as a perceptual subject placed under correction.

2. **The question marks a rupture of familiarity.** Fixed `ء ن س/B003` supplies familiar comfort that removes estrangement. Structurally, the ordinarily inhabited earth first convulses and expels contents, after which the human asks what is with it; the familiar ground has become an object of inquiry. The dormant familiarity branch assigns the human an affective relation to the earth, and `مَا لَهَا` marks that relation's failure. The assumption that prior earthly stability generated familiarity is inferred. The exclamation then reads as estrangement from what had been the human's most familiar material setting.

3. **Human speech opens a commissioned question-and-testimony exchange.** Fixed `ق و ل/B001` supplies articulated utterance, while `B011` supplies saying with the force of supposition, especially in a question. In the next two ayat the earth gives report-like speech (`ح د ث/B003`) grounded in tested inner knowledge (`خ ب ر/B001`), and that speech is traced to hidden imparting (`و ح ي/B001`). The fixed saying's role is the conjectural first turn; the earth's report supplies the informed response. The inference is that the later report answers this precise question. The ayah consequently reads as the opening of an evidentiary exchange rather than only an uncontrolled cry.

### Retrospective surprises

99:4-5 supplied an informed, commissioned answer to the human's conjectural question, turning the apparent monologue into an exchange. More surprisingly, 99:6-8 shift from the human's attempted awareness (`ء ن س/B002`) to being made to see and then directly seeing (`ر ء ي/B012`, `B001`). The repeated singular conditionals in 99:7-8 further correct a species-level reading: the perceptual correction reaches each acting person.

## 99:4 - يَوْمَئِذٍۢ تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا

*Focus window: 99:0-99:8.*

### Activated readings

1. **The event is converted into its own narration.** Fixed `ح د ث/B001` supplies an event coming into being, `B005` a calamity of time, and `B003` speech arising as report. The temporal `يَوْمَئِذٍ` points back to the quake and extraction, while the form `تُحَدِّثُ` turns that occurrence-root into an act of telling. The root's assigned role is to join happening and narration: what occurs becomes what is recounted. The claim that the material event itself encodes the report is inferred. Earth therefore does not merely comment on a calamity external to its speech; its event becomes its testimony.

2. **The earth discloses tested interior knowledge.** Fixed `ح د ث/B006` supplies disclosure or bringing out, and fixed `خ ب ر/B001` supplies both report and tested knowledge of an affair's interior rather than its outward appearance. This follows concealment being made to emerge (`خ ر ج/B002`) and precedes deeds being made visible (`ر ء ي/B012`). The two fixed words serve as the epistemic middle stage between extraction and display. The inference is that the earth has retained an interior record that its report now externalizes. `أَخْبَارَهَا` accordingly reads as evidentiary knowledge from within the earth, not generic news.

3. **The report can function as an agricultural ledger.** Fixed `خ ب ر/B002` supplies soft, low, water-gathering ground, and `B003` supplies cultivation for a known share of what the earth produces. Repeated `ٱلْأَرْضُ` activates the ground image; 99:6-8 then name intentional deeds (`ع م ل/B001`) and exact weight (`ث ق ل/B004`). The fixed `أَخْبَارَهَا` takes the role of a yield-account: the earth tells what each person's work produced. The move from human acts to planted or cultivated inputs is inferred, so this is exploratory. It makes the news a reckoning of produce rather than a detached verbal chronicle.

4. **A private interior becomes a public and individualized tale.** Fixed `ح د ث/B004` supplies becoming a tale people tell. The earth speaks in the singular here, after human `ق و ل/B001`, but 99:6 disperses the people and 99:7-8 end with each person seeing the relevant act. The assigned role of `تُحَدِّثُ` is dissemination: one earth-record reaches separated recipients. The transmission from singular testimony to every individual is an inferred arrow. The report thus reads as the conversion of hidden local history into a universally distributed public record.

### Retrospective surprises

99:5 immediately corrected any reading of autonomous earth-speech by supplying its cause and authorization in `أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا`. Ayat 99:6-8 then revealed the report's practical output: deeds are displayed to dispersed people and resolved into singular, minute instances. That later movement activated fixed `ح د ث/B004` as a tale distributed to people and sharpened `خ ب ر/B001` from general news into evidence that culminates in sight.

## 99:5 - بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا

*Focus window: 99:0-99:8.*

### Activated readings

1. **A hidden commission causes public disclosure.** Fixed `و ح ي/B001` supplies covert imparting of knowledge and `B004` a divine message; the causal `بِأَنَّ` links that imparting to the earth's report in 99:4. Later, 99:6-8 move from report to deeds being shown and seen (`ر ء ي/B012`, `B001`). The fixed revelation's role is both causal and authorizing: it gives the earth knowledge and licenses its testimony. The packet states the causal link to speech, but the extension from speech to the later visual display is inferred. `أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا` therefore governs a covert-to-public evidentiary chain.

2. **The earth can be read exploratorily as an inscribed archive.** Fixed `و ح ي/B003` supplies writing and inscription, while `B010` supplies a secret or plainly incised message in stone. The recipient is the earth (`لَهَا`), whose own reports precede the showing of possessed deeds (`أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ`). Those features assign the inscription branches a storage role: acts become a legible terrestrial record. The packet never explicitly says that deeds were written into earth, so inscription-as-recording is the abductive step. On this reading the revelation is not only a momentary command to speak but the protocol by which earth can retain and expose a record.

3. **Lordship administers a staged completion through apparent destruction.** Fixed `ر ب ب/B001` supplies ownership and obeyed authority; `B002` supplies repairing, administering, nurturing, and completing something stage by stage. The surah's sequence moves through shaking, extraction, report, human departure, display, and exact seeing. The fixed `رَبَّكَ` takes the role of process governor holding those stages together. The inference is that the disruptive stages serve completion rather than constituting uncontrolled collapse. "Your Lord" consequently reads as the one who brings earth and human action to their completed disclosure, not only the issuer of a single command.

4. **Material upheaval becomes a mode of indication before speech.** Fixed `و ح ي/B002` supplies gesture, hint, or indication that makes an addressee understand without direct statement. The earth first shakes and expels; only afterward does 99:4 name its report. The gesture branch assigns those earlier material acts a nonverbal communicative role. The claim that they enact the divine indication is inferred, since the packet explicitly attaches revelation only as the cause of reporting. This lets `أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا` encompass an embodied sequence of signs as well as propositional speech.

### Retrospective surprises

99:6-8 showed that the commission does not terminate in speech: it proceeds to the showing of deeds and to direct individual sight. This retrospectively strengthened `و ح ي/B003` and `B010` as an exploratory inscription/archive mechanism, because the communicated earth-record proves displayable at exact scale. The same later completion also sharpened `ر ب ب/B002`: staged administration, rather than a one-off command, best accounts for the full sequence.

## 99:6 - يَوْمَئِذٍۢ يَصْدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًۭا لِّيُرَوْا۟ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ

*Focus window: 99:1-99:8.*

### Activated readings

1. **The issuing is an ordered departure after encounter.** Fixed `ص د ر/B003` supplies departing a watering-place, land, or affair after first coming to it. The repeated `يَوْمَئِذٍ` aligns this movement with the earth's report, and `لِّيُرَوْا۟` gives the departure an explicit purpose. The branch assigns `يَصْدُرُ` a two-phase role: prior encounter followed by dispatch. A prior gathering or arrival is not stated in the packet and is the abductive assumption. The people therefore read as leaving a common station under an ordered purpose, not simply bursting out in random motion.

2. **Ground oscillation propagates into collective instability and separation.** Fixed `ن و س/B001` supplies swaying or wavering to the root assigned to `ٱلنَّاسُ`, and fixed `ش ت ت/B001` supplies scattering of a gathered group. Within this ayah's window, 99:1 has already supplied physical shaking (`ز ل ز ل/B001`). The dormant swaying branch makes the people the moving middle term between the quake and their dispersed state. The causal arrow from ground-motion to collective oscillation to social separation is inferred. `ٱلنَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا` thus preserves the quake's motion at the human scale.

3. **Scattering performs individualized partition rather than mere disorder.** Fixed `ص د ر/B006` supplies a portion or group, while fixed `ش ت ت/B003` supplies distance and loss of union. The plural crowd and `أَشْتَاتًا` are followed in 99:7-8 by the twice-repeated singular conditional `مَن يَعْمَلْ ... يَرَهُ`. The fixed branches assign dispersion a sorting role: the collective is divided into accountable units. The inference is that separation is purposive because it enables individual attribution. The ayah consequently reads as an adjudicative partition of people, not only a chaotic scattering.

4. **People are confronted with outputs that issued from themselves.** Fixed `ص د ر/B004` supplies a source from which actions issue. In the same ayah, fixed `ع م ل/B001` names intentional acts, the possessive `أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ` attaches those acts to the people, and fixed `ر ء ي/B012` makes them be shown; 99:7-8 then shift to each actor directly seeing (`ر ء ي/B001`). These elements form an actor-output feedback structure. The packet does not state that the semantic "source" is each person, so that return arrow is inferred. `يَصْدُرُ` then resonates with deeds issuing from agents and being presented back to those agents.

5. **Visibility is taken out of the actor's control.** Fixed `ر ء ي/B005` supplies acting so that people will see, while fixed `B012` supplies causing another to see. Here `ٱلنَّاسُ`, `أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ`, and the causative/passive `يُرَوْا۟` reverse the ostentatious arrangement: people do not control a display to others; they are made recipients of the display of their own acts. The branch serves as a corrective foil. The inference is that this compelled audience replaces socially managed visibility. The purpose clause therefore reads as exposure to one's work, not performance before a chosen audience.

### Retrospective surprises

The twice-repeated singular `مَن يَعْمَلْ ... يَرَهُ` in 99:7-8 was the decisive later correction: it showed that `أَشْتَاتًا` is not exhausted by chaotic dispersal but can function as partition for person-specific attribution. The shift from fixed `يُرَوْا۟` (`ر ء ي/B012`, being shown) to later `يَرَهُ` (`B001`, directly seeing) also completed the actor-output feedback loop and confirmed that the purpose clause removes control over visibility.

## 99:7 - فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًۭا يَرَهُۥ

*Focus window: 99:2-99:8.*

### Activated readings

1. **A cosmic load is resolved into a minute standard unit.** Fixed `ث ق ل/B004` supplies a known measure and the act of determining weight, while `B001` supplies heaviness. Five ayat earlier, 99:2 used the same root for the earth's plural `أَثْقَالَهَا`; here it is narrowed by fixed `ذ ر ر/B001`, a single tiny mote or ant. The recurrence assigns the fixed `مِثْقَالَ` a scale-conversion role, from the earth's macro-load to an individual micro-measure. The inference is that both uses belong to one accounting system. The ayah thus makes the immense disclosure exact at the smallest retained scale.

2. **Social dispersal and particle dispersal become one distributive mechanism.** Fixed `ذ ر ر/B001` supplies tiny scattered motes and `B002` the scattering of grains or powder. Immediately before, `ش ت ت/B001` has divided the people into dispersed groups; the fixed ayah then uses singular `مَن`, a singular act, and the singular object pronoun in `يَرَهُ`. The particle branch's role is to furnish an accounting unit that matches the socially separated recipient. The inferred move is that crowd-partition enables act-attribution. `ذَرَّةٍ` consequently marks distributive precision, not size alone.

3. **Even concealed good gently emerges into sight.** Fixed `ذ ر ر/B004` supplies the first gentle emergence of herbage from earth, and fixed `ر ء ي/B001` supplies sensory or insightful seeing. Within the focus window, 99:2 has the earth bring out concealed loads (`خ ر ج/B002`), 99:4 has it disclose reports, and 99:6 has deeds shown. The fixed particle's role is a minimal emergent shoot in that extraction-to-visibility sequence. The inference is that a good act can remain latent like growth under ground before disclosure. The ayah then reads as the surfacing of even the least visible good, not only the recollection of an already public act.

4. **Good is a chosen branch whose consequence remains distinct.** Fixed `خ ي ر/B001` supplies desired, beneficial good opposed to evil, while `B003` supplies choosing or seeking the better option; fixed `ع م ل/B001` marks intentional doing. The next ayah repeats the full construction but substitutes fixed `ش ر ر/B001`. This exact syntactic fork assigns `خَيْرًا` the role of a selected moral path whose result is preserved without merger into its opposite. The inference is that the paired conditionals formalize a choice structure. Good therefore reads not as vague benefit but as an intentional alternative that remains visible down to a mote's weight.

5. **The final audience corrects the dormant possibility of ostentation.** Fixed `ر ء ي/B005` supplies doing something so people will see it; fixed `B001` supplies direct seeing. The prior ayah puts people and their deeds into a compelled display, but this ayah ends with the actor seeing the good itself. The dormant ostentation branch serves as a reversed possibility: an act once oriented toward others' sight is finally presented to its doer. That change of audience is inferred rather than explicit. The reading is therefore exploratory, but it makes `يَرَهُ` a correction of controlled public appearance by unavoidable self-confrontation.

### Retrospective surprises

99:8 repeats the entire measuring and seeing construction while replacing only good with fixed `ش ر ر/B001`. That exact later substitution activated fixed `خ ي ر/B001` as a true opposed alternative and `B003` as a choice fork; it also corrected any good-specific account of the mote-scale mechanism, since identical precision and visibility govern the other branch.

## 99:8 - وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍۢ شَرًّۭا يَرَهُۥ

*Focus window: 99:3-99:8.*

### Activated readings

1. **Exact formal symmetry removes any threshold asymmetry.** Fixed `ش ر ر/B001` supplies evil as the opposite of good; fixed `ث ق ل/B004` supplies exact known weight, fixed `ذ ر ر/B001` the tiny unit, fixed `ع م ل/B001` intentional action, and fixed `ر ء ي/B001` seeing. The construction duplicates 99:7 and changes only `خَيْرًا` to `شَرًّا`. The repeated frame assigns evil the same measurement and visibility mechanism as good. No additional causal assumption is needed beyond treating formal identity as procedural identity. The ayah therefore denies a reading in which small evil escapes by a different threshold.

2. **Aggregate evil is disassembled into attributable particles.** Fixed `ش ر ر/B004` supplies shredding, splitting, and shaking loose; fixed `ذ ر ر/B002` supplies scattering powder or grains, and fixed `ث ق ل/B004` supplies measurement. The preceding `أَشْتَاتًا` (`ش ت ت/B001`) has already separated the people before this ayah separates an act down to a mote's weight. These branches assign `شَرًّا` a disaggregation role within the measuring procedure. The inference is that the disclosure process decomposes aggregate conduct into individually attributable pieces. Evil consequently cannot remain hidden inside a larger undifferentiated whole.

3. **A tiny evil can be minute in mass yet expansive in propagation.** Fixed `ش ر ر/B003` supplies flying sparks, while fixed `ذ ر ر/B002` supplies scattering fine material. The nearby dispersed people provide a social field across which a small originating unit could spread. The branches supply spark and scattering; the arrow from one small act to wider effects is inferred. This changes `مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا` from "negligible harm" into a compact source whose consequences need not share its small scale.

4. **Hidden evil is laid out for inspection.** Fixed `ش ر ر/B002` supplies spreading something in the sun to dry, and fixed `ر ء ي/B001` supplies seeing. The local sequence moves from earth-report in 99:4, through deeds being shown in 99:6, to the direct `يَرَهُ` here. The spreading branch gives evil the role of material placed on an exposed surface. The move from drying-display to evidentiary exposure is analogical and inferred. The final verb then reads as inspection of what has been laid open, not merely inward remembrance.

5. **Evil can be a trailing, weighted attachment to its actor.** Fixed `ش ر ر/B006` supplies trailing appendages and burdens, while fixed `ث ق ل/B001` supplies heaviness and fixed `ع م ل/B001` intentional action. The previous possessive `أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ` attaches deeds to their agents, and this ayah's object pronoun carries the evil through to `يَرَهُ`. The branch's role is attachment: the act trails the doer as a burden until seen. That persistence is inferred, so this reading is exploratory. It makes the measured evil something still borne by its source rather than an event severed from the actor.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah falls inside this fixed ayah's clipped window, so no later surprise emerged.
