# Surah 27 Ayah Walk - Eleven-Ayah Focus

Each activated reading below is restricted to the fixed ayah's clipped eleven-ayah window. Structural cues are packet observations; directional links identified as assumptions are abductive inferences. The retrospective entries were added only after the complete packet-order walk.

## 27:0 - بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `س م و` B005, a name as a raised designation. **Structural cue:** The naming formula immediately precedes the written signs and book in 27:1, while mercy is repeated inside the formula. **Assigned role:** The name raises the discourse under a declared authority and disposition before any claim is made. **Abductive assumption:** The opening designation governs how the following signs are to be received, rather than functioning as an unattached label. **Reading change:** The formula becomes a textual threshold that places reading under named mercy.

### Retrospective surprises

27:30 repeats the complete formula inside Solomon's letter. That later reuse confirms that the designation can govern a new communicative act within the surah, not only open the surah itself.

## 27:1 - طسٓ ۚ تِلْكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلْقُرْءَانِ وَكِتَابٍۢ مُّبِينٍ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ك ت ب` B001, joining one thing to another. **Structural cue:** The ayah coordinates signs, recitation, and a clarifying book; 27:2-3 immediately convert that textual complex into guidance, good news, and sustained practices. **Assigned role:** Joining makes discrete signs into an operative textual arrangement. **Abductive assumption:** Their clarifying force arises partly from ordered relation, not from isolated tokens. **Reading change:** The book is read as an architecture of joined signs whose sequence produces direction.

### Retrospective surprises

The letter in 27:28-31 and the comprehensive book in 27:75 later separate two scales of `ك ت ب`: a joined message that provokes a response and a record in which nothing absent escapes inscription.

## 27:2 - هُدًۭى وَبُشْرَىٰ لِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ه د ي` B001, gentle guidance toward a way or truth. **Structural cue:** 27:1 supplies the joined signs, 27:3 gives prayer, almsgiving, and certainty as enacted continuations, and 27:4 shows aimless wavering when assent is absent. **Assigned role:** Guidance supplies direction that becomes visible in maintained conduct. **Abductive assumption:** The sequence from text to practice is a directional arrow inferred from the adjacency; the packet does not state that the practices exhaust guidance. **Reading change:** Guidance is not merely information delivered to believers but a course stabilized through repeated action.

### Retrospective surprises

In 27:35, the same root appears as B004, a gracious gift sent to another. The diplomatic gift unexpectedly materializes guidance as something sent to disclose another party's orientation.

## 27:3 - ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤْتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُم بِٱلْءَاخِرَةِ هُمْ يُوقِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ق و م` B004, care, guardianship, and preservation. **Structural cue:** Prayer is causatively maintained beside giving and settled certainty; 27:4 then depicts people wandering after their deeds are made attractive. **Assigned role:** The form `يُقِيمُونَ` presents prayer as something actively kept upright and preserved. **Abductive assumption:** Preserved practice protects orientation by repeatedly reconnecting deed and final horizon. **Reading change:** Establishing prayer becomes maintenance work on a moral direction, not a one-time posture.

### Retrospective surprises

27:39-40 later contrast standing from one's station with an act completed before an eye returns. Those ayat expose bodily or positional capacity as limited, sharpening the present emphasis on sustained guardianship rather than momentary standing.

## 27:4 - إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ بِٱلْءَاخِرَةِ زَيَّنَّا لَهُمْ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ فَهُمْ يَعْمَهُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ز ي ن` B002, making beauty show. **Structural cue:** The beautified deeds are followed by `يَعْمَهُونَ`, `ع م ه` B001, bewildered wavering without guidance; this stands between the directional practices of 27:2-3 and the loss named in 27:5. **Assigned role:** Displayed attractiveness becomes a perceptual surface that sustains wandering. **Abductive assumption:** The inferred arrow is not beauty to error in general, but self-validating presentation to reduced corrective feedback in this construction. **Reading change:** Adornment becomes an epistemic enclosure, not merely decoration.

### Retrospective surprises

27:24 repeats the adornment of deeds and then explicitly supplies the intermediate mechanism: Satan's adornment blocks the path, after which the people do not receive guidance.

## 27:5 - أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَهُمْ سُوٓءُ ٱلْعَذَابِ وَهُمْ فِى ٱلْءَاخِرَةِ هُمُ ٱلْأَخْسَرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `س و ء` B002, harm and distress. **Structural cue:** The ayah turns the wandering of 27:4 into an adverse outcome, while 27:2-3 had joined guidance to a final-horizon certainty and 27:6 returns to receiving the recitation from wisdom and knowledge. **Assigned role:** `سُوءُ` marks harm as the realized condition of a misdirected course. **Abductive assumption:** The surrounding sequence licenses a trajectory from enclosed perception to loss, though the exact causal interval remains unstated. **Reading change:** The punishment is read as the terminal disclosure of direction, not an isolated penalty notice.

### Retrospective surprises

27:11 later places `سُوء` inside a replaceable sequence: wrongdoing can be followed by a changed good. That prevents the present adverse trajectory from being mistaken for an unalterable identity.

## 27:6 - وَإِنَّكَ لَتُلَقَّى ٱلْقُرْءَانَ مِن لَّدُنْ حَكِيمٍ عَلِيمٍ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ل ق ي` B011, receiving or imparting speech. **Structural cue:** The emphatic passive reception of the recitation follows its description as guidance and precedes Moses' encounter with a perceived fire and then a voice in 27:7-9. **Assigned role:** Reception identifies revelation as an encounter in which speech arrives from a wise, knowing source. **Abductive assumption:** The Moses sequence functions locally as a concrete model of received address, while not equating the two events in every respect. **Reading change:** Revelation becomes a directed transfer that repositions its recipient, not self-produced textual knowledge.

### Retrospective surprises

27:28-29 later activates `ل ق ي` B005 when a letter is cast and passively received. The material delivery clarifies how a placed message can initiate an encounter while preserving a distinct sender and receiver.

## 27:7 - إِذْ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِأَهْلِهِۦٓ إِنِّىٓ ءَانَسْتُ نَارًۭا سَـَٔاتِيكُم مِّنْهَا بِخَبَرٍ أَوْ ءَاتِيكُم بِشِهَابٍۢ قَبَسٍۢ لَّعَلَّكُمْ تَصْطَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ء ن س` B002, perceiving something into awareness by sight or sense. **Structural cue:** Moses first construes the perceived fire as a source of news or a burning brand; 27:8-9 then turns the same approach into an addressed, blessed encounter. **Assigned role:** Perception opens action but does not yet determine the identity or function of what is perceived. **Abductive assumption:** The utility forecast is a provisional human model corrected by subsequent address. **Reading change:** `ءَانَسْتُ` becomes the beginning of interpretation, not the completion of recognition.

### Retrospective surprises

The transparent floor in 27:44 later produces another perceptual construal that must be corrected. Together the episodes show sensed visibility as actionable but revisable.

## 27:8 - فَلَمَّا جَآءَهَا نُودِىَ أَنۢ بُورِكَ مَن فِى ٱلنَّارِ وَمَنْ حَوْلَهَا وَسُبْحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ب ر ك` B004, fixed and growing good or blessing. **Structural cue:** The fire sought for portable utility in 27:7 becomes a center whose blessing includes who is in it and around it; 27:9 identifies the speaker and 27:10 issues a transformative command. **Assigned role:** Blessing settles around an encounter and expands its operative range beyond the perceived object. **Abductive assumption:** The shift from taking a brand to standing within a blessed field reverses Moses' initial direction of use. **Reading change:** The fire is no longer principally a resource to carry away but a site that relocates and commissions the perceiver.

### Retrospective surprises

27:60 later depicts rain producing gardens of visible growth. That material growth gives a concrete downstream analogue for B004's combination of settledness and increase.

## 27:9 - يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ح ك م` B001, restraining and turning back in order to set right. **Structural cue:** Divine might and wisdom are conjoined immediately before Moses' flight is stopped and his fear answered in 27:10-11. **Assigned role:** Wisdom qualifies power as force governed toward correction. **Abductive assumption:** The local sequence permits restraint to be read as the mechanism by which might becomes trustworthy address rather than uncontrolled threat. **Reading change:** `ٱلْحَكِيمُ` is not only possession of knowledge; it is power held to a rectifying course.

### Retrospective surprises

27:78 later joins decisive judgment, `ح ك م`, might, and knowledge. It confirms that the restraint latent here can become a settlement between disputing parties.

## 27:10 - وَأَلْقِ عَصَاكَ ۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهَا تَهْتَزُّ كَأَنَّهَا جَآنٌّۭ وَلَّىٰ مُدْبِرًۭا وَلَمْ يُعَقِّبْ ۚ يَٰمُوسَىٰ لَا تَخَفْ إِنِّى لَا يَخَافُ لَدَىَّ ٱلْمُرْسَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ع ق ب` B003, turning back on the heel. **Structural cue:** Moses turns away and does not return; the direct prohibition of fear interrupts that trajectory. Within the same window, 27:14 uses `عَاقِبَةُ`, B006, for the outcome of corruption. **Assigned role:** The heel image makes immediate bodily retreat the first course that address must reverse. **Abductive assumption:** The later outcome vocabulary in 27:14 lets the physical rearward turn expose how a momentary response can initiate a longer trajectory. **Reading change:** “Not turning back” becomes a directional crisis answered by commissioning speech.

### Retrospective surprises

27:51 and 27:69 repeatedly command inspection of an `عَاقِبَة`. Those later uses enlarge the brief heel-turn into a surah-wide demand to read where courses finally lead.

## 27:11 - إِلَّا مَن ظَلَمَ ثُمَّ بَدَّلَ حُسْنًۢا بَعْدَ سُوٓءٍۢ فَإِنِّى غَفُورٌۭ رَّحِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ب د ل` B002, alteration or transformation. **Structural cue:** The construction preserves sequence and agency: wrongdoing, then changed good after harm, then forgiveness and mercy. It is flanked by the transformed staff in 27:10 and the altered appearance of the hand in 27:12. **Assigned role:** Replacement provides a moral transition without erasing the prior state. **Abductive assumption:** The adjacent material transformations make ethical change more process-like and concrete, while not reducing it to spectacle. **Reading change:** Repentant change is read as a retained subject entering a new form, not as a relabeling of the old act.

### Retrospective surprises

27:41 later orders the queen's throne altered into an unfamiliar state. That controlled alteration demonstrates how changed form can expose, rather than destroy, continuity of identity.

## 27:12 - وَأَدْخِلْ يَدَكَ فِى جَيْبِكَ تَخْرُجْ بَيْضَآءَ مِنْ غَيْرِ سُوٓءٍۢ ۖ فِى تِسْعِ ءَايَٰتٍ إِلَىٰ فِرْعَوْنَ وَقَوْمِهِۦٓ ۚ إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا۟ قَوْمًۭا فَٰسِقِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `س و ء` B003, bodily affliction, explicitly negated by `مِنْ غَيْرِ سُوءٍ`; `ب ي ض` B001 supplies whiteness. **Structural cue:** The hand exits visibly white but without pathology, is counted among signs, and is followed by the recipients' attempt in 27:13 to classify the signs as manifest sorcery. **Assigned role:** The negation blocks a bodily-disease explanation before hostile relabeling occurs. **Abductive assumption:** Removing an ordinary pathological cause intensifies the demand to interpret the visible change as a sign. **Reading change:** Whiteness becomes a discriminating sign whose reading depends on causal classification, not an undifferentiated marvel.

### Retrospective surprises

27:44 later makes a transparent material surface look like deep water. That episode reinforces the need to distinguish what is visibly present from the causal category assigned to it.

## 27:13 - فَلَمَّا جَآءَتْهُمْ ءَايَٰتُنَا مُبْصِرَةًۭ قَالُوا۟ هَٰذَا سِحْرٌۭ مُّبِينٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ب ص ر` B002, inner sight or insight, activated alongside B001 visible seeing. **Structural cue:** The signs themselves are `مُبْصِرَةً`, yet 27:14 says the recipients deny them while their own selves have settled certainty; 27:18 later shows another group failing to perceive an approaching force. **Assigned role:** The causative or illuminating form presents the signs as making a matter seeable, while reception remains separable from visibility. **Abductive assumption:** The packet supports a split between disclosed evidence and status-governed acknowledgment. **Reading change:** A clarifying sign can successfully disclose and still be verbally recoded as sorcery.

### Retrospective surprises

27:86 later calls daytime `مُبْصِرًا`. The echo makes visibility an environmental affordance rather than a guarantee that observers will form the right judgment.

## 27:14 - وَجَحَدُوا۟ بِهَا وَٱسْتَيْقَنَتْهَآ أَنفُسُهُمْ ظُلْمًۭا وَعُلُوًّۭا ۚ فَٱنظُرْ كَيْفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلْمُفْسِدِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ع ل و` B003, haughty domination; `ع ق ب` B006 marks the end or outcome. **Structural cue:** Denial and inward certainty are explicitly simultaneous, with wrongdoing and elevation naming the intervening pressures; the command then redirects sight to the resulting outcome. **Assigned role:** Social elevation explains how certainty can fail to govern public acknowledgment. **Abductive assumption:** The inferred arrow runs from defended superiority to denial despite knowledge, then to a historically visible end. **Reading change:** The ayah diagnoses denial as a power-maintenance action, not an information deficit.

### Retrospective surprises

Solomon's letter in 27:31 directly prohibits `ع ل و`, and the queen's eventual surrender in 27:44 abandons self-elevation without the coerced abasement feared in 27:34.

## 27:15 - وَلَقَدْ ءَاتَيْنَا دَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيْمَٰنَ عِلْمًۭا ۖ وَقَالَا ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِى فَضَّلَنَا عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٍۢ مِّنْ عِبَادِهِ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ف ض ل` B003, beneficence or bounty, with B001's excess remaining visible behind the comparative form. **Structural cue:** Knowledge is given, followed by praise rather than self-claim; 27:16 names inheritance and comprehensive provision, while 27:19 turns favor into a request for gratitude and right action. **Assigned role:** `فَضَّلَنَا` frames surplus capacity as received bounty that creates an answerable difference. **Abductive assumption:** Praise is the regulating response that keeps comparative advantage from becoming B004, a claim to superiority. **Reading change:** Preference becomes entrusted surplus, not autonomous rank.

### Retrospective surprises

27:40 later explicitly calls extraordinary capacity a `فَضْل` that tests gratitude or ingratitude. It confirms the accountability latent in the present praise.

## 27:16 - وَوَرِثَ سُلَيْمَٰنُ دَاوُۥدَ ۖ وَقَالَ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ عُلِّمْنَا مَنطِقَ ٱلطَّيْرِ وَأُوتِينَا مِن كُلِّ شَىْءٍ ۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلْفَضْلُ ٱلْمُبِينُ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ن ط ق` B002, speech-like indication by which something discloses meaning. **Structural cue:** Solomon is taught the birds' `مَنطِق`; within the window, an ant's utterance in 27:18 is understood and answered by Solomon's prayer in 27:19. **Assigned role:** The root makes communicative capacity broader than ordinary human articulation while retaining meaningful disclosure. **Abductive assumption:** The inherited gift includes interpretive receptivity to nonhuman indication, not merely control over nonhuman bodies. **Reading change:** Dominion is recast as the capacity to hear a world that can address the ruler.

### Retrospective surprises

27:82 later gives speech to an earth-creature, while 27:85 removes speech from wrongdoers. Those reversals show that articulate testimony is allocated agency, not a permanent human monopoly.

## 27:17 - وَحُشِرَ لِسُلَيْمَٰنَ جُنُودُهُۥ مِنَ ٱلْجِنِّ وَٱلْإِنسِ وَٱلطَّيْرِ فَهُمْ يُوزَعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `و ز ع` B001, restraining and holding ranks in order. **Structural cue:** The gathered forces are actively regulated; 27:18 reveals the crushing risk of their mass, and 27:19 uses the same root in a prayer for inward prompting toward gratitude. **Assigned role:** Restraint converts a heterogeneous host from mere power into governed order. **Abductive assumption:** The recurrence in 27:19 aligns external command with internal self-command. **Reading change:** The army's defining competence is controlled coordination, not unbounded force.

### Retrospective surprises

27:83 later uses the same B001 branch for gathered contingents at judgment. The echo removes the root from Solomon's exclusive control and places all collective order under a larger gathering.

## 27:18 - حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَتَوْا۟ عَلَىٰ وَادِ ٱلنَّمْلِ قَالَتْ نَمْلَةٌۭ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّمْلُ ٱدْخُلُوا۟ مَسَٰكِنَكُمْ لَا يَحْطِمَنَّكُمْ سُلَيْمَٰنُ وَجُنُودُهُۥ وَهُمْ لَا يَشْعُرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ح ط م` B006, a mass or ravaging force crushing what it meets. **Structural cue:** The warning follows the ordered gathering in 27:17 and precedes Solomon's receptive laughter and self-regulating prayer in 27:19; the lack of perception is explicitly attached to the potential crush. **Assigned role:** Crushing names the externality that organized power can produce without intending or sensing it. **Abductive assumption:** The ant's warning supplies information absent from the army's command structure and thereby enables restraint to become ethically responsive. **Reading change:** Innocence of awareness no longer cancels the danger created by scale.

### Retrospective surprises

27:88 later depicts mountains as apparently fixed while passing like clouds. That much larger moving mass confirms that scale and motion can exceed ordinary perception even when their effects remain real.

## 27:19 - فَتَبَسَّمَ ضَاحِكًۭا مِّن قَوْلِهَا وَقَالَ رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِىٓ أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِىٓ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَىَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَىَّ وَأَنْ أَعْمَلَ صَٰلِحًۭا تَرْضَىٰهُ وَأَدْخِلْنِى بِرَحْمَتِكَ فِى عِبَادِكَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `و ز ع` B002, inward prompting or attachment. **Structural cue:** The prayer repeats the root used for ordering the army in 27:17, after the ant exposes the army's unseen risk in 27:18; gratitude, acceptable action, mercy, and entry among sound servants form a sequence. **Assigned role:** Inward prompting becomes the ruler's requested regulator. **Abductive assumption:** External order is insufficient unless power's possessor is internally restrained toward gratitude and repair. **Reading change:** Gratitude becomes governance of the self before it is an expression of satisfaction.

### Retrospective surprises

27:40 later compresses the same issue into an explicit test: extraordinary favor discloses whether Solomon will thank or reject, and gratitude returns benefit to the grateful self.

## 27:20 - وَتَفَقَّدَ ٱلطَّيْرَ فَقَالَ مَا لِىَ لَآ أَرَى ٱلْهُدْهُدَ أَمْ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلْغَآئِبِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `غ ي ب` B001, being hidden from sight or knowledge. **Structural cue:** A review of the birds produces a visual absence; 27:21 requires either sanction or clarifying proof, and 27:22 returns with a claim to knowledge beyond Solomon's present grasp. **Assigned role:** Absence creates an epistemic gap inside otherwise ordered command. **Abductive assumption:** The interrogative self-check, `مَا لِىَ لَا أَرَى`, keeps nonappearance from being immediately equated with guilt. **Reading change:** The missing bird becomes a limit on the ruler's field of vision before it becomes a disciplinary case.

### Retrospective surprises

27:65 later reserves complete knowledge of the unseen to God, and 27:75 places every absent thing in a clear record. The local commander's gap thus anticipates a categorical limit on creaturely knowledge.

## 27:21 - لَأُعَذِّبَنَّهُۥ عَذَابًۭا شَدِيدًا أَوْ لَأَا۟ذْبَحَنَّهُۥٓ أَوْ لَيَأْتِيَنِّى بِسُلْطَٰنٍۢ مُّبِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `س ل ط` B002, an overpowering proof. **Structural cue:** Two coercive sanctions are suspended by a third alternative, a clarifying warrant; 27:22 supplies a report, and 27:27 delays acceptance for verification. **Assigned role:** Proof becomes the authorized force capable of defeating the presumption generated by absence. **Abductive assumption:** The three-part alternative establishes an evidentiary threshold within command, even though it does not specify a full procedure. **Reading change:** `سُلْطَان` redirects power from punishment toward reasons strong enough to overrule it.

### Retrospective surprises

27:64 later challenges claimants to bring their `بُرْهَان`. The later demand generalizes the present rule: assertion must acquire evidentiary force before it can govern judgment.

## 27:22 - فَمَكَثَ غَيْرَ بَعِيدٍۢ فَقَالَ أَحَطتُ بِمَا لَمْ تُحِطْ بِهِۦ وَجِئْتُكَ مِن سَبَإٍۭ بِنَبَإٍۢ يَقِينٍ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `ح و ط` B004, complete grasp in knowledge or possession. **Structural cue:** The doubled root explicitly distributes grasp unevenly between bird and king; 27:23-25 then unfolds political, cultic, and concealed information, while 27:27 still tests truthfulness. **Assigned role:** Encompassment marks a local knowledge advantage without making the report self-authenticating. **Abductive assumption:** The report can correct hierarchy because access, not rank, determines this particular grasp. **Reading change:** Solomon's comprehensive dominion is shown to contain a correctable epistemic perimeter.

### Retrospective surprises

27:84 later condemns denial of signs before the deniers have encompassed them in knowledge. The echo turns the hoopoe's successful grasp into a standard against premature judgment.

## 27:23 - إِنِّى وَجَدتُّ ٱمْرَأَةًۭ تَمْلِكُهُمْ وَأُوتِيَتْ مِن كُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ وَلَهَا عَرْشٌ عَظِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Root and branch:** `م ل ك` B003, kingship and dominion; `ع ر ش` B001 supplies the raised royal throne. **Structural cue:** The queen's governing capacity, comprehensive provision, and great throne are immediately followed by a misdirected cult in 27:24 and by the divine `رَبُّ ٱلْعَرْشِ ٱلْعَظِيمِ` in 27:26. **Assigned role:** Human dominion is presented at maximum visible coherence before being rescaled. **Abductive assumption:** The near verbal parallel between the two great thrones is a deliberate comparison of bounded and unbounded authority. **Reading change:** Material completeness no longer demonstrates ultimate orientation.

### Retrospective surprises

27:38-42 later moves, settles, and alters the human throne. Its transportability reveals that the visible support of a polity is contingent even when described as great.

## 27:24 - وَجَدتُّهَا وَقَوْمَهَا يَسْجُدُونَ لِلشَّمْسِ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيْطَٰنُ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ فَصَدَّهُمْ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ فَهُمْ لَا يَهْتَدُونَ

### Activated readings
