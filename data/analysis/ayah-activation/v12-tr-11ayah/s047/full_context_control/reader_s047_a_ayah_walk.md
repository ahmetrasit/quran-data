# Surah 47 Full-Context Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

Packet evidence is cited as `root:branch`. Every statement labeled **Abductive move** is an inferred directional arrow from packet evidence and local structure, not an additional packet datum.

## 47:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

*Focus window: 47:0–5.*

### Activated readings

1. **The opening name governs the sequence as a reparative commission.** **Lexical evidence:** `س م و:B005` supplies the image of a name as a raised designation, while `ا ل ه:B002` supplies the fixed divine name. **Structural cue:** the designation precedes every contrast and every act in 47:1–5. **Assigned role:** the basmalah establishes the authority under which loss, covering, repair, and guidance are sorted. **Abductive move:** because the named source stands before both judgment in 47:1 and restoration in 47:2 and 47:5, I infer that the sequence is framed as ordered governance rather than an unconnected alternation of severity and favor. **Reading change:** the basmalah becomes an operative heading for the surah’s first causal chain, not a detachable preface.

2. **Mercy is activated as restoration that reaches damaged inner condition.** **Lexical evidence:** both adjectives retain `ر ح م:B001`, tender mercy and compassionate care. **Structural cue:** 47:2 covers harmful consequences and repairs `بَالَهُمْ`, and 47:5 repeats guidance plus that repair. **Assigned role:** the repeated mercy root supplies the governing quality of the repair mechanism. **Abductive move:** the move from covered wrongs to repaired inward state is directional inference from 47:2 and 47:5; the packet itself supplies the mercy and repair branches, not that arrow. **Reading change:** mercy is read as active rehabilitation, not only affective kindness.

### Retrospective surprises

Later 47:2 and 47:5 made the second reading more specific: the opening mercy is realized through both protective covering and restoration of inward condition. No later evidence in the focus window displaced the governing-name reading.

## 47:1 — ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ وَصَدُّوا۟ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَضَلَّ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:0–6.*

### Activated readings

1. **Blocked passage makes deeds lose their destination.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك ف ر:B003` is rejection through concealing truth; `ص د د:B001` is turning away or blocking; `س ب ل:B001` is a traversable way or means; `ض ل ل:B003` is loss or failure to locate; and `ع م ل:B001` is intentional work. **Structural cue:** rejection and blocking are coordinated as causes, while God is the subject of the resulting `أَضَلَّ` and deeds are its object. **Assigned role:** concealment initiates the obstruction, obstruction closes the route, and loss names the works’ failed arrival. **Abductive move:** I infer a path mechanism: practices that block the divine way reproduce that blockage in their own products, so the products no longer reach a valid end. **Reading change:** “lost deeds” is not mere deletion; it is purposive work deprived of course and destination.

2. **The clause establishes a reversible covering contrast.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك ف ر:B003` here covers truth, whereas the same root at 47:2 activates `ك ف ر:B009`, atoning cover that effaces harmful consequence. **Structural cue:** the two clauses stand back-to-back with opposed subjects and opposed outcomes. **Assigned role:** the repeated root makes the kind and direction of covering, not the bare occurrence of covering, decisive. **Abductive move:** because one covering blocks disclosure while the next covering protects from wrong, I infer that moral valence is assigned by what is covered and to what end. **Reading change:** the root is no longer flattened into one gloss; the local contrast activates two opposed branch functions.

### Retrospective surprises

47:2 supplied the unexpected counter-branch `ك ف ر:B009`, and 47:4 later repeated the threatened loss of works under an explicit negation. Together they sharpened 47:1 into a route-and-result mechanism rather than a generic condemnation.

## 47:2 — وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَءَامَنُوا۟ بِمَا نُزِّلَ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍۢ وَهُوَ ٱلْحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمْ ۙ كَفَّرَ عَنْهُمْ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمْ وَأَصْلَحَ بَالَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:0–7.*

### Activated readings

1. **Trusting assent is doubled to join general security with a specific received disclosure.** **Lexical evidence:** both occurrences of `ء م ن` activate `ء م ن:B002`, trusting assent and confirmation, with the settled-security image of `ء م ن:B001` remaining locally available; `ن ز ل:B002` is sending down, including disclosure. **Structural cue:** the first `ءَامَنُوا` is followed by sound works, while the second takes `بِمَا نُزِّلَ` as its complement and is appositionally certified as `ٱلْحَقُّ`. **Assigned role:** the repetition prevents inward assent, practical work, and reception of the sent-down material from being separated. **Abductive move:** I infer that the second specification tests and stabilizes the first: trust becomes concrete by attaching itself to a determinate disclosure and producing sound action. **Reading change:** faith is read as a linked assent–reception–practice structure, not an unqualified interior state.

2. **A two-stage remedy first covers harm and then repairs operating condition.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك ف ر:B009` is atoning cover or effacement; `س و ء:B001` supplies badness or ugliness; `ص ل ح:B001` is soundness opposed to corruption; and `ب و ل:B003` is inward mind or concern, with `ب و ل:B004` the state of affairs or well-being. **Structural cue:** the two perfect verbs share God as subject and the same beneficiaries, but take different objects: harmful acts and `بَال`. **Assigned role:** covering removes exposure to consequence; repair restores the person’s inward and practical condition. **Abductive move:** the ordered coordination suggests that pardon is not the endpoint: cleared liability opens into renewed capacity. **Reading change:** the verse describes rehabilitation rather than a single forensic cancellation.

### Retrospective surprises

47:5 repeats `يُصْلِحُ بَالَهُمْ`, confirming that repair is an extended process, while 47:6 makes the repaired condition lead into entry and recognition. That later sequence strengthened the rehabilitation reading and corrected any temptation to stop at expiation.

## 47:3 — ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ ٱتَّبَعُوا۟ ٱلْبَٰطِلَ وَأَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ ٱتَّبَعُوا۟ ٱلْحَقَّ مِن رَّبِّهِمْ ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضْرِبُ ٱللَّهُ لِلنَّاسِ أَمْثَٰلَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:0–8.*

### Activated readings

1. **Following attaches each group to the stability or instability of its object.** **Lexical evidence:** `ت ب ع:B001` is following after; `ب ط ل:B001` is voidness lacking truth or stability; and `ح ق ق:B001` is firm truth. **Structural cue:** two matched `أَنَّ` clauses repeat `ٱتَّبَعُوا` and change only the followers and followed object. **Assigned role:** following is the transfer mechanism by which an object’s firmness or voidness becomes the trajectory of its adherents and their works. **Abductive move:** because 47:1–2 have already assigned opposed work outcomes, I infer that attachment to a stable or void object explains those outcomes. **Reading change:** truth and falsehood function as trajectory-bearing objects, not only propositions receiving assent.

2. **The final clause makes the groups themselves an exhibited likeness.** **Lexical evidence:** `ض ر ب:B003` is setting forth an example whose effect appears beyond itself; `م ث ل:B003` is parable or proverb. **Structural cue:** `كَذَٰلِكَ` points back to the balanced comparison, and the possessive `أَمْثَٰلَهُمْ` makes the people’s patterned states the exhibited material. **Assigned role:** the example externalizes an otherwise abstract causal contrast so it can be inspected. **Abductive move:** I infer that the immediately preceding mirrored syntax is itself the form of the example, not merely background to a later illustration. **Reading change:** “striking examples” becomes a structural act already performed by the verse’s parallel construction.

### Retrospective surprises

47:4 immediately activates `ض ر ب:B001`, direct impact, after 47:3’s `ض ر ب:B003`. The lexical hinge did not merge parable with physical striking; it made the displayed distinction acquire embodied consequences.

## 47:4 — فَإِذَا لَقِيتُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ فَضَرْبَ ٱلرِّقَابِ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَثْخَنتُمُوهُمْ فَشُدُّوا۟ ٱلْوَثَاقَ فَإِمَّا مَنًّۢا بَعْدُ وَإِمَّا فِدَآءً حَتَّىٰ تَضَعَ ٱلْحَرْبُ أَوْزَارَهَا ۚ ذَٰلِكَ وَلَوْ يَشَآءُ ٱللَّهُ لَٱنتَصَرَ مِنْهُمْ وَلَٰكِن لِّيَبْلُوَا۟ بَعْضَكُم بِبَعْضٍۢ ۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ قُتِلُوا۟ فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَلَن يُضِلَّ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:0–9.*

### Activated readings

1. **The combat instruction is a bounded sequence that changes state at each `حَتَّىٰ`.** **Lexical evidence:** `ض ر ب:B001` is direct impact; `ث خ ن:B003` is making heavy, disabling, or overwhelming; `ش د د:B001` is fastening; `و ث ق:B003` is binding or restraint; `م ن ن:B004` is free release without compensation; and `و ز ر:B003` is war gear or burden. **Structural cue:** encounter leads to impact only until disabling control is reached; that state triggers binding, then the explicit alternatives of release or ransom, and the whole sequence terminates when war lays down its gear. **Assigned role:** the roots mark successive operational phases rather than interchangeable permissions. **Abductive move:** I infer from the two temporal limits and the alternative post-capture outcomes that the verse regulates escalation and de-escalation as a process. **Reading change:** the clause is not read as an unbounded command to kill; its grammar repeatedly closes one phase and opens another.

2. **Human conflict is the medium by which latent quality becomes visible, while work preservation remains the final measure.** **Lexical evidence:** `ب ل و:B002` is testing that discloses the true state of a person or deed; `ب ع ض:B001` is a portion set against a whole; `ض ل ل:B001` is deviation from an aim or right course; and `ع م ل:B001` is intentional work. **Structural cue:** God’s possible direct victory is counterposed with `وَلَٰكِن لِّيَبْلُوَا۟ بَعْضَكُم بِبَعْضٍ`, followed by the emphatic negation that the slain persons’ works will never be made to deviate or be lost. **Assigned role:** reciprocal human encounter is the test instrument, and preserved work is the disclosed outcome. **Abductive move:** because divine direct action is stated as possible but not selected, I infer that the mediated ordeal is for manifestation of human quality, not for supplying divine lack. **Reading change:** the conflict is subordinated to disclosure and preservation of work rather than treated as the surah’s independent end.

### Retrospective surprises

47:7’s reciprocal aid and firm feet, followed by 47:8’s face-down stumble, showed what the test discloses on each side. Those later bodily images reinforced the process reading and its work-centered endpoint.

## 47:5 — سَيَهْدِيهِمْ وَيُصْلِحُ بَالَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:0–10.*

### Activated readings

1. **Guidance restores both direction and the inward condition needed to travel it.** **Lexical evidence:** `ه د ي:B001` is gentle guidance to a way or truth; `ه د ي:B002` is course, manner, or direction; `ص ل ح:B001` is restoration to soundness; and `ب و ل:B003` is inward mind or concern. **Structural cue:** two future verbs share the same beneficiaries immediately after 47:4 guarantees that their works will not be lost. **Assigned role:** guidance supplies course, while repair makes the traveler inwardly capable of sustained movement. **Abductive move:** I infer that preserved works require not only an intact record but a reoriented agent; the paired futures answer both needs. **Reading change:** post-conflict guidance is read as continuing rehabilitation, not merely information delivered after death.

### Retrospective surprises

47:6 turns repaired direction into entry and recognition, while 47:7 adds firmly established feet. The later sequence made the spatial force of `ه د ي:B002` more prominent and confirmed that inner repair has an onward function.

## 47:6 — وَيُدْخِلُهُمُ ٱلْجَنَّةَ عَرَّفَهَا لَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:1–11.*

### Activated readings

1. **Entry is into an abode made recognizable and familiar, not an anonymous reward-space.** **Lexical evidence:** `د خ ل:B001` is entering; `ع ر ف:B003` is recognition by a distinguishing mark; `ع ر ف:B004` supplies scenting or perfuming, and its packet scope explicitly includes `عَرَّفَهَا لَهُمْ` as making the place fragrant or adorned. **Structural cue:** entry is followed by a perfect verb with the same place as object and the entrants as beneficiaries. **Assigned role:** recognition supplies cognitive familiarity, while the `B004` image adds prepared sensory welcome as a coexisting activation. **Abductive move:** I infer that the prior guidance and repair in 47:5 have a destination already fitted to its recipients, so arrival completes a relation rather than beginning acquaintance from nothing. **Reading change:** the garden is read as a prepared, identifiable home whose signs answer the guided traveler.

### Retrospective surprises

47:10’s demand to travel and inspect prior destinations, and 47:11’s patronage contrast, retrospectively sharpened recognition as secure orientation. No later item in the focus window forced a choice between the recognition and perfuming images; both remain coherent.

## 47:7 — يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ إِن تَنصُرُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ يَنصُرْكُمْ وَيُثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَكُمْ

*Focus window: 47:2–12.*

### Activated readings

1. **Reciprocal support becomes stabilized capacity to advance.** **Lexical evidence:** both uses of `ن ص ر` activate `ن ص ر:B001`, aid that makes its recipient prevail; `ث ب ت:B003` is making firm; and `ق د م:B001` is the bodily foot, with `ق د م:B004` supplying advance. **Structural cue:** the conditional repeats the same support root across protasis and apodosis, then coordinates divine support with the making-firm of feet. **Assigned role:** human support names enacted allegiance, divine support answers it, and fixed feet are the embodied result. **Abductive move:** 47:4 has already said that God could prevail directly, so I infer that “supporting God” means supporting the disclosed way in action; the response converts that allegiance into steadiness under trial. **Reading change:** reciprocity is not divine dependence but a testable relation whose human side becomes durable movement.

### Retrospective surprises

47:8’s `تَعْسًا` activates the exact opposite bodily trajectory, and 47:10 later commands travel through the land. Firm feet therefore became both moral stability and capacity to continue a course.

## 47:8 — وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ فَتَعْسًۭا لَّهُمْ وَأَضَلَّ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:3–13.*

### Activated readings

1. **The opponents’ work-loss is imaged as a fall from which movement does not recover.** **Lexical evidence:** `ت ع س:B001` is stumbling and falling face-down without recovery; `ض ل ل:B003` is loss or failure to locate; and `ع م ل:B001` is intentional work. **Structural cue:** the nominal downfall formula is immediately joined to the causative loss of works, while 47:7 has just ended with feet being made firm. **Assigned role:** bodily collapse supplies the image; failed work supplies its purposive consequence. **Abductive move:** from the adjacency of firm feet and unrecovered stumbling, I infer that the two groups are being measured by whether their action can remain upright and reach its aim. **Reading change:** `تَعْسًا` is not a free-standing curse; it is the bodily model of how works lose course.

### Retrospective surprises

47:9’s `ح ب ط:B001` adds an internal wasting image, and 47:10–13 add ruined societies with no supporter. The fall therefore widened from an individual stumble to a failed action-system and collective trajectory.

## 47:9 — ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمْ كَرِهُوا۟ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ فَأَحْبَطَ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:4–14.*

### Activated readings

1. **Aversion to the sent-down disclosure hollows work from within.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك ر ه:B001` is aversion opposed to satisfaction; `ن ز ل:B002` is sending down, including disclosure; `ح ب ط:B001` images harmful grazing that swells the belly and ends in death; and `ع م ل:B001` is intentional work. **Structural cue:** the causal `بِأَنَّهُمْ` joins inward aversion to the causative outcome `فَأَحْبَطَ`, whose object is their works. **Assigned role:** aversion determines what cannot be assimilated, while `ح ب ط:B001` supplies a deceptive enlargement that destroys productive life. **Abductive move:** I infer that the works may retain visible volume while losing viable yield, because the cause is a rejected source of guidance and the consequence uses an image of fatal internal overfilling. **Reading change:** nullified work is read as metabolically ruined productivity, not simply an externally erased total.

### Retrospective surprises

47:12’s comparison to livestock eating and 47:14’s beautified bad work confirmed the intake-and-appearance mechanism: consumption and visible activity can persist while reflective assimilation and sound yield fail.

## 47:10 — ۞ أَفَلَمْ يَسِيرُوا۟ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ فَيَنظُرُوا۟ كَيْفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ ۚ دَمَّرَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيْهِمْ ۖ وَلِلْكَٰفِرِينَ أَمْثَٰلُهَا

*Focus window: 47:5–15.*

### Activated readings

1. **Travel is an evidence procedure that converts traces into foresight.** **Lexical evidence:** `س ي ر:B001` is travel or progression; `ن ظ ر:B001` is directed sight, insight, and reflection; `ع ق ب:B006` is outcome; and `د م ر:B002` is destructive ruin or eradication. **Structural cue:** the interrogative chains movement in the land to looking, then makes the object of looking the predecessors’ outcome. **Assigned role:** travel provides encounterable evidence, looking processes it, and outcome supplies the temporal object of inquiry. **Abductive move:** I infer that the command is not tourism or bare recollection: visible remains are to be read backward to causes and forward as warning. **Reading change:** history becomes an empirical reasoning discipline in which spatial movement trains consequence-reading.

2. **The final likeness projects a repeatable pattern without erasing historical particularity.** **Lexical evidence:** `م ث ل:B001` is likeness or counterpart, while `ك ف ر:B003` is truth-concealing rejection. **Structural cue:** `وَلِلْكَٰفِرِينَ أَمْثَٰلُهَا` follows the singular narrated outcome and gives later rejecters its counterparts. **Assigned role:** likeness carries the causal shape of the earlier event into the present. **Abductive move:** because the verse says counterparts rather than identity, I infer recurrence at the level of pattern, not a claim that every later event must duplicate one historical form. **Reading change:** the warning is analogical and diagnostic rather than a flat prediction of identical ruins.

The packet supplies no accepted branch inventory for `ك ي ف`; this reading therefore assigns no branch to that root and rests on the other fixed-ayah branches.

### Retrospective surprises

47:12’s opposed residences, 47:13’s stronger destroyed settlement, and 47:15’s paired destinations made the inspected “outcome” increasingly concrete. The retrospective strengthened, rather than corrected, the evidence-procedure reading.

## 47:11 — ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَوْلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَأَنَّ ٱلْكَٰفِرِينَ لَا مَوْلَىٰ لَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:6–16.*

### Activated readings

1. **Patronage is active loyal support grounded in proximity.** **Lexical evidence:** `و ل ي:B004` is loyal alliance and support, while `و ل ي:B001` is nearness without an intervening gap; `ء م ن:B002` is trusting assent. **Structural cue:** two balanced `أَنَّ` clauses affirm a `مَوْلَى` for one group and negate any `مَوْلَى` for the other. **Assigned role:** trusting assent establishes the relation, nearness describes its intimacy, and loyal support describes its operative effect. **Abductive move:** 47:7 has already linked allegiance to aid, and 47:10 has shown exposed outcomes; I infer that patronage names the relation that carries a group through those outcomes, not a passive title. **Reading change:** `مَوْلَى` is read as proximity issuing in effective backing, with negation marking relational isolation.

### Retrospective surprises

47:13’s `فَلَا نَاصِرَ لَهُمْ` restates the negative side as absence of effective aid, while 47:16 shows listeners physically near the messenger but inwardly sealed. The latter corrected any equation of patronal nearness with mere spatial proximity.

## 47:12 — إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدْخِلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ جَنَّٰتٍۢ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَٰرُ ۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ يَتَمَتَّعُونَ وَيَأْكُلُونَ كَمَا تَأْكُلُ ٱلْأَنْعَٰمُ وَٱلنَّارُ مَثْوًۭى لَّهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:7–17.*

### Activated readings

1. **The verse contrasts sustaining flow with consumption that terminates in fixed lodging.** **Lexical evidence:** `د خ ل:B001` is entering; `ج ر ي:B001` is flow; `ن ه ر:B001` is a river cutting and carrying through ground; `م ت ع:B001` is temporary use or enjoyment; `أ ك ل:B001` is ingestion; and `ث و ي:B001` is staying or settling. **Structural cue:** the first group enters plural gardens characterized by ongoing under-flow, while the second is described by present enjoyment and repeated eating before fire becomes its settled residence. **Assigned role:** flow represents continuing provision, ingestion represents immediate appropriation, and settlement fixes the trajectory produced by that mode of life. **Abductive move:** I infer that the contrast is not pleasure versus no pleasure; it is provision received within a durable order versus appetite consuming the near term without reflective orientation. **Reading change:** present enjoyment is not denied but exposed as temporally thin when detached from destination.

2. **Animal-like eating names a failure of interpretive uptake rather than an attack on embodiment.** **Lexical evidence:** `أ ك ل:B001` is literal ingestion, while `ء م ن:B002` and `ع م ل:B001` in the matched first clause bind reception to intentional work. **Structural cue:** the simile modifies how the rejecters eat; it does not say that eating itself distinguishes the groups. **Assigned role:** the simile isolates appetite without the assent–work structure assigned to the other side. **Abductive move:** 47:9’s harmful-ingestion image and 47:14’s desire-following suggest that intake becomes destructive when it is not interpreted and ordered. **Reading change:** the animal comparison is read as a critique of unintegrated consumption, not of bodily need.

### Retrospective surprises

47:15 intensifies the intake contrast through pure and corrupting liquids, 47:16 applies it to hearing, and 47:17 shows guidance increasing when received. These later ayat generalized the verse from food to every form of uptake.

## 47:13 — وَكَأَيِّن مِّن قَرْيَةٍ هِىَ أَشَدُّ قُوَّةًۭ مِّن قَرْيَتِكَ ٱلَّتِىٓ أَخْرَجَتْكَ أَهْلَكْنَٰهُمْ فَلَا نَاصِرَ لَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:8–18.*

### Activated readings

1. **Collective strength cannot substitute for support at the decisive outcome.** **Lexical evidence:** `ق ر ي:B001` is a gathered settlement; `ش د د:B002` is intensity or strength; `ق و ي:B001` is assembled strength; `ه ل ك:B001` is perishing or destruction; and `ن ص ر:B001` is aid that makes one prevail. **Structural cue:** a stronger settlement is compared directly with the settlement that expelled the addressee, then the stronger polity is destroyed and denied any supporter. **Assigned role:** settlement and force name accumulated social capacity; destruction tests its limit; absent aid exposes its inability to secure final continuance. **Abductive move:** I infer that expelling the messenger is not validated by institutional strength, because the comparison deliberately grants superior force to an earlier community and still shows it failing. **Reading change:** power is relativized as a gathered instrument, not treated as self-authenticating protection.

### Retrospective surprises

47:15’s enduring versus destructive residences and 47:18’s sudden arrival shifted the comparison from military scale to temporal exposure. Greater stored force does not control the timing or quality of the outcome.

## 47:14 — أَفَمَن كَانَ عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَةٍۢ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ كَمَن زُيِّنَ لَهُۥ سُوٓءُ عَمَلِهِۦ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓا۟ أَهْوَآءَهُم

*Focus window: 47:9–19.*

### Activated readings

1. **The contrast is between disclosed evidence and an aesthetic feedback loop around bad work.** **Lexical evidence:** `ب ي ن:B004` is visibility, clarity, or proof; `ز ي ن:B002` is making or showing something beautiful; `س و ء:B001` is badness or ugliness; `ع م ل:B001` is intentional work; and `ه و ي:B004` is desire or inclination. **Structural cue:** the interrogative compares one person situated “upon” clear evidence with another whose bad work is passively beautified, followed by plural desire-following. **Assigned role:** evidence provides an external criterion, beautification masks the work’s actual quality, and desire supplies the reinforcing direction. **Abductive move:** I infer a closed loop: desire produces or selects work, beautification conceals its badness, and the concealed badness authorizes further desire-following. **Reading change:** the opposition is epistemic and causal, not merely a static comparison of good and bad persons.

2. **Desire’s falling image remains latent beneath its attractive surface.** **Lexical evidence:** alongside desire in `ه و ي:B004`, `ه و ي:B002` supplies falling into a void or abyss. **Structural cue:** desire-following closes a verse that began with being stably “upon” clear evidence. **Assigned role:** the coactivated branch gives the directional underside of inclination. **Abductive move:** because 47:8 has already imaged unrecovered stumbling, I infer that the attractive pull of desire is simultaneously a downward course. **Reading change:** desire is read not only as preference but as gravity that can carry the follower away from stable evidence.

### Retrospective surprises

## 47:15 — مَّثَلُ ٱلْجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِى وُعِدَ ٱلْمُتَّقُونَ ۖ فِيهَآ أَنْهَٰرٌۭ مِّن مَّآءٍ غَيْرِ ءَاسِنٍۢ وَأَنْهَٰرٌۭ مِّن لَّبَنٍۢ لَّمْ يَتَغَيَّرْ طَعْمُهُۥ وَأَنْهَٰرٌۭ مِّنْ خَمْرٍۢ لَّذَّةٍۢ لِّلشَّٰرِبِينَ وَأَنْهَٰرٌۭ مِّنْ عَسَلٍۢ مُّصَفًّۭى ۖ وَلَهُمْ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ وَمَغْفِرَةٌۭ مِّن رَّبِّهِمْ ۖ كَمَنْ هُوَ خَٰلِدٌۭ فِى ٱلنَّارِ وَسُقُوا۟ مَآءً حَمِيمًۭا فَقَطَّعَ أَمْعَآءَهُمْ

### Retrospective surprises

47:16’s listening without retained understanding and 47:20’s diseased-heart response transferred the intake mechanism from the body to cognition. The retrospective therefore elevated assimilation, rather than sheer sensory contrast, as the verse’s organizing relation.

## 47:17 — وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱهْتَدَوْا۟ زَادَهُمْ هُدًۭى وَءَاتَىٰهُمْ تَقْوَىٰهُمْ

## 47:18 — فَهَلْ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا ٱلسَّاعَةَ أَن تَأْتِيَهُم بَغْتَةًۭ ۖ فَقَدْ جَآءَ أَشْرَاطُهَا ۚ فَأَنَّىٰ لَهُمْ إِذَا جَآءَتْهُمْ ذِكْرَىٰهُمْ

### Retrospective surprises

47:20’s death-like gaze anticipates the sudden encounter bodily, and 47:23’s deafness and blindness show the present sensory closures that make prior signs ineffective. These later ayat relocated the failure from lack of signs to disabled reception.

## 47:19 — فَٱعْلَمْ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَٱسْتَغْفِرْ لِذَنۢبِكَ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَٱلْمُؤْمِنَٰتِ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مُتَقَلَّبَكُمْ وَمَثْوَىٰكُمْ

### Retrospective surprises

## 47:20 — وَيَقُولُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ لَوْلَا نُزِّلَتْ سُورَةٌۭ ۖ فَإِذَآ أُنزِلَتْ سُورَةٌۭ مُّحْكَمَةٌۭ وَذُكِرَ فِيهَا ٱلْقِتَالُ ۙ رَأَيْتَ ٱلَّذِينَ فِى قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٌۭ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَيْكَ نَظَرَ ٱلْمَغْشِىِّ عَلَيْهِ مِنَ ٱلْمَوْتِ ۖ فَأَوْلَىٰ لَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:15–25.*

### Activated readings

1. **A bounded, decisive disclosure converts a verbal request into an exposure event.** **Lexical evidence:** `س و ر:B003` is the “bounded section or station of discourse” variant; `ن ز ل:B002` is sending down disclosure; `ح ك م:B004` is making firm, perfected, and decisive; and `ذ ك ر:B004` is explicit verbal mention. **Structural cue:** believers first ask why a sura has not been sent, but the `فَإِذَا` clause changes the scene when a firm sura arrives and combat is mentioned within it. **Assigned role:** the bounded discourse gives the request a determinate object, firmness removes interpretive evasion, and explicit mention supplies the test-content. **Abductive move:** I infer that indeterminate desire for revelation is easy to voice, whereas a bounded directive reveals whether the speaker can bear its practical entailment. **Reading change:** revelation here is not only information; its formal decisiveness acts diagnostically on its hearers.

### Retrospective surprises

## 47:27 — فَكَيْفَ إِذَا تَوَفَّتْهُمُ ٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ يَضْرِبُونَ وُجُوهَهُمْ وَأَدْبَٰرَهُمْ

The packet supplies no accepted branch inventory for `ك ي ف`; this reading therefore assigns no branch to that root.

### Retrospective surprises

47:30’s recognizability through marks and speech anticipates exposure before death, and 47:32 relocates attempted harm back onto the opponents’ own works. The terminal scene is thus the completion, not the first appearance, of legibility.

## 47:28 — ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمُ ٱتَّبَعُوا۟ مَآ أَسْخَطَ ٱللَّهَ وَكَرِهُوا۟ رِضْوَٰنَهُۥ فَأَحْبَطَ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:23–33.*

### Activated readings

1. **Affective orientation toward displeasure and away from approval hollows work.** **Lexical evidence:** `ت ب ع:B001` is following; `س خ ط:B001` is displeasure opposed to satisfaction; `ك ر ه:B001` is aversion; `ر ض و:B002` is abundant or sought approval; `ح ب ط:B001` is fatal swelling from harmful intake; and `ع م ل:B001` is intentional work. **Structural cue:** the causal clause coordinates following what causes displeasure with aversion to approval, then makes hollowed works the result. **Assigned role:** following gives positive direction, aversion gives negative direction, and `ح ب ط` names the resulting loss of viable yield. **Abductive move:** I infer that work quality is governed not by visible exertion alone but by the approval-system toward which desire and aversion orient it. **Reading change:** annulment becomes the internal consequence of a reversed value compass, not an unrelated later penalty.

### Retrospective surprises

47:29–31 trace hidden rancor into visible speech and tested report, while 47:32–33 repeat work-nullification as both consequence and warning. The later sequence made affective orientation empirically legible in action.

## 47:29 — أَمْ حَسِبَ ٱلَّذِينَ فِى قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٌ أَن لَّن يُخْرِجَ ٱللَّهُ أَضْغَٰنَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:24–34.*

### Activated readings

1. **The diseased heart is a containing interior whose hidden rancor will be forced outward.** **Lexical evidence:** `ح س ب:B002` is supposition or mental reckoning; `ق ل ب:B001` is the inward heart; `م ر ض:B001` is departure from health and balance; `خ ر ج:B002` is causing something hidden to emerge; and `ض غ ن:B001` is inwardly folded rancor. **Structural cue:** the interrogative challenges a negative expectation, `أَن لَّن يُخْرِجَ`, and makes concealed rancor the object God will bring out. **Assigned role:** faulty reckoning imagines durable concealment, disease names the disordered container, and extraction reverses inward folding. **Abductive move:** I infer that the disease’s pressure is not indefinitely private: under divine exposure it produces observable outputs. **Reading change:** heart-disease is read as a latent state with an eventual disclosure path, not an inaccessible interior label.

### Retrospective surprises

47:30 immediately specifies marks and speech-inflection as disclosure channels, and 47:31 adds testing and reports; 47:34 then shows the terminal consequence of the revealed trajectory. These later ayat replaced an imagined sudden unveiling with a staged evidentiary process.

## 47:30 — وَلَوْ نَشَآءُ لَأَرَيْنَٰكَهُمْ فَلَعَرَفْتَهُم بِسِيمَٰهُمْ ۚ وَلَتَعْرِفَنَّهُمْ فِى لَحْنِ ٱلْقَوْلِ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ أَعْمَٰلَكُمْ

*Focus window: 47:25–35.*

### Activated readings

1. **Recognition is presented as inference from signs rather than unrestricted access to hidden minds.** **Lexical evidence:** `ش ي ء:B001` activates its willing variant in `نَشَاءُ`; `ر ء ي:B012` is causing someone to see; `ع ر ف:B003` is recognition by a distinguishing mark; and `س و م:B004` is a visible distinguishing sign. **Structural cue:** the counterfactual first offers direct showing, then the actual recognition clauses specify marks and speech as media. **Assigned role:** divine willing governs possible direct disclosure, showing would present the people, and marks make them inferentially recognizable. **Abductive move:** because the verse moves from possible showing to recognition through signs, I infer a disciplined evidentiary mode rather than a grant of unmediated mind-reading. **Reading change:** hidden allegiance is not treated as permanently inaccessible, but neither is recognition detached from observable cues.

2. **Speech carries a bend that reveals the orientation it does not state openly.** **Lexical evidence:** `ل ح ن:B001` is speech shifted toward implication, intent, or allusion; `ق و ل:B001` is uttered speech; `ع ر ف:B003` is recognition; `ع ل م:B001` is knowing; and `ع م ل:B001` is intentional work. **Structural cue:** emphatic recognition is located `فِى لَحْنِ ٱلْقَوْلِ`, then divine knowledge of the addressees’ works closes the verse. **Assigned role:** the inflection of speech leaks inward alignment, while works provide a broader field against which the inference can be checked. **Abductive move:** I infer that repeated verbal choices, implication, and direction become behavioral evidence when read alongside action. **Reading change:** tone is not a mystical signal; it is a patterned deviation whose meaning is corroborated by works.

### Retrospective surprises

47:31 turns recognition into formal testing of effort, restraint, and reports, while 47:32–35 repeatedly return to work outcomes. The later evidence confirmed that recognition is provisional evidence to be tested by action, not a substitute for it.

## 47:31 — وَلَنَبْلُوَنَّكُمْ حَتَّىٰ نَعْلَمَ ٱلْمُجَٰهِدِينَ مِنكُمْ وَٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ وَنَبْلُوَا۟ أَخْبَارَكُمْ

*Focus window: 47:26–36.*

### Activated readings

1. **Testing converts latent qualities into manifested, reportable history.** **Lexical evidence:** both forms of `ب ل و` activate `ب ل و:B002`, testing that discloses the true state of a person or deed; `ع ل م:B001` is knowing; and `خ ب ر:B001` is report together with tested inward knowledge. **Structural cue:** emphatic testing runs until the strivers and restrainers are known, then testing takes the addressees’ reports as its object. **Assigned role:** trial is the disclosure instrument, knowledge names manifested distinction, and reports are the resulting record and reputation subjected to verification. **Abductive move:** because the packet’s `خ ب ر:B001` joins report with tested inner knowledge, I infer that events make inward quality narratable and publicly assessable; the testing does not supply previously absent divine information. **Reading change:** “until We know” is read as knowledge made manifest in history, not transition from ignorance.

2. **Effort and restraint are complementary qualities under one test.** **Lexical evidence:** `ج ه د:B001` is spending one’s capacity under strain; `ص ب ر:B001` is restraining the self from panic or blameworthy impulse. **Structural cue:** `ٱلْمُجَٰهِدِينَ` and `ٱلصَّٰبِرِينَ` are coordinated under the same verb of knowing. **Assigned role:** effort supplies outward expenditure of capacity; restraint governs inward continuity under pressure. **Abductive move:** I infer that reliable action requires both propulsion and containment: effort without restraint can become uncontrolled, while restraint without effort can become inert. **Reading change:** the tested ideal is not sheer intensity but disciplined endurance in action.

### Retrospective surprises

47:32–33 make works the object that can be hollowed or self-nullified, and 47:36 names recompense for protected action. These later ayat made `أَخْبَارَكُمْ` read less as verbal news alone and more as the tested history generated by work.

## 47:32 — إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ وَصَدُّوا۟ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ وَشَآقُّوا۟ ٱلرَّسُولَ مِنۢ بَعْدِ مَا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلْهُدَىٰ لَن يَضُرُّوا۟ ٱللَّهَ شَيْـًۭٔا وَسَيُحْبِطُ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:27–37.*

### Activated readings

1. **Opposition fractures the human common cause but cannot transfer harm to its divine target.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك ف ر:B003` is rejection through concealing truth; `ص د د:B001` is blocking or turning away; `س ب ل:B001` is a traversable way; `ش ق ق:B004` is a group splitting in conflict; `ر س ل:B002` is messenger and carried message; and `ض ر ر:B001` is harm or loss. **Structural cue:** three coordinated acts accumulate before the emphatic negation `لَن يَضُرُّوا۟ ٱللَّهَ شَيْـًٔا`; the opposition also occurs after guidance became clear. **Assigned role:** blocking impedes human passage, splitting opposes the messenger’s common cause, and the negation denies that these acts reach God as damage. **Abductive move:** I infer that attempted harm returns to the human relational and practical field in which the opposition operates. **Reading change:** the verse does not minimize the acts’ human effects; it redirects their ultimate efficacy away from God and toward the actors’ own trajectory.

2. **The causal return lands on the opponents’ works.** **Lexical evidence:** `ح ب ط:B001` is fatal internal swelling from harmful intake, and `ع م ل:B001` is intentional work. **Structural cue:** the future nullification of works follows immediately after the negated harm to God. **Assigned role:** the second future supplies the actual site of consequence. **Abductive move:** because harm cannot reach the intended divine object, I infer a recoil: the oppositional orientation hollows the very works through which the actors sought effect. **Reading change:** nullification is the endogenous result of misdirected opposition, not a disconnected sanction.

### Retrospective surprises

47:33 warns believers not to reproduce work-nullification themselves, 47:35 promises their works will not be diminished, and 47:37 shows pressure extracting rancor. The later window made work preservation a live conditional issue for both groups.

## 47:33 — ۞ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ أَطِيعُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُوا۟ ٱلرَّسُولَ وَلَا تُبْطِلُوٓا۟ أَعْمَٰلَكُمْ

*Focus window: 47:28–38.*

### Activated readings

1. **Believers can make their own works void by fracturing obedience.** **Lexical evidence:** both imperatives activate `ط و ع:B001`, willing and pliant obedience; `ب ط ل:B002` is causative annulment or making something void; and `ع م ل:B001` is intentional work. **Structural cue:** obedience to God and obedience to the messenger are separately repeated, then the addressees themselves become the subject of the prohibition against nullifying their works. **Assigned role:** doubled obedience preserves relational alignment; causative annulment names the self-generated danger when that alignment breaks. **Abductive move:** 47:32 has assigned hollowed works to explicit opponents, but the direct address here shows that group identity alone does not secure work; I infer that sustained obedience is the preserving relation. **Reading change:** nullification is not only something imposed on an external enemy; it is a warned-against capacity within believing practice.

### Retrospective surprises

47:35 promises no diminution of works, and 47:36–38 test allegiance through wealth and spending. Those later ayat clarified that work-preservation is compatible with costly expenditure and is threatened by withholding, not by faithful giving.

## 47:34 — إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ وَصَدُّوا۟ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ مَاتُوا۟ وَهُمْ كُفَّارٌۭ فَلَن يَغْفِرَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُمْ

*Focus window: 47:29–38.*

### Activated readings

1. **Death seals a sustained blocking trajectory by ending the interval for protective covering.** **Lexical evidence:** `ك ف ر:B003` is truth-concealing rejection; `ص د د:B001` is blocking or turning away; `س ب ل:B001` is a traversable way; `م و ت:B001` is loss of vitality and life; and `غ ف ر:B002` is protective covering from consequence. **Structural cue:** `ثُمَّ` carries the reject-and-block sequence to death, while the circumstantial `وَهُمْ كُفَّارٌ` specifies persistence in that state; the emphatic future then negates covering. **Assigned role:** blocking defines the lived course, death closes its revisability, and denied covering leaves its consequences exposed. **Abductive move:** I infer that the decisive feature is not death in isolation but death while the concealing posture still governs the person. **Reading change:** non-forgiveness is read as terminal closure of an unreversed trajectory, not as an arbitrary consequence attached to mortality.

### Retrospective surprises

47:35’s promise not to diminish faithful works and 47:38’s warning of replacement sharpen the finality here: persons may be replaced and faithful work preserved, but death ends this actor’s opportunity to reverse the blocking course.

## 47:35 — فَلَا تَهِنُوا۟ وَتَدْعُوٓا۟ إِلَى ٱلسَّلْمِ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلْأَعْلَوْنَ وَٱللَّهُ مَعَكُمْ وَلَن يَتِرَكُمْ أَعْمَٰلَكُمْ

*Focus window: 47:30–38.*

### Activated readings

1. **The prohibited call is peace sought from slackened resolve while holding the stronger position.** **Lexical evidence:** `و ه ن:B001` is weakness of strength and slackening of resolve; `د ع و:B001` is calling to draw another near; `س ل م:B004` is reconciliation or peace opposed to war; and `ع ل و:B004` is prevailing or gaining mastery. **Structural cue:** one prohibition coordinates weakening and calling to peace, while the circumstantial `وَأَنتُمُ ٱلْأَعْلَوْنَ` specifies the addressees’ position. **Assigned role:** weakness supplies the defective motive, calling externalizes it as policy, peace names the proposed settlement, and superiority makes surrender to fear incongruous. **Abductive move:** I infer from the coordination and circumstance that the target is a weakness-driven abandonment of a sound position, not the lexical category of reconciliation in every setting. **Reading change:** the verse is read as a diagnosis of capitulation under internal collapse, with its condition retained rather than generalized away.

2. **Divine accompaniment answers fear with guaranteed integrity of work.** **Lexical evidence:** `و ت ر:B003` is diminution or deprivation of a due, reward, or work; `ع م ل:B001` is intentional work; and `ا ل ه:B002` is the fixed divine name. **Structural cue:** `وَٱللَّهُ مَعَكُمْ` is followed by emphatic negation that God will diminish the addressees’ works. **Assigned role:** accompaniment supplies relational security, and non-diminution protects the value of prior and continuing action. **Abductive move:** I infer that weakness may arise from fear that steadfastness wastes sacrifice; the closing assurance directly removes that premise. **Reading change:** courage is grounded in protected work-value, not in denial of cost.

### Retrospective surprises

47:36–38 immediately shift to wealth, request, stinginess, and spending. That later sequence clarified that “works” include costly resource response and that non-diminution is precisely what makes expenditure non-loss.

## 47:36 — إِنَّمَا ٱلْحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنْيَا لَعِبٌۭ وَلَهْوٌۭ ۚ وَإِن تُؤْمِنُوا۟ وَتَتَّقُوا۟ يُؤْتِكُمْ أُجُورَكُمْ وَلَا يَسْـَٔلْكُمْ أَمْوَٰلَكُمْ

*Focus window: 47:31–38.*

### Activated readings

1. **The nearer life becomes aimless play when diversion displaces accountable work.** **Lexical evidence:** `د ن و:B002` is the nearer, lower, or first side and explicitly includes worldly life; `ل ع ب:B001` is non-serious action without a sound aim; and `ل ه و:B001` is occupation that diverts from another concern. **Structural cue:** the restrictive `إِنَّمَا` compresses worldly life into play and diversion before the conditional reopens a path through faith and guarding. **Assigned role:** nearness explains immediate attraction, play names action detached from a sound end, and diversion names the displacement of the more consequential concern. **Abductive move:** I infer that the verse does not deny all worldly action; it diagnoses worldly life when immediacy severs action from the outcome-reading demanded throughout the surah. **Reading change:** “play and diversion” becomes a functional description of mis-aimed temporality, not a claim that embodied life contains no serious work.

2. **The reward economy distinguishes granted recompense from total extraction of wealth.** **Lexical evidence:** `ء م ن:B002` is trusting assent; `و ق ي:B001` is protective guarding; `ء ت ي:B002` is granting; `أ ج ر:B001` is recompense for work; `س ء ل:B001` is asking or requesting; and `م و ل:B001` is possessed wealth. **Structural cue:** the conditional pairs faith and guarding with granted recompense, then negates asking the addressees for their wealth. **Assigned role:** allegiance and protection define the qualifying action, recompense is its return, and the negated request blocks an extractive reading. **Abductive move:** I infer that divine command is not a transfer driven by divine need; the giver receives recompense rather than supplying a deficient owner. **Reading change:** the verse’s economy is relational and remunerative, not confiscatory.

### Retrospective surprises

47:37 introduces a counterfactual exhaustive request and 47:38 gives an actual call to spend. Those later ayat corrected any reading of `وَلَا يَسْـَٔلْكُمْ أَمْوَٰلَكُمْ` as “no material demand at all”: the negation concerns totalizing extraction, not calibrated expenditure.

## 47:37 — إِن يَسْـَٔلْكُمُوهَا فَيُحْفِكُمْ تَبْخَلُوا۟ وَيُخْرِجْ أَضْغَٰنَكُمْ

*Focus window: 47:32–38.*

### Activated readings

1. **Exhaustive extraction is posed as a counterfactual stress test of attachment.** **Lexical evidence:** `س ء ل:B001` is asking or requesting; `ح ف و:B002` is exhaustive pressing that wears out the one asked; `ب خ ل:B001` is withholding possessions where they should not be withheld; `خ ر ج:B002` is causing a hidden thing to emerge; and `ض غ ن:B001` is inwardly folded rancor. **Structural cue:** the conditional links requesting the wealth, pressing to exhaustion, stinginess, and emergence of rancor in a tight result chain. **Assigned role:** exhaustive pressure is the test condition, stinginess is the immediate response, and extracted rancor is the latent state made visible. **Abductive move:** I infer that possessive attachment is not measured only by what one owns but by what pressure makes one withhold and resent. **Reading change:** the hypothetical does not accuse every addressee of present rancor; it models the threshold at which concealed attachment would become observable.

### Retrospective surprises

47:38 replaces exhaustive pressure with a bounded call to spend and states that stinginess rebounds on the self. The later ayah turned the counterfactual into a control case that clarifies the fairness and diagnostic force of the actual request.

## 47:38 — هَٰٓأَنتُمْ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ تُدْعَوْنَ لِتُنفِقُوا۟ فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَمِنكُم مَّن يَبْخَلُ ۖ وَمَن يَبْخَلْ فَإِنَّمَا يَبْخَلُ عَن نَّفْسِهِۦ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ ٱلْغَنِىُّ وَأَنتُمُ ٱلْفُقَرَآءُ ۚ وَإِن تَتَوَلَّوْا۟ يَسْتَبْدِلْ قَوْمًا غَيْرَكُمْ ثُمَّ لَا يَكُونُوٓا۟ أَمْثَٰلَكُم

*Focus window: 47:33–38.*

### Activated readings

1. **Spending is outward passage along a way, while stinginess blocks the withholder’s own benefit.** **Lexical evidence:** `د ع و:B001` is a call drawing the addressee toward action; `ن ف ق:B002` is wealth going out as expenditure; `س ب ل:B001` is a traversable way or means; `ب خ ل:B001` is wrongful withholding; and `ن ف س:B012` is the very self or thing itself. **Structural cue:** the call gives expenditure a directed location, `فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ`, then the root for stinginess is repeated three times and restricted by `فَإِنَّمَا` to action against one’s own self. **Assigned role:** expenditure moves resources into a meaningful course, withholding stops that movement, and the self receives the loss produced by the stop. **Abductive move:** in light of 47:35’s guarantee that works will not be diminished and 47:36’s recompense, I infer that outgoing wealth is not net loss; refusal blocks the withholder’s participation in the returning benefit. **Reading change:** stinginess is self-deprivation, not successful asset preservation.

2. **Need belongs to the addressed community, and replacement preserves the task while changing its human bearer and pattern.** **Lexical evidence:** `غ ن ي:B001` is self-sufficiency and lack of need; `ف ق ر:B002` is need and material poverty; `و ل ي:B007` is turning away; `ب د ل:B001` is one person or group taking another’s place; `ق و م:B001` is a group of people; `غ ي ر:B005` is otherness or difference; and `م ث ل:B001` is likeness or counterpart. **Structural cue:** divine self-sufficiency is directly contrasted with human need, then withdrawal triggers substitution by another people who will not be the addressees’ likenesses. **Assigned role:** sufficiency denies divine dependency on the expenditure, poverty identifies the beneficiaries’ need, replacement maintains agency for the task, and non-likeness marks a changed behavioral pattern. **Abductive move:** I infer that the mission is not hostage to one community: refusal removes that community from its role, while another group performs the function differently. **Reading change:** replacement is not merely demographic exchange; it is functional succession accompanied by correction of the failed pattern.

### Retrospective surprises

There is no later ayah in the packet. On retrospective review, 47:33–37 already supply the needed controls: obedience preserves work, steadfast work is not diminished, recompense answers expenditure, and exhaustive extraction is explicitly distinguished from the actual call. No correction or unresolved surprise remains.
