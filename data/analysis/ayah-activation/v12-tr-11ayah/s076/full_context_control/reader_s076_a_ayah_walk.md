# Surah 76 Full-Context Control: Eleven-Ayah Ayah Walk

## 76:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A generative enclosure.** Lexically, the repeated ر ح م in *al-raḥmān al-raḥīm* activates ر ح م B003, “womb,” alongside B001, mercy and tenderness. The structural cue is the doubled mercy-root at the threshold followed, within the focus window, by the human made from a mixed drop in 76:2. The womb branch assigns mercy the role of a bounded medium in which formation can occur. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies mercy and embryonic mixture; I infer that encompassing mercy enables formed life to emerge. **Reading change:** the opening no longer invokes only an attitude of compassion; it also opens the surah inside a generative, protective space.

2. **Naming as emergence from non-mention.** Lexically, س م و B005 is the “name/raised designation” variant used for *bismi*, and ء ل ه B002 is the fixed divine name used for *Allāh*. The structural cue is their initial coupling before 76:1 says the human was not a mentioned thing, while 76:2–3 makes and guides that human. The names function as acts of designation that precede the transition into mentionability. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies an invoked name and a formerly unmentioned human; I infer that authoritative designation makes the coming account publicly addressable. **Reading change:** *bismi llāh* reads as an inaugurating act that raises the discourse and its human subject into named presence.

### Retrospective surprises

76:31 unexpectedly returns to ر ح م B003 through the spatial *fī raḥmatihi*: the opening’s doubled mercy can now be reread as the enclosure into which the close causes entry. The later endpoint strengthens the generative-domain reading, while 76:25’s renewed “name of your Lord” shows that the initial designation also becomes a repeated discipline.

## 76:1 — هَلْ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلْإِنسَٰنِ حِينٌۭ مِّنَ ٱلدَّهْرِ لَمْ يَكُن شَيْـًۭٔا مَّذْكُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **Existence crosses a perceptibility threshold.** Lexically, ء ن س B001 gives *al-insān* visible human presence and B002 gives perception by sight or hearing; ش ي ء B001 uses its “knowable thing” variant; ذ ك ر B004 gives spoken mention; and ك و ن B001 gives temporal being or occurrence. The structural cue is the negative chain *lam yakun shayʾan madhkūran*, followed immediately in 76:2 by making the human hearing and seeing. These branches assign “human” the role of a being who becomes both perceptible and perceptive. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies non-mention followed by sensory formation; I infer that formed sensory presence enables the human to become knowable and reportable. **Reading change:** the ayah marks not mere prior nothingness but a transition from unavailable-to-discourse into detectable, accountable presence.

2. **Time arrives as pressure, not an empty container.** Lexically, ء ت ي B001 is coming or arrival, ح ي ن B001 is a due occasion or span, and د ه ر B001 is an overmastering force rather than only B002’s stretch of time. The structural cue is *atā ʿalā* placing the arriving span over the human, while 76:4–6 soon divides the ensuing condition into confinement/fire and cup/spring. The force branch assigns *al-dahr* an active temporal role. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies an arriving interval and later state change; I infer that temporal pressure carries the human from non-description into consequential differentiation. **Reading change:** the opening question becomes a probe into what time does to the human, not simply how long the human was absent.

### Retrospective surprises

The “unmentioned” condition became sharper when 76:25 commanded mention of the Lord’s name and 76:29 called the discourse a *tadhkirah*. The later ذ ك ر B009 reminder converts the human’s move into mentionability into an obligation to remember; 76:30’s ع ل م B001 knowledge then closes the arc from a non-reportable thing to a fully known chooser.

## 76:2 — إِنَّا خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِن نُّطْفَةٍ أَمْشَاجٍۢ نَّبْتَلِيهِ فَجَعَلْنَٰهُ سَمِيعًۢا بَصِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **Sensation is test equipment.** Lexically, خ ل ق B001 is measuring and proportioning, ن ط ف B002 is a drop or reproductive fluid, م ش ج B001 is mixture, ب ل و B002 is testing that discloses, ج ع ل B002 is turning into a state, س م ع B001 is hearing, and ب ص ر B001 is sight. The structural cue is the ordered chain mixture → testing → *fa*-marked making into hearing and seeing, followed in 76:3 by a traversable way and alternatives of response. The senses therefore serve as instruments of disclosure. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies measured mixture, test, faculties, and path; I infer that hearing and sight enable the test by making signs and consequences detectable. **Reading change:** the closing predicates are not a biological inventory; they specify the operational capacities through which the mixed creature is tested.

2. **Mixed formation leaves room for disclosed character.** Lexically, خ ل ق B004 is inner character, م ش ج B002 is mixed reproductive or bodily substance, and ب ل و B002 is testing/disclosure. The structural cue is that *amshāj* modifies the originating drop, while 76:3 presents gratitude and covering as alternative predicates rather than ingredients. The character branch assigns creation an inward, not only bodily, dimension. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies mixture and later alternatives; I infer that mixed formation creates a field of dispositions whose orientation is revealed, rather than mechanically fixed, by testing. **Reading change:** creation can be read as the formation of a layered moral constitution. This is exploratory because B004 is activated through the root of the finite verb, while the ayah’s overt syntax most directly supports originating and proportioning.

### Retrospective surprises

76:19’s pearls unexpectedly activates ن ط ف B001, “pearl/earring,” behind the fixed B002 reproductive drop. It does not change *nuṭfah* into a pearl; instead, the later jewel image creates a root-level trajectory from minute fluid origin to luminous perceived form. 76:28 then repeats creation and bodily fastening, confirming that the sensory test depends on maintained embodiment.

## 76:3 — إِنَّا هَدَيْنَٰهُ ٱلسَّبِيلَ إِمَّا شَاكِرًۭا وَإِمَّا كَفُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **Guidance makes a route usable before it sorts responses.** Lexically, ه د ي B001 is gentle guidance and س ب ل B001 is a traversed way or means. The structural cue is the completed guidance clause followed by the balanced *immā ... wa-immā* predicates, while 76:7–8 supplies concrete acts of vow-keeping and feeding within the same focus window. Guidance functions as route disclosure; the alternatives function as ways of using what was disclosed. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies a made-known route and divergent responses; I infer that making the route traversable enables, and therefore makes visible, accountable response. **Reading change:** the ayah does not say guidance is one of the two outcomes; it places guidance prior to the grateful or covering mode of travel.

2. **Provision is either made to thrive or covered over.** Lexically, ش ك ر B002 is the branch in which a little provision visibly thrives, while ك ف ر B001 is covering/concealing and B004 is ingratitude. The structural cue is that the two active-state predicates precede the cup and spring of 76:5–6 and the food transfer of 76:8. The dormant provision branch assigns *shākir* a productive role, while covering blocks visibility of the gift. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies provision imagery and two responses; I infer that reception either makes a gift’s effect visibly increase or suppresses its acknowledged source. **Reading change:** gratitude and ingratitude become rival resource processes, not only rival interior attitudes.

### Retrospective surprises

76:22 makes the grateful branch reciprocal by applying ش ك ر to the travelers’ *saʿy*, and 76:29 repeats س ب ل B001 while changing the construction from a way already guided to a way deliberately taken. The later relation corrects any passive reading: guidance supplies traversability, but acknowledged striving and appropriation complete the route mechanism.

## 76:4 — إِنَّآ أَعْتَدْنَا لِلْكَٰفِرِينَ سَلَٰسِلَا۟ وَأَغْلَٰلًۭا وَسَعِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **Concealment is answered by an engineered enclosure.** Lexically, ع ت د B001 is something prepared and present, ك ف ر B003 is rejection of truth with B001’s covering image still active, س ل س ل B002 is a linked chain, غ ل ل B006 is a shackle, and س ع ر B001 is blazing fire. The structural cue is the prepared triad: linked restraint, enclosing restraint, and environmental heat, immediately contrasted by freely consumed cup and bursting spring in 76:5–6. The three punishment branches divide control over movement and surroundings. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies covering/rejection and a total restraint system; I infer a counter-form in which chosen concealment results in the agent’s own enclosure. **Reading change:** the list reads as a coordinated mechanism of confinement rather than three interchangeable threats.

2. **The root ك ف ر pivots at the ayah boundary.** Lexically, fixed *al-kāfirīn* uses ك ف ر B003, rejecters of truth, while the next ayah’s *kāfūran* activates the same root’s B011, camphor or an aromatic spring. The structural cue is direct adjacency: the agent plural is followed by a cup whose mixture bears the root in a different form and branch. The morphology keeps B011 from replacing the fixed word’s agentive reading; its role is contrastive. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies one root in punitive and refreshing frames; I infer that the root-level pivot deliberately separates covering response from a cooling, infused provision. **Reading change:** *al-kāfirīn* enters a lexical reversal in which shared sound intensifies, rather than softens, the difference between the two outcomes.

### Retrospective surprises

76:18’s *salsabīlan* made the fixed س ل س ل inventory newly contrastive: B002 supplies linked chains here, whereas B001 supplies smooth flow and easy drinking around the later named spring. The same consonantal structure can therefore bind movement or facilitate it. This distant reversal makes the prepared chains part of a surah-wide opposition between obstructed and frictionless passage.

## 76:5 — إِنَّ ٱلْأَبْرَارَ يَشْرَبُونَ مِن كَأْسٍۢ كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا كَافُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **The cup materializes a prior social disposition.** Lexically, ب ر ر B002 is expansive righteousness and B003 is dutiful kindness, ش ر ب B001 is drinking, ك أ س B001 is a cup together with its contents, and م ز ج B001 is mixing. The structural cue is that *al-abrār* are first shown receiving a mixed drink, then 76:7–9 identifies them through vow-keeping and feeding without desired return. Righteousness supplies the social role; the cup supplies its returned material form. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies outward provision by these agents and later provision to them; I infer that their expansion of access is answered by an accessible, composed drink. **Reading change:** the cup is not detached luxury; it embodies the same provisioning relation that their conduct establishes.

2. **A life runs from one mixture to another.** Lexically, م ز ج B002 is bodily temperament, and fixed *kāfūran* activates ك ف ر B011, camphor or an aromatic spring; 76:2’s م ش ج B002 supplies the contextual mixed reproductive/body substance. The structural cue is the repeated mixture pattern: mixed origin in 76:2, disclosed response in 76:3–4, and a second mixture here, with fire in 76:4 as the immediate foil. The temperament branch assigns the cup an inwardly regulating role. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies an initial bodily mixture and a later cooling mixture; I infer that tested orientation leads from given constitution to a restorative constitution. **Reading change:** *mizājuhā* can describe not only flavor composition but a recalibration of the drinker formed from *amshāj*.

### Retrospective surprises

76:17 repeats both cup and mixture, and 76:21 identifies the Lord as giver of a purifying drink. Those later ayat turn this first cup into the opening member of a paired provisioning system: م ز ج B002’s temperament role is no longer a local analogy only, because the final drink explicitly supplies transformation through ط ه ر B004/B005.

## 76:6 — عَيْنًۭا يَشْرَبُ بِهَا عِبَادُ ٱللَّهِ يُفَجِّرُونَهَا تَفْجِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Recipients become water-routing agents.** Lexically, ع ي ن B006 is a spring, ش ر ب B001 is drinking, ع ب د B003 is worshipful submission, ء ل ه B002 is the fixed divine name, and ف ج ر B001 is a wide split with water outflow. The structural cue is the movement from the cup in 76:5 to a spring here, followed by the intensive cognate pair *yufajjirūnahā tafjīran*. The worshippers receive drink but also occupy the active role of opening or directing its source. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies a spring and emphatic agentive bursting; I infer that submitted recipients are entrusted with extending access to the resource. **Reading change:** the reward is participatory water agency, not merely passive consumption.

2. **Bursting water models generosity.** Lexically, ف ج ر B005 is overflowing generosity, activated alongside B001 by the causative verb and emphatic verbal noun. The structural cue is the spring’s abundant release before the feeding actions of 76:8–9 and the protective gift of 76:11. B005 assigns the outflow an ethical role: provision moves beyond its initial holder. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies hydraulic overflow and nearby voluntary food transfer; I infer that generosity propagates resources as a split channel propagates water. **Reading change:** *yufajjirūnahā tafjīran* becomes a material analogy for distributive giving. This reading is exploratory because the generosity branch is activated by contextual analogy rather than a distinct grammatical complement.

### Retrospective surprises

76:21 unexpectedly clarifies the agency hierarchy by making *rabbuhum* the explicit subject of giving drink. The worshippers’ emphatic bursting of the spring remains real, but it now reads as delegated routing within a provision whose ultimate giver is the Lord. That correction prevents participatory agency from being mistaken for independent sourcehood.

## 76:7 — يُوفُونَ بِٱلنَّذْرِ وَيَخَافُونَ يَوْمًۭا كَانَ شَرُّهُۥ مُسْتَطِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Foresight converts fear into completed obligation.** Lexically, و ف ي B001 is completion and keeping a vow, ن ذ ر B002 is a vow, خ و ف B001 is fear, ي و م B003 is a severe event-day, ك و ن B001 is temporal occurrence, ش ر ر B001 is evil, and ط ي ر B002 is spreading or scattering, activated by *mustaṭīran*. The structural cue coordinates present vow-completion with fear of a day whose evil was spreading; 76:11 then blocks that evil and 76:12 recompenses endurance. Vow-keeping is the preventive action and fear is its anticipatory sensor. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies feared spreading harm, completed duty, and later protection; I infer that foresight causes present fidelity, which is then met by protection. **Reading change:** fear is productive temporal intelligence rather than paralysis, and the day’s evil is read as propagation that must be intercepted.

### Retrospective surprises

76:27’s *yawman thaqīlan* returns to the feared day with ث ق ل B001/B005, adding burden and consequential weight to the earlier ط ي ر B002 spreading evil. 76:31 then gives that feared propagation a terminal form in prepared painful punishment. The later closure confirms that vow-keeping is oriented toward a real deferred consequence, not generalized caution.

## 76:8 — وَيُطْعِمُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ مِسْكِينًۭا وَيَتِيمًۭا وَأَسِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **One transfer repairs three different ruptures.** Lexically, ط ع م B002 is feeding, س ك ن B006 is poverty, ي ت م B001 is the orphan’s severance from parental support, and ء س ر B001 is binding or captivity. The structural cue is the single feeding verb governing the equal accusative triad *miskīnan wa-yatīman wa-asīran*. The three branches assign distinct social roles to the recipients: immobilized provision, broken kin support, and coerced confinement. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies one food transfer across three deprivations; I infer that shared nourishment partially repairs each rupture by restoring material access and social inclusion. **Reading change:** the list becomes a diagnostic map of deprivation addressed by one distributive act, not a generic list of needy people.

2. **Love supplies resistance rather than a settled object.** Lexically, ح ب ب B002 is love or attachment, and the fixed *ʿalā ḥubbihi* places that attachment before the recipients. The pronoun’s referent remains structurally open; it may orient attachment toward the food, the act, or the divine without requiring one final choice. Love’s assigned role is the force across which giving occurs. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies attachment plus transfer; I infer that giving has diagnostic force because it redirects something held inwardly toward another person. **Reading change:** feeding is no longer expenditure from neutral surplus; it is a costly conversion of attachment into provision while the object of attachment remains productively unresolved.

### Retrospective surprises

76:28 reuses ء س ر but activates B002, the strong bodily frame, after fixed *asīran* used B001 captivity. The later ayah shows that binding can either coerce a person or beneficially hold a body together. That surprise sharpens the feeding act: it responds to a socially harmful bond while the same root later names the cohesion that enables human agency.

## 76:9 — إِنَّمَا نُطْعِمُكُمْ لِوَجْهِ ٱللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمْ جَزَآءًۭ وَلَا شُكُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **The act exits the recipient-return economy.** Lexically, ط ع م B002 is feeding, و ج ه B002 is orientation and B008 is the proper course, ء ل ه B002 is the fixed divine name, ر و د B001 is willing or desiring, ج ز ي B001 is recompense, and ش ك ر B001 is gratitude. The structural cue is *innamā* restriction followed by the parallel negations *lā nurīdu ... jazāʾan wa-lā shukūran*; 76:11–12 then supplies divine protection and recompense. Orientation assigns the act a destination other than its human recipients. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies refusal of human return and later return from God; I infer that removing recipient debt leaves the transfer directed toward the divine and permits recompense to arrive from another source. **Reading change:** the feeding becomes a deliberately rerouted economy, not unrewarded action.

2. **A sequence of faces transmits affect.** Lexically, و ج ه B001 is face or front, used in the fixed *li-wajhi llāh*. The structural cue is the progression from this divine face, to the frowning day in 76:10, to the freshness placed upon the recipients in 76:11. The fixed face functions as the chosen orientation through an opposing facial field. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies divine orientation, threatening countenance, and received radiance; I infer that maintaining the first orientation through the second enables the transformation manifested in the third. **Reading change:** “for the face of God” becomes the first station in an affective relay rather than an isolated motive formula.

### Retrospective surprises

76:22 exactly returns both denied terms from another source: the surrounding realm is *jazāʾan* and the effort is *mashkūran*. This later pairing confirms the rerouted economy. Refusal of recipient recompense and gratitude does not erase return; it prevents the recipients from being made debtors and leaves divine acknowledgment to answer the act.

## 76:10 — إِنَّا نَخَافُ مِن رَّبِّنَا يَوْمًا عَبُوسًۭا قَمْطَرِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A threatening day is treated as an approaching agent.** Lexically, خ و ف B001 is fear, ر ب ب B002 is nurturing toward completion, ي و م B003 is a severe event-day, ع ب س B001 is a facial scowl with B002’s harsh-day scope, and ق م ط ر B001 is a day of harsh, spreading trouble. The structural cue is the double personifying predicate *ʿabūsan qamṭarīran* after the speakers explain their feeding in 76:8–9; 76:11 immediately answers with protection. The day functions as a hostile encounter, while the Lord functions as the source from whom protection can come. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies anticipated threat, altruistic action, and later warding; I infer that fear of the approaching day helps cause present food transfer and directs reliance toward the nurturer. **Reading change:** the confession describes action-producing foresight under a vivid agentive threat, not diffuse anxiety.

2. **Trouble accumulates before it spreads.** Lexically, ق م ط ر B002 is piling or drawing together, coactivated with B001 by the intensive *qamṭarīran*. The structural cue is the accumulation of two severe adjectives, followed in 76:11 by a singular act that wards off the day’s evil and then by distributed comforts in 76:12–15. The piling branch assigns the day the role of a convergence point for harms. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies adjectival accumulation and later comprehensive protection; I infer that gathered pressures produce the day’s outward harshness. **Reading change:** the day reads as concentrated burden, which explains why the response must be protective before it can be rewarding. This is exploratory because the piling branch is carried by the adjective’s root image rather than overt object syntax.

### Retrospective surprises

76:27’s heavy day adds ث ق ل B001 physical/assessed weight and B005 moral gravity to the scowl and accumulated harshness here. 76:31 then specifies prepared pain. The later ayat turn the personified threat into a temporally deferred but operationally weighty outcome, strengthening fear’s role as rational foresight.

## 76:11 — فَوَقَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ شَرَّ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْيَوْمِ وَلَقَّىٰهُمْ نَضْرَةًۭ وَسُرُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Protection performs a qualitative conversion.** Lexically, و ق ي B001 is a protective barrier, ء ل ه B002 is the fixed divine name, ش ر ر B001 is evil, ي و م B003 is the severe event-day, ل ق ي B004 is meeting or encounter, ن ض ر B001 is radiant freshness, and س ر ر B010 is joy and ease. The structural cue is causal *fa-* followed by two coordinated divine actions: first warding the day’s evil, then making the people encounter freshness and joy. The barrier blocks one encounter so that another can occur. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies intercepted evil and imparted opposite qualities; I infer that protection preserves the persons long enough for threat to be replaced by embodied freshness and ease. **Reading change:** salvation is not simple subtraction of harm; it is a two-stage conversion from blocked contact to positive encounter.

2. **The encounter becomes materially embodied.** Lexically, ل ق ي B004 gives *laqqāhum* face-to-face encounter rather than an abstract possession transfer, while ن ض ر B001 and س ر ر B010 name visible and affective states. The structural cue is that 76:12–16 immediately expands these states into garden, silk, reclining, shade, fruit, and vessels. The meeting branch assigns the reward an experiential role. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies qualities here and material supports afterward; I infer that the later environment is the means by which freshness and ease are made encounterable. **Reading change:** *laqqāhum* opens an inhabited sensory condition, not merely a ledger entry of reward.

### Retrospective surprises

76:21 extends the conversion inward: the same protected people receive a Lord-given purifying drink after the outward radiance and joy named here. Then 76:22 points to the whole scene as recompense. The later sequence shows that و ق ي B001’s barrier is not the endpoint; it preserves the subject for a coordinated outward, affective, and inward remaking.

## 76:12 — وَجَزَىٰهُم بِمَا صَبَرُوا۟ جَنَّةًۭ وَحَرِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Restraint returns as protective covering.** Lexically, ج ز ي B001 is recompense, ص ب ر B001 is self-restraint, ج ن ن B003 is garden with B001’s covering image, and ح ر ر B010 is silk. The structural cue is causal *bi-mā ṣabarū* followed by two direct objects, then 76:13–17 elaborates enclosure, shade, vessels, and drink. Restraint supplies the prior bounded action; garden-cover and silk supply outward protection and ease. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies endured self-containment and later encompassing materials; I infer an exact-form return in which disciplined containment is recompensed by benign containment. **Reading change:** garden and silk read as a material answer to the mechanism of patience, not merely adjacent prizes.

2. **Silk carries a latent release from binding.** Lexically, fixed *ḥarīran* overtly uses ح ر ر B010, silk, while the same root’s B002 is freedom; 76:8’s captive activates ء س ر B001, binding, inside the focus window. The structural cue is the movement from feeding a bound person to receiving a garment whose root also contains release. The freedom branch assigns silk a relational overtone. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies captivity and silk reward; I infer that the textile can mark release from coercive constraint as well as tactile comfort. **Reading change:** *ḥarīr* becomes a sign of unbinding. This is exploratory because B002 does not replace the fixed noun’s secure textile branch; it operates through root-level resonance.

### Retrospective surprises

76:24 later turns the completed *ṣabarū* back into the imperative *fa-ṣbir*. That recurrence makes ص ب ر B001 both the criterion retrospectively rewarded here and the ongoing control method required under pressure. The reward scene therefore does not close patience as past biography; it demonstrates what the newly commanded practice produces.

## 76:13 — مُّتَّكِـِٔينَ فِيهَا عَلَى ٱلْأَرَآئِكِ ۖ لَا يَرَوْنَ فِيهَا شَمْسًۭا وَلَا زَمْهَرِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Rest depends on calibrated climate.** Lexically, و ك ء B004 is reclining with support, ء ر ك B004 is a couch in a curtained chamber, ر ء ي B001 is seeing, ش م س B001 is the sun, and ز م ه ر B001 is intense cold. The structural cue places supported posture before a balanced double negation of heat-source and cold extreme; 76:14–18 then supplies near shade, accessible fruit, measured vessels, and springs. The couch and climate branches form one environmental system. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies posture, removed extremes, and nearby resources; I infer that environmental calibration enables sustained repose. **Reading change:** the ayah describes engineered habitability rather than an ornamental couch plus unrelated weather notes.

2. **Visibility need not depend on the sun.** Lexically, ز م ه ر B003 is a flashing star, coactivated exploratorily alongside the fixed cold branch, while ر ء ي B001 foregrounds visual perception and ش م س B001 is explicitly absent. The structural cue is the coming silver and glass of 76:15–16, materials capable of glinting within a sunless scene. The star-flash branch assigns *zamharīr* a dormant luminosity relation. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies seeing without sun and reflective vessels; I infer that distributed material gleam can replace a single harsh light source. **Reading change:** the negation can participate in a softer visual ecology, not only thermal balance. The weak link is morphological: B003 is a remote root image and does not displace the adjective’s direct intense-cold reading.

### Retrospective surprises

76:20’s doubled *raʾayta* returns emphatic sight after this ayah’s “they do not see,” and 76:21 supplies green textile and silver surfaces without restoring the sun. The later visual abundance supports the correction that the negation removes harsh sources and extremes, not visibility itself; seeing is intensified inside the calibrated habitat.

## 76:14 — وَدَانِيَةً عَلَيْهِمْ ظِلَٰلُهَا وَذُلِّلَتْ قُطُوفُهَا تَذْلِيلًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **The environment closes the effort gap.** Lexically, د ن و B001 is nearness, ظ ل ل B003 is canopy and B004 shelter, ذ ل ل B003 is ease or accessibility after difficulty and B005 is low-hanging, and ق ط ف B002 is picked fruit or a low cluster. The structural cue joins spatial nearness with the intensive passive pair *dhullilat ... tadhlīlan*. Shade and fruit approach from above while the recipients remain reclining in 76:13. These branches assign the environment anticipatory service. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies near shelter and deliberately yielded clusters; I infer that induced accessibility removes the need for the recipient to seek, climb, or strain. **Reading change:** abundance becomes an ergonomic relation between body and environment, not merely a quantity of fruit.

2. **Humiliation is redirected away from the person.** Lexically, ذ ل ل B001 is humiliation, but B002 is gentle yielding and B003 is ease/accessibility. The structural cue makes fruit clusters, not the rewarded people, the grammatical object of the intensive passive; 76:12–13 has already established honor and repose. B001 supplies a force relation that the syntax corrects: what is subdued is environmental resistance. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies a potentially abasive root but a nonhuman patient; I infer that power is transferred into making provision yield rather than making the recipient low. **Reading change:** the root’s force is preserved while its social valence reverses into hospitable accessibility.

### Retrospective surprises

The structural cue is directional *ilā rabbihi* after the regimen of patience, remembrance, and prostration in 76:24–26. B009 assigns the path a character-forming role rather than treating it as a single step. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies repeated practices and a taken way; I infer that sustained traversal reshapes the traveler into the conduct patterned by that route. **Reading change:** *ittakhadha ... sabīlan* denotes adopted formation as well as chosen direction.

### Retrospective surprises

76:30 immediately qualifies *man shāʾa* by nesting it within divine willing, preventing ء خ ذ B010 adoption from being read as self-originating. 76:31 then changes the directional *ilā rabbihi* into causative entry *fī raḥmatihi*. The path is thus retrospectively an adopted means whose successful arrival is enabled and completed by another agent.

## 76:30 — وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Human willing is real but nested.** Lexically, both forms of ش ي ء use B002’s “will toward a thing” variant, whose accepted scope can include bringing about or attaining what is willed; ء ل ه B002 is the fixed divine name; ك و ن B001 is temporal predication; ع ل م B001 is knowing as a thing becomes clear; and ح ك م B003 is wisdom. The structural cue places human willing inside an exception dependent on divine willing, then appends knowledge and wisdom as qualifications. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies both wills and the exception relation; I infer an enabling arrow from divine willing to human willing, not the erasure of the latter. **Reading change:** the ayah presents dependent agency whose enabling source is characterized as knowing and wise rather than arbitrary.

2. **Knowledge marks the route and wisdom bounds it.** Lexically, ع ل م B002 is a distinguishing mark that points toward something, and ح ك م B001 is restraint for repair, coactivated with the adjectives’ direct B001 knowledge and B003 wisdom. The structural cue is the immediately preceding path in 76:29 and the outcome allocation in 76:31. These dormant branches assign the closing attributes navigational and limiting roles. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies a path, divine attributes, and terminal outcomes; I infer that knowledge makes the route legible while wisdom constrains how willing becomes effective. **Reading change:** *ʿalīman ḥakīman* can describe governance of a marked and bounded way, not only static qualities. This is exploratory because the adjective forms directly favor knowing and wisdom; mark and restraint are contextual root resonances.

### Retrospective surprises

76:31 immediately makes the inferred enabling arrow concrete: divine willing is followed by causative entry, while prepared punishment shows effective arrangement. The later syntax confirms that ش ي ء B002’s “will toward a thing” may carry bringing-about force here; knowledge and wisdom qualify an agency that actually allocates outcomes.

## 76:31 — يُدْخِلُ مَن يَشَآءُ فِى رَحْمَتِهِۦ ۚ وَٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ أَعَدَّ لَهُمْ عَذَابًا أَلِيمًۢا

### Activated readings

1. **Mercy is an entered, generative domain.** Lexically, د خ ل B001 is entering or causing entry; ش ي ء B002 uses the “will toward a thing” variant; and ر ح م B001 is mercy while B003 is womb. The structural cue is the spatial construction *yudkhilu ... fī raḥmatihi*, following created bodily cohesion in 76:28 and nested willing in 76:30. Entry supplies movement into an enclosure; the womb branch supplies a generative model for that enclosure. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies causative entry, mercy, and recent creation language; I infer that divine willing transfers the person into a protecting medium capable of renewed formation. **Reading change:** mercy becomes an inhabited, regenerative space rather than only a favorable disposition. This is exploratory because B003 is activated by spatial and creation cues while B001 remains the direct reading.

2. **The two outcomes have asymmetric logistics.** Lexically, ظ ل م B004 is concrete misplacement or mistiming and B008 is withholding a right; ع د د B002 is preparing what is readied; ع ذ ب B005 is torment or punishment; and ء ل م B002 is causing pain or being painful. The structural cue directly causes an unspecified person to enter mercy, but fronts *al-ẓālimīn* and then states that painful punishment was prepared for them. Misplacement/withholding supplies the agents’ disorder; preparation supplies a separately readied consequence. **Inferred arrow:** the packet supplies persistent misplacement or withholding and prepared pain; I infer that those practices lead into a fitted punitive provision, while mercy is described by direct inclusion. **Reading change:** the close does not present two syntactically identical containers; it contrasts active admission into mercy with a prepared consequence assigned to wrongdoers.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah exists in the packet, so no later surprise emerged. As the terminal ayah, it instead retrospectively closes the opening ر ح م B001/B003 enclosure, the path-and-will sequence of 76:29–30, and the prepared punishment first introduced in 76:4.
