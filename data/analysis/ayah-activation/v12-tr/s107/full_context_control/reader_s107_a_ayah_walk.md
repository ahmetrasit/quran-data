# S107 v12 full-context ayah walk

## 107:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Invocation as elevated counter-name.** Evidence: `س م و B005` name as raised designation, `س م و B008` good repute spread, `ء ل ه B002` fixed divine name in oath/invocation, and the later `ر ء ي B005` showing off before people in 107:6. Structure: the packet opens with the Name, then later shows a competing public visibility economy. Mechanism: a name can elevate either by true invocation or by reputational display. Abductive move: I supply the contrast between divine designation and human display; the packet supplies name/elevation and showing-to-be-seen. Reading change: the basmalah becomes not only a formula of beginning but a diagnostic standard for whether public religious signs are raised toward God or toward audience.

2. **Mercy as repaired kinship and provision.** Evidence: `ر ح م B001` mercy/tenderness, `ر ح م B002` kinship bond, `ر ح م B003` womb; activated by `ي ت م B001` orphaned child cut from protector in 107:2, `س ك ن B006` needy abasement in 107:3, and `م ع ن B005` small available benefit in 107:7. Structure: doubled mercy precedes later ruptures in care, food, and household benefit. Mechanism: the divine merciful names set a kinship-like shelter against social severance. Abductive move: I infer that mercy is not merely feeling but a reparative social relation; the packet supplies orphaning, need, and withheld benefit. Reading change: the opening reads as an activation of care-lines that the rest of the surah exposes as broken.

3. **Womb/container pressure against expulsion.** Evidence: `ر ح م B003` womb as child-vessel, `ر ح م B004` womb pain after birth, activated by `د ع ع B001` forceful thrusting in 107:2 and `د ع ع B002` agitating a vessel until it fills. Structure: womb/vessel imagery meets pushing and filling imagery. Mechanism: a protected container that should hold the vulnerable is reversed into a space that ejects or jars them. Abductive move: I supply the maternal-container analogy; the packet supplies womb, orphan, and thrust/agitation branches. Reading change: the basmalah's mercy becomes bodily and protective, not abstract benevolence.

### Retrospective surprises

The later `ر ء ي B005` in 107:6 most strongly reconfigured the basmalah: “name” and “mercy” became contested by public religious reputation. The final `م ع ن B001/B005` also made the double mercy feel like open flow and small available benefit, not only an attribute-title.

## 107:1 — أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ

### Activated readings

1. **“Have you seen?” as a demanded social diagnosis.** Evidence: `ر ء ي B013` tell-me/alert formula, with `ر ء ي B001` eye or insight; activated by the return of `ر ء ي B005` showing off before people in 107:6. Structure: the same seeing-root frames the surah at the question and at ostentation. Mechanism: the opening question asks the hearer to see through the later performer who acts to be seen. Abductive move: I infer that later public display clarifies the first seeing as discernment, not mere observation. Reading change: the ayah becomes an invitation to identify a type whose religion is visually legible in social behavior.

2. **Denying الدين as breaking a debt/reckoning circuit.** Evidence: `ك ذ ب B002` declaring false/denying, `د ي ن B002` reckoning and recompense, `د ي ن B003` debt and credit; activated by `ط ع م B002` feeding another and `م ع ن B005` small lent household benefit. Structure: denial of dīn is followed by failures in feeding and lending. Mechanism: obligation, repayment, and recompense form one economy; the denier acts as if no account is due. Abductive move: I supply the causal arrow from denied reckoning to withheld goods; the packet supplies the debt/recompense and giving/withholding lexicon. Reading change: “denies the dīn” reads materially: he cancels the due of God and neighbor.

3. **Falsehood as failed performance, not only false speech.** Evidence: `ك ذ ب B001` falsehood in speech/action, `ك ذ ب B004` a charge proving false by failing, and `ك ذ ب B006` milk failing expectation; activated by `ص ل و B003` prescribed worship in 107:4-5 and `س ه و B001` heedlessness in prayer. Structure: the person may still be among the praying, yet the act fails its expected yield. Mechanism: visible rite promises care, attention, and mercy; neglect of the vulnerable reveals the promise false. Abductive move: I infer that prayer is tested by its output in care; the packet supplies prayer, heedlessness, and failure branches. Reading change: تكذيب becomes embodied falsification, a life proving its religious claim untrue.

4. **Dīn as customary regime or subjugating order.** Evidence: `د ي ن B005` habit/customary state and `د ي ن B004` subjugation/possession; activated by `د ع ع B001` harsh driving of the orphan and `س ك ن B006` abasement of the miskīn. Structure: the abstract object of denial is immediately translated into treatment of weak persons. Mechanism: denying dīn can mean refusing the ordered moral regime and replacing it with domination. Abductive move: I supply the social-order inference; the packet supplies habit, authority, pushing, and abasement. Reading change: the ayah can read as exposure of an anti-dīn social custom: a habit of mastery over the dependent.

### Retrospective surprises

107:6 retrospectively made the first `أرأيت` sharper: the hearer is asked to see a person whose own vice is being-seen. 107:7 also made `د ي ن B003` debt/credit newly visible, because the denial of dīn ends in refusing even the smallest payable benefit.

## 107:2 — فَذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِى يَدُعُّ ٱلْيَتِيمَ

### Activated readings

1. **The orphan is physically and legally thrust away.** Evidence: `د ع ع B001` rough thrusting or forceful driving, `ي ت م B001` offspring cut from protecting parent; activated by `م ن ع B002` barrier between a person and what he seeks in 107:7. Structure: pushing comes early; explicit withholding comes last. Mechanism: the push is not only bodily harshness but the creation of a barrier between the orphan and due support. Abductive move: I supply the connection from physical force to social/legal exclusion; the packet supplies thrust, orphan-cutoff, and barrier branches. Reading change: يدع اليتيم reads as active displacement from protection, not merely rudeness.

2. **Orphaning as intensified isolation.** Evidence: `ي ت م B002` isolated singleness/peerless separation, with `ي ت م B004` delay in care; activated by `س ك ن B003` household inhabitants and `م ع ن B006` dwelling/stopping-place. Structure: household/dwelling roots later define what the orphan lacks. Mechanism: the denier converts a child already cut off into a socially un-housed solitary figure. Abductive move: I infer that the later domestic lexicon casts the orphan as excluded from household circulation. Reading change: the ayah becomes about isolating the vulnerable from the house, not only pushing one child.

3. **Resource-packing without mercy.** Evidence: `د ع ع B002` agitating a vessel until it fills; activated by `ط ع م B004` provision/livelihood and `م ع ن B005` small available benefit. Structure: the same root that can move a container toward fullness appears in an ayah about pushing away need. Mechanism: goods can be packed and retained while the dependent is driven off. Abductive move: I supply the economic inversion: movement that should help a vessel receive becomes social violence that keeps resources inside. Reading change: يدع becomes a pressure of hoarding as much as a shove.

4. **Heedless shortfall around the orphan.** Evidence: `ي ت م B003` heedlessness/falling short and `ي ت م B004` delayed kindness; activated by `س ه و B001` heedlessness in 107:5. Structure: later “heedless of prayer” echoes a dormant orphan-root branch. Mechanism: ritual inattention and social delay are the same posture of missed attention. Abductive move: I infer a parallel between failure to attend to prayer and failure to attend to the orphan; the packet supplies both heedlessness branches. Reading change: the orphan ayah becomes an early sign of the later prayer-defect.

### Retrospective surprises

The later `س ه و B001` surprised me by activating `ي ت م B003/B004`: orphan-care is delayed or missed before prayer is missed. The final `م ن ع B002` also clarified the shove as barrier-making, not only momentary violence.

## 107:3 — وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلْمِسْكِينِ

### Activated readings

1. **Failure to urge is a broken public mechanism.** Evidence: `ح ض ض B001` urging/inciting toward a thing, `ط ع م B002` feeding another, `س ك ن B006` poverty and humble abasement; activated by plural/social forms in 107:4-7. Structure: the ayah condemns not only non-feeding but non-mobilizing. Mechanism: food for the needy depends on communal pressure and exhortation; the denier blocks even the speech that would move resources. Abductive move: I supply the social-catalyst role of urging; the packet supplies urging, feeding, and needy abasement. Reading change: the ayah targets collapse of advocacy, not only private charity.

2. **Low ground and abasement.** Evidence: `ح ض ض B002` low ground at a mountain-foot, `س ك ن B006` abasement/poverty; activated by `س م و B001` rising high in 107:0 and `س م و B005` raised designation. Structure: high divine naming contrasts with low social placement. Mechanism: the miskīn is not merely poor but held at the bottom while the actor seeks elevated name or display. Abductive move: I infer vertical social mapping; the packet supplies high/low and abasement branches. Reading change: “على طعام المسكين” becomes a scene of refused lifting from low ground.

3. **Food as provisionary order, not morsel.** Evidence: `ط ع م B001` food/taste, `ط ع م B004` livelihood/legal share/revenue, `ط ع م B005` ripening/produce; activated by `د ي ن B003` debt/credit and `م ع ن B005` small available benefit. Structure: food sits between dīn-debt and maʿūn-benefit. Mechanism: feeding is part of an economy of due provision, shares, produce, and circulating small goods. Abductive move: I supply that “food” stands for access to livelihood channels; the packet supplies provision, legal share, debt, and lent benefit. Reading change: the ayah criticizes refusal to sustain the needy’s place in the provisioning system.

4. **Need as lack of settled rest.** Evidence: `س ك ن B001` cessation/stillness, `س ك ن B002` dwelling, `س ك ن B004` comfort/rest, `س ك ن B010` provision enabling staying. Structure: miskīn is built from a root whose branches include house, rest, and food that lets one remain. Mechanism: hunger destabilizes residence; feeding restores the capacity to stay and be calm. Abductive move: I infer that food repairs motion/agitation and homelessness; the packet supplies rest, dwelling, provision, and needy branches. Reading change: طعام المسكين reads as restoration of settled human life, not just caloric relief.

### Retrospective surprises

107:7 changed the food ayah most: `م ع ن B005` turned “food of the miskīn” into one node in a larger domestic circulation of small benefits. 107:5 also made the lack of `ح ض ض B001` feel like inertness: the problem is not only no food, but no movement of others toward food.

## 107:4 — فَوَيْلٌۭ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Woe to practitioners when prayer is severed from mercy.** Evidence: `ص ل و B003` prescribed worship, activated by `ر ح م B001` mercy and `ط ع م B002` feeding another. Structure: prayer enters only after orphan and miskīn failures. Mechanism: the ritual is judged by whether it carries mercy into social action. Abductive move: I infer that the preceding care-failures contextualize “those who pray”; the packet supplies order and branches. Reading change: the woe does not target prayer as such but a praying identity hollowed out by denied care.

2. **Prayer as supplication/mercy inverted.** Evidence: `ص ل و B002` supplication, praise, mercy; activated by the double `ر ح م` in 107:0 and non-urging for food in 107:3. Structure: the prayer-root shares mercy language with the opening but is surrounded by merciless conduct. Mechanism: a practice whose branch can be mercy becomes evidence against its doer when it fails to generate mercy. Abductive move: I supply the evaluative inversion; the packet supplies mercy in both root inventories. Reading change: المصلين can mean people near a mercy-channel who have blocked its output.

3. **Fire/heat behind woe.** Evidence: `ص ل و B001` meeting fire and heat; activated by the threat-word ويل and by `س ك ن B007` knife stilling an animal through death. Structure: a root used for prayer also carries fire/roasting hardship. Mechanism: the praying group is linguistically exposed to the root’s punitive heat branch. Abductive move: I infer that woe lets the fire branch resonate; the packet supplies heat/fire and woe context. Reading change: the ayah acquires a latent punitive materiality: the same root that names worship can disclose burning consequence.

4. **A snare of religious form.** Evidence: `ص ل و B004` set snare/trap; activated by `ر ء ي B005` showing off in 107:6 and `م ن ع B006` reciprocal resistance over a thing. Structure: visible prayer, ostentation, and withholding form a trap-like social mechanism. Mechanism: public worship can become a device for catching status while resisting claims of the needy. Abductive move: I supply the trap mechanism; the packet supplies snare, visibility, and resistance branches. Reading change: “for the praying ones” can read as a warning that their own religious display is the trap in which they are caught.

### Retrospective surprises

107:5-6 narrowed the warning: the woe falls on a praying identity specifically when ṣalāh is detached from attention and redirected toward visibility. 107:7 then made `ص ل و B004` snare more plausible, because visible worship and withheld maʿūn together form a trap of status.

## 107:5 — ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Heedlessness as turned-away heart.** Evidence: `س ه و B001` heedlessness of heart, including in prayer, with `ص ل و B003` prescribed worship. Structure: the preposition `عن` marks being away from their ṣalāh, not simply within it. Mechanism: the rite exists, but attention has departed from its meaning and obligations. Abductive move: I infer that “away from” spatializes the heart’s departure; the packet supplies prayer and heedlessness. Reading change: the ayah reads as alienation from prayer rather than accidental forgetfulness alone.

2. **Still prayer, still conscience.** Evidence: `س ه و B002` stillness; activated by `س ك ن B001` cessation of motion and `ح ض ض B001` urging. Structure: the actor does not urge; his prayer is “still” where it should move him and others. Mechanism: سهو becomes inertness: a calm, unmoved religious posture before hunger. Abductive move: I supply the moral reading of stillness as failure to be moved; the packet supplies stillness and urging. Reading change: ساهون can carry paralysis, not only distraction.

3. **Overlooking fault versus refusing to overlook need.** Evidence: `س ه و B003` easy dealing by overlooking another's slip; activated by harsh `د ع ع B001` toward the orphan. Structure: a branch of social gentleness is absent where the vulnerable appears. Mechanism: the person “overlooks” prayer’s claim while not gently overlooking the orphan’s weakness. Abductive move: I supply the reversal between indulgence toward self and harshness toward dependent; the packet supplies easy-dealing and rough-driving branches. Reading change: the ayah exposes a crooked distribution of leniency.

4. **The hidden star of missed obligation.** Evidence: `س ه و B005` the tiny star missed by sight; activated by `ر ء ي B001/B013` seeing/tell-me and `ر ء ي B005` showiness. Structure: seeing is thematized at 107:1 and 107:6, while this ayah names what is missed. Mechanism: showy vision amplifies what is obvious and misses the small, dim obligation. Abductive move: I infer that small duties are like the hidden star; the packet supplies visual and hidden/missed branches. Reading change: prayer-heedlessness becomes failure to perceive quiet claims such as orphan, miskīn, and maʿūn.

### Retrospective surprises

107:6 made the hidden-star branch (`س ه و B005`) more forceful than expected: the ostentatious person sees the audience and misses the small duty. 107:7 also made stillness (`س ه و B002`) social: unmoved prayer culminates in the unmoved hand that withholds.

## 107:6 — ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ يُرَآءُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Public visibility closes the opening question.** Evidence: `ر ء ي B005` showing off before people, activated by `ر ء ي B013` tell-me formula and `ر ء ي B001` seeing/insight in 107:1. Structure: the surah starts by asking the hearer to see and then identifies people who act to be seen. Mechanism: the object of diagnosis manipulates the same visual field used for diagnosis. Abductive move: I supply the recursive visual mechanism; the packet supplies repeated root and ostentation branch. Reading change: يراءون is not an extra vice but the answer to “Have you seen?”

2. **Mirror appearance replacing inner judgment.** Evidence: `ر ء ي B006` visible appearance/mirror and `ر ء ي B002` reflective judgment; activated by `س ه و B001` heart-heedlessness in 107:5. Structure: immediately after inattentive prayer, the visual surface becomes dominant. Mechanism: the mirror-like surface substitutes for inner رأي and attention. Abductive move: I infer displacement from inward judgment to outward appearance; the packet supplies mirror/appearance and heart-judgment branches. Reading change: ostentation reads as epistemic corruption: appearance governs what should have been insight.

3. **Mutual visibility as social theater.** Evidence: `ر ء ي B004` mutual visibility/facing, with `ر ء ي B005` showing off; activated by plural praying group in 107:4-5. Structure: repeated plurals create a scene of people facing and seeing each other. Mechanism: worship becomes a field of reciprocal spectatorship. Abductive move: I infer audience formation from plural morphology and mutual-visibility branch; the packet supplies both. Reading change: the ayah expands from one hypocritical display to a social theater of religious reputation.

4. **Raised repute versus divine Name.** Evidence: `ر ء ي B005` includes ostentation and reputation, activated by `س م و B008` good repute spread and `س م و B005` name as raised designation. Structure: beginning-name and near-ending display compete. Mechanism: the performer seeks a raised social name through acts that should have been under the divine Name. Abductive move: I supply the competition of reputational elevation; the packet supplies name/repute and showing-off. Reading change: يراءون becomes a counter-basmalah: action done in the name of audience.

### Retrospective surprises

107:7 retrospectively grounded riya in economics: the performer does not only seek eyes but uses that visible piety while blocking ordinary benefit. Looking back to 107:0, the surprise is that `س م و B008` good repute can be hijacked; public religious visibility becomes a counterfeit raised name.

## 107:7 — وَيَمْنَعُونَ ٱلْمَاعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Withholding small benefit completes the denial of dīn.** Evidence: `م ن ع B001` holding back the hand from giving, `م ع ن B005` maʿūn as small available benefit; activated by `د ي ن B003` debt/credit and `د ي ن B002` reckoning. Structure: the surah begins with denial of dīn and ends with refusal of available benefit. Mechanism: the final action proves the opening denial by refusing even slight dues. Abductive move: I infer that small goods are test-cases of the larger account; the packet supplies debt, reckoning, withholding, and maʿūn. Reading change: الماعون is not trivial; its smallness makes the denial sharper.

2. **Blocking the flow.** Evidence: `م ن ع B002` barrier between seeker and sought, `م ع ن B001` flowing visible water, and `م ع ن B005` including water where named; activated by `ط ع م B001/B002` food and feeding, and `س ك ن B010` provisions enabling staying. Structure: food, dwelling-provision, water/benefit form a flow of life-support. Mechanism: the actor interrupts circulation, turning flow into blockage. Abductive move: I supply the hydraulic/social-flow model; the packet supplies water-flow, feeding, provision, and barrier branches. Reading change: يمنعون الماعون becomes damming the small streams by which a community lives.

3. **Refusing household access.** Evidence: `م ع ن B005` household benefit/lent tools, `م ع ن B006` dwelling/lodging-place; activated by `س ك ن B002` dwelling and `س ك ن B003` household inhabitants. Structure: the last noun is domestic; earlier miskīn/orphan scenes show exclusion from settled household care. Mechanism: withholding maʿūn seals the house against those who need its ordinary implements. Abductive move: I infer domestic closure; the packet supplies household, dwelling, and withholding branches. Reading change: the ayah reads as refusal of neighborly infrastructure, not merely refusal of charity.

4. **Possessive protection inverted into selfish fortress.** Evidence: `م ن ع B003` protective strength/fortress, activated by `ي ت م B001` lacking protector and `ر ح م B002` kinship bond. Structure: the vulnerable need protective manʿah; the actor uses manʿ as private defense of goods. Mechanism: protection that should guard weak kin becomes fortification against them. Abductive move: I supply the inversion of protection into hoarding; the packet supplies protection, kinship, orphaning, and withholding. Reading change: يمنعون is ambivalent: the same root that could protect instead excludes.

### Retrospective surprises

The whole packet made the last ayah function as proof, not appendix. The opening `د ي ن` branches and the middle food/prayer branches converge here: debt, mercy, household, flow, and attention are all tested by whether a person lets small benefit pass.
