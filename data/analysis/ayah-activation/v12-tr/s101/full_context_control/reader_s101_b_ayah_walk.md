# S101 v12 full-context ayah walk

## 101:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. Name as a high protective heading. Evidence: س م و B005, "name as raised designation"; س م و B001/B004, rising and overhead cover; ء ل ه B002, fixed divine name in invocation; ر ح م B001, mercy and tenderness. Structural cue: the surah begins with naming before the repeated striking-name of 101:1-3. Mechanism: the named divine cover is placed above a later world of impact, scattering, and fire. Abductive move: I supply the directional arrow that a prefatory name can frame and overarch the catastrophe that follows. Reading change: the basmalah becomes not a detached formula but an elevated rubric over the entire scene of destabilization.

2. Mercy as kinship/womb before a reversed mother-image. Evidence: ر ح م B002, kinship bond; ر ح م B003, womb; ء م م B001/B002 later in 101:9, mother as bearer and mother-source. Structural cue: the packet moves from رحم at the opening to أمه هاوية near the end. Mechanism: nurturing containment is activated before its dark inversion, where "mother/source" becomes an abyss. Abductive move: I infer a reversal relation between the opening رحم-field and the later أم-field, not supplied explicitly by the grammar. Reading change: mercy is heard as the normal matrix against which the "mother as Hawiyah" becomes shocking.

3. Invocation before unknowability. Evidence: ء ل ه B002, oath/invocation formulas; د ر ي B001 in 101:3 and 101:10, making known; ق ر ع B006, a warning that strikes the hearer. Structural cue: the divine name precedes two "what makes you know" formulas. Mechanism: invocation creates the only stable address before cognition is struck and exceeded. Abductive move: I treat the opening formula as a speech-act that authorizes later disclosure. Reading change: the basmalah reads as the authorized entry-point into knowledge that ordinary inquiry cannot seize.

### Retrospective surprises

Later 101:9 made the رحم-field sharper than expected: the opening womb/kinship branches are retrospectively inverted by "his mother is Hawiyah." Later 101:11 also made the opening "name/cover" stand over heat and fire, so the basmalah functions as a high mercy-frame around the final burning answer.

## 101:1 — ٱلْقَارِعَةُ

### Activated readings

1. The blow that opens the whole mechanism. Evidence: ق ر ع B001, striking one thing against another; ق ر ع B006, warning/rebuke that checks the hearer. Structural cue: the one-word nominal opening is repeated in 101:2 and made unknowable in 101:3. Mechanism: impact first arrives as a name, then as an interrogated object, then as an epistemic shock. Abductive move: I supply the causal movement from sound-impact to cognitive arrest. Reading change: القارعة is not only an event-label; it is a blow whose first target is attention and recognition.

2. A public exposed place. Evidence: ق ر ع B010, open high surface of road/courtyard; ق ر ع B008/B011, bare or scraped surface. Structural cue: 101:4-5 display people and mountains stripped from stable places into visible dispersed matter. Mechanism: the "striking" event also creates an exposed arena where coverings and settled forms are removed. Abductive move: I infer that repeated naming turns the event into the place/plane on which later transformations appear. Reading change: القارعة can be read spatially as the exposed surface of manifestation, not only temporally as a coming disaster.

3. A selector before the scales. Evidence: ق ر ع B004, casting lots/drawing shares; و ز ن B002 in 101:6/8, balance of justice; ث ق ل B001 and خ ف ف B001, heaviness/lightness. Structural cue: the surah moves from the repeated قارعة to paired outcomes by weight. Mechanism: the striking event functions like a decisive selection process that separates shares into heavy and light cases. Abductive move: I supply the legal-apportioning analogy between lot-casting and weighing. Reading change: القارعة becomes the onset of adjudicative sorting.

### Retrospective surprises

101:6-8 surprised me by activating ق ر ع B004: the first "striking" also behaves like a selector before the scales. 101:4-5 also made ق ر ع B010/B008 visible: the blow creates an exposed, stripped field where people and mountains are displayed.

## 101:2 — مَا ٱلْقَارِعَةُ

### Activated readings

1. Question as magnifying impact. Evidence: ق ر ع B001/B006; د ر ي B001 appears in the next ayah. Structural cue: bare nominal repetition is now preceded by ما. Mechanism: the question does not request ordinary information; it makes the strike reverberate by withholding definition. Abductive move: I infer that repetition plus interrogation intensifies rather than explains. Reading change: "what is al-Qari'ah" reads as a second knock, not a neutral query.

2. The event as unassigned share. Evidence: ق ر ع B004, lots and shares; و ز ن B001/B002 later, weighing and justice. Structural cue: ما leaves the referent undetermined before the later measured division. Mechanism: unknownness marks the event before its outcomes have been distributed. Abductive move: I supply an analogy between interrogative indeterminacy and lot/scale indeterminacy before decision. Reading change: the ayah becomes the suspense before apportionment: the share has not yet fallen.

3. The open road/courtyard as question-space. Evidence: ق ر ع B010, exposed road/courtyard; ف ر ش B001/B002 in 101:4, spreading and prepared surface. Structural cue: the question is followed by human dispersal across a broad scene. Mechanism: the undefined قارعة opens a visible field into which people will be spread. Abductive move: I infer spatial expansion from the later similes. Reading change: ما القارعة reads as opening an arena rather than merely asking for a definition.

### Retrospective surprises

The later paired outcomes in 101:6-9 made the interrogative ما feel less like mere wonder and more like pre-adjudicative suspense. 101:11 also showed that the question-space is not filled by explanation but by a terse material answer.

## 101:3 — وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْقَارِعَةُ

### Activated readings

1. Knowledge struck into insufficiency. Evidence: د ر ي B001, knowing and causing another to know; ق ر ع B006, a striking warning/rebuke. Structural cue: أدراك is inserted between two ما-elements and the repeated قارعة. Mechanism: the address is made to know by being checked at the limit of knowing. Abductive move: I supply the paradox that making-known can operate by showing inadequacy. Reading change: the ayah becomes an epistemic blow: knowledge begins where mastery fails.

2. Aiming at a hidden event. Evidence: د ر ي B002, aiming at or setting upon something; د ر ي B003, stealth/concealment in hunting; ق ر ع B010, open place; ن و ر B005 later, beacon/landmark. Structural cue: the addressed "you" is oriented toward an event that remains undefined until 101:11. Mechanism: the question points the mind like a hunter toward a concealed target, with later fire as the revealed marker. Abductive move: I infer that أدراك can activate directed pursuit, not only cognition. Reading change: the ayah reads as guided orientation toward a hidden endpoint.

3. Sharpness against the head-core. Evidence: د ر ي B004, sharp horn/comb-like implement; ق ر ع B001, striking; ء م م B003 later, head-core and wound reaching it. Structural cue: striking terminology surrounds a knowledge-verb whose branch includes pointed instruments, later answered by an "umm/head-core" field. Mechanism: knowing is figured as an impact or penetration into the guarded center. Abductive move: I supply the bodily analogy linking mental knowing to head-core contact. Reading change: أدراك becomes not soft explanation but pointed, forceful disclosure.

### Retrospective surprises

101:10 unexpectedly repeated the أدراك formula, making 101:3 the first of two epistemic shocks rather than a single preface. 101:9 activated the sharper bodily reading through ء م م B003 and ه و ي B007, so knowledge and striking both press toward a hidden core.

## 101:4 — يَوْمَ يَكُونُ ٱلنَّاسُ كَٱلْفَرَاشِ ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ

### Activated readings

1. Event-day as dispersed movement. Evidence: ي و م B003, severe event-day; ك و ن B001, occurrence in time; ن و س B001, swaying/dangling oscillation; ف ر ش B005, fluttering moth-like insect; ب ث ث B001, scattering. Structural cue: the first explanatory image after the triple قارعة is kinetic and plural. Mechanism: the strike converts "people" into unstable suspended motion and then into broadcast scattering. Abductive move: I infer that the event-day causes the bodily-social form of الناس to lose coordinated direction. Reading change: the ayah reads less as a static simile and more as a physical mechanism of disorientation.

2. Exposure on a laid-out surface. Evidence: ف ر ش B001/B002, spreading, bedding, prepared surface; ق ر ع B010 earlier, open road/courtyard; ب ث ث B001, laying/scattering out. Structural cue: قارعة as exposed place is followed by فرش/مبثوث. Mechanism: the people are not merely flying; they are displayed across the open plane like matter laid out after impact. Abductive move: I supply the spatial arrow from the named event-place to the human distribution on it. Reading change: الناس become evidence spread across a surface, not simply a frightened crowd.

3. Hidden inner distress made visible. Evidence: ب ث ث B002, disclosing what is hidden in the self; د ر ي B001 in 101:3, making known; و ز ن B002 later, accounting of deeds. Structural cue: the sequence moves from unknowability to visible human dispersal and then to balances. Mechanism: scattering also discloses what had been interior or unreckoned. Abductive move: I infer that external dispersion reveals inner/accountable state. Reading change: المبثوث can carry disclosure, so the ayah reads as the surfacing of concealed moral condition.

4. Herd without handler. Evidence: ن و س B002, driving camels; ن ف ش B003 later, night-roaming livestock without shepherd; ف ر ش B004, small/low livestock. Structural cue: human plurality is analogized with light creatures, then mountains with animal/textile wool. Mechanism: society loses guidance and becomes animal-like motion without a driver. Abductive move: I supply the absence of a controlling shepherd/driver from the uncontrolled spread. Reading change: الناس are read socially as a herd whose normal direction has collapsed.

### Retrospective surprises

101:11 made the moth branch of ف ر ش B005 more dangerous: the swarm is implicitly oriented toward fire. 101:6-8 also turned ب ث ث B002 into moral disclosure; the visible scattering anticipates the later weighing of what had been hidden.

## 101:5 — وَتَكُونُ ٱلْجِبَالُ كَٱلْعِهْنِ ٱلْمَنفُوشِ

### Activated readings

1. Hard mass converted into loose fiber. Evidence: ج ب ل B001, high solid mass; ع ه ن B003, dyed soft wool; ن ف ش B001, teased/fluffed wool; ك و ن B001, occurrence/predication. Structural cue: this ayah parallels 101:4 with تكون + subject + كـ-simile. Mechanism: the same event that scatters people also turns the most solid landmarks into separated fibers. Abductive move: I infer a shared destabilizing force from the syntactic parallel. Reading change: mountains are read not as scenery but as the proof that weight and solidity have been unmade.

2. Formed nature unraveling. Evidence: ج ب ل B004, innate formed nature; ج ب ل B007, firm woven make of cloth; ن ف ش B001, fluffing by beating/spreading; ع ه ن B002, limp bending without separation. Structural cue: a root of formed constitution stands next to textile branches. Mechanism: what was "jiblah" or firm make is not annihilated instantly but loosened into slack, teased structure. Abductive move: I infer ontological unweaving from the wool image. Reading change: the ayah reads as de-constitution: created firmness becomes unstructured softness.

3. Abundance loses rank. Evidence: ج ب ل B002, mountain-like multitude/wealth; ج ب ل B012, chiefs like mountains; ع ه ن B001, ready accessible property; ث ق ل B005 later, precious weight/high worth. Structural cue: mountains precede the weighing of human worth in 101:6-8. Mechanism: large visible magnitude becomes valueless fluff, so only measured moral weight remains meaningful. Abductive move: I supply the contrast between apparent greatness and adjudicated worth. Reading change: الجبال can activate social rank and wealth, both reduced before the scales.

4. Unmanaged night-spread. Evidence: ن ف ش B003, livestock spreading at night without a shepherd; ف ر ش/ب ثث in 101:4, scattered moth-people; ي و م B003, severe occasion. Structural cue: the people-scattering image and wool-fluffing image share dispersion. Mechanism: the world becomes an unguarded field where both human and mountain bodies spread without keeper or boundary. Abductive move: I infer loss of cosmic management from parallel uncontrolled dispersion. Reading change: المنفوش carries more than texture; it suggests unsupervised release.

### Retrospective surprises

101:6 surprised me by making the mountain scene a negative prelude to moral weight: physical mass is undone before real weight is measured. 101:8-11 also made ن ف ش B003 feel relevant, because lightness and fall continue the uncontrolled-release pattern.

## 101:6 — فَأَمَّا مَن ثَقُلَتْ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ

### Activated readings

1. Moral weight after cosmic de-weighting. Evidence: ث ق ل B001, heaviness against lightness; و ز ن B002, balance of justice and accounting; ج ب ل B001 with ن ف ش B001 in 101:5, solid mountains fluffed apart. Structural cue: the فَأَمَّا division follows the dissolution of mountains. Mechanism: once physical heaviness is destabilized, the scale introduces another register of weight that still counts. Abductive move: I infer contrastive sequencing: cosmic mass loses force so accountable weight can appear. Reading change: heavy scales read as moral gravity, not simply quantity.

2. Preciousness preserved. Evidence: ث ق ل B005, precious protected weight/high worth; و ز ن B007, standing or value that carries weight; ر ح م B001 at 101:0, mercy/beneficence; ع ي ش B001 in 101:7, good lived condition. Structural cue: heavy scales immediately lead to a satisfying life. Mechanism: the scale recognizes worth and protects it into the next state. Abductive move: I supply the protective consequence between measured value and pleasant living. Reading change: ثقلت means the person carries precious significance, not only a heavy balance-pan.

3. Calibration and justice. Evidence: و ز ن B001, measuring by weight or estimation; و ز ن B002, equity/justice/accounting; ث ق ل B004, standard weight and known measure. Structural cue: موازين is plural and paired later with خفت موازينه. Mechanism: multiple measures cross-check a person, converting dispersed life into determinate judgment. Abductive move: I infer a forensic accounting process from the plural scales. Reading change: the ayah reads as measured adjudication, not a single metaphor of heaviness.

4. Steadiness against swarm-instability. Evidence: و ز ن B005, steady judgment; ث ق ل B006, slowness/dragging heaviness; ن و س B001 and ف ر ش B005 in 101:4, swaying and fluttering lightness. Structural cue: the syntax moves from chaotic motion to conditional stability. Mechanism: weight arrests the earlier oscillation; it gives a person enough steadiness to resist the swarm-state. Abductive move: I infer that weight functions dynamically as stabilization. Reading change: "heavy scales" becomes the recovery of settledness after disorientation.

### Retrospective surprises

101:7 confirmed that heavy weight is protective and preservative, not merely quantitative. 101:8 sharpened the contrast, while 101:11 made the alternative containment fiery, increasing the sense that the scales decide which enclosure receives the person.

## 101:7 — فَهُوَ فِى عِيشَةٍۢ رَّاضِيَةٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. Life as condition matched to weight. Evidence: ع ي ش B001, life and lived condition; ر ض و B001, acceptance opposed to displeasure; و ز ن B002/B007 in 101:6, just balance and valued standing. Structural cue: فَهُوَ marks direct consequence after ثقلت موازينه. Mechanism: adjudicated worth produces an inhabited condition that itself is accepting/accepted. Abductive move: I infer that the life is not merely pleasant but congruent with the scale's judgment. Reading change: عيشة راضية reads as an environment aligned with justice.

2. Livelihood after stripped provisions. Evidence: ع ي ش B002, means of living/livelihood; ع ه ن B001 in 101:5, ready accessible property/food/drink; ف ر ش B002, prepared resting place. Structural cue: after worldly supports are scattered/fluffed, this ayah supplies a new "in" space. Mechanism: the rewarded person receives not only survival but a complete provision-place. Abductive move: I supply replacement: collapsed earthly provisioning is answered by a different livelihood. Reading change: عيشة is read materially as sustaining habitation, not only abstract life.

3. Mutual consent/restored relation. Evidence: ر ض و B003, reciprocal satisfaction; ر ض و B004, making another satisfied; ر ح م B002 at 101:0, kinship bond. Structural cue: the singular "he" enters a feminized عيشة راضية after the communal chaos of الناس. Mechanism: isolated judgment is reabsorbed into a relation of acceptance and care. Abductive move: I infer social/affective repair from رضى branches and the opening kinship field. Reading change: the ayah reads as reconciled belonging rather than solitary reward.

4. Obedient/guaranteeing life. Evidence: ر ض و B006, raḍiyy as obedient, loving, or guaranteeing; ك و ن B003, suretyship/responsibility; ث ق ل B005, precious protected worth. Structural cue: the life follows a successful scale result. Mechanism: the new life acts almost as guarantor for the one whose weight was accepted. Abductive move: I supply a juridical protection relation from "guaranteeing" to the rewarded state. Reading change: راضية can be heard as a life that receives, loves, and secures its inhabitant.

### Retrospective surprises

101:9 retrospectively made the relational language of عيشة راضية stronger: the bad outcome also has a feminine receiving-place, but as mother/abyss. 101:11 clarified that the good "in a life" contrasts with being enclosed in a heated zone.

## 101:8 — وَأَمَّا مَنْ خَفَّتْ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ

### Activated readings

1. Lightness as failed counterweight. Evidence: خ ف ف B001, lightness against heaviness; و ز ن B002, justice/accounting; ث ق ل B001 in 101:6, opposing heaviness. Structural cue: وأما creates the formal counterpart to 101:6. Mechanism: the same measuring apparatus that preserves worth now exposes lack of weight. Abductive move: I infer that lightness is privative: it is not freedom but insufficiency before justice. Reading change: خفت reads as devaluation rather than harmless lightness.

2. Agitated motion resumes. Evidence: خ ف ف B004, levity, foolishness, agitation; ن و س B001 and ف ر ش B005 in 101:4, swaying and fluttering; ه و ي B008 in 101:9, fast going/thrown travel. Structural cue: light scales are followed immediately by Hawiyah. Mechanism: moral lightness lets the person rejoin the earlier unstable motion, now as downward rush. Abductive move: I supply the kinetic consequence from underweight to falling. Reading change: the ayah becomes the pivot from weighing to plunge.

3. Contemptuous insignificance. Evidence: خ ف ف B005, contempt/disparagement and treating a right as insignificant; و ز ن B007, standing/value with weight; ء م م B013 later, slight/insignificant thing. Structural cue: the light case is answered by a terrifying "mother" rather than honorable life. Mechanism: what treated rights as light is itself found weightless and redirected to an abased origin. Abductive move: I infer a retributive mirroring between making things insignificant and becoming insignificant. Reading change: خفت موازينه can read ethically as the exposure of contempt.

4. Rapid departure from camp. Evidence: خ ف ف B002, swift movement/departure; ق ر ع B010, road/courtyard; ء م م B012 in 101:9, aiming/heading toward; ه و ي B002, falling into abyss. Structural cue: the verse's condition is immediately followed by destination imagery. Mechanism: the light one is not settled in a pleasing life but is hurried out toward the falling-place. Abductive move: I supply travel-direction from the following "mother/Hawiyah" answer. Reading change: the ayah reads as departure under judgment, not just a balance report.

### Retrospective surprises

101:9 made خفة kinetic and directional: the light scales do not merely fail; they send the person toward Hawiyah. 101:11 then made the lightness tragically moth-like, since the final destination is fire.

## 101:9 — فَأُمُّهُۥ هَاوِيَةٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. Mother-source reversed into abyss. Evidence: ء م م B001, mother as bearer/nurturer; ء م م B002, origin/gathering point/reference; ه و ي B002, falling into an abyss. Structural cue: the light scales of 101:8 are followed by فَأُمُّهُ. Mechanism: the place one returns to as source becomes the place into which one falls. Abductive move: I supply reversal: origin and refuge are inverted into destination and destruction. Reading change: "his mother" reads as a horrifying homecoming, not only a euphemism for outcome.

2. Void as parent. Evidence: ه و ي B001, air/void/hollowness; ء م م B002, gathering point; ق ر ع B008/B011 earlier, stripped bare surface; خ ف ف B001, lightness. Structural cue: weightlessness leads to Hawiyah, and the surah has stripped people and mountains of stable mass. Mechanism: the underweighted person belongs to hollow space; emptiness becomes the gathering center. Abductive move: I infer ontological affinity between lightness, strippedness, and void. Reading change: هاوية is not only a pit but the hollow parent of a hollowed self.

3. The wound-mouth of the struck head. Evidence: ء م م B003, head-core and wound reaching it; ه و ي B007, gaping wound or hollowed body; ق ر ع B001, striking. Structural cue: the surah begins with striking and later says "mother/head-core" near a gaping Hawiyah. Mechanism: the Qarīʿah's blow opens the protected core into a mouth-like abyss. Abductive move: I supply a bodily compression of event, head, wound, and pit. Reading change: أمه هاوية can be read somatically: the inner core opened by judgment.

4. Mis-aimed desire becomes destination. Evidence: ء م م B012, aiming/heading toward; ه و ي B004, desire/inclination; د ر ي B002 in 101:3/10, aiming at something; خ ف ف B004, being unsettled or misled. Structural cue: the interrogative guidance of 101:3 and 101:10 surrounds this outcome. Mechanism: what the self inclines toward becomes the path it is aimed down. Abductive move: I infer moral trajectory from desire-branch to destination-branch. Reading change: Hawiyah reads as both wanted direction and imposed fall.

### Retrospective surprises

101:11 was decisive: Hawiyah is not left as a bare abyss but becomes fire, heat, enclosure, and black depth. That later gloss made the mother-source reversal more severe and also activated ه و ي B001/B007 as hollow opening rather than only falling.

## 101:10 — وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا هِيَهْ

### Activated readings

1. Second epistemic blow, now reduced to "it." Evidence: د ر ي B001, knowing/causing to know; ق ر ع B006 from 101:1-3, striking warning; ه و ي B002 in 101:9, Hawiyah as abyss. Structural cue: the formula of 101:3 returns, but القارعة is replaced by the pronoun هيه. Mechanism: the first unknown event narrows into a second unknown referent whose identity is still withheld. Abductive move: I infer intensification by pronominal compression. Reading change: the ayah makes Hawiyah more uncanny: nearer in grammar, still beyond grasp.

2. Directed approach to the hidden target. Evidence: د ر ي B002, aiming/setting upon; د ر ي B003, stalking concealed game; ن و ر B005 in 101:11, beacon/visible marker. Structural cue: "what makes you know" immediately precedes the final disclosure, نار حامية. Mechanism: the question aims the hearer at the concealed object, and the next ayah flashes it into view. Abductive move: I supply the hunting/targeting model for the delayed reveal. Reading change: أدراك becomes guided approach to the fire, not merely intellectual notification.

3. Cutting away false speech. Evidence: د ر ي B004, sharp horn/comb implement; ه و ي B010, false and idle speech; ع ه ن B006 in 101:5, speech cast without reflection. Structural cue: the surah's repeated questions forbid easy verbal filling. Mechanism: the sharp knowing-instrument strips away careless speech until only the final terse answer remains. Abductive move: I infer a corrective function from the contrast between speculative speech branches and the compressed answer. Reading change: the ayah reads as discipline of language before eschatic reality.

### Retrospective surprises

101:11 immediately answered the pronoun with fire, confirming that the second أدراك formula delays disclosure only for one beat. No further ayah follows, so the surprise is local: the hidden "it" becomes an elemental rather than discursive answer.

## 101:11 — نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌۢ

### Activated readings

1. Fire as final illumination and burning. Evidence: ن و ر B002, burning fire; ن و ر B001, light/illumination; ح م ي B001, heat and heating. Structural cue: the last ayah finally answers the repeated ما. Mechanism: the hidden referent is disclosed by the same thing that burns; illumination and punishment coincide. Abductive move: I infer that final knowledge is experiential because the answer is heat-light, not definition. Reading change: نار حامية reads as the event's revelation, not only its penalty.

2. Protected zone that excludes escape. Evidence: ح م ي B002, protection/prevention and a protected preserve; ه و ي B002, abyss; ق ر ع B010, open road/courtyard; خ ف ف B002, rapid departure. Structural cue: the light one is sent to a "mother" and then identified as hot fire. Mechanism: Hawiyah is a bounded preserve in reverse: a zone one is kept in, not kept from. Abductive move: I supply the enclosure relation from حمى to the abyss-fire. Reading change: حامية means not merely hot but inescapably guarded.

3. Heated anger and hostile flare. Evidence: ح م ي B003, zeal/pride/anger; ن و ر B007, enmity flaring between people; ق ر ع B002, mutual combat. Structural cue: the surah begins with a striking event and ends with heated fire. Mechanism: conflict-heat is externalized as the final fiery state. Abductive move: I infer affective transmutation: social/moral contention becomes environmental heat. Reading change: the fire reads as the world of heated opposition made objective.

4. Black pit and well-lining. Evidence: ح م ي B006, black foul mud; ح م ي B011, well-lining stones; ح م ي B012, blackening of night/cloud; ه و ي B001/B002, void and abyss. Structural cue: "Hawiyah" as pit is immediately glossed by "hot fire." Mechanism: the final place combines depth, blackness, enclosure, and heat. Abductive move: I infer a pit-well image from the adjacency of Hawiyah and حامية branches. Reading change: نار حامية is not only flame upward; it is a dark contained depth that burns.

5. Poisoning heat. Evidence: ح م ي B007, stinging poison and burning force; ف ر ش B005, insects drawn to fire; ب ث ث B001, scattered creatures. Structural cue: moth-like people appear before the final fire. Mechanism: what drew the swarm becomes a lethal burning force, like poison entering the body. Abductive move: I supply attraction-to-destruction from the moth/fire ecology. Reading change: حامية carries invasive harm, not only ambient temperature.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah follows. The retrospective movement here runs backward: the final fire reactivates 101:4's moths, 101:9's abyss, and 101:1-3's striking-warning as parts of one heat-light disclosure.
