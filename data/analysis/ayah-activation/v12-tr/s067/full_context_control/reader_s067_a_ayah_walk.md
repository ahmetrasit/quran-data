# Surah 67 Full-Context Control Reader: Ayah Walk

## 67:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Designation as an elevated entry.** `س م و B005` makes *ism* a designation with raised mention, while `ر ح م B001` supplies mercy and beneficence. The designation precedes doubled mercy forms, assigning the name the role of entry into that quality. The packet supplies name + repeated mercy; I infer that naming initiates the discourse under mercy. The formula thus does more than identify: it frames the ensuing speech.
2. **Mercy as prior control on sovereignty and threat.** The two `ر ح م B001` forms intensify one field of compassionate beneficence. Their position before the surah, and the later recurrence of *al-Rahman*, makes mercy a persistent frame rather than a late exception. The packet supplies recurrence; I infer continuity through the severe scenes. Judgment remains, but it is read inside an already stated mercy-frame.

### Retrospective surprises

67:19 surprisingly makes the opening mercy materially visible as continuous holding, and 67:29 names the same al-Rahman as the object of faith and reliance. These later uses confirm that the doubled mercy here frames both cosmic support and human trust, not only an opening formula.

## 67:1 — تَبَٰرَكَ ٱلَّذِى بِيَدِهِ ٱلْمُلْكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ قَدِيرٌ

### Activated readings

1. **Sovereignty as held, effective control.** `ي د ي B004` supplies possession/control, `م ل ك B003` dominion, and `ق د ر B003` effective mastery. Their adjacency assigns custody, scope, and efficacy. The packet supplies the sequence; I infer that dominion is operational, not merely titular. It extends to every knowable entity (`ش ي ء B001`, “knowable thing” variant).
2. **Rule as fixed, proliferating good.** `ب ر ك B004` is fixed and growing good; `م ل ك B003` is sovereignty. Initial `تَبَٰرَكَ` governs what follows. The packet gives this order; I infer that power's effects are presented as productive good, not only coercion.
3. **Power through exact apportionment.** `ق د ر B001` is measure reaching a thing's extent alongside `B003` capacity. Later proportioned heavens and testing activate the measuring branch. The packet supplies universal scope plus ordered creation; I infer that mastery assigns each thing its fitting limit. `قَدِير` retains both ability and measure.

### Retrospective surprises

67:19–21 retrospectively concretize “hand-held dominion”: birds are held, provision may be withheld, and no substitute force can compensate. 67:30 narrows “every thing” to the immediate case of water, making universal capacity experientially specific.

## 67:2 — ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلْمَوْتَ وَٱلْحَيَوٰةَ لِيَبْلُوَكُمْ أَيُّكُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًۭا ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْغَفُورُ

### Activated readings

1. **Death and life as a disclosure apparatus.** `خ ل ق B001/B002` gives proportioning and origination; `ب ل و B002` testing that discloses a state; `ع م ل B001` intentional doing. The purpose clause makes the created pair serve disclosure through action. The packet supplies creation → testing → action; I infer that mortality and vitality expose hidden dispositions. “Test” becomes a designed disclosure mechanism.
2. **“Better” as quality and skill.** `ح س ن B001` is goodness/beauty and `B002` beneficent or skillful action. The superlative modifies singular `عَمَلًا`. The packet supplies that grammar; I infer a criterion of apt, beneficial performance rather than quantity alone.
3. **Might and forgiveness bound the exposure.** `ع ز ز B001/B002` gives invulnerable might and overcoming; `غ ف ر B002` gives pardon shielding from consequence, backed by protective covering (`B001`). After disclosed deeds, might makes exposure inescapable and forgiveness protects the exposed offender. The packet supplies the pair; I infer complementary outcomes, not cancellation.

### Retrospective surprises

67:23 identifies hearing, sight, and hearts as created instruments through which the test discloses response, while 67:27 shows disclosure becoming visible on faces. 67:28's unresolved destruction-or-mercy alternative also preserves the coexistence of might and forgiveness rather than resolving it.

## 67:3 — ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمَٰوَٰتٍۢ طِبَاقًۭا ۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِى خَلْقِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ مِن تَفَٰوُتٍۢ ۖ فَٱرْجِعِ ٱلْبَصَرَ هَلْ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Layers as matched coordination.** `ط ب ق B002` supplies superposed layers and `B003` matching; `ف و ت B002` is divergence or defect between things. Negated disparity follows “layers.” The packet supplies this structure; I infer coordinated strata, not merely stacked objects.
2. **Inspection as repeatable stress test.** `ر ء ي B001` is sensory or insightful seeing, `ر ج ع B001` return, `ب ص ر B001` visual faculty, and `ف ط ر B001` fissure. The imperative returns sight to a specific defect-question. The packet supplies command and target; I infer permission to stress-test the claim. Sight is not asked merely to admire.
3. **Mercy as non-failing construction.** `ر ح م B001` supplies beneficent care, while `خ ل ق B001/B003` supplies proportion and well-formedness. “Creation of al-Rahman” links mercy to the inspected artifact. The packet supplies the attribution; I infer that coherence is a material effect of care.

### Retrospective surprises

67:19 corrects the purpose of looking: sight that first searches for a fissure is later directed to the support mechanism visible in birds. 67:30 then ends with the water-eye (`ع ي ن B006`), making inspection culminate in recognition of an ordinary sustaining source.

## 67:4 — ثُمَّ ٱرْجِعِ ٱلْبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيْنِ يَنقَلِبْ إِلَيْكَ ٱلْبَصَرُ خَاسِئًۭا وَهُوَ حَسِيرٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Scrutiny reverses onto the scrutinizer.** `ك ر ر B001` is repetition after a turn, `ق ل ب B004` reversal, and `ر ج ع B001` return. Sight is sent outward, then turns back. The packet supplies that grammatical reversal; I infer that failed detection shifts attention from creation's alleged defect to sight's limit.
2. **Sight is repelled and depleted.** `خ س ء B002` gives recoiling, dim, humiliated sight; `ح س ر B003` gives exhaustion from continued looking. Their sequence assigns failed reach and spent capacity. The packet supplies both; I infer cumulative failure without construing the ayah as a ban on inquiry.
3. **Repeated looking uncovers the observer.** `ح س ر B001` can mean uncovering, alongside direct exhaustion (`B003`). No flaw is uncovered in the sky, but finite vision is exposed. The packet supplies repetition and return; I infer this secondary uncovering as exploratory rather than replacing the direct branch.

### Retrospective surprises

67:19 and 67:23 unexpectedly rehabilitate sight as insight and as a created gift. Their later use confirms that exhaustion here diagnoses the limits of unaided defect-search, not the worthlessness of repeated observation itself.

## 67:5 — وَلَقَدْ زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنْيَا بِمَصَٰبِيحَ وَجَعَلْنَٰهَا رُجُومًۭا لِّلشَّيَٰطِينِ ۖ وَأَعْتَدْنَا لَهُمْ عَذَابَ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

### Activated readings

1. **One luminous arrangement bears beauty and defense.** `ز ي ن B002` is making beauty show, `ص ب ح B005` lamp/light, `ج ع ل B002` assignment into a state, and `ر ج م B003` expulsion/banishment within the projectile wording. The pronoun returns the lamps to a second role. The packet supplies identity across roles; I infer a guarded architecture in which adornment is functional.
2. **The nearer sky is the visible defensive face.** `د ن و B002` is the nearer/lower side and `س م و B004` the overhead cover. After the visual test, proximity identifies this sky as an accessible interface. The packet supplies near-ness and visible lights; I infer the first protective face of the layered system.
3. **Prepared fire extends exclusion into consequence.** `ع ت د B001` is prepared/present; `س ع ر B001/B004` is blaze and burning affliction. Made projectiles and prepared punishment form two stages. The packet supplies both; I infer immediate repulsion followed by reserved consequence.

### Retrospective surprises

67:7–8 turn the prepared blaze into a breathing, boiling, near-rending system, sharpening the second stage from reserved penalty into active reception. 67:19's merciful aerial holding also reveals a contrast: the same overhead order that excludes destructive intrusion sustains vulnerable flight.

## 67:6 — وَلِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ بِرَبِّهِمْ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ ۖ وَبِئْسَ ٱلْمَصِيرُ

### Activated readings

1. **Denial covers a nurturing relation.** `ك ف ر B003` is rejection of truth and `B004` failure to acknowledge favor; `ر ب ب B002` is nurture and staged completion. Attachment to “their Lord” places denial against ongoing care. The packet supplies this relation; I infer that the offense is at once unbelief and ingratitude.
2. **Destination as realized endpoint.** `ص ي ر B001` is becoming into an endpoint and `B007` a settling-place; `ب ء س B004` is fixed blame. The packet supplies trajectory and terminus; I infer that denial matures into settlement. The verdict evaluates both the end and its producing course.

### Retrospective surprises

67:15 and 67:21 make the denied lordship concrete as prepared earth and continuously supplied provision. 67:27 then realizes `ص ي ر B001` as an endpoint that moves from disputed report to near visual encounter.

## 67:7 — إِذَآ أُلْقُوا۟ فِيهَا سَمِعُوا۟ لَهَا شَهِيقًۭا وَهِىَ تَفُورُ

### Activated readings

1. **Refusers of warning become compelled hearers.** `ل ق ي B005` is casting, `س م ع B001` auditory perception, and `ش ه ق B002` inhaled breath/high cry. Passive casting removes agency; hearing becomes unavoidable. The packet supplies this sequence; I infer reversal from voluntary reception to forced audition.
2. **The enclosure behaves as a pressurized body.** `ف و ر B001` is boiling or surging; `ش ه ق B002/B003` gives inhalation and anger with deep sound. The packet supplies bodily-material predicates; I infer pressure cycling through intake and eruption. The fire is active, not static.
3. **Arrival triggers a first violent impulse.** `ف و ر B002` is the immediate first impulse before settling. Conditional `إِذَا` links casting to surge. The packet gives that link; I infer event-triggered activation with each arrival.

### Retrospective surprises

67:10 reveals the deepest reversal: those compelled to hear the fire identify their earlier failure as not truly hearing. 67:23 later names hearing as a bestowed faculty, so forced audition also exposes misuse of a gift.

## 67:8 — تَكَادُ تَمَيَّزُ مِنَ ٱلْغَيْظِ ۖ كُلَّمَآ أُلْقِىَ فِيهَا فَوْجٌۭ سَأَلَهُمْ خَزَنَتُهَآ أَلَمْ يَأْتِكُمْ نَذِيرٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Rage strains containment toward rupture.** `م ي ز B002` is coming apart from rage; `غ ي ظ B001` is pent-up anger. `ك و د B002` keeps rupture near but unrealized. The packet supplies pressure → near-separation; I infer violently strained yet controlled containment.
2. **Successive cohorts undergo custodial audit.** `ف و ج B001` is a successive human batch, `خ ز ن B003` keeper, `س ء ل B001` asking, and `ن ذ ر B001` cautioning warning. `كُلَّمَا` repeats the process. The packet supplies batch → keeper-question; I infer administered intake and accountability.
3. **The question tests arrival.** `ء ت ي B001` is coming/arriving and `ن ذ ر B001` the warning. The packet frames the issue as whether a warner entered their field; I infer responsibility tied to an encountered summons.

### Retrospective surprises

67:9 immediately confirms that the warner did arrive, validating the keepers' audit rather than leaving the question open. 67:26 later separates clear warning from hidden timing, correcting any assumption that a valid warning must disclose the event's date.

## 67:9 — قَالُوا۟ بَلَىٰ قَدْ جَآءَنَا نَذِيرٌۭ فَكَذَّبْنَا وَقُلْنَا مَا نَزَّلَ ٱللَّهُ مِن شَىْءٍ إِنْ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا فِى ضَلَٰلٍۢ كَبِيرٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Their answer reverses their former negation.** `ب ل و B006` is *balā*, reversal of a negative question; `ج ي ء B001` arrival; `ك ذ ب B002` declaring false. “Yes” confirms arrival before recounting rejection. The packet supplies this self-correction; I infer confession reconstructing the decision point.
2. **Denial expands from messenger to any sent thing.** `ن ز ل B002` is sending down scripture or command; `ش ي ء B001` here uses the “knowable thing” variant. Indefiniteness under negation totalizes the claim. The packet supplies warning → falsification → “nothing”; I infer escalation to denial of divine communication as a category.
3. **The accusation of great error rebounds.** `ض ل ل B001` is deviation from aim and `ك ب ر B001/B010` makes it great and weighty. Their quoted judgment locates warners in error, while the outcome locates the speakers on the failed path. The packet supplies quote and consequence; I infer dramatic reversal, not lexical reassignment.

### Retrospective surprises

67:10 turns the quoted charge of error into explicit self-diagnosis, and 67:29 reuses `ض ل ل B001` for the path whose outcome will become manifest. The later echo confirms that “great error” was a reversed judgment by the speakers.

## 67:10 — وَقَالُوا۟ لَوْ كُنَّا نَسْمَعُ أَوْ نَعْقِلُ مَا كُنَّا فِىٓ أَصْحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

### Activated readings

1. **Hearing failed as compliance, not acoustics.** `س م ع B003` is understanding, accepting, and obeying, while `B001` remains audition. Since a warner arrived, mere sound was not missing. The packet supplies arrival + regret; I infer receptive compliance in `نَسْمَعُ`.
2. **Reason should have restrained momentum.** `ع ق ل B001` is understanding as restraint, backed by the tether image in `B002`. Joined to hearing, it is a second arresting faculty. The packet supplies the pair; I infer that testimony or disciplined inference could have checked the course.
3. **Companionship is acquired location.** `ص ح ب B001` is sustained association and `س ع ر B001/B004` blaze and affliction. Counterfactual faculties lead to negated membership. The packet supplies this relation; I infer that repeated refusal forms settled affiliation.

### Retrospective surprises

67:22 gives the failure of restraint a body: a walker can keep moving while overturned and misoriented. 67:23 then discloses that hearing and inward judgment were created gifts, making the regret simultaneously practical failure and ingratitude.

## 67:11 — فَٱعْتَرَفُوا۟ بِذَنۢبِهِمْ فَسُحْقًۭا لِّأَصْحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ

### Activated readings

1. **Recognition becomes confession after prevention has expired.** `ع ر ف B003` is recognition by a mark and `B009` confession; `ذ ن ب B001` is culpable offense with bad consequence. The result particle links failed faculties to acknowledgment. The packet supplies regret → confession; I infer that they now identify the deed-outcome trace.
2. **Sin carries its trailing consequence.** Besides culpability, `ذ ن ب B002` is a tail or rear end. Punishment follows the confessed `ذنب`. The packet supplies offense and endpoint; I infer a lexical-material analogy: the deed's tail catches its doer. This supplements, not replaces, `B001`.
3. **Distance is verdict and expulsion.** `س ح ق B002` is remoteness/imprecation and `B008` forceful driving away. After confession it marks exclusion, not ignorance. The packet supplies the verdict; I infer that recognition after completed arrival does not reverse the trajectory.

### Retrospective surprises

67:27 shows why the timing of recognition matters: seeing the promised event near produces visible distress but no reopened decision. This later manifestation sharpens confession here as accurate recognition arriving after its action-guiding function has ended.

## 67:12 — إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخْشَوْنَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلْغَيْبِ لَهُم مَّغْفِرَةٌۭ وَأَجْرٌۭ كَبِيرٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Awe operates without compulsory visibility.** `خ ش ي B001` is reverent awe and `غ ي ب B001` absence from sight or knowledge. The phrase qualifies awe's unseen condition. The packet supplies both; I infer restraint before forced seeing in 67:27. This contrasts trustful response with belated manifestation.
2. **Forgiveness covers while reward adds.** `غ ف ر B002` is pardon shielding consequence, backed by protective covering (`B001`); `ء ج ر B001` is recompense for work. Coordination assigns removal of harm and positive return. The packet supplies both; I infer acquittal plus surplus.
3. **“Great” is reoriented from error to reward.** `ك ب ر B001/B003` supplies greatness and treating something as weighty. The same root qualified “error” in 67:9. The packet supplies the echo; I infer correction of the earlier value scale.

### Retrospective surprises

67:27 supplies the surprising inverse of `بِٱلْغَيْبِ`: the deniers finally see the event near and their faces are harmed. 67:29 positively develops unseen awe into trusting assent and reliance on al-Rahman.

## 67:13 — وَأَسِرُّوا۟ قَوْلَكُمْ أَوِ ٱجْهَرُوا۟ بِهِۦٓ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمٌۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ

### Retrospective surprises

67:23's created hearts identify the inward site whose contents are known, while 67:27 makes concealed denial externally legible on faces. The later progression from inner faculty to visible countenance confirms that secrecy delays manifestation but does not prevent it.

## 67:14 — أَلَا يَعْلَمُ مَنْ خَلَقَ وَهُوَ ٱللَّطِيفُ ٱلْخَبِيرُ

### Activated readings

1. **Formation grounds intimate knowledge.** `خ ل ق B001/B002` is proportioning/originating and `ع ل م B001` apprehension. The rhetorical question links maker and knowing. The packet supplies this relation; I infer that the one who proportioned an interior is not blocked by its concealment.
2. **Subtle access matches subtle objects.** `ل ط ف B002` is minute hidden fineness; `خ ب ر B001` tested inner knowledge beyond appearance. The pair supplies mode and depth. I infer that imperceptible thoughts are known inwardly rather than through crude exposure.
3. **Gentle care coexists with exact knowledge.** `ل ط ف B001` is gentle kindness and `خ ب ر B001` inner acquaintance. The packet places these after a warning about hidden speech; I infer that knowledge can tactfully aid, not only prosecute. Both readings of `لطيف` remain active.

### Retrospective surprises

67:19 makes subtle knowledge practical: al-Rahman holds alternating wings without losing any bird, and universal `بَصِير` joins awareness to care. 67:30 similarly makes hidden hydrological access the object of an exact, beneficent knowledge able to bring water forth.

## 67:15 — هُوَ ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلْأَرْضَ ذَلُولًۭا فَٱمْشُوا۟ فِى مَنَاكِبِهَا وَكُلُوا۟ مِن رِّزْقِهِۦ ۖ وَإِلَيْهِ ٱلنُّشُورُ

### Activated readings

1. **The earth is tractable, not humiliated.** `ذ ل ل B003` is pliant ease after roughness and includes accessible path; `ج ع ل B002` makes into a state. Walking and eating select this branch over abasement (`B001`). The packet supplies made-easy → use; I infer engineered usability.
2. **Walking the shoulders makes terrain load-bearing.** `ن ك ب B004` is shoulder/flank and earth tract; `م ش ي B001` voluntary movement. The metaphor assigns terrain the role of support. The packet supplies the anatomy; I infer humans traversing a bearing structure they did not make.
3. **Consumption ends in reopening.** `ر ز ق B001/B002` is allotted nourishment, `ء ك ل B001` ingestion, and `ن ش ر B002` resurrection, with opening/spreading in `B001`. The packet supplies eat → resurrection; I infer livelihood inside a larger cycle of reopened life.

### Retrospective surprises

67:16 immediately removes any impression that the earth's tractability is permanent, while 67:21 makes provision subject to continued release. 67:30 is the strongest correction: even the water enabling travel and food can sink beyond reach.

## 67:16 — ءَأَمِنتُم مَّن فِى ٱلسَّمَآءِ أَن يَخْسِفَ بِكُمُ ٱلْأَرْضَ فَإِذَا هِىَ تَمُورُ

### Retrospective surprises

67:19 surprisingly turns the space above from a threat-axis into the location of visibly sustained birds. 67:30 mirrors `خ س ف B001` with water's `غ و ر B001`, extending the instability of ground into the hidden depth of what lies within it.

## 67:18 — وَلَقَدْ كَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ فَكَيْفَ كَانَ نَكِيرِ

## 67:19 — أَوَلَمْ يَرَوْا۟ إِلَى ٱلطَّيْرِ فَوْقَهُمْ صَٰٓفَّٰتٍۢ وَيَقْبِضْنَ ۚ مَا يُمْسِكُهُنَّ إِلَّا ٱلرَّحْمَٰنُ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِكُلِّ شَىْءٍۭ بَصِيرٌ

1. **Flight is stable through alternation.** `ص ف ف B001` is even extension, `ق ب ض B006` contraction, and `م س ك B001` holding. Spread and contraction coexist under an external holder. The packet supplies this; I infer dynamic coordination rather than motionless support.
2. **Mercy prevents falling.** `ر ح م B001` is beneficent care and `م س ك B001` preservation. Exclusivity assigns holding to al-Rahman. The packet supplies the identity; I infer aerial suspension as concrete mercy.
3. **Seeing is redirected from defect-search to support-recognition.** `ر ء ي B001` is seeing and `ب ص ر B002` insight. Earlier vision hunted fissures; here it observes birds and asks what holds them. The packet supplies the echo; I infer pedagogical correction from fault-finding to mechanism-recognition.
4. **Every held thing is precisely known.** `ش ي ء B001` uses the “knowable thing” variant; `ب ص ر B002` gives penetrating knowledge. The packet closes an individual support scene with universal scope; I infer that precise holding entails precise awareness.

### Retrospective surprises

67:21 reuses `م س ك B001` for withheld provision, revealing that holding has no single surface effect: it can prevent birds from falling or prevent sustenance from reaching people. 67:30 then gives the withheld provision a concrete form in inaccessible water.

## 67:20 — أَمَّنْ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِى هُوَ جُندٌۭ لَّكُمْ يَنصُرُكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ۚ إِنِ ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ إِلَّا فِى غُرُورٍ

### Activated readings

1. **Aggregation does not create effective aid.** `ج ن د B001` is gathered helpers and `ن ص ر B001` aid making one prevail. The question demands a force able to rescue apart from al-Rahman. The packet supplies army + efficacy test; I infer that collectivity remains dependent.
2. **The substitute is other and lower.** `د و ن B003` is besides and beneath in rank; `ر ح م B001` identifies the excluded source as mercy. The packet supplies this hierarchy; I infer that imagined protectors are inferior substitutions.
3. **Delusion is trust captured by appearance.** `غ ر ر B008` is deception by false appearance/promise and `B007` unguarded trust; `ك ف ر B003/B004` denial/ingratitude. The packet supplies supposed army + delusion; I infer visible force creating a false enclosure of security.

### Retrospective surprises

67:21 replaces the imagined army with the harder question of who can continuously provide, and 67:28 replaces general aid with juridical protection from punishment. These later questions progressively strip the supposed force of military, economic, and legal sufficiency.

## 67:21 — أَمَّنْ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِى يَرْزُقُكُمْ إِنْ أَمْسَكَ رِزْقَهُۥ ۚ بَل لَّجُّوا۟ فِى عُتُوٍّۢ وَنُفُورٍ

### Activated readings

1. **Provision is a continuously held allotment.** `ر ز ق B001/B002` is allotted gift and nourishment; `م س ك B001` is holding or withholding. Repetition of the provision root around the conditional makes the supply itself the object whose flow can be stopped. The packet supplies provider → withholding → provision; I infer dependence on continued release, not merely an original grant.
2. **The holder of birds can withhold livelihood.** `م س ك B001` held birds aloft in 67:19 and now can hold provision back. The same operation preserves in one setting and withholds in another. The packet supplies the root echo; I infer one governing control whose effect depends on what is held. This prevents “holding” from being read as intrinsically punitive or beneficent.
3. **Obstinacy behaves like entry into a deep, noisy current.** `ل ج ج B001` is stubborn persistence; `B002` a deep surging sea and `B005` clamor. `ع ت و B001` supplies insolent overstepping, and `ن ف ر B001` recoil away from truth. The packet supplies persistence “in” arrogance and flight; I infer a self-sustaining current: resistance deepens while its participants move away. The ayah reads as continued motion despite dependence being exposed.

### Retrospective surprises

67:30 is the decisive later surprise: the abstract possibility that provision is held becomes water sunk beyond access. The sea-like persistence activated in `ل ج ج B002` is thereby inverted; obstinacy surges while the water actually needed for life can disappear.

## 67:22 — أَفَمَن يَمْشِى مُكِبًّا عَلَىٰ وَجْهِهِۦٓ أَهْدَىٰٓ أَمَّن يَمْشِى سَوِيًّا عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٍۢ مُّسْتَقِيمٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Two walkers differ by orientation, not by motion alone.** `م ش ي B001` occurs on both sides; `ك ب ب B001` gives face-down overturning, while `س و ي B002` gives sound straight completion and `ق و م B008` upright straightness. Repetition holds walking constant and changes posture. The packet supplies this controlled contrast; I infer that activity is not guidance unless bodily and directional relations are ordered.
2. **The face ceases to guide when it becomes the impact surface.** `و ج ه B001` is face/front and `B002` orientation; `ك ب ب B001/B003` is overturning and tumbling into a pit. The organ normally facing the destination is pressed downward. The packet supplies face + overturning; I infer collapse of orientation into collision. The first walker advances while losing the faculty of directing that advance.
3. **Guidance is fit between bearing and path.** `ه د ي B001/B002` is guidance toward a way and intended course; `ص ر ط B001` is road; `ق و م B008` makes it straight/upright. `س و ي B002` assigns bodily soundness to the walker. The packet supplies upright walker on upright path; I infer correspondence between internal posture and external course. Neither the person nor road alone exhausts guidance.
4. **The forceful onset reading remains exploratory.** `ك ب ب B005` is a charge or forceful onset. Beside the persistent rush of 67:21, it allows an exploratory image of energetic but face-down advance. The packet supplies adjacent obstinacy and motion; I infer that speed can intensify misorientation. This reading supplements the direct overturning branch.

### Retrospective surprises

67:27 returns to the face, but now faces visibly register the consequence of a misdirected course. 67:29's “manifest error” confirms that uprightness and overturning here are mechanisms for eventual disclosure, not merely static moral labels.

## 67:23 — قُلْ هُوَ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنشَأَكُمْ وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمْعَ وَٱلْأَبْصَٰرَ وَٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةَ ۖ قَلِيلًۭا مَّا تَشْكُرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **The failed faculties are made gifts, not self-owned tools.** `ن ش ء B006` is bringing into being, `ج ع ل B001` making, `س م ع B001/B003` hearing and receptive understanding, and `ب ص ر B001/B002` sight and insight. After 67:10's regret, the list identifies the source of those capacities. The packet supplies failure then gift-origin; I infer that non-use is also ingratitude toward the giver.
2. **The heart is an inner kindling that should integrate perception.** `ف ء د B002` is the heart understood as inner heat/kindling; `س م ع` and `ب ص ر` supply incoming perception. Its placement after ear and eyes assigns the heart an integrating role. The packet supplies the sequence; I infer that sensory reports should ignite inward judgment rather than remain unprocessed.
3. **Gratitude means making a received favor visible.** `ش ك ر B001` is recognizing and displaying benefaction; `ق ل ل B001` is fewness and rarity. The packet supplies created faculties → little gratitude; I infer that proper use would visibly acknowledge their source. “Little” diagnoses rare conversion of capacity into recognition, not scarcity of the capacities.

### Retrospective surprises

67:27 makes the relation among faculties visible: seeing the event near ignites inward distress and alters faces. 67:30's repeated `أَرَءَيْتُمْ` then turns those same gifts toward a present, ordinary dependency rather than a remote spectacle.

## 67:24 — قُلْ هُوَ ٱلَّذِى ذَرَأَكُمْ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ وَإِلَيْهِ تُحْشَرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Dispersion in earth is answered by driven collection.** `ذ ر ء B001` is bringing creatures forth and multiplying; `ح ش ر B001` is gathering while driving toward a destination. The prepositional contrast moves from being distributed in earth to being collected toward him. The packet supplies outward multiplication → inward muster; I infer a reversible population movement under one agency.
2. **Human propagation carries a sowing image.** `ذ ر ء B003` is sowing seed in ground, while `ء ر ض B001/B002` is lower ground and fertile earth. The direct branch remains creature-origination (`B001`), but their adjacency activates sowing as material analogy. The packet supplies creatures “in earth”; I infer dispersed embodied lives like seed distributed through a field, later gathered. This is exploratory and does not replace creation.
3. **Gathering is not neutral aggregation.** `ح ش ر B001` includes dislodging and driving a group to a muster place. The packet supplies the passive future; I infer loss of control over destination. The ayah changes from a general return claim to a directed collection that reverses present dispersion.

### Retrospective surprises

67:27 gives the gathered destination an anticipatory form: the promised event advances into nearness before the final collection is narrated. The later proximity sharpens `تُحْشَرُونَ` as movement toward a fixed center, not mere regrouping.

## 67:25 — وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلْوَعْدُ إِن كُنتُمْ صَٰدِقِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **The challenge compresses promise into an appointment demand.** `و ع د B001` is a spoken promise opening expectation, `B002` a threat of harm, and `B003` an appointed time/place. `مَتَىٰ` selects the temporal branch while the context preserves promise and threat. The packet supplies all three within one word; I infer that the speakers attempt to test truth by demanding the appointment date.
2. **Truth is reduced to immediate realization.** `ص د ق B001` is truth in speech and `B004` confirmation or fulfillment of a promise. The conditional challenges the warners to produce fulfillment as proof of correspondence. The packet supplies promise + truth claim; I infer an imposed evidentiary rule: only visible execution counts as truth. This rule is later corrected by 67:26's division between knowledge and warning.
3. **Their question remains utterance and held doctrine.** `ق و ل B001` is spoken saying, while `B013` is held opinion. The recurrent challenge therefore expresses more than curiosity; it voices an epistemic stance. The packet supplies repeated speech scenes; I infer that the demand manifests the inward doctrine already addressed in 67:13.

### Retrospective surprises

67:26 directly corrects the challenge's evidentiary rule by separating possession of timing-knowledge from truthful warning. 67:27 then fulfills `و ع د B004` through near sight, but on a schedule not controlled by the challengers.

## 67:26 — قُلْ إِنَّمَا ٱلْعِلْمُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَإِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ نَذِيرٌۭ مُّبِينٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Knowledge and warning are assigned different custody.** `ع ل م B001` is clear apprehension; `ع ن د B004` proximity/presence; `ن ذ ر B001` warning; `ب ي ن B004/B005` clarity and disclosed meaning. Repeated `إِنَّمَا` partitions roles: timing-knowledge is with God, while the speaker's task is clear warning. The packet supplies this division; I infer that truthful warning does not require possession of the event's schedule.
2. **“At God” marks presence, not spatial transfer.** `ع ن د B004` includes figurative nearness in judgment and rank. The challenge asks for a date, but the answer locates knowledge with God rather than transmitting it. The packet supplies demand and custody response; I infer a boundary around authorized knowledge. The ayah protects epistemic restraint from being mistaken for falsehood.
3. **Clarity belongs to the warning's expression, not to total disclosure.** `ب ي ن B005` is revealing intended meaning through speech or sign, and `B004` becoming apparent. Since event knowledge remains withheld, `مبين` cannot mean that every unknown has been exposed. The packet supplies both limitation and clarity; I infer sufficient intelligibility of the warning within acknowledged limits.

### Retrospective surprises

67:27 immediately demonstrates the partition: the warning remains clear before the date is known to the messenger, then the event itself becomes clear through sight. The later ayah distinguishes clarity of communication from clarity by manifestation.

## 67:27 — فَلَمَّا رَأَوْهُ زُلْفَةًۭ سِيٓـَٔتْ وُجُوهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ وَقِيلَ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِى كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَدَّعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Deferred promise becomes near visual encounter.** `ر ء ي B001` is seeing, while `ز ل ف B001` is advancing into nearness and `B002` near standing. The temporal clause converts the debated future into close sight. The packet supplies demand → withheld date → near vision; I infer that proximity, not timetable, supplies the eventual answer.
2. **The face publicly registers inward harm.** `و ج ه B001` is the outward face meeting an observer; `س و ء B002` is distress/harm and `B005` marking an act as bad. The passive harm to faces follows seeing. The packet supplies visual encounter → altered faces; I infer that inward denial becomes externally legible at manifestation. Hidden doctrine from 67:13 reaches its visible surface.
3. **What they called for arrives against them.** `د ع و B001` is calling to draw near, and `B004` calling down something unwelcome; `ز ل ف B001` supplies actual nearness. The packet supplies earlier demand and current proximity; I infer a reversal in which summons obtains the disliked event. This reading preserves the call/demand force of `تَدَّعُونَ`.
4. **What they claimed is confronted by the thing itself.** `د ع و B002` is claiming a right or affiliation, while `ر ء ي B001` supplies direct seeing. The packet's morphology also permits a claim-oriented activation: speech that arrogated a position is answered by manifest encounter. I infer that “this” functions as the concrete referent testing their claim. The call and claim readings remain distinct.

### Retrospective surprises

67:28 and 67:30 repeat `أَرَءَيْتُمْ` in its “tell me” function (`ر ء ي B013`), unexpectedly shifting from compelled sight back to present reasoning. This confirms that 67:27's visual shock is one mode of disclosure, while the audience is still addressed through inference before it.

## 67:28 — قُلْ أَرَءَيْتُمْ إِنْ أَهْلَكَنِىَ ٱللَّهُ وَمَن مَّعِىَ أَوْ رَحِمَنَا فَمَن يُجِيرُ ٱلْكَٰفِرِينَ مِنْ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **The attention formula redirects from the messenger's fate to the deniers' protection.** `ر ء ي B013` is the “tell me” formula, not only ocular seeing; `ه ل ك B001` is ruin/death; `ر ح م B001` mercy. The conditional preserves two outcomes for the speaker, then the apodosis changes subject. The packet supplies death-or-mercy → who protects deniers; I infer that neither outcome resolves their own exposure.
2. **Mercy and destruction are not evidence controlled by opponents.** `ه ل ك B008` includes “whatever happens, in every case,” while direct `B001` supplies destruction. Paired with `ر ح م B001`, it spans adverse and beneficent possibilities. The packet supplies the exhaustive alternative; I infer invariance of the central protection-question across the messenger's fate.
3. **Protection is sanctuary against painful consequence.** `ج و ر B003` is refuge or granted protection from punishment; `ع ذ ب B005` is torment; `ء ل م B002` is causing pain. The packet supplies protector → from punishment; I infer juridical sanctuary rather than mere physical assistance. The question asks who can establish immunity for the deniers.

### Retrospective surprises

67:29 names al-Rahman as the one trusted, answering the question of protection without turning the messenger's personal outcome into proof. 67:30 then restates absence of an alternative through water: who can actually bring the inaccessible source?

## 67:29 — قُلْ هُوَ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنُ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ وَعَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْنَا ۖ فَسَتَعْلَمُونَ مَنْ هُوَ فِى ضَلَٰلٍۢ مُّبِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Trust answers the repeated security-question.** `ء م ن B002` is trusting assent, while `ء م ن B001` in 67:16–17 was presumed safety. `و ك ل B002` is reliance and `ر ح م B001` merciful beneficence. The packet supplies false security then faith/reliance; I infer a correction from feeling unthreatened to entrusting oneself to the sustaining source.
2. **Reliance is active delegation, not incapacity.** `و ك ل B001` is handing a matter to an authorized agent and `B002` trust; `B003` is the incapable dependent person. The form and first-person plural can preserve reliance without selecting incapacity. The packet supplies confession and directed `عليه`; I infer deliberate entrustment of affairs, while rejecting the branch that would make reliance mere passivity.
3. **Manifest error will become known by outcome.** `ض ل ل B001` is deviation from aim, `ب ي ن B004` visible clarity, and `ع ل م B001` knowledge. The future knowledge echoes 67:17. The packet supplies competing orientations plus later disclosure; I infer that paths will be identified by where they lead. The ayah does not choose a compromise between present trust and future exposure.

### Retrospective surprises

67:30 gives reliance an immediate material test. Trust in al-Rahman is not left at the level of stated affiliation; the final question exposes dependence on the one who can restore water from hidden depth.

## 67:30 — قُلْ أَرَءَيْتُمْ إِنْ أَصْبَحَ مَآؤُكُمْ غَوْرًۭا فَمَن يَأْتِيكُم بِمَآءٍۢ مَّعِينٍۭ

### Activated readings

1. **A familiar morning can become a state of inaccessible water.** `ص ب ح B010` is becoming with *aṣbaḥa*, while `م و ه B001` is water and `غ و ر B001` sinking into depth. The conditional turns routine morning (`ص ب ح B001`) into sudden changed condition. The packet supplies becoming → sunken water; I infer that ordinary recurrence can conceal radical dependence.
2. **The final contrast is hidden depth versus visible flowing source.** `غ و ر B001` is water sunk inward; `ع ي ن B006` is a springing, visible water-source; `ء ت ي B002` is bringing/granting. The packet supplies inaccessible water → who brings flowing water; I infer restoration requiring a giver who can reverse concealment. `مَعِين` reads materially as an appearing spring, not merely generic water.
3. **Seeing culminates in the water-eye.** `ر ء ي B013` again opens with “tell me,” while `ع ي ن B001/B002` supplies eye and face-to-face visibility behind the direct spring branch `B006`. Earlier eyes searched the sky, watched birds, and saw the promise near. The packet supplies this visual sequence; I infer a closing wordplay in which the demanded proof is an everyday source visible like an eye. This is secondary to the lexical spring reading and does not merge with it.
4. **Provision is exposed as hydrological control.** `م و ه B002/B003` includes water appearing abundantly and being supplied; `ر ز ق B003` earlier named rain as provision, while 67:21 asked who provides if provision is held. The packet supplies the cross-root material relation; I infer that the abstract withholding question becomes a concrete water-loss scenario. The final ayah makes dependence drinkable, visible, and immediate.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah remains to trigger a retrospective change. Within this closing ayah itself, however, the final `مَعِين` preserves the distinct spring, visibility, and eye associations without collapsing them into one reading.
