# Surah 10 Full-Context Control Reader A: Ayah Walk

## 10:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
### Activated readings
1. `س م و B005` (“name as raised designation”) and `ر ح م B001` (“mercy and tenderness”) make the opening name a governing merciful rubric. The sequence assigns naming a framing role; I infer that all later warning, rescue, and judgment operate under this agency, so the line is a protocol as well as an invocation.
2. `ر ح م B003` (“womb”), activated by the doubled adjectives and later rescues, adds containing care. I infer that mercy preserves life by enclosing it; this coexists with tenderness rather than replacing it.
### Retrospective surprises
10:86 and 10:107 made the opening mercy unexpectedly concrete: it separates a threatened community from danger and governs release from pressure as well as arriving favor.

## 10:1 — الٓر ۚ تِلْكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ
### Activated readings
1. `ء ي ي B003` (visible sign), `ك ت ب B001` (joining), and `ح ك م B001/B004` (corrective restraint; firm perfection) make the book an ordered mechanism that joins signs and checks misdirection. I infer that coherence performs governance; “wise” describes functional integrity, not only propositional wisdom.
### Retrospective surprises
10:61’s all-inclusive register and 10:82’s truth-establishing words sharpened “wise book” into joined inscription that both records and decisively orders.

## 10:2 — أَكَانَ لِلنَّاسِ عَجَبًا أَنْ أَوْحَيْنَآ إِلَىٰ رَجُلٍۢ مِّنْهُمْ أَنْ أَنذِرِ ٱلنَّاسَ وَبَشِّرِ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ أَنَّ لَهُمْ قَدَمَ صِدْقٍ عِندَ رَبِّهِمْ ۗ قَالَ ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَسَٰحِرٌۭ مُّبِينٌ
### Activated readings
1. `ق د م B002` (precedent merit), `ص د ق B002` (solidity), and `ء م ن B001` (settled security) make `قدم صدق` a secured prior standing. I infer that trusting assent establishes durable precedence near the Lord; the good news concerns status as well as forward movement.
2. `و ح ي B001` (hidden imparting), `ب ش ر B007` (first visible signs), and `س ح ر B002` (deceptive diversion) form rival causal accounts. I infer that opponents misname revelation’s visible effects as magic; “magician” is a competing diagnosis, not a random insult.
### Retrospective surprises
The repeated magic diagnosis in 10:76-81 clarified that “magician” is a durable elite strategy for recoding arrived truth, while 10:82 supplies its public reversal.

## 10:3 — إِنَّ رَبَّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ فِى سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍۢ ثُمَّ ٱسْتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلْعَرْشِ ۖ يُدَبِّرُ ٱلْأَمْرَ ۖ مَا مِن شَفِيعٍ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعْدِ إِذْنِهِۦ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمْ فَٱعْبُدُوهُ ۚ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `خ ل ق B001` (proportioning), `س و ي B003` (settling upon), and `ع ر ش B001` (royal throne) join measured creation to stable command. I infer that proportion requires continuing administration; creation and governance are one causal system.
2. Later ship danger activates `ر ب ب B017` (mariners’ chief) beside `B001` (lordship). I infer that exclusive permission works like command aboard a vessel when ordinary control fails; lordship becomes operational authority, not title alone.
### Retrospective surprises
10:22 supplied the strongest later surprise: the ship strips away delegated control, activating the mariners’ chief image in `ر ب ب B017` and making permission operational.

## 10:4 — إِلَيْهِ مَرْجِعُكُمْ جَمِيعًۭا ۖ وَعْدَ ٱللَّهِ حَقًّا ۚ إِنَّهُۥ يَبْدَؤُا۟ ٱلْخَلْقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُۥ لِيَجْزِىَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ بِٱلْقِسْطِ ۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ لَهُمْ شَرَابٌۭ مِّنْ حَمِيمٍۢ وَعَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌۢ بِمَا كَانُوا۟ يَكْفُرُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ر ج ع B001`, `ج م ع B001`, `ج ز ي B001`, and `ق س ط B004` supply return, reassembly, matching recompense, and straight balance. I infer that a whole agent is restored so acts can be matched to their doer; return reads as forensic reconstruction.
2. `ب د ء B002` pairs beginning with return, while `ع و د B004` supplies practiced recurrence. Later day/night, rain/growth, and rescue/reversion cycles act as rehearsals; I infer that worldly recurrence makes final return intelligible without reducing it to those cycles.
### Retrospective surprises
10:56 compresses the life-death-return circuit, while 10:90-103 enact drowning, preserved evidence, exception, and rescue; these later ayat made return a sorting reconstruction rather than simple recurrence.

## 10:5 — هُوَ ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ ٱلشَّمْسَ ضِيَآءًۭ وَٱلْقَمَرَ نُورًۭا وَقَدَّرَهُۥ مَنَازِلَ لِتَعْلَمُوا۟ عَدَدَ ٱلسِّنِينَ وَٱلْحِسَابَ ۚ مَا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ ذَٰلِكَ إِلَّا بِٱلْحَقِّ ۚ يُفَصِّلُ ٱلْءَايَٰتِ لِقَوْمٍۢ يَعْلَمُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ق د ر B005` (deliberate arrangement), `ن ز ل B003` (station), and `خ ل ق B001` (proportioning) turn the celestial lights into calibrated public infrastructure for counting. I infer that reliable knowledge depends on stable placement; cosmic order enables human reckoning.
2. `ء ي ي B003` and `ب ي ن B004/B005` make patterned distinction disclose meaning. I infer that a sign communicates through intervals and contrast; detailing is relational articulation, not only explanation.
### Retrospective surprises
10:61’s minute-to-cosmic register and 10:101’s command to inspect heaven and earth confirmed that measured creation is also an evidence and accounting infrastructure.

## 10:6 — إِنَّ فِى ٱخْتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيْلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ لَءَايَٰتٍۢ لِّقَوْمٍۢ يَتَّقُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `خ ل ف B006` (ordered alternation) and `ء ي ي B003` (visible sign) make difference a coordinated replacement system. I infer that dependable contrast discloses governance; `اختلاف` is productive succession, not mere opposition.
2. `خ ل ف B001` (one standing after another) anticipates human successors in 10:14. I infer that cosmic replacement supplies an ethical analogy: occupying a vacated position carries ordered responsibility.
### Retrospective surprises
10:67 repeats night/day in explicitly functional terms, confirming alternation as coordinated service; 10:93 then shows that human difference can instead arise after knowledge.

## 10:7 — إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرْجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا وَرَضُوا۟ بِٱلْحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنْيَا وَٱطْمَأَنُّوا۟ بِهَا وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ عَنْ ءَايَٰتِنَا غَٰفِلُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ء ي ي B003` (visible sign) and `B001` (pausing/waiting) interact with satisfaction and repose. I infer that signs should interrupt settled momentum and point beyond themselves; heedlessness is failed directional uptake, not lack of exposure.
### Retrospective surprises
10:92 surprisingly shows a deliberately preserved bodily sign still met by heedlessness; visibility alone therefore cannot supply the missing pause or uptake.

## 10:8 — أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ مَأْوَىٰهُمُ ٱلنَّارُ بِمَا كَانُوا۟ يَكْسِبُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ك و ن B006` (bad state or condition) is activated by ongoing `كانوا يكسبون`. The causal construction assigns repeated acquisition a state-forming role; I infer that accumulated acts stabilize into environment, so the abode is also the final condition their conduct made.
### Retrospective surprises
10:52’s “taste what you earned” retrospectively confirms that acquisition consolidates into experienced condition, not merely an external sentence.

## 10:9 — إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ يَهْدِيهِمْ رَبُّهُم بِإِيمَٰنِهِمْ ۖ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهِمُ ٱلْأَنْهَٰرُ فِى جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ
### Activated readings
1. `ء م ن B001`, `ه د ي B001`, and `ج ر ي B001` join settled trust, guidance, and flow. I infer that inward orientation enables unhindered movement; the garden is the external ecology of a guided interior state.
2. `ج ر ي B006` (ongoing provision), `ج ن ن B003` (tree-covered garden), and `ن ع م B001` (bestowed favor) make reward dynamically sustained rather than static.
### Retrospective surprises
10:57 relocates the same guidance and mercy into the chest, strengthening the interior-to-ecology reading; 10:103 adds deliverance as the route’s public outcome.

## 10:10 — دَعْوَىٰهُمْ فِيهَا سُبْحَٰنَكَ ٱللَّهُمَّ وَتَحِيَّتُهُمْ فِيهَا سَلَٰمٌۭ ۚ وَءَاخِرُ دَعْوَىٰهُمْ أَنِ ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ
### Activated readings
1. `د ع و B001`, `س ب ح B002`, and `س ل م B004` make call, purification, and peace a complete speech circuit. I infer that purified address removes rivalry and distance; paradise’s social order is enacted through speech.
2. Final `ر ب ب B002` (nurture and completion) gives praise a closing role: the circuit ends by acknowledging what sustained it.
### Retrospective surprises
The crisis-prayers of 10:71 and 10:84-86 reveal the worldly formation of this final speech order: call, reliance, submission, rescue, then praise.

## 10:11 — ۞ وَلَوْ يُعَجِّلُ ٱللَّهُ لِلنَّاسِ ٱلشَّرَّ ٱسْتِعْجَالَهُم بِٱلْخَيْرِ لَقُضِىَ إِلَيْهِمْ أَجَلُهُمْ ۖ فَنَذَرُ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرْجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا فِى طُغْيَٰنِهِمْ يَعْمَهُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ع ج ل B001` (bringing forward) and `ق ض ي B004` (completion/expiration) equate immediate harm with a completed term. I infer that delay preserves room for reversal; non-immediacy is active protection.
2. `خ ي ر B003` (choosing the better option) corrects their haste for what they call good. I infer that desire mislabels an immediate object “better” because it cannot see the consequence.
### Retrospective surprises
10:98 supplies the decisive correction: delayed punishment leaves room for a whole community’s timely reversal, confirming delay as preserved possibility rather than neglect.

## 10:12 — وَإِذَا مَسَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ ٱلضُّرُّ دَعَانَا لِجَنۢبِهِۦٓ أَوْ قَاعِدًا أَوْ قَآئِمًۭا فَلَمَّا كَشَفْنَا عَنْهُ ضُرَّهُۥ مَرَّ كَأَن لَّمْ يَدْعُنَآ إِلَىٰ ضُرٍّۢ مَّسَّهُۥ ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ زُيِّنَ لِلْمُسْرِفِينَ مَا كَانُوا۟ يَعْمَلُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ض ر ر B005` (pressing nearness), `ج ن ب B001` (bodily side), and `د ع و B001` (calling near) compress every posture into dependence. I infer that constraint reveals a true causal relation; after relief, restored mobility permits the person to pass by that insight.
### Retrospective surprises
10:22-23 scales this bodily sequence into a shipboard community: pressure produces exclusive calling, rescue restores space, and restored space is immediately misused.

## 10:13 — وَلَقَدْ أَهْلَكْنَا ٱلْقُرُونَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ لَمَّا ظَلَمُوا۟ ۙ وَجَآءَتْهُمْ رُسُلُهُم بِٱلْبَيِّنَٰتِ وَمَا كَانُوا۟ لِيُؤْمِنُوا۟ ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجْزِى ٱلْقَوْمَ ٱلْمُجْرِمِينَ
### Activated readings
1. `ب ي ن B004` (becoming clear), `ء م ن B002` (confirming assent), and `ج ز ي B001` (matching recompense) make clarity the condition of attributable refusal. I infer that disclosed meaning creates answerability; consequence follows intelligibility rather than mere ignorance.
### Retrospective surprises
10:74 repeats the full mechanism and adds sealing, showing how prior clear refusal becomes the durable condition governing later reception.

## 10:14 — ثُمَّ جَعَلْنَٰكُمْ خَلَٰٓئِفَ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ مِنۢ بَعْدِهِمْ لِنَنظُرَ كَيْفَ تَعْمَلُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `خ ل ف B001` (standing after another) and `ع م ل B001` (intentional work) make succession transferred responsibility. I infer that inheriting a vacated place creates a live test of replacement conduct.
2. `خ ل ف B010` (renewal after loss) preserves a second reading: succession is warning and granted restart, with its outcome enacted through work.
### Retrospective surprises
10:73 supplies the concrete successor-event: rescued companions become successors after deniers drown, joining renewal directly to differentiated response.

## 10:15 — وَإِذَا تُتْلَىٰ عَلَيْهِمْ ءَايَاتُنَا بَيِّنَٰتٍۢ ۙ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَرْجُونَ لِقَآءَنَا ٱئْتِ بِقُرْءَانٍ غَيْرِ هَٰذَآ أَوْ بَدِّلْهُ ۚ قُلْ مَا يَكُونُ لِىٓ أَنْ أُبَدِّلَهُۥ مِن تِلْقَآئِ نَفْسِىٓ ۖ إِنْ أَتَّبِعُ إِلَّا مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَىَّ ۖ إِنِّىٓ أَخَافُ إِنْ عَصَيْتُ رَبِّى عَذَابَ يَوْمٍ عَظِيمٍۢ
### Activated readings
1. `ت ل و B001`, `ب د ل B001`, `ت ب ع B004`, and `و ح ي B001` oppose sequence-following to replacement. I infer that altering a unit breaks the received chain; refusal to edit is structural fidelity.
2. `غ ي ر B003` (substitution) and `ن ف س B013` (hidden intent) show that requested textual change would relocate authorship into private intent. The ayah guards causal source, not wording alone.
### Retrospective surprises
10:109 closes the surah by repeating follow-revelation and adding patience until judgment, confirming transmission as disciplined succession rather than editable possession.

## 10:16 — قُل لَّوْ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مَا تَلَوْتُهُۥ عَلَيْكُمْ وَلَآ أَدْرَىٰكُم بِهِۦ ۖ فَقَدْ لَبِثْتُ فِيكُمْ عُمُرًۭا مِّن قَبْلِهِۦٓ ۚ أَفَلَا تَعْقِلُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ل ب ث B001`, `ع م ر B001`, and `ع ق ل B001` make shared long residence a control condition restraining the fabrication hypothesis. I infer that observed biography is negative authorship evidence; reasoning inhibits an implausible claim.
2. `ت ل و B001` and the willing variant of `ش ي ء B001` make recitation downstream of divine will, not self-originating capacity.
### Retrospective surprises
10:94 adds prior readers as a second cross-temporal control, surprising the initial biographical argument with an external continuity check.

## 10:17 — فَمَنْ أَظْلَمُ مِمَّنِ ٱفْتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبًا أَوْ كَذَّبَ بِـَٔايَٰتِهِۦٓ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفْلِحُ ٱلْمُجْرِمُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ف ر ي B003`, `ك ذ ب B002`, and `ف ل ح B001` join fabrication, declaring false, and cleaving. False addition and false subtraction both sever statement from reality; I infer that such discourse cannot produce `ف ل ح B005`, lasting good.
### Retrospective surprises
10:69 repeats the fabrication/non-flourishing relation in stripped form, while 10:81 explains the failure mechanically as unsound work that cannot be preserved.

## 10:18 — وَيَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَضُرُّهُمْ وَلَا يَنفَعُهُمْ وَيَقُولُونَ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ شُفَعَٰٓؤُنَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ۚ قُلْ أَتُنَبِّـُٔونَ ٱللَّهَ بِمَا لَا يَعْلَمُ فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَلَا فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ سُبْحَٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `د و ن B001` (nearness short of a limit), `ع ن د B004` (presence), and `ش ر ك B001` (partnership) expose an invented spatial model of access. I infer that claimed proximity is not authorized arrival; the intermediary bridge fails.
2. `س ب ح B002` (purifying declaration) reverses `ش ر ك B001`. I infer that transcendence strips unauthorized shares from the social map; it is corrective relation as well as praise.
### Retrospective surprises
10:28-29 stages the alleged partnership at judgment and has the partners deny the relation, converting the failed access model into visible forensic separation.

## 10:19 — وَمَا كَانَ ٱلنَّاسُ إِلَّآ أُمَّةًۭ وَٰحِدَةًۭ فَٱخْتَلَفُوا۟ ۚ وَلَوْلَا كَلِمَةٌۭ سَبَقَتْ مِن رَّبِّكَ لَقُضِىَ بَيْنَهُمْ فِيمَا فِيهِ يَخْتَلِفُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ء م م B002` (source/gathering point), `و ح د B005` (one in a shared respect), and `خ ل ف B004` (divergent ways) make disagreement divergence from a shared orientation, not proof of unrelated origins.
2. `ك ل م B002`, `س ب ق B001`, `ق ض ي B001`, and `ب ي ن B001` make the prior word a temporal condition blocking immediate separation. I infer that delay is fidelity to an earlier governing utterance.
### Retrospective surprises
10:93 surprisingly places disagreement after secure settlement, provision, and arrived knowledge, sharpening difference as divergent uptake rather than informational lack.

## 10:20 — وَيَقُولُونَ لَوْلَآ أُنزِلَ عَلَيْهِ ءَايَةٌۭ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ ۖ فَقُلْ إِنَّمَا ٱلْغَيْبُ لِلَّهِ فَٱنتَظِرُوٓا۟ إِنِّى مَعَكُم مِّنَ ٱلْمُنتَظِرِينَ
### Activated readings
1. `ن ز ل B002`, `ء ي ي B003`, and `غ ي ب B001` frame their demand as forced transfer from hidden to visible. I infer that timing belongs to the hidden event’s owner; shared waiting is epistemic discipline.
2. `ء ي ي B001` (pausing/waiting) makes delay itself diagnostic. The requested sign tests how claimants inhabit non-control.
### Retrospective surprises
10:102 repeats the shared-waiting formula but fills delay with patterned event-days from earlier peoples, correcting any reading of waiting as empty suspension.

## 10:21 — وَإِذَآ أَذَقْنَا ٱلنَّاسَ رَحْمَةًۭ مِّنۢ بَعْدِ ضَرَّآءَ مَسَّتْهُمْ إِذَا لَهُم مَّكْرٌۭ فِىٓ ءَايَاتِنَا ۚ قُلِ ٱللَّهُ أَسْرَعُ مَكْرًا ۚ إِنَّ رُسُلَنَا يَكْتُبُونَ مَا تَمْكُرُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ر ح م B001`, `ض ر ر B005`, and `ء ي ي B003` make relief contested evidence after pressure. I infer that scheming tries to overwrite mercy’s causal meaning.
2. `ك ت ب B003` (binding inscription) gives recording the counter-role: hidden plotting becomes fixed, reviewable action. Divine swiftness includes immediate conversion of evasion into record.

## 10:100 — وَمَا كَانَ لِنَفْسٍ أَن تُؤْمِنَ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِ ٱللَّهِ ۚ وَيَجْعَلُ ٱلرِّجْسَ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعْقِلُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `ن ف س B011`, `ء م ن B001/B002`, `ج ع ل B002`, and `ع ق ل B001/B002` place faith between divine enablement and the self’s restraining understanding. I infer that permission enables rather than coerces; refusal to tether ignorance permits impurity to become a settled state.
### Retrospective surprises
10:101 shows what permission does not replace: signs and warnings still require uptake, and 10:108 assigns guidance or straying back to the self.

## 10:101 — قُلِ ٱنظُرُوا۟ مَاذَا فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ وَمَا تُغْنِى ٱلْءَايَٰتُ وَٱلنُّذُرُ عَن قَوْمٍۢ لَّا يُؤْمِنُونَ
### Activated readings
1. `س م و B004`, `ء ر ض B001`, `ء ي ي B003`, `ن ذ ر B001`, and `ء م ن B002` place the whole vertical field before viewers, then deny automatic benefit. I infer that signs and warnings supply evidence and caution but cannot replace assenting uptake; perception has a non-transferable causal step.
### Retrospective surprises
10:102 turns ineffective viewing into temporal waiting for patterned event-days, revealing that ignored signs can become embodied historical consequence.

## 10:102 — فَهَلْ يَنتَظِرُونَ إِلَّا مِثْلَ أَيَّامِ ٱلَّذِينَ خَلَوْا۟ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ ۚ قُلْ فَٱنتَظِرُوٓا۟ إِنِّى مَعَكُم مِّنَ ٱلْمُنتَظِرِينَ
### Activated readings
1. `م ث ل B001/B011`, `ي و م B003`, and `س ب ق B001` make earlier severe event-days counterparts and lessons for present waiting. I infer that delay is not empty time: prior patterns give its possible shape, while shared waiting refuses false control of appointment.
### Retrospective surprises
10:103 supplies the surprise inside waiting: patterned event-days include not only destruction but a due separation and rescue of messengers and believers.

## 10:103 — ثُمَّ نُنَجِّى رُسُلَنَا وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ حَقًّا عَلَيْنَا نُنجِ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ
### Activated readings
1. Repeated `ن ج و B001`, `ء م ن B001`, and `ح ق ق B002/B005` make deliverance by separation a due pattern made to stand. I infer that rescue is neither accidental exception nor human entitlement detached from God; it is an authoritative self-committed relation to trust.
### Retrospective surprises
10:108 keeps due rescue from erasing personal agency, and 10:109 assigns the messenger patient following rather than control of the adjudicative timetable.

## 10:104 — قُلْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِن كُنتُمْ فِى شَكٍّۢ مِّن دِينِى فَلَآ أَعْبُدُ ٱلَّذِينَ تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَٰكِنْ أَعْبُدُ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِى يَتَوَفَّىٰكُمْ ۖ وَأُمِرْتُ أَنْ أَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ
### Activated readings
1. `د ي ن B001`, `د و ن B001`, `ء ل ه B001`, `و ف ي B002`, and `ء م ن B002` make worship answer to the one who receives every soul in full, not to near-but-short objects. I infer that death exposes ultimate possession; the speaker’s religious distinction is grounded in causal jurisdiction.
### Retrospective surprises
10:105-107 unfold the declaration into fixed orientation, refusal of ineffective invocation, and exclusive control of harm and favor; religion becomes a complete causal practice.

## 10:105 — وَأَنْ أَقِمْ وَجْهَكَ لِلدِّينِ حَنِيفًۭا وَلَا تَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلْمُشْرِكِينَ
### Activated readings
1. `د ي ن B001` and `ش ر ك B008/B001` oppose settled obedient orientation to a divided, preoccupied mind and shared allegiance. I infer that “establish your face” is whole-person directional stabilization; single orientation repairs the cognitive split partnership produces.
### Retrospective surprises
10:106 identifies the divided orientation’s practical output as calling what cannot benefit or harm, while 10:107 supplies the exclusive agency that repairs the split.

## 10:106 — وَلَا تَدْعُ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مَا لَا يَنفَعُكَ وَلَا يَضُرُّكَ ۖ فَإِن فَعَلْتَ فَإِنَّكَ إِذًۭا مِّنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ
### Activated readings
1. `د ع و B001`, `د و ن B001`, `ن ف ع B001`, `ض ر ر B001`, and `ظ ل م B004` make invocation of ineffective near-but-short objects a misplaced causal act. I infer that injustice here is practical mislocation of dependence, not only doctrinal error.
### Retrospective surprises
10:107 immediately replaces the ineffective object with the only agent who removes pressure and whose favor cannot be repelled, confirming injustice as causal misplacement.

## 10:107 — وَإِن يَمْسَسْكَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرٍّۢ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ وَإِن يُرِدْكَ بِخَيْرٍۢ فَلَا رَآدَّ لِفَضْلِهِۦ ۚ يُصِيبُ بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنْ عِبَادِهِۦ ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ
### Activated readings
1. `ض ر ر B005`, `ر د د B002`, `خ ي ر B005`, the willing variant of `ش ي ء B001`, and `ر ح م B001` establish asymmetric exclusive agency: pressure has no remover but God, while intended beneficence has no repeller. I infer that mercy governs both release from constraint and unstoppable arrival of favor.
### Retrospective surprises
10:108 prevents exclusive divine agency from being misread as erased human uptake: after truth arrives, guidance and straying still shape the self reflexively.

## 10:108 — قُلْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ قَدْ جَآءَكُمُ ٱلْحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكُمْ ۖ فَمَنِ ٱهْتَدَىٰ فَإِنَّمَا يَهْتَدِى لِنَفْسِهِۦ ۖ وَمَن ضَلَّ فَإِنَّمَا يَضِلُّ عَلَيْهَا ۖ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ عَلَيْكُم بِوَكِيلٍۢ
### Activated readings
1. `ء ت ي B001`, `ح ق ق B001`, `ه د ي B001/B002`, `ن ف س B011`, `ض ل ل B001`, and `و ك ل B006` distribute agency after truth arrives. I infer that guidance and straying recursively shape their own self, while the messenger is not the guardian who can undertake each person’s uptake.
### Retrospective surprises
10:109 defines the messenger’s non-guardian role positively: follow received revelation, remain with it patiently, and do not preempt divine judgment.

## 10:109 — وَٱتَّبِعْ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيْكَ وَٱصْبِرْ حَتَّىٰ يَحْكُمَ ٱللَّهُ ۚ وَهُوَ خَيْرُ ٱلْحَٰكِمِينَ
### Activated readings
1. `ت ب ع B003/B012`, `و ح ي B001`, `ص ب ر B001`, and `ح ك م B002/B003` form the closing operating rule: track revelation consistently, restrain panic, and leave adjudication to rightly guided judgment. I infer that patience prevents the transmitter from preempting the judge; following and waiting are active fidelity.
2. `ص ب ر B003` (bearing surety/standing with someone) preserves a social reading: patience means remaining with the entrusted message until judgment, not merely private endurance.
### Retrospective surprises
No later ayah remains to produce a retrospective surprise; the line itself closes the surah by converting its repeated follow/wait/judge pattern into an explicit operating rule.
