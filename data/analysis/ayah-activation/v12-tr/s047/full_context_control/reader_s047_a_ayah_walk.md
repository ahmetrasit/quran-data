# Surah 47 Full-Context Control Reader Ayah Walk

## 47:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
### Activated readings
1. **Mercy as restored relation.** Doubled `ر ح م` activates B001, “mercy and tenderness,” while 47:22 later activates B002, “kinship bond,” through the cutting of `أرحامكم`. The packet supplies the lexical overlap and severance image; I infer that mercy preserves or restores relation against rupture. The opening invokes not only tenderness but bond-maintaining power.
### Retrospective surprises
47:22 surprisingly converts the opening’s abstract mercy into an anti-severance mechanism; 47:38’s threatened communal replacement makes preserved relation a surah-wide stake.

## 47:1 — ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ وَصَدُّوا۟ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَضَلَّ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Obstruction makes labor lose its destination.** `ص د د` B005, “blocking mountain,” stands against `س ب ل` B001, “extended traversed way”; `ض ل ل` B003 adds failure to locate, and `ع م ل` B011 permits “traveled road.” The packet supplies blocking, path, loss, and work; I infer that obstructing the divine route rebounds by depriving their own activity of a terminus. `أضل أعمالهم` is functional route-collapse, not mere negative valuation.
2. **Concealment is answered by disappearance.** `ك ف ر` B003 covers or rejects truth, while `ض ل ل` B002 is disappearance. The ayah’s sequence supplies concealment first and vanished works last; I infer a measure-for-measure reversal. Chosen covering makes action’s product itself unavailable.
### Retrospective surprises
47:32 repeats the same obstruction nearly verbatim after guidance has become clear, sharpening 47:1 from generic opposition into deliberate route-blocking; 47:38 finally tests the same “way” through expenditure.

## 47:2 — وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَءَامَنُوا۟ بِمَا نُزِّلَ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍۢ وَهُوَ ٱلْحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمْ ۙ كَفَّرَ عَنْهُمْ سَيِّـَٔاتِهِمْ وَأَصْلَحَ بَالَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Covering reverses into protective effacement.** Here `ك ف ر` B009, “covering or effacing sin,” contrasts with 47:1’s B003, “covering or rejecting truth.” Form and sequence are supplied; I infer that assent to disclosed `حق` redirects covering from human refusal into divine removal of harmful traces. Reward is a reversal of the same mechanism.
2. **Repair reaches conduct and operating condition.** Repeated `ص ل ح` activates B001, soundness against corruption, and B003, suitability; `بال` activates `ب و ل` B003/B004, inner concern and state of affairs. Righteous acts precede repair of `بال`; I infer feedback between enacted soundness and a newly fitted interior. Their inward condition is made suitable for continued sound action.
### Retrospective surprises
47:5 repeats repair of `بال` after death, extending this mechanism beyond present psychology; 47:22’s `فساد` later supplies the exact systemic opposite of `صلاح`.

## 47:3 — ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ ٱتَّبَعُوا۟ ٱلْبَٰطِلَ وَأَنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ ٱتَّبَعُوا۟ ٱلْحَقَّ مِن رَّبِّهِمْ ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يَضْرِبُ ٱللَّهُ لِلنَّاسِ أَمْثَٰلَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Following manufactures outcomes step by step.** Doubled `ت ب ع` activates B003/B004, stepwise tracking and ordered succession; its objects activate `ب ط ل` B001, voidness, and `ح ق ق` B001, firm truth. Symmetric grammar supplies two trajectories; I infer that repeated following progressively conforms followers to the stability or voidness of their objects. The groups become their likenesses through accumulated tracking.
2. **The example strikes and impresses.** `ض ر ب` B003 directly sets forth an example, while B001 makes an impact and B009 supplies impressed disposition; `م ث ل` B003 is parable. The collocation is supplied; I infer that comparison is intended to form the hearer by impact. Presentation and formative striking coexist.
### Retrospective surprises
47:27’s striking of faces and backs makes the bodily force dormant in `يضرب` newly audible, while 47:38’s `أمثالكم` turns example into the danger of being replaced by a non-matching counterpart.

## 47:4 — فَإِذَا لَقِيتُمُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ فَضَرْبَ ٱلرِّقَابِ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَثْخَنتُمُوهُمْ فَشُدُّوا۟ ٱلْوَثَاقَ فَإِمَّا مَنًّۢا بَعْدُ وَإِمَّا فِدَآءً حَتَّىٰ تَضَعَ ٱلْحَرْبُ أَوْزَارَهَا ۚ ذَٰلِكَ وَلَوْ يَشَآءُ ٱللَّهُ لَٱنتَصَرَ مِنْهُمْ وَلَٰكِن لِّيَبْلُوَا۟ بَعْضَكُم بِبَعْضٍۢ ۗ وَٱلَّذِينَ قُتِلُوا۟ فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ فَلَن يُضِلَّ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Force converts into custody and then release.** `ض ر ب` B001 supplies impact, `ث خ ن` B003 disabling heaviness, `و ث ق` B003 restraint, `م ن ن` B004 free release, and `ف د ي` B002 captive exchange. The ordered sequence and terminal `حتى` are supplied; I infer that each achieved objective changes power’s permitted function. The ayah bounds combat through staged relational conversion.
2. **War lays down equipment and burden.** `و ض ع` B001 is setting down; `و ز ر` activates both B003, war gear, and B002, heavy burden. Personified war and plural loads are supplied; I infer that cessation deposits both apparatus and carried moral-social weight. The endpoint exceeds disarmament alone.
3. **The ordeal discloses rather than supplies divine power.** `ب ل و` B002 is testing that discloses state, framed by the counterfactual `ن ص ر` B001, aid that makes prevail. The packet says direct victory could occur but people are set against one another; I infer that encounter is selected as disclosure. The final non-loss of faithful works prevents ordeal from becoming effacement.
### Retrospective surprises
47:31 explicitly repeats `بلو` with knowledge, fighters, patient ones, and reports, confirming disclosure as the hidden aim; 47:35’s prohibition on calling for peace while weakening corrects any reading that detaches release from the ayah’s staged conditions.

## 47:5 — سَيَهْدِيهِمْ وَيُصْلِحُ بَالَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Guidance continues as composure after death.** `ه د ي` B001 gives gentle direction and B010 calm bearing; `ص ل ح` B001 restores soundness, while `ب و ل` B003/B004 locates repair in inner concern and condition. The killed of 47:4 supply the context; I infer that guidance continues as post-ordeal settling, not only pre-death route selection.
2. **Repair fits the person to the destination.** `ه د ي` B002 activates intended course and `ص ل ح` B003 suitability. Direction followed by fitting is supplied; I infer that arrival requires transformation into fitness for what follows. Guidance is preparation as well as indication.
### Retrospective surprises
47:6 immediately supplies entry as the destination, and 47:17 later presents guidance as compounding increase; both make 47:5 a transitional process rather than a static reward.

## 47:6 — وَيُدْخِلُهُمُ ٱلْجَنَّةَ عَرَّفَهَا لَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Entry is recognition of a prepared interior.** `د خ ل` B001/B003 supplies entering and hidden interior; `ج ن ن` B003/B001 supplies tree-covered garden and concealment; `ع ر ف` B003 is recognition by trace. The packet gives entry into a concealed place followed by recognition; I infer that guidance and repaired inward condition make its signs legible. `عرفها` is intimate orientation, not merely naming.
2. **The garden is known by connected features.** `ع ر ف` B001 images connected succession and B013 visible features by which land is known. The ordered landscape of 47:15 supplies those features; I infer a coherent terrain whose marks connect. Recognition becomes spatial wayfinding.
### Retrospective surprises
47:15’s rivers, tastes, fruits, and contrasting hot water unexpectedly fill the sparse `عرفها` with sensory landmarks; 47:30 later reuses recognition for identifying concealed persons by marks and speech.

## 47:7 — يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ إِن تَنصُرُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ يَنصُرْكُمْ وَيُثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَكُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Reciprocal aid stabilizes advance.** Repeated `ن ص ر` activates B001, aid that makes prevail; `ث ب ت` B003 makes firm, and `ق د م` B004/B006 supplies forward precedence and bold undertaking. Conditional symmetry and feet supply the mechanism; I infer that divine aid does not replace movement but keeps its leading edge from collapsing.
2. **Firm feet are verified footing.** `ث ب ت` B005/B006 is verification and proof; `ق د م` B002 is precedent merit. Later contrasts between spoken obedience and tested conduct supply the context; I infer that stable action becomes evidentially reliable. The promise includes confirmation of standing.
### Retrospective surprises
47:20-21 makes verbal eagerness falter when combat is specified, retrospectively defining firm feet as the passage from speech to resolved action; 47:31 then makes that passage an explicit test.

## 47:8 — وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ فَتَعْسًۭا لَّهُمْ وَأَضَلَّ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Failed aim becomes face-down fall.** `ت ع س` B001 is stumbling face-down without recovery and B004 missing one’s aim; `ض ل ل` B001/B002 is straying and disappearance. Bodily fall followed by lost works is supplied; I infer that misorientation manifests as both failed motion and vanished consequence. The two clauses form one collapsed trajectory.
### Retrospective surprises
47:27 later places striking specifically on faces and backs, giving this fall directional anatomy; 47:33 warns believers not to produce their own version of work-nullification.

## 47:9 — ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمْ كَرِهُوا۟ مَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ فَأَحْبَطَ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Rejected descent becomes swollen but fruitless work.** `ن ز ل` B002 supplies what is brought down, `ك ر ه` B001 aversion, and `ح ب ط` B001 belly swelling from harmful grazing. The causal sequence is supplied, while 47:12 later supplies animal-like eating; I infer a metabolic image in which wrong intake enlarges without nourishing. Nullification is pathological inflation, not simple subtraction.
2. **Nullification is relapse.** `ح ب ط` B004 is wound relapse or lingering scar effect. The causal `فـ` supplies the arrow from aversion to collapse; I infer that outwardly present works can reopen into failure when their relation to revelation is diseased.
### Retrospective surprises
47:12’s grazing simile strongly activates the harmful-forage variant; 47:28 repeats aversion plus `أحبط أعمالهم`, confirming a recurrent causal syndrome rather than an isolated verdict.

## 47:10 — ۞ أَفَلَمْ يَسِيرُوا۟ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ فَيَنظُرُوا۟ كَيْفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ ۚ دَمَّرَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيْهِمْ ۖ وَلِلْكَٰفِرِينَ أَمْثَٰلُهَا
### Activated readings
1. **Travel is an evidentiary method.** `س ي ر` B001/B002 is forward travel and conduct; `ن ظ ر` B001 directs sight or insight; `ع ق ب` B011/B006 is remaining trace and outcome. Movement, looking, and aftermath are sequenced; I infer that traversal contacts traces from which conduct and consequence can be reconstructed. This is field inquiry, not generic reflection.
2. **Outcome is a repeatable counterpart.** `ع ق ب` B005/B009 supplies succession and repeated return; `م ث ل` B001/B002 supplies counterpart and exemplary punishment. Future disbelievers receive `أمثالها`; I infer that earlier outcomes are reproducible patterns under matched conditions.
### Retrospective surprises
47:32 repeats the antecedent conditions after guidance, while 47:38 ends with a threatened substitute people who will not be “your likes”; likeness thus shifts from recurring punishment to recurring or replaced community type.

## 47:11 — ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَوْلَى ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَأَنَّ ٱلْكَٰفِرِينَ لَا مَوْلَىٰ لَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Guardianship is effective nearness.** `و ل ي` B001 activates adjacency, B003 assuming charge, and B004 loyal support. Mirrored grammar supplies operative presence on one side and absence on the other; I infer that protection works through sustained proximity that takes charge. `مولى` explains different outcomes as a relational position, not a distant title.
2. **No patron means no continuity through collapse.** `و ل ي` B002 is continuous succession. Prior destruction and 47:12’s final lodging supply opposing continuities; I infer that a patron carries the person through transitions, whereas present enjoyment alone does not.
### Retrospective surprises
47:22 and 47:38 reuse `تولي` as withdrawal, revealing the human-side reversal of divine nearness: refusing the sustaining relation opens corruption or replacement.

## 47:12 — إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُدْخِلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ جَنَّٰتٍۢ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَٰرُ ۖ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ يَتَمَتَّعُونَ وَيَأْكُلُونَ كَمَا تَأْكُلُ ٱلْأَنْعَٰمُ وَٱلنَّارُ مَثْوًۭى لَّهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Use contrasts with settled habitation.** `م ت ع` B001/B007 is enjoyed benefit and prolonged use; `ث و ي` B001/B002 is settled lodging and guest relation. Present consumption contrasts with final `مثوى`; I infer that enjoyment without orientation cannot become secure habitation but delivers the consumer to another host.
2. **Consumption erases discernment.** `ء ك ل` B001 is ingestion but B012 is sound judgment; `ن ع م` B005 supplies grazing livestock. The explicit simile is supplied; I infer that appetite-only repetition narrows the human function of eating away from discernment. The comparison diagnoses an operating mode.
3. **Flowing gardens are maintained provision.** `ج ر ي` B001/B006 is flow and ongoing provision; `ن ه ر` B001/B003 is a water-cut channel that opens flow. Repeated under-flow supplies a support system; I infer sustained circulation rather than static possession.
### Retrospective surprises
47:15 expands the flow into differentiated liquids and reverses it with internally cutting hot water; 47:36 later reduces worldly life’s attractive use to diversion, correcting any reading that treats `يتمتعون` as durable flourishing.

## 47:13 — وَكَأَيِّن مِّن قَرْيَةٍ هِىَ أَشَدُّ قُوَّةًۭ مِّن قَرْيَتِكَ ٱلَّتِىٓ أَخْرَجَتْكَ أَهْلَكْنَٰهُمْ فَلَا نَاصِرَ لَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Collective strength can expel its condition of survival.** `ق ر ي` B001 images a gathered settlement, `ق و ي` B001 strength assembled like rope-strands, and `خ ر ج` B002 causing exit. A stronger city expels, then meets `ه ل ك` B001 ruin; I infer that cohesion directed toward expulsion can remove a relation that would preserve the collective. The ayah exposes self-defeating assembled power.
2. **Internal force cannot substitute for aid.** Rope-like `ق و ي` B001 stands beside absence of `ن ص ر` B001, aid that makes prevail. Superior force but no helper is supplied; I infer that aggregate capacity alone is not sustaining alliance.
### Retrospective surprises
47:32 says such opposition cannot harm God at all, relocating the damage into the opposing collective; 47:38’s replacement threat shows that a gathered people remains contingent despite its apparent strength.

## 47:14 — أَفَمَن كَانَ عَلَىٰ بَيِّنَةٍۢ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ كَمَن زُيِّنَ لَهُۥ سُوٓءُ عَمَلِهِۦ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓا۟ أَهْوَآءَهُم
### Activated readings
1. **Clarity separates; adornment recruits.** `ب ي ن` B004 is becoming clear and B001 separation; `ز ي ن` B002 makes beauty show over `س و ء` B001 badness, followed by `ت ب ع` B003 stepwise tracking. The contrast supplies two perceptual mechanisms; I infer that evidence creates discriminating distance, whereas beautification removes distance from harm by making it attractive.
2. **Desire is inclination and fall.** `ه و ي` B004 is self-inclination and B002 falling into an abyss. Desire is repeatedly followed; I infer that sustained inclination becomes downward trajectory. Both affective and spatial readings remain active.
### Retrospective surprises
47:16 repeats following desire after a sealed heart, converting the visual beautification here into a stabilized cognitive pattern; 47:25 later names the beautifying agency through `سول`.

## 47:15 — مَّثَلُ ٱلْجَنَّةِ ٱلَّتِى وُعِدَ ٱلْمُتَّقُونَ ۖ فِيهَآ أَنْهَٰرٌۭ مِّن مَّآءٍ غَيْرِ ءَاسِنٍۢ وَأَنْهَٰرٌۭ مِّن لَّبَنٍۢ لَّمْ يَتَغَيَّرْ طَعْمُهُۥ وَأَنْهَٰرٌۭ مِّنْ خَمْرٍۢ لَّذَّةٍۢ لِّلشَّٰرِبِينَ وَأَنْهَٰرٌۭ مِّنْ عَسَلٍۢ مُّصَفًّۭى ۖ وَلَهُمْ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ وَمَغْفِرَةٌۭ مِّن رَّبِّهِمْ ۖ كَمَنْ هُوَ خَٰلِدٌۭ فِى ٱلنَّارِ وَسُقُوا۟ مَآءً حَمِيمًۭا فَقَطَّعَ أَمْعَآءَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **An ecology without degrading transitions.** `ن ه ر` B001 supplies channels cut by flow; `ء س ن` B001 stale alteration, `غ ي ر` B003 alteration, and `ص ف و` B001 purity without admixture. Negated change and affirmed filtering are supplied; I infer circulation that preserves distinctions without decay. The garden is maintained process, not a static inventory.
2. **Pleasure permeates without corruption.** `ش ر ب` B007 permits permeation beside B001 drinking; `ل ذ ذ` B001 supplies delight, while `خ م ر` B001 covering coexists with B002 fermented drink. The packet gives pleasurable drink while excluding neighboring decay; I infer, exploratorily, delight without cognitive covering. The weak link is that cognition is not explicit, so this remains distinct and tentative.
3. **The counter-scene reverses nourishing flow into internal severance.** `س ق ي` B001 gives drink, `ح م م` B002 hot water, `ق ط ع` B001/B021 severing and internal rupture, and `م ع ي` B001 intestine. Externally administered liquid causes internal cutting; I infer an inverted hydraulic system.
4. **Forgiveness is protective covering amid total fruit.** `ث م ر` B003 is fruit as benefit; `غ ف ر` B001/B002 is protective covering and shielding from consequence. Plenitude followed by protection is supplied; I infer that complete benefit preserves rather than exposes the person.
### Retrospective surprises
47:19’s request for forgiveness for an entire community socializes the protective covering here; 47:36-38’s money-demand sequence makes the garden’s unforced provision contrast with worldly attachment under pressure.

## 47:16 — وَمِنْهُم مَّن يَسْتَمِعُ إِلَيْكَ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا خَرَجُوا۟ مِنْ عِندِكَ قَالُوا۟ لِلَّذِينَ أُوتُوا۟ ٱلْعِلْمَ مَاذَا قَالَ ءَانِفًا ۚ أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ طَبَعَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِمْ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓا۟ أَهْوَآءَهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Auditory contact fails to become compliant understanding.** `س م ع` B001 is hearing, while B003 is understanding and compliance; `خ ر ج` B001 marks passing out from within. Listening, departure, and immediate loss of `ق و ل` B001 speech are supplied; I infer that the message exits because acoustic reception never becomes responsive comprehension.
2. **“Just now” exposes repeated reset.** `ء ن ف` B001 is taking from the beginning and B004 fresh newness. The question immediately after leaving is supplied; I infer that speech remains perpetually new because never integrated.
3. **The seal fixes a turning faculty into a pattern.** `ط ب ع` B001/B006 is sealing and pattern; `ق ل ب` B004 is turning. Seal followed by tracked desires is supplied; I infer that mobility hardens into repeated reversal toward desire.
### Retrospective surprises
47:24’s locked hearts confirms disabled turning, while 47:30’s recognition through speech-pattern shows that language still reveals what failed listening concealed.

## 47:17 — وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱهْتَدَوْا۟ زَادَهُمْ هُدًۭى وَءَاتَىٰهُمْ تَقْوَىٰهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Guidance compounds through enacted direction.** `ه د ي` B001/B002 is gentle guidance and intended course; `ز ي د` B001 is growth. Initial self-orientation followed by increase is supplied; I infer a feedback loop in which taking the course creates capacity for finer direction.
2. **Protective caution is a granted resource.** `ء ت ي` B002 grants, while `و ق ي` B001/B002 is barrier and self-protective caution. The sequence supplies bestowed protection after increased guidance; I infer usable defense against desire-driven drift.
### Retrospective surprises
47:25’s retreat after guidance becomes clear proves that clarity alone is not this compounding process; 47:36 reconnects faith and protection to recompense under material demand.

## 47:18 — فَهَلْ يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا ٱلسَّاعَةَ أَن تَأْتِيَهُم بَغْتَةًۭ ۖ فَقَدْ جَآءَ أَشْرَاطُهَا ۚ فَأَنَّىٰ لَهُمْ إِذَا جَآءَتْهُمْ ذِكْرَىٰهُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Waiting manufactures surprise by ignoring forerunners.** `ن ظ ر` B002 watches for a time, `ب غ ت` B001 is suddenness, and `ش ر ط` B002 early signs. Waiting, sudden arrival, and signs already arrived are supplied; I infer that passive expectation refuses present marks as operative time.
2. **Reminder loses function after due completion.** `ذ ك ر` B009 is admonition; `ء ن ي` B003 is due completion and B005 asks how/whence. Repeated arrival closes a threshold; I infer that reminder guides only before the event completes its approach.
### Retrospective surprises
47:31’s ongoing test and 47:37’s pressure on wealth identify present forerunners inside ordinary action, correcting a reading that confines the Hour’s signs to spectacle.

## 47:19 — فَٱعْلَمْ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَٱسْتَغْفِرْ لِذَنۢبِكَ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَٱلْمُؤْمِنَٰتِ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مُتَقَلَّبَكُمْ وَمَثْوَىٰكُمْ
### Activated readings
1. **Knowledge becomes mark and shelter.** `ع ل م` B001/B002 is clear knowing and distinguishing mark; `غ ف ر` B001/B002 is protective covering and shielding. Knowing followed by seeking protection for the community is supplied; I infer that recognized unity marks practice through protective concern.
2. **Every turning is read against lodging.** `ق ل ب` B004/B005 is reversal and destination; `ث و ي` B001/B002 is settled lodging. Movement and rest are paired objects of knowledge; I infer a complete map of trajectories and endpoints.
3. **Sin trails action.** `ذ ن ب` B001 is culpable consequence and B002 a tail. Positioned between knowledge and intercession, it activates what remains attached behind action unless covered.
