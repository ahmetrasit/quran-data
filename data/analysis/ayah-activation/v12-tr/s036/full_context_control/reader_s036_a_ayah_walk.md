# Surah 36 Full-Context Ayah Walk

## 36:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Protective naming.** Lexically, `س م و` B005 makes *ism* a raised designation, while `ر ح م` B001 supplies mercy and tenderness. Structurally, the named mercy recurs at 36:5, 36:11, 36:23, 36:44, 36:52, 36:58, and 36:83 closes with comprehensive dominion. I infer that placing an act under a repeatedly disclosed name makes the name govern how the act is received. The opening thus reads not merely as identification but as entry into a mercy-framed order.
2. **Generative enclosure.** `ر ح م` B003 supplies the womb image alongside B001 mercy. The surah repeatedly moves from enclosed or inert states to emergence: dead earth produces grain (36:33), hidden graves release people (36:51), and a drop becomes an articulate opponent (36:77). The packet supplies those transitions; I infer that womb-like enclosure is their enabling model. The doubled mercy can therefore be heard as life-bearing containment as well as tenderness.

### Retrospective surprises

36:51's emergence from graves and 36:77's development from a drop made the womb branch of `ر ح م` more than an opening ornament: mercy became a recurrent containment-to-life mechanism.

## 36:2 — وَٱلْقُرْءَانِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Recitation as gathered and regulated motion.** `ق ر ء` B001 gathers speech into recitation and B011 supplies a directed course; `ح ك م` B001 restrains in order to set right and B004 makes firm and decisive. The later sequence of measured celestial courses (36:38-40) and the final effortless command (36:82) gives the pairing a functional analogue. I infer that the recitation's wisdom operates by gathering potentially scattered signs into a bounded course. The ayah reads as an oath by discourse that actively orders, not only discourse possessing wisdom.
2. **Decisive adjudication.** `ح ك م` B002 activates judgment among people. The packet later separates companions of the garden from criminals (36:55-64) and makes limbs testify (36:65). I supply the arrow that the recitation announces and structures that adjudication. *Al-hakim* therefore remains both wisely formed and judgment-bearing.

### Retrospective surprises

36:38-40 unexpectedly supplied a physical analogue for `ق ر ء` B011 and `ح ك م` B001: gathered discourse behaves like bounded motion whose restraint preserves its course.

## 36:3 — إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Dispatch within a succession.** `ر س ل` B002 gives messenger/message, while B005 gives succession in batches. The singular addressee is grammatically placed among a plural class; 36:13-17 then stages two envoys reinforced by a third, and 36:30 generalizes recurring messengers. I infer that repeated dispatch is the packet's continuity mechanism. The ayah reads not only as certification of one messenger but as his insertion into a recurrent relay.
2. **Release that must move.** `ر س ل` B001 supplies being sent forth and released, sharpened by the straight course of 36:4 and the runner from the city's farthest edge in 36:20. I infer that release entails outward passage toward recipients. The assertion assigns kinetic obligation, not a static title.

### Retrospective surprises

36:69-70 returned to recitation and warning, confirming that the messenger's placement in a succession is also the means by which the opening oath reaches living recipients.

## 36:4 — عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٍۢ مُّسْتَقِيمٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **A traversable path held upright.** `ص ر ط` B001 supplies the road; `ق و م` B008 supplies straightness and evenness, while B004 adds care and preservation. The preposition *on* makes the messenger's position relational, and 36:61 repeats the exact path formula as the commanded worship relation. I infer that the path remains straight because it is maintained by that relation. The ayah reads as stable placement on a preserved route, not merely possession of correct ideas.
2. **Passage that absorbs the traveler.** Exploratorily, `ص ر ط` B002 supplies disappearance in passage or swallowing. The later contrasts between emergence from graves (36:51), motion along courses (36:38-40), and return to God (36:83) make total passage a visible spatial mechanism. I infer that a true route can claim and carry the whole traveler. This adds a demanding reading: straightness is comprehensive transit, not detached orientation.

### Retrospective surprises

36:61's exact path formula corrected any purely spatial reading: the path is constituted by directed worship. 36:66 then showed that merely racing toward it without sight does not amount to guidance.

## 36:5 — تَنزِيلَ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Merciful force descending into reach.** `ن ز ل` B002 supplies bringing down; `ع ز ز` B001 gives might and B003 inaccessibility; `ر ح م` B001 gives mercy. The juxtaposition makes descent bridge inaccessibility, while 36:44 names mercy as the exception that preserves from drowning. I infer that mercy directs otherwise inaccessible power toward recipients. The ayah reads as power made reachable without ceasing to be power.
2. **Ordered placement.** `ن ز ل` B004 supplies placing in proper order, reinforced by measured lunar stations in 36:39 and comprehensive enumeration in 36:12. I infer that the descending discourse is installed in an ordered sequence. *Tanzil* thus names arrangement as well as vertical descent.

### Retrospective surprises

36:28's emphatic refusal to send down an army sharpened the special character of this descent: what descends in 36:5 is ordered, merciful communication rather than coercive mass.

## 36:6 — لِتُنذِرَ قَوْمًۭا مَّآ أُنذِرَ ءَابَآؤُهُمْ فَهُمْ غَٰفِلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Warning repairs an unmarked inheritance.** `ن ذ ر` B001 is fear-awakening warning; `ء ب و` B001 is parental nurture; `غ ف ل` B002 includes leaving something unwritten or unmarked. The ayah links present inattention to the interruption of ancestral warning, while 36:12 later records deeds and traces. I infer that warning restores a missing intergenerational mark. The people read as heirs to an informational break, not simply inattentive individuals.
2. **Heedlessness as managed exclusion.** `غ ف ل` B004 permits deliberate or studied disregard, activated by later repeated turning aside from signs (36:46) and argumentative deflection (36:47-48, 36:77-78). I infer that sustained rejection converts inherited unawareness into practiced avoidance. The fixed ayah preserves both passive lapse and an incipient active refusal.

### Retrospective surprises

36:12's writing of deeds and traces made `غ ف ل` B002's unmarked branch unexpectedly concrete, while 36:60's recalled ancestral covenant showed how a neglected inheritance can later be reactivated.

## 36:7 — لَقَدْ حَقَّ ٱلْقَوْلُ عَلَىٰٓ أَكْثَرِهِمْ فَهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Speech becomes an incurred due.** `ح ق ق` B002 is due obligation or deserved claim, B005 makes truth stand, and `ق و ل` B001 is uttered speech. The preposition *upon* construes the saying as a burden now attached to them; 36:70 repeats the formula after warning the living. I infer that persistent non-assent is what makes announced speech become an enforceable due. The ayah reads as consequence crystallizing from response, not as bare description.
2. **Security refused.** `ء م ن` B001 supplies settled security and trust, while B002 supplies trusting assent. The barriers and blindness of 36:8-9 provide the bodily result of refusal. I infer a reciprocal arrow: rejecting assent leaves them outside security and stabilizes their defensive enclosure. *They do not believe* therefore also reads as *they do not enter trust*.

### Retrospective surprises

36:70 repeats the established saying after the discourse has warned the living; this later placement clarified that the saying becomes due through sustained covering, not as an isolated opening assertion.

## 36:8 — إِنَّا جَعَلْنَا فِىٓ أَعْنَٰقِهِمْ أَغْلَٰلًۭا فَهِىَ إِلَى ٱلْأَذْقَانِ فَهُم مُّقْمَحُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Restraint fixes receptivity away from its source.** `غ ل ل` B006 is a collar binding a limb, `ذ ق ن` B006 is support under the chin, and `ق م ح` B001 is raising the head away from water. The upward chain reaches the chin and produces the stated posture; 36:33-35 and 36:73 later make water and nourishment signs of provision. I infer that the posture blocks taking in what sustains. The image reads as engineered non-reception, not generic punishment.
2. **Hidden hostility becomes bodily apparatus.** `غ ل ل` B005 is rancor hidden in the chest, while `ج ع ل` B002 turns something into a state. Later secret and public contents are both known (36:76), and mouths are sealed while limbs disclose acquisition (36:65). I infer that inward rancor is externalized as a constraining posture. The collars can therefore materialize an internal refusal without replacing their literal binding image.

### Retrospective surprises

36:73's drinks unexpectedly returned to `ق م ح` B001: the raised head is specifically a posture away from provision. 36:65 also confirmed the conversion of inward states into bodily evidence.

## 36:9 — وَجَعَلْنَا مِنۢ بَيْنِ أَيْدِيهِمْ سَدًّۭا وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ سَدًّۭا فَأَغْشَيْنَٰهُمْ فَهُمْ لَا يُبْصِرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **A closed temporal field.** `ي د ي` B008 supplies what lies before the hands; `خ ل ف` B001 supplies what follows and replaces; `س د د` B001 closes breaches; `غ ش و` B001 covers with a veil. The paired front/back barriers encompass prospective and inherited directions, later echoed by the warning concerning what is before and behind in 36:45. I infer that closing both directions removes corrective comparison across time. The ayah reads as temporal enclosure as well as spatial entrapment.
2. **Blocked insight.** `ب ص ر` B002 is inner sight, while `س د د` B008 is a defect blocking speech or answer. Later signs repeatedly arrive but receive only aversion (36:46) or dispute (36:77-78). I infer that the barrier blocks the transition from sign to responsive articulation. *They do not see* retains ocular blindness while extending to disabled discernment.

### Retrospective surprises

36:45 reverses the same front/back field into an object of protective caution, and 36:67 translates front/back barriers into lost capacity to advance or return. The image thus moved from obstruction to a full model of agency.

## 36:10 — وَسَوَآءٌ عَلَيْهِمْ ءَأَنذَرْتَهُمْ أَمْ لَمْ تُنذِرْهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Equality produced by a closed receiver.** `س و ي` B001 gives equivalence, `ن ذ ر` B001 warning, and `ء م ن` B002 trusting assent. The alternatives differ at the sender but converge at the unreceptive audience after 36:8-9. I infer that their enclosure, not the warning's content, supplies the equalizing arrow. The ayah reads as diagnostic equivalence at the point of reception, not equivalence in the acts themselves.
2. **A completed, sealed interior.** `س و ي` B002 is internal straightness or completed formation, ironically activated by the collars and barriers. I infer that resistance has settled into a self-consistent internal arrangement. This makes *equal upon them* also expose the rigidity that prevents warning from creating difference.

### Retrospective surprises

36:46's repeated turning away showed how apparently different arriving signs are actively made equivalent at reception; the equalizing condition is sustained aversion.

## 36:11 — إِنَّمَا تُنذِرُ مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلذِّكْرَ وَخَشِىَ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنَ بِٱلْغَيْبِ ۖ فَبَشِّرْهُ بِمَغْفِرَةٍۢ وَأَجْرٍۢ كَرِيمٍ

### Activated readings

1. **Warning works as trackable sequence.** `ت ب ع` B003 is stepwise tracking, `ذ ك ر` B009 reminder/admonition, and `خ ش ي` B001 reverent fear. The grammar orders following, awe in hiddenness, then brightening news; 36:20-27 embodies the same movement in the city man. I infer that warning becomes effective by being enacted step by step. The ayah reads as a reception pathway rather than a simple audience restriction.
2. **Covering that preserves future value.** `غ ف ر` B001 is protective covering and B002 shields from consequence; `ء ج ر` B001 is recompense; `ك ر م` B010 is something highly valued. The later resurrection and exact recompense (36:51-54) activate a preservation mechanism: what is covered from destructive consequence remains available for valued return. I supply that causal link. Forgiveness reads as protective conservation, not mere erasure.
3. **Hidden fear disclosed on the face.** `غ ي ب` B001 is hiddenness; `ب ش ر` B005 is face-brightening tidings. The sequence moves from an unseen inward relation to a visible surface effect. I infer that good news externalizes a hidden orientation. The ayah thus links interior awe and outward transformation without collapsing them.

### Retrospective surprises

The city man's sequence in 36:20-27 supplied an enacted instance of following, hidden trust, public declaration, forgiveness, and honor. 36:58 then completed good news as merciful speech that establishes peace.

## 36:12 — إِنَّا نَحْنُ نُحْىِ ٱلْمَوْتَىٰ وَنَكْتُبُ مَا قَدَّمُوا۟ وَءَاثَٰرَهُمْ ۚ وَكُلَّ شَىْءٍ أَحْصَيْنَٰهُ فِىٓ إِمَامٍۢ مُّبِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **The record joins act to surviving trace.** `ك ت ب` B001 is joining and B002 joins letters into writing; `ق د م` B004 is forward precedence; `ء ث ر` B003 is a remaining sign pointing to what was. The coordination explicitly binds prior deeds and aftereffects, while 36:65 later lets limbs testify to acquisition. I infer that inscription preserves causal continuity between an act and what outlives it. The writing reads as relational accounting, not a flat list.
2. **A guiding matrix, not only a ledger.** `ح ص ي` B002 counts until encompassing; `ء م م` B002 is source and reference, while B009 is a followed guide; `ب ي ن` B004 makes clear. The packet later displays measured courses (36:38-40) and exact recompense (36:54). I infer that comprehensive enumeration serves both retrospective record and prospective ordering. The clear *imam* can therefore be read as an encompassing reference-pattern.
3. **Revival reactivates apparently dead effects.** `ح ي ي` B003 is animate life and B013 life as saving benefit; `م و ت` B012 is still inactivity. Because traces are written after mention of reviving the dead, I infer that resurrection also makes dormant consequences operative. The ayah reads as reactivation of persons and their causal histories.

### Retrospective surprises

36:65's hand-and-foot testimony made the record bodily, and 36:79's comprehensive knowledge supplied a mechanism for preserving created measure through decay. The *imam* became both ledger and recoverable pattern.

## 36:13 — وَٱضْرِبْ لَهُم مَّثَلًا أَصْحَٰبَ ٱلْقَرْيَةِ إِذْ جَآءَهَا ٱلْمُرْسَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **An example designed to make impact.** `ض ر ب` B003 is setting forth an example, but B001 is making an impact; `م ث ل` B011 is a lesson or sign. The imperative and the following enacted narrative turn comparison into intervention. I infer that the example is meant to strike the present audience's posture established in 36:8-10. The ayah reads as applied diagnostic force, not decorative storytelling.
2. **Arrival tests gathered social order.** `ق ر ي` B001 is people gathered in a settlement and B006 a common course; `ج ي ء` B001 is arrival, and `ر س ل` B005 is successive dispatch. The envoys enter an already coordinated collectivity. I infer that arrival exposes whether a common social course can admit correction. The village becomes a model of group-level reception.

### Retrospective surprises

36:78's opponent also *strikes an example*, but forgets his own creation. That later reversal showed that an example can either disrupt closure or reproduce it depending on what evidence it admits.

## 36:14 — إِذْ أَرْسَلْنَآ إِلَيْهِمُ ٱثْنَيْنِ فَكَذَّبُوهُمَا فَعَزَّزْنَا بِثَالِثٍۢ فَقَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّآ إِلَيْكُم مُّرْسَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Rejection triggers reinforcement, not novelty.** `ث ن ي` B001 makes two by joining one to one; `ع ز ز` B004 reinforces and strengthens; `ث ل ث` B003 makes a third; `ر س ل` B008 gives reciprocal sending/matching. The sequence is dispatch, denial, reinforcement, renewed declaration. The packet supplies the sequence; I infer that added witness answers rejection by strengthening the same message rather than replacing it. The ayah reads as resilient corroboration.
2. **False-declaration reverses onto the accusers.** `ك ذ ب` B002 is declaring another false, while B001 is falsehood against truth. In 36:15 the townspeople move from declaring the envoys false to accusing them of active lying. I infer that refusal shifts from evaluating a claim to assigning corrupt agency. The fixed ayah begins a visible escalation in social conflict.

### Retrospective surprises

36:53's one cry gathering everyone retrospectively magnified the economy already latent here: numerical reinforcement is appropriate to human witness, while divine command later needs no accumulated force.

## 36:15 — قَالُوا۟ مَآ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا بَشَرٌۭ مِّثْلُنَا وَمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنُ مِن شَىْءٍ إِنْ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا تَكْذِبُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Visible likeness is used to deny vertical mediation.** `ب ش ر` B002 is the visibly skinned human, `م ث ل` B001 likeness, and `ن ز ل` B002 bringing down. The speakers infer from horizontal bodily similarity that no descent can occur through these men. That directional arrow is theirs, not supplied by the packet; 36:79-81 later counters it by reasoning from creation to renewed creation. The ayah reads as a category error that makes visible sameness cancel unseen commissioning.
2. **Mercy is denied precisely as transmission.** `ر ح م` B001 is mercy and B003 womb; `ش ي ء` B001 is a knowable thing. The denial encloses *anything* under what the Merciful did not send, while later mercy preserves life amid danger (36:44) and speaks peace (36:58). I infer that their claim blocks mercy from taking transmissible form. This sharpens the denial from factual skepticism to refusal of mediated mercy.

### Retrospective surprises

36:77-81 overturned the inference from visible human form by tracing that form to a drop and then to the creator of heaven and earth. Bodily likeness became evidence for mediation and recreation rather than against them.

## 36:16 — قَالُوا۟ رَبُّنَا يَعْلَمُ إِنَّآ إِلَيْكُمْ لَمُرْسَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Knowledge replaces failed social validation.** `ر ب ب` B001 supplies lordship and authority, B002 nurture and completion; `ع ل م` B001 is knowing as clear; `ر س ل` B002 is messenger/message. After human denial, the envoys appeal to a knowing authority rather than escalating proof. I infer that divine knowledge secures the commission independently of audience recognition. The ayah reads as re-anchoring authorization outside the contested social field.
2. **The destination remains constitutive.** The emphatic *to you* is paired with `ر س ل` B001, being sent forth, and later 36:17 limits duty to clear conveyance. I infer that dispatch is defined by directed arrival even when reception fails. The statement therefore retains the rejecters as true addressees without making acceptance a condition of mission.

### Retrospective surprises

36:76's knowledge of hidden and public contents strengthened the appeal to divine knowledge: the commission is secured by a field of knowing that also contains the rejecters' unspoken motives.

## 36:17 — وَمَا عَلَيْنَآ إِلَّا ٱلْبَلَٰغُ ٱلْمُبِينُ

### Activated readings

1. **Duty ends at achieved conveyance.** `ب ل غ` B001 is reaching a limit and B002 conveying a message; `ب ي ن` B005 discloses meaning by expression. The restrictive construction sets a boundary around responsibility after the audience's denial. I infer that clarity is the criterion by which conveyance reaches its proper endpoint. The ayah reads as a jurisdictional limit, not indifference to outcome.
2. **Clarity makes the message cross the interval.** `ب ي ن` B002 supplies the interval between things and B004 appearance. Against the social gap opened in 36:15, I infer that lucid communication traverses separation even if assent does not follow. *Mubin* describes the message's gap-crossing function as well as its evident content.

### Retrospective surprises

36:69's *clear recitation* and 36:70's bifurcating warning clarified that clear conveyance reaches its own limit even when its effects divide between living response and incurred consequence.

## 36:18 — قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّا تَطَيَّرْنَا بِكُمْ ۖ لَئِن لَّمْ تَنتَهُوا۟ لَنَرْجُمَنَّكُمْ وَلَيَمَسَّنَّكُم مِّنَّا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Projected omen becomes coercive contact.** `ط ي ر` B001 supplies bird-flight and B005 unleashed recklessness; `ن ه ي` B001 restrains from action; `ر ج م` B002 is hurtful speech like stoning and B003 expulsion; `م س س` B001 is contact. The townspeople convert an interpreted sign into a demand for silence and then into threatened bodily contact. I infer that projection authorizes coercion by relocating their disorder onto the envoys. The ayah reads as an escalation mechanism from omen-making to violence.
2. **Punishment is claimed as local possession.** `ع ذ ب` B005 is torment, `ء ل م` B002 painful causation, and the phrase *from us* assigns its source. Later the single cry comes from beyond their control (36:29), reversing the direction of punitive agency. The threat therefore reads as an attempted monopoly over who may inflict consequence.

### Retrospective surprises

The single cry of 36:29 reverses their threatened direction of pain without army or prolonged contact. Their claimed punitive agency is exposed as local speech overtaken by a more concentrated act.

## 36:19 — قَالُوا۟ طَٰٓئِرُكُم مَّعَكُمْ ۚ أَئِن ذُكِّرْتُم ۚ بَلْ أَنتُمْ قَوْمٌۭ مُّسْرِفُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **The sign is returned to its producer.** `ط ي ر` B002 permits spreading loose; `ذ ك ر` B009 is admonition; `س ر ف` B001 is overstepping the limit. The reply reverses the townspeople's outward projection: their *bird* remains with them, especially when reminder exposes their excess. I infer that the omen is the spread effect of their own conduct. The ayah reads as causal reassignment from messenger to audience.
2. **Reminder reveals missed limits.** `س ر ف` B002 includes missing or overlooking, while `ذ ك ر` B003 brings a thing back to mind. The conditional question places reminder at the point where their overlooked boundary could become visible. I infer that their violent response is evidence of the very excess recalled to them. The ayah diagnoses rejection as self-disclosing.

### Retrospective surprises

36:47's theological excuse and 36:77-78's dispute showed further forms of the same excess: reminder is resisted by exporting responsibility outward while the person's own causal history remains *with him*.

## 36:20 — وَجَآءَ مِنْ أَقْصَا ٱلْمَدِينَةِ رَجُلٌۭ يَسْعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰقَوْمِ ٱتَّبِعُوا۟ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Peripheral insight crosses the city.** `ق ص و` B001 is remoteness, `م د ن` B001 the fortified settlement, `ر ج ل` B003 on-foot movement, and `س ع ي` B001 purposeful brisk going. The man travels from the spatial margin into the conflict center. I infer that distance from the city's coordinated refusal enables a corrective trajectory, though the packet only supplies his origin and motion. The ayah reads as peripheral witness actively penetrating collective closure.
2. **Following is enacted before it is commanded.** `ت ب ع` B001 follows after and B003 tracks stepwise; `ر س ل` B001 is sending forth. His own purposeful arrival mirrors the envoys' directed movement before he says *follow*. I infer that embodied imitation gives his imperative force. The ayah reads as performative advocacy, not detached advice.

### Retrospective surprises

36:66's futile race for the path made this man's `س ع ي` look more precise: his brisk motion succeeds because it is already oriented by hearing and following, not because motion itself guarantees arrival.
## 36:21 — ٱتَّبِعُوا۟ مَن لَّا يَسْـَٔلُكُمْ أَجْرًۭا وَهُم مُّهْتَدُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Unpaid guidance breaks transactional suspicion.** `ت ب ع` B001 is following, `س ء ل` B001 asking, `ء ج ر` B001 recompense for work, and `ه د ي` B001 guidance toward a way. The negated request removes private extraction from the relation between guide and follower. I infer that freedom from audience payment is evidence that the guides' direction is not bent toward gain. The ayah reads as an economic test of trustworthy guidance.
2. **Guides are themselves moving on a course.** `ه د ي` B002 supplies course and intended direction, while the plural participle describes the envoys rather than only their audience. With 36:4's straight path and 36:20's enacted following, I infer that one follows them because they are already being led. Guidance reads as transmitted orientation, not personal possession.

### Retrospective surprises

36:74-75 later inverted the economic test: supposed gods are taken for aid but cannot provide it, whereas these messengers ask no recompense and still offer direction.

## 36:22 — وَمَا لِىَ لَآ أَعْبُدُ ٱلَّذِى فَطَرَنِى وَإِلَيْهِ تُرْجَعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Worship answers origination with return.** `ع ب د` B003 is worshipful submission, `ف ط ر` B001 opening/splitting and B002 initiating existence, and `ر ج ع` B001 return. The first-person origin is paired with the audience's second-person return, producing a shared trajectory despite the pronoun shift. I infer that the originator's claim persists through the creature's entire arc. Worship reads as alignment of beginning and destination.
2. **Creation opens what return closes.** `ف ط ر` B001 supplies splitting open, while `ر ج ع` B001 reverses movement. Later the graves open into swift movement (36:51) and worn bones are revived (36:78-79). I infer a reversible material logic: the one who first opens existence can reopen its closed state. The ayah quietly carries resurrection force before the explicit challenge.

### Retrospective surprises

36:51's grave-exit and 36:79's appeal to first origination made the opening/return structure materially explicit: return is a reopening by the same originating agency.
