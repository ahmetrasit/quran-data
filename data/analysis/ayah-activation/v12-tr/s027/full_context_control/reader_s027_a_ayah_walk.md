# Surah 27 Full-Context Reader: Ayah Walk

## 27:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. `س م و/B005` supplies the image of a name as a raised designation, while `ر ح م/B002` and `ر ح م/B003` supply kinship and womb. The formula therefore does more than label an action: it places the action under a publicly raised designation whose governing quality is womb-like attachment. The packet supplies designation and kinship; I infer that naming establishes the action's relational enclosure.

### Retrospective surprises

27:30 repeats the formula inside royal correspondence, sharpening the opening as an authority-bearing relational frame.

## 27:1 — طسٓ ۚ تِلْكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلْقُرْءَانِ وَكِتَابٍۢ مُّبِينٍ

### Activated readings

1. `ء ي ي/B003` makes the ayat visible signs, `ق ر ء/B001` makes the Qur'an a gathering of speech, and `ك ت ب/B001` makes the book a joining of parts. Their sequence turns the line into an information architecture: discrete signs are gathered in recitation and joined in inscription, while `ب ي ن/B005` assigns that architecture the role of disclosing meaning. I infer that clarity arises through ordered collection, not isolated display.

### Retrospective surprises

27:75 and 27:92 separate inscription, recitation, and disclosure, confirming that the opening joins media without flattening them.

## 27:2 — هُدًۭى وَبُشْرَىٰ لِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ

### Activated readings

1. `ه د ي/B001` gives gentle direction toward a way; `ب ش ر/B007` gives first visible signs or forerunners; `ء م ن/B001` gives settled security. The pair reads as a temporal mechanism: guidance sets a course, while good tidings let its destination become visible in advance to those already capable of trust. The packet supplies direction, advance signs, and security; I infer that anticipation stabilizes continued movement.

### Retrospective surprises

27:63 makes winds advance signs of mercy, and 27:77 repeats guidance and mercy directly; both sharpen the anticipatory reading.

## 27:3 — ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤْتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُم بِٱلْءَاخِرَةِ هُمْ يُوقِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. In `يُقِيمُونَ`, `ق و م/B004` activates care, guardianship, and preservation alongside standing. Prayer is thus maintained as an institution requiring custodial continuity, while `ز ك و/B001` makes the transferred zakah productive growth and `ي ق ن/B001` makes the later end settled knowledge. I infer a feedback loop: sustained worship and circulating increase make future accountability operatively present.

### Retrospective surprises

27:82 names lack of certainty about signs, making this early ritual-certainty linkage a deliberate frame for the surah.

## 27:4 — إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ بِٱلْءَاخِرَةِ زَيَّنَّا لَهُمْ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ فَهُمْ يَعْمَهُونَ

### Activated readings

1. `ز ي ن/B002` is the making of beauty visible, while `ع م ه/B001` is bewildered wavering without guidance. The causal sequence becomes perceptual: deeds are given an attractive visible surface, and attachment to that surface prevents stable orientation toward their later consequence (`ء خ ر/B001`). The packet supplies display, action, lateness, and wavering; I infer that surface evaluation blocks temporal depth.

### Retrospective surprises

27:24 repeats adornment, blocked guidance, and deed-language at communal scale, revealing 27:4 as the general mechanism later dramatized in Saba.

## 27:5 — أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَهُمْ سُوٓءُ ٱلْعَذَابِ وَهُمْ فِى ٱلْءَاخِرَةِ هُمُ ٱلْأَخْسَرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. `خ س ر/B002` activates trade loss, sharpening the superlative beyond generic deficiency. Their embellished works in 27:4 function like mispriced assets; at the later settlement (`ء خ ر/B001`), the apparent gain is exposed as the greatest loss. `ع ذ ب/B003` also supplies withholding and weaning away. I infer a terminal accounting in which false valuation ends in deprivation from what had seemed profitable.

### Retrospective surprises

27:89-90 stages asymmetric recompense, sharpening the loss here as final valuation rather than immediate pain alone.

## 27:6 — وَإِنَّكَ لَتُلَقَّى ٱلْقُرْءَانَ مِن لَّدُنْ حَكِيمٍ عَلِيمٍ

### Activated readings

1. `ل ق ي/B011` makes reception an imparting of speech, and `ل د ن/B002` marks a proximal start-boundary. The Qur'an is therefore not merely encountered but transmitted from a near point of origin. `ح ك م/B001` assigns restraint and correction to its wisdom, while `ع ل م/B002` makes knowledge a distinguishing mark. I infer a controlled transfer whose source governs both content and discriminating function.

### Retrospective surprises

27:76-79 connects Qur'anic recounting, judgment, disclosed truth, and reliance, expanding controlled transfer into communal effects.

## 27:7 — إِذْ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِأَهْلِهِۦٓ إِنِّىٓ ءَانَسْتُ نَارًۭا سَـَٔاتِيكُم مِّنْهَا بِخَبَرٍ أَوْ ءَاتِيكُم بِشِهَابٍۢ قَبَسٍۢ لَّعَلَّكُمْ تَصْطَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. `ء ن س/B002` makes the fire something perceived into awareness, not yet fully identified. Moses proposes two outputs: tested/report knowledge (`خ ب ر/B001`) or a flame physically taken from a larger fire (`ق ب س/B001`). The alternatives make distant visibility into either information or portable heat. I infer that approach is driven by converting an ambiguous sign into something the household can use.

2. `ق ب س/B002` also permits borrowed light of knowledge or guidance, while `ص ل ي/B003` supplies encounter with heat. This preserves a second reading in which the promised material ember already prefigures transmissible guidance; the weak link is analogical, but later speech from the fire makes it visible.

### Retrospective surprises

27:8-9 confirms the exploratory light-of-knowledge reading: the approached fire yields an identifying call rather than merely portable heat.

## 27:8 — فَلَمَّا جَآءَهَا نُودِىَ أَنۢ بُورِكَ مَن فِى ٱلنَّارِ وَمَنْ حَوْلَهَا وَسُبْحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

### Activated readings

1. `ب ر ك/B004` is fixed, growing good, and `ح و ل/B004` is the space around a thing. The blessing is spatially distributed across center and circumference, then `س ب ح/B002` prevents that spatial manifestation from enclosing God. I infer a two-step correction: holiness saturates the site, but transcendence blocks identification of the Lord with the bounded phenomenon.

### Retrospective surprises

27:26 names God as Lord of the great throne, sharpening the distinction between a bounded manifestation and the Lord who exceeds every support of power.

## 27:9 — يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ

### Activated readings

1. `ع ز ز/B002` supplies overcoming and subduing, while `ح ك م/B001` supplies restraint that turns back and sets right. Their apposition makes power self-governing: the speaker can overcome, but power arrives already bridled toward correction. I infer that the identification resolves the fire's ambiguity by naming not raw force but force constrained by right order.

### Retrospective surprises

27:78 repeats might with judgment and knowledge, confirming that power throughout the surah is read through informed restraint.

## 27:10 — وَأَلْقِ عَصَاكَ ۚ فَلَمَّا رَءَاهَا تَهْتَزُّ كَأَنَّهَا جَآنٌّۭ وَلَّىٰ مُدْبِرًۭا وَلَمْ يُعَقِّبْ ۚ يَٰمُوسَىٰ لَا تَخَفْ إِنِّى لَا يَخَافُ لَدَىَّ ٱلْمُرْسَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. The staff's `ع ص و/B004` governance/discipline role is reversed by `ه ز ز/B001`, which shakes it into motion, and `ج ن ن/B001`, concealment, in the snake-like form. Moses then enacts `د ب ر/B003`, turning his back, without `ع ق ب/B008`, following up to challenge. I infer that the sign first dismantles the messenger's reliance on a governing instrument before restoring security near the sender.

### Retrospective surprises

27:44 makes accurate sight generate a false material inference, revealing this first fear-response as an opening instance of perception requiring correction.

## 27:11 — إِلَّا مَن ظَلَمَ ثُمَّ بَدَّلَ حُسْنًۢا بَعْدَ سُوٓءٍۢ فَإِنِّى غَفُورٌۭ رَّحِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. `ب د ل/B001` (substitution/change) is structurally bounded by badness before and good after (`س و ء/B001`, `ح س ن/B001`). The exception therefore does not leave wrongdoing as a permanent messenger identity; it defines a reversible sequence. `غ ف ر/B001` supplies covering protection and `ر ح م/B001` tenderness. I infer that moral replacement, not an untouched past, is the condition through which fear is removed.

### Retrospective surprises

27:44 pairs the queen's self-wronging with submission, giving this early replacement sequence a later political and perceptual enactment.

## 27:12 — وَأَدْخِلْ يَدَكَ فِى جَيْبِكَ تَخْرُجْ بَيْضَآءَ مِنْ غَيْرِ سُوٓءٍۢ ۖ فِى تِسْعِ ءَايَٰتٍ إِلَىٰ فِرْعَوْنَ وَقَوْمِهِۦٓ ۚ إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا۟ قَوْمًۭا فَٰسِقِينَ

### Activated readings

1. `د خ ل/B001` and `خ ر ج/B001` form an inward/outward reversal: the hand enters concealment and exits visibly altered. `ب ي ض/B002` supplies white brightness of face and honor, while "without harm" excludes `س و ء/B003`, affliction or leprosy. I infer that the sign's force lies in controlled transformation whose visible brightness cannot be dismissed as bodily damage.

### Retrospective surprises

27:13-14 shows that controlled visible transformation can still be relabeled magic despite inward certainty, correcting any assumption that visibility compels assent.

## 27:13 — فَلَمَّا جَآءَتْهُمْ ءَايَٰتُنَا مُبْصِرَةًۭ قَالُوا۟ هَٰذَا سِحْرٌۭ مُّبِينٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. `ب ص ر/B002` gives inner sight to the signs, while `س ح ر/B002` gives diversion and deception. The signs arrive as agents that enable seeing, yet the audience redescribes that enablement as manipulated perception. Repeated `ب ي ن/B005` from the surah's opening makes the clash sharper: disclosure itself is labeled deception. I infer a defensive reversal in which evidence is neutralized by reclassifying its perceptual effect.

### Retrospective surprises

27:41-44 develops explicit recognition tests, making the conflict here between sight-giving sign and deceptive relabeling a recurring epistemic pattern.

## 27:14 — وَجَحَدُوا۟ بِهَا وَٱسْتَيْقَنَتْهَآ أَنفُسُهُمْ ظُلْمًۭا وَعُلُوًّۭا ۚ فَٱنظُرْ كَيْفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلْمُفْسِدِينَ

### Activated readings

1. `ج ح د/B001` is knowing denial, explicitly paired with `ي ق ن/B001`, settled knowledge. This removes ignorance as the causal gap. `ن ف س/B013` locates recognition in hidden intent, while `ع ل و/B003` assigns haughty domination to its suppression. I infer that injustice converts inward certainty into outward refusal; `ع ق ب/B006` then makes the commanded look an inspection of that mechanism's outcome.

### Retrospective surprises

27:84 condemns declaring signs false without encompassing them in knowledge, retrospectively turning this knowing denial into the model procedural failure.

## 27:15 — وَلَقَدْ ءَاتَيْنَا دَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيْمَٰنَ عِلْمًۭا ۖ وَقَالَا ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِى فَضَّلَنَا عَلَىٰ كَثِيرٍۢ مِّنْ عِبَادِهِ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ

### Activated readings

1. `ع ل م/B002` makes granted knowledge a distinguishing mark, and `ف ض ل/B001` makes preference an excess or remainder rather than self-generated rank. Their praise (`ح م د/B001`) redirects that surplus to its giver. I infer an anti-hoarding mechanism: distinction remains legitimate only when read as received excess within, not separation from, the community of trusting servants.

### Retrospective surprises

27:40 makes surplus bounty a test of gratitude, sharpening this praise as the correct handling of received distinction.

## 27:16 — وَوَرِثَ سُلَيْمَٰنُ دَاوُۥدَ ۖ وَقَالَ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ عُلِّمْنَا مَنطِقَ ٱلطَّيْرِ وَأُوتِينَا مِن كُلِّ شَىْءٍ ۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلْفَضْلُ ٱلْمُبِينُ

### Activated readings

1. `و ر ث/B003` activates inherited knowledge or virtue, not only transferred possession. `ن ط ق/B002` allows speech-like indication, widening bird-language beyond human articulation, while `ط ي ر/B001` contributes airborne mobility. I infer that inheritance here is a capacity to decode distributed signs; the "clear bounty" (`ب ي ن/B005`, `ف ض ل/B003`) is a communicative faculty that makes nonhuman indications legible.

### Retrospective surprises

27:18 and 27:22 show nonhuman speech and observation correcting royal power, making inherited bird-language an instrument of accountable rule.

## 27:17 — وَحُشِرَ لِسُلَيْمَٰنَ جُنُودُهُۥ مِنَ ٱلْجِنِّ وَٱلْإِنسِ وَٱلطَّيْرِ فَهُمْ يُوزَعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. `ح ش ر/B001` is gathering and driving toward a destination; `ج ن د/B001` is a collected force of helpers; `و ز ع/B001` restrains and holds in order. The heterogeneous host is therefore not merely amassed but regulated against its own scale. I infer that restraint is the enabling cause of collective movement: ordered limits turn potentially overwhelming plurality into a functioning force.

### Retrospective surprises

27:83 repeats gathering and ordered restraint for deniers, transferring royal logistics into the machinery of judgment.

## 27:18 — حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَتَوْا۟ عَلَىٰ وَادِ ٱلنَّمْلِ قَالَتْ نَمْلَةٌۭ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّمْلُ ٱدْخُلُوا۟ مَسَٰكِنَكُمْ لَا يَحْطِمَنَّكُمْ سُلَيْمَٰنُ وَجُنُودُهُۥ وَهُمْ لَا يَشْعُرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. `و د ي/B005` presents the valley as a flood channel, while `ح ش ر/B001` and the approaching host give it a human torrent. The ants' settlements (`س ك ن/B002`) become refuges from `ح ط م/B001`, dry things crumbled by pressure. I infer a scale mechanism: disciplined power at the army's level can still become an unperceived flood at the ants' level.

2. `ش ع ر/B005` is subtle awareness. Its negation does not accuse the army of intended harm; it identifies the exact failure mode as damage beneath the threshold of perception. This preserves a reading about inadvertent structural violence distinct from deliberate aggression.

### Retrospective surprises

27:50 repeats lack of subtle awareness during a hidden reversal, confirming that unperceived harm and unperceived counteraction share one threshold problem.

## 27:19 — فَتَبَسَّمَ ضَاحِكًۭا مِّن قَوْلِهَا وَقَالَ رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِىٓ أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِىٓ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَىَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَىَّ وَأَنْ أَعْمَلَ صَٰلِحًۭا تَرْضَىٰهُ وَأَدْخِلْنِى بِرَحْمَتِكَ فِى عِبَادِكَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ

### Activated readings

1. The army's `و ز ع/B001` external restraint becomes, in `أَوْزِعْنِى`, `و ز ع/B002`, inward prompting and attachment. Solomon asks that command over others be mirrored as command within himself. `ش ك ر/B002` adds being sustained by little and showing its effect; the tiny ant's warning thus becomes enough to produce visible gratitude and sound action (`ص ل ح/B001`). I infer that attentive rule converts minute input into ethical self-restraint.

### Retrospective surprises

27:40 repeats gratitude under extraordinary power, showing that the ant's small warning and the transported throne test the same inward restraint.

## 27:20 — وَتَفَقَّدَ ٱلطَّيْرَ فَقَالَ مَا لِىَ لَآ أَرَى ٱلْهُدْهُدَ أَمْ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلْغَآئِبِينَ

### Activated readings

1. `ف ق د/B002` is seeking what is absent, while `غ ي ب/B001` is being hidden from sight or knowledge. The inspection distinguishes an unexplained local absence from membership among the absent. I infer an administrative diagnostic: before assigning fault, Solomon tests whether the gap lies in his perception (`ر ء ي/B001`), the formation's accounting, or the bird's actual withdrawal.

### Retrospective surprises

27:75 places every absent thing in a disclosing book, widening this local inspection into a contrast between limited and complete accounting.

## 27:21 — لَأُعَذِّبَنَّهُۥ عَذَابًۭا شَدِيدًا أَوْ لَأَا۟ذْبَحَنَّهُۥٓ أَوْ لَيَأْتِيَنِّى بِسُلْطَٰنٍۢ مُّبِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. The escalating alternatives move from punishment (`ع ذ ب/B005`) to throat-cutting (`ذ ب ح/B001`), but terminate in `س ل ط/B002`, overpowering proof. Evidence can therefore interrupt coercive force. `ب ي ن/B005` assigns disclosure to that proof. I infer a legal mechanism: severe authority remains defeasible when the absent party brings an explanation strong enough to master the initial presumption.

### Retrospective surprises

27:27 immediately enacts the proof clause as verification, confirming that threatened force is suspended by an evidentiary process.

## 27:22 — فَمَكَثَ غَيْرَ بَعِيدٍۢ فَقَالَ أَحَطتُ بِمَا لَمْ تُحِطْ بِهِۦ وَجِئْتُكَ مِن سَبَإٍۭ بِنَبَإٍۢ يَقِينٍ

### Activated readings

1. `م ك ث/B002` gives deliberate composure without haste, then `ح و ط/B004` gives complete grasp in knowledge. The hoopoe's counterclaim is epistemic, not merely geographic: the subordinate's range encloses what the ruler's does not. `ن ب ء/B002` is brought report and `ي ق ن/B001` settled knowledge. I infer that ordered authority is corrected by mobile peripheral observation.

### Retrospective surprises

27:84 repeats complete epistemic enclosure and indicts denial without it, making the hoopoe's claim a positive model of proportionate assertion.

## 27:23 — إِنِّى وَجَدتُّ ٱمْرَأَةًۭ تَمْلِكُهُمْ وَأُوتِيَتْ مِن كُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ وَلَهَا عَرْشٌ عَظِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. `م ل ك/B003` supplies kingship and dominion, while `ع ر ش/B006` supplies a standing support of power. The throne is not inert luxury but the visible infrastructure by which rule stands; `ع ظ م/B002` can make it the bulk or greater part of the report's political evidence. I infer that the observer reads material arrangement as a sign of institutional capacity.

### Retrospective surprises

27:38-44 detaches, alters, and interprets the throne, confirming that its material form is treated as political and cognitive infrastructure.

## 27:24 — وَجَدتُّهَا وَقَوْمَهَا يَسْجُدُونَ لِلشَّمْسِ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيْطَٰنُ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ فَصَدَّهُمْ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ فَهُمْ لَا يَهْتَدُونَ

### Activated readings

1. `ز ي ن/B002` again makes deeds display beauty, now with an explicit agent, while `ص د د/B005` activates a blocking mountain and `س ب ل/B001` an extended traversed way. I infer that adornment becomes topographical obstruction: attractive practice rises between a people and the road, so lack of guidance is not absence of a route but inability to see past a constructed barrier.

### Retrospective surprises

27:43 states that worship itself blocked the queen, confirming the inferred conversion of attractive practice into a route-obstructing structure.

## 27:25 — أَلَّا يَسْجُدُوا۟ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِى يُخْرِجُ ٱلْخَبْءَ فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَيَعْلَمُ مَا تُخْفُونَ وَمَا تُعْلِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. `خ ب ء/B007` activates hidden places of earth and cultivation, while `خ ر ج/B002` is causing something to emerge. This links cosmic disclosure to germination-like release: what is stored below becomes visible above. The human pair `خ ف ي/B001` and `ع ل ن/B001` repeats that hidden/manifest axis. I infer that prostration belongs to the power that controls transitions across concealment, not to the visible sun alone.

### Retrospective surprises

27:44 and 27:74-75 revisit concealed and manifest realities, expanding germination-like disclosure into perceptual and documentary disclosure.

## 27:26 — ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلْعَرْشِ ٱلْعَظِيمِ ۩

### Activated readings

1. Repetition of `ع ر ش/B006`, standing support of power, scales the queen's throne into a contrastive model. Her great throne supports a local dominion; God's lordship (`ر ب ب/B001`) owns the very support-system of power. I infer that the shared root invites comparison while the construction reverses dependence: political rule stands on a throne, but the ultimate throne stands under divine lordship.

### Retrospective surprises

27:38-44 moves the queen's throne while preserving divine lordship, sharpening the contrast between transferable political support and ultimate authority.

## 27:27 — ۞ قَالَ سَنَنظُرُ أَصَدَقْتَ أَمْ كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلْكَٰذِبِينَ

### Activated readings

1. `ن ظ ر/B010` activates examining a matter through mutual looking or debate, and the binary question places `ص د ق/B001` against `ك ذ ب/B001`. Solomon does not collapse the hoopoe's confident report into truth; he initiates a verification procedure. I infer that a claim's internal certainty becomes public knowledge only after an independent test links report to observable response.

### Retrospective surprises

27:41 names the throne experiment as a guidance test, confirming that looking here initiates a procedure rather than passive observation.

## 27:28 — ٱذْهَب بِّكِتَٰبِى هَٰذَا فَأَلْقِهْ إِلَيْهِمْ ثُمَّ تَوَلَّ عَنْهُمْ فَٱنظُرْ مَاذَا يَرْجِعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. `ك ت ب/B003` makes inscription a binding decree, but `ل ق ي/B005` casts it into the recipients' space and `و ل ي/B007` withdraws the courier. The sender deliberately removes coercive presence so the recipients can produce a return (`ر ج ع/B005`, answer or reply). I infer an experimental design: the letter tests the polity by letting its unprompted response reveal its internal order.

## 27:88 — وَتَرَى ٱلْجِبَالَ تَحْسَبُهَا جَامِدَةًۭ وَهِىَ تَمُرُّ مَرَّ ٱلسَّحَابِ ۚ صُنْعَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَتْقَنَ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ خَبِيرٌۢ بِمَا تَفْعَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. `ج ب ل/B001` is high solid mass and `ج م د/B001` liquid becoming solid, yet `م ر ر/B001` makes that apparent fixity pass like `س ح ب/B002`, drawn-moving cloud. The queen's optical misreckoning becomes cosmic: stable appearance can conceal motion. `ص ن ع/B002` and `ت ق ن/B001` assign artisan mastery and firmness to the paradox. I infer that perfected order includes movements too large or smooth for ordinary estimation.

## 27:89 — مَن جَآءَ بِٱلْحَسَنَةِ فَلَهُۥ خَيْرٌۭ مِّنْهَا وَهُم مِّن فَزَعٍۢ يَوْمَئِذٍ ءَامِنُونَ

## 27:90 — وَمَن جَآءَ بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِ فَكُبَّتْ وُجُوهُهُمْ فِى ٱلنَّارِ هَلْ تُجْزَوْنَ إِلَّا مَا كُنتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ

I infer strict correspondence here, unlike 27:89's surplus good: harmful doing returns as the orientation it built.

## 27:91 — إِنَّمَآ أُمِرْتُ أَنْ أَعْبُدَ رَبَّ هَٰذِهِ ٱلْبَلْدَةِ ٱلَّذِى حَرَّمَهَا وَلَهُۥ كُلُّ شَىْءٍۢ ۖ وَأُمِرْتُ أَنْ أَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلْمُسْلِمِينَ

1. `ء م ر/B002` supplies directive, `ع ب د/B003` submissive worship, `ب ل د/B001` a bounded tract, and `ح ر م/B003` protected inviolable bounds. Universal ownership is localized as care for a specific protected city. `س ل م/B012` makes the messenger's commanded identity a letting go. I infer that universal lordship does not erase place; it grounds bounded protection and located obligation.

### Retrospective surprises

## 27:92 — وَأَنْ أَتْلُوَا۟ ٱلْقُرْءَانَ ۖ فَمَنِ ٱهْتَدَىٰ فَإِنَّمَا يَهْتَدِى لِنَفْسِهِۦ ۖ وَمَن ضَلَّ فَقُلْ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلْمُنذِرِينَ

### Activated readings

1. `ت ل و/B001` is following in sequence, so reciting the Qur'an performs ordered following, while `ه د ي/B002` makes guidance an intended course. The repeated reflexive construction places the path's benefit in the traveler's own self; the messenger remains `ن ذ ر/B001`, an awakener of caution, not the controller of response. I infer that sequential proclamation opens a route but does not transfer agency away from the hearer.

### Retrospective surprises

27:93 promises future showing and recognition, completing the route opened by recitation without transferring responsibility from the hearer.

## 27:93 — وَقُلِ ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ سَيُرِيكُمْ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ فَتَعْرِفُونَهَا ۚ وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِغَٰفِلٍ عَمَّا تَعْمَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. `ء ي ي/B003` gives visible signs, `ر ء ي/B012` making seen, and `ع ر ف/B003` recognition by trace or mark. The future sequence is pedagogical: signs are displayed until their identifying marks become undeniable. `غ ف ل/B002` is knowingly leaving something unmarked; its negation means deeds are neither overlooked nor left without record. I infer reciprocal disclosure: humans come to recognize signs while their own actions remain continuously marked for divine knowledge.

2. `غ ف ل/B004`, deliberate disregard, preserves a distinct reading: the closing denial excludes not only accidental inattention but chosen neglect. This directly answers the surah's knowing denials, where recognized signs were outwardly refused.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah remains; the closing relation itself retrospectively gathers the surah's signs, recognitions, denials, and deeds.
