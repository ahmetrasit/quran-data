# S105 v12 full-context ayah walk

## 105:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **The opening name as the frame that makes the later event readable.** Lexical evidence: س م و B005, "name as raised designation"; س م و B001, "rising high"; ء ل ه B002, the fixed divine name in invocation formulas; ر ء ي B012/B013 in 105:1, "showing and making seen" and the "tell me" alerting formula. Structural cue: the packet begins with a named invocation, then immediately asks "أَلَمْ تَرَ" and "كَيْفَ" before unfolding the sequence 105:2-5. Assigned role: the basmalah raises the acting name into view before the reader is asked to see the manner of action. Abductive move: I supply the arrow that the opening formula is not only a preface but a viewing device for the subsequent demonstration. Reading change: the basmalah reads as an activation of visibility and intelligibility, not only as a devotional beginning.

2. **Mercy as guarded custody rather than softness alone.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B001, "mercy and tenderness"; ر ح م B002/B003, kinship bond and womb; ر ب ب B002 in 105:1, "repair, nurture, and completion"; ح ج ر B001/B005 in 105:4, enclosure/prohibition and sheltered custody; ص ح ب B002 in 105:1, protective accompaniment. Structural cue: mercy appears before a narrative in which one company is struck by stones whose root also carries enclosure and custody. Assigned role: ر ح م supplies the vulnerable interior bond, while ح ج ر supplies the boundary that can protect it. Abductive move: I infer that destructive action can function as mercy when it guards an enclosed dependent good; the packet supplies mercy, lordly care, companionship, and stones/enclosure, but I supply that protective causal arrow. Reading change: الرَّحْمَٰن/الرَّحِيم do not disappear when judgment arrives; they can color the later throwing as boundary-making mercy.

3. **Mercy as the opposite of being made consumable debris.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B001, beneficence toward the one shown mercy; ج ع ل B002 in 105:5, turning into a state; ع ص ف B001, dry crop chaff and broken straw; ء ك ل B006/B007, being eaten away/decay and prey or animal set aside for eating. Structural cue: the repeated divine names of mercy stand at the head of a sequence whose final state is "كَعَصْفٍ مَّأْكُولٍ". Assigned role: the final image activates the negative space of mercy: what is not held in mercy becomes edible, worn-away residue. Abductive move: I infer a contrastive mechanism between sheltered life and consumable remainder; the packet supplies the branch fields but not the explicit contrast. Reading change: the basmalah becomes a life-preserving threshold against the final image of life reduced to fodder-like remains.

### Retrospective surprises

The later "تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ" made the mercy reading sharper than expected: ح ج ر B001/B005 changed mercy from general tenderness into guarded boundary. The final "كَعَصْفٍ مَّأْكُولٍ" also surprised the opening: ر ح م B001 became visible by contrast with ء ك ل B006/B007, as mercy's opposite is not merely punishment but exposure to being eaten away. The repeated جعل in 105:2 and 105:5 retrospectively made the basmalah's divine name a heading over state-conversion.

## 105:1 — أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصْحَٰبِ ٱلْفِيلِ

### Activated readings

1. **The ayah as an instruction to inspect a mechanism, not merely recall an event.** Lexical evidence: ر ء ي B001, seeing by eye or insight; ر ء ي B012, showing and making seen; ر ء ي B013, "tell me" formula; ف ع ل B001, bringing an act into being. ك ي ف has no accepted non-contaminated branch inventory in the packet, so I use it only as the surface interrogative "how" and do not assign a branch ID. Structural cue: 105:1 asks about manner before 105:2-5 give sequential operations: making the plot lost, sending birds, throwing stones, making them chaff. Assigned role: ر ء ي makes the reader/viewer an observer of staged causation; ف ع ل names the whole sequence as an effected act. Abductive move: I infer that the later ayat are the answer to "how"; the packet supplies the order, while I supply the forensic reading of that order. Reading change: "أَلَمْ تَرَ" reads less as simple "did you not see" and more as "watch the way the action is made visible."

2. **Lordship as corrective administration.** Lexical evidence: ر ب ب B001, lordship, ownership, and authority; ر ب ب B002, repair, nurture, and completion; ج ع ل B002 in 105:2 and 105:5, turning something into a state; ض ل ل B001/B003, going astray from aim and losing/failing to locate. Structural cue: "رَبُّكَ" occurs before two instances of جعل that convert their كيد into تضليل and then convert them into عصف مأكول. Assigned role: ر ب ب B001 supplies sovereign capacity; ر ب ب B002 supplies a caretaker's power to set a damaged order right. Abductive move: I supply that the two later make/turn operations are administrative acts of the Lord, not separate narrative facts. Reading change: the Lord's "doing" reads as a corrective governance of states and outcomes, not only an overwhelming blow.

3. **"Companions of the elephant" as exposed dependence.** Lexical evidence: ص ح ب B001, companionship and close association; ص ح ب B004, making something accompany or carrying it along; ف ي ل B004, the elephant; ف ي ل B001, slackness and weak judgment. Structural cue: the human group is identified by companionship with the animal before their كيد is placed in تضليل. Assigned role: ص ح ب binds the group identity to what it accompanies; ف ي ل B004 supplies visible bulk, while ف ي ل B001 supplies a latent weakness of judgment activated by the later lost plot. Abductive move: I infer that the phrase does not merely label ownership but reveals the support system by which they understand themselves. Reading change: "أصحاب الفيل" becomes a diagnosis of dependence on heavy animal power and weak discernment, not a neutral historical tag.

4. **Mighty animal force inverted by airborne and granular force.** Lexical evidence: ف ي ل B004, elephant; ط ي ر B001 in 105:3, airborne lightness and birds; ء ب ل B003, successive or scattered groups; ر م ي B001 and ح ج ر B003 in 105:4, casting hard stones. Structural cue: a single emblematic ground animal in 105:1 is answered by many airborne groups and small hard projectiles in 105:3-4. Assigned role: the elephant supplies mass and ground-bound prestige; the birds and stones supply distributed vertical force. Abductive move: I infer a reversal between the mode of confidence and the mode of defeat; the packet supplies animal/bird/projectile imagery, while I supply the directional inversion. Reading change: the ayah starts to read as the setup of a material reversal: weight below is overcome by lightness above carrying hardness.

### Retrospective surprises

The final جعل in 105:5 was the strongest retrospective correction: it made 105:1's "فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ" less like a single deed and more like a chain of transformations. 105:3's أبابيل also revised "أصحاب الفيل": the elephant-company was not only defeated by birds, but by a rival pattern of association, scattered and successive rather than heavy and centralized. The missing ك ي ف inventory remained important: the "how" had to be supplied structurally from the sequence, not lexically by invented branches.

## 105:2 — أَلَمْ يَجْعَلْ كَيْدَهُمْ فِى تَضْلِيلٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Plot turned into a state of misdirection.** Lexical evidence: ج ع ل B002, turning something into a state; ك ي د B002, plotting and stratagem; ض ل ل B001, going astray from guidance and aim; ض ل ل B003, losing or failing to locate something. Structural cue: the construction "يجعل كيدهم في تضليل" places the plot inside a field of loss, and 105:5 repeats جعل with the people themselves as object. Assigned role: كيد supplies intentional planning; جعل supplies state conversion; ضلل supplies the new medium in which the plan operates. Abductive move: I infer that the plot is not merely blocked from outside but made to continue inside a wrong space; the packet supplies the containment phrase "في" and the branches of turning/loss. Reading change: the ayah reads as misdirection of agency itself, not only failure of a scheme.

2. **Their war-effort reclassified as self-consuming labor.** Lexical evidence: ك ي د B001, forceful handling of a thing; ك ي د B004, war and battle; ك ي د B003, struggle as the soul departs; ع ص ف B005 in 105:5, earning/resourceful striving; ء ك ل B006 in 105:5, being eaten away and decay. Structural cue: the effort named كيد precedes the final passive image "مأكول"; the exertion of 105:2 ends as consumed residue in 105:5. Assigned role: كيد B001/B004 makes their plan a strenuous martial undertaking; the final عصف/أكل complex gives that undertaking a material endpoint. Abductive move: I infer that the labor of the plot feeds the process that undoes it; the packet supplies effort/war and eaten debris, while I supply the self-consuming arrow. Reading change: the ayah reads as the beginning of their exhaustion and depletion, not just a statement that a plan went wrong.

3. **Disappearance as the internal logic of "تضليل".** Lexical evidence: ض ل ل B002, being hidden or disappearing; ض ل ل B005, stray animal in a wasteland; ص ح ب B001 and ف ي ل B004 from 105:1, a company tied to an animal; ر م ي B001/ح ج ر B003 in 105:4, stones cast against them. Structural cue: the known, named company of 105:1 loses its way before becoming unrecognizable chaff in 105:5. Assigned role: ضلل B002/B005 turns the military company into something lost from its owner/path and then into vanishing material. Abductive move: I infer a progression from strategic deviation to social/material disappearance; the packet supplies "companions," "elephant," "misdirection," and final debris. Reading change: "تضليل" includes erasure and stray-ness, not only intellectual error.

4. **A non-symmetric answer to scheming.** Lexical evidence: ك ي د B002, stratagem; ر س ل B001 in 105:3, sending forth/release; ء ب ل B003, successive or scattered groups; ر م ي B001, throwing/casting. Structural cue: their كيد is answered not by another explicit كيد-root term, but by release, grouping, and projectile action. Assigned role: كيد defines their mode as contrivance; إرسال/أبابيل/رمي define the counter-mode as ordered dispatch. Abductive move: I supply that divine action avoids mirroring their strategy and instead changes the operating field. Reading change: the ayah reads as asymmetrical defeat: a scheme is not out-plotted so much as displaced into a system where it cannot aim.

### Retrospective surprises

105:5 made the two جعل clauses behave like brackets: first the كيد is turned into تضليل, then the plotters themselves are turned into عصف مأكول. That later parallel forced me to reread 105:2 as the inner conversion before the outer/material conversion. 105:3 also surprised "تضليل": the scattered/successive bird groups supplied a concrete spatial mechanism for lost aim, so misdirection was not merely mental. 105:4's سجل B004 added a judicial note: the lost plot is answered by a recorded/secured counter-act.

## 105:3 — وَأَرْسَلَ عَلَيْهِمْ طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ

### Activated readings

1. **Release in ordered waves.** Lexical evidence: ر س ل B001, being sent forth and released; ر س ل B005, succession in batches; ط ي ر B001, airborne lightness and birds; ء ب ل B003, successive or scattered groups. Structural cue: "أرسل" is immediately followed by "طيرا أبابيل", and the branch inventories for ر س ل and ء ب ل both contain successive group movement. Assigned role: ر س ل supplies the dispatch; طير supplies the airborne medium; أبابيل supplies the batch-pattern. Abductive move: I infer that the sending is not just release but controlled sequencing; the packet supplies the repeated batch semantics. Reading change: the ayah reads as a patterned deployment, not merely "birds were sent."

2. **The sky-herd countering the elephant-company.** Lexical evidence: ء ب ل B001, camels and their tending/herds; ء ب ل B003, successive/scattered groups; ف ي ل B004 in 105:1, elephant; ص ح ب B001 in 105:1, companionship and close association; ر ب ب B014 in 105:1, herd or gathered wild cattle. Structural cue: 105:1 names a ground animal and its human company; 105:3 names airborne animals in grouped succession. Assigned role: ء ب ل B001/B003 lets "أبابيل" carry herd/group resonance as well as the surface descriptor of scattered bands. Abductive move: I infer an animal-social inversion: the elephant-company is met by another kind of company, a dispersed sky-herd. Reading change: "طيرا أبابيل" becomes a counter-formation to "أصحاب الفيل", not just a species plus adjective.

3. **Message-bearing dispatch.** Lexical evidence: ر س ل B002, messenger and message; س ج ل B004 in 105:4, the record that gathers and secures; ر ء ي B012/B013 in 105:1, showing/alerting; ف ع ل B001 in 105:1, bringing an act into being. Structural cue: the sent birds are followed by stones "من سجيل", whose root inventory includes record/deed, and the whole sequence is introduced as something to see. Assigned role: ر س ل B002 makes the sent element potentially communicative; سجل B004 supplies what can be "record-like" or secured; رأي supplies the audience of the sign. Abductive move: I infer that the birds carry not merely impact but a readable verdict; the packet supplies sending, seeing, and record branches, while I supply the message relation. Reading change: the birds read as envoys of a legible act, not only as weapons.

4. **Scattering that defeats concentrated plotting.** Lexical evidence: ط ي ر B002, spreading as if flying loose; ء ب ل B003, scattered/successive groups; ك ي د B002 in 105:2, plotting and stratagem; ض ل ل B001/B003 in 105:2, deviation and loss. Structural cue: the compact noun "كيدهم" is followed by distributed plural movement from above. Assigned role: the bird branches supply dispersion; the plot branches supply concentrated intent. Abductive move: I infer that distributed movement breaks the plot's ability to coordinate; the packet supplies scattering and lost aim, but I supply the interference mechanism. Reading change: the ayah becomes the spatial means by which their كيد enters تضليل.

### Retrospective surprises

105:4 changed the birds after the fact: because the next ayah gives رمي and سجيل, the birds became carriers of a stony/recorded act, not only moving creatures. 105:5 then made their lightness more consequential: عصف B002/B004 allowed the airborne scene to become the prelude to a sweeping chaff-making force. No later ayah erased the surface bird reading; rather, later ayat multiplied the birds' roles as batches, counter-herd, messengers, and storm-agents.

## 105:4 — تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍۢ مِّن سِجِّيلٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Hard rain: thrown stones as poured-out descent.** Lexical evidence: ر م ي B001, throwing or casting a thing; ر م ي B004, thrown-together cloud or heavy rain cloud; ح ج ر B003, hard stone; س ج ل B001, filled bucket and outpouring; س ج ل B003, unrestricted giving poured out; س م و B004 in 105:0, overhead sky/cover, cloud, rain. Structural cue: birds from above throw stones, while سجل contributes bucket/outpouring imagery and سمو contributes overhead/rain imagery from the opening. Assigned role: رمي supplies projectile motion, حجر supplies hardness, سجل supplies the poured measure, and سمو supplies vertical overhead space. Abductive move: I infer a "rain of hard matter" mechanism; the packet supplies the branch ingredients but not the meteorological metaphor as a full causal model. Reading change: the ayah reads as controlled stony downpour, not just individual missiles.

2. **Stone as boundary and prohibition.** Lexical evidence: ح ج ر B001, keeping back by enclosure/prohibition; ح ج ر B004, enclosed place; ح ج ر B005, lap and sheltered custody; ر ح م B002/B003 in 105:0, kinship/womb; ص ح ب B001 in 105:1, close association. Structural cue: the material "حجارة" arrives at the point where the elephant-company is stopped. Assigned role: ح ج ر B003 gives physical stone; ح ج ر B001/B004/B005 activate the stone as barrier, enclosure, and guarded interior. Abductive move: I infer that the projectiles do not only injure bodies but mark a boundary the company cannot cross; the packet supplies the stone/enclosure branches, while I supply their blocking function in the episode. Reading change: "بحجارة" reads as interdiction and guarded limit, not only ammunition.

3. **Sijjil as record-like verdict.** Lexical evidence: س ج ل B004, record/deed/registration that gathers and secures; ر س ل B002 in 105:3, messenger and message; ر ء ي B012/B013 in 105:1, showing/alerting formula; ف ع ل B001, bringing an act into being. Structural cue: the sent birds carry stones whose source word has a branch of secured writing/registration, and the whole action is displayed to the viewer. Assigned role: سجل B004 gives the stones evidentiary/judicial quality; رسل B002 makes their delivery message-like; رأي makes the result visible. Abductive move: I infer that the material blow also functions as a visible registration of judgment; the packet supplies the lexical record branch, not a fully stated legal scene. Reading change: "من سجيل" reads as more than material source: the impact is also a documented/secured act.

4. **Thrown accusation returned as material strike.** Lexical evidence: ر م ي B008, throwing accusation in speech; ر م ي B001, physical throwing; ك ي د B002 in 105:2, stratagem; ض ل ل B001, deviation from aim. Structural cue: a plotting group is first placed in misdirection, then becomes the target of throwing. Assigned role: رمي B008 supplies a social/legal "casting against"; رمي B001 supplies the physical projectile. Abductive move: I infer a reflexive return: the wrongful directional intent of the plot is answered by a cast-against-them event. This is exploratory because the ayah's surface uses physical throwing, while the speech/accusation branch is activated only by the social logic of كيد and the visible verdict mechanism. Reading change: the throwing can be heard as both impact and charge, without replacing the physical sense.

### Retrospective surprises

105:5 supplied the effect I did not yet have in 105:4: the stones do not merely hit; they produce عصف B001, broken crop refuse, and ء ك ل B006, eaten-away decay. That later result strengthened the "hard rain" reading, because the downpour ends in shredded agricultural debris. It also sharpened the boundary reading: the barrier is successful not only because it blocks, but because the would-be crossing company is converted into residue.

## 105:5 — فَجَعَلَهُمْ كَعَصْفٍۢ مَّأْكُولٍۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Final state-conversion into crop refuse.** Lexical evidence: ج ع ل B002, turning something into a state; ع ص ف B001, dry crop chaff and broken straw; ء ك ل B001, ingesting food; ء ك ل B006, being eaten away/decay. Structural cue: this is the second جعل in the surah after 105:2, now with "هم" as object and a simile as the resulting condition. Assigned role: جعل supplies transformation; عصف supplies the target material state; مأكول supplies consumption or eaten-away degradation. Abductive move: I infer that the final simile is the result-state of the whole sequence, not decorative comparison; the packet supplies the repeated جعل and the material branches. Reading change: the ayah reads as a completed conversion of an organized company into spent organic residue.

2. **Storm-force hidden inside the chaff image.** Lexical evidence: ع ص ف B002, violent wind that sweeps things away; ع ص ف B004, sweeping destructive force; ط ي ر B001/B002 in 105:3, airborne lightness and scattering; ر م ي B004 in 105:4, cloud/heavy-rain usage; س ج ل B001, outpouring bucket. Structural cue: the prior ayat give airborne bands and thrown-down material before the result "عصف". Assigned role: عصف B001 supplies the visible debris; عصف B002/B004 supplies the force that produces such debris. Abductive move: I infer a storm-like process from birds, outpouring, and projectile descent; the packet supplies several air/descent/destruction branches, while I supply the combined storm mechanism. Reading change: "كعصف" reads both as what they become and as the trace of the destructive force that made them so.

3. **The eaters/possessors become consumable prey.** Lexical evidence: ء ك ل B007, prey or animal set aside for eating; ء ك ل B004, consuming or taking wealth; ص ح ب B001/B004 in 105:1, company/possession; ف ي ل B004, elephant and its keeper/companion; ف ي ل B001, weak judgment. Structural cue: a group named by possessing/accompanying a great animal ends as "مأكول", passive and consumable. Assigned role: أصحاب الفيل supplies an owning/companion identity; ء ك ل B007 supplies the prey-state; ء ك ل B004 tentatively activates reversal of taking/consuming into being consumed. Abductive move: I infer reversal from controlling an animal/resource to becoming matter available to consumption; the packet supplies the ownership/company and eating branches, while I supply the reversal arrow. Reading change: the final passive adjective reads as social inversion: the powerful animal-company is rendered prey-like.

4. **Their resourceful striving yields worthless residue.** Lexical evidence: ع ص ف B005, earning and resourceful striving; ك ي د B001/B002 in 105:2, forceful handling and stratagem; ض ل ل B001, deviation from aim; ج ع ل B002, turning into state. Structural cue: the sequence moves from their intentional كيد to an عصف-state that can itself carry striving/acquisition resonance. Assigned role: كيد names the planned effort; عصف B005 names an alternate branch of busy acquisition; ضلل names the deviation of that effort's end. Abductive move: I infer ironic yield: their exertion "produces" only dry chaff. This is exploratory because عصف on the surface is the chaff image, while B005 is activated by the prior labor/stratagem complex. Reading change: the final ayah becomes not only annihilation but a failed harvest of their own planning.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah follows 105:5, so there is no later textual surprise. The retrospective surprise here comes from rereading earlier activators into the endpoint: 105:2's first جعل makes this second جعل feel like completion; 105:3's airborne scattering and 105:4's stony outpouring make عصف B002/B004 active inside the chaff image; and the basmalah's ر ح م B001 makes the final eaten-away state stand as the outside of mercy/protection rather than an isolated image of ruin.
