# S1 Full-Context Ayah Walk — Reader B

## 1:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **The opening name as a raised public marker of agency.** Evidence: `س م و B005` name as raised designation, with `س م و B008` good repute spread; `ء ل ه B002` fixed divine name in invocation; `ح م د B001/B003` later praise and praiseworthy naming in 1:2. Mechanism: the initial “name” is not only a label but the visible elevation of the agent under whom the later acts of praise, worship, help-seeking, and guidance will occur. The packet supplies name, divine invocation, and praise; I infer the directional arrow from naming to public orientation. Abductive move: if the speech begins by raising a name, later praise becomes the social confirmation of that raised designation. Reading change: the basmalah reads less as a detachable formula and more as the banner under which the whole passage is organized.

2. **Mercy as the enclosure that precedes both nurture and judgment.** Evidence: `ر ح م B001` mercy and tenderness, `ر ح م B002` kinship bond, `ر ح م B003` womb; activated by `ر ب ب B002` nurture/completion in 1:2 and by `م ل ك B003` plus `د ي ن B002` dominion and reckoning in 1:4. Mechanism: the double mercy surrounds the move from ربّ care to ملك/دين judgment, making the later legal-sovereign scene enter through an already merciful enclosure. The packet supplies mercy, nurture, dominion, and reckoning; I infer that mercy frames rather than cancels judgment. Abductive move: a sequence that names mercy before ownership of reckoning asks the reader to carry tenderness into the tribunal. Reading change: “الرحمن الرحيم” becomes a governing atmosphere for the whole surah, not only a pair of attributes.

3. **Invocation as relation before demand.** Evidence: `ء ل ه B001` worship and the worshipped one, `ء ل ه B002` invocation formula, `ع ب د B003` worship/submissive obedience in 1:5, `ع و ن B001` help/backing in 1:5. Mechanism: the opening invocation establishes the addressee before the “You alone” repetition appears; worship and help-seeking are later disclosed as the practical shape of the name invoked here. The packet supplies the divine name and later direct address; I infer that the name authorizes the later second-person turn. Abductive move: one invokes a name before asking because the relation must be recognized before dependence is voiced. Reading change: the basmalah becomes a relational threshold into petition, not merely a pious preface.

### Retrospective surprises

The later path material in 1:6–1:7 made `س م و B005` feel more like a road-sign or banner than a static “name”: the name is the first marker before any صراط is requested. The sharper surprise came from 1:4: mercy is not simply soft affect, because ملك/دين activates a scene of ownership, reckoning, and recompense inside the already-named mercy.

## 1:2 — ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Praise as tested recognition, not only spontaneous acclaim.** Evidence: `ح م د B001` praise/thanksgiving, `ح م د B002` finding something praiseworthy after testing, `ن ع م B001` favor in 1:7, and `غ ض ب B001`/`ض ل ل B001` negative alternatives in 1:7. Mechanism: the later contrast between favored, angered, and lost paths makes الحمد sound like a verdict reached after discriminating between outcomes. The packet supplies praise, favor, anger, and straying; I infer the evaluative testing relation. Abductive move: praise becomes meaningful when the path has alternatives that could have ended in anger or loss. Reading change: “الحمد” reads as thankful recognition after moral-path testing, not only as opening doxology.

2. **Rabb as caretaker who brings many marked worlds to completion.** Evidence: `ر ب ب B002` repair, nurture, and completion; `ع ل م B002` distinguishing mark/landmark; `ه د ي B001` guidance toward way/truth in 1:6; `ق و م B009` support/mainstay in 1:6. Mechanism: “العالمين” is activated by the branch of signs and markers: worlds are readable, marked domains that require ربّ care, and guidance later asks to move through those marks rightly. The packet supplies Rabb, worlds/knowledge-sign branches, guidance, and straightness; I infer that nurture makes the marked order navigable. Abductive move: a ربّ of many worlds is not only owner of plurality but the one who maintains their intelligibility. Reading change: “رب العالمين” becomes a claim about sustaining a legible cosmos, not only possessing all beings.

3. **Lordship as authority, covenant, and administered dependence.** Evidence: `ر ب ب B001` lordship/ownership/authority, `ر ب ب B011` covenant or protection pact, `م ل ك B003` kingship/dominion and `د ي ن B001/B002` obedience/reckoning in 1:4, plus `ع ب د B003` worship in 1:5. Mechanism: later obedience, judgment, and worship activate the legal-political side of ربّ: praise is directed to one whose care is also binding authority. The packet supplies authority, covenant, obedience, and worship; I infer that praise acknowledges administered dependence. Abductive move: if the speaker will later declare exclusive service, the ربّ here must already be more than a nurturer; he is the binding governor of the relation. Reading change: the ayah preserves both care and rule, rather than disambiguating Rabb into either tenderness or sovereignty.

### Retrospective surprises

1:6 unexpectedly made `ع ل م B002` important: “worlds” began to look like marked terrain whose signs need guidance, not just a plural totality. 1:7 then fed back into الحمد through `ن ع م B001`; praise became gratitude for favor and also a recognition that some outcomes are not praiseworthy because they end in anger or loss.

## 1:3 — ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Mercy as the hinge between nurture and reckoning.** Evidence: `ر ح م B001` mercy/tenderness, preceded by `ر ب ب B002` nurture/completion in 1:2 and followed by `م ل ك B003` kingship plus `د ي ن B002` reckoning in 1:4. Mechanism: the repeated mercy sits structurally between ربّ care and ملك يوم الدين, preventing either side from being read alone. The packet supplies the sequence; I infer the mediating function. Abductive move: an attribute repeated at the transition point is meant to govern both what came before and what comes after. Reading change: the ayah becomes a hinge that carries tenderness into sovereignty and sovereignty back into care.

2. **Mercy as kinship-making around the praying “we.”** Evidence: `ر ح م B002` kinship bond and `ر ح م B003` womb; activated by the first-person plural in 1:5–1:6 and by “الذين أنعمت عليهم” in 1:7; supported by `ر ب ب B005` fostered child/step-family relation. Mechanism: the later “we/us” and “those upon whom” form a social body; mercy supplies the bond by which speakers and favored predecessors can belong together. The packet supplies kinship, womb, foster-care, and collective pronouns; I infer the family-making relation. Abductive move: a group seeking one path needs more than instruction; it needs a bond that makes shared guidance possible. Reading change: the repeated divine mercy reads as incorporation into a protected relation, not only as compassion toward isolated individuals.

3. **Exploratory: mercy includes vulnerable after-birth repair.** Evidence: `ر ح م B004` womb ailment after birth, activated by `ر ب ب B009` animal just after birth/freshness and `ر ب ب B002` stage-by-stage nurture, with later `ه د ي B008` supported walking. Mechanism: this is a weaker but visible material analogy: the surah moves from birth-like invocation into supported walking on a path; mercy includes the painful vulnerability after emergence. The packet supplies postpartum ailment, freshness after birth, nurture, and supported walking; I infer the lifecycle arc. Abductive move: if guidance is requested after naming and praise, the speaker may be newly born into dependence rather than already stable. Reading change: mercy becomes restorative care for fragile transition, not only benevolent feeling.

### Retrospective surprises

The later “إِيَّاكَ” and “اهدنا” made the kinship/womb branch less decorative: mercy gathers a “we” before the path is requested. 1:7 also sharpened the contrast; mercy is the alternative atmosphere to both `غ ض ب B001` retaliatory anger and `ض ل ل B001/B003` loss of aim or owner.

## 1:4 — مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ

### Activated readings

1. **The severe event-day of reckoning under dominion.** Evidence: `م ل ك B003` kingship/dominion, `ي و م B003` event-day or severe occasion, and `د ي ن B002` reckoning/recompense. Mechanism: the three roots lock into a forensic-sovereign scene: dominion specifies who controls the event, يوم gives the occasion weight, and دين supplies accounting. The packet supplies these three elements; I infer their convergence into a tribunal-like mechanism. Abductive move: when “day” is paired with recompense rather than ordinary sequence, it becomes an event of settlement. Reading change: the ayah reads as ownership of the decisive occasion, not only ownership on a calendar day.

2. **The path has a controlling center and main course.** Evidence: `م ل ك B006` center/main course of road or valley, activated by `ص ر ط B001` road/path in 1:6–1:7; `ق و م B008` straightness/uprightness; `ه د ي B001` showing the road. Mechanism: later ṣirāṭ turns ملك from sheer possession toward the “main course” that governs where one can travel. The packet supplies road, straightness, guidance, and the road-center branch; I infer that ملك can function as the central channel of the path. Abductive move: a path becomes safe when its governing center is known and not confused with side routes. Reading change: “مالك” also activates as the holder of the road’s central line, so judgment is connected to navigation.

3. **Debt, favor, and repayment.** Evidence: `د ي ن B003` debt/credit, `ن ع م B001` bestowed favor in 1:7, `ح م د B001` thanksgiving in 1:2, and `ع و ن B001` help in 1:5. Mechanism: favor and help create received benefit; دين as debt/recompense turns the day into a reckoning of dependence. The packet supplies favor, help, thanksgiving, and debt; I infer an economy of received bounty and answerability. Abductive move: one who receives نعمة and عون stands in a relation that must be acknowledged rather than possessed as private merit. Reading change: the ayah can read as the day when benefaction, gratitude, obligation, and recompense are brought into one account.

4. **Standing before the owner.** Evidence: `ق و م B013` resurrection and people standing before their Lord, activated by `ق و م` in “المستقيم” at 1:6; `ر ب ب B001` lordship; `د ي ن B002` reckoning. Mechanism: the later straight-standing root carries a resurrection branch backward into يوم الدين: the path of uprightness anticipates standing before the Lord. The packet supplies the standing/resurrection branch and the reckoning frame; I infer temporal reversal from future standing back to present path. Abductive move: a straight path matters because it trains or leads to the final standing. Reading change: “يوم الدين” is not only an external future event; it presses backward into present posture.

### Retrospective surprises

1:6–1:7 changed this ayah most: the later صراط made `م ل ك B006` visible, so ملك was no longer only royal possession but also the center or main channel of a road. 1:7’s نعمة also made `د ي ن B003` debt/credit unexpectedly relevant: the day of דין can account for received favor, not only punish wrongdoing.

## 1:5 — إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ

### Activated readings

1. **Exclusive service as the practical form of the invoked name.** Evidence: `ع ب د B003` worship/submissive obedience, activated by `ء ل ه B001` worshipped one and `ء ل ه B002` invocation in 1:0–1:2; reinforced by `د ي ن B001` obedience/submission in 1:4. Mechanism: the repeated fronted “إياك” narrows worship and help to the same addressee named earlier. The packet supplies invocation, worship, obedience, and repetition; I infer exclusivity as a relational narrowing. Abductive move: direct address is not a new topic but the activated consequence of naming Allah at the start. Reading change: the ayah becomes the moment where prior praise turns into pledged practice.

2. **Servitude that is backed, not abandoned.** Evidence: `ع ب د B001` owned/slave status and `ع ب د B004` being made slave-like, held together with `ع و ن B001` help/backing; activated by `م ل ك B002` ownership/disposal and `د ي ن B004` subjugation/possession in 1:4. Mechanism: the vocabulary could harden into ownership and subjugation, but the paired استعانة supplies backing from the same addressee. The packet supplies servitude, ownership, subjugation, and help; I infer that help transforms dependence from abandonment into supported service. Abductive move: if the servant immediately asks for aid, the relation is not naked domination but dependent empowerment. Reading change: worship reads as dependence that receives support, preserving both humility and agency.

3. **Road-smoothing before the request for the road.** Evidence: `ع ب د B005` physical subduing/smoothing, especially a passable road; activated by `ص ر ط B001` path and `ق و م B008` straightness in 1:6–1:7; paired with `ع و ن B001` assistance. Mechanism: before the explicit request for صراط, “نعبد” activates the making-passable branch: service prepares or smooths the way, while help provides the power to move on it. The packet supplies road-smoothing, path, straightness, and help; I infer preparation-before-navigation. Abductive move: one asks to be guided to a path after first submitting to the work that makes a path traversable. Reading change: the ayah is not only ritual allegiance; it becomes roadwork that precedes guidance.

4. **Worship as the undoing of prideful anger.** Evidence: `ع ب د B008` prideful aversion/anger, activated by `غ ض ب B001` anger in 1:7 and contrasted with `ع ب د B003` submissive obedience. Mechanism: the same root-field can hold offended pride and worshipful submission; later غضب shows the danger if pride hardens. The packet supplies pride/anger and divine-directed worship; I infer an inner reversal from self-assertive refusal to service. Abductive move: exclusive worship redirects the energy that otherwise becomes aversion and wrath. Reading change: “نعبد” becomes affective discipline, not only outward service.

### Retrospective surprises

The next ayah made `ع ب د B005` unexpectedly strong: “we worship” began to sound like smoothing and submitting the road before asking for the straight path. 1:7 then made the emotional branch `ع ب د B008` visible; worship is partly protection from the pride-anger complex that would place the speaker among the wrong route-groups.

## 1:6 — ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ

### Activated readings

1. **Gentle showing of the straight road.** Evidence: `ه د ي B001` gentle guidance toward way/truth, `ص ر ط B001` straight road, and `ق و م B008` straightness/uprightness/evenness. Mechanism: the three roots form the surface mechanism: an addressee is asked to show a road whose defining quality is uprightness. The packet supplies guidance, road, and straightness; I infer that guidance is not only information but movement toward an aligned course. Abductive move: a straight path must be both indicated and sustained, because the speaker cannot generate it by worship alone. Reading change: the ayah reads as a request for enabled orientation, not merely for knowledge of a route.

2. **A leading front, not only an abstract direction.** Evidence: `ه د ي B003` leading front part/guide, activated by `م ل ك B008` leading forepart in animals and by `ص ر ط B001` road; supported by `ق و م B003` resolve/rising to undertake a matter. Mechanism: guidance becomes embodied as something or someone going ahead; straightness is followed through a leading edge. The packet supplies leading-part branches and the road; I infer a caravan-like directionality. Abductive move: those who ask “guide us” need a front to follow, not only a map. Reading change: “اهدنا” becomes a request to be led from ahead into undertaking, not only to be instructed from a distance.

3. **The path as passage that can swallow, hide, or cut through.** Evidence: `ص ر ط B002` disappearing in passage/swallowing and `ص ر ط B003` cutting sword passing through, activated by `ض ل ل B002/B003` hiddenness/loss in 1:7 and `غ ض ب B004/B008` hard rock/tough hide. Mechanism: the requested path is not a neutral line; it is a passage through conditions where things disappear, are lost, or meet hard resistance. The packet supplies passage/swallowing, cutting-through, hidden loss, and hard compactness; I infer a dangerous transit mechanism. Abductive move: straightness matters because passage itself can consume or deflect the traveler. Reading change: الصراط المستقيم becomes a protected passage through risk, not only a morally correct road.

4. **Guidance as gift and offering.** Evidence: `ه د ي B004` gracious gift, `ه د ي B005` offering sent to sanctuary, activated by `ن ع م B001` bestowed favor in 1:7 and `ح م د B001` thanksgiving in 1:2. Mechanism: the request for guidance receives the gift-field of هدي: the path is something graciously sent or given, and the response to it is praise. The packet supplies gift/offering, favor, and thanksgiving; I infer a reciprocal devotional economy. Abductive move: what guides is not earned by the speaker’s service; it is bestowed, then answered by praise and worship. Reading change: “اهدنا” becomes request for a gift-bearing guidance, not a claim to entitlement.

### Retrospective surprises

1:7 made this ayah more concrete: الصراط is immediately restated as the path of a group, so guidance is social and historical, not only abstract direction. The negative end of 1:7 also activated `ص ر ط B002`; a path can become disappearance if it is not مستقيم, which made “straight” feel protective rather than merely geometrical.

## 1:7 — صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

### Activated readings

1. **The road is identified by marked recipients of favor.** Evidence: `ص ر ط B001` road/path, `ن ع م B001` bestowed favor and pleasant well-being, `ن ع م B003` praise as excellent, and `ع ل م B002` distinguishing mark/landmark from 1:2. Mechanism: the path is not defined abstractly but through “those” upon whom favor has landed; they function as markers that make the road recognizable. The packet supplies path, favor, praise, and sign/landmark branches; I infer that favored people become road-markers. Abductive move: if a path is hard to find, the visible trace of those already favored can identify it. Reading change: “صراط الذين أنعمت عليهم” reads as a marked social route, not merely a class of fortunate people.

2. **Negation splits the path away from retaliation and aimless deviation.** Evidence: `غ ي ر B005` otherness/exception/negation, `غ ض ب B001` intensified anger toward retaliation, and `ض ل ل B001` going astray from guidance/aim. Mechanism: “غير … ولا” creates two exclusions: the path is not the retaliation-field and not the deviation-field. The packet supplies negation, anger, and straying; I infer that the syntax functions as route-separation. Abductive move: guidance must name avoided trajectories as well as desired direction, because error can resemble movement. Reading change: the ayah becomes a boundary-drawing map: favor is read together with explicit separation from anger and loss.

3. **Repair and substitution away from damaged routes.** Evidence: `غ ي ر B003` altering/substituting one thing for another, `غ ي ر B001` repair through provision/watering/setting right, activated by `ر ب ب B002` repair/nurture in 1:2 and `ه د ي B001` guidance in 1:6. Mechanism: غير does more than exclude; it also activates replacement and repair, as if the requested path substitutes a rightly provisioned route for one ending in anger or loss. The packet supplies repair, substitution, nurture, and guidance; I infer a corrective route-change. Abductive move: to be guided is to have one route changed out for another, not only to receive a label saying “wrong.” Reading change: “غير المغضوب” reads as active redirection and repair, not bare negation.

4. **Herding: favored creatures are led; lost creatures become strays.** Evidence: `ن ع م B005` grazing livestock, especially camels; `ض ل ل B005` stray animal in a wasteland; `ه د ي B008` supported/swaying walking and heavy camels; `ر ب ب B014` herd or gathered wild cattle. Mechanism: the animal-material branches convert the social contrast into a pastoral mechanism: favor gathers and leads, while ضلال leaves an animal in a place of loss with no known owner. The packet supplies livestock, straying, supported walking, and herd gathering; I infer the shepherding relation. Abductive move: “upon them” can be read as care resting on a group so that they do not become ownerless strays. Reading change: the final ayah becomes a request to be gathered and led under care, not scattered into wasteland-loss.

5. **Anger as hardened material obstruction.** Evidence: `غ ض ب B004` hard compact rock, `غ ض ب B008` tough hide/folded leather like a shield, activated by `ص ر ط B003` cutting sword passing through and `ق و م B008` straightness. Mechanism: anger is not only affect; its branches materialize as hard compactness that blocks or armors against the path. The packet supplies hard rock/tough hide, cutting-through, and straightness; I infer obstruction. Abductive move: a straight path must cut through or avoid hardened states that retaliation produces. Reading change: “المغضوب عليهم” carries a material image of hardened blockage, not only a moral category.

### Retrospective surprises

Looking back from the end, the basmalah’s mercy now covers even the exclusions: the final negations are not outside mercy but define what merciful guidance saves from. 1:5 also changed the reading of this ayah: the group on the favored path are not passive beneficiaries only; their way is entered through worship, help-seeking, and the smoothing of the path. The most surprising activation was pastoral: `ن ع م B005` and `ض ل ل B005` make favor versus loss feel like gathered herd versus stray animal, which gives “اهدنا” a strongly protective and communal force.
