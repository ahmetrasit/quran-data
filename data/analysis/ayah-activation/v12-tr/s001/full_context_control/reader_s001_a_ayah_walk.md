# v12 Full-Context Control Reader: S001

## 1:1 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Invocation as raised designation governing the whole act.** Lexical evidence: س م و B005, “name as raised designation”; ء ل ه B002, fixed divine name in invocation; ر ح م B001, mercy and tenderness. Structural cue: the opening places “name” before any praise, judgment, worship, help, or guidance. Assigned role: the name is not a neutral label but an elevated designation under which the following speech is performed. Abductive move: the packet supplies the sequence name → praise → address/request; I supply the arrow that beginning “in the name” authorizes and channels the later acts. Reading change: the ayah reads as a formal entry into a governed speech-act, not only as a pious preface.

2. **Mercy as the covering atmosphere before lordship and judgment.** Lexical evidence: س م و B004, overhead sky/cover; ر ح م B001, compassion; ر ب ب B002/B008 in 1:2, nurturing and cloud imagery; د ي ن B002 in 1:4, reckoning. Structural cue: mercy is doubled before “Lord of the worlds” and before “Owner of the Day of dīn.” Assigned role: the two mercy-forms become the upper cover beneath which nurture and reckoning are later heard. Abductive move: the packet gives cover/cloud/nurture/reckoning as branch material; I infer that the opening sequence frames later authority through mercy rather than adding mercy after authority. Reading change: divine judgment and lordship are pre-activated as occurring inside a merciful canopy.

3. **Mercy as generative relation, not only affect.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B002, kinship bond; ر ح م B003, womb; ر ب ب B005 in 1:2, fostered child/caretaker relation; ن ع م B001 in 1:7, bestowed favor. Structural cue: the same mercy root is repeated, then the packet later gives Lord/caretaker, worshipper, helper, and favored people. Assigned role: ر ح م supplies a relational matrix that can produce, bind, and maintain dependents. Abductive move: the packet supplies kinship/womb/caretaker/favor; I infer a social-biological mechanism of dependency and care. Reading change: “al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm” reads as the source of a family-like and womb-like order in which beings can be nurtured, guided, and favored.

### Retrospective surprises

Later 1:6–1:7 made the “name” less static than it first appeared: the named opening becomes the gate into a path. The strongest surprise was that 1:7’s stray/lost animal branch under ض ل ل B005 and favor under ن ع م B001 made the opening mercy feel like protective ownership and kinship, not simply tenderness.

## 1:2 — ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Praise as an evaluative verdict after testing the order.** Lexical evidence: ح م د B001, praise opposite blame; ح م د B002, finding something satisfactory after testing; ع ل م B001, knowing as clarity. Structural cue: praise follows the named mercy and precedes the list of lordship, judgment, worship, and guidance. Assigned role: الحمد is both spoken praise and a judgment that the whole disclosed order proves praiseworthy. Abductive move: the packet gives praise/testing/clarity; I infer that the following ayat function as the evidentiary field that makes praise intelligible. Reading change: “all praise” reads less like a formula and more like the verdict produced by seeing the whole mechanism.

2. **Lordship as stage-by-stage cultivation of worlds.** Lexical evidence: ر ب ب B001, lordship/ownership/authority; ر ب ب B002, repair, nurture, completion; ع ل م B002, distinguishing signs and road markers. Structural cue: “Lord” is attached to “worlds,” while later 1:6 asks for a road and 1:7 identifies the path by people marked by favor. Assigned role: رب is the maintainer who forms worlds into legible, navigable fields. Abductive move: the packet supplies nurture and markers; I infer that the worlds are not merely objects owned but terrains cultivated into guidance-bearing order. Reading change: “Lord of the worlds” reads as “caretaker and completer of sign-marked realms.”

3. **The worlds as signs, not only domains.** Lexical evidence: ع ل م B002, a distinguishing mark that points to something; ه د ي B001 in 1:6, guidance to road/truth; ص ر ط B001 in 1:6–1:7, road. Structural cue: عَٰلَمِينَ sits immediately after رب and later the path request asks for orientation. Assigned role: worlds act as marked surfaces through which lordship becomes knowable and directional. Abductive move: the packet supplies “worlds” through the root علم and “path/guidance” later; I infer that visibility of marks enables movement. Reading change: the ayah’s cosmology becomes semiotic: the worlds are readable signs under رب, not inert scenery.

4. **Praise resists benefaction-credit rivalry.** Lexical evidence: ح م د B005, claiming praise by favor; ن ع م B001 in 1:7, bestowed favor; ء ل ه B001, worshipped object. Structural cue: 1:7 mentions favor bestowed by “You,” while 1:5 confines worship and help to “You.” Assigned role: الحمد prevents favor from becoming a human credit-claim. Abductive move: the packet supplies favor and exclusive address; I infer that all benefaction-credit is redirected to Allah. Reading change: praise is not only gratitude; it blocks rival ownership of benefaction.

### Retrospective surprises

1:7 sharpened رب العالمين: “favored,” “angered,” and “astray” showed that the worlds are not abstract cosmic plurality but a morally sorted field. The animal/stray branch of ض ل ل B005 also made رب sound unexpectedly like owner-care: the Lord of worlds contrasts with ownerless wandering.

## 1:3 — ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Mercy repeated as bridge between cosmic lordship and legal reckoning.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B001, mercy and beneficence; ر ب ب B001/B002 in 1:2, authority and nurture; د ي ن B002 in 1:4, reckoning/recompense. Structural cue: this ayah repeats 1:1’s mercy immediately after “Lord of the worlds” and immediately before “Owner of the Day of dīn.” Assigned role: mercy is the hinge that prevents lordship from reading as bare power and reckoning from reading as bare penalty. Abductive move: the packet supplies the adjacency; I infer that repetition performs a framing function across the transition. Reading change: the ayah reads as a structural brace in the surah’s sequence, not only as a restated attribute.

2. **Mercy as kinship-like continuity across the prayer.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B002, kinship bond; ر ح م B003, womb; ع ب د B003 in 1:5, worship/submissive obedience; ه د ي B004 in 1:6, gracious gift; ن ع م B001 in 1:7, favor. Structural cue: the prayer moves from divine names to “we worship,” “guide us,” and “those You favored.” Assigned role: mercy supplies the relational continuity that lets command, help, guidance, and favor belong to one household of dependence. Abductive move: the packet supplies kinship/womb, worship, gift, and favor; I infer a dependency-system rather than isolated attributes. Reading change: the double mercy reads as the generative bond within which the worshipping “we” can speak.

3. **Mercy as softening force against later hardening.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B001, tenderness; ن ع م B002 in 1:7, softness/easeful living; غ ض ب B004/B005 in 1:7, hard compact rock and thick intense redness. Structural cue: tenderness and mercy precede the negative images of anger and deviation. Assigned role: mercy supplies material softness before the packet later shows hardened wrath. Abductive move: the packet supplies tenderness/softness and hardness/anger; I infer a material contrast in the moral landscape. Reading change: the ayah reads as a softening condition that keeps the path from becoming only a hard juridical surface.

### Retrospective surprises

The later negative pair in 1:7 made 1:3 more dynamic. “Merciful” became not only benevolent but anti-hardening: it stands before the hard compactness of غضب and the disappearance/loss of ضلال as a preserving, softening force.

## 1:4 — مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ

### Activated readings

1. **Ownership of reckoning as custody of the whole account.** Lexical evidence: م ل ك B002, ownership and disposal; د ي ن B002, reckoning and recompense; ن ع م B001 in 1:7, bestowed favor; غ ض ب B001 in 1:7, anger toward retaliation. Structural cue: the ayah sits before the worship/help pledge and before the sorting of favored, angered, and astray. Assigned role: مالك holds the account in which favor and liability are settled. Abductive move: the packet supplies ownership, recompense, favor, and wrath; I infer an accounting mechanism whose final disposal belongs to the owner. Reading change: the Day of dīn reads as custodial settlement, not only a scene of sovereignty.

2. **Dīn as obedience now and recompense later.** Lexical evidence: د ي ن B001, obedience/submission/law; د ي ن B002, reckoning/recompense; ع ب د B003 in 1:5, worship and submissive obedience. Structural cue: “Day of dīn” is immediately followed by “You we worship.” Assigned role: dīn links the present act of submission to a later order of reckoning. Abductive move: the packet supplies the adjacency; I infer a temporal arrow from present obedience to future accounting. Reading change: the ayah reads not as an isolated eschatological line but as the horizon under which 1:5 becomes intelligible.

3. **Debt and favor as an unsettled economy of dependence.** Lexical evidence: د ي ن B003, financial debt/credit; ن ع م B001, benefaction given to another; ع و ن B001 in 1:5, seeking help. Structural cue: the speaker asks for help and later names people upon whom favor was bestowed. Assigned role: dīn’s debt branch makes favor and help feel like obligations that cannot be self-settled. Abductive move: the packet supplies debt, help, and favor; I infer that dependence creates an account held by the مالك. Reading change: “Day of dīn” also reads as the day when received aid and favor are accounted for.

4. **Malik as mainstay and center of the path-order.** Lexical evidence: م ل ك B005, mainstay of a matter; م ل ك B006, center/main course of road or valley; ص ر ط B001 in 1:6–1:7, straight road; ق و م B009 in 1:6, support/mainstay. Structural cue: the path appears after this ayah, and “straight” is built from standing/support. Assigned role: مالك becomes the central controlling support that keeps the road-order from scattering. Abductive move: the packet supplies road/center/mainstay; I infer that judgment is the axis around which the requested path is stabilized. Reading change: the ayah reads as the structural center of the surah’s movement, not only as a title of rule.

5. **The “day” as severe event rather than mere date.** Lexical evidence: ي و م B003, event-day or severe occasion; غ ض ب B001 in 1:7, intensified anger toward retaliation; ض ل ل B002/B003, disappearance/loss. Structural cue: the day is defined by dīn and later by contrasted destinies. Assigned role: يوم marks the crisis-time when hidden loss and provoked anger become manifest. Abductive move: the packet supplies severe occasion and later moral outcomes; I infer that the “day” is an event of exposure and sorting. Reading change: the ayah reads as an occurrence of decisive manifestation, not merely a calendrical period.

### Retrospective surprises

1:6’s path language unexpectedly activated م ل ك B006, the main course of road or valley. That made مالك less exclusively regal and more axial: the Owner of the Day also owns the centerline by which movement and judgment are held together.

## 1:5 — إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ

### Activated readings

1. **Exclusive address as redirecting worship and aid away from rivals.** Lexical evidence: ع ب د B003, worship and submissive obedience; ع و ن B001, help/backing; ء ل ه B001 in 1:1–1:2, worshipped object. Structural cue: إِيَّاكَ is repeated before both verbs. Assigned role: the doubled object sets a boundary around devotion and reliance. Abductive move: the packet supplies repeated exclusivity and branches for worship/help; I infer that the repetition blocks competing objects of worship or sources of backing. Reading change: the ayah reads as a double act of reallocation: service and dependency are assigned to one addressee.

2. **Worship as making the self/path passable.** Lexical evidence: ع ب د B005, physical subduing/smoothing of road; ص ر ط B001/B002 in 1:6, road and passage/swallowing; ق و م B008, straightness/uprightness. Structural cue: “we worship” is followed immediately by “guide us to the straight path.” Assigned role: worship supplies disciplined smoothing; guidance supplies direction; straightness supplies stable form. Abductive move: the packet supplies the sequence worship → request for path; I infer that worship prepares the worshipper to be traversed by guidance or to traverse the path. Reading change: نعبد is not only ritual service; it becomes bodily and practical path-conditioning.

3. **Seeking help as supported walking.** Lexical evidence: ع و ن B001, assistance/backing; ه د ي B008 in 1:6, walking while leaning on support; ه د ي B001, guidance; ق و م B009, support/subsistence. Structural cue: نَسْتَعِينُ precedes ٱهْدِنَا. Assigned role: help becomes the support that makes guided movement possible. Abductive move: the packet supplies help and later supported walking; I infer that the request for aid is concretized as being borne along the path. Reading change: “we ask for help” reads less like generic assistance and more like support for unstable walkers.

4. **Help as strength in a repeated conflict.** Lexical evidence: ع و ن B003, seasoned repeated war; غ ض ب B003 in 1:7, defiance/opposition; ض ل ل B001, deviation from aim. Structural cue: after the request for help, the path is defined against angered and astray patterns. Assigned role: استعانة supplies backing in a conflict where deviation and defiant opposition recur. Abductive move: the packet supplies war/repetition and later opposition/deviation; I infer a struggle-mechanism rather than a single moment of need. Reading change: the ayah reads as enlistment of divine backing for a continuous contest over direction.

### Retrospective surprises

1:6 made ع ب د B005 unexpectedly important: the next ayah’s road shows worship as smoothing and subduing, not only inward devotion. 1:7 then made the repeated إياك a boundary-marker against “otherness” under غير B005.

## 1:6 — ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ

### Activated readings

1. **Guidance as leading from the front, not only informing.** Lexical evidence: ه د ي B001, gentle guidance toward way/truth; ه د ي B003, leading front part; ص ر ط B001, road; ق و م B008, straightness/uprightness. Structural cue: the imperative “guide us” asks for a road, not merely knowledge. Assigned role: هدى is the forward guide; صراط is the traversable field; مستقيم is the upright shape of movement. Abductive move: the packet supplies guide/front/road/straightness; I infer that guidance includes being led ahead. Reading change: the ayah reads as a request for conducted movement, not just instruction.

2. **The straight path as maintained support.** Lexical evidence: ق و م B004, care/guardianship/preservation; ق و م B009, support/mainstay/subsistence; م ل ك B005 in 1:4, mainstay; ر ب ب B002 in 1:2, nurturing/completion. Structural cue: مستقيم is built from ق و م and appears after Lordship, ownership, worship, and help. Assigned role: straightness is the maintained condition by which the path keeps standing. Abductive move: the packet supplies nurture, mainstay, support, and straightness; I infer that the path remains straight because it is upheld, not because it is merely geometrically direct. Reading change: “straight” reads as sustained uprightness and managed stability.

3. **The path as passage that can swallow the traveler.** Lexical evidence: ص ر ط B002, disappearing in passage and swallowing; ض ل ل B002 in 1:7, hiddenness/disappearance; ض ل ل B003, loss. Structural cue: the same صراط is repeated in 1:7 and then contrasted with lost/astray groups. Assigned role: صراط carries an ambivalent passage-image: movement can be safe traversal or disappearance. Abductive move: the packet supplies swallowing and later disappearance/loss; I infer that the prayer asks for a passage that does not erase or lose the traveler. Reading change: the road becomes perilous and absorbing, not merely a clear line.

4. **Guidance as gift/favor.** Lexical evidence: ه د ي B004, gracious gift sent to one close; ن ع م B001 in 1:7, bestowed favor; ر ح م B002/B003 in 1:1–1:3, kinship/womb relation. Structural cue: the next ayah identifies the desired path by those on whom “You bestowed favor.” Assigned role: guidance is a gracious transfer that places the receiver within a favored relation. Abductive move: the packet supplies gift, favor, and relational mercy; I infer that being guided is a received benefaction, not a self-generated achievement. Reading change: “Guide us” reads as “grant us the path as a favoring gift.”

5. **Guidance as composed, non-panicked bearing.** Lexical evidence: ه د ي B010, calm stillness and composed bearing; ع و ن B001 in 1:5, help/backing; غ ض ب B001 and ض ل ل B001 in 1:7, anger and deviation. Structural cue: the request for help precedes the request for guidance, and the following ayah names destabilizing alternatives. Assigned role: الهداية steadies the petitioner against panic, anger, and drift. Abductive move: the packet supplies calm bearing and later destabilizing states; I infer that guidance has an affective-postural dimension. Reading change: the ayah asks for a composed way of moving, not only the correct route.

### Retrospective surprises

1:7 most strongly changed this ayah: the repeated صراط revealed that “straight” is populated by a history of favored people and defined by avoided failure-modes. Also, ض ل ل B002 made ص ر ط B002 surprising: a path can become a swallowing disappearance unless upheld and guided.

## 1:7 — صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

### Activated readings

1. **The path becomes socially embodied.** Lexical evidence: ص ر ط B001, straight road; ن ع م B001, bestowed favor; ع ل م B002 in 1:2, distinguishing mark; ه د ي B001 in 1:6, guidance. Structural cue: 1:6 asks for the path; 1:7 restates it as the path of people, not an abstract route. Assigned role: the favored ones are living markers of the road. Abductive move: the packet supplies road, favor, and sign-marking; I infer that people become waymarks by received favor. Reading change: the path reads as a transmitted social trajectory rather than a private mental direction.

2. **Favor as softening and making the route livable.** Lexical evidence: ن ع م B001, favor/good condition; ن ع م B002, softness/easeful living; ه د ي B004, gracious gift; ق و م B009 in 1:6, subsistence/support. Structural cue: the favored group is opposed to anger and loss. Assigned role: favor makes the path inhabitable, supported, and softened. Abductive move: the packet supplies softness/support and the negative alternatives; I infer that favor alters the material feel of the route. Reading change: أنعمت عليهم reads not only as “gave them blessings” but as “made their way livable and sustained.”

3. **“Not” as active differentiation, not a footnote.** Lexical evidence: غ ي ر B005, otherness/difference/exception/negation; غ ي ر B003, altering/substituting one thing for another. Structural cue: غير stands between the favored path and two failure groups. Assigned role: غير performs the boundary operation that keeps the requested path from being confused with nearby alternatives. Abductive move: the packet supplies difference and substitution; I infer that negation actively changes orientation by excluding false routes. Reading change: the final clause reads as path-engineering through differentiation, not merely as a warning.

4. **Wrath as hardened opposition.** Lexical evidence: غ ض ب B001, intensified anger toward retaliation; غ ض ب B003, defiance/opposition; غ ض ب B004, hard compact rock; ر ح م B001 in 1:1–1:3, tenderness. Structural cue: “angered” is opposed to mercy/favor and placed before “astray.” Assigned role: غضب is a hardened, oppositional state that attracts judgment. Abductive move: the packet supplies anger, defiance, hardness, and earlier tenderness; I infer that wrath marks a hardening against the merciful order. Reading change: المغضوب عليهم reads as people fixed in resistant hardness, not only recipients of an emotion.

5. **Going astray as disappearance, loss, and ownerless wandering.** Lexical evidence: ض ل ل B001, deviation from guidance/aim; ض ل ل B002, hiddenness/disappearance; ض ل ل B003, losing/failing to locate; ض ل ل B005, stray animal in wasteland; ر ب ب B001/B002 in 1:2, owner/caretaker. Structural cue: the surah began with Lord/caretaker and now ends with those who are lost or stray. Assigned role: ضلال is the breakdown of guidance, visibility, ownership, and care. Abductive move: the packet supplies Lord/caretaker and lost/stray branches; I infer that straying is not only error but loss from a care-system. Reading change: الضالين reads as the ownerless/lost condition from which the prayer seeks rescue.

6. **The final contrast as legal-moral settlement.** Lexical evidence: د ي ن B002/B003 in 1:4, recompense and debt; غ ي ر B002, legal compensation alternative; غ ض ب B001, anger toward retaliation; ن ع م B001, benefaction. Structural cue: “Day of dīn” precedes “favored / angered / astray.” Assigned role: the final triad shows the account’s possible settlements: favor, wrath, or loss. Abductive move: the packet supplies recompense/debt/compensation and the triadic outcome; I infer a legal-moral sorting mechanism. Reading change: 1:7 reads as the requested verdict-shape of the worshipper’s account, not only a route description.

### Retrospective surprises

Because this is the final ayah, no later ayah supplied a further surprise. Its own backward activation was strong: it reinterpreted earlier mercy as protective kinship, رب as care/ownership, دِين as account-settlement, worship as path-smoothing, and guidance as rescue from disappearance.

# Turkish Prose Synthesis

## 1:1 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Bu başlangıç yalnızca bir besmele formülü değil; sözün tamamını Allah’ın yükseltilmiş adı altında başlatan bir eşik gibi okunur. Rahmet iki kez gelerek sonraki rablik, hesap, kulluk ve hidayet taleplerinin üstüne bir örtü kurar. Sonraki ayetler geri dönüp bu rahmeti sadece şefkat değil, yakınlık, rahim, bakım ve korunmuş aidiyet düzeni olarak da gösterir.

## 1:2 — ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

Hamd, yalnızca övgü sözü değil, görülen düzenin denenip övülmeye layık bulunmasıdır. “Âlemlerin Rabbi” ifadesinde Rab, sadece sahip ve hükümran değil, âlemleri aşama aşama yetiştiren, onaran ve tamamlayan bakıcıdır. Âlemler de işaretsiz boş alanlar değil, yolu gösteren alametlerle dolu okunabilir sahalardır. Son ayetteki nimet, gazap ve dalalet ayrımı bu hamdi ahlaki olarak sınıflanmış bir dünya içinde duyulur kılar.

## 1:3 — ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Rahmetin tekrarı, âlemlerin Rabbi ile din gününün sahibi arasında bir köprü kurar. Böylece hükümranlık çıplak güç, hesap da çıplak ceza gibi duyulmaz; ikisi de rahmet çerçevesinde okunur. Daha sonra gazabın sertliği ve dalaletin kayboluşu görünür olunca bu ayet, yolun sertleşmeye ve yitip gitmeye karşı yumuşatıcı bağı gibi belirir.

## 1:4 — مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ

“Din gününün sahibi” ifadesi, hesabın tamamını elinde tutan malik anlamına gelir. Din burada hem şimdi yaşanan itaat ve kulluk düzenini, hem de ilerideki hesap ve karşılığı taşır. Nimet, yardım ve borç çağrışımlarıyla bu ayet, alınan lütufların ve yönelişlerin hesabının kime ait olduğunu gösterir. Yol imgeleri geri dönünce Malik, sadece kral değil, yolu ve hesabı ayakta tutan merkez eksen gibi de okunur.

## 1:5 — إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ

Tekrar edilen “yalnız sana” ifadesi kulluğu ve yardım talebini başka bütün odaklardan çekip Allah’a yöneltir. Kulluk, sadece ibadet hali değil, insanı yola elverişli kılan bir terbiye ve düzleştirme olarak açılır. Yardım istemek de soyut bir destek talebi olmaktan çıkar; sonraki hidayet ayetiyle birlikte, zayıf yürüyenin tutulup yolda yürütülmesi gibi duyulur. Gazap ve dalalet karşısında bu yardım sürekli bir yön mücadelesinin desteğidir.

## 1:6 — ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ

“Bizi hidayet et” demek yalnızca bilgi ver demek değildir; önden götür, yolu göstererek yürüt demektir. Sırat, üzerinde yürünecek yol olduğu kadar insanı içine alıp kaybettirebilecek bir geçiş imgesi de taşır. “Müstakim” oluşu, yolun yalnız geometrik düzlüğü değil, korunmuş, ayakta tutulan ve desteklenen istikametidir. Son ayet bu yolu soyut olmaktan çıkarır: nimet verilenlerin yolu ve gazap ile dalaletten ayrılan bir toplumsal iz haline getirir.

## 1:7 — صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

İstenen yol, nimet verilmiş insanların yolu olarak somutlaşır; bu insanlar yolun canlı işaretleri olur. Nimet, sadece ihsan değil, yolu yaşanabilir, yumuşak ve destekli kılan bir lütuftur. “Gayr” kelimesi basit bir “değil”den fazlasını yapar: yolu öteki ihtimallerden ayırır. Gazaba uğrayanlar rahmet düzenine karşı sertleşmiş muhalefeti, dalalette olanlar ise görünürlüğünü, yönünü ve sahibini kaybetmiş başıboşluğu gösterir. Böylece son ayet, bütün surenin talebini açar: rahmetle başlayan söz, bakım ve hesap içinde, kaybolmayan ve sertleşmeyen bir yola çevrilmek ister.
