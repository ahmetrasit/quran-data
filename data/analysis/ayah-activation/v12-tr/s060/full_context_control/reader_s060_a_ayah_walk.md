# Surah 60 Full-Context Reader: Ayah Walk

## 60:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Mercy as kin-making care.** Lexically, both divine adjectives carry ر ح م B001 (mercy and tenderness), while the same root later appears as أَرْحَامُكُمْ in 60:3 under B002 (kinship bond). The structural cue is the root's movement from divine naming to human kinship, followed by the protection and reception of displaced women in 60:10-12. The divine adjectives supply beneficent care; kinship supplies the social form that such care can create. I infer that mercy can generate obligations analogous to kinship even when biological ties fail. The opening therefore reads not only as an invocation of compassion but as the enabling relation for a community reorganized around protected persons.

2. **A named authority over competing public designations.** س م و B005 makes ٱسْم a raised designation, and ء ل ه B001 supplies the worshipped one. Later, 60:1 contrasts what is hidden and declared, while 60:4 and 60:12 stage declarations of allegiance and covenant. The name functions as the publicly raised authority under which those affiliations are tested. I supply the directional claim that invocation places the ensuing social judgments beneath that designation. The basmalah thus reads as an act of jurisdiction, not merely a prefatory label.

### Retrospective surprises

60:3 unexpectedly reactivates ر ح م as literal kinship, making the opening mercy available as a model for bonds that biological kin cannot guarantee. The migrant-women sequence in 60:10-12 then gives that mercy institutional form through protection, settlement, covenant, and forgiveness.

## 60:1 — يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ لَا تَتَّخِذُوا۟ عَدُوِّى وَعَدُوَّكُمْ أَوْلِيَآءَ تُلْقُونَ إِلَيْهِم بِٱلْمَوَدَّةِ وَقَدْ كَفَرُوا۟ بِمَا جَآءَكُم مِّنَ ٱلْحَقِّ يُخْرِجُونَ ٱلرَّسُولَ وَإِيَّاكُمْ ۙ أَن تُؤْمِنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ رَبِّكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ خَرَجْتُمْ جِهَٰدًۭا فِى سَبِيلِى وَٱبْتِغَآءَ مَرْضَاتِى ۚ تُسِرُّونَ إِلَيْهِم بِٱلْمَوَدَّةِ وَأَنَا۠ أَعْلَمُ بِمَآ أَخْفَيْتُمْ وَمَآ أَعْلَنتُمْ ۚ وَمَن يَفْعَلْهُ مِنكُمْ فَقَدْ ضَلَّ سَوَآءَ ٱلسَّبِيلِ

### Activated readings

1. **Alliance as an adopted operating position.** ء خ ذ B009 permits “taking on a path or character,” while و ل ي B003 and B004 supply charge/authority and loyal alliance. The sequence تَتَّخِذُوا ... أَوْلِيَاءَ makes the act more than private warmth: it installs an enemy relation as a governing affiliation. I infer that what one adopts begins to direct one's conduct. The prohibition therefore reads as resistance to letting a hostile party become an organizing authority, not as a ban on every form of contact.

2. **Affection cast as actionable transmission.** ل ق ي B005 gives casting or placing something before another, and و د د B001 gives affection and attachment; the same مَوَدَّة is repeated after تُسِرُّونَ, whose س ر ر B001 supplies inward concealment and private communication. The casting image assigns affection the role of a transferred social signal, with secrecy serving as its channel. I infer that a concealed affective signal can enable the hostile expulsion already named in the ayah. The reading shifts from “feeling love” to covertly delivering attachment in a way that changes allegiance.

3. **A route displaced from its fair middle.** س ب ل B001 supplies a traversed means or course, س و ي B006 supplies the middle, fairness, and an even meeting place, and ض ل ل B001 supplies deviation from an aim. The ayah first names “My path” and closes with “the middle/equity of the path,” framing the intervening concealment as a route error. I infer that the decisive deviation is misdirected relational traffic, not mere spatial wandering. The close therefore diagnoses loss of the path's fair alignment between declared striving and hidden conduct.

### Retrospective surprises

60:7 is the strongest correction: the same و د د that is covertly cast toward active enemies here can later be made into a legitimate relation by God. Then 60:8-9 supplies the missing criterion, separating noncombatants eligible for kindness and equity from fighters, expellers, and their backers; this confirms that the target is hostile governing allegiance rather than affection as such.

## 60:2 — إِن يَثْقَفُوكُمْ يَكُونُوا۟ لَكُمْ أَعْدَآءًۭ وَيَبْسُطُوٓا۟ إِلَيْكُمْ أَيْدِيَهُمْ وَأَلْسِنَتَهُم بِٱلسُّوٓءِ وَوَدُّوا۟ لَوْ تَكْفُرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Encounter becomes capture and full-spectrum reach.** ث ق ف B003 supplies catching sight of and getting hold of someone; ب س ط B004 supplies extending the hand for taking or striking, while B005 supplies openness or facility of tongue. Their sequence moves from acquisition to bodily and verbal extension. I infer that once the hostile party gains positional control, latent capacities become active channels of harm. The conditional reads as a mechanics of exposure: being found is the switch that releases both coercive force and speech.

2. **Their desired result is covering, not merely disagreement.** و د د B002 gives wishing that something occur, while ك ف ر B003 gives rejection or concealment of truth and B001 retains the physical image of covering. The final wish follows hands and tongues extended بِالسُّوءِ, making the desired endpoint the addressees' own covering-over of what they affirm. I supply the causal arrow that pressure aims to produce inward or public abandonment. The ayah thus reads as an attempted conversion of external harm into self-concealment.

### Retrospective surprises

60:8-9 retrospectively narrows this conditional exposure mechanism to parties engaged in combat and displacement. The bodily “hands” here also acquire a striking contrast in 60:12, where hands and feet mark responsibility for fabrication inside a covenant rather than instruments extended from outside in harm.

## 60:3 — لَن تَنفَعَكُمْ أَرْحَامُكُمْ وَلَآ أَوْلَٰدُكُمْ ۚ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ يَفْصِلُ بَيْنَكُمْ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Kinship ceases to function as an advantage-bearing bond.** ر ح م B002 supplies the kinship bond and ن ف ع B001 benefit as the opposite of harm. ف ص ل B001 supplies separation, while ب ي ن B003 unusually preserves the tie or connection between parties. The structure says that the very “between” which normally joins kin will be acted on by separation. I infer that kinship benefits only while its connective force is allowed to transfer advantage. The ayah reads as disabling a social conduit, not denying that the conduit exists.

2. **Judgment articulates relations according to enacted evidence.** ف ص ل B002 supplies judgment separating truth from falsehood, ع م ل B001 intentional action, and ب ص ر B002 inner sight and penetrating knowledge. Their order moves from relational separation to divine sight of conduct. I infer that action provides the differentiating evidence by which relations are reassigned. The day of rising therefore reads as a disclosure and adjudication of what bonds actually did, not an arbitrary severance of family.

### Retrospective surprises

60:10-11 supplies a concrete later analogue of separation: marital bonds crossing hostile affiliation are unbound, yet their financial residue is adjudicated and compensated. This makes يَفْصِلُ read less like indiscriminate rupture and more like precise differentiation of relations and their remaining claims.

## 60:4 — قَدْ كَانَتْ لَكُمْ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌۭ فِىٓ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥٓ إِذْ قَالُوا۟ لِقَوْمِهِمْ إِنَّا بُرَءَٰٓؤُا۟ مِنكُمْ وَمِمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ كَفَرْنَا بِكُمْ وَبَدَا بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَكُمُ ٱلْعَدَٰوَةُ وَٱلْبَغْضَآءُ أَبَدًا حَتَّىٰ تُؤْمِنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ وَحْدَهُۥٓ إِلَّا قَوْلَ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ لِأَبِيهِ لَأَسْتَغْفِرَنَّ لَكَ وَمَآ أَمْلِكُ لَكَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مِن شَىْءٍۢ ۖ رَّبَّنَا عَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ أَنَبْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ ٱلْمَصِيرُ

### Activated readings

1. **The exemplar is simultaneously a pattern and a treatment.** ء س و B003 supplies an exemplar to follow, but B001 supplies healing a wound and B002 reconciliation through shared support; ح س ن B002 adds skillful beneficent action. The repeated أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ in 60:6 confirms a reproducible model, while this ayah's damaged “between” gives it something to treat. I infer that correct imitation repairs communal integrity by exposing compromised attachment. The example reads not only as conduct to copy but as a disciplined remedy for a wounded allegiance.

2. **Disavowal is a clearing operation that makes the relation visible.** ب ر ء B002 gives clearance and separation; ك ف ر B005 gives disavowal; ب د و B001 gives visible emergence and B004 open declaration of hostility; ب ي ن B003 supplies the relation between parties. The sequence clears an affiliation, repudiates it, and then makes the resulting relational state appear. I infer that the hostility is not manufactured by speech but becomes operationally visible once concealed compromise is removed. The ayah reads as public clarification of an already incompatible worship relation.

3. **The exception limits human control while redirecting dependence.** م ل ك B002 supplies ownership and disposal in أَمْلِكُ, و ك ل B001 and B002 supply handing over and reliance, ن و ب B001 habitual return, and ص ي ر B001 the final endpoint. The exception concerning Abraham's father is followed by a denial of disposal, then a triple directional movement toward God. I infer that promised intercession fails when treated as possessed leverage; reliance must move to the authority controlling the endpoint. The close converts relational helplessness into delegated trust and recurring return rather than private guarantee.

### Retrospective surprises

60:7 surprisingly places divinely made affection after the declaration of enduring enmity, revealing the duration here as condition-bound by حَتَّىٰ rather than an absolute command to preserve hatred. The permission for beneficence in 60:8 further corrects the exemplar toward principled disavowal of hostile worship and allegiance, not generalized social harshness.

## 60:5 — رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةًۭ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ وَٱغْفِرْ لَنَا رَبَّنَآ ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ

### Activated readings

1. **A community can be made into hostile evidence.** ج ع ل B002 supplies turning something into a state, and ف ت ن B001 supplies assaying by trial while B003 supplies diversion from the right path. The object نَا is what may be made into the test for those who reject. I infer that the community's defeat, misconduct, or vulnerability could be read by opponents as assay-results validating rejection. The prayer therefore asks not to become material from which others manufacture a misleading verdict.

2. **Forgiveness is protective containment before decisive governance.** غ ف ر B001 supplies protective covering and B002 forgiveness that shields from consequence; ع ز ز B001 and B002 supply invulnerable might and prevailing, while ح ك م B001 and B003 supply restraint that sets right and wise judgment. Their order moves from requested shielding to attributes able to restrain and decide. I infer that forgiveness prevents faults from becoming an uncontrolled social weapon while wisdom corrects them. The prayer seeks neither concealment without correction nor exposure without protection.

### Retrospective surprises

60:10's م ح ن B001 examination that reveals what is within makes the assaying force of ف ت ن B001 newly concrete: the danger is becoming misleading test-material, whereas a valid test uses bounded evidence. The return of غ ف ر and ر ح م in 60:12 also shows protective forgiveness operating at the admission of new covenant members.

## 60:6 — لَقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِيهِمْ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌۭ لِّمَن كَانَ يَرْجُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَٱلْيَوْمَ ٱلْءَاخِرَ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلْغَنِىُّ ٱلْحَمِيدُ

### Activated readings

1. **The model trains expectation beyond the immediate relation.** ء س و B003 supplies the followable exemplar, ر ج و B001 expectant hope, and ء خ ر B001 what comes later. The exemplary break in 60:4 is now assigned specifically to one whose expectation extends toward God and the later day. I infer that deferred evaluation enables costly present conduct. The model reads as temporal training: it relocates the expected return that would otherwise be sought from allies or kin.

2. **Turning away cannot impoverish the authority refused.** و ل ي B007 supplies turning away and withdrawal in يَتَوَلَّ, while غ ن ي B001 and B002 supply self-sufficiency and needing no substitute; ح م د B001 supplies praise for praiseworthy action. The conditional withdrawal is answered not by threat of divine loss but by sufficiency and praiseworthiness. I infer that refusal damages the refuser's alignment, not God's resources or standing. The close reads as a correction of bargaining logic: allegiance is not a contribution God needs.

### Retrospective surprises

60:7 immediately prevents the exemplar from hardening into permanent relational closure: enmity can become affection. The final recurrence of تَتَوَلَّوْا in 60:13 clarifies that the rejected turn is toward a group whose loss of hope closes the later horizon that this ayah explicitly asks the reader to expect.

## 60:7 — ۞ عَسَى ٱللَّهُ أَن يَجْعَلَ بَيْنَكُمْ وَبَيْنَ ٱلَّذِينَ عَادَيْتُم مِّنْهُم مَّوَدَّةًۭ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ قَدِيرٌۭ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٌۭ رَّحِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **The relational interval can be remade rather than merely crossed.** ج ع ل B002 supplies transformation into a state, ب ي ن B003 supplies the tie or connection between parties, ع د و B003 supplies enmity, and و د د B001 supplies affectionate attachment. The double بَيْنَ locates the change in the relation itself: enmity's “between” may be made into affection's “between.” I infer that changed conditions can reorganize the shared social field without denying the prior hostility. The ayah reads as transformation of relation, not simply a private change of feeling.

2. **Power, forgiveness, and mercy form the causal conditions of reversal.** ق د ر B003 gives capacity reaching effective control, غ ف ر B002 gives forgiveness shielding from consequence, and ر ح م B001 gives beneficent tenderness. Their sequence follows the proposed creation of affection. I supply the arrow that effective power makes reversal possible, forgiveness removes retained offense, and mercy gives the new relation positive content. The divine attributes therefore operate as a mechanism for a socially improbable repair.

### Retrospective surprises

60:8-9 reveals the human conditions under which this remaking can become socially legible: equity remains open toward noncombatants, while alliance remains barred toward the machinery of expulsion. The procedures in 60:10-12 then show that restored relation is administered through examination, reciprocal claims, and covenant rather than sentiment alone.

## 60:8 — لَّا يَنْهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمْ يُقَٰتِلُوكُمْ فِى ٱلدِّينِ وَلَمْ يُخْرِجُوكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمْ أَن تَبَرُّوهُمْ وَتُقْسِطُوٓا۟ إِلَيْهِمْ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلْمُقْسِطِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Non-aggression leaves a positive field of expansive good.** ن ه ي B001 supplies restraint from an act, ق ت ل B011 reciprocal combat, and خ ر ج B002 forced emergence; their negation removes the conditions that would trigger restraint. ب ر ر B002 then supplies expansive righteousness and broad good, while B003 adds kindness against estrangement. I infer that absence of combat and expulsion is not a neutral vacuum but permits active beneficence. The ayah reads as authorization of robust social good, not mere tolerance.

2. **Equity is directed toward the other party and loved as a repeated practice.** ق س ط B001 supplies giving each due share in both تُقْسِطُوا and ٱلْمُقْسِطِينَ, and ح ب ب B002 supplies clinging attachment in يُحِبُّ. The verb is directed إِلَيْهِمْ and returns as an agent noun, turning one equitable act into a stable identity. I infer that due-sharing preserves relation without assigning hostile authority. This corrects any reading of 60:1 that would collapse justice toward outsiders into forbidden allegiance.

### Retrospective surprises

60:10-11 turns ق س ط's due-sharing into a detailed reciprocal payment system across broken marriages, confirming that benevolence does not erase claims. In 60:12, مَعْرُوف constrains covenantal obedience by recognizable good, a later ethical counterpart to the equity authorized here.

## 60:9 — إِنَّمَا يَنْهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ قَٰتَلُوكُمْ فِى ٱلدِّينِ وَأَخْرَجُوكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمْ وَظَٰهَرُوا۟ عَلَىٰٓ إِخْرَاجِكُمْ أَن تَوَلَّوْهُمْ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّهُمْ فَأُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **The prohibited relation is defined by an aggression stack.** ق ت ل B011 supplies mutual combat, خ ر ج B002 causing removal, and ظ ه ر B006 backing and support. The sequence distinguishes direct fighting, direct expulsion, and logistical or political support for expulsion. I infer that these roles accumulate into a hostile system even when one participant does not perform every act. The restrictive إِنَّمَا therefore reads as a criterion for alliance based on participation in coercive displacement.

2. **Taking them into one's charge misplaces and withholds right.** و ل ي B003 and B004 give assuming charge and loyal support, while ظ ل م B004 gives concrete misplacement and B008 withholding rights. The repeated تَوَلَّوْهُمْ / يَتَوَلَّهُمْ attaches the wrong to an adopted support relation. I infer that loyalty to the expelling system relocates protective support away from those deprived of home and thereby withholds their due. “Wrongdoers” reads as a relational and distributive diagnosis, not only a generic moral label.

### Retrospective surprises

60:10 responds directly to expulsion by forbidding the return of tested migrant women and by adjudicating the bonds and expenditures left across the divide. This makes “backing expulsion” here visible as support for a whole coercive order whose consequences require legal repair, not as a loose association with disagreeable people.

## 60:10 — يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ إِذَا جَآءَكُمُ ٱلْمُؤْمِنَٰتُ مُهَٰجِرَٰتٍۢ فَٱمْتَحِنُوهُنَّ ۖ ٱللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ بِإِيمَٰنِهِنَّ ۖ فَإِنْ عَلِمْتُمُوهُنَّ مُؤْمِنَٰتٍۢ فَلَا تَرْجِعُوهُنَّ إِلَى ٱلْكُفَّارِ ۖ لَا هُنَّ حِلٌّۭ لَّهُمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحِلُّونَ لَهُنَّ ۖ وَءَاتُوهُم مَّآ أَنفَقُوا۟ ۚ وَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيْكُمْ أَن تَنكِحُوهُنَّ إِذَآ ءَاتَيْتُمُوهُنَّ أُجُورَهُنَّ ۚ وَلَا تُمْسِكُوا۟ بِعِصَمِ ٱلْكَوَافِرِ وَسْـَٔلُوا۟ مَآ أَنفَقْتُمْ وَلْيَسْـَٔلُوا۟ مَآ أَنفَقُوا۟ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ حُكْمُ ٱللَّهِ ۖ يَحْكُمُ بَيْنَكُمْ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Human examination reveals without claiming divine penetration.** ه ج ر B002 supplies movement from one abode to another; م ح ن B001 supplies testing that reveals what is within and B002 refinement after testing; ع ل م B001 supplies apprehended knowledge. The sequence places ٱمْتَحِنُوهُنَّ between the women's arrival and the contrast “God knows best / if you come to know.” I infer that humans must establish actionable knowledge through a procedure while leaving exhaustive inward knowledge to God. The examination reads as a bounded evidentiary assay, not authorization to presume omniscience.

2. **Migration changes which marital hold can protect.** ر ج ع B001 supplies sending back to a prior place or owner; ح ل ل B001 gives untying and B003 removal of prohibition; م س ك B001 gives holding and restraining; ع ص م B001 and B002 give a protective hold and fastening. The ayah first forbids return, then states reciprocal non-licitness, then forbids holding the former bonds. I infer that a bond loses protective validity when it would function as a fastening back into the rejecting polity. The legal language reads as reclassification of the bond's function, not treatment of the woman as transferable property.

3. **Reciprocal expenditure repairs the material edge of separation.** ء ت ي B002 gives granting, ن ف ق B002 wealth going out, ء ج ر B001 contractual recompense including dowry, س ء ل B001 requesting, and ح ك م B002 adjudication among people. The repeated spending and asking clauses mirror both sides around new marriage. I infer that relational rupture leaves financial outflows which judgment must balance so protection of migrants does not erase recoverable claims. The ruling therefore couples asylum and marital freedom with reciprocal economic accounting.

### Retrospective surprises

60:11 addresses the residual case where the reciprocal accounting described here does not arrive through the first channel; succession and equivalent payment repair that asymmetry. Then 60:12 receives believing women through their own reciprocal pledge, confirming their role as accountable covenant agents after the evidentiary protection of this ayah.

## 60:11 — وَإِن فَاتَكُمْ شَىْءٌۭ مِّنْ أَزْوَٰجِكُمْ إِلَى ٱلْكُفَّارِ فَعَاقَبْتُمْ فَـَٔاتُوا۟ ٱلَّذِينَ ذَهَبَتْ أَزْوَٰجُهُم مِّثْلَ مَآ أَنفَقُوا۟ ۚ وَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنتُم بِهِۦ مُؤْمِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **An unreachable spouse becomes a compensable gap, not a recoverable possession.** ف و ت B001 supplies being beyond reach and B004 a gap between things; ز و ج B002 supplies spouse and B001 paired counterpart; ذ ه ب B006 supplies departure. The syntax moves from what escapes reach to the spouses who have gone, without commanding their physical retrieval. I infer that once the personal relation cannot be restored, the remaining legally tractable loss is the material gap. The ayah reads as compensation after relational departure, not forced repossession.

2. **Succession converts asymmetric loss into equivalent return.** ع ق ب B005 supplies following, replacing, or alternating, ء ت ي B002 granting, م ث ل B001 likeness/equivalence, and ن ف ق B002 expended wealth. The following turn supplies funds matching what went out. I infer that the communal sequence can carry value forward when the original bilateral exchange fails. The mechanism is restorative substitution: equivalent expenditure is returned to the one whose pairing has departed.

3. **Protective caution limits the compensation mechanism.** و ق ي B002 gives placing oneself in protective moral caution, and ء م ن B002 gives trusting assent. Their final proximity makes faith the stated relation within which the settlement operates. I infer that fear of opportunistic overclaiming or retaliatory treatment requires self-restraint beyond accounting alone. The close reads as a guardrail on restitution.

### Retrospective surprises

60:12 shifts from compensating a departed pairing to constituting new membership by reciprocal pledge. That later move prevents the present settlement from defining displaced women only through marital and financial loss.

## 60:12 — يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِىُّ إِذَا جَآءَكَ ٱلْمُؤْمِنَٰتُ يُبَايِعْنَكَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن لَّا يُشْرِكْنَ بِٱللَّهِ شَيْـًۭٔا وَلَا يَسْرِقْنَ وَلَا يَزْنِينَ وَلَا يَقْتُلْنَ أَوْلَٰدَهُنَّ وَلَا يَأْتِينَ بِبُهْتَٰنٍۢ يَفْتَرِينَهُۥ بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِنَّ وَأَرْجُلِهِنَّ وَلَا يَعْصِينَكَ فِى مَعْرُوفٍۢ ۙ فَبَايِعْهُنَّ وَٱسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُنَّ ٱللَّهَ ۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورٌۭ رَّحِيمٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **The pledge is a reciprocal compact that converts arrival into accountable membership.** ب ي ع B003 supplies a pledge of obedience framed as compact, and its repetition in يُبَايِعْنَكَ / فَبَايِعْهُنَّ makes the action mutual; ج ي ء B001 supplies arrival. The women arrive as agents offering a covenant and receive a corresponding imperative of acceptance. I infer that membership is constituted through reciprocal undertaking rather than passive admission. The ayah reads as formal incorporation of the arriving believers.

2. **Fabricated accusation has both an authorship and a bodily-spatial reading.** ب ه ت B002 supplies a confounding false accusation, ف ر ي B003 fabrication, ي د ي B009 deeds attributed to one's hands, and ر ج ل B001 bodily feet/legs. The construction places the fabrication “between their hands and feet.” On one reading, the limbs assign the lie to embodied authorship: it is produced and carried by their own action. On a coexisting spatial reading, ب ي ن B002 marks the literal bodily interval in which a falsely attributed child or offense could be presented. The packet supplies the body and fabrication; I infer the two causal scopes and retain both rather than collapse them.

3. **Obedience is bounded by recognizable good.** ع ص ي B001 supplies leaving obedience, while ع ر ف B003 gives recognition by a trace and B005 recognized good approved as proper conduct. The prepositional bound فِى مَعْرُوفٍ attaches non-disobedience to a knowable moral field. I infer that the pledge does not create unqualified personal control; commands remain answerable to recognizable good. The covenant reads as principled obedience, not unrestricted submission.

4. **Forgiveness protects the new covenant from being reduced to prior fault.** غ ف ر B002 supplies pardon and shielding from consequence, and ر ح م B001 adds beneficent tenderness. The command to accept the pledge is immediately followed by seeking forgiveness for the pledgers. I infer that admission requires both present commitments and a protected route beyond prior violations. The close makes mercy part of covenant administration rather than an unrelated blessing.

### Retrospective surprises

60:13's final alliance prohibition makes the pledge's limit فِى مَعْرُوفٍ more consequential: covenantal obedience cannot be redirected into loyalty to a polity defined by adverse judgment and despair of the later life. No other later ayah adds a further correction.

## 60:13 — يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ لَا تَتَوَلَّوْا۟ قَوْمًا غَضِبَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيْهِمْ قَدْ يَئِسُوا۟ مِنَ ٱلْءَاخِرَةِ كَمَا يَئِسَ ٱلْكُفَّارُ مِنْ أَصْحَٰبِ ٱلْقُبُورِ

### Activated readings

1. **The final alliance warning concerns a polity closed to future correction.** و ل ي B003 and B004 supply assuming charge and loyal alliance; ق و م B001 supplies a social group; غ ض ب B001 supplies intensified displeasure moving toward requital; ي ء س B001 supplies loss of expectation; and ء خ ر B001 supplies what comes later. Their sequence defines the group by both adverse judgment and despair of the later horizon. I infer that alliance with such a group installs a temporal regime in which no later reckoning can correct present interest. The prohibition reads as resistance to governance by foreclosed hope.

2. **Companionship at the graves supports two retained scopes of despair.** ص ح ب B001 supplies companionship or close association, while ق ب ر B001 supplies the resting place of the dead and B002 hidden inward sinking. In one scope, the disbelievers despair of the grave's companions: the buried are beyond expected return or aid. In another, the comparison projects the hopeless condition associated with those enclosed and hidden in graves onto the living rejecters. The grammar supplies comparison but not its directional explanation; I supply these two mechanisms and retain both. Either way, the final image makes despair a severance of effective companionship across death.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah exists in the packet, so no retrospective activation emerged.
