# Surah 23 Full-Context Ayah Walk

## 23:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A named field of generative mercy.** `س م و B005` makes *ism* a raised designation, while the doubled `ر ح م B001/B003` supplies tenderness and womb-like enclosure. The later creation sequence (23:12–14) supplies the structural cue: life is formed inside measured, protected stages. I infer that naming establishes the governing field under which those stages operate; thus the formula reads not only as invocation but as entry into a life-producing economy of mercy.

### Retrospective surprises

The womb branch became materially vivid only after 23:12–14, and 23:118 retrospectively turned the opening mercy into the surah's closing request.

## 23:1 — قَدْ أَفْلَحَ ٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Success as cultivated cleavage.** Besides lasting success (`ف ل ح B005`), `ف ل ح B001/B003` contributes cleaving and tilling. The packet moves from disciplined practices (23:2–9) to inheritance (23:10–11), then later returns to heavy scales and *mufliḥūn* (23:102). I supply the arrow sustained practices → opened/cultivated ground → durable yield; the ayah therefore announces a process-bearing success, not a bare verdict.
2. **Security becoming confirmation.** `ء م ن B001/B002` preserves both settled trust and assenting confirmation. The paired opening and closing uses of `ف ل ح` (23:1, 102) make belief read simultaneously as inward security and as a publicly weighed commitment.

### Retrospective surprises

23:102 unexpectedly supplies the surah's second half of *falāḥ*: what is announced here is later rendered as measurable weight.

## 23:2 — ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ فِى صَلَاتِهِمْ خَٰشِعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Prayer as lowered, receptive terrain.** `ص ل و B003` anchors prescribed worship; `خ ش ع B001/B002` adds humble lowering and low desolate ground. The later measured descent of water into earth (23:18–20) provides a material analogy. I infer that lowering removes resistance and makes reception possible; prayer reads as a cultivated receptive condition, not merely an performed rite.
2. **Following under discipline.** `ص ل و B006` preserves following a leader in second place. In proximity to the guarded repetition of prayers at 23:9, this branch assigns prayer the role of recurrent alignment behind an ordering lead; the reading changes from isolated devotion to trained following.

### Retrospective surprises

23:18–20 made the low-ground image productive rather than merely abject, while 23:9 corrected any one-time reading by insisting on repeated guarding.

## 23:3 — وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ عَنِ ٱللَّغْوِ مُعْرِضُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Refusal to let noise count.** `ل غ و B001/B003` combines what is excluded from the count with raised noise; `ع ر ض B005` is turning one's side away. Later, boastful night talk and raving speech (23:67) are set against deliberation of the saying (23:68). I infer attention confers practical weight; turning aside blocks noise → counted guidance, so the ayah reads as active control of the economy of attention.
2. **Side-turning as protection from deviation.** `ل غ و B005` allows deviation from right, while `ع ر ض B005` gives a bodily side-turn. The path/sideward pair at 23:73–74 clarifies the mechanism: one avoids becoming a path-veering person by first turning away from deviant speech.

### Retrospective surprises

23:67–74 transformed an apparently verbal ethic into path mechanics: night speech, failed deliberation, and sideward deviation all expose what this early turning-away prevents.

## 23:4 — وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ لِلزَّكَوٰةِ فَٰعِلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Purification as enacted production.** `ز ك و B001/B002` preserves growth and purification; `ف ع ل B001` makes an act come into being. The water–garden–food sequence (23:18–21) supplies a growth chain rather than a static possession. I infer giving/doing removes blockage and enables increase; *zakāh* reads as a produced ecology of cleansing growth.
2. **Suitability made real.** `ز ك و B004` is fitness or suitability, and `ف ع ل B002` can denote conduct. Set beside 23:51's good food and sound work, the ayah reads as repeatedly making one's resources and conduct fit for their proper use.

### Retrospective surprises

23:18–21 supplied the agricultural mechanism, while 23:51 widened *doing zakāh* into the linkage between sound intake and sound action.

## 23:5 — وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ لِفُرُوجِهِمْ حَٰفِظُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Guarding the vulnerable opening.** `ف ر ج B003/B004/B007` retains private parts, a vulnerable frontier gap, and exposure of what should be covered; `ح ف ظ B001/B006` supplies preservation of sanctity and chastity. The following legal exceptions (23:6–7) define the boundary. I infer that an opening becomes harmful when detached from covenantal protection; the ayah reads as frontier governance, bodily and social at once.

### Retrospective surprises

23:62 later sharpened the justice dimension: guarded boundaries belong to a wider order in which no self is burdened beyond capacity and none is wronged.

## 23:6 — إِلَّا عَلَىٰٓ أَزْوَٰجِهِمْ أَوْ مَا مَلَكَتْ أَيْمَٰنُهُمْ فَإِنَّهُمْ غَيْرُ مَلُومِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Authorized relation as cohesive right.** `ز و ج B001/B002` gives paired counterpart and spouse; `م ل ك B001/B002` gives cohesion plus lawful disposal; `ي م ن B002/B004` supplies right hand and rightful force. Grammar marks these as exceptions to 23:5. I infer recognized relation → protected access → absence of blame (`ل و م B001`); the ayah reads as defining accountable permission, not suspending accountability.
2. **Otherness negated.** `غ ي ر B005` performs explicit otherness/negation before `ل و م B001`. Against 23:7's boundary-crossers, this construction reads as a sharply delimited non-blame category rather than a general license.

### Retrospective surprises

The repeated ownership/dominion language at 23:88, 91, and 116 later placed human *milk* under a much larger sovereignty, limiting any autonomous reading of possession.

## 23:7 — فَمَنِ ٱبْتَغَىٰ وَرَآءَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْعَادُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Desire crossing a concealed far side.** `ب غ ي B001/B003` combines pursuit with oppressive overstepping; `و ر ي B005/B006` gives concealment and the far side/beyond; `ع د و B001/B004` gives boundary-crossing. I supply pursuit → passage beyond the defined exception → injustice. The ayah reads spatially: transgression begins when seeking relocates itself past the visible covenantal line.
2. **Haste as boundary failure.** `ع د و B002` adds running/hastening. Later false acceleration toward imagined goods (23:55–56) contrasts with racing in goods (23:61); this preserves a second reading in which speed is judged by the boundary and object it serves.

### Retrospective surprises

23:55–61 unexpectedly made speed morally nonuniform: hasty extension can deceive, while disciplined racing can precede in goods.

## 23:8 — وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ لِأَمَٰنَٰتِهِمْ وَعَهْدِهِمْ رَٰعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Trust as a tended living charge.** `ء م ن B001` supplies security/trust; `ع ه د B001/B002/B003` gives tending over time, entrusted charge, and covenant; `ر ع ي B001/B002` gives grazing and shepherding. The repeated preservation language at 23:5 and 23:9 provides structural framing. I infer entrusted things deteriorate without ongoing care; the ayah reads as active husbandry of obligations, not passive non-betrayal.
2. **Covenant as retained trace.** `ع ه د B004/B005` permits a remembered trace and familiar place returned to. With return dominating 23:60, 99–100, and 115, fidelity also reads as repeatedly returning an affair to its originating commitment.

### Retrospective surprises

The many return scenes made `ع ه د B004/B005` newly visible: a covenant persists as a trace that can guide one back before the irreversible barrier of 23:100.

## 23:9 — وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ عَلَىٰ صَلَوَٰتِهِمْ يُحَافِظُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Prayer maintained as a repeated line.** `ص ل و B003/B006` gives worship and following; form IV `ح ف ظ B003/B004` supplies steady observance and watchful caution. Its return after 23:2 creates an inclusio. I infer recurrent guarding stabilizes the whole intervening ethic; the ayah reads as maintenance of an ordering rhythm rather than protection of an isolated ritual object.
2. **A clear road kept open.** `ح ف ظ B007` preserves a clear unbroken road. The straight path and sideward veering at 23:73–74 retrospectively make prayers route maintenance: repeated observance keeps passage continuous.

### Retrospective surprises

23:73–74 activated the road branch of `ح ف ظ`; prayer's guarding now appears as keeping the straight route traversable.

## 23:10 — أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْوَٰرِثُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Inheritance as an unbought allotment.** `و ر ث B001/B002` gives transfer from former holders and allotment without bargain or toil. The preceding practices qualify the heirs, while 23:11 names the inherited domain. I infer qualification is not purchase: disciplined custody fits one to receive what cannot be manufactured. The ayah reads as succession into a gift.
2. **Inherited virtue.** `و ر ث B003` includes transmitted knowledge or virtue. Because 23:1–9 is a behavioral inventory, “heirs” can also name people in whom a moral pattern survives and continues.

### Retrospective surprises

23:44's vanished generations becoming reports sharpened the succession mechanism: one lineage becomes mere narrative, another inherits enduring habitation.

## 23:11 — ٱلَّذِينَ يَرِثُونَ ٱلْفِرْدَوْسَ هُمْ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Allotment becoming settled attachment.** `و ر ث B002` gives an unbought allotment; `خ ل د B001/B002` gives permanence and clinging stay. Later stable abodes (`ق ر ر` at 23:13, 50) and the barrier at 23:100 contrast reversible residence with final residence. I infer that rightful receipt enables unbroken attachment; the ayah reads as durable belonging, not merely endless duration.

### Retrospective surprises

23:100's temporal barrier and 23:103's opposed *khālidūn* made permanence relational: both outcomes are fixed attachments, but to radically different habitations.

## 23:12 — وَلَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِن سُلَٰلَةٍۢ مِّن طِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Measured humanity drawn from mixed matter.** `خ ل ق B001/B002` supplies proportioning and origination; `ء ن س B001/B003` gives visible human presence and familiar sociality; `س ل ل B001/B003` gives gentle extraction of essence/lineage; `ط ي ن B001` gives earth-water mixture. I infer extraction preserves selected continuity from mixture → proportioned human presence. The ayah reads as controlled derivation, neither ex nihilo abstraction nor undifferentiated materialism.
2. **Disposition formed in clay.** `ط ي ن B004` permits formed innate disposition, activated by `خ ل ق B004` (inner character). The later moral split shows the same formed creature developing contrary dispositions; the ayah can therefore foreground moral plasticity within a measured constitution.

### Retrospective surprises

23:115's denial of purposeless creation retroactively makes every extraction and proportion here teleological; 23:78–80 then unfolds the sensory and temporal capacities built into that constitution.

## 23:13 — ثُمَّ جَعَلْنَٰهُ نُطْفَةًۭ فِى قَرَارٍۢ مَّكِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **A fluid placed into secured settlement.** `ج ع ل B002` marks transformation into a state; `ن ط ف B002/B003` gives clear seminal fluid and dripping wetness; `ق ر ر B003/B006` gives fixed settlement and a low bottom; `م ك ن B001` anchors established capacity. Sequence supplies containment after extraction. I infer secured placement blocks dispersal and permits staged formation; the ayah reads as engineered lodging.
2. **Settled acknowledgment.** `ق ر ر B007` allows acknowledgment. With later demanded recognition (23:84–90), the protected biological settlement also becomes an embodied fact to which the creature ought to assent.

### Retrospective surprises

23:50 repeats `ق ر ر` beside shelter and flowing water, revealing a recurring habitat formula: stable settlement is paired with provision, not bare enclosure.

## 23:14 — ثُمَّ خَلَقْنَا ٱلنُّطْفَةَ عَلَقَةًۭ فَخَلَقْنَا ٱلْعَلَقَةَ مُضْغَةًۭ فَخَلَقْنَا ٱلْمُضْغَةَ عِظَٰمًۭا فَكَسَوْنَا ٱلْعِظَٰمَ لَحْمًۭا ثُمَّ أَنشَأْنَٰهُ خَلْقًا ءَاخَرَ ۚ فَتَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ أَحْسَنُ ٱلْخَٰلِقِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Creation as repeated proportioning across attached stages.** Repeated `خ ل ق B001/B002/B003` supplies measurement, origination, and completed form; `ع ل ق B001` supplies clinging attachment; `ك س و B001` clothes bone with flesh; `ن ش ء B001` initiates a new emergence. The explicit sequence is packet-supplied; I infer each retained stage enables the next. The ayah reads as serial transformation with continuity, not a single undifferentiated event.
2. **Blessing as productive increase.** `ب ر ك B001/B002` (abiding blessing/increase) is structurally delayed until “another creation,” while `ح س ن B001` marks fitting beauty/excellence. I infer the evaluation responds to coordinated emergence; *tabāraka* reads as praise of inexhaustibly productive ordering.

### Retrospective surprises

23:17–22 scales the same mechanism outward: layered heavens, measured water, plants, animals, and vessels repeat the pattern of proportion, containment, and enabling support.

## 23:15 — ثُمَّ إِنَّكُم بَعْدَ ذَٰلِكَ لَمَيِّتُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Death as the mandated next interval.** `ب ع د B001` supplies spatial/temporal afterness and `م و ت B001` cessation of life. The emphatic construction interrupts 23:12–14's ascent, then 23:16 resumes with raising. I infer formed life does not cancel sequence; it enables a bounded term whose closure precedes another stage. The ayah reads as transition within a longer architecture.

### Retrospective surprises

23:99 makes death arrive as an agent, while 23:112–114 compresses earthly duration; together they turn this “after” into a decisive but surprisingly brief boundary.

## 23:16 — ثُمَّ إِنَّكُمْ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ تُبْعَثُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Raising as standing and dispatch.** `ق و م B001/B002` contributes standing/upright establishment; `ب ع ث B001/B002` supplies stirring and sending forth. Coming after death, the passive form makes the directional arrow explicit in grammar: external agency moves the dead → standing assembly. The ayah reads as reactivation into accountable presence.

### Retrospective surprises

23:79's dispersal then gathering, 23:101's horn, and 23:102's scales progressively fill this initially compact “raising” with spatial, acoustic, and judicial mechanics.

## 23:17 — وَلَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا فَوْقَكُمْ سَبْعَ طَرَآئِقَ وَمَا كُنَّا عَنِ ٱلْخَلْقِ غَٰفِلِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Layered routes under continuous oversight.** `خ ل ق B001` supplies proportioning; `ف و ق B001` gives above-ness; `ط ر ق B001/B002` gives paths and layered/struck routes; `غ ف ل B001` is inattentive neglect, explicitly negated. The sevenfold structure stands over the creature. I infer ordered routes require ongoing attention, so cosmic architecture reads as maintained infrastructure rather than abandoned construction.
2. **Creation as the watched created community.** The second `خ ل ق` is a noun and can gather created beings; proximity to “not heedless” makes oversight extend from the overhead structure to those living beneath it.

### Retrospective surprises

23:86's “seven heavens” and throne later identifies the upper routes as a sovereignty structure, while 23:62's speaking record gives oversight an accountable form.

## 23:18 — وَأَنزَلْنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءًۢ بِقَدَرٍۢ فَأَسْكَنَّٰهُ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ ۖ وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰ ذَهَابٍۭ بِهِۦ لَقَٰدِرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Measured descent, storage, and removable provision.** `ن ز ل B001` gives descent; `س م و B004` the overhead cover; `ق د ر B001/B002` measure and capability; `س ك ن B001` settling; `ذ ه ب B001` departure. Grammar repeats `ق د ر` as quantity then power. I infer measured supply → terrestrial storage → possible withdrawal; water reads as a governed trust, not an autonomous resource.
2. **Water as peace-making settlement.** `س ك ن B002` can denote stillness/rest. In contrast with overwhelming water at 23:27, the same material becomes inhabitable because force is settled by measure.

### Retrospective surprises

23:27's flood reverses every control here, and 23:50's spring restores the settled-provision pattern; the surah makes water's benefit depend on measured placement.

## 23:19 — فَأَنشَأْنَا لَكُم بِهِۦ جَنَّٰتٍۢ مِّن نَّخِيلٍۢ وَأَعْنَٰبٍۢ لَّكُمْ فِيهَا فَوَٰكِهُ كَثِيرَةٌۭ وَمِنْهَا تَأْكُلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Hidden enclosure converting water into abundant food.** `ن ش ء B001` supplies emergence; `ج ن ن B001` gives covered garden; `ك ث ر B001` abundance; `ء ك ل B001` consumption. The pronoun “by it” causally links 23:18's stored water to gardens and eating. I infer enclosure and rooted growth mediate water → diverse edible yield; the ayah reads as a conversion system built for beneficiaries.

### Retrospective surprises

23:51 turns this food chain into an ethical chain: eating from good things is followed immediately by sound work.

## 23:20 — وَشَجَرَةًۭ تَخْرُجُ مِن طُورِ سَيْنَآءَ تَنۢبُتُ بِٱلدُّهْنِ وَصِبْغٍۢ لِّلْءَاكِلِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Growth that exits bearing both fuel and transformation.** `ش ج ر B001` anchors branching tree; `خ ر ج B001` emergence; `ن ب ت B001` growth; `د ه ن B001` oil; `ص ب غ B001/B002` dipping/coloring and condiment; `ء ك ل B001` eating. I infer rooted uptake transforms stored water and soil into oil that both nourishes and alters what receives it; the tree reads as productive mediation, not botanical ornament.

### Retrospective surprises

23:96's command to repel by what is better echoes this transformation pattern: an input is not merely resisted but converted into a different-quality output.

## 23:21 — وَإِنَّ لَكُمْ فِى ٱلْأَنْعَٰمِ لَعِبْرَةًۭ ۖ نُّسْقِيكُم مِّمَّا فِى بُطُونِهَا وَلَكُمْ فِيهَا مَنَٰفِعُ كَثِيرَةٌۭ وَمِنْهَا تَأْكُلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Crossing from hidden interior to public benefit.** `ع ب ر B001/B002` gives crossing and lesson; `ب ط ن B001` gives interior/belly; `س ق ي B001` gives drink; `ن ف ع B001` benefit; `ء ك ل B001` consumption. The repeated “from/in them” traces outputs from concealed interior. I infer transformation inside the animal crosses outward as drink, uses, and food; *ʿibrah* reads as learning by tracing a hidden-to-visible passage.
2. **Benefit as a bridge, not an endpoint.** The same `ع ب ر` crossing prepares 23:22's carrying. Material benefit becomes evidence that creatures are configured to convey humans across needs and spaces.

### Retrospective surprises

23:22 literalizes the crossing branch: the animals that provide inward-to-outward nourishment also carry bodies across terrain.

## 23:22 — وَعَلَيْهَا وَعَلَى ٱلْفُلْكِ تُحْمَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Two media sharing a carrying function.** `ف ل ك B001` anchors the vessel; `ح م ل B001/B002` supplies bearing and transporting. Repeated *ʿalā* aligns animals and ships, despite different materials. I infer functional equivalence overrides material difference: both are support systems that transfer a dependent load. The ayah reads as a compact taxonomy of conveyance.
2. **Carrying as preparation for rescue.** The ship reappears in 23:27–29. That later narrative activates `ح م ل` as preservation under danger, not ordinary mobility alone.

### Retrospective surprises

23:27–29 turns the generic vessel into a survival enclosure and makes “you are carried” imply dependence on prepared means during crisis.

## 23:23 — وَلَقَدْ أَرْسَلْنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوْمِهِۦ فَقَالَ يَٰقَوْمِ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنْ إِلَٰهٍ غَيْرُهُۥٓ ۖ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **A message sent to restore protective alignment.** `ر س ل B001` gives sending; `ع ب د B001` worship/service; `ء ل ه B001` the worshipped one; `غ ي ر B005` excludes otherness; `و ق ي B001` gives protective guarding. The address repeats *qawm* to bind messenger and people. I infer exclusive service establishes the protection for which the closing question asks; *taqwā* reads as entering the shelter of right allegiance.
2. **The people as a standing collective.** `ق و م B001` carries standing/established group. Their later collective drowning shows that standing together can stabilize either response or refusal.

### Retrospective surprises

23:32 repeats the message almost verbatim, and 23:52 universalizes it as one community; the local call is a recurring trans-generational structure.

## 23:24 — فَقَالَ ٱلْمَلَؤُا۟ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ مِن قَوْمِهِۦ مَا هَٰذَآ إِلَّا بَشَرٌۭ مِّثْلُكُمْ يُرِيدُ أَن يَتَفَضَّلَ عَلَيْكُمْ وَلَوْ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَأَنزَلَ مَلَٰٓئِكَةًۭ مَّا سَمِعْنَا بِهَٰذَا فِىٓ ءَابَآئِنَا ٱلْأَوَّلِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Elite fullness converting equality into status suspicion.** `م ل ء B001` gives the filled/leading assembly; `ك ف ر B001` covers/rejects; `ب ش ر B001` visible human flesh; `م ث ل B001` likeness; `ف ض ل B001/B002` surplus excellence and favor. Their sequence moves from “human like you” to alleged self-elevation. I infer status anxiety recodes shared humanity as a bid for surplus rank; the speech reads as political defense disguised as theological objection.
2. **Ancestral hearing as a closed archive.** `س م ع B001` anchors hearing, `ء ب و B001` ancestry, and `ء و ل B001` precedence. I infer absence from inherited hearing is treated as impossibility; 23:68 later exposes this archive rule as refusal to deliberate new speech.

### Retrospective surprises

23:46–47 repeats the status logic under Pharaoh, while 23:68 explicitly asks whether novelty relative to ancestors can justify rejection.

## 23:25 — إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا رَجُلٌۢ بِهِۦ جِنَّةٌۭ فَتَرَبَّصُوا۟ بِهِۦ حَتَّىٰ حِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Diagnostic enclosure used to justify strategic delay.** `ر ج ل B002` anchors man; `ج ن ن B001/B002` supplies concealment and mental covering; `ر ب ص B001/B002` watchful waiting and withholding for advantage; `ح ي ن B001/B005` due time and awaiting it. I infer labeling speech as concealed-minded blocks engagement and makes delay appear rational. The ayah reads as a tactic for neutralizing a challenger without answering him.

### Retrospective surprises

23:70 repeats the madness charge but immediately identifies aversion to truth; the later verse exposes diagnosis as displaced dislike.

## 23:26 — قَالَ رَبِّ ٱنصُرْنِى بِمَا كَذَّبُونِ

### Activated readings

1. **Denied truth converted into a plea for redress.** `ر ب ب B001/B002` gives authority and nurturing completion; `ن ص ر B001/B002` gives prevailing aid and redress after oppression; `ك ذ ب B002` gives active falsification. The causal *bi-mā* ties their denial to the request. I infer public falsification creates an unresolved grievance requiring superior adjudication; “help me” reads specifically as vindicatory redress.

### Retrospective surprises

The identical plea at 23:39 and the answering sequence at 23:40–41 reveal this as a repeatable narrative hinge from contested speech to decisive outcome.

## 23:27 — فَأَوْحَيْنَآ إِلَيْهِ أَنِ ٱصْنَعِ ٱلْفُلْكَ بِأَعْيُنِنَا وَوَحْيِنَا فَإِذَا جَآءَ أَمْرُنَا وَفَارَ ٱلتَّنُّورُ ۙ فَٱسْلُكْ فِيهَا مِن كُلٍّۢ زَوْجَيْنِ ٱثْنَيْنِ وَأَهْلَكَ إِلَّا مَن سَبَقَ عَلَيْهِ ٱلْقَوْلُ مِنْهُمْ ۖ وَلَا تُخَٰطِبْنِى فِى ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوٓا۟ ۖ إِنَّهُم مُّغْرَقُونَ

### Activated readings
