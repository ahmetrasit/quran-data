# S94 v12 Full-Context Ayah Walk

## 94:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. The basmalah functions as a raised designation over the whole passage. Lexical evidence: `س م و B005` name as raised designation, with `س م و B001/B008` rising and good repute; `ء ل ه B002` fixed divine name in invocation; `ر ح م B001` mercy and tenderness. Structural cue: the packet later says `رَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ` in 94:4, where `ر ف ع B002/B005` and `ذ ك ر B004/B007` make public elevation of mention explicit. Assigned role: the opening name is not only a formula but the first instance of elevation-by-naming. Abductive move supplied by me: the initial invocation establishes the kind of agency by which later “raising mention” occurs. Reading change: the basmalah reads as the superscription that already contains the later restoration of name and voice.

2. Mercy is activated as an enveloping kin/womb field for the bodily sequence of chest, burden, and back. Lexical evidence: `ر ح م B001` mercy and tenderness, `ر ح م B002` kinship bond, `ر ح م B003` womb; later `ص د ر B001` chest, `و ض ع B002` laying down a pregnancy in birth, `ع س ر B006` difficult childbirth, and `ي س ر B006` productive increase in livestock. Structural cue: the passage moves from inner chest opening (94:1), to load being set down (94:2), to a back that had creaked (94:3), then to ease (94:5-6). Assigned role: mercy supplies the protective, generative container for that bodily relief. Abductive move supplied by me: the later body/load language lets the repeated mercy names be heard as care that carries a constricted life through delivery, not merely as abstract kindness. Reading change: the basmalah becomes the affective and generative matrix of the surah.

3. The divine name is paired with final lordship as invocation and governance. Lexical evidence: `ء ل ه B001/B002` worship and fixed invocation; 94:8 `ر ب ب B001` lordship/ownership/authority and `ر ب ب B002` repair, nurture, and completion. Structural cue: the packet begins with `ٱللَّهِ` and ends with `رَبِّكَ`, enclosing the ayat between invoked deity and addressed Lord. Assigned role: the opening name authorizes the later imperatives and the final direction of desire. Abductive move supplied by me: the frame implies that relief, raising, labor, and desire are governed by one continuous divine agency. Reading change: the basmalah reads less as detached preface and more as the first half of a divine frame completed by `رَبِّكَ`.

### Retrospective surprises

94:4 was the strongest later surprise for 94:0: `رَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ` made the initial `بِسْمِ` feel less like a neutral formula and more like the first raised-name event. 94:8 then closed the frame by replacing the invoked `ٱللَّهِ` with addressed `رَبِّكَ`; this made the basmalah’s mercy retrospectively practical, because lordship in `ر ب ب B002` repairs and nurtures the opened, unloaded person. The womb/delivery reading also became stronger only after 94:5-6 supplied `ع س ر B006` and `ي س ر B006`; before that it was available but less structurally necessary.

## 94:1 — أَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ

### Activated readings

1. The chest opening is a bodily decompression caused by the following load-removal sequence. Lexical evidence: `ش ر ح B001` opening and clarifying, `ص د ر B001` chest as bodily front; later `و ض ع B001` setting down, `و ز ر B002` heavy burden, `ن ق ض B005` creaking under strain, `ظ ه ر B002` back opposite belly. Structural cue: 94:1 names the front/interior; 94:2-3 name the burden and the back strained by it. Assigned role: `شرح` releases the front while `وضع` removes what had crushed the back. Abductive move supplied by me: front expansion and back relief belong to one bodily mechanism of pressure removed from the torso. Reading change: the ayah becomes somatic relief, not only inner consolation.

2. The opening is also interpretive clarification of a hard affair. Lexical evidence: `ش ر ح B001` opening a meaning so it becomes clear; `ص د ر B002` foremost/beginning of an affair or speech, and `ص د ر B004` source from which forms issue; later `ذ ك ر B003/B004/B009` recall, mention on the tongue, and reminder. Structural cue: after the chest is opened, the “mention” is raised in 94:4 and then the repeated maxim of hardship/ease appears in 94:5-6. Assigned role: `صدر` is the internal source or head of speech/meaning, and `شرح` makes what issues from it intelligible. Abductive move supplied by me: the passage treats relief as a cognitive-linguistic opening that allows the burdened condition to be named and remembered differently. Reading change: the ayah reads as the opening of understanding and speech, not only expansion of emotion.

3. The opening has a guarded-cultivation reading. Lexical evidence: `ش ر ح B006` guarding and preserving crops or young palms; `ر ح م B001/B003` tenderness and womb; later `ر ب ب B002` nurture stage by stage. Structural cue: the basmalah’s mercy precedes the chest, while final `رَبِّكَ` names the caretaker toward whom desire turns. Assigned role: the opened chest is vulnerable new growth needing protection. Abductive move supplied by me: opening without guarding would expose the interior; the mercy/lordship frame supplies protection for the opened interior. Reading change: `نشرح لك صدرك` becomes not just making space but preserving what has been opened.

4. A secondary desire reading is activated but remains ambivalent. Lexical evidence: `ش ر ح B005` expansive inclination toward worldly things; later `ر غ ب B001` desire turning toward or away in 94:8. Structural cue: the first ayah opens inward space, while the last ayah directs رغبة specifically `إلى ربك`. Assigned role: `شرح` creates capacity for desire, and 94:8 governs its direction. Abductive move supplied by me: expanded inward capacity can become undirected appetite unless the final ayah orients it. Reading change: the chest-opening includes a latent risk and remedy: expansion is good when desire is turned toward the Lord.

### Retrospective surprises

94:7 changed the feel of 94:1 most sharply. The opened `صدر` did not remain a quiet interior; `فَرَغْتَ فَٱنصَبْ` made the opening look like emptying a vessel and then setting it upright for use. 94:8 also disciplined the otherwise risky `ش ر ح B005` branch: expansive inclination is not allowed to drift, because رغبة is directed `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ`. The later repeated hardship/ease lines made the opening read less like simple comfort and more like a capacity produced inside pressure.

## 94:2 — وَوَضَعْنَا عَنكَ وِزْرَكَ

### Activated readings

1. The primary mechanism is load removal from the body. Lexical evidence: `و ض ع B001` setting something down or in its place, `و ز ر B002` heavy burden; 94:3 `ن ق ض B005` creaking under strain and `ظ ه ر B002` anatomical back. Structural cue: 94:2 names the load’s removal, and 94:3 immediately identifies the load by its effect on the back. Assigned role: `وضع` is the downward transfer, `وزر` is the load, and `ظهر` is the support that had borne it. Abductive move supplied by me: removal is inferred as causal explanation for the chest opening and later ease. Reading change: the ayah reads as a mechanical unburdening whose evidence appears in the next ayah’s strained sound.

2. The burden is also a false refuge or transferred support. Lexical evidence: `و ز ر B001` refuge/shelter, `و ز ر B004` supporting minister/helper, and `ر ب ب B001/B002` final lordship and nurture. Structural cue: the burden is taken “from you,” while the passage ends by directing رغبة to `رَبِّكَ`. Assigned role: `وزر` can be what one relies on for protection or help; its removal clears the relation so support is no longer carried by the addressed person. Abductive move supplied by me: some shelters become loads when they are borne as self-support. Reading change: the ayah is not only forgiveness/relief of weight; it is relocation of dependence from carried support to divine lordship.

3. The laying-down is activated as birth/delivery. Lexical evidence: `و ض ع B002` laying down a pregnancy in birth; `ر ح م B003/B004` womb and womb pain after birth; `ع س ر B006` difficult childbirth; `ي س ر B001` ease/readiness and `ي س ر B006` productive increase. Structural cue: mercy/womb terms open the packet; the doubled hardship/ease couplet follows the burden episode. Assigned role: the `وزر` is a carried thing brought to term and set down. Abductive move supplied by me: the context’s womb, hardship, and ease branches let “placing down” be heard as delivery out of constriction. Reading change: the ayah becomes release-through-birth, not merely dropping an object.

4. A weaker legal-economic reading is preserved. Lexical evidence: `و ض ع B004` reducing capital through loss, `ص د ر B005` settling liability on money, `ع س ر B002/B003` insolvency and pressing an insolvent debtor, `ي س ر B003` prosperity and means, `ذ ك ر B008` rights document. Structural cue: the sequence from burden to repeated ease activates debt/means vocabulary across roots. Assigned role: the “burden” can behave like a liability that is abated. Abductive move supplied by me: the passage’s relief may include release from an accounting claim. Reading change: the ayah can be heard as cancellation or reduction of an imposed liability, though this reading is secondary because the immediate body/back cue is stronger.

### Retrospective surprises

The later surprise was that `وَضَعْنَا` did not end the action; 94:7’s `فَٱنصَبْ` showed a down/up alternation: the burden is set down so the person can be set upright. 94:8 also made the refuge/helper senses of `و ز ر B001/B004` more important than they first appeared, because the final desire moves toward `رَبِّكَ` as the true support. The financial-liability reading gained support from 94:5-6 only after `ع س ر` and `ي س ر` activated insolvency/means; without those later ayat it would have remained much weaker.

## 94:3 — ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنقَضَ ظَهْرَكَ

### Activated readings

1. The direct image is a back audibly strained by a load. Lexical evidence: `ن ق ض B005` creaking under strain from joints, loads, saddles, or a burdened back; `ظ ه ر B002` anatomical back; 94:2 `و ز ر B002` heavy burden. Structural cue: the relative phrase in 94:3 specifies the `وزر` of 94:2 by its effect. Assigned role: `أنقض` supplies audible evidence of pressure; `ظهر` supplies the support surface. Abductive move supplied by me: sound is treated as the symptom of hidden load. Reading change: the ayah makes prior suffering materially audible, not merely conceptually heavy.

2. The burden also threatens the collapse of a constructed order. Lexical evidence: `ن ق ض B001` breaking or undoing what was made firm, such as rope, structure, covenant, oath, argument; `ظ ه ر B006/B016` backing/support and supporting people; `ر ب ب B011` covenant or pact as binding bundle. Structural cue: the passage later raises mention (94:4) and directs desire to the Lord (94:8), after the old support has been strained. Assigned role: `نقض` is undoing; `ظهر` is the system of backing. Abductive move supplied by me: a burden can break not only a body but the social/covenantal support on which a person stands. Reading change: the ayah reads as near-unraveling of support, not only back pain.

3. Travel-weariness enters through animal and road branches. Lexical evidence: `ن ق ض B002` a camel worn down by travel; `ظ ه ر B005` mounts and spare riding animals; `و ض ع B003` urging a mount into rapid travel; `ن ص ب B010` day-long easy march; `ف ر غ B003` breadth in a road. Structural cue: later 94:7 gives a post-relief imperative that can be read as renewed movement/labor. Assigned role: the back is also a mount/backing that has been overtraveled. Abductive move supplied by me: the strained back is a vehicle image for mission-fatigue, preparing for a different kind of onward travel. Reading change: the ayah becomes exhaustion from carrying a journey, not only static weight.

4. A disclosure reading is activated by `ظهر`. Lexical evidence: `ظ ه ر B001` coming out into view, `ظ ه ر B008` finding out/access to hidden matter, and `ش ر ح B001` opening what was hidden. Structural cue: 94:1 opens the chest; 94:3 names the back/surface; 94:4 raises mention into public view. Assigned role: the pressure on the back brings an invisible burden into manifestation. Abductive move supplied by me: what creaks becomes known; the body exposes what was hidden. Reading change: the ayah reads as revelation of hidden strain as well as the strain itself.

### Retrospective surprises

94:7 retrospectively made the travel-worn animal branch of `ن ق ض B002` more visible: after the back has creaked, the command does not cancel movement but reorders it. 94:4 also surprised the reading of `ظهر`: the strained support is answered not merely by relief but by public elevation of `ذكر`, so hidden pressure becomes manifest honor. 94:8 strengthened the covenant/support reading by making `ر ب ب B011` available as a repaired binding relation after the old backing had nearly come undone.

## 94:4 — وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ

### Activated readings

1. Raised mention reverses the downward load. Lexical evidence: `ر ف ع B002` high rank/elevated mention, `ذ ك ر B007` honorable mention and renown; earlier `و ض ع B001` setting down and `و ز ر B002` burden. Structural cue: 94:2 says the burden was put down; 94:4 says the mention was raised. Assigned role: vertical reversal transforms the addressed person’s public condition from pressed-down to lifted-up. Abductive move supplied by me: the same divine agency that lowers the load raises the name. Reading change: the ayah is not isolated honor; it is the upward counterpart to unburdening.

2. Mention becomes public transmission, not only reputation. Lexical evidence: `ر ف ع B005` announcing a report, `ر ف ع B010` highness of voice, `ذ ك ر B004` mention on the tongue, and later `ن ص ب B009` raised-voice song/chant. Structural cue: the passage moves from opened chest/source of speech (94:1) to raised mention (94:4) and then to renewed activity (94:7). Assigned role: `ذكر` is verbal circulation; `رفع` gives it height and audibility. Abductive move supplied by me: inner expansion becomes outward voice. Reading change: the ayah reads as broadcast and performative utterance, not only status.

3. The raised mention can function as reminder and retained memory. Lexical evidence: `ذ ك ر B003` bringing to mind, `ذ ك ر B009` reminder/admonition, `ظ ه ر B019` memory without a book. Structural cue: 94:5-6 repeats the hardship/ease statement, making remembrance itself a structural act. Assigned role: the raised `ذكر` keeps the relief pattern present against forgetting. Abductive move supplied by me: repetition is a memory device generated by raised mention. Reading change: the ayah makes public remembrance a means of sustaining ease through hardship.

4. A legal/right-document reading is possible but narrower. Lexical evidence: `ذ ك ر B008` rights document or legal instrument; `ص د ر B005` liability settlement; `ع س ر B003` pressing an insolvent debtor; `ن ص ب B005/B006` assigned share and fixed threshold. Structural cue: 94:2’s burden-removal and 94:5-6’s hardship/ease activate an economy of liability, means, and assigned portions. Assigned role: the raised `ذكر` can be the acknowledged document of right after liability is lifted. Abductive move supplied by me: public mention can formalize restored standing. Reading change: the ayah may read as official recognition, not just praise.

5. The shackle-lifting branch creates a liberation image. Lexical evidence: `ر ف ع B009` cord by which a bound person lifts his shackle; earlier `و ز ر B002` burden and `ن ق ض B005` strained joints/back. Structural cue: the shift from load and creak to elevation suggests a constrained body regaining movement. Assigned role: `رفع` lifts what impedes, while `ذكر` is the person’s re-emergent identity. Abductive move supplied by me: raised mention is the social equivalent of lifting a restraint. Reading change: the ayah becomes emancipation into voice and movement.

### Retrospective surprises

94:7’s `ن ص ب B009` made the vocal side of raised `ذكر` more vivid than it was on first pass: raised mention can become raised-voice chant or proclamation. 94:5-6 also made 94:4 operate as a memory device; the raised mention is immediately followed by a repeated formula, so `ذ ك ر B003/B009` becomes enacted in the surah’s own structure. 94:8 finally changed the status reading: honor is not self-enclosed reputation but a lifted identity redirected toward the Lord.

## 94:5 — فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

### Activated readings

1. The ease is structurally with the prior bodily hardship. Lexical evidence: `ع س ر B001` difficulty/severity and `ي س ر B001` ease/readiness after hardship; earlier `و ز ر B002` heavy burden, `ن ق ض B005` creaking strain. Structural cue: the `فـ` links the statement to the preceding relief sequence. Assigned role: hardship is the field; ease is the companion capacity disclosed inside or alongside it. Abductive move supplied by me: the earlier intervention is offered as evidence for the general pattern. Reading change: the ayah reads as the logic extracted from the chest-load-back episode, not as a detached aphorism.

2. A financial/liability mechanism is activated. Lexical evidence: `ع س ر B002/B003` straitness of means and pressing an insolvent debtor; `ي س ر B003` prosperity/means; `و ض ع B004` reduction of capital or abatement; `ذ ك ر B008` rights document. Structural cue: 94:2 removes a `وزر`, and 94:4 raises a `ذكر`; together they can behave as liability lifted and status recognized. Assigned role: `عسر` is insolvency/pressure, `يسر` is restored means or lenient dealing. Abductive move supplied by me: the passage converts debt-pressure into solvency-space. Reading change: the ayah can be read as release from constricting claims into means.

3. The left-side branches produce a surprising non-triumphal ease. Lexical evidence: `ع س ر B005` the left side/left-handedness and `ي س ر B004` the left side/left hand. Structural cue: the apparent opposite roots share a lateral semantic zone; the packet repeats the pair in 94:5-6. Assigned role: the shared “left” field makes ease not simply the opposite side of hardship but a skillful handling of the same difficult side. Abductive move supplied by me: one can read `مع` as coexistence in one bodily orientation rather than replacement. Reading change: the ayah becomes about ambidexterity within constraint, not clean escape from constraint.

4. The childbirth and productive-growth reading is activated. Lexical evidence: `ع س ر B006` difficult childbirth; `ي س ر B006` milk and offspring increase; earlier `ر ح م B003/B004` womb and womb pain, `و ض ع B002` laying down pregnancy. Structural cue: the passage’s early mercy/womb and laying-down branches precede the doubled hardship/ease formula. Assigned role: `عسر` is labor difficulty, `يسر` is successful delivery and growth. Abductive move supplied by me: the burden set down in 94:2 can be reimagined as birth, so ease is not the absence of travail but its outcome and accompaniment. Reading change: the ayah reads as generative hardship.

5. A readiness-before-action reading prepares 94:7. Lexical evidence: `ي س ر B001` becoming ready/possible, `ع س ر B008` riding or taking before readiness; later `ف ر غ B001/B006` completion and deliberate turning, `ن ص ب B004` exertion. Structural cue: 94:5-6 stand between relief and the imperative to work. Assigned role: ease is readiness created inside hardship, guarding against premature coercive action. Abductive move supplied by me: the passage pauses to establish readiness before renewed labor. Reading change: ease becomes preparation for the next task, not mere rest.

### Retrospective surprises

94:7 was the key later correction: ease is not an invitation to stop but readiness for `نصب`, including renewed toil. That made `ي س ر B001` read more as preparation than comfort. 94:8 also redirected the prosperity/means reading; `ي س ر B003` is not merely restored capacity in general, because desire is channeled toward `رَبِّكَ`. The left-side overlap between `ع س ر B005` and `ي س ر B004` remained the most surprising lexical activation, since it preserves coexistence rather than clean opposition.

## 94:6 — إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. The repeated line shifts the pattern from consequence to durable rule. Lexical evidence: the same `ع س ر B001` difficulty and `ي س ر B001` ease/readiness recur. Structural cue: 94:5 begins with `فَإِنَّ`, while 94:6 repeats without the initial `فـ`. Assigned role: the first occurrence follows from the preceding acts; the second reasserts the relation as stable in itself. Abductive move supplied by me: repetition changes rhetorical force from inference to anchoring. Reading change: the ayah reads as a second fastening of the ease-hardship relation.

2. The repetition itself behaves like raised memory. Lexical evidence: `ذ ك ر B003/B009` recalling and reminder; `ر ف ع B005` announcing a report. Structural cue: 94:4 raises `ذكر`, and 94:5-6 immediately repeat the saying. Assigned role: the doubled sentence is the content being kept present. Abductive move supplied by me: the raised mention expresses itself as repeated remembrance. Reading change: 94:6 reads not only as semantic repetition but as enacted remembrance.

3. The paired lines enact coexistence rather than sequence. Lexical evidence: `ع س ر B004` entanglement/making difficult, `ي س ر B005` pliant compliant movement, and the shared left-side branches `ع س ر B005` / `ي س ر B004`. Structural cue: the exact pair `العسر / يسرا` is held together twice by `مع`. Assigned role: hardship is an entangling field; ease is the pliant movement possible within it. Abductive move supplied by me: repeated co-presence makes coexistence more salient than before/after progression. Reading change: the ayah becomes training in movement under constraint.

4. A measured-share reading is activated from later `نصب`. Lexical evidence: `ي س ر B007` lots and apportioned camel shares; later `ن ص ب B005` assigned share/portion and `ن ص ب B006` fixed threshold; `ر ب ب B010` container gathering arrows/lots. Structural cue: the later imperative `فانصب` introduces allotted portion/base vocabulary, and final `ربك` includes a branch for gathered lots. Assigned role: ease may be a distributed share within hardship, not total abundance. Abductive move supplied by me: the repeated indefinite `يسرا` can be heard as apportioned openings assigned within one named hardship. Reading change: 94:6 reads as measured provision, not unlimited relief.

### Retrospective surprises

The later ayah that most changed 94:6 was 94:7: `فَإِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَٱنصَبْ` made the second ease statement feel like a hinge into action. The repetition is not only reassurance; it prepares a person to stand up under a new assigned portion or strain. 94:8 then made the measured-share reading more coherent, because `ر ب ب B010/B011` gathers lots/covenant while `ر غ ب B004` seeks bounty; ease becomes a provision within directed dependence.

## 94:7 — فَإِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَٱنصَبْ

### Activated readings

1. Completion leads to renewed upright exertion. Lexical evidence: `ف ر غ B001` emptiness/freedom after occupation or completion of work; `ن ص ب B001` setting something upright/prominent and `ن ص ب B004` weariness, strain, toil. Structural cue: after relief and repeated ease (94:5-6), the imperative comes with `فـ` and `إذا`. Assigned role: `فرغت` marks emptied/completed capacity; `فانصب` turns that capacity into upright work. Abductive move supplied by me: ease is not an endpoint but the condition that makes new exertion possible. Reading change: the ayah reads as a rhythm of completion and re-engagement rather than rest after hardship.

2. The vessel-pouring branch makes the chest-opening concrete. Lexical evidence: `ف ر غ B002` pouring out and emptying a vessel; earlier `ش ر ح B001` opening and `ص د ر B001` chest; `و ض ع B001/B002` setting down and delivery. Structural cue: the passage opened the chest, removed a load, then says “when you have emptied/finished.” Assigned role: the person is like a vessel emptied of constricting contents, ready to be set upright. Abductive move supplied by me: inner relief is modeled as evacuation before reorientation. Reading change: the ayah becomes a post-emptying instruction, not merely scheduling.

3. A deliberate-turning reading bridges to final desire. Lexical evidence: `ف ر غ B006` turning deliberately to a matter/devoting oneself; `ر غ ب B001` desire turning toward or away; `ر ب ب B001/B002` lordship and nurture. Structural cue: 94:7 immediately precedes `وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرْغَب`. Assigned role: `فراغ` is not vacancy but undivided availability for the next object. Abductive move supplied by me: the command to set oneself up is incomplete until the next ayah supplies the direction. Reading change: the ayah reads as focused readiness for devotion.

4. The upright-marker/cistern reading gives the imperative spatial form. Lexical evidence: `ن ص ب B003` upright marker/boundary or cistern stones; `س م و B004` overhead sky/cover including rain, `ر ب ب B008/B013` low cloud and abundant gathered water, `ف ر غ B002` pouring. Structural cue: water/cloud/pouring branches cluster around the final half of the passage once `فرغ` appears. Assigned role: `انصب` sets a marker or rim after pouring, making a bounded place for reception. Abductive move supplied by me: the opened/emptied self becomes a prepared site rather than a spent vessel. Reading change: the imperative can be spatially read as establishing a receiving place.

5. Raised-voice chant is activated by the prior raised mention. Lexical evidence: `ن ص ب B009` raised-voice song/chant; `ر ف ع B010` highness of voice; `ذ ك ر B004` mention on the tongue. Structural cue: `رفعنا لك ذكرك` precedes `فانصب`. Assigned role: the renewed action may include voiced praise/proclamation. Abductive move supplied by me: when mention has been raised, the next upright exertion can be vocal as well as bodily. Reading change: the ayah includes performative utterance, not only labor.

### Retrospective surprises

94:8 completed 94:7. On first pass `فانصب` could point toward exertion, uprightness, marker, chant, or fatigue; `وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرْغَب` supplied the missing directional arrow. The action is not mere busyness after free time but a setting-up of the whole emptied self toward the Lord. The earlier `رفعنا لك ذكرك` also kept surprising this ayah: `نصب` as raised-voice song became more plausible because mention had already been raised.

## 94:8 — وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرْغَب

### Activated readings

1. Desire is finally given direction. Lexical evidence: `ر غ ب B001` desire turning toward or away; `ر ب ب B001` lordship/ownership/authority and `ر ب ب B002` repair, nurture, completion. Structural cue: the prepositional phrase `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` precedes the imperative, making the destination explicit. Assigned role: `رب` is the proper object of the capacity opened in 94:1 and emptied in 94:7. Abductive move supplied by me: the passage assumes desire must be governed, not merely intensified. Reading change: the ayah reads as orientation of the newly freed self toward its completing caretaker.

2. The final desire reverses worldly expansion. Lexical evidence: earlier `ش ر ح B005` expansive inclination toward worldly things; `ر غ ب B001` includes desire toward and aversion/turning away. Structural cue: 94:1 opens the chest; 94:8 directs رغبة to the Lord. Assigned role: the same inner expansion that could spread toward possession is narrowed and purified by direction. Abductive move supplied by me: `إلى ربك` both attracts desire toward the Lord and implies turning away from rival objects. Reading change: the ayah becomes disciplined desire, not generic longing.

3. Lordship is read as nurturing completion of the whole bodily process. Lexical evidence: `ر ب ب B002` repair, nurture, completion stage by stage; `ر ح م B001/B003` mercy and womb; `و ض ع B002` birth; `ع س ر B006` difficult labor; `ي س ر B006` productive increase. Structural cue: the surah begins with mercy, moves through chest/burden/back, repeats hardship/ease, and ends at `ربك`. Assigned role: the Lord is the one who tends the process from constriction to delivery to growth. Abductive move supplied by me: the final `رب` gathers the earlier maternal/generative branches into a theology of nurture. Reading change: the ayah reads as return to the caretaker of growth, not only submission to authority.

4. Spacious desire answers spacious ease.
