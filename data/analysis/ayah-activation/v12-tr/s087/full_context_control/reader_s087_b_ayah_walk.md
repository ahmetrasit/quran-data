## 87:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Invocation as raised designation entering a care-cycle.** Lexical evidence: س م و B005 "name as raised designation"; ء ل ه B002 fixed divine name in invocation; ر ح م B001 mercy/tenderness. Structural cue: this same "name + Lord" complex returns imperatively in 87:1 and ritually in 87:15. Assigned role: the basmalah names the authority under whose care the whole sequence unfolds. Abductive move: the packet supplies repeated naming and mercy; I supply the directional claim that naming opens the later pedagogical and ritual sequence. Reading change: the basmalah is not only a formulaic opening but the raised sign under which creation, teaching, reminder, purification, and inherited scriptures are read as one merciful administration.

2. **Mercy as kinship/womb background for creation and preservation.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B002 kinship bond and B003 womb; later خلق B002 originating, ربب B002 nurture, قرء B003/B004 womb-gathering, حيي B002 life by rain/growth and B006 sparing alive. Structural cue: mercy appears before creation, pasture, teaching, memory, and survival language. Assigned role: رحم gives the coming world a generative container rather than a bare command. Abductive move: the packet gives womb/kin branches and later biological/growth images; I infer that mercy supplies the protected medium in which formed life and retained recitation can emerge. Reading change: "the Merciful, the Compassionate" becomes an originating and preserving bond that anticipates both bodily creation and textual preservation.

3. **Divine name as public utterance rather than private label.** Lexical evidence: س م و B008 good repute spread; جهر B001 open voicing and B002 visible manifestation in 87:7; ذكر B004 mention on the tongue and B007 honorable renown in 87:9, 87:10, 87:15. Structural cue: the packet moves from name to command, then from hidden/open knowledge to public reminder. Assigned role: the name is the utterable marker around which remembrance circulates. Abductive move: the packet supplies "name", "open", and "mention"; I infer circulation from the repeated movement into recitation and reminder. Reading change: the basmalah sounds like the first publicizable marker in a later economy of voiced remembrance.

### Retrospective surprises

Later 87:6-9 surprised the opening: قرء/نسي and جهر/خفى make the basmalah's "name" less static and more like the first unit in a memory-and-disclosure system. Later 87:18-19 also made the formula feel archival: the named mercy at the opening is linked retrospectively to written صحف, not merely immediate speech.
