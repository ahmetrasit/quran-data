# Surah 51 Full-Context Control Reader: Ayah Walk

## 51:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A named beginning under generative care.** `س م و/B005` makes *ism* a raised designation, while the doubled `ر ح م/B001` supplies mercy and beneficence. The later sequence of provision, creation, warning, and worship gives the name an initiating role over the whole mechanism. The packet supplies naming plus mercy; I infer the arrow **invoked designation -> merciful governance of what follows**. The formula therefore reads not only as an opening but as placing the entire surah's movements under a named economy of care.
2. **Mercy with a womb-like horizon (exploratory).** `ر ح م/B003` provides the womb as a containing place of growth, and 51:29-30 later presents impossible-seeming birth while 51:47-49 presents building, spreading, and pairing. The fixed adjectives remain mercy-forms; the womb image supplies their spatial role. I infer **encompassing mercy -> protected emergence of life and order**. This adds generative containment without replacing the direct mercy reading.

### Retrospective surprises

51:29-30 unexpectedly activated the womb branch of `ر ح م/B003`; 51:47-49 then widened that image from childbirth to the protected emergence of cosmic order.

## 51:1 — وَٱلذَّٰرِيَٰتِ ذَرْوًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Scattering as the first stage of sorting.** Repeated `ذ ر و/B004` supplies wind-scattering and winnowing. The immediate progression carrying -> flowing -> apportioning (51:2-4), followed much later by pairs and allotted shares (51:49, 59), makes dispersal function as separation before ordered distribution. The packet supplies the sequence; I infer **scattering -> discriminable parts -> apportionment**. The oath thus reads as an opening operation in an ordered material process, not isolated atmospheric motion.
2. **Swift passage.** `ذ ر و/B008` supplies swift passing, sharpened by `ج ر ي/B001` in 51:3 and the repeated arrivals and removals in 51:24-46. I infer **rapid passage -> accelerated transfer toward an appointed outcome**. The verse can retain a kinetic reading beside winnowing.

### Retrospective surprises

51:59's matching bucket-shares made the opening scattering look retrospectively like winnowing before exact allotment, not dispersal without destination.

## 51:2 — فَٱلْحَٰمِلَٰتِ وِقْرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Transport under consequential weight.** `ح م ل/B001` gives outward bearing and `و ق ر/B002` a heavy borne load. Positioned after scattering and before easy flow and apportionment, bearing is the middle transport stage. Later `ذ ن ب/B007` in 51:59 introduces a full bucket and `ر ز ق/B001` an allotment, activating cargo as a measured share delivered toward consequence. The inferred arrow is **gathered load -> transport -> assigned destination**. The verse reads as purposeful carriage rather than mere heaviness.
2. **Moral burden carried toward reckoning.** `ح م ل/B003` accepts responsibility or sin, while 51:6 uses `د ي ن/B002` for recompense and 51:59 uses `ذ ن ب/B001` for culpable consequence. I infer **borne responsibility -> eventual requital**. This ethical reading coexists with the concrete load because the fixed participle and *wiqr* remain its anchors.

### Retrospective surprises

51:58's firm load-bearing strength and 51:59's full bucket unexpectedly joined the heavy cargo to the surah's final distribution of provision and consequence.

## 51:3 — فَٱلْجَٰرِيَٰتِ يُسْرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A flow made tractable.** `ج ر ي/B001` supplies running or flowing and `ي س ر/B001` ease/readiness; `ي س ر/B005` adds light, pliant movement. Between heavy carriage (51:2) and division (51:4), ease serves as low-friction transfer. The packet supplies motion and sequence; I infer **reduced resistance -> reliable delivery for apportionment**. The line reads as controlled conveyance, not simply pleasant speed.
2. **Provision that continues.** `ج ر ي/B006` supplies ongoing provision or grant, later activated by *rizq* in 51:22, 57-58. I infer **continuous course -> sustained allotment**, with *yusr* marking its ready availability. This keeps a provisioning reading beside the physical-flow reading.

### Retrospective surprises

51:22 and 51:58 activated `ج ر ي/B006`: the easy course can carry a continuing grant, not only move a vessel or wind.

## 51:4 — فَٱلْمُقَسِّمَٰتِ أَمْرًا

### Activated readings

1. **Apportioning an operative affair.** `ق س م/B003` supplies division into shares; `ء م ر/B001` gives matter/affair and `ء م ر/B002` directive. The prior material sequence culminates here, while 51:22 links provision and promise and 51:34 locates marked stones “with your Lord.” The packet supplies division and command; I infer **directive -> differentiated assignments -> execution**. *Amr* can therefore be both the affair being distributed and the directive that makes distribution operative.
2. **The opening oaths enact their own allotment.** `ق س م/B004` supplies oath-taking, while the surface form *muqassimāt* is anchored primarily in B003. Because 51:1-4 are oath constructions and 51:4 itself names division, I infer **formal oath sequence -> verbal apportionment of roles**. The verse becomes reflexive: the oath-series itself distributes functions before asserting 51:5-6.

### Retrospective surprises

The marked recipients in 51:34 and matched shares in 51:59 made the distributed *amr* newly visible as recipient-specific administration.

## 51:5 — إِنَّمَا تُوعَدُونَ لَصَادِقٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Promise as already moving into fulfillment.** `و ع د/B001` supplies a spoken promise opening expectation and `ص د ق/B004` confirmation/fulfillment. The staged operations of 51:1-4 precede the claim, while 51:60 repeats the promised day. The packet supplies expectation and fulfillment; I infer **ordered execution already underway -> promise confirmed in event**. *Ṣādiq* thus reads dynamically as realization, not only truthfulness.
2. **Promise and threat remain simultaneous.** `و ع د/B002` accepts a threat toward harm, activated by the fire scene (51:13-14) and final woe (51:60), while 51:15-19 activates promised benefit. I infer **one appointment -> divergent outcomes by recipient**. The verse should preserve beneficent promise and punitive threat rather than collapse them.

### Retrospective surprises

The split between 51:15-19 and 51:60 prevented *tūʿadūn* from resolving into only good promise or only threat; both remain active under one fulfillment.

## 51:6 — وَإِنَّ ٱلدِّينَ لَوَٰقِعٌۭ

### Activated readings

1. **Recompense descending into fixed reality.** `د ي ن/B002` supplies reckoning/recompense; `و ق ع/B001` gives occurrence that becomes fixed or obligatory, and B002 a hard overwhelming event. The later sequence repeatedly has messengers, forces, and punishment arrive upon peoples (51:32-46). The packet supplies those outcomes; I infer **account rendered -> consequence lands**. The line reads as recompense becoming materially unavoidable.
2. **A debt coming due.** `د ي ن/B003` supplies debt/credit, while 51:19 names an owned due (`ح ق ق/B003`) in wealth and 51:59 names an allotted share (`ذ ن ب/B006`). I infer **unsettled obligation -> due share -> settlement**. This legal-economic mechanism remains distinct from general judgment.

### Retrospective surprises

51:44 showed consequence literally seizing a people, while 51:59 rendered it an allotted share; together they activated both descent and debt-settlement.

## 51:7 — وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلْحُبُكِ

### Activated readings

1. **A visibly patterned construction.** `س م و/B004` gives the overhead sky/cover; `ح ب ك/B001` supplies firm fine workmanship and B002 patterned tracks or ripples. The later “built it with strength” and “expanding” (51:47) supplies the construction cue. I infer **firm building -> visible patterned order**. The sky reads as an engineered fabric whose tracks evidence its making.
2. **A fastening network.** `ح ب ك/B003` supplies tight binding and fastening. Near 51:8's divergent sayings and the surah's later paired creation (51:49), I infer **bound relations hold differentiated parts together**. This makes *hubuk* relational structure, not only ornament.

### Retrospective surprises

51:47's explicit building with reinforcing strength corrected a merely decorative reading of *hubuk* toward patterned, load-bearing construction.

## 51:8 — إِنَّكُمْ لَفِى قَوْلٍۢ مُّخْتَلِفٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Speech split into incompatible courses.** `ق و ل/B001` supplies uttered speech and `خ ل ف/B004` divergence, opposition, or taking another way. The tightly woven sky of 51:7 contrasts with speech whose parts do not hold together, and 51:52 repeats the fixed labels “magician or madman.” I infer **recycled labeling -> divergent verbal courses -> failed recognition**. The verse reads as diagnosis of a speech-system, not merely many opinions.
2. **A held doctrine in alternating succession.** `ق و ل/B013` supplies doctrine/opinion, while `خ ل ف/B006` supplies alternation. The repeated historical formula in 51:52-53 activates a transgenerational mechanism: **successive groups -> same doctrinal alternatives**. Difference within each group's speech coexists with repetition across groups.

### Retrospective surprises

51:52-53 surprisingly made divergent speech recurrent across generations: local disagreement and historical repetition coexist rather than cancel each other.

## 51:9 — يُؤْفَكُ عَنْهُ مَنْ أُفِكَ

### Activated readings

1. **Diversion recursively reproduces diversion.** Both verbs are anchored in `ء ف ك/B001`, turning from a proper direction. Their passive repetition, following divergent speech in 51:8, creates a feedback structure. The packet supplies repetition; I infer **prior turning -> further turning from the referent**. The line reads as self-propagating displacement rather than a single mistaken conclusion.
2. **Falsehood as the turning instrument.** `ء ف ك/B002` supplies a lie diverted from truth. Later formulaic accusations in 51:39 and 52 provide the speech mechanism. I infer **false attribution -> attention redirected away**. This retains a distinct linguistic cause alongside the spatial turning image.

### Retrospective surprises

The repeated “magician or madman” labels in 51:39 and 52 supplied the missing instrument of diversion: false categorization turns attention away.

## 51:10 — قُتِلَ ٱلْخَرَّٰصُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Conjecturers annulled by the event they estimate.** `خ ر ص/B001` supplies conjectural estimation and B002 assertions without knowledge; `ق ت ل/B001` supplies killing, while B002 supplies subduing until force is softened. Against 51:5-6's fixed occurrence and 51:12's timing-question, I infer **unsupported estimate meets fixed event -> estimating authority collapses**. *Qutila* can retain lethal force while also reading as total rhetorical subdual.
2. **Measurement detached from allotment.** 51:4 uses `ق س م/B003` for actual apportionment, whereas *kharrāṣūn* use conjectural measure. I infer **guessing without access to the directive -> counterfeit division of what is due**. Their fault is not calculation itself but calculation severed from the surah's operative order.

### Retrospective surprises

51:59's exact matched allotment sharpened the contrast between conjectural measuring (`خ ر ص/B001`) and an actually administered share.

## 51:11 — ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ فِى غَمْرَةٍۢ سَاهُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Cognitive submersion produces heedlessness.** `غ م ر/B003` gives a covering haze of ignorance or heedlessness and `س ه و/B001` the heart's turning away. The doubled semantic relation makes a causal enclosure: the packet supplies covering and inattention; I infer **obscuring medium -> failed attention**. They are not merely distracted but located inside what keeps truth from becoming visible.
2. **A crowd can function as the covering medium.** `غ م ر/B005` supplies a dense multitude in which people cover one another. Recurrent group labels (*qawm*) and inherited accusations in 51:39, 43-46, 52-53 activate a social reading. I infer **crowd reinforcement -> individual heedlessness**. This coexists with the inward haze.

### Retrospective surprises

51:52-53 made `غ م ر/B005` newly useful: recurrent collective speech can be the crowd-cover that sustains individual heedlessness.

## 51:12 — يَسْـَٔلُونَ أَيَّانَ يَوْمُ ٱلدِّينِ

### Activated readings

1. **A request for timing that treats recompense as deferrable.** `س ء ل/B001` supplies asking, `ء ي ي/B006` supplies *ayyān* as a time-question, `ي و م/B003` an event-day, and `د ي ن/B002` recompense. Juxtaposed with “hastening” in 51:14 and 59, the question functions as temporal pressure. I infer **demanded date -> attempt to manage or discount the event**. The line reads as more than neutral inquiry.
2. **The event-day is an accounting date.** `د ي ن/B003` supplies debt and `ي و م/B003` a severe occasion. Together with the “due” in wealth (51:19) and allotted portion (51:59), I infer **accrued account -> appointed settlement day**. This legal-temporal reading remains alongside eschatological recompense.

### Retrospective surprises

51:59's command not to hasten retrospectively exposed the timing-question as pressure on the appointment, not a neutral request for information.

## 51:13 — يَوْمَ هُمْ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ يُفْتَنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Fire as assay and exposure.** `ن و ر/B002` supplies burning fire; `ف ت ن/B001` supplies assaying by trial, explicitly including metals tested by fire. The conjecturers of 51:10 are thus moved from unverifiable claims into an assay that proves quality. The packet supplies fire and testing; I infer **exposure to fire -> hidden quality made manifest**. The verse reads as evidentiary disclosure as well as punishment.
2. **Actual burning remains distinct.** `ف ت ن/B002` directly supplies burning or fiery torment. The preposition *ʿalā* and next verse's “taste” support bodily exposure. I infer **placement upon fire -> experienced burning**. The assay reading does not dilute this material one.

### Retrospective surprises

51:44's watchers being seized while looking clarified the assay reading: exposure can prove a condition even when direct sight had failed to teach it.

## 51:14 — ذُوقُوا۟ فِتْنَتَكُمْ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِى كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَسْتَعْجِلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **The demanded proof becomes tasted experience.** `ذ و ق/B002` supplies undergoing/testing, `ف ت ن/B001` assay and B004 ordeal, and `ع ج ل/B001` seeking before its time. The deictic *hādhā* reverses the earlier temporal question into present encounter. I infer **premature demand for verification -> verification arriving as ordeal**. Their epistemic challenge is answered through embodied experience.
2. **Haste is reversed into an inescapable present.** `ع ج ل/B001` contrasts immediate with deferred; `ك و ن/B001` marks their continuing prior state. The later repeated prohibition on hastening (51:59) closes the pattern. I infer **habitual acceleration -> appointment made immediate against the demander**. This is a temporal reversal, not merely rebuke.

### Retrospective surprises

51:59 repeats the haste root but restores deferment, revealing 51:14 as the completed reversal in which premature demand has become immediate experience.

## 51:15 — إِنَّ ٱلْمُتَّقِينَ فِى جَنَّٰتٍۢ وَعُيُونٍ

### Activated readings

1. **Protective caution enters protective enclosure.** `و ق ي/B002` supplies self-guarding caution; `ج ن ن/B003` gives tree-covered gardens and B008 protective cover. The preposition *fī* makes the spatial correspondence visible. I infer **practiced guarding -> placement within a guarding environment**. The gardens read as the material completion of an earlier ethical posture.
2. **Gardens fed by visible sources.** `ع ي ن/B006` supplies water-springs, and later 51:22 places provision in the sky while 51:48 describes prepared ground. I infer **allotted water -> covered growth**. The line forms an ecological mechanism of source, provision, and shelter, while `ع ي ن/B003` also leaves room for watchful care as a secondary overtone.

### Retrospective surprises

51:22's sky-provision and 51:48's prepared earth supplied the missing ecological chain connecting springs, covered growth, and protected habitation.

## 51:16 — ءَاخِذِينَ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمْ رَبُّهُمْ ۚ إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا۟ قَبْلَ ذَٰلِكَ مُحْسِنِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Reception answers prior beneficence.** `ء خ ذ/B001` gives taking/receiving, `ء ت ي/B002` granting, `ر ب ب/B002` nurture and completion, and `ح س ن/B002` beneficent action. The grammar moves from their present reception to their prior conduct. I infer **prior benefit given by them -> nurtured gift now received**, while the actual direction of recompense remains supplied by sequence, not the roots themselves. The verse reads as reciprocal economy rather than disconnected reward.
2. **They take on a completed mode of life.** `ء خ ذ/B009` supplies taking on a path or character and `ر ب ب/B002` stagewise completion. Because 51:17-19 specifies their prior habits, I infer **repeated good practice -> formed character -> capacity to receive**. This character-formation reading coexists with literal reception.

### Retrospective surprises

51:57-58 clarified the direction of giving: their reception answers prior good action, but the sustaining gift originates in the self-sufficient Provider.

## 51:17 — كَانُوا۟ قَلِيلًۭا مِّنَ ٱلَّيْلِ مَا يَهْجَعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Night sleep is deliberately kept to a small measure.** `ق ل ل/B001` supplies fewness, `ل ي ل/B001` night, and `ه ج ع/B001` a light or early-night sleep spell. The following predawn seeking (51:18) makes reduced sleep a temporal preparation. I infer **limited sleep -> wakeful access to predawn practice**. The line reads as calibrated rhythm rather than generalized sleeplessness.
2. **Heedlessness is what becomes little.** `ه ج ع/B002` supplies sleepy foolish heedlessness, activated retrospectively by `س ه و/B001` in 51:11. I infer **ethical vigilance -> little participation in heedless yielding**. This exploratory reading remains anchored in *yahjaʿūn* and does not replace literal sleep.

### Retrospective surprises

51:55's beneficial reminder reinforced the exploratory heedlessness branch: their reduced *hujūʿ* is part of sustained receptivity, not sleep deprivation alone.

## 51:18 — وَبِٱلْأَسْحَارِ هُمْ يَسْتَغْفِرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Predawn becomes a recurring shelter-seeking interval.** `س ح ر/B004` supplies the last part of night before morning; `غ ف ر/B002` gives forgiveness as shielding from consequence, with B001 the underlying protective cover. Placed after curtailed sleep and before material right-giving, seeking forgiveness is one phase in a disciplined cycle. I infer **wakeful predawn exposure -> seeking protective pardon**. The act reads as entering cover precisely as night-cover recedes.
2. **Inner repair precedes social payment.** The sequence 51:18-19 joins forgiveness-seeking to giving a due share. The packet does not state causation; I infer **acknowledged liability -> repaired relation -> recognition of others' claims**. `غ ف ر/B002` performs the repair role within the fixed ayah.

### Retrospective surprises

51:59-60 made forgiveness's protective function sharper: predawn seeking anticipates liability before the allotted consequence becomes its owner's day.

## 51:19 — وَفِىٓ أَمْوَٰلِهِمْ حَقٌّۭ لِّلسَّآئِلِ وَٱلْمَحْرُومِ

### Activated readings

1. **Wealth contains another's owned claim.** `م و ل/B001` supplies possessed wealth, `ح ق ق/B003` a particular owned right, `س ء ل/B001` the requester, and `ح ر م/B008` deprivation. The preposition *fī* locates the right inside the property rather than outside as optional generosity. I infer **possession includes an embedded claim -> transfer answers deprivation**. The line reads juridically, not merely philanthropically.
2. **The protected due must not be withheld.** `ح ق ق/B007` supplies a guarded due and `ح ر م/B006` an inviolable claim, while `ح ر م/B008` names deprivation. I infer **inviolable entitlement blocked -> deprivation; entitlement honored -> social repair**. This preserves both the deprived condition and the claimant's protected standing.

### Retrospective surprises

51:59's `ظ ل م/B008` branch of withholding rights unexpectedly closes the legal mechanism: one may honor the embedded claim or receive a matched share for blocking it.

## 51:20 — وَفِى ٱلْأَرْضِ ءَايَٰتٌۭ لِّلْمُوقِنِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Ground becomes a field of visible marks.** `ء ر ض/B001` supplies lower ground and `ء ي ي/B003` visible sign; `ي ق ن/B001` supplies settled knowledge after doubt. Later destroyed settlements leave a sign (51:37), and the earth is spread/prepared (51:48). I infer **material trace apprehended -> doubt removed**. The signs are not detached symbols but marks embedded in terrain.
2. **Fertility itself can be evidentiary.** `ء ر ض/B002` supplies soft fertile ground, activated by provision from the sky (51:22), rain-linked branches, and the spreading of earth (51:48). I infer **provisioning process -> visible growth -> settled recognition**. This ecological sign-reading coexists with historical ruins.

### Retrospective surprises

51:37 and 51:48 forced two earth-signs to coexist: ruined residue warns, while spread fertile ground displays sustaining preparation.
## 51:21 — وَفِىٓ أَنفُسِكُمْ ۚ أَفَلَا تُبْصِرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **The self is both evidence and observer.** `ن ف س/B012` supplies the very thing or self, while `ب ص ر/B001` gives ocular seeing and B002 inner sight. Following signs in the earth, the deictic field contracts inward. I infer **observer turns upon its own formed identity -> external sign becomes self-witness**. The verse reads reflexively: the instrument seeking evidence is itself evidence.
2. **Breath makes life-sign immediately recurrent.** `ن ف س/B001` supplies breath leaving the body and B011 the living soul-self. Later 51:56 distinguishes hidden beings and visible humans, while 51:57-58 denies that their nourishment sustains God. I infer **repeated breath received -> creaturely dependence becomes perceptible**. This physiological reading remains beside introspective insight.

### Retrospective surprises

51:56-58 made breath and living self read as signs of creaturely dependence: the one who perceives is sustained by a Provider who does not need feeding.

## 51:22 — وَفِى ٱلسَّمَآءِ رِزْقُكُمْ وَمَا تُوعَدُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **The overhead cover is a provisioning source.** `س م و/B004` includes sky, cloud, and rain; `ر ز ق/B001` gives allotted provision and B003 rain as provision; `و ع د/B001` supplies promise. The earth-signs and springs of 51:15, 20 are thereby linked vertically to a source. I infer **heavenly water/allotment -> terrestrial sustenance -> promise made visible**. The verse reads as an ecological causal chain as well as a declaration of location.
2. **Provision and threatened appointment share one administration.** `و ع د/B002-3` supplies threat and appointed time/place, while `ر ز ق/B001` supplies allotment. Later 51:34 and 58-60 distribute marked punishment and sustaining provision under the same lordship. I infer **single apportioning authority -> beneficial and punitive shares**. The coordination *rizqukum wa-mā tūʿadūn* should not erase their divergent outcomes.

### Retrospective surprises

51:58 identifies the Provider, while 51:60 identifies the promised event-day; the coordinated nouns therefore remain two allotments under one source.

## 51:23 — فَوَرَبِّ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ إِنَّهُۥ لَحَقٌّۭ مِّثْلَ مَآ أَنَّكُمْ تَنطِقُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Truth is as embodied as articulation.** `ح ق ق/B001` supplies firm reality; `م ث ل/B001` likeness; `ن ط ق/B001` articulated audible speech. The oath by `ر ب ب/B001-2` joins authority with nurture over sky and ground. I infer **immediate bodily production of speech -> analogue for the promised reality's undeniability**. The claim is not only propositionally true but experientially present.
2. **The cosmos speaks by indication.** `ن ط ق/B002` supplies speech-like disclosure by signs, while 51:20-22 places signs in earth, selves, and sky. `ح ق ق/B005` gives making truth stand. I infer **ordered signs disclose their source -> truth stands through nonverbal “speech.”** This reading preserves literal human articulation while adding a signifying cosmos.

### Retrospective surprises

51:52 showed that speech can also misattribute and obscure, making the comparison more exacting: articulation proves immediacy, not automatic truthfulness.

## 51:24 — هَلْ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ضَيْفِ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ ٱلْمُكْرَمِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **A report arrives about those who arrived.** `ء ت ي/B001` gives coming/arrival; `ح د ث/B003` gives report or conversation; `ض ي ف/B002` the one who turns in as guest. The interrogative makes narration replicate event structure: the report comes just as the guests came. I infer **guest-arrival -> event -> report-arrival to a new hearer**. The verse activates transmission as renewed hospitality to the event.
2. **Honor is tested through hosting.** `ك ر م/B001` supplies nobility/generosity and `ض ي ف/B002` includes lodging and feeding a guest. Subsequent food preparation and refusal to eat (51:26-28) supply the social test. I infer **honored status -> hospitality obligation -> recognition problem when food is untouched**. *Mukramīn* describes both received honor and the conduct their arrival elicits.

### Retrospective surprises

51:52's repeated messenger-arrival made this arriving report one instance of a larger transmission pattern, but here hospitality opens recognition rather than defensive labeling.

## 51:25 — إِذْ دَخَلُوا۟ عَلَيْهِ فَقَالُوا۟ سَلَٰمًۭا ۖ قَالَ سَلَٰمٌۭ قَوْمٌۭ مُّنكَرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Peaceful speech crosses an unknown boundary.** `د خ ل/B001` gives entering; `س ل م/B001` soundness/safety; `ن ك ر/B001` nonrecognition. The repeated *qālū/qāla* mirrors greeting and reply, yet *qawmun munkarūn* leaves identity unresolved. I infer **reciprocal safety formula -> provisional social bridge despite failed recognition**. The verse holds welcome and uncertainty together.
2. **An entrant can remain socially unmixed.** `د خ ل/B005` supplies an outsider mixed into a group, while `ق و م/B001` supplies a people-group and `ن ك ر/B003` the unfamiliar/disquieting. Because the entrants neither eat nor initially disclose their mission, I infer **physical entry without recognized affiliation -> latent threat**. This social-structural reading explains why peace does not immediately remove apprehension.

### Retrospective surprises

51:36's single house of *muslimīn* retrospectively made *salām* more than etiquette: soundness can identify a household even amid an unrecognized people.

## 51:26 — فَرَاغَ إِلَىٰٓ أَهْلِهِۦ فَجَآءَ بِعِجْلٍۢ سَمِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Stealth protects hospitality's speed.** `ر و غ/B001` gives stealthy veering; `ء ه ل/B001` household; `ج ي ء/B004` bringing; `ع ج ل/B002` calf; `س م ن/B001` fatness. The guests are unknown, yet Abraham quietly redirects to the household and returns with substantial food. I infer **concealed preparation -> unburdened guests -> generous provision**. The movement reads as tactful hospitality rather than evasiveness toward them.
2. **The calf interrupts the root's haste branch.** Although the surface *ʿijl* is anchored in `ع ج ل/B002`, the surrounding haste pattern (`ع ج ل/B001` at 51:14, 59) makes the calf a material counter-image: prepared quickly, but not demanded prematurely.
