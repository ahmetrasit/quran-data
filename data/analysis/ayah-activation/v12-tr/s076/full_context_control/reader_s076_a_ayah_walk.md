# Surah 76 Full-Context Reader: Ayah Walk

## 76:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A raised designation over the whole movement.** The lexical anchors are **س م و B005** (“name as raised designation”) in *بسم* and **ء ل ه B001** (“worship and the worshipped one”) in *الله*. The structural cue is the later movement from the human being as not *مذكور* (76:1) to commanded naming and remembrance (76:25). The name functions as the designation under which that movement occurs. I supply the assumption that an opening designation governs the sequence it heads. The formula therefore reads not only as an invocation but as the elevation of divine designation over a surah about emergence into mention.

2. **Mercy as an enclosing origin and destination.** **ر ح م B001** (“mercy and tenderness”) is explicit, while **ر ح م B003** (“womb”) is activated by the reproductive creation of 76:2 and the final entry *في رحمته* in 76:31. The repeated root gives mercy the role of a containing medium around the human trajectory. I infer, rather than the packet stating, that the womb image supplies a material analogy for merciful enclosure. The two adjectives can thus be heard as framing both origination and final admission, without collapsing mercy into the womb sense.

### Retrospective surprises

The return of **ر ح م** in 76:31 unexpectedly turns the opening mercy from a static attribute into a surah-wide container; 76:25 likewise makes *اسم* recur as an enacted practice rather than an opening label only.

## 76:1 — هَلْ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلْإِنسَٰنِ حِينٌۭ مِّنَ ٱلدَّهْرِ لَمْ يَكُن شَيْـًۭٔا مَّذْكُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **An interval under an overmastering duration.** *حين* activates **ح ي ن B001** (“the time or due occasion of a thing”), while *الدهر* permits both **د ه ر B002** (“the stretch of time”) and **د ه ر B001** (“overmastering force”). The later severe event-days (76:7, 10, 27) make time repeatedly exert pressure rather than merely measure succession. I supply the causal assumption that the later personified and heavy days disclose a latent force in the earlier duration. The question consequently reads as human emergence from an interval in which time had mastery, not simply as a date before existence.

2. **From knowability to mention.** *شيئًا* activates the “knowable thing” variant of **ش ي ء B001**, and *مذكورًا* activates **ذ ك ر B003** (“bringing a thing to mind”), **B004** (“mention on the tongue”), and, prospectively, **B007** (“honorable mention and renown”). The sequence later supplies perception (76:2), reminder (76:29), and a striving made *مشكورًا* (76:22). I infer that being mentionable requires entry into relations of knowledge, address, and accountable action. The negation can therefore concern absence from the field of recognized, reportable significance, alongside the ordinary absence of a mentioned entity.

3. **Humanity as manifest presence.** **ء ن س B001** gives *الإنسان* the image of “visible human presence over against hidden or wild beings.” The immediate making of hearing and sight in 76:2 and the dense seeing vocabulary in 76:13, 19, and 20 structurally foreground manifestation. I supply the arrow from sensory endowment to social visibility. *الإنسان* thus reads as the being brought into perceptible presence, sharpening the contrast with having been nothing mentioned.

### Retrospective surprises

The command *واذكر اسم ربك* in 76:25 and the declaration *هذه تذكرة* in 76:29 reverse the opening lack of mention. The later **ع ل م B001** in 76:30 also makes the “knowable thing” variant of **ش ي ء B001** newly salient.

## 76:2 — إِنَّا خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِن نُّطْفَةٍ أَمْشَاجٍۢ نَّبْتَلِيهِ فَجَعَلْنَٰهُ سَمِيعًۢا بَصِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **Creation as calibrated composition.** *خلقنا* activates **خ ل ق B001** (“measuring and proportioning a thing”) beside **B002** (“originating and bringing into being”); *أمشاج* supplies **م ش ج B001** (“mixing and blending”) and **B004** (“interlaced threads”). Exact measure returns in *قدروها تقديرًا* (76:16), and bodily fastening returns in 76:28. The branches assign creation the role of proportioning mixed material into a coherent frame. I infer that later measured vessels and tightened bodies reveal a common craft logic. The ayah reads as engineered composition as well as origination.

2. **Perception as the apparatus by which a state is disclosed.** *نبتليه* anchors **ب ل و B002** (“testing and disclosure of state”), while *سميعًا* and *بصيرًا* anchor **س م ع B001/B003** (hearing; understanding and compliance) and **ب ص ر B001/B002** (ocular and inner sight). The grammar places the trial before the resulting sensory predicates, and 76:3 immediately presents a path and two responses. I supply the causal claim that hearing and seeing enable the disclosure rather than merely accompany it. The senses therefore read as morally operative trial equipment, not neutral biological gifts.

3. **A mixed origin that can become a visible disposition.** **م ش ج B003** offers “mixed color or visibly blended substance,” while **خ ل ق B004** offers “inner disposition and character.” Later alternatives of gratitude and covering (76:3), followed by differentiated outcomes, supply the structural transformation from mixture to disposition. I infer that an initially blended substrate does not mechanically determine either outcome but makes differentiation possible. The ayah can therefore stage a passage from undifferentiated mixture to disclosed character.

### Retrospective surprises

The repeated measuring in 76:16 and the bodily fastening in 76:28 retrospectively strengthen the craft reading. The many later acts of seeing, hearing, choosing, and obeying confirm that the sensory predicates are instruments in a trial sequence.

## 76:3 — إِنَّا هَدَيْنَٰهُ ٱلسَّبِيلَ إِمَّا شَاكِرًۭا وَإِمَّا كَفُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **Guidance establishes a traversable direction.** *هديناه* activates **ه د ي B001** (“gentle guidance toward a way or truth”) and **B002** (“course, manner, and intended direction”); *السبيل* activates **س ب ل B001** (“extended traversed way”). The same way is actively *taken* in 76:29. I supply the assumption that guidance makes a route practicable but does not perform the later taking. The ayah reads as placement before a directional possibility, preserving rather than erasing the two responses.

2. **Gratitude as making a small provision show.** *شاكرًا* supports **ش ك ر B001** (“recognizing a favor and praising the benefactor”) and **B002** (“being sustained by little and showing its effect”). Feeding despite attachment to food (76:8) and the eventual declaration that striving is *مشكور* (76:22) give the latter branch a visible mechanism. I infer that received provision becomes legible through onward action. Gratitude thus reads not only as verbal acknowledgment but as making a gift bear social fruit.

3. **Covering as the counter-use of disclosed faculties.** *كفورًا* activates **ك ف ر B001** (“covering and concealment”), **B003** (“covering or rejecting truth”), and **B004** (“covering over a favor”). The preceding ayah supplies hearing and sight; 76:27 later depicts people putting the heavy day behind them. I infer that concealment can be enacted by turning perception away from what has been made available. The contrast is therefore between manifesting received guidance and covering it, not merely between two labels.

### Retrospective surprises

The return of *سبيلًا* in 76:29 corrects any deterministic reading: the path must still be taken. The reversal from recipients’ unrequested thanks in 76:9 to divinely thanked striving in 76:22 deepens the active **ش ك ر B002** reading.

## 76:4 — إِنَّآ أَعْتَدْنَا لِلْكَٰفِرِينَ سَلَٰسِلَا۟ وَأَغْلَٰلًۭا وَسَعِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **A closed system of linkage, restraint, and excitation.** *سلاسل* anchors **س ل س ل B002** (“linked chain-like connection”), *أغلالًا* anchors **غ ل ل B006** (“a collar binding a limb”), and *سعيرًا* anchors **س ع ر B001** (“fire kindling and blazing”) and **B004** (“burning agitation”). The coordinated list moves from linked extension to bodily closure to internal or external blaze. I supply the causal model that restraint prevents escape while agitation supplies continuous force within the enclosure. The punishment reads as an integrated mechanism, not three unrelated objects.

2. **Prepared consequence answers practiced covering.** *أعتدنا* activates **ع ت د B001** (“prepared and present”), while *الكافرين* activates **ك ف ر B003/B004** (covering truth or favor). The perfect verb presents the apparatus as ready before the later scene of people choosing the immediate and obscuring the heavy day (76:27). I infer that repeated covering makes an already prepared consequence operative; the packet gives readiness and later conduct, while this directional link is mine. The ayah therefore acquires juridical immediacy rather than describing a remote improvisation.

3. **Punitive binding is later answered by yielding and freedom.** The fixed anchors remain **س ل س ل B002** and **غ ل ل B006**. Later silk activates **ح ر ر B002** (“freedom opposed to bondage,” 76:12), and fruit is made gently yielding in 76:14. I infer that the reward sequence deliberately reverses the mechanics of this ayah. Chains and collars then read as one side of a surah-wide contrast between imposed constraint and responsive ease.

### Retrospective surprises

The bracelet encircling the wrist in 76:21 most sharply transforms the collar image: bodily encirclement reappears as adornment rather than restraint. The strengthened *أسرهم* in 76:28 also reveals that binding can constitute a body before it becomes punitive captivity.

## 76:5 — إِنَّ ٱلْأَبْرَارَ يَشْرَبُونَ مِن كَأْسٍۢ كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا كَافُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **Righteousness as conduct that holds true.** *الأبرار* activates **ب ر ر B001** (“truth that makes word or deed hold”) beside **B002** (“expansive righteousness and obedience”). Vow fulfillment in 76:7 and feeding without exchange in 76:8–9 supply the later acts that make the designation functional. I infer that the following relative sequence explains why their word and deed “hold.” The drinker is therefore identified by reliable enacted truth, not by an unexplained status.

2. **Moral and material mixture mirror one another without becoming identical.** *مزاجها* anchors **م ز ج B001** (“mixing one thing with another”); the mixed reproductive substance of 76:2 and the second cup-mixture in 76:17 flank the moral narrative. I supply the analogy that the surah repeatedly forms consequential wholes through composition. The cup reads as the beneficial completion of a trajectory that began in mixed origin and passed through disclosed choice.

3. **The covering root is materially transposed.** *كافورًا* directly anchors **ك ف ر B011** (“camphor or aromatic kāfūr”), while proximity to *الكافرين* in 76:4 keeps **ك ف ر B001/B003** (covering; covering truth) audible in the root field. The packet does not state that camphor reverses unbelief; I infer a deliberate lexical-material contrast in which the same root inventory moves from culpable concealment to an aromatic ingredient. The reading preserves the botanical/aromatic branch while hearing a local transformation of “covering” from moral blockage into a pleasurable mixture.

### Retrospective surprises

The ginger mixture in 76:17 confirms that *مزاج* names a repeated compositional system. The final contrast between mercy and prepared torment in 76:31 makes the immediate turn from *الكافرين* to *كافورًا* look even more deliberately reversible.

## 76:6 — عَيْنًۭا يَشْرَبُ بِهَا عِبَادُ ٱللَّهِ يُفَجِّرُونَهَا تَفْجِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A source that is also an image of watchful provision.** *عينًا* directly activates **ع ي ن B006** (“the springing water-eye”), while **B003** (“the eye of watchful care”) is activated by the earlier gift of sight and the later Lord-given drink (76:21). The spring supplies drink; the eye branch supplies the relational image of provision under care. I infer the analogy between a watched-over community and a maintained source. The spring can thus be read materially and as a visible sign of care, without replacing one branch with the other.

2. **Recipients become distributors of abundance.** The doubled *يفجرونها تفجيرًا* anchors **ف ج ر B001** (“wide splitting and outflow”) and activates **B005** (“overflowing generosity”). The servants first drink and then actively open channels; feeding others follows in 76:8. I supply the causal arrow from receiving to routing outward. Their drinking is therefore not terminal consumption: it equips an agency that makes the source flow broadly.

3. **Servitude combines obedience with effective environmental action.** *عباد* anchors **ع ب د B003** (“worship and submissive obedience”), while the transitive intensive verb assigns the servants forceful agency. Later the garden itself yields in 76:14. I infer that obedience here enables rather than cancels capacity. “Servants of Allah” consequently reads as a social role whose submission produces active distribution.

### Retrospective surprises

The passive service of cups in 76:15 and the Lord’s direct giving of drink in 76:21 reveal a layered supply network: servants can open sources while also receiving service. The second named spring in 76:18 makes the eye/source image recur as an organized feature.

## 76:7 — يُوفُونَ بِٱلنَّذْرِ وَيَخَافُونَ يَوْمًۭا كَانَ شَرُّهُۥ مُسْتَطِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Chosen binding counters imposed binding.** *يوفون* activates **و ف ي B001** (“complete fullness without shortfall”), and *النذر* activates **ن ذ ر B002** (“binding oneself to an otherwise non-obligatory vow”). Chains and collars precede in 76:4, while bodily fastening returns in 76:28. I infer that voluntary self-binding to a promise prevents the moral drift that ends in imposed restraint. Fulfillment reads as a complete chosen bond, structurally opposed to punitive bonds.

2. **Fear contains a harm imagined as flight-like spread.** *يخافون* anchors **خ و ف B001** (“fearful anticipation of harm”); *مستطيرًا* activates **ط ي ر B002** (“spreading as if flying loose”), and *شره* can also evoke **ش ر ر B003** (“flying sparks of fire”) beside **B001** (“evil and badness”). The nearby blazing fire gives the spread a material analogy. I supply the claim that anticipatory fear motivates containment through vows and feeding. Fear therefore reads as prospective moral intelligence, not immobilization.

3. **The day is an event rather than a calendar unit.** *يومًا* activates **ي و م B003** (“an event-day or severe occasion”). Its evil spreads here, its face contracts in 76:10, and its weight returns in 76:27. I infer that these predicates construct one operative event. The day is read as a concentrated encounter whose effects reach backward into present conduct.

### Retrospective surprises

The “frowning” day in 76:10 gives a face to the spread feared here, while divine protection in 76:11 supplies the later barrier that stops it. The “heavy” day in 76:27 confirms that the event exerts pressure before it arrives.

## 76:8 — وَيُطْعِمُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ مِسْكِينًۭا وَيَتِيمًۭا وَأَسِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **Attachment is converted into provision.** *حبه* activates **ح ب ب B002** (“love as clinging attachment”), while the doubled feeding vocabulary activates **ط ع م B002** (“feeding and asking for food”) and **B004** (“provision, livelihood, and good condition”). The prepositional construction places feeding across or despite attachment. I infer that relinquishing what clings to the giver converts private value into another’s sustaining condition. The act reads as an engineered release of attachment, not generic generosity.

2. **Food addresses distinct forms of interrupted support.** *مسكينًا* activates **س ك ن B001** (“cessation of motion”) and **B006** (“poverty and humble abasement”); *يتيمًا* activates **ي ت م B001** (“offspring cut off from its protecting parent”); *أسيرًا* activates **ء س ر B001** (“binding with a tether and holding captive”). The three objects form a sequence of immobilization, severed protection, and imposed binding. I infer that food restores a minimum capacity across these different constraints. Feeding thus reads as targeted repair of broken social and bodily mobility.

3. **The captive anticipates the body as a binding.** The fixed anchor **ء س ر B001** is later paired with **ء س ر B002** (“strength of bodily frame and joints”) in 76:28. I infer that the later bodily branch reveals an ambiguity in restraint: fastening can sustain a body or hold a person. Calling the recipient *أسيرًا* therefore makes care reach even the person whose binding has become social captivity.

### Retrospective surprises

The strengthened *أسرهم* of 76:28 unexpectedly reopens the captive’s root as bodily cohesion. The condemnation of loving the immediate in 76:27 also clarifies that the problem is not love itself but attachment that refuses release.

## 76:9 — إِنَّمَا نُطْعِمُكُمْ لِوَجْهِ ٱللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمْ جَزَآءًۭ وَلَا شُكُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **The act is given a direction rather than an exchange price.** *لوجه* activates **و ج ه B002** (“orientation and direction”) and **B008** (“proper aspect or course”), while *نريد* anchors **ر و د B001** (“will and intention”). The two negations remove return from the recipients, and 76:29 later uses an explicit way *إلى ربه*. I infer that divine orientation supplies the act’s governing direction when horizontal repayment is refused. “For the face of Allah” therefore reads as an intentional course-setting construction as well as devotional address.

2. **Matching recompense is declined at one level and restored at another.** *جزاء* anchors **ج ز ي B001** (“recompense matching an act”), and *شكورًا* anchors **ش ك ر B001** (“recognizing a favor and praising the benefactor”). The speakers reject both from those fed; 76:12 and 76:22 later assign recompense and thanks from another source. I supply the vertical redirection: the packet supplies refusal here and return there, while their causal linkage is inferred. The ayah reads as refusal of a closed donor-recipient transaction, not refusal that action have any answer.

3. **Desire is prevented from scouting a return.** **ر و د B003** (“seeking and scouting something”) is a dormant branch in *نريد*. The double *لا* and *منكم* tightly specify what is not sought. I infer that the language blocks the giver from monitoring recipients for social yield. Intention is thus disciplined not merely at the moment of giving but in the afterward search for recognition.

### Retrospective surprises

The declaration *كان سعيكم مشكورًا* in 76:22 is the decisive reversal: gratitude refused from recipients returns as divine recognition of the givers’ striving. The explicit recompense scenes in 76:12 and 76:22 preserve rather than cancel the non-transactional character here.

## 76:10 — إِنَّا نَخَافُ مِن رَّبِّنَا يَوْمًا عَبُوسًۭا قَمْطَرِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **The event compresses itself into a face.** *عبوسًا* activates **ع ب س B001** (“facial frowning from distress or anger”) and **B002** (“a harsh grim day”); *قمطريرًا* activates **ق م ط ر B002** (“piling up and drawing together”) beside **B001** (“a harsh day spreading trouble”). Facial contraction and accumulating trouble form one structural image. I infer that the day’s distributed pressure is rendered as a contracted countenance. The day reads simultaneously as environment and confronting face.

2. **Fear occurs within a relation of nurture and authority.** *ربنا* supports **ر ب ب B001** (“lordship, ownership, and authority”) and **B002** (“repair, nurture, and completion”). The feared day comes “from our Lord,” yet 76:11 immediately depicts protection and 76:21 direct giving of a purifying drink. I infer that authority and nurture are not separate agents in the sequence. Fear therefore reads as seriousness before a formative lordship, not fear of an arbitrary hostile power.

### Retrospective surprises

The next ayah answers the contracted face with *نضرة* and the inward pressure with *سرور*. The Lord’s direct service in 76:21 confirms that the relational title here also carries nurture.

## 76:11 — فَوَقَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ شَرَّ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْيَوْمِ وَلَقَّىٰهُمْ نَضْرَةًۭ وَسُرُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Protection interrupts a previously spreading force.** *وقاهم* activates **و ق ي B001** (“warding harm off with a protective barrier”), while *شر* retains **ش ر ر B001** (“evil and badness”) and the preceding flight-like spread of 76:7. The *fa-* presents protection as the answer to their feared event. I supply the directional model that a barrier arrests propagation. Divine guarding reads as active interception, not mere absence of injury.

2. **The meeting replaces one face with another.** *لقاهم* activates **ل ق ي B004** (“two things coming face to face”) and **B007** (“what one meets of good or ill”); *نضرة* activates **ن ض ر B001** (“radiant freshness and beauty”). The prior day was facially *عبوس*; now what is made to meet them is radiance. I infer a deliberate counter-encounter. The ayah reads as substitution of the day’s threatening face with a fresh face borne by the protected persons.

3. **Outer radiance and inward joy form a two-layer repair.** *سرورًا* directly activates **س ر ر B010** (“joy and ease”), while **B001** (“keeping something hidden inwardly”) supplies the root’s spatial depth. The coordinate pair places visible freshness beside inward affect. I infer that protection repairs both exposed appearance and concealed interior. *سرور* therefore reads as joy installed within, complementing rather than duplicating *نضرة*.

### Retrospective surprises

The later garments, bracelets, and purifying drink in 76:21 materialize the outer/inner pairing: adornment carries visible freshness, while drink enters and cleanses inwardly. No later ayah overturns the barrier model.

## 76:12 — وَجَزَىٰهُم بِمَا صَبَرُوا۟ جَنَّةًۭ وَحَرِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Recompense matches sustained self-restraint.** *جزاهم* activates **ج ز ي B001** (“recompense matching an act”), and *صبروا* activates **ص ب ر B001** (“self-restraint from panic”). Vow fulfillment, released attachment, and refusal of repayment in 76:7–9 specify what that restraint looked like. I infer that the garden is not matched to bare endurance but to disciplined continuity across those acts. The causal *بما* reads as a mechanism of return, not an unexplained gift label.

2. **Covering is transformed into shelter.** *جنة* activates **ج ن ن B003** (“tree-covered garden”) and its underlying **B001** (“covering and concealment”). This follows culpable covering in **ك ف ر B001/B003** and precedes lowered shade in 76:14. I infer that the reward converts the material logic of covering from obstruction into protective habitat. The garden reads as beneficial concealment rather than simply a location.

3. **Silk carries a latent release from bondage.** *حريرًا* directly activates **ح ر ر B010** (“fine silk cloth”), while chains and collars in 76:4 activate the contrastive **ح ر ر B002** (“freedom opposed to bondage”). Later these textiles are worn in 76:21. I infer that the cloth gives bodily form to release. Silk therefore reads both as luxurious material and as the tactile opposite of punitive binding.

### Retrospective surprises

The textile description in 76:21 completes the silk branch as clothing, while bracelets there convert another form of encirclement into adornment. The repeated command to *اصبر* in 76:24 also projects the rewarded quality back into the present task of the addressee.

## 76:13 — مُّتَّكِـِٔينَ فِيهَا عَلَى ٱلْأَرَآئِكِ ۖ لَا يَرَوْنَ فِيهَا شَمْسًۭا وَلَا زَمْهَرِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Held endurance becomes supported rest.** *متكئين* activates **و ك ء B004** (“leaning or relying on a support”), and *الأرائك* activates **ء ر ك B004** (“a couch or bed inside a curtained chamber”). This posture follows *صبروا* and precedes an environment made near and yielding. I infer a reversal from self-maintained restraint to externally supplied support. Reclining therefore reads as the bodily resolution of endurance, not merely scenery.

2. **Sight now verifies the absence of extremes.** *يرون* activates **ر ء ي B001** (“seeing by eye or insight”), *شمسًا* activates **ش م س B001** (“sun and daylight”), and *زمهريرًا* activates **ز م ه ر B001** (“intense cold”). The human was made seeing in 76:2; here sight encounters neither thermal pole. I infer that the coordinated negation defines a regulated habitat by what perception no longer has to suffer. The ayah reads as sensory confirmation of balance.

3. **An enclosure without withholding.** The curtained chamber of **ء ر ك B004** and garden-covering of **ج ن ن B001/B003** surround the residents, but 76:14 makes shade and fruit accessible. I infer that enclosure here shelters without blocking provision. The fixed couch-in-chamber image thus participates in a corrective form of covering, unlike the concealment and restraint associated with rejection.

### Retrospective surprises

The measured vessels of 76:16 and purifying drink of 76:21 extend the absence of excess into calibrated consumption. The repeated *رأيت* in 76:19–20 broadens sight from verifying climate to surveying an entire order.

## 76:14 — وَدَانِيَةً عَلَيْهِمْ ظِلَٰلُهَا وَذُلِّلَتْ قُطُوفُهَا تَذْلِيلًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **The habitat closes practical distance.** *دانية* activates **د ن و B001** (“nearness and approximation”), *ظلالها* activates **ظ ل ل B003** (“overhead canopy”), and *قطوفها* activates **ق ط ف B002** (“picked fruit or low-hanging cluster”). Above, near, and low form a coherent spatial arrangement around the residents. I infer that the environment actively removes the reach-cost of provision. The ayah reads as designed accessibility rather than a loose inventory of shade and fruit.

2. **Yielding is explicitly separated from humiliation.** The doubled *ذللت ... تذليلًا* permits **ذ ل ل B002** (“gentle yielding without humiliation”), **B003** (“pliant ease after difficulty”), and **B005** (“low hanging or low-set parts”), alongside the rejected alternative **B001** (“abasement under overpowering”). The grammatical patient is the fruit clusters, not the residents. I infer that agency has been transferred to the environment so that ease does not require human abasement. The repetition intensifies accessibility while preserving dignity.

3. **Harvest-time has arrived.** **ق ط ف B003** gives “harvest time drawing near or arriving,” activated by *دانية* and by the prior causal recompense. I infer that spatial nearness also marks temporal ripeness. The clusters read as reward brought to its due moment, not merely positioned low.

### Retrospective surprises

The exact sizing of vessels in 76:16 repeats the same removal of friction: fruit and containers are both fitted to use. The bracelets of 76:21 confirm that lowered, yielding provision does not lower the recipients’ rank.

## 76:15 — وَيُطَافُ عَلَيْهِم بِـَٔانِيَةٍۢ مِّن فِضَّةٍۢ وَأَكْوَابٍۢ كَانَتْ قَوَارِيرَا۠

### Activated readings

1. **Provision reaches them through circulation.** *يطاف* activates **ط و ف B001** (“circling around something”), *آنية* activates **ء ن ي B004** (“vessel or container”), and *أكواب* activates **ك و ب B001** (“cup without a handle”). The passive construction centers recipients while service moves around them; 76:19 supplies mobile attendants. I infer an organized circulation system linking containers, servers, and seated users. The scene reads dynamically, not as a static display of tableware.

2. **The vessels are both material objects and visual rest.** *قواريرا* directly activates **ق ر ر B012** (“glass vessel”), while **ق ر ر B002** (“settled comfort of the eye”) is activated by their visual comparison and the repeated seeing sequence. *فضة* remains anchored in **ف ض ض B003** (“white silver metal”). I infer that the unusual material pairing makes the vessels’ visibility itself comforting. They read as functional containers whose appearance participates in settled ease.

3. **Completion precedes exact measure.** In *آنية*, **ء ن ي B003** (“reaching due completion”) is activated by 76:16’s immediate *قدروها تقديرًا*. The branch does not replace **B004**; it adds a readiness image to the containers. I infer that what circulates has already reached its proper finished state. The vessels are thus prepared-to-fit instruments rather than generic abundance.

### Retrospective surprises

The next ayah supplies the decisive calibration mechanism and prevents “silver glass” from remaining decorative paradox. The circling youths of 76:19 later identify the animate layer of the circulation left implicit here.

## 76:16 — قَوَارِيرَا۟ مِن فِضَّةٍۢ قَدَّرُوهَا تَقْدِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Measure is exact, deliberate, and fitted.** The cognate repetition *قدروها تقديرًا* activates **ق د ر B001** (“measure reaching a thing’s extent”), **B005** (“arranging by estimate and deliberation”), and **B006** (“fitting proportion and middle measure”). Its object is the glassware carried forward from 76:15. I infer that repeated root and object relation foreground user-fit, not merely quantity. The vessels read as calibrated to their service and drinkers.

2. **Fragility and stable material are held together.** *قواريرا* anchors **ق ر ر B012** (“glass vessel”) and *فضة* anchors **ف ض ض B003** (“white silver metal”); **ف ض ض B001** (“breaking by separating”) is latently activated by the glass form. I infer that exact proportion is what lets ordinarily opposed material expectations cohere in the image. The phrase reads as controlled conjunction of transparency, metallic substance, and breakability rather than an unexamined luxury label.

3. **Creation and reward share a proportional logic.** The fixed **ق د ر B001/B006** branches reactivate **خ ل ق B001** (“measuring and proportioning”) from 76:2. I supply the analogy that the same logic that fitted the human constitution now fits what serves it. The ayah therefore makes reward responsive to embodied measure.

### Retrospective surprises

The bodily strengthening in 76:28 confirms that proportion and fastening belong to both persons and their environment. No later ayah corrects the fitted-service reading.

## 76:17 — وَيُسْقَوْنَ فِيهَا كَأْسًۭا كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا زَنجَبِيلًا

### Activated readings

1. **The cup includes what it carries.** *كأسًا* activates the sole **ك ء س B001** (“the cup with what it contains”), and *مزاجها* activates **م ز ج B001** (“mixing one thing with another”). The passive *يسقون* makes the contents, not possession of a vessel, the operative gift. I infer that container and mixture form a single serving unit. The “cup” therefore reads metonymically as administered drink, not empty tableware.

2. **A second mixture preserves plurality rather than forcing one flavor.** The same **م ز ج B001** appeared with camphor in 76:5; here the complement changes to ginger. Repetition of construction with altered ingredient is the structural cue. I infer that the reward system accommodates distinct compositions while maintaining the same mixing relation. This cup should therefore remain a second reading and experience, not be merged with the first into a compromise flavor.

3. **Agency shifts across the drinking sequence.** *يسقون* activates **س ق ي B001** (“giving drink to a drinker”), whereas 76:5 used active *يشربون* and 76:6 made servants open the source. I infer a staged relation: they consume, distribute, and are served in different scenes. The passive here reads as reception within a reciprocal ecology of provision, not passivity as a fixed status.

### Retrospective surprises

The named spring in 76:18 grounds this mixture in a source, while 76:21 culminates the sequence with the Lord directly giving a purifying drink. These later distinctions preserve, rather than flatten, the multiple drinking relations.

## 76:18 — عَيْنًۭا فِيهَا تُسَمَّىٰ سَلْسَبِيلًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A material source becomes a recognized identity.** *عينًا* anchors **ع ي ن B006** (“the springing water-eye”) and *تسمى* anchors **س م و B005** (“name as raised designation”). The passive naming follows an unnamed spring in 76:6 and anticipates commanded naming in 76:25. I infer that designation lifts a source from generic function into shared recognition. The ayah reads as institutional naming of provision as well as identification of a spring.

2. **The source itself, not only its water, is foregrounded.** **ع ي ن B013** (“the thing itself”) is activated by the compact appositional construction after the mixed cup. The previous ayah gives the served composition; this ayah points to the source in its own right. I infer a move from effect back to origin. *عينًا* can therefore stress the source-as-such, preserving a second reading beside the ordinary water-eye.

### Retrospective surprises

The return of *اسم* in 76:25 makes the spring’s naming part of a larger passage from namelessness to deliberate designation. The opening *بسم* is thereby retrospectively linked to this local act of naming.

## 76:19 — ۞ وَيَطُوفُ عَلَيْهِمْ وِلْدَٰنٌۭ مُّخَلَّدُونَ إِذَا رَأَيْتَهُمْ حَسِبْتَهُمْ لُؤْلُؤًۭا مَّنثُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **The service system acquires mobile agents.** *يطوف* activates **ط و ف B001** (“circling around something”), matching the passive circulation of vessels in 76:15. *ولدان* activates **و ل د B001** (“born offspring”) and **B004** (“young born one”). I infer that these figures are the animate carriers within the previously implicit circuit. The ayah reads as coordinated service in motion, not a detached portrait.

2. **Permanence becomes embodied adornment.** *مخلدون* activates **خ ل د B001** (“enduring permanence”) and **B003** (“adornment fixed on the body”); *لؤلؤًا* activates **ل ء ل ء B001** (“pearl-existence”) and **B002** (“gleaming sparkle”). The simile joins enduring bodies to ornament, while *منثورًا* adds **ن ث ر B001** (“scattering a thing apart”). I infer that distributed attendants visually ornament the whole space. They can be read both as permanently youthful agents and as fixed-yet-scattered living adornment.

3. **Seeing is explicitly distinguished from reckoning.** *رأيتهم* activates **ر ء ي B001** (“seeing by eye or insight”), while *حسبتهم* activates **ح س ب B002** (“reckoning as supposition”). The conditional grammar stages perception first and inference second. I infer that the pearl judgment is invited but marked as an observer’s construal. The ayah therefore teaches a disciplined double reading: actual persons and pearl-like visual array coexist without identification.

### Retrospective surprises

The doubled *رأيت* in 76:20 immediately expands the observer’s field from these distributed figures to the total environment. That expansion confirms that their scattering helps organize a larger visual order.

## 76:20 — وَإِذَا رَأَيْتَ ثَمَّ رَأَيْتَ نَعِيمًۭا وَمُلْكًۭا كَبِيرًا

### Activated readings

1. **Repeated seeing scales from point to system.** The doubled *رأيت* activates **ر ء ي B001** (“seeing by eye or insight”) and **B006** (“visible appearance and mirror”). The prior ayah focused on youths mistaken for pearls; *ثم* now leaves the object open until well-being and dominion fill the field. I infer that the repetition performs a zoom from local appearance to systemic comprehension. Seeing here reads as inspection of an order, not a glance at one luxury.

2. **Dominion is cohesive capacity as well as possession.** *ملكًا* activates **م ل ك B001** (“strength and cohesion”), **B002** (“ownership and disposal”), and **B003** (“kingship and dominion”); *كبيرًا* activates **ك ب ر B001** (“greatness”) and **B002** (“the chief share or bulk of an affair”). Circulating service, measured vessels, near fruit, and regulated climate are the structural cues. I infer that their coordination is what makes dominion “great.” The phrase can therefore name a large, coherent operating order, not only extensive property.

3. **Well-being is ease bestowed through design.** *نعيمًا* activates **ن ع م B001** (“pleasant well-being and bestowed favor”) and **B002** (“softness and easeful living”). The packet has already shown support, shade, access, and exact measure. I infer that ease is produced by those relations. *نعيم* reads as the experienced state generated by the preceding mechanisms, not a free-floating abstraction.

### Retrospective surprises

The clothing, bracelets, and direct drink in 76:21 add embodied and relational layers to the system just surveyed. The statement of recompense in 76:22 then assigns this ordered whole a moral source.

## 76:21 — عَٰلِيَهُمْ ثِيَابُ سُندُسٍ خُضْرٌۭ وَإِسْتَبْرَقٌۭ ۖ وَحُلُّوٓا۟ أَسَاوِرَ مِن فِضَّةٍۢ وَسَقَىٰهُمْ رَبُّهُمْ شَرَابًۭا طَهُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **The outer surface manifests a returned state.** *عاليهم* activates **ع ل و B005** (“the upper side and from above”), and *ثياب* activates **ث و ب B003** (“clothing and the self as garment”). **ث و ب B002** (“recompense returning to the doer”) is activated by 76:22’s immediate *جزاء*. I infer that garments externalize a state returned from prior action. Clothing reads both as what lies over them and as recompense made bodily visible.

2. **Green cloth joins growth to ease.** *خضر* activates **خ ض ر B002** (“fresh green growth”) and **B003** (“fresh ease and prosperity”), while the garden and low fruit provide the ecological cue. I infer that vegetal freshness has migrated into textile color and social condition. The garments therefore preserve a living-growth reading beside their material description.

3. **Encirclement is transformed from restraint into honor.** *أساور* directly activates **س و ر B004** (“bracelet encircling the wrist”); chains and collars in 76:4 provide the reversed structural cue. I infer that both constrain a circumference, but only the earlier apparatus blocks movement. The bracelet reads as liberated encirclement and adornment, retaining the contrast rather than merging punishment and reward.

4. **The final drink cleanses inwardly under nurturing lordship.** *سقاهم* activates **س ق ي B001** (“giving drink to a drinker”), *ربهم* activates **ر ب ب B002** (“repair, nurture, and completion”), and *طهورًا* activates **ط ه ر B004** (“purifying water or medium”) and **B005** (“cleansing the self and conduct from blame”). The sequence moves from outer garments and ornaments to ingested drink. I infer that the Lord’s service completes an inner repair corresponding to outer adornment. The drink remains a beverage while also functioning as a medium of purification.

### Retrospective surprises

The next ayah explicitly names recompense and thanked striving, confirming the return-image latent in **ث و ب B002**. The later reminder and obedience commands show that cleansing of conduct remains a live category rather than decorative purity.

## 76:22 — إِنَّ هَٰذَا كَانَ لَكُمْ جَزَآءًۭ وَكَانَ سَعْيُكُم مَّشْكُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **The whole preceding order is a matching return.** *جزاء* activates **ج ز ي B001** (“recompense matching an act”), while the demonstrative *هذا* gathers the garden, service, clothing, and drink into one referent. The earlier refusal of recipient repayment in 76:9 is the key reversal. I infer that the return is displaced from those fed to divine address. Recompense reads as a comprehensive answered pattern, not a single prize.

2. **Striving itself becomes the recipient of gratitude.** *سعيكم* activates **س ع ي B001** (“purposeful going or brisk movement”) and **B002** (“work, earning, and active endeavor”); *مشكورًا* activates **ش ك ر B001** (“recognizing a favor and praising the benefactor”) and **B002** (“being sustained by little and showing its effect”). The passive adjective reverses the agents’ refusal to seek *شكورًا* in 76:9. I infer that the small acts whose effects they made visible are now themselves recognized. Gratitude thus circulates without becoming a human exchange contract.

3. **Motion is acknowledged after a sequence of released constraints.** The fixed **س ع ي B001** is sharpened by chains (76:4), the immobilized poor and captive (76:8), and easeful access (76:14). I infer that “striving” names purposeful movement through constraints, both bodily and moral. The ayah reads as recognition of a traversed course, reconnecting it to *السبيل* in 76:3.

### Retrospective surprises

The return to patience, judgment, and obedience in 76:24 shows that the recognized striving is also a model for the addressee’s present work. The path language in 76:29 later makes its directional structure explicit.

## 76:23 — إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا عَلَيْكَ ٱلْقُرْءَانَ تَنزِيلًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Descent is emphatic and ordered.** The doubled verb/verbal noun *نزلنا ... تنزيلًا* activates **ن ز ل B002** (“bringing down or sending down”) and **B004** (“placing in proper order”). Pronoun emphasis and cognate repetition make agency and process unusually explicit. I infer that the descent’s repetition foregrounds ordered delivery rather than a bare transfer from above. The Quran reads as deliberately placed communication.

2. **Recitation gathers the preceding field.** *القرآن* activates the “gathering speech into recitation” variant of **ق ر ء B001** and the reading/reciting variant of **B002**. Its position after many linked material and moral scenes and before commands to endure, remember, and prostrate gives gathering an operational role. I infer that recitation collects those relations into actionable address. The noun reads as gathered speech that reorganizes the listener’s conduct, not only as a title.

3. **Revelation can be heard as guest-provision.** **ن ز ل B005** gives “guest-provision,” activated by the long hospitality sequence of cups, food, service, and drink in 76:5–21. The fixed descent root remains the anchor; the feast imagery is the structural cue. I infer an exploratory analogy in which sent-down speech provisions its recipient as hospitality provisioned the righteous. This reading is secondary because the packet does not explicitly call the addressee a guest.

### Retrospective surprises

The immediate commands in 76:24–26 confirm that gathered recitation is meant to produce ordered practice. The final *تذكرة* in 76:29 clarifies its mnemonic function without erasing the exploratory provision image.

## 76:24 — فَٱصْبِرْ لِحُكْمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تُطِعْ مِنْهُمْ ءَاثِمًا أَوْ كَفُورًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **The rewarded restraint becomes a present imperative.** *اصبر* activates **ص ب ر B001** (“self-restraint from panic”), repeating the quality rewarded in 76:12. The *fa-* ties the command to the ordered descent of 76:23. I infer that reception of revelation requires the same sustained restraint embodied earlier by the righteous. The command reads as entry into their mechanism, not an isolated demand to wait.

2. **Judgment functions as corrective restraint.** *حكم* activates **ح ك م B001** (“restraining and turning back to set right”), **B002** (“judgment and adjudication”), and **B004** (“making firm, perfected, and decisive”). The coordinated prohibition *ولا تطع* supplies the rival pressure that must be restrained. I infer that the Lord’s judgment sets a boundary that turns the addressee back from competing obedience. *لحكم ربك* thus reads as patience for and within a corrective governing order.

3. **Two rival modes are delay and covering.** *آثمًا* activates **ء ث م B001** (“slowing and lagging from good”), while *كفورًا* activates **ك ف ر B003/B004** (covering truth or favor). *تطع* activates **ط و ع B001** (“pliant obedience and willing yielding”) and **B006** (“making an act easy to oneself”). I infer that obeying either figure would internalize one of two obstructions: lagging from good or concealing it. The prohibition reads as refusal to make those obstructive courses easy to oneself.

### Retrospective surprises

The next two ayat supply the positive replacement for rival obedience: naming, prostration, and praise. The people who love the immediate in 76:27 then exemplify the lagging/covering mechanism rather than introducing an unrelated group.

## 76:25 — وَٱذْكُرِ ٱسْمَ رَبِّكَ بُكْرَةًۭ وَأَصِيلًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Mention reverses primordial unmentionedness.** *اذكر* activates **ذ ك ر B003** (“bringing a thing to mind”) and **B004** (“mention on the tongue”); *اسم* activates **س م و B005** (“name as raised designation”). The opening human was not *مذكورًا* (76:1), while the spring was named in 76:18. I infer that deliberate divine mention is the practice by which the addressee resists falling out of mindful relation. The command reads as active reversal of forgetful or unmentioned existence.

2. **The daily span is both temporal and productive.** *بكرة* activates **ب ك ر B001** (“early morning and going early”) and **B002** (“the first or earliest yield”); *أصيلًا* directly activates **ء ص ل B003** (“evening time after afternoon”), while **B001** (“root and foundation”) remains latently available. Morning and evening form the stated span. I infer that first-yield and foundation images make repeated naming both the day’s first product and its grounding practice. The ordinary temporal reading remains distinct from this productive-rooted reading.

3. **Naming participates in nurture.** *ربك* activates **ر ب ب B002** (“repair, nurture, and completion”). It sits between the named object and repeated times. I infer that regular mention is a means by which the Lord-recipient relation is sustained. The divine name reads not as detached recital but as a repeated formative relation.

### Retrospective surprises

The extended night practice in 76:26 fills the interval beyond the named morning/evening poles. The *تذكرة* of 76:29 later expands personal mention into the reminder-character of the whole discourse.

## 76:26 — وَمِنَ ٱلَّيْلِ فَٱسْجُدْ لَهُۥ وَسَبِّحْهُ لَيْلًۭا طَوِيلًا

### Activated readings

1. **Night practice has a spatial choreography.** Repeated *ليل* activates **ل ي ل B001** (“night as the opposite of day and its darkness”), *اسجد* activates **س ج د B001** (“lowering in humble submission”), and *سبحه* activates both **س ب ح B001** (“worship through praise and prayer”) and **B004** (“motion by swimming or coursing”). Lowering and coursing occur within an extended darkness. I infer a navigational mechanism: prostration establishes orientation while praise carries practice through the night. The ayah reads as embodied movement, not only scheduled utterance.

2. **Length is sustained duration and deliberately given time.** *طويلًا* activates **ط و ل B001** (“length and extension”), **B003** (“long duration”), and **B007** (“prolonging, delaying, and giving time”). The second *ليلًا* plus adjective marks more than a punctual act. I infer that the worshipper intentionally yields a substantial interval rather than merely encountering a naturally long night. The phrase therefore reads as durational commitment.

3. **Praise is also purification.** *سبحه* activates **س ب ح B002** (“purification and declaring free”), sharpened by the purifying drink in 76:21 and the coming critique of attachment in 76:27. I infer that repeated declaration clears the field of rival valuations. Praise can thus be read as a cleansing cognitive act alongside prayer.

### Retrospective surprises

The immediate exposure of love for the *عاجلة* in 76:27 clarifies what the night’s durational practice resists: compressed attention to what arrives first. No later ayah displaces the embodied reading.

## 76:27 — إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ يُحِبُّونَ ٱلْعَاجِلَةَ وَيَذَرُونَ وَرَآءَهُمْ يَوْمًۭا ثَقِيلًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Attachment fastens onto what is brought forward.** *يحبون* activates **ح ب ب B002** (“love as clinging attachment”), and *العاجلة* activates **ع ج ل B001** (“hastening and bringing forward”) and **B005** (“hurried provision or food”). The feeding and drinking sequence gives “hurried provision” a concrete contrast: the righteous release food, whereas these people cling to immediacy. I infer that the immediate is treated as quickly consumable provision. The critique reads as attachment to accelerated availability, not a condemnation of every present good.

2. **Putting the day behind them is active perceptual concealment.** *يذرون* activates **و ذ ر B003** (“leaving or letting something be”); *وراءهم* activates **و ر ي B005** (“covering something and putting it out of sight”) and **B006** (“the far side: behind, ahead, or beyond”). The day has already been made perceptible as spreading, grim, and severe. I infer that “behind” functions psychologically: they position an approaching reality outside their operative field of view. The ayah reads as chosen occlusion, sharpening the earlier **ك ف ر** covering mechanism.

3. **The day is burdensome and potentially weighty in value.** *ثقيلاً* directly activates **ث ق ل B001** (“heaviness against lightness”), while **B005** (“weight as preciousness and high worth”) remains activated by the prior “great dominion” and ensuing reminder. I infer two coexisting readings: they abandon a day whose consequences are burdensome, and they misvalue a day of immense weight. The evaluative branch should not be merged into the punitive branch.

### Retrospective surprises

The replacement warning in 76:28 shows that their attachment to immediacy does not secure their continuity. The reminder and path in 76:29 make the abandoned day’s second, high-value reading more visible.

## 76:28 — نَّحْنُ خَلَقْنَٰهُمْ وَشَدَدْنَآ أَسْرَهُمْ ۖ وَإِذَا شِئْنَا بَدَّلْنَآ أَمْثَٰلَهُمْ تَبْدِيلًا

### Activated readings

1. **The body is a tightened bond.** *خلقناهم* activates **خ ل ق B001** (“measuring and proportioning”) and **B002** (“originating”), *شددنا* activates **ش د د B001** (“fastening a bond”) and **B002** (“intensity of strength”), and *أسرهم* activates **ء س ر B002** (“strength of bodily frame and joints”). The sequence assigns creation, fastening, and frame as successive roles. I infer that bodily strength results from measured connections being tightened. The ayah reads as an anatomy of cohesion, not merely a claim of power.

2. **The same binding field can sustain or imprison.** The fixed *أسرهم* also activates **ء س ر B001** (“binding with a tether and holding captive”), already embodied by *أسيرًا* in 76:8 and echoed by chains in 76:4. I infer that constitution and captivity are two outcomes of binding relations: one holds a body together, the other holds a person down. The ayah therefore makes human solidity contingent rather than autonomous.

3. **Strong individuals remain replaceable counterparts.** *بدلنا ... تبديلًا* activates **ب د ل B001** (“replacement or substitution”) and **B002** (“alteration or transformation”); *أمثالهم* activates **م ث ل B001** (“likeness and counterpart”). The threat follows the assertion of bodily strength. I infer that created cohesion does not imply irreplaceable possession of one’s place. The second half reads as a correction to self-secured permanence.

### Retrospective surprises

The nested willing of 76:30 later places the conditional *إذا شئنا* inside a larger account of volition. Entry into mercy in 76:31 offers a different use of created dependence from the replacement threatened here.

## 76:29 — إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦ تَذْكِرَةٌۭ ۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **The discourse is an instrument that restores what can be forgotten.** *تذكرة* activates **ذ ك ر B009** (“reminder or admonition”) and **B003** (“bringing a thing to mind”). It closes a sequence that began with the human as not *مذكورًا* and included commanded mention in 76:25. I infer that the demonstrative gathers the preceding scenes into a mnemonic device. The ayah reads as reactivation of accountable awareness, not a detached summary label.

2. **The path must be appropriated as a course.** *اتخذ* activates **ء خ ذ B009** (“taking on a path or character”) and **B010** (“taking or making something for oneself”); *سبيلًا* activates **س ب ل B001** (“extended traversed way”). Its direction *إلى ربه* activates **ر ب ب B002** (“repair, nurture, and completion”). I infer that taking the way means embodying a course whose destination also forms the traveler. The path is therefore practiced and appropriated, not merely indicated.

3. **Willing remains real but not yet self-explanatory.** *شاء* uses the “willing” variant of **ش ي ء B001** and also activates the “will toward a thing” variant of **B002**. The conditional opens a genuine human uptake after guidance in 76:3. I infer only that volition supplies the transition from reminder to taking; the next ayah will qualify its scope. This reading remains distinct from any claim of independent willing.

### Retrospective surprises

The immediate qualification in 76:30 corrects an isolated reading of human choice without deleting the grammatical invitation here. The final bifurcation in 76:31 shows the path’s stakes as entry versus prepared pain.

## 76:30 — وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Human willing is nested within divine willing.** Both *تشاءون* and *يشاء* use the “willing” variant of **ش ي ء B001** and the “will toward a thing” variant of **B002**. Negation plus exception supplies the structural containment. I infer a nested rather than erased agency: the human act named in 76:29 remains, but its possibility is not self-originating. The ayah preserves both readings instead of choosing autonomous or null volition.

2. **The containing will is informed and ordering.** *عليمًا* activates **ع ل م B001** (“knowing a thing as it becomes clear”); *حكيمًا* activates **ح ك م B003** (“wisdom and rightly guided knowledge”) and **B004** (“making firm, perfected, and decisive”). These predicates follow the volitional exception as its stated characterization. I infer that knowledge and wise ordering explain how divine willing governs without being presented as arbitrary. The exception therefore reads as epistemically and structurally qualified.

3. **Knowability closes the opening question.** The “knowable thing” variant of **ش ي ء B001**, though the surface forms here are verbs of willing, is reactivated by **ع ل م B001** and by 76:1’s *شيئًا مذكورًا*. I infer a lexical retrospective: the being once outside mention now wills within divine knowledge. This is a secondary root-field reading and does not replace the verbal volition sense.

### Retrospective surprises

The next ayah immediately turns divine willing into two concrete dispositions: admitting into mercy and preparing painful punishment. That outcome prevents the nested-will reading from remaining abstract.

## 76:31 — يُدْخِلُ مَن يَشَآءُ فِى رَحْمَتِهِۦ ۚ وَٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ أَعَدَّ لَهُمْ عَذَابًا أَلِيمًۢا

### Activated readings

1. **Mercy is an interior entered and an enclosure like origin.** *يدخل* activates **د خ ل B001** (“going in / entering”), **B003** (“inner side / hidden interior”), and **B008** (“interleaving and inner layers”); *رحمته* activates **ر ح م B001** (“mercy and tenderness”) and **B003** (“womb”). The preposition *في* makes mercy spatially containing, while 76:2 supplies reproductive origin and 76:0 supplied opening mercy. I infer the womb as a material analogy for protective interiority. Entry into mercy reads as a second enclosure answering the first formation, without reducing mercy to biology.

2. **Wrongdoing is misplacement and withholding.** *الظالمين* activates the accepted **ظ ل م B004** (“doing something at the wrong concrete time or place”) and **B008** (“prevention and withholding”). The surah has contrasted due measure, proper direction, feeding, and those who put the heavy day out of sight. I infer that the wrongdoers’ mechanism is to misplace attention and withhold what should move toward others or toward its due end. The label therefore reads functionally as disordered placement and blocked right, not as an unexplained moral category.

3. **Prepared torment opposes the surah’s prepared provision.** *أعد* activates **ع د د B002** (“preparing what is readied”), *عذابًا* directly activates **ع ذ ب B005** (“torment and punishment”), and *أليمًا* activates **ء ل م B002** (“causing pain and painful”). Cups, springs, vessels, shade, and garments were likewise made ready for use, while 76:4 used another root for prepared chains and fire. I infer that preparation is outcome-neutral until its object and recipient specify it. The closing punishment reads as a deliberately provisioned painful system parallel to, but not merged with, the system of reward.

4. **Torment also carries an exploratory deprivation contrast.** The fixed root **ع ذ ب** contains **B001** (“fresh sweet palatability”), **B002** (“bodily abstention from food and drink”), and **B003** (“withholding and weaning away”) beside direct **B005**. The surah’s sustained abundance of food and drink activates those dormant branches by opposition. I infer that painful punishment may include the reversal or withholding of palatable provision; the packet supplies the contrast, but not that causal detail. This remains an exploratory second reading, not a replacement for torment.

### Retrospective surprises

As the final ayah, it receives no activation from later ayat. Its strongest retrospective surprise is instead the closure of the opening **ر ح م** frame and the transformation of the surah’s food, drink, containment, and preparation mechanisms into a final bifurcation.
