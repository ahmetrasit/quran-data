# Surah 32 Full-Context Ayah Walk

## 32:0 بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A raised designation placed over the whole process.** Lexically, **س م و B005** supplies "name as raised designation," while **ر ح م B001** supplies mercy and beneficence. Structurally, the name precedes every later descent, creation, death, and return. I infer that initial naming governs the sequence as its declared quality. The formula therefore reads not only as an invocation but as a heading under which every subsequent mechanism, including judgment, is placed.
2. **Mercy as generative enclosure (exploratory).** **ر ح م B003** supplies the womb image, and its repetition in الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ is later activated by the clay, extracted fluid, shaping, and enlivening sequence in 32:7-9. The packet supplies the shared root and later embryonic sequence; I supply the inference that mercy is imagined as an enclosing condition that carries weak material into formed life. The two adjectives can thus retain ordinary tenderness (**B001**) while also acquiring a maternal, life-bearing spatial resonance (**B003**).

### Retrospective surprises

The womb branch of **ر ح م** became newly visible only after 32:7-9 described clay, generative fluid, shaping, and spirit, and 32:27 repeated life emerging through water. The final judgment sequence did not cancel the opening mercy; it made the initial placement of the name more structurally consequential.

## 32:2 تَنزِيلُ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ لَا رَيْبَ فِيهِ مِن رَّبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Revelation as a governed descent that produces terrestrial effects.** **ن ز ل B002** gives the sending down of scripture, command, and water; **ر ب ب B002** gives stage-by-stage administration and completion. The vertical route is sharpened by the command descending and ascending in 32:5 and water being driven into barren earth in 32:27. I infer that the same governing pattern connects these scenes. The Book reads as an effective downward delivery within an administered circuit, not merely as text with a heavenly origin.
2. **The Book as joined text and binding order.** **ك ت ب B002** supplies letters joined into writing, while **ك ت ب B003** supplies inscription as decree or judgment. The packet later places another Book with guidance and leaders (32:23-24), then a separating judgment (32:25). I infer that textual joining enables communal and normative ordering. الكتاب consequently remains a written object but also reads as an order whose parts bind and direct a community.
3. **Freedom from the anxious turns of time.** Alongside ordinary doubt in **ر ي ب B001**, **ر ي ب B002** supplies uncertain reversals of time and death. The claim is followed by measured cosmic time (32:5), death and return (32:11), and a fixed day of opening (32:29). I infer that these later specifications answer not only intellectual suspicion but anxiety that events are directionless. "No rayb in it" can therefore mean that the Book is not exposed to temporal accident: it maps death, recurrence, and outcome into an ordered course.

### Retrospective surprises

The strongest later activators were **نُزُلًا** in 32:19, which made the opening "descent" also resemble provision prepared for a guest, and the driven water of 32:27, which gave descent a visible life-producing analogue. The judgment of 32:25 strengthened the decree branch of **ك ت ب**.

## 32:3 أَمْ يَقُولُونَ ٱفْتَرَىٰهُ ۚ بَلْ هُوَ ٱلْحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكَ لِتُنذِرَ قَوْمًۭا مَّآ أَتَىٰهُم مِّن نَّذِيرٍۢ مِّن قَبْلِكَ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَهْتَدُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Fabrication versus tightly established truth.** **ف ر ي B003** supplies fabricated falsehood, but **ف ر ي B001-B002** retain the underlying images of cutting to make and cutting destructively. Against these, **ح ق ق B001** gives established truth and **ح ق ق B010** gives tight-made firmness. The accusation and rebuttal are placed as opposed constructions. I infer that the disputed issue is whether the discourse has been arbitrarily cut and assembled or is firmly fitted to reality. الحق therefore reads as structural integrity, not only correct content.
2. **Warning as a course-correcting intervention.** **ن ذ ر B001** supplies a warning that awakens fear and precaution; **ه د ي B001-B002** supplies guidance toward a way and an intended course. The doubled warning root and purpose clause connect alert to movement. I infer the causal arrow "warning awakens caution, which permits a changed course." The ayah thus reads as an intervention in trajectory, later embodied by hearing, seeing, returning, and turning away.
3. **A people addressed as capable of rising to a task (exploratory).** The surface قوم is directly covered by **ق و م B001**, while **ق و م B003** supplies rising with resolve to undertake a matter. Later, bodies fall in prostration (32:15), sides rise from beds (32:16), and all stand at the Resurrection (**ق و م B013**, 32:25). I infer that naming the audience قوم faintly activates their capacity to stand into response. The group is not only an ethnic or social aggregate; it is a body being called to take up guidance.

### Retrospective surprises

The bodily responses of 32:15-16 made the dormant "rising to undertake" branch of **ق و م** more visible. The contrast between fabricated speech here and denial of the fire in 32:20 also showed that competing speech-acts eventually become experienced outcomes.

## 32:4 ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا فِى سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍۢ ثُمَّ ٱسْتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلْعَرْشِ ۖ مَا لَكُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ مِن وَلِىٍّۢ وَلَا شَفِيعٍ ۚ أَفَلَا تَتَذَكَّرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Measured creation completed as governing support.** **خ ل ق B001-B003** supplies measuring, originating, and complete proportion; **س و ي B003** supplies rising and settling with dominance; **ع ر ش B001** supplies the royal throne and **ع ر ش B006** the sustaining support of authority. Sequence is decisive: measured making over six days precedes settlement on the throne. I infer that successful proportioning grounds governance. The throne scene reads as the administrative completion of creation rather than a detached spatial image.
2. **No parallel chain of protective adjacency.** **د و ن B003** supplies "besides or below," **و ل ي B001, B003-B004** supply adjacency, charge, and allied support, and **ش ف ع B001-B002** supply adding another as advocate or helper. Their coordinated negation follows the statement of comprehensive creation and rule. I infer that an adjacent helper would amount to inserting another link into a complete governing chain. The denial concerns not only cultic rivals but any imagined alternate layer of custody or access.
3. **Created relational space later becomes judicially differentiated.** **ب ي ن B002-B003** supplies both the interval between things and the tie connecting parties. The same root returns in 32:25, where the Lord separates "between them." I infer that what lies "between" heaven and earth is not empty remainder but an ordered field of relations. The ayah consequently includes relations within the created totality, while the later judgment sorts relations that have diverged.

### Retrospective surprises

The delegated angel of 32:11 clarified that delegated custody can exist without becoming the rival ولي or شفيع denied here. The return of **بَيْنَهُمْ** with **يَفْصِلُ** in 32:25 surprisingly turned the created "between" into a space eventually subjected to distinction and judgment.

## 32:5 يُدَبِّرُ ٱلْأَمْرَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ إِلَى ٱلْأَرْضِ ثُمَّ يَعْرُجُ إِلَيْهِ فِى يَوْمٍۢ كَانَ مِقْدَارُهُۥٓ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍۢ مِّمَّا تَعُدُّونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Outcome-aware circulation rather than one-way command.** **د ب ر B006** is management by considering outcomes; **ء م ر B001-B002** supplies affair and directive; **ع ر ج B004** supplies ascent. The explicit down-route and return-route form a circuit. The packet supplies those directions; I infer that ascent returns the effects or account of what descended. يُدَبِّرُ therefore reads as feedback-aware governance, not merely issuing commands from above.
2. **A calibrated scale beyond human enumeration.** **ق د ر B001, B005** supplies measured extent and arrangement by calculation; **ع د د B001** supplies human counting, while **ي و م B002** allows a span of time not confined to a solar day. The ayah places divine measure against "what you count." I infer that the thousand-year comparison translates one scale into another without equating their modes of reckoning. The day reads as a calibrated operational span, not a contradiction in ordinary chronology.
3. **A cosmic cycle made visible in the water cycle.** **س م و B004** and **ء ر ض B001** establish upper cover and lower ground; the directional sequence later recurs when water is driven to barren earth and produces crops in 32:27. I supply the analogy that the later material cycle displays the efficacy of the earlier administrative one. The "affair" can thus be read as circulation that turns remote origin into local growth and returns outcomes upward.

### Retrospective surprises

The death-return sequence in 32:11 and the return sought or induced in 32:12 and 32:21 made the upward leg of this circuit ethically important. The water-to-crop mechanism in 32:27 supplied the clearest later material analogue for descent, terrestrial effect, and renewed movement.

## 32:6 ذَٰلِكَ عَٰلِمُ ٱلْغَيْبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

### Activated readings

1. **Knowledge spanning hidden and present domains.** **ع ل م B001** supplies knowledge as clarity; **غ ي ب B001** supplies what is absent from sight or knowledge; **ش ه د B001-B002** supplies presence, witnessing, and testimony. The paired genitives define two opposed access conditions. I infer that the knower's scope joins what creatures can witness to what they cannot. Later hidden reward (32:17), visible ruins (32:26), and visible growth (32:27) become cases within this single epistemic field.
2. **The inaccessible made knowable through marks.** **ع ز ز B003** supplies rarity and inaccessibility, **ر ح م B001** mercy, and **ع ل م B002** a distinguishing mark that points. The surah later repeats آيات and sensory verbs. I infer that merciful communication places marks within the witnessed domain that point beyond it to otherwise inaccessible knowledge. "Knower of the unseen and witness" consequently also reads as the source of an evidentiary bridge between them.

### Retrospective surprises

The hidden recompense of 32:17 preserved the unseen side, while ruins in 32:26 and irrigated earth in 32:27 supplied visible signs on the witnessed side. Their pairing made **ع ل م B002**, knowledge through a distinguishing mark, much more active than it first appeared.

## 32:7 ٱلَّذِىٓ أَحْسَنَ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ خَلَقَهُۥ ۖ وَبَدَأَ خَلْقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنِ مِن طِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Creation as skilled proportioning.** **ح س ن B002** supplies beneficent or skillful good action, **خ ل ق B001-B003** measuring, originating, and well-proportioned form, and **ط ي ن B001** the earth-water material of clay. The relative clause places goodness in the act of making each thing, not only in later evaluation. I infer that "made well" names skilled transformation of low material into fitting form. The ayah reads as craftsmanship and proportion, sharpened by the explicit shaping in 32:9.
2. **Beginning already oriented toward return.** **ب د ء B001** supplies opening and initiation, while **ب د ء B002** specifically pairs beginning with return or repetition. Death, transfer, and return follow in 32:10-12. I infer that calling this stage a beginning leaves the process structurally incomplete until return. Human creation from clay is therefore the first movement of a reversible sequence, not a one-time origin isolated from resurrection.
3. **Visible humanity from low material.** **ء ن س B001** gives visible human presence over against hidden beings, while **ط ي ن B001** gives mixed earth and water. The prior ayah opposed unseen and witnessed, and 32:13 later pairs hidden jinn with people. I infer that الإنسان is the manifest, socially present creature produced from terrestrial material. The ayah reads as a transition from hidden divine knowledge to embodied, visible human existence.

### Retrospective surprises

The objection to "new creation" in 32:10 and the commanded return in 32:11 activated **ب د ء B002**: beginning and return became a deliberate pair. The barren earth producing visible growth in 32:27 later supplied a terrestrial demonstration of good formation from apparently inert material.

## 32:8 ثُمَّ جَعَلَ نَسْلَهُۥ مِن سُلَٰلَةٍۢ مِّن مَّآءٍۢ مَّهِينٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Lineage as gentle extraction from weakness.** **ن س ل B001** supplies successive progeny, **س ل ل B001, B003** gentle drawing-out and extracted lineage or essence, **م و ه B004** male fluid placed in the womb, and **م ه ن B006** scant weak generative fluid. The nested مِن phrases repeatedly trace an output back through its source. I infer that continuity is achieved through controlled extraction rather than material strength. Human succession thus reads as a precisely drawn lineage carried by what appears weak.
2. **A hidden chain that resists the image of total dispersal.** **س ل ل B005** supplies chainlike connection, while **ن س ل B001** supplies successive procreation. Later speakers imagine themselves disappearing into earth (32:10). I infer that the managed succession here is a model of identity persisting through material transitions. The ayah can therefore activate a linked-continuity reading: what seems like a vanishing drop is already part of a chain capable of renewed embodiment.
3. **Generative water inside a merciful enclosure (exploratory).** **م و ه B004** explicitly supplies placement of male fluid in the womb, and the repeated **ر ح م B003** from 32:0 supplies the womb as vessel. The packet does not state the causal arrow "mercy carries the fluid"; I infer it from the opening formula and the birth sequence. The weak water is read not as self-sufficient matter but as material preserved within an enclosing process.

### Retrospective surprises

The claim of disappearance in earth at 32:10 made the chain branch of **س ل ل** unexpectedly important: continuity can be hidden without being lost. The water driven into cut-off land in 32:27 later enlarged the generative-water image from the body to the landscape.

## 32:9 ثُمَّ سَوَّىٰهُ وَنَفَخَ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِهِۦ ۖ وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمْعَ وَٱلْأَبْصَٰرَ وَٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةَ ۚ قَلِيلًۭا مَّا تَشْكُرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Integrated completion: form, life, and receptive faculties.** **س و ي B002** supplies sound completion and straight formation, **ن ف خ B001** blowing breath into something, and **ر و ح B001** the life-spirit. The sequence then assigns hearing, sights, and hearts. I infer that shaping becomes complete only when formed matter can receive and answer its environment. سَوَّىٰهُ therefore includes functional and epistemic integration, not shape alone.
2. **Breath as a growth-enabling influx.** Besides ordinary blowing in **ن ف خ B001**, **ن ف خ B003** supplies spring swelling and the earth putting forth growth. The later water-driven emergence of crops in 32:27 supplies a material parallel. I infer that the infused spirit relates to the body as a life-enabling influx relates to land. The breath image consequently acquires a vegetative, emergence-producing resonance without replacing its life-spirit sense.
3. **Faculties as obligations to produce response.** **س م ع B003** supplies understanding and compliance, **ب ص ر B002** inner sight, **ف ء د B002** the heart as inner kindling, and **ش ك ر B002** visible thriving on little provision. The list is followed immediately by scarce gratitude. I infer that these faculties are inputs expected to kindle recognition and visible response. The rebuke is therefore not about insufficient verbal thanks alone but about perceptive capacities failing to bear fruit.

### Retrospective surprises

The criminals' "we saw and heard" in 32:12 showed the same faculties finally producing insight and compliance, but after their useful interval. The visible crop response in 32:27 made **ن ف خ B003** and **ش ك ر B002** surprisingly concrete: life received should show an effect.

## 32:10 وَقَالُوٓا۟ أَءِذَا ضَلَلْنَا فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ أَءِنَّا لَفِى خَلْقٍۢ جَدِيدٍۭ ۚ بَلْ هُم بِلِقَآءِ رَبِّهِمْ كَٰفِرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Disappearance into earth, not merely straying.** The construction ضَلَلْنَا فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ activates **ض ل ل B002**, being hidden, absorbed, or buried until no longer visible, more directly than directional error in **B001**. The question joins that disappearance to renewed creation. I infer that their material premise is "what cannot be located cannot be restored." The objection therefore concerns recoverability of dispersed embodiment, not simply ignorance about guidance.
2. **Newness after severance.** **ج د د B004** supplies newness after cutting, while **خ ل ق B001-B003** supplies measured re-formation. The preceding ayat described lineage drawn from weak fluid; this ayah imagines that chain cut by absorption into earth. I infer that "new creation" names renewed form after apparent severance. Their own phrase inadvertently preserves the lexical mechanism needed to answer their objection.
3. **Encounter covered over.** **ل ق ي B004** supplies two parties coming face to face, while **ك ف ر B001, B003** supplies covering and rejection of truth. The corrective بَلْ redirects from material speculation to denial of meeting the Lord. I infer that the resurrection objection functions as a cover over aversion to encounter. The ayah reads less as failed natural philosophy and more as refusal of a face-to-face reckoning.

### Retrospective surprises

The complete taking and guarded transfer of the self in 32:11 directly corrected the premise that earthly disappearance means loss. The face-to-face abasement of 32:12 then made the denied **لِقَاءِ** bodily visible.

## 32:11 ۞ قُلْ يَتَوَفَّىٰكُم مَّلَكُ ٱلْمَوْتِ ٱلَّذِى وُكِّلَ بِكُمْ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُمْ تُرْجَعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Death as complete receipt and guarded transfer.** **و ف ي B002** supplies receiving the soul in full as death, **و ف ي B001** completeness without shortfall, **و ك ل B001, B006** entrusted agency and a guarding undertaker, and **ر ج ع B001** return. The sequence answers "we disappear" with a chain of custody. I infer that entrusted taking preserves rather than loses the person. Death therefore reads as complete transfer through custody on the way to return, not uncontrolled dissolution.
2. **Delegation without rival sovereignty.** Within the packet's root assignment, **م ل ك B003** supplies dominion and governance, while **و ك ل B001** marks the figure as commissioned. The agent is grammatically bounded by "who was entrusted with you," and the destination is your Lord. I infer that his authority is operational rather than independent. This preserves the earlier denial of any rival ولي or شفيع: delegation executes the single rule instead of adding another source of it.

### Retrospective surprises

The criminals' demand "return us" in 32:12 showed that the promised return here is not equivalent to being granted another working interval. The lesser punishment designed to make people return in 32:21 later distinguished ethical return from the irreversible transfer described here.

## 32:12 وَلَوْ تَرَىٰٓ إِذِ ٱلْمُجْرِمُونَ نَاكِسُوا۟ رُءُوسِهِمْ عِندَ رَبِّهِمْ رَبَّنَآ أَبْصَرْنَا وَسَمِعْنَا فَٱرْجِعْنَا نَعْمَلْ صَٰلِحًا إِنَّا مُوقِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **A reversal from raised self-direction to downcast perception.** **ن ك س B001, B006** supplies inversion and lowering the head in shame; **ر ء س B001-B002** supplies the head and leadership; **ب ص ر B002** supplies inner sight and **س م ع B003** understanding with compliance. Head-lowering precedes the confession that sight and hearing now work. I infer that their former directing center must be inverted before their faculties acknowledge truth. The scene reads as an epistemic reversal embodied in posture.
2. **Return requested as restoration to fit action.** **ر ج ع B001** supplies return to a prior place or state, **ع م ل B001** intentional action, and **ص ل ح B003** fitness or suitability. The object of the request is not stated as a place but as a renewed ability to work fittingly. I infer that "return us" means restore us to the actionable state in which perception can produce conduct. صالحًا reads as action fitted to the now-seen reality.
3. **Certainty as the late removal of the Book's denied doubt.** **ي ق ن B001** supplies settled knowledge after doubt is removed; **ب ص ر B001-B002** and **س م ع B001-B003** supply its sensory and inward basis. This answers the "no doubt" claim of 32:2 from the opposite temporal end. I infer that certainty is not absent from the system but can arrive after agency has closed. The confession reads as valid knowledge rendered ineffective by lateness.

### Retrospective surprises

The non-equivalence of believer and boundary-breaker in 32:18, followed by their distinct abodes in 32:19-20, showed why a correct confession here does not erase the earlier history of action. The denial of late faith's benefit in 32:29 supplied the final correction to their request.

## 32:13 وَلَوْ شِئْنَا لَءَاتَيْنَا كُلَّ نَفْسٍ هُدَىٰهَا وَلَٰكِنْ حَقَّ ٱلْقَوْلُ مِنِّى لَأَمْلَأَنَّ جَهَنَّمَ مِنَ ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ أَجْمَعِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Guidance as a grant that could reach every self.** **ء ت ي B002** supplies granting or bringing, **ن ف س B011-B012** the living self and whole identity, and **ه د ي B001** guidance toward the way. The unreal condition separates capacity to grant from the actual order announced after وَلَٰكِنْ. I infer that absence of universal guidance is not inability or shortage. The ayah preserves guidance as an effective gift while locating the difference in the enacted order.
2. **A word that becomes due and fills its container.** **ح ق ق B002** supplies a due obligation and **ح ق ق B005** making truth stand; **ق و ل B001** supplies the uttered word; **م ل ء B001** supplies fullness matching a container; **ج م ع B001** supplies scattered parts gathered into a whole. The clauses move from word to filling to total gathering. I infer the causal arrow "the established word determines the container's completed population." The statement reads as execution of a settled measure rather than uncontrolled excess.
3. **Visible and hidden populations gathered together.** **ج ن ن B005** supplies hidden spirit beings and **ج ن ن B001** their concealment image; the packet's **ن و س B001** for الناس supplies wavering human movement. The closing أَجْمَعِينَ removes separation between these otherwise unlike populations. I infer that judgment gathers both the hidden and the manifest/wavering into one accountable total. The phrase thus extends the unseen-witness pairing of 32:6 into a shared outcome.

### Retrospective surprises

The two distinct destinations in 32:19-20 clarified that "all together" here concerns the fullness of the named population, not erasure of distinctions among all selves. The separating judgment of 32:25 made the difference between gathering and indiscriminate merging explicit.

## 32:14 فَذُوقُوا۟ بِمَا نَسِيتُمْ لِقَآءَ يَوْمِكُمْ هَٰذَآ إِنَّا نَسِينَٰكُمْ ۖ وَذُوقُوا۟ عَذَابَ ٱلْخُلْدِ بِمَا كُنتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Reciprocal abandonment, not symmetrical amnesia.** **ن س ي B002** supplies leaving or abandoning a covenant or command and divine leaving as recompense; **ذ و ق B002** supplies undergoing an experience. The repeated نسي is bound by بِمَا into a stated causal relation. The packet gives the acts and consequence; I infer that the second "forgetting" answers the first as measured abandonment. The ayah reads as enacted reciprocity, not a claim of failed divine recall.
2. **A missed encounter becomes an enduring condition.** **ل ق ي B004** supplies face-to-face encounter, **ي و م B003** an event-day, **ع ذ ب B005** punishment, and **خ ل د B001** enduring permanence. The first taste concerns neglect of a meeting; the second names the lasting state produced by habitual action. I infer that refusing temporal encounter hardens into continuous consequence. The "day" is therefore not just a date forgotten but an appointment whose neglected relation becomes durable.
3. **Punishment as deprivation of palatable provision (exploratory).** Beside punishment in **ع ذ ب B005**, **ع ذ ب B001-B003** supplies sweet water, abstention, and withholding; ذوق makes this taste-field unusually audible. Later, believers receive prepared guest-provision (32:19), whereas fire-dwellers repeatedly taste punishment (32:20). I infer a negative-provision mechanism: what they taste is the withholding of the good sustenance available in the alternative abode. This reading coexists with direct torment rather than replacing it.

### Retrospective surprises

The prepared **نُزُلًا** of 32:19 and repeated tasting in 32:20 sharpened the contrast between hospitality and punitive experience. The water and food chain in 32:27 made the dormant sweet-water and abstention branches of **ع ذ ب** materially vivid.

## 32:15 إِنَّمَا يُؤْمِنُ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِّرُوا۟ بِهَا خَرُّوا۟ سُجَّدًۭا وَسَبَّحُوا۟ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّهِمْ وَهُمْ لَا يَسْتَكْبِرُونَ ۩

### Activated readings

1. **A sign-to-body response chain.** **ء ي ي B003** supplies visible signs, **ذ ك ر B009** admonitory reminder, **خ ر ر B001** falling or collapsing, and **س ج د B001** humbled submission. The إذا construction makes sign-reminder the trigger for bodily descent. I infer that faith here is a successful conversion of perception into posture. Belief reads as an embodied response mechanism, directly contrasting with faculties that produce little gratitude in 32:9.
2. **Praise as tested acknowledgment without self-enlargement.** **س ب ح B002** supplies declaring free or pure, **ح م د B002** finding something praiseworthy after testing, **ر ب ب B002** nurturing administration, and **ك ب ر B006** self-magnification and refusal to submit. I infer that the signs function as evidence by which the Lord's governance is found praiseworthy, while the self refuses to compete in greatness. Their praise is therefore an evidentiary verdict joined to de-centering of the self, not ornamental speech.
3. **Security expressed as immediate compliance.** **ء م ن B001-B002** supplies trust, security, and confirming assent; **س م ع B003** from the earlier faculty list supplies the wider contextual role of compliant hearing. The restrictive إِنَّمَا and immediate إذا response make assent behaviorally diagnostic. I infer that trust becomes visible when reminder meets no defensive delay. "Believe in Our signs" consequently includes allowing the sign to move the body.

### Retrospective surprises

The sides leaving beds in 32:16 extended the single fall of prostration into a repeated bodily discipline. Later reminders followed by turning away in 32:22 supplied the exact failed inverse of this response chain.

## 32:16 تَتَجَافَىٰ جُنُوبُهُمْ عَنِ ٱلْمَضَاجِعِ يَدْعُونَ رَبَّهُمْ خَوْفًۭا وَطَمَعًۭا وَمِمَّا رَزَقْنَٰهُمْ يُنفِقُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Physical separation enabling relational nearness.** **ج ف و B001** supplies standing away from contact, **ج ن ب B001** the bodily flank, **ض ج ع B001** the lying-place, and **د ع و B001** calling in order to draw near. The body moves away from one contact while the voice seeks another proximity. I infer that detachment from the bed creates the interval in which calling can operate. Their wakefulness reads as spatial reorientation, not mere sleep deprivation.
2. **Action held between fear and desire.** **خ و ف B001** supplies anticipation of harm, **ط م ع B001** heartfelt hope, **ر ز ق B001** allotted provision, and **ن ف ق B002** wealth going out as expenditure. The paired accusatives bracket prayer, and expenditure follows received provision. I infer that fear prevents complacency while hope prevents paralysis; together they keep granted resources moving outward. Devotion reads as a regulated flow rather than a single affect.
3. **Provision passing through an outlet (exploratory).** **ن ف ق B003** supplies a tunnel with an exit, a concrete image dormant beneath يُنفِقُونَ; the surah repeatedly depicts descent, ascent, channels, and water movement. I infer that the recipients act as passages rather than terminal stores for provision. Spending thus reads as allowing what entered from the provider to find a social outlet.

### Retrospective surprises

The hidden recompense in 32:17 made outward expenditure and unseen return a paired circulation. The water driven through barren ground to feed livestock and selves in 32:27 later supplied a material model for provision that must pass onward to become fruitful.

## 32:17 فَلَا تَعْلَمُ نَفْسٌۭ مَّآ أُخْفِىَ لَهُم مِّن قُرَّةِ أَعْيُنٍۢ جَزَآءًۢ بِمَا كَانُوا۟ يَعْمَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Concealed recompense precisely matched to work.** **خ ف ي B001-B002** supplies hiddenness and what lies under cover, **ج ز ي B001** recompense matching an act, and **ع م ل B001** intentional work. The reward is hidden, but the causal بِمَا and matching-recompense branch make it non-arbitrary. I infer that concealment concerns present access, not lack of specification. The ayah reads as an unseen but measured return on action.
2. **The eye moves from restless perception to settled joy.** **ق ر ر B002-B003** supplies the eye's cooled comfort and settled fixation; **ع ي ن B001** supplies the seeing eye. Earlier eyes either fail to yield gratitude (32:9) or awaken too late (32:12). I infer that faithful perception reaches its proper end when the eye no longer searches anxiously but becomes settled by what it meets. "Comfort of eyes" is thus the completion of an epistemic journey as well as a pleasure.
3. **Unknown to the inner self as well as the senses.** **ن ف س B013** supplies inward thought and hidden intent, while **ع ل م B001** supplies knowledge as clarity. The syntax denies knowledge to any self before naming what is hidden for them. I infer that the reward exceeds both public observation and private projection. Its hiddenness protects it from being reduced to the seeker's current imaginative measure.

### Retrospective surprises

The watered earth and feeding scene of 32:27 made the coolness in **ق ر ر B001-B002** more material without exhausting it: water can settle the eye because it visibly converts barrenness into provision. The day of opening in 32:29 clarified when present concealment ends.

## 32:18 أَفَمَن كَانَ مُؤْمِنًۭا كَمَن كَانَ فَاسِقًۭا ۚ لَّا يَسْتَوُۥنَ

### Activated readings

1. **Security versus breakout from a containing order.** **ء م ن B001-B002** supplies secure trust and confirming assent. The packet's sole accepted **ف س ق B002** supplies the concrete image of a date emerging from its peel. **س و ي B001** then denies equivalence. I infer that the peel image assigns the فاسق the role of one who has broken out from a containing norm, while the believer remains in trusting relation. The contrast reads spatially as held alignment versus boundary-exit, without reducing either moral category to the image.
2. **Different states cannot be flattened into equal rank.** **ك و ن B001-B002** supplies being in a state and position or rank; its repetition creates formally parallel clauses, while **س و ي B001** rejects their equality. I infer that grammatical symmetry is used to expose substantive asymmetry. The ayah therefore warns that identical human form or shared present location does not make the two enacted states equivalent.

### Retrospective surprises

The next two ayat assign distinct shelters, and 32:20 repeatedly blocks attempted خروج from fire. That later irony made the peel-emergence branch of **ف س ق** decisive: the one defined by wrongful exit enters an abode from which exit no longer succeeds.

## 32:19 أَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فَلَهُمْ جَنَّٰتُ ٱلْمَأْوَىٰ نُزُلًۢا بِمَا كَانُوا۟ يَعْمَلُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Layered hospitality: covering, refuge, and guest-provision.** **ج ن ن B003** supplies tree-covered gardens, **ء و ي B001** a sheltering gathering point, and **ن ز ل B005** prepared provision for a guest or lodger. These nouns accumulate rather than merely repeat "paradise." I infer a nested hospitality mechanism: covering receives them, refuge gathers them, and provision sustains them. Their destination reads as actively prepared reception, not only scenic reward.
2. **The opening descent becomes hospitality at the end.** The same **ن ز ل** root that marked the Book's descent in 32:2 now appears as **B005**, guest-provision, while **ع م ل B001** is repeated around the causal بِمَا. I infer that the earlier descending Book was itself advance provision enabling the works for which final provision is prepared. Revelation and reward become two stages of one host-guest relation.
3. **Shelter as drawing-together and repair.** The scope of **ء و ي B001** includes a wound drawing together toward healing, while **ص ل ح B001** supplies soundness against corruption. The workers of sound deeds are assigned مأوى. I infer that their refuge gathers what worldly exertion left exposed and brings it to settled soundness. The abode reads as reparative shelter as well as residence.

### Retrospective surprises

The punitive "home" in 32:20 made مأوى morally neutral as a gathering destination until its contents are named; gardens and prepared hospitality distinguish this مأوى. The agricultural sign in 32:27 later gave the covered garden a visible causal prehistory in water, earth, and growth.

## 32:20 وَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ فَسَقُوا۟ فَمَأْوَىٰهُمُ ٱلنَّارُ ۖ كُلَّمَآ أَرَادُوٓا۟ أَن يَخْرُجُوا۟ مِنْهَآ أُعِيدُوا۟ فِيهَا وَقِيلَ لَهُمْ ذُوقُوا۟ عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ ٱلَّذِى كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Wrongful emergence answered by failed emergence.** **ف س ق B002** supplies a date emerging from its peel, **خ ر ج B001** passing out from within, and **ع و د B001** being made to return and repeat. The moral label is followed by a literal, repeatedly frustrated attempt to exit. I infer a reversal in which unbounded emergence during agency produces enforced containment afterward. The repeated كُلَّمَا makes the punishment a failed-exit mechanism, not a single barred departure.
2. **An anti-refuge.** **ء و ي B001** supplies a sheltering or gathering destination, but its predicate is **ن و ر B002**, burning fire. The same shelter noun named the garden destination in 32:19. I infer that مأوى names the inescapable place toward which a life has gathered, while the predicate determines whether gathering heals or confines. Fire therefore reads as a refuge-form inverted against its protective purpose.
3. **Fire exposes what denial covered.** **ن و ر B001-B002** preserves both illumination and burning fire; **ك ذ ب B002** supplies declaring something false, and **ذ و ق B002** experiencing what was denied. The packet gives denial followed by direct experience; I infer that burning illumination removes the cover maintained by false declaration. The command "taste" converts a rejected report into unavoidable verification.

### Retrospective surprises

The lesser punishment of 32:21 revealed that not every taste of punishment has this closed-return structure; some painful experience still aims at ethical return. The day when late faith no longer benefits in 32:29 confirmed why attempted exit here cannot reopen agency.

## 32:21 وَلَنُذِيقَنَّهُم مِّنَ ٱلْعَذَابِ ٱلْأَدْنَىٰ دُونَ ٱلْعَذَابِ ٱلْأَكْبَرِ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَرْجِعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Near punishment before the greater threshold.** **د ن و B001-B002** supplies nearness and the nearer or first side; **د و ن B001** supplies what is near yet short of a limit; **ك ب ر B001-B002** supplies the greater scale and chief burden. The two punishments are ordered by distance and magnitude, and the purpose clause remains open. I infer that the nearer experience occurs on the still-reversible side of a threshold. الأدنى therefore reads temporally and functionally as an advance intervention, not only a smaller quantity of pain.
2. **Punishment as diagnostic testing.** **ذ و ق B002** supplies experiencing and testing; **ع ذ ب B005** supplies punishment; **ر ج ع B003** supplies turning back from an act or prior course. The explicit لَعَلَّهُمْ supplies the desired direction. I infer that this taste reveals the trajectory early enough to prompt reversal. The painful experience has a corrective function distinct from the closed recurrence of 32:20.

### Retrospective surprises

The reminder followed by turning away in 32:22 showed how the corrective opening can be refused. The final opening in 32:29, when faith no longer benefits and no respite is granted, later fixed the far boundary implied by **دُونَ** here.

## 32:22 وَمَنْ أَظْلَمُ مِمَّن ذُكِّرَ بِـَٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِۦ ثُمَّ أَعْرَضَ عَنْهَآ ۚ إِنَّا مِنَ ٱلْمُجْرِمِينَ مُنتَقِمُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Wrong as directional withholding after disclosure.** **ذ ك ر B009** supplies admonitory reminder, **ء ي ي B003** visible signs, **ع ر ض B005** turning one's side away, and **ظ ل م B008** prevention or withholding. The sequence is not ignorance followed by accident: reminder is received, then the body is directionally averted. I infer that the wrong consists in withholding attention and response from a disclosed claim. أَظْلَمُ therefore reads as maximal misdirection after access, not merely a generic label for sin.
2. **Earned offense meets punitive requital.** **ج ر م B003-B004** supplies earning/causing to incur and guilt or offense; **ن ق م B002** supplies punitive requital for what was done. The second clause names a class and a corresponding action. I infer that the offender's action incurs the requital rather than merely identifying a disliked group. Vengeance reads here as answer to acquired liability.
3. **The inverse of believing response.** In 32:15, sign and reminder caused falling submission; here the same roots are followed by **ع ر ض B005**, turning away. I infer that the shared input isolates response as the differentiating variable. The ayah reads as a controlled contrast: signs do not mechanically compel one posture, but they make posture accountable.

### Retrospective surprises

The guidance and certainty of 32:23-24 showed the constructive alternative to this turn: a Book can become a transmitted path and form leaders. The command to "turn away from them" in 32:30 surprisingly mirrors their own verb, converting their chosen aversion into a temporary relational consequence.

## 32:23 وَلَقَدْ ءَاتَيْنَا مُوسَى ٱلْكِتَٰبَ فَلَا تَكُن فِى مِرْيَةٍۢ مِّن لِّقَآئِهِۦ ۖ وَجَعَلْنَٰهُ هُدًۭى لِّبَنِىٓ إِسْرَٰٓءِيلَ

### Activated readings

1. **The Book as transmitted construction of a lineage.** **ء ت ي B002** supplies granting, **ك ت ب B002** written composition, **ج ع ل B002** making something into a state, **ه د ي B001** guidance, and **ب ن ي B007** filiation and derived belonging. The gifted Book is made guidance "for the sons" of a named lineage. I infer that transmitted writing builds continuity by giving descendants a common course. The Book reads as social construction across generations, not only an object granted to one recipient.
2. **No doubt about an encounter.** **م ر ي B004** supplies doubt and hesitation; **ل ق ي B004** supplies face-to-face meeting. The pronoun in لِقَائِهِ is left open by the packet, so two readings remain active: encounter with the earlier messenger/book confirms continuity with the current revelation; encounter with the Lord resumes the denied meeting of 32:10. In both, I infer that the earlier gift makes the relevant meeting structurally credible. I do not collapse the two referents.
3. **Receiving the Book as receiving transmitted speech.** **ل ق ي B011** supplies receiving or imparting speech and revelation, while **ك ت ب B002-B003** supplies written text and binding decree. If لِقَائِهِ points toward the Book or its delivery, the phrase can activate reception rather than only visual meeting. I infer that freedom from miryah concerns faithful transmission into an operative guide. This reading coexists with the face-to-face readings because the packet does not settle the pronoun.

### Retrospective surprises

The leaders who guide by the command in 32:24 made the Book's social effect explicit: granted text becomes embodied direction through patient, certain agents. The separating judgment in 32:25 kept the Lord-encounter reading active alongside the transmission reading.

## 32:24 وَجَعَلْنَا مِنْهُمْ أَئِمَّةًۭ يَهْدُونَ بِأَمْرِنَا لَمَّا صَبَرُوا۟ ۖ وَكَانُوا۟ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا يُوقِنُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Leadership as transmitted directional authority.** **ء م م B009** supplies a leader, guide, exemplar, road, or line followed; **ه د ي B001-B003** supplies guidance and a leading front; **ء م ر B002-B003** supplies directive and delegated authority. The leaders guide "by Our command," which bounds their role. I infer that they stand at the front as relays of direction, not independent sources. The ayah reads as a human transmission layer within the single governance established in 32:4-5.
2. **Leaders as gathering points and references.** Besides the direct imam branch, **ء م م B002** supplies a mother-source, origin, gathering point, and reference. The plural leaders arise "from among them" after the Book has been made guidance for the group. I infer that exemplary persons concentrate a dispersed community around an intelligible path. أئمة can therefore carry both forward-leading and center-forming spatial roles.
3. **Patience lets signs settle into certainty.** **ص ب ر B001** supplies self-restraint and steadiness, **ء ي ي B003** visible signs, and **ي ق ن B001** settled knowledge with doubt removed. The causal لَمَّا ties leadership to patience, while the final clause gives a sustained epistemic condition. I infer that restraint holds a person in contact with signs long enough for knowledge to settle and become transmissible. Leadership reads as temporally formed, not instant status.

### Retrospective surprises

The next ayah's divine separation prevented these leaders from being read as final judges; they guide within history, while the Lord resolves divergence. Walking among earlier dwellings in 32:26 then showed how later generations can still be led by predecessors who have become evidentiary reference points.

## 32:25 إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ يَفْصِلُ بَيْنَهُمْ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ فِيمَا كَانُوا۟ فِيهِ يَخْتَلِفُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Judgment separates divergent courses within a shared field.** **ف ص ل B002** supplies judgment separating truth from falsehood, **ب ي ن B001-B003** supplies separation, interval, and the tie between parties, and **خ ل ف B004** supplies each taking a way different from the other. The roots distinguish present divergence from future decisive separation. I infer that judgment articulates differences now entangled within one relational field. It does not manufacture divergence; it makes its consequences distinct.
2. **All stand for the separating decision.** **ق و م B013** supplies resurrection and standing before the Lord, **ي و م B003** an event-day, and **ر ب ب B001** authority. The emphatic هُوَ reserves the separating act to the Lord. I infer that common standing does not imply equal standing: gathering creates the forum in which differences can be ruled upon. القيامة reads as both bodily rising and judicial assembly.
3. **Separation as explanatory detailing.** **ف ص ل B013** supplies clarifying a matter by setting out its parts and meanings. Since the object is what they differed about, I infer that the decision can reveal how the disputed parts fit before assigning outcome. يَفْصِلُ therefore preserves both verdict (**B002**) and disclosure-by-articulation (**B013**).

### Retrospective surprises

The visible historical signs in 32:26 and ecological signs in 32:27 showed that some clarification is available before the final فصل. The "opening" asked about in 32:28-29 then supplied another lexical frame for the same terminal disclosure and judgment.

## 32:26 أَوَلَمْ يَهْدِ لَهُمْ كَمْ أَهْلَكْنَا مِن قَبْلِهِم مِّنَ ٱلْقُرُونِ يَمْشُونَ فِى مَسَٰكِنِهِمْ ۚ إِنَّ فِى ذَٰلِكَ لَءَايَٰتٍ ۖ أَفَلَا يَسْمَعُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Earlier generations lead later walkers.** **ه د ي B001** supplies guidance and **ه د ي B003** the leading front part; **ق ر ن B005** supplies people joined in one age or generation; **م ش ي B001** supplies walking; **س ك ن B002** dwellings. The dead generations are temporally in front, while current people physically traverse their residences. I infer that prior occupants become guides through the traces left for later walkers. History reads as a path whose vanished inhabitants still lead by evidence.
2. **Destroyed dwellings as arrested life, not erased signs.** **ه ل ك B001** supplies ruin and extinction, while **ه ل ك B005** supplies barren land without rain; **س ك ن B001** supplies cessation of motion. Yet the dwellings remain walkable and are explicitly called signs (**ء ي ي B003**). I infer that destruction stills a social world while preserving enough form to testify. The ayah reads ruin as legible cessation rather than total absence.
3. **Hearing as compliance with visible history.** **س م ع B003** supplies understanding, acceptance, and action, while the evidence is described as visible signs and walked space. I infer a cross-sensory demand: the ruins "speak" by indication, and proper hearing means accepting their directive force. أَفَلَا يَسْمَعُونَ asks for obedient uptake, not mere acoustic perception.

### Retrospective surprises

The immediately following barren earth in 32:27 activated **ه ل ك B005** and corrected a possible inference that stillness is intrinsically final: barren land can be revived, while ruined societies remain warnings. The contrast separates divine capacity to restore life from the moral lesson of historical destruction.

## 32:27 أَوَلَمْ يَرَوْا۟ أَنَّا نَسُوقُ ٱلْمَآءَ إِلَى ٱلْأَرْضِ ٱلْجُرُزِ فَنُخْرِجُ بِهِۦ زَرْعًۭا تَأْكُلُ مِنْهُ أَنْعَٰمُهُمْ وَأَنفُسُهُمْ ۖ أَفَلَا يُبْصِرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **A visible restoration chain.** **س و ق B001** supplies driving forward, **م و ه B001-B003** water and its delivery, **ج ر ز B001-B002** land cut off and stripped of growth, **خ ر ج B002** causing emergence, and **ز ر ع B001** crop growth. The فَ sequence gives the causal order; I infer only that the sequence is offered as a model of restoration. The scene reads as an observable answer to the claim that material lost in earth cannot emerge again.
2. **Provision joins livestock and living selves.** **ن ع م B005** supplies grazing livestock and **ن ف س B008, B011** supplies life-sustaining water and the living self; **ء ك ل B001-B003** supplies ingestion, yield, and consumable provision. One crop output feeds two classes of embodied life. I infer that driven water becomes provision by passing through earth and plant into consumers. The ayah reads as a multi-stage economy of life rather than an isolated miracle of vegetation.
3. **Seeing expected to become insight.** **ر ء ي B001** supplies initial seeing by eye or insight, while **ب ص ر B001-B002** supplies sight and inward penetration. The ayah begins with "have they not seen" and ends with "will they not perceive." I infer a demanded movement from observing the chain to grasping its implication. The repeated visual framing makes resurrection and governance an abductive conclusion from a material process.
4. **Landscape generation echoes bodily generation (exploratory).** **م و ه B004** supplies male fluid placed in the womb, **ز ر ع B004** a man's fluid as seed, and **ء ر ض B002** fertile ground. These variant branch images are activated by the earlier weak-fluid lineage in 32:8. I infer an analogy in which earth receives water and brings out growth as the body receives generative fluid and brings out progeny. The agricultural scene becomes a large-scale replay of embodied creation without collapsing the two processes.

### Retrospective surprises

The next question about **الفتح** activated **ف ت ح B005**, water released from an opening, so the driven water here also became a preview of a blocked outcome being opened. The final denial of respite in 32:29 marked a limit: visible renewal demonstrates power to restore, but it does not imply that every temporal opportunity remains open forever.

## 32:28 وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلْفَتْحُ إِن كُنتُمْ صَٰدِقِينَ

### Activated readings

1. **Demand for the dispute to be opened by judgment.** **ف ت ح B003** supplies settling a closed dispute by judgment; **ق و ل B009** supplies mutual verbal negotiation; **ص د ق B001** supplies correspondence between speech and reality. Their question challenges the speakers to produce the event that will close the argument. I infer that الفتح is the adjudicative opening of a dispute presently kept in words. The demand reads as a request for truth to become public decision.
2. **Demand for victory as fulfillment.** **ف ت ح B004** supplies victory or conquest, while **ص د ق B004** supplies realization and fulfillment of a promise or action. I infer that the questioners frame the claimed outcome as a victory that must arrive to verify the claimants. This remains distinct from the judicial reading: one emphasizes one side prevailing, the other a dispute being decided.
3. **A blocked consequence imagined as released flow (exploratory).** **ف ت ح B005** supplies water released from an opening into channels and irrigation. Coming immediately after water is driven to barren earth, this branch gives الفتح a material activation. I infer that the awaited event is pictured as a pent-up consequence suddenly given an outlet. The question then asks when the unseen process will break into visible history, not merely when a date will arrive.

### Retrospective surprises

The answer in 32:29 preserved judgment and victory together but removed the fruitful side of opening: unlike released water, this opening does not generate a new usable interval for those who rejected. The reciprocal waiting in 32:30 showed that the consequence remains temporally withheld before that release.

## 32:29 قُلْ يَوْمَ ٱلْفَتْحِ لَا يَنفَعُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوٓا۟ إِيمَٰنُهُمْ وَلَا هُمْ يُنظَرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Opening as closure of adjudication.** **ف ت ح B003** supplies decisive judgment, **ي و م B003** the major event-day, **ن ف ع B001** benefit, and **ن ظ ر B002** delay or respite. The day opens the disputed truth precisely by closing further profitable response-time. I infer that public decision changes the status of faith from actionable trust to compelled recognition. The opening is therefore epistemic disclosure and temporal closure at once.
2. **Victory that reverses concealment too late.** **ف ت ح B004** supplies victory, **ك ف ر B001, B003** covering and rejecting truth, and **ء م ن B001-B002** secure trusting assent. The opening removes the cover, but the verse denies benefit to assent produced after that removal. I infer that faith's value depends on trust before forced visibility. The outcome validates the truth without retroactively converting late acknowledgment into earlier faithful action.
3. **The end of granted waiting.** **ن ظ ر B002** specifically supplies both watching for a time and granting respite. The next ayah repeats the root in reciprocal forms. I infer that present waiting is a bounded concession whose endpoint is this day. "They will not be given respite" reads as termination of the very temporal relation enacted in 32:30.

### Retrospective surprises

The command to wait in 32:30 made the denial of respite here sharper: waiting is available now, but it is not an indefinitely renewable resource. The shared **ن ظ ر** root turns the final command into the last open interval before the day described here.

## 32:30 فَأَعْرِضْ عَنْهُمْ وَٱنتَظِرْ إِنَّهُم مُّنتَظِرُونَ

### Activated readings

1. **Mirrored withdrawal and bounded suspension.** **ع ر ض B005** supplies turning one's side away; **ن ظ ر B002** supplies waiting for the time and granting delay. Their earlier turning from signs in 32:22 is now answered by a command using the same side-turning root, while both sides are described as waiting. I infer that withdrawal suspends fruitless engagement without resolving the dispute by force. The command reads as a temporary relational consequence bounded by the opening day of 32:29.
2. **Waiting keeps opponents mutually oriented (exploratory).** **ن ظ ر B003** supplies facing so that parties remain visible to one another, while the reciprocal repetition انتظر / منتظرون creates formal symmetry. The explicit physical verb says turn aside; I therefore do not erase separation. I infer instead that shared orientation toward one future event keeps the separated parties in a temporal face-off. They are apart in posture but still confronted by the same awaited disclosure.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah follows to reactivate this section. Its surprise is retrospective in the reverse direction: the repeated **ن ظ ر** closes the surah by converting the denied future respite of 32:29 into a present, explicitly temporary interval of mutual waiting.
