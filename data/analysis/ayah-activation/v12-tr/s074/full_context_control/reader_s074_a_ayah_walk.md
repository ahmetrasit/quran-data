# Surah 74 Full-Context Reader: Ayah Walk

## 74:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A named frame of mercy.** `س م و:B005` supplies the name as a raised designation, `ء ل ه:B002` the fixed divine name, and the doubled `ر ح م:B001` mercy and tenderness. The later commands to magnify the Lord (74:3) and remember the reminder (74:54–56) make naming function as the act that places the entire warning under divine mercy; the packet supplies name, mercy, and warning, while I infer that the opening designation governs what follows. The basmalah therefore reads not as a detached formula but as the interpretive frame of the severe discourse.
2. **Protective, generative care.** The two mercy adjectives also admit `ر ح م:B003` (womb) alongside `ر ح م:B001`. The surah moves from creation in isolation (74:11), through moral formation, to protective caution and forgiveness (74:56); I infer a care-to-formation arrow from the womb image. This leaves a second reading in which mercy shelters and brings forth the addressee even while the ensuing warning disciplines him.

### Retrospective surprises

The closing pairing of protective caution and forgiveness in 74:56 unexpectedly returns the opening mercy from sentiment to a mechanism of shelter from consequence.

## 74:1 — يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْمُدَّثِّرُ

### Activated readings

1. **The wrapped person summoned out of enclosure.** `د ث ر:B002` is the outer wrap over an inner garment. The immediate `قُمْ` in 74:2 and the later commands concerning garments in 74:4 make the covering a spatial state from which public action begins; the packet gives covering followed by rising, while I infer that rising reverses enclosure. The address becomes a transition from protected inwardness to exposed warning.
2. **The one amid abundance.** `د ث ر:B001` is piled-up wealth or abundance. The later portrait of extended wealth, present sons, prepared ease, and desire for increase (74:12–15) activates this branch by contrast; I infer that the title can make accumulated coverings and accumulated goods structurally analogous. On this reading, the call also warns against being immobilized by abundance.
3. **The hidden idle sleeper, exploratorily.** `د ث ر:B006` names a hidden, idle, sleepy man. Its fit is weaker because the surface form directly favors wrapping, but `قُمْ` supplies the corrective motion. The address can thus carry a diagnostic edge: hidden inactivity is being interrupted.

### Retrospective surprises

The wealthy figure of 74:11–15 and the fleeing herd of 74:50–51 make the opening wrap look less merely domestic: it belongs to a surah concerned with coverings, concealment, and forced emergence.

## 74:2 — قُمْ فَأَنذِرْ

### Activated readings

1. **Bodily rising into a task.** `ق و م:B002` supplies upright bodily rising, `ق و م:B003` resolve to undertake a matter, and `ن ذ ر:B001` a warning that awakens fear and caution. Their imperative sequence supplies motion then speech; I infer that standing enables the warning. The ayah reads as mobilization, not only instruction: assume an upright public station and make others alert.
2. **A proleptic resurrection call.** `ق و م:B013` is resurrection and rising of the Hour, while 74:8–10 supplies the struck instrument and severe event-day. I supply the retrospective arrow from that future rising back to the present imperative. The caller's own rising becomes a small enacted anticipation of the universal rising he warns about.

### Retrospective surprises

The explicit `نَذِيرًا لِّلْبَشَرِ` at 74:36 confirms the warning role, while 74:37 surprisingly turns standing into a choice of advancing or delaying.

## 74:3 — وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرْ

### Activated readings

1. **Recognize authority as incomparable greatness.** `ر ب ب:B001` gives lordship, ownership, and authority; `ك ب ر:B001` gives greatness, and `ك ب ر:B003` the act of regarding something as great. The fronted object marks the Lord as the exclusive target; I infer that recognition of authority is enacted by magnification. The command concerns reordered valuation, not physical enlargement.
2. **Magnify the nurturer.** `ر ب ب:B002` supplies repair, nurture, and completion. Placed before purification, severance, restraint, and patience (74:4–7), this branch assigns the Lord the role of forming the warned agent; I infer that the commands are instruments of that nurture. The ayah can therefore magnify sustaining formation as well as dominion.

### Retrospective surprises

The man's `ٱسْتَكْبَرَ` in 74:23 and the `كُبَر` of 74:35 create a three-way correction: greatness belongs to the Lord, self-greatness withdraws from truth, and the warning itself is great.

## 74:4 — وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرْ

### Activated readings

1. **Clean garments and enacted purification.** `ث و ب:B003` directly supplies clothing, while `ط ه ر:B001` removes defilement and `ط ه ر:B003` supplies practical washing. The object-command construction makes garments the material patient; I infer no further transfer. This remains a concrete preparation of the outward layer for the public task.
2. **Clean the self and conduct.** The same `ث و ب:B003` explicitly includes garment imagery for the self, and `ط ه ر:B005` cleanses self, heart, and conduct from blame. The following command to sever from disturbance (74:5) and the later list of failed practices (74:43–46) supply the moral field; I infer that garment condition displays agent condition. The ayah then orders ethical decontamination, not merely laundering.
3. **Prepare the garment for returning recompense.** `ث و ب:B002` images recompense returning to the doer. This dormant branch is activated by `كُلُّ نَفْسٍ بِمَا كَسَبَتْ رَهِينَةٌ` in 74:38; I infer that cleansing what clothes the self changes what returns from its action. The fixed ayah can be heard as preparation for moral return.

### Retrospective surprises

The hostage-for-earnings formula of 74:38 makes the root's recompense branch newly audible, and the absence of prayer and feeding in 74:43–44 specifies what unclean conduct can look like.

## 74:5 — وَٱلرُّجْزَ فَٱهْجُرْ

### Activated readings

1. **Sever the source of destabilization.** `ر ج ز:B001` gives disturbance and successive motion; `ه ج ر:B001` gives severance and separation. The object-first command makes disturbance the thing from which connection is cut; I infer that separation blocks its continued transmission. The ayah reads as removal from a destabilizing process, not simply dislike of an object.
2. **Abandon corrupt speech.** `ه ج ر:B003` is foul, improper speech, and `ر ج ز:B002` is rajaz verse. The later manufactured verdict, “magic handed down” and “human speech” (74:24–25), followed by communal plunging into talk (74:45), activates a verbal mechanism; I infer that patterned or circulating speech can carry disturbance. The command can therefore include severing oneself from debasing discourse and its social repetition.

### Retrospective surprises

The sequence of calculated speech in 74:18–25 and reciprocal immersion in 74:45 makes the verbal reading much stronger than it appears locally.

## 74:6 — وَلَا تَمْنُن تَسْتَكْثِرُ

### Activated readings

1. **Do not weaponize favor to obtain more.** `م ن ن:B003` is placing a weighty favor on someone or reminding them of it; `ك ث ر:B001` is increase and `ك ث ر:B002` rivalry in abundance. The juxtaposed verbs supply favor and sought abundance, while I infer the transactional arrow “favor → larger return.” The prohibition rejects warning or service as leverage for accumulation.
2. **Do not interrupt the task by counting it as much.** `م ن ن:B001` is cutting off or diminishing an extension, and `ك ث ر:B003` includes attaching abundance to one's speech or claims. With the sustained imperative chain of 74:2–7, I infer that self-assessment as “much” can cut continuity. The ayah can prohibit both interruption and inflated reckoning of one's contribution.

### Retrospective surprises

The man's extended wealth and demand for further increase in 74:12–15 supplies the concrete economy that the prohibition refuses; 74:31 later contrasts it with an increase in faith.

## 74:7 — وَلِرَبِّكَ فَٱصْبِرْ

### Activated readings

1. **Restraint directed exclusively to the Lord.** `ر ب ب:B001` supplies lordship and `ص ب ر:B001` restraint from panic. The fronted prepositional phrase assigns the restraint's beneficiary or reason before the command; I infer that orientation to the Lord stabilizes the agent. Patience reads as governed self-restraint rather than passive delay.
2. **Endurance within severe, inescapable pressure.** `ص ب ر:B006` is severe inescapable trouble, activated by the arduous ascent of 74:17 and Saqar's exhaustive action in 74:26–29. `ر ب ب:B002` adds nurturing completion; I infer that the Lord's formation enables endurance under pressure. The command anticipates a mission whose resistance is structurally hard, not momentarily inconvenient.

### Retrospective surprises

The repeated coercive and exhaustive images of 74:17 and 74:26–29 turn patience into containment under real pressure, while 74:31's unknown “forces of your Lord” enlarges its support horizon.

## 74:8 — فَإِذَا نُقِرَ فِى ٱلنَّاقُورِ

### Activated readings

1. **A strike into a hollow that initiates the event.** The doubled root joins `ن ق ر:B001`, striking until a mark or hole is made, with `ن ق ر:B005`, a hollowed wooden vessel. The passive verb and instrument construction provide impact and receptacle; I infer impact → emitted summons. The ayah reads as a material trigger whose force comes from percussion and hollowness together.
2. **A minimal sound with maximal reach.** `ن ق ر:B002` is a quick click or faint rustle, while `ن ق ر:B008` is selectively calling one out from a group. The later division of people into rejecters, believers, right-hand companions, and criminals activates selection; I infer that the sounded signal sorts those whom it summons. The event can begin with a sharply discrete sound rather than a diffuse spectacle.

### Retrospective surprises

The precise count in 74:30–31 and the later sorting of persons make the selective-call branch unexpectedly functional: one signal exposes differentiated responses.

## 74:9 — فَذَٰلِكَ يَوْمَئِذٍۢ يَوْمٌ عَسِيرٌ

### Activated readings

1. **A severe occasion rather than merely a calendar day.** `ي و م:B003` is an event-day or severe occasion, and `ع س ر:B001` is difficulty and severity. The deictic consequence of the sounding in 74:8 supplies the event boundary; I infer signal → onset of ordeal. The ayah identifies a concentrated crisis.
2. **An entangling day.** `ع س ر:B004` supplies opposition, entanglement, and making difficult. Later every self is fixed by what it earned (74:38), while intercession gives no benefit (74:48); I infer that accumulated commitments obstruct release. The day's difficulty is thus relational and juridical, not only affective.

### Retrospective surprises

The hostage and failed-intercession mechanism of 74:38 and 74:48 explains why the day is hard: prior acquisitions have become binding impediments.

## 74:10 — عَلَى ٱلْكَٰفِرِينَ غَيْرُ يَسِيرٍۢ

### Activated readings

1. **Not open or ready for those who covered truth.** `ك ف ر:B003` supplies rejection or covering of truth; `غ ي ر:B005` supplies negation or otherness; `ي س ر:B001` is ease and ready opening. The preposition places the difficulty upon the rejecters, while I infer their covering blocks the opening. The line reads causally: concealment of signs produces a closed passage.
2. **Nothing slight for the concealment-practitioners.** `ك ف ر:B001` gives covering generally, and `ي س ر:B002` littleness or small amount. The later fire that neither leaves a residue nor spares (74:28) activates magnitude; I infer that exhaustive exposure reverses attempted concealment. `غَيْرُ يَسِير` can therefore deny smallness as well as ease.

### Retrospective surprises

The uncovering dawn of 74:34 and the exposed skin of 74:29 retrospectively reveal a covering-versus-disclosure system behind the rejecters' difficulty.

## 74:11 — ذَرْنِى وَمَنْ خَلَقْتُ وَحِيدًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Leave the originator alone with the originated one.** `و ذ ر:B003` is leaving or letting be; `خ ل ق:B002` is originating into being; `و ح د:B001` is isolation. The coordinated object construction isolates speaker and created person from third-party intervention; I infer that sole origination grounds sole adjudication. The command removes imagined mediators from the relation.
2. **The created one began as a single, proportioned unit.** `خ ل ق:B001` gives measuring and proportioning, while `و ح د:B002` gives one as a single unit. The following inventory of wealth, sons, and prepared ease (74:12–14) supplies later accretion; I infer origin in singularity → subsequent endowment. `وَحِيدًا` then punctures the person's apparent self-sufficiency by recalling his unaccompanied starting condition.

### Retrospective surprises

Saqar's “does not leave” in 74:28 reverses `ذَرْنِي`, while every self's individual pledge in 74:38 returns the wealthy man to the singular condition named here.

## 74:12 — وَجَعَلْتُ لَهُۥ مَالًۭا مَّمْدُودًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Granted wealth stretched across reach and time.** `ج ع ل:B001` is bringing or making into being, `م و ل:B001` acquiring and having wealth, and `م د د:B001` lengthwise extension. The first-person giver and beneficiary construction assign source and recipient; I infer that extension is supplied, not self-generated. The wealth reads as an endowed field with broad reach.
2. **A connected supply that keeps increasing.** `م د د:B002` is increase that supplies another, and `م د د:B004` is extended time or respite. The later desire for further increase (74:15) activates both continuity and duration; I infer ongoing supply → expectation of entitlement. The verse describes not only quantity but a replenishing temporal arrangement.

### Retrospective surprises

The count made a trial in 74:30–31 and every self pledged by acquisition in 74:38 convert this extension from proof of privilege into accountable provision.

## 74:13 — وَبَنِينَ شُهُودًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Sons present as social support.** `ب ن ي:B007` supplies filiation and derived belonging; `ش ه د:B001` supplies presence with witnessing. Following wealth and preceding prepared ease, the sons occupy the role of available household strength; I infer presence → social reinforcement. `شُهُودًا` reads as continually at hand, not merely counted offspring.
2. **Sons as witnesses to the endowment and response.** `ش ه د:B002` is testimony from knowledge and `ش ه د:B008` a witnessing sign. The later distinction among groups according to response (74:31) and the dialogue exposing deeds (74:40–47) activates evidentiary presence; I infer those who share the endowed life can attest to it. The sons become part of the person's evidence environment.

### Retrospective surprises

The public classifications of 74:31 and the self-incriminating dialogue of 74:40–47 make “present” unexpectedly judicial: social nearness can become testimony.

## 74:14 — وَمَهَّدتُّ لَهُۥ تَمْهِيدًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **A thoroughly smoothed field of life.** The verb-noun repetition intensifies `م ه د:B002`, smoothed bedding or a spread place, and `م ه د:B003`, preparing and smoothing a matter. The preceding wealth and sons supply the materials of ease; I infer preparation → unobstructed movement. The ayah describes arranged conditions, not a single gift.
2. **A cradle-like preparation.** `م ه د:B001` is a child's prepared cradle. Creation “alone” in 74:11 and the later demand for more in 74:15 activate a developmental contrast; I infer cradle-like care → grown entitlement. The prepared life can be read as sustaining dependence that the recipient misrecognizes as personal possession.

### Retrospective surprises

The demanded private scrolls of 74:52 repeat the entitlement pattern: a prepared common field is rejected in favor of individually tailored provision.

## 74:15 — ثُمَّ يَطْمَعُ أَنْ أَزِيدَ

### Activated readings

1. **Hope hardened into an expectation of further grant.** `ط م ع:B001` is heartfelt desire and hope; `ز ي د:B001` is increase and growth. `ثُمَّ` places the desire after an already extensive endowment, and the first-person verb retains the giver; I infer prior grant → presumed future increase. The line exposes desire as entitlement rather than neutral hope.
2. **Bidding for more.** `ز ي د:B004` is seeking or bidding for more, and `ط م ع:B002` is enticement that makes desire grow. Extended wealth and prepared ease (74:12–14) provide the enticement; I infer abundance stimulates its own escalation. The ayah reads as a feedback loop in which provision becomes the argument for further provision.

### Retrospective surprises

Believers “increase in faith” in 74:31, revealing a surprising contrast between two growth systems: accumulation seeks more of the grant, while the sign increases trust.

## 74:16 — كَلَّآ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ لِءَايَٰتِنَا عَنِيدًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Resistance to visible signs.** `ء ي ي:B003` supplies a visible sign, and `ع ن د:B001` deviation from straightness and resistance to truth. The dative relation makes the signs the object against which resistance is maintained; I infer visible indication → deliberate counter-direction. The refusal is not lack of evidence but active opposition to it.
2. **Sideward separation from the sign-bearing group.** `ع ن د:B002` is sideward leaning and separation from the group. The later turning away (74:23, 49) and herd-flight (74:50–51) activate bodily lateral motion; I infer inward resistance becomes outward withdrawal. `عَنِيدًا` thus reads socially and spatially as well as intellectually.

### Retrospective surprises

The repeated side-turning and flight in 74:23 and 74:49–51 turn resistance into a visible trajectory, not merely a hidden opinion.

## 74:17 — سَأُرْهِقُهُۥ صَعُودًا

### Activated readings

1. **Coercive burdening with an arduous ascent.** `ر ه ق:B003` is coercive burdening and `ص ع د:B003` arduous uphill hardship. The causative future and direct object supply imposer, burdened person, and imposed ascent; I infer resistance → compelled climb. The line reads as sustained labor under pressure rather than one instantaneous blow.
2. **An overwhelming rise that catches up.** `ر ه ق:B001` is covering or overwhelming and `ر ه ق:B002` nearing and catching up; `ص ع د:B001` is upward ascent. The man's prior expectation of continued increase activates upward motion, while the threat reverses its value; I infer sought ascent → punitive ascent that overtakes him. “More” becomes an enclosing climb.

### Retrospective surprises

The advance-or-delay choice of 74:37 reveals that ascent has a voluntary counterpart; the coerced ascent here follows refusal to advance toward warning.

## 74:18 — إِنَّهُۥ فَكَّرَ وَقَدَّرَ

### Activated readings

1. **Internal deliberation followed by calculated arrangement.** `ف ك ر:B001` is turning a matter over in the mind, and `ق د ر:B005` arranging by estimate and deliberation. The verb sequence gives reflection before formulation; I infer thought → engineered verdict. The line diagnoses the later saying as a product, not a spontaneous perception.
2. **Measuring the message down to a usable proportion.** `ق د ر:B001` is measure reaching a thing's extent and `ق د ر:B006` fitting proportion. The concise labels in 74:24–25 provide the output; I infer that the thinker compresses what he encounters into a socially manageable category. The verse then reads as rhetorical calibration.

### Retrospective surprises

The exact number and its interpretive effects in 74:30–31 make “measuring” unexpectedly thematic: one calculation fabricates dismissal, while another count exposes readers.

## 74:19 — فَقُتِلَ كَيْفَ قَدَّرَ

### Activated readings

1. **A death-formula directed at the calculation.** `ق ت ل:B001` supplies killing, while `ق د ر:B005` supplies arrangement by estimate. The passive malediction interrupts the process after 74:18; I infer that condemnation targets the engineered method as well as its agent. The line does not admire ingenuity: it marks the calculation as self-destructive.
2. **Irony of claimed mastery.** `ق ت ل:B003` is complete mastery of a fact, set against the interrogative force of `كَيْفَ` for which the packet has no accepted branch inventory. The packet supplies mastery and repeated questioning, while I infer irony from their collision: “what mastery was this?” The repeated measure is exposed as pseudo-command of the matter.

### Retrospective surprises

The later settled knowledge and doubt in 74:31 sharpen the irony: calculated mastery is not certainty, and its product destabilizes rather than settles.

## 74:20 — ثُمَّ قُتِلَ كَيْفَ قَدَّرَ

### Activated readings

1. **Re-condemnation after an interval.** `ق ت ل:B001` and `ق د ر:B005` recur unchanged, but `ثُمَّ` adds sequence after the first malediction. The packet supplies literal repetition and delay; I infer that rereading the calculation yields renewed condemnation rather than revision. The line performs iterative rejection of the fabricated estimate.
2. **The estimator subdued by his own procedure.** `ق ت ل:B002` is subduing or training until force is softened. Repetition encloses the calculating verbs before the visible sequence of looking, frowning, retreating, and speaking (74:21–25); I infer the procedure progressively masters its maker. This exploratory reading makes the repeated passive a loss of agency.

### Retrospective surprises

The bodily sequence in 74:21–23 fills the interval implied by `ثُمَّ`: calculation is not clean cognition but a process that imprints itself on face and movement.

## 74:21 — ثُمَّ نَظَرَ

### Activated readings

1. **Looking to apprehend after calculating.** `ن ظ ر:B001` is directing sight or insight to grasp a thing. Its position after thinking and estimating makes sight a verification stage; I infer internal model → outward inspection. The ayah exposes a staged decision process rather than an immediate report.
2. **Watching for an opportune formulation.** `ن ظ ر:B002` includes watching for a time and granting respite, while `ن ظ ر:B010` is examining through mutual looking or debate. The later public sayings (74:24–25) activate a social audience; I infer the look gauges when and how a verdict will work. The glance can be strategic as well as perceptual.

### Retrospective surprises

The later demand for private open scrolls in 74:52 suggests that the problem was never absence of something to inspect; the look was oriented toward control of the response.

## 74:22 — ثُمَّ عَبَسَ وَبَسَرَ

### Activated readings

1. **Distress fixed on the face.** `ع ب س:B001` is frowning from distress or anger, and `ب س ر:B002` grimacing with severe displeasure. Their coordination assigns the face the role of externalizing the calculation; I infer inward resistance → visible contraction. The ayah makes interpretation corporeal.
2. **A verdict forced before ripeness.** `ب س ر:B001` is freshness before its proper time. Coming after deliberation yet before withdrawal and speech, it activates an ironic timing mechanism; I infer pressure for a usable verdict → premature formulation. The grimace can mark an unripe conclusion being forced into readiness.

### Retrospective surprises

The immediate slogans of 74:24–25 validate the premature-fruit reading: a complex sign is reduced to ready labels before it is allowed to disclose itself.

## 74:23 — ثُمَّ أَدْبَرَ وَٱسْتَكْبَرَ

### Activated readings

1. **Bodily retreat joined to self-magnification.** `د ب ر:B003` is turning the back and withdrawing; `ك ب ر:B006` is grandeur and self-magnification. The coordinated verbs make retreat and pride one event; I infer that self-enlargement enables withdrawal from the sign. The ayah reads arrogance as directional movement.
2. **Perverse management of outcomes.** `د ب ر:B006` is managing by considering outcomes, while `ك ب ر:B003` is regarding something as great. After 74:18's calculation, this branch suggests strategic consequence-management; I infer he turns the matter toward a status-preserving result and magnifies himself within it. This exploratory reading does not replace physical retreat but preserves a second mechanism beneath it.

### Retrospective surprises

Night's retreat in 74:33 reverses this act: his turning back deepens concealment, whereas the night's turning back enables dawn's disclosure.

## 74:24 — فَقَالَ إِنْ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحْرٌۭ يُؤْثَرُ

### Activated readings

1. **A spoken charge of transmitted deception.** `ق و ل:B001` supplies uttered speech, `س ح ر:B002` diversion and deception, and `ء ث ر:B002` transmitting a report until handed down. The restrictive formula compresses the object into one category; I infer transmission → suspicion of copied manipulation. The statement alleges an inherited technique rather than direct disclosure.
2. **A circulating slogan that leaves a trace.** `ق و ل:B007` is circulating talk or public report, and `ء ث ر:B003` is a remaining sign pointing to what was. Because 74:18–23 shows manufacture before speech, I infer this utterance is designed to circulate and shape later perception. `يُؤْثَرُ` can therefore make the accusation itself the handed-down artifact.
3. **Prioritized magic, exploratorily.** `ء ث ر:B001` is putting something first. The restrictive syntax suppresses alternatives; I infer the speaker gives “magic” priority over every other account. This branch changes the line from a neutral classification into an act of forced ranking.

### Retrospective surprises

The reminder's repeated offer in 74:49 and 74:54–56 reveals the accusation's irony: what he calls inherited manipulation is presented later as an admonition that each person may actively remember.

## 74:25 — إِنْ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا قَوْلُ ٱلْبَشَرِ

### Activated readings

1. **Reduction to human utterance.** `ق و ل:B001` is uttered speech and `ب ش ر:B002` the human being as visibly skinned creature. The restrictive construction excludes other sources; I infer visible human mediation → claim of merely human origin. The verdict collapses message into the surface status of its speaker.
2. **A public doctrine attached to outward appearance.** `ق و ل:B013` is held doctrine and `ق و ل:B007` circulating report; `ب ش ر:B001` is visible outer skin or surface. The later `لَوَّاحَةٌ لِّلْبَشَرِ` (74:29) activates the same root's surface branch; I infer fixation on surface → a socially repeatable doctrine of reduction. The ayah then criticizes an epistemology that treats visible mediation as exhaustive explanation.

### Retrospective surprises

Saqar's marking of `البشر` in 74:29 turns the word from “humanity” toward exposed surface, making the speaker's surface-based reduction recoil upon the human surface itself.

## 74:26 — سَأُصْلِيهِ سَقَرَ

### Activated readings

1. **Causing direct encounter with fire.** `ص ل ي:B003` is encountering fire and its heat. The future causative and named destination provide agent, patient, and thermal field; I infer resistance → imposed contact. The threat is experiential, not merely locative.
2. **Fire as harsh straightening.** `ص ل ي:B004` includes kindling, roasting, and straightening by fire. The preceding deviation `عَنِيدًا` (74:16) activates straightening by contrast; I infer crooked resistance → coercive thermal correction. This is a distinct material reading, not a claim that correction succeeds morally.
3. **A trap entered.** `ص ل ي:B005` supplies snares and traps. The later question “what inserted you into Saqar?” (74:42) activates enclosure through a path; I infer the accumulated acts lead into a terminal catch. Saqar reads as capture as well as heat.

### Retrospective surprises

The path/insertion verb in 74:42 and the failed help of 74:48 make the trap branch unexpectedly coherent: entry is traceable, but joining assistance cannot release the caught.

## 74:27 — وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا سَقَرُ

### Activated readings

1. **A limit placed on ordinary knowing.** `د ر ي:B001` gives knowing and awareness. The doubled interrogative structure supplies an epistemic gap; I infer that the named object exceeds prior categories. The line suspends the reader before the following functional description.
2. **Being set toward the target.** `د ر ي:B002` is aiming at and setting upon something, while `د ر ي:B003` adds concealed hunting. The later donkeys fleeing a hunting force (74:50–51) activates this dormant field; I infer the question does not only inform but turns the addressee's attention toward a pursuing reality. The ayah can function as orientation under threat.

### Retrospective surprises

The explicit hunt image in 74:50–51 retroactively sharpens the root's aiming and concealed-hunt branches, making epistemic attention feel like being brought within range.

## 74:28 — لَا تُبْقِى وَلَا تَذَرُ

### Activated readings

1. **Neither residue nor spared subject.** `ب ق ي:B002` is a remaining portion or residue, `ب ق ي:B003` sparing or keeping alive, and `و ذ ر:B003` leaving something be. The paired negations cover object and process; I infer comprehensive action → no remainder and no exempted patient. The line defines Saqar through exhaustive non-preservation.

## 74:55 — فَمَن شَآءَ ذَكَرَهُۥ

### Activated readings

1. **Voluntary appropriation of the reminder.** The willing variant of `ش ي ء:B001` supplies volition, and `ذ ك ر:B003` supplies bringing to mind. The indefinite relative opens the response to any willing person; I infer willing attention → actual recollection. The reminder becomes personally operative rather than merely publicly present.
2. **Choosing to mention or heed it.** `ذ ك ر:B004` is mention on the tongue and `ذ ك ر:B009` admonition. The pronoun keeps the act anchored to the fixed reminder; I infer remembrance can become acknowledgment and conduct. Cognitive recall and voiced heeding remain coexisting outcomes.

### Retrospective surprises

The very next ayah supplies the decisive correction: human willing is not erased, but it is nested within divine willing.

## 74:56 — وَمَا يَذْكُرُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ ۚ هُوَ أَهْلُ ٱلتَّقْوَىٰ وَأَهْلُ ٱلْمَغْفِرَةِ

### Activated readings

1. **Remembering nested within divine willing.** `ذ ك ر:B003` is bringing to mind, and the willing variant of `ش ي ء:B001` supplies willing. The exception construction places human recollection inside divine will; the packet supplies both acts, while I infer enablement rather than coercive identity between them. This corrects an autonomous reading of 74:55 without cancelling its human subject.
2. **The one fit to receive protective caution.** `ء ه ل:B003` is being fit or worthy, and `و ق ي:B002` is placing the self in protective caution. Repetition of `أَهْل` assigns fittingness to God in two relations; I infer recognition of divine worth → self-guarding before harm and wrongdoing. `أَهْلُ ٱلتَّقْوَى` reads as the proper object and ground of caution.
3. **The one fit to shield from consequence.** `غ ف ر:B002` is forgiving and shielding from consequence, while `غ ف ر:B001` is protective covering. Paired with caution, forgiveness supplies protection after failure as caution supplies protection before it; I infer the two form a temporal shelter around accountable action. The surah closes severe warning with a non-identical pair: preventive guarding and consequential covering.
4. **Belonging and familiar company, exploratorily.** `ء ه ل:B001` is people bound by closeness and belonging, and `ء ه ل:B004` familiar habitation. The duplicated `أَهْل` can therefore suggest that caution and forgiveness properly belong with God; I infer divine willing can bring remembering into a field of belonging rather than estrangement. This relational reading remains secondary to “fit/worthy” but is visibly supported by the repeated noun.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah follows. The closing surprise is retrospective across the whole surah: the final roots turn warning, fear, and exposure into a paired architecture of preventive protection and forgiving shelter.
