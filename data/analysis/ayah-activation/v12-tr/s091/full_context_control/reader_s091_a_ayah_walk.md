# S91 v12 full-context ayah walk

## 91:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Invocation as raised designation that frames the whole oath-chain.** Lexical evidence: س م و B005 "name as raised designation"; ء ل ه B002 "Allah as a fixed name in oath and invocation formulas"; ر ح م B001 "mercy and tenderness." Structural cue: the basmalah's `بسم` stands before a surah that opens with a sequence of oath-like cosmic nouns. Assigned role: the "name" is not only a label but a raised public designation under which the following witnessed order is read. Abductive move: I supply the arrow that an initial formula can govern the evidentiary atmosphere of later oaths; the packet supplies the formula and the repeated cosmic witnessing. Reading change: the basmalah becomes an opening authorization for reading sun, moon, sky, earth, and soul as signs operating under merciful divine naming, not a detachable preface.

2. **Mercy as the counter-pressure beneath destruction.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B001 "mercy and tenderness"; ر ب ب B002 "repair, nurture, and completion" in 91:14; د م د م B001 "complete eradication" in 91:14; ع ق ب B006/B007 outcome and punitive consequence in 91:15. Structural cue: the packet moves from doubled mercy to a later Lord who crushes a people without fear of aftermath. Assigned role: mercy supplies the default relational field; later punitive leveling is a breach-response inside that field. Abductive move: I infer that the final punishment is not arbitrary force but a repair of violated order; the packet supplies divine names, messenger, she-camel, watering, denial, hamstringing, and eradication. Reading change: the basmalah reads with a latent judicial tenderness: mercy is not sentimentality but the matrix in which violated limits receive consequence.

3. **Womb/kinship as hidden context for the nafs.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B002 "kinship bond" and B003 "womb"; ن ف س B005 "birth and postpartum bleeding," B011 "living soul-self," and B013 "inner mind" in 91:7. Structural cue: a doubled رحم opening precedes a later fashioned and inspired نفس. Assigned role: رحم branches give the soul's formation an organic and relational background. Abductive move: I supply the analogy between womb/kinship and the later fashioned self; the packet supplies both the رحم inventory and the نفس root inventory. Reading change: the basmalah's mercy can be heard as generative enclosure, so the later soul is not a naked moral unit but something brought forth within care and relation.

### Retrospective surprises

Later 91:13–15 made the opening mercy more judicial than I first expected: `رسول الله`, `ناقة الله`, and `ربهم` all reactivate ء ل ه B002 as a repeated divine attribution, so the basmalah's formula becomes the source of protected belonging and authorized consequence. The harshest surprise is that ر ح م B003/B004, read beside ع ق ر B004/B007, makes the hamstringing feel like an attack on generativity itself; mercy is therefore not only soft beneficence but the defended condition of life.

## 91:1 — وَٱلشَّمْسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Sunlight as exposure that makes moral states inspectable.** Lexical evidence: ش م س B001 "sun and daylight"; ض ح و B002 "open exposure to sun and visible prominence"; ج ل و B001 "uncovering and manifestation" in 91:3; د س و B001 "hidden insertion or concealment" in 91:10. Structural cue: the first oath pairs the sun with its forenoon brightness, then the packet later contrasts manifestation with concealment. Assigned role: ضحى is the exposing surface on which hidden moral insertion can be judged. Abductive move: I supply the direction from physical brightness to epistemic exposure; the packet supplies daylight, unveiling, and concealment. Reading change: the ayah is not merely cosmic scenery; it initiates a visibility regime in which the later self cannot keep its دسّى hidden.

2. **Daylight as productive, public time.** Lexical evidence: ض ح و B001 "forenoon daylight extending through the day"; ض ح و B003 "forenoon meal and early-day grazing"; ف ل ح B003 "tiller who splits the ground" and B005 "success with lasting good" in 91:9; ز ك و B001 "growth and increase" in 91:9. Structural cue: early light leads, in the packet's middle, to cultivation/growth language around the soul. Assigned role: forenoon is an activator of work, feeding, and growth. Abductive move: I infer that moral purification is figured by productive daylight cultivation; the packet supplies forenoon/grazing plus tilling/growth branches. Reading change: "her duha" becomes an ecological and agricultural opening in which successful self-purification is like growth under usable light.

3. **The sun as a potentially worshipped object recontained by the divine name.** Lexical evidence: ش م س B001 sun; ء ل ه B001 "worship and the worshipped one, including the sun when named for being worshipped"; ء ل ه B002 fixed Allah-name in 91:0 and 91:13. Structural cue: the sun appears after the basmalah and before a later "messenger of Allah" and "she-camel of Allah." Assigned role: the sun's possible object-of-worship branch is activated only to be subordinated by the framing divine name. Abductive move: I infer that the oath's object is witness, not deity; the packet supplies the root branch where sun may be worshipped and the repeated Allah anchoring. Reading change: the ayah holds a polemical edge: the magnificent sun is not a rival god but a witnessed creature under Allah's name.

### Retrospective surprises

91:10 and 91:14 sharpened the first light-image: the sun's ضحى did not simply expose the soul but also exposed the she-camel's violated body. The no-spark branch of خ ي ب B002 made the earlier sunlight unexpectedly diagnostic: a world filled with sun/dawn/unveiling can still meet a self that produces no answering flame.

## 91:2 — وَٱلْقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Following as ordered succession, later mirrored by consequence.** Lexical evidence: ت ل و B001 "following in sequence"; ع ق ب B005 "coming after in succession" and B006 "the end and outcome" in 91:15. Structural cue: moon follows sun near the beginning; outcome follows deed at the end. Assigned role: تلا supplies the orderly secondness that عُقبى later moralizes. Abductive move: I supply the analogy from celestial succession to moral succession; the packet supplies both follow-roots and final consequence. Reading change: the moon's following becomes an early model of a universe where things come after one another lawfully, making the last aftermath less sudden.

2. **Moonlight as derivative disclosure with ambivalence.** Lexical evidence: ق م ر B001 "moon and its light"; ق م ر B003 "approaching or hunting by moonlight"; ق م ر B009 "sleeplessness in moonlight"; ج ل و B008 "searching gaze" in 91:3. Structural cue: moon follows a feminine `ها`, then day unveils and night covers. Assigned role: moonlight is a secondary light that can guide, expose, hunt, and disturb. Abductive move: I infer that a reflected or following light can both reveal and make vulnerable; the packet supplies moonlight/hunting/sleeplessness and later searching gaze. Reading change: the ayah is no longer a simple night-sky image; it activates a watchful, derivative illumination that prepares for the soul's exposed inner knowledge.

3. **Remainder and liability after following.** Lexical evidence: ت ل و B003 "remainder after what came before"; ت ل و B004 "liability or right that follows its holder"; ذ ن ب B006 "portion or allotted share" in 91:14; ع ق ب B010 "substitution and retained liability" in 91:15. Structural cue: a following moon early in the oath-chain anticipates retained consequences after the denial/hamstringing narrative. Assigned role: تلا is read not only as spatial/temporal following but as residual claim. Abductive move: I supply the legal/obligational arrow from "what follows" to "what remains owing"; the packet supplies branch inventories of remainder, liability, sin/share, and outcome. Reading change: the moon's following becomes a quiet juridical grammar: what comes after carries claims from what preceded.

### Retrospective surprises

91:15 made تلا more important than قمر itself: ع ق ب B005/B006 turns the moon's following into the surah's first grammar of consequence. I was also surprised that ت ل و B004 liability after following resonated with ذ ن ب B006 and ع ق ب B010, making the moon's ordered following a quiet legal preview of retained consequence after the crime.

## 91:3 — وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Day as active unveiling, not merely time.** Lexical evidence: ن ه ر B002 "day opening with light"; ج ل و B001 "uncovering and manifestation"; ج ل و B007 "daylight and clear sky." Structural cue: the verb جلّى intensifies the noun نهار, and this sits between moon-following and night-covering. Assigned role: day performs disclosure. Abductive move: I supply the causal arrow that daylight removes a cover from the feminine referent; the packet supplies day/opening/uncovering branches and the later cover root غ ش و. Reading change: the ayah becomes an event of revelation, a functional counterpart to both divine inspiration and public exposure of wrongdoing.

2. **Unveiling as bride-like presentation of the hidden object.** Lexical evidence: ج ل و B003 "bridal display"; ر ح م B002/B003 kinship/womb in 91:0; ن ف س B011/B013 soul-self and inner mind in 91:7. Structural cue: repeated `ها` links the cosmic object across sun, moon, day, night, sky, earth, soul. Assigned role: the bridal-display variant makes manifestation relational and guarded, not just optical. Abductive move: I infer a transferred social scene: something is being presented for recognition; the packet supplies the repeated feminine pronoun and the display branch. Reading change: جلّىها can be heard as a formal unveiling of a reality that will later be internalized as the self's disclosed moral condition.

3. **River/opening branch makes disclosure a channeling of flow.** Lexical evidence: ن ه ر B001 "river cutting the ground with flowing water"; ن ه ر B003 "opening or widening until something flows"; ف ج ر B001 "wide splitting and outflow" in 91:8; س ق ي B003 "irrigation share and channel" in 91:13. Structural cue: day/unveiling occurs before fissure/outflow and watering rights. Assigned role: نهار is activated as open channel, making disclosure a path for flow. Abductive move: I supply an ecological analogy between light opening and water channels opening; the packet supplies river/opening, fajr-splitting, and watering-channel branches. Reading change: the ayah is not only visual; it also becomes hydraulic, opening channels through which life or breach will later run.

### Retrospective surprises

91:13's سُقياها and 91:14's بذنبهم made the river/opening branch of ن ه ر more active than a simple day/night contrast. The later water-right shows that disclosure is also channeling: what opens must be rightly apportioned, and when the channel/right is violated the Lord's leveling answers it.

## 91:4 — وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا يَغْشَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Night as covering that makes concealment morally intelligible.** Lexical evidence: ل ي ل B001 "night as opposite of day and its darkness"; غ ش و B001 "covering veil"; د س و B001 "hidden insertion or concealment" in 91:10; ك ذ ب B009 "deceptively variegated dyed cloth" in 91:11/14. Structural cue: night covers after day unveils, and later failure is expressed as burying/covering the soul. Assigned role: night provides a neutral cosmic cover that later moral concealment imitates or abuses. Abductive move: I infer that the same material action, covering, can be innocent cosmically and corrupt morally; the packet supplies both cover and concealment branches. Reading change: the ayah becomes the morally ambiguous shadow-side of the disclosure sequence, not a negative symbol by itself.

2. **Overwhelming affliction latent in the cover.** Lexical evidence: غ ش و B002 "overwhelming affliction"; د م د م B001 "complete eradication" in 91:14; ط غ ي B002 "overflowing and overwhelming force" in 91:11. Structural cue: a gentle night-covering early in the packet is later matched by overwhelming destruction. Assigned role: غشى carries a dormant disaster branch that later damdama activates. Abductive move: I supply the arrow from covering to engulfing: when bounds are violated, cover becomes total overtaking. Reading change: night covering acquires a latent threat: the same enveloping motion that can cloak can also crush.

3. **Visitation/coming-upon as the form of moral event.** Lexical evidence: غ ش و B003 "coming upon or visiting"; ب ع ث B002 "dispatching and directing" in 91:12; ر س ل B001/B002 sending and messenger in 91:13. Structural cue: night "comes over" the object, later the wretched one is roused and the messenger is sent. Assigned role: غشى supplies the grammar of an event arriving upon a scene. Abductive move: I infer that the narrative events come upon the people the way night comes upon the day-field; the packet supplies coming-upon, rousing, and sending. Reading change: the ayah frames later moral crisis as an arrival/visitation, not only an internal choice.

### Retrospective surprises

91:14 retrospectively made غشى darker: the cover of night can become the engulfing totality of damdama when concealment and denial harden into violence. Still, the day/night pair kept the reading from making night evil by itself; the later crime shows the abusive human imitation of covering, not a condemnation of cosmic covering.

## 91:5 — وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Sky as built upper cover, making creation architectural.** Lexical evidence: س م و B004 "overhead sky and cover"; ب ن ي B001 "building by joining parts"; ب ن ي B002 "built form and constitution." Structural cue: the ayah pairs the upper expanse with "what built it," followed by earth spread out and soul proportioned. Assigned role: سماء supplies vertical covering; بنى supplies constructed joining and constitution. Abductive move: I infer that the cosmic roof models a made order whose parts belong together; the packet supplies upper cover, building, earth spreading, and soul leveling. Reading change: the sky is not just an object sworn by but evidence of constructive composition, later mirrored in the soul's inward formation.

2. **Name/height resonance binds basmalah and sky.** Lexical evidence: س م و B005 "name as raised designation" in 91:0; س م و B001 "rising high" and B004 "overhead sky and cover" in 91:5; ء ل ه B002 fixed Allah-name. Structural cue: the same root occurs in `بسم` and `السماء`, one at the opening formula and one in the oath-chain. Assigned role: the root س م و bridges linguistic elevation and cosmic elevation. Abductive move: I supply the analogy that naming raises and the sky is raised; the packet supplies the repeated root and branch inventory. Reading change: the ayah makes the opening "name" spatially visible: divine designation and cosmic height activate one another.

3. **Building as kinship/filiation and generated belonging.** Lexical evidence: ب ن ي B007 "filiation and derived belonging"; ر ح م B002 "kinship bond"; ن ف س B011 "living soul-self" in 91:7. Structural cue: after "what built the sky," the packet moves through earth to a fashioned soul. Assigned role: بنى's sonship branch makes created structures belong to their source rather than merely being assembled artifacts. Abductive move: I infer a generated relation between builder and built thing; the packet supplies construction and filiation branches. Reading change: "what built it" can be heard as establishing dependence and belonging, preparing "naqat Allah" as a creature marked by divine belonging later.

### Retrospective surprises

The later `ناقة الله` made ب ن ي B007 filiation/belonging feel less optional: built creation, named belonging, messenger of Allah, and she-camel of Allah all form a chain of derived dependence. 91:14 also changed "what built it" by contrast: divine building/proportioning is answered by divine re-leveling when the creature marked as belonging to Allah is cut down.

## 91:6 — وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا طَحَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Earth as lower counterpart to the built cover.** Lexical evidence: ء ر ض B001 "lower ground opposite the sky"; ط ح و B001 "spreading and extending"; س م و B004 overhead sky in 91:5. Structural cue: sky/built is followed immediately by earth/spread. Assigned role: earth receives the horizontal expansion corresponding to sky's vertical cover. Abductive move: I infer that the two ayat form a coordinated spatial frame for the later soul; the packet supplies opposite sky/earth and spreading. Reading change: earth is not isolated geography; it is the extended lower plane inside a cosmic enclosure.

2. **Spread ground as field for cultivation and purification.** Lexical evidence: ء ر ض B002 "soft fertile ground"; ط ح و B001 spreading; ف ل ح B003 "tiller who splits the ground"; ز ك و B001 "growth and increase" in 91:9. Structural cue: spread earth precedes the soul's successful زكّى and failure دسّى. Assigned role: earth becomes the cultivable substrate, while soul purification later becomes a crop-like operation. Abductive move: I infer a material analogy from land cultivation to inner cultivation; the packet supplies fertile ground, tilling, and growth branches. Reading change: the ayah turns into the outer agricultural model for the inner moral work of 91:9.

3. **Sprawling/flattening foreshadows punitive leveling.** Lexical evidence: ط ح و B006 "sprawling against the ground"; س و ي B002 "straightness and sound completion" and B009 "wide or smooth open ground"; د م د م B001 complete eradication in 91:14. Structural cue: طحىها spreads the earth early; فسوىها levels after punishment late. Assigned role: طحى's spreading can be benign creation, while later سوى becomes imposed leveling. Abductive move: I infer a reversal between creative flattening and punitive flattening; the packet supplies both spread-ground and final leveling roots. Reading change: the spread earth becomes an ominous template: what is made level for life can also be leveled over a destroyed community.

### Retrospective surprises

91:14's `فسوىها` made the benign spreading of earth unexpectedly reversible. طحىها first looked like hospitable extension, but later leveling after eradication shows a second, punitive flattening; the same horizontal field that supports cultivation can become the surface on which a community is erased.

## 91:7 — وَنَفْسٍۢ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **The self as fashioned equilibrium within cosmic measures.** Lexical evidence: ن ف س B011 "living soul-self"; س و ي B002 "internal straightness and sound completion"; س و ي B006 "middle, fairness, and an even meeting place." Structural cue: after sky built and earth spread, the soul is made even; then inspiration divides فجور/تقوى. Assigned role: سوى supplies balanced constitution before moral alternatives appear. Abductive move: I infer that the later dual moral disclosure depends on an already proportioned inner structure; the packet supplies building/spreading/fashioning sequence. Reading change: the ayah makes the self a deliberately balanced site, not a chaotic interior suddenly receiving commands.

2. **Breath/opening connects soul to dawn and day.** Lexical evidence: ن ف س B001 "breath leaving the body"; ن ف س B009 "opening or spreading like breath"; ف ج ر B002 "dawn breaking from night" in 91:8; ن ه ر B002 "day opening with light" in 91:3. Structural cue: the soul comes after cosmic opening images and before فجور/تقوى. Assigned role: نفس is read as living breath that opens, making moral inspiration an inward dawn. Abductive move: I supply the metaphor that inner discernment opens like breath/daybreak; the packet supplies breath/opening and dawn/day branches. Reading change: "and the soul" becomes respiratory and luminous: the self is a place where dawn-like distinctions can break open.

3. **Inner mind as the target of inspired intake.** Lexical evidence: ن ف س B013 "inner mind, thought, or hidden intent"; ل ه م B001 "swallowing and taking something in completely" in 91:8; د س و B001 hidden insertion in 91:10. Structural cue: "what proportioned it" is followed by "then inspired it." Assigned role: the inner mind is the vessel; ilham is the intake; dass is a rival illicit insertion. Abductive move: I infer that inspiration is an inward placing/uptake rather than only external instruction; the packet supplies inner thought, swallowing intake, and hidden insertion. Reading change: the ayah prepares the self as a receptive interior whose moral knowledge can be taken in or buried.

4. **The very thing itself: the soul as whole identity.** Lexical evidence: ن ف س B012 "the very thing itself"; س و ي B001 "equality and equivalence" and B002 sound completion. Structural cue: the repeated pronoun `ها` finally lands on نفس after cosmic referents. Assigned role: نفس B012 concentrates the prior "her" into an individuated essence. Abductive move: I infer that the cosmic pronoun-chain narrows into selfhood; the packet supplies repeated feminine pronouns and the root branch for essence. Reading change: the ayah can be read as the hinge where external order becomes internal identity.

### Retrospective surprises

The biggest later activation came from عقرواها: the soul's `سوّىها` in 91:7 is echoed by `فسوىها` after punishment, and the contrast turns inner proportion into a criterion for social consequence. The she-camel also made the body more important: نفس as life-breath and living self now sits in a sequence where another living body is protected, wounded, and avenged.

## 91:8 — فَأَلْهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقْوَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Inspiration as complete intake of two moral disclosures.** Lexical evidence: ل ه م B001 "swallowing and taking something in completely"; ف ج ر B004 "departure from right and breach of restraint"; و ق ي B002 "placing the self in protective caution." Structural cue: the verb ألهمها takes the soul as object, then gives two paired possessives: its فجور and its تقوى. Assigned role: ilham is ingestion/internalization; fujur and taqwa are the two knowable inner directions. Abductive move: I infer that the soul receives not merely data but an internal capacity to recognize both breach and guard; the packet supplies intake, breach, and caution branches. Reading change: the ayah reads less like external command and more like implanted discernment swallowed into the self.

2. **Fujur as split/outflow, taqwa as barrier/containment.** Lexical evidence: ف ج ر B001 "wide splitting and outflow"; و ق ي B001 "warding harm off with a protective barrier"; ن ه ر B003 opening/widening until flow in 91:3; س ق ي B003 irrigation channel in 91:13. Structural cue: the two nouns stand as opposites after the fashioned self. Assigned role: فجور is uncontrolled breach-flow; تقوى is protective containment. Abductive move: I supply the hydraulic moral model: water-like force must be channeled rather than burst out destructively; the packet supplies split/outflow, barrier, river opening, and watering. Reading change: the moral pair becomes material: sin is rupture of bounds, piety is guarding the channel.

3. **Taqwa as guarded gait after pain.** Lexical evidence: و ق ي B003 "guarded gait from hoof pain"; ع ق ر B002 "cutting an animal down" and B003 "disabling back or movement" in 91:14; ن و ق B002 she-camel in 91:13. Structural cue: the later narrative centers on a she-camel being hamstrung. Assigned role: the hoof-pain branch gives taqwa a bodily caution around vulnerable movement. Abductive move: I infer retroactively that proper guarding includes not violating another creature's protected movement; the packet supplies hoof-guarding, she-camel, and hamstringing. Reading change: تقواها is not abstract religiosity only; it becomes careful movement around the vulnerable body and its rights.

4. **Fujur as false dawn versus true dawn.** Lexical evidence: ف ج ر B002 "dawn breaking from night, true and false dawn distinction"; ك ذ ب B001 falsehood and B002 declaring false in 91:11/14; ج ل و B001 manifestation. Structural cue: the packet pairs revelation/unveiling with later denial. Assigned role: fujur's dawn branch introduces an ambiguous light that can mislead if not guarded. Abductive move: I infer that the soul must distinguish real disclosure from deceptive appearance; the packet supplies dawn distinction and deception/denial branches. Reading change: فجورها can include not only immoral overflow but a false-light epistemology that prepares the people's تكذيب.

### Retrospective surprises

91:13–14 made تقوى more concrete than abstract piety: the protected she-camel and her watering right activate و ق ي as guarding a vulnerable boundary in the material world. The hoof-pain branch of و ق ي, which first seemed marginal, became surprisingly apt once عقر appeared as disabling the animal's movement.

## 91:9 — قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Success as cultivation of the fashioned self.** Lexical evidence: ف ل ح B003 "tiller who splits the ground"; ف ل ح B005 "success with lasting good"; ز ك و B001 "growth and increase"; ء ر ض B002 fertile ground in 91:6. Structural cue: after earth-spreading and soul-fashioning, the one who purifies/increases it succeeds. Abductive move: I infer that success comes from properly working the soul as cultivated ground; the packet supplies tilling, growth, and fertile earth branches. Reading change: the ayah reads as disciplined cultivation, not a purely forensic declaration of success.

2. **Purification as restoring internal straightness.** Lexical evidence: ز ك و B002 "purity and rectitude"; س و ي B002 "internal straightness and sound completion" in 91:7; و ق ي B002 protective caution in 91:8. Structural cue: the soul was made even before it was morally inspired; now someone performs زكّىها. Assigned role: purification protects and restores the soul's original sound constitution. Abductive move: I infer that moral action is maintenance of a given design rather than self-invention; the packet supplies proportioning, guarding, and purification branches. Reading change: the ayah reads as keeping faith with an already well-made self.

3. **Pair/evenness branch makes success relationally balanced.** Lexical evidence: ز ك و B005 "pair and even number"; س و ي B001 equality/equivalence and B006 even meeting place; ف ج ر/و ق ي paired in 91:8. ### Retrospective surprises

The later narrative made `زكّىها` social and ecological, not only inward. Respecting the she-camel's water-share in 91:13 is a public form of guarding growth; عقر in 91:14 is the anti-zakā act, cutting growth/source and producing barrenness. This retrospectively made فلاح's agricultural branch central rather than decorative.

## 91:10 — وَقَدْ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Failure as burial of the self's visible truth.** Lexical evidence: خ ي ب B001 "missing the sought thing and being deprived of fortune"; د س و B001 "hidden insertion or concealment"; ض ح و B002 open exposure in 91:1; ج ل و B001 uncovering in 91:3. Structural cue: the loser is paired against the purifier and after a chain of unveiling images. Assigned role: دسّى is the counter-act to cosmic disclosure: hiding what should stand in light. Abductive move: I infer that failure results when the soul's inspired distinction is pushed under cover; the packet supplies failure, concealment, and exposure branches. Reading change: the ayah becomes an anti-epiphany: loss is self-concealment against the world of unveiling.

2. **Failure as fire-stick without spark.** Lexical evidence: خ ي ب B002 "a fire-stick that gives no spark"; ش م س B001 sunlight; ف ج ر B002 dawn; ل ه م B005 engulfing calamity. Structural cue: the surah begins with intense light but the failing person cannot kindle benefit from it. Assigned role: khayb B002 provides a concrete mechanism: a tool expected to produce fire fails to ignite. Abductive move: I infer that the self has access to light but cannot generate living response because it buried the capacity; the packet supplies light-sequence and no-spark failure. Reading change: خاب is not generic disappointment but wasted ignition in a light-filled cosmos.

3. **Dass as corrupt insertion rather than mere hiding.** Lexical evidence: د س و B003 "straying and spoiling"; ل ه م B001 inspired intake in 91:8; ك ذ ب B009 deceptive variegated cloth in 91:11/14. Structural cue: ألهمها puts moral knowledge into the soul; دسّىها later inserts/hides something corrupting. Assigned role: دسو B003 makes the act an adulteration of the soul's received contents. Abductive move: I infer a rival implantation: corrupt concealment competes with inspired intake; the packet supplies both verbs' inward mechanics. Reading change: the ayah reads as sabotage of inner composition, not only neglect.

### Retrospective surprises

91:11–14 clarified that دسّىها is not private self-failure only. Thamud's public denial and bodily violence show concealment turning outward as falsification of a sign. The surprise was خ ي ب B002: after all the light imagery, failure as a fire-stick that gives no spark became a compact image for moral non-response.

Reading change: the ayah reads as the social eruption of the inner فجور described earlier.

2. **Takdhib as falsifying the messenger and the protected sign.** Lexical evidence: ك ذ ب B002 "declaring false"; ر س ل B002 "messenger and message" and ء ل ه B002 in 91:13; ن و ق B002 she-camel; س ق ي B001/B002 watering right. 3. **Falsehood as failed charge, later enacted bodily.** Lexical evidence: ك ذ ب B004 "fulfilling or failing a charge"; ك ذ ب B005 "not delaying to act"; ع ق ر B002 "cutting an animal down" in 91:14. Structural cue: verbal denial in 91:11 escalates into physical hamstringing in 91:14. Assigned role: the "charge" branch makes takdhib an action that either proves itself in motion or fails morally. Abductive move: I infer that their denial becomes performative: they make their false stance true as aggression by not delaying into action; the packet supplies denial/charge/no-delay and hamstringing. 4. **Smooth high rock branch makes transgression spatial.** Lexical evidence: ط غ ي B005 "smooth rock or high outcrop"; ش ق و B004 "long rising mountain flank" in 91:12; ع ق ب B012 "steep pass and jutting rock" in 91:15. Structural cue: طغوى is followed by أشقىها and final عقبها, both with mountain/pass branches. Assigned role: ṭughwa's outcrop gives rebellion a raised, hard surface. Abductive move: I infer a terrain-image for moral excess: the group has climbed onto a high exposed transgressive stance. Reading change: "by its طغوى" gains a topographical feel, as if their rebellion is a dangerous elevation from which the wretched one is roused.

### Retrospective surprises

91:13–14 supplied the object of the denial, so 91:11 changed from a general collective moral diagnosis into the opening of a legal case: messenger, divine she-camel, and watering right are what their طغوى overruns. 91:15 then added that the overstepping creates an aftermath no one can appeal.

## 91:12 — إِذِ ٱنۢبَعَثَ أَشْقَىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **The wretched one as roused instrument of collective overflow.** Lexical evidence: ب ع ث B001 "rousing a still thing into motion"; ش ق و B002 "hardship and strenuous suffering"; ط غ ي B001/B002 overstepping/overflow in 91:11. Structural cue: `إذ` links the collective denial to the moment the most wretched is roused. Assigned role: انبعث is the activation event; أشقىها is the person in whom collective طغوى becomes motion. Abductive move: I infer that the group condition selects or energizes the individual agent; the packet supplies collective denial followed by singular rousing. Reading change: the ayah is not an isolated villain portrait but a mechanism of social transgression becoming embodied action.

2. **Dispatch without rightful sending.** Lexical evidence: ب عث B002 "dispatching and directing"; ر س ل B001/B002 sending/messenger in 91:13; ء ل ه B002 divine formula/name. Structural cue: a roused individual appears immediately before the messenger of Allah speaks. Assigned role: بعث creates a dark counter-sending, contrasted with the رسل mission. Abductive move: I infer two missions in collision: one unauthorized by collective rebellion, one authorized by Allah; the packet supplies the juxtaposition of انبعث and رسول الله. Reading change: the ayah reads as anti-messengerhood: a social impulse dispatches its agent against the divinely sent word.

3. **Hardship contest and mountain flank.** Lexical evidence: ش ق و B003 "prevailing in a hardship contest"; ش ق و B004 "long rising mountain flank"; ط غ ي B005 high outcrop in 91:11; ع ق ب B012 steep pass in 91:15. Structural cue: the root after طغوى and before عقر/عقبها is one with both suffering and climbable terrain. Assigned role: أشقىها may be the one most sunk in hardship or the one who "wins" the contest of ruin. Abductive move: I infer that moral extremity is pictured as a contest/climb where the worst reaches the decisive position; the packet supplies contest and terrain branches. Reading change: "most wretched" becomes dynamic: the one who presses farthest along the hard slope of transgression.

### Retrospective surprises

The next two ayat made `انبعث` a counter-mission. Before reading 91:13, the roused wretched one could be only an impulsive criminal; after `رسول الله`, he becomes a socially dispatched anti-messenger whose movement collides with the authorized sending. The terrain branches in ط غ ي, ش ق و, and ع ق ب also made his wretchedness feel like movement along a hard slope toward consequence.

## 91:13 — فَقَالَ لَهُمْ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ نَاقَةَ ٱللَّهِ وَسُقْيَٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **Messenger speech as boundary-setting utterance.** Lexical evidence: ق و ل B001 "uttered speech"; ر س ل B002 "messenger and message"; ء ل ه B002 fixed Allah-name; و ق ي B001/B002 protection/caution in 91:8. Structural cue: the messenger's saying appears between the roused wretched one and the hamstringing. Assigned role: the utterance installs a protective boundary around "she-camel of Allah" and "her watering." Abductive move: I infer that the elliptical phrase functions as warning/preservation; the packet supplies speech, messenger, divine attribution, and protective root nearby. Reading change: the ayah reads less as bare quotation and more as a verbal fence drawn around a creature and its water-right.

2. **The she-camel as elevated, selected sign.** Lexical evidence: ن و ق B002 "she-camel and its shape"; ن و ق B001 "elevation and height"; ن و ق B006 "choosing or selecting"; ء ل ه B002 "Allah as fixed name." Structural cue: the noun is directly annexed to Allah, paralleling `رسول الله`. Assigned role: ناقة is not generic livestock but an elevated/selected creature marked by divine belonging. Abductive move: I infer that genitive attribution turns her into a sign with inviolable status; the packet supplies she-camel, elevation/selection, and divine name. Reading change: "naqat Allah" becomes a protected selected presence, so harming her is rebellion against the ordering name, not property damage.

3. **Watering as social right and ecological share.** Lexical evidence: س ق ي B001 "giving drink"; B002 "providing a watering source"; B003 "irrigation share and channel"; ذ ن ب B006 "portion or allotted share" in 91:14. 4. **Speech as public report against circulating falsehood.** Lexical evidence: ق و ل B007 "circulating talk or public report"; ك ذ ب B001/B002 falsehood/declaring false in 91:11 and 91:14; ق و ل B014 "thing's saying as indication." ### Retrospective surprises

91:14 made the messenger's clipped saying heavier: `ناقة الله وسقياها` functions as a boundary whose violation is immediately narrated. 91:15 then made the speech legally final in retrospect: their denial challenges the messenger's public word, but the Lord's aftermath admits no follow-up challenge.

## 91:14 — فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَعَقَرُوهَا فَدَمْدَمَ عَلَيْهِمْ رَبُّهُم بِذَنۢبِهِمْ فَسَوَّىٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **From declaring false to cutting down: speech becomes bodily disabling.** Lexical evidence: ك ذ ب B002 "declaring false"; ع ق ر B002 "cutting an animal down"; ع ق ر B003 "disabling back or movement"; ن و ق B002 she-camel in 91:13. Structural cue: the fa-sequence connects denial directly to hamstringing. Assigned role: takdhib supplies the verbal refusal; aqar supplies the physical act that makes refusal irreversible. Abductive move: I infer that the body of the she-camel is the site where denial is enacted; the packet supplies the sequence and branches. Reading change: the ayah reads as the materialization of falsehood in a disabled creature.

2. **Punitive leveling as counter-building/repair.** Lexical evidence: د م د م B001 "complete eradication"; ر ب ب B001 "lordship, ownership, and authority"; ر ب ب B002 "repair, nurture, and completion"; س و ي B002 "straightness and sound completion"; س و ي B009 "wide or smooth open ground." Structural cue: the Lord crushes them for their ذنب, then سوىها echoes the earlier soul-fashioning. Assigned role: رب is the authorized owner-repairer; damdama removes the corruption; sawa levels/reorders the violated field. Abductive move: I infer that eradication functions as judicial repair of a damaged order; the packet supplies lordship, repair, eradication, and leveling. Reading change: the punishment reads not as uncontrolled rage but as sovereign re-leveling after a breach.

3. **Sin as tail/trace and allotted share.** Lexical evidence: ذ ن ب B001 "sin and culpable consequence"; ذ ن ب B002 "tail or rear end"; ذ ن ب B006 "portion or allotted share"; ع ق ب B006/B007 end/punitive consequence in 91:15. Abductive move: I infer that their act drags a tail of liability behind it and receives its allotted return; the packet supplies tail, share, and aftermath branches. 4. **Aqar as cutting the source of growth and succession.** Lexical evidence: ع ق ر B004 "barrenness and stopped generation"; B007 "cutting the source of growth"; B005 "last rare thing without successor"; ر ح م B003 womb in 91:0; ز ك و B001 growth in 91:9. ### Retrospective surprises

91:15 sharpened ذنب as tail and aftermath: `بذنبهم` is not just a cause phrase but the hinge between act and عُقبى. Looking back to 91:7, the repeated سوى also surprised me: the Lord who proportioned the soul now levels the community, so the same root marks both formative balance and judicial equalization.

## 91:15 — وَلَا يَخَافُ عُقْبَٰهَا

### Activated readings

1. **No fear of aftermath because authority owns the consequence.** Lexical evidence: خ و ف B001 "fearful anticipation of harm"; ع ق ب B006 "the end and outcome"; ر ب ب B001 "lordship, ownership, and authority" in 91:14. Structural cue: the line follows the Lord's damdama/sawa. Assigned role: خوف would be anticipation of retaliatory harm; عقبى is the result or aftermath of the act. Abductive move: I infer that the subject has no fear because the action belongs to sovereign authority, not exposed wrongdoing; the packet supplies Lordship and aftermath. Reading change: the ayah closes as judicial fearlessness, not heedless indifference.

2. **No claimant can follow up or reverse the judgment.** Lexical evidence: ع ق ب B008 "following up and challenging"; ق و ل B010 "arrogating judgment over someone" in 91:13; ك ذ ب B002 denial; ر ب ب B001 authority. Structural cue: the denial/challenge of the messenger is followed by a judgment that no one can challenge. Assigned role: عقبها is the possible review/appeal after a ruling. Abductive move: I infer a legal scene where a punished party might seek reversal, but the divine act admits no counter-claim; the packet supplies challenge/review and authority branches. Reading change: the ayah becomes a statement of non-appealability: no "معقب" can overturn the result.

3. **Outcome as the rear trace of the people's own act.** Lexical evidence: ع ق ب B002 "heel and rear trace"; B005 "coming after in succession"; ذ ن ب B002 "tail or rear end" and B006 "share" in 91:14; ت ل و B001/B003 following/remainder in 91:2. Structural cue: the packet begins with orderly following and ends with aftermath. Assigned role: عقبى is the step immediately behind the action, the trace they made. Abductive move: I infer that the punishment follows in the track of their own deed; the packet supplies follow/remainder/tail/after. Reading change: the final line reads as moral kinematics: consequence treads the heel-print of action.

4. **Aftermath as steep pass or hard residue.** Lexical evidence: ع ق ب B012 "steep pass and jutting rock"; B011 "residue and remaining trace"; ط غ ي B005 high outcrop in 91:11; ش ق و B004 mountain flank in 91:12. Structural cue: terrain branches recur across transgression, wretchedness, and consequence. Assigned role: عقبها becomes the hard terrain after the event: the pass, ledge, or residue left behind. Abductive move: I infer that moral aftermath has a topography that must be traversed by the guilty but does not threaten the judging Lord. Reading change: "her aftermath" is not abstract time after punishment only; it is the difficult remaining terrain created by rebellion.

5. **Fear as diminished/taken-away state refused to the divine actor.** Lexical evidence: خ و ف B004 "diminishing by taking away"; د م د م B001 complete eradication; ع ق ب B007 punitive consequence. Structural cue: the negated fear follows a total taking-away of the people. Assigned role: fear's "diminishing" branch is activated only by contrast: the Lord's act diminishes them, not Himself. Abductive move: I infer a reversal of vulnerability: those who overstepped are reduced; the punisher is not reduced by anticipating consequence. Reading change: لا يخاف عقبها becomes a compact statement that divine punitive action carries no vulnerability or depletion.

### Retrospective surprises

No later ayah follows within the packet, but rereading the whole sequence made the final line gather the entire following-chain: تلا, ذنب, and عقب all speak of what comes after. The ending is therefore not an appended threat; it is the last disclosure of the ordered succession already modeled by moon after sun, deed after denial, and consequence after sin.
